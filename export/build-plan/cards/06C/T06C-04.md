## Goal
Cascade RANGE pools on Meridian (fee tiers 0.05%, 0.30%, 1.00%): positions of liquidity `L` on `[p_a, p_b]`, virtual reserves while the price is inside, swaps that move `√p` within the active range and cross boundaries in price order updating `activeLiquidity` (at most 256 crossings per swap, excess input refunded), fees accruing to in-range positions in proportion to `L` through `feeGrowthGlobal` accounting, adding and removing positions through the shared intents of T06C-02 (`range` argument / `positionId`), and the TWAP and LP marks for range positions. Deterministic numeric rules make the swap output match the analytic piecewise formula.

## Implementation steps
1. Pool state: `sqrtPrice`, `activeLiquidity`, the sorted list of initialized boundaries `{sqrtBoundary, liquidityNet}` (+L at lower, −L at upper), `feeGrowthGlobalA/B` (per unit L), per-boundary `feeGrowthOutside`, positions with `feeGrowthInsideLast`. Fee tiers 5, 30, 100 bps; protocol share as configured in 22 (default 1/6, like Tidepool).
2. Add (§3.12.3): `L = liquidityForAmounts(...)` at the current price; the user deposits `amountsForLiquidity(L, ...)` (rounded up in micro-units), excess returned; boundaries initialized; if the range contains `√p`, `activeLiquidity += L`. Positions are owned by the wallet's account (`RangeLiquidity.ownerId`); `DEFI_LP_DEPOSIT` postings.
3. Swap: loop: next boundary in the direction of the trade (price order); compute the input needed to reach it at current `activeLiquidity` (after fee: `in' = in·(1 − fee)`); if the remaining input is smaller, move `√p` within the range (`swapStepYIn`/`swapStepXIn`) and stop; else move exactly to the boundary, cross it (`activeLiquidity += ±liquidityNet`, flip `feeGrowthOutside`), count the crossing, continue. Fees on each step accrue `feeGrowthGlobal += fee/activeLiquidity`. Stop after 256 crossings and refund the unused input. Output accumulated per step; reject if `< minOut` (gas still charged). With zero active liquidity between boundaries, jump to the next initialized boundary without output.
4. Remove: amounts at the current price for the position's L (rounded down), plus fees `L·(feeGrowthInside − feeGrowthInsideLast)`; `DEFI_LP_WITHDRAW` and `DEFI_LP_FEE` postings; boundaries de-initialized when their net liquidity becomes 0.
5. Pool price for oracle/TWAP is `sqrtPrice²`; range positions are not collateral (§3.12.4 table: LP tokens 0).

## Implementation steps
1. Bridge (§3.12.9): `mkt.defi.bridge` (bridge OPEN, wallet on the source chain, token is the bridge's locked asset or its wrapped twin for the reverse direction): source-chain block op locks the amount (`moveQty` to the bridge vault account, `DEFI_BRIDGE_FEE` 0.10% to `npc_ARC`); a `bridgeTransfer` row arrives at `lockTick + transferTicks (10)`: mint wrapped on the destination (`mint(…, 'CRY_TOKEN_MINT')`, quantity only) — reverse: burn wrapped, release locked. Invariant `wrappedSupply ≤ locked` is asserted in P13 by `assertBridgeInvariant` (registered as a 06C tick check that fails the tick, X22; the numbered 20 invariant owner is 20).
2. Impairment: when 12 resolves a successful attack (abstract outcome delivered by `ConflictPort06.bridgeDrain` — amount only, no method), `locked −= drained`, status IMPAIRED, `recoveryRatio = locked/wrappedSupply`, PAUSED for `PARAM.bridge_pause_ticks` (720) after any impairment: no new transfers; redemptions afterwards pro rata (`release = amount × recoveryRatio`). In-flight transfers (edge 15) are counted as locked (pro-rata recovery) and their mint on arrival is scaled by `recoveryRatio`. `mkt.bridge.status` PUBLIC `{bridge, status, recoveryRatio}`. The wrapped token's V for 05 = `recoveryRatio × underlying` (T06C-03 anchors read `wrappedValue`).
3. Keystone: custodial AUC → wAUC (Aurum transfer to the custodian's wallet, mint wAUC on Lattice) and ORE (1 ORE = 1 oz gold in the vault); the custodian's balance sheet can be impaired by 12/16 outcomes → same IMPAIRED/recovery logic.
4. NPC venue arbitrage (§3.12.10): each block, for each (token, CEX line, pool) with both prices: if `|pool − CEX|/CEX > fee_pool + fee_CEX + 0.10%`, the NPC arbitrageur swaps the pool toward the CEX mid (op enqueued with a deterministic id so it sorts by the hash rule) and lays off on the CEX next tick (`ExternalOrder`), capacity `PARAM.npc_defi_arb_cap` ($250k) × `activityScale` (N_a) per pool per tick; gaps larger than capacity persist for several ticks.
5. NPC actors (§6 item 6): miners sell each day's AUC rewards uniformly through the day (720 equal slices, NPC pool orders on the CEX); stakers keep 60% of LTX/MRD supply staked; LPs keep 80% of seed liquidity in launch pools, withdraw 30% when fear > 0.6 (liquidity vanishes when needed, 01 §7.2) and redeposit when fear < 0.4 (hysteresis: documented decision); keepers are T06C-05's; the meme launcher T06C-03's; Vesta and Mirage governance T06C-06's.

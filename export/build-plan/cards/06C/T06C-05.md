## Goal
The Quill oracle of 06 §3.12.5 (median of CEX 30-tick TWAP, deepest-pool AMM TWAP and the last oracle value; 5% deviation guard; staleness rules) also used by 06B perps' index price; the lending markets of Ferro (Lattice) and Harbor (Meridian) per §3.12.4: kinked rate model, per-block compounding indices, collateral factors / liquidation thresholds / bonuses by asset, health factor, the borrow and withdraw gate (post-op HF ≥ 1 with collateral factors), keeper liquidations by any LIC2 account up to the close factor 0.5 for exactly `repay·(1 + bonus)` of collateral at the oracle price, NPC keepers (profit after gas > $50), bad debt absorbed by reserves then socialized through a supply-index write-down (PUBLIC), the pause of new borrows on a stale oracle (edge 12), and the no-bundles / no-flash-loan rule of §3.12.11 (X9).

## Implementation steps
1. Oracle (§3.12.5), per asset feed, updated at the first block of each tick before DeFi ops: `cexTwap` = 30-tick TWAP of 05 batch clearing prices of the asset's CEX line; `ammTwap` = 30-tick TWAP of the deepest pool (`deepestPool`, `twap`); if both present and `|CEX − AMM|/CEX > 5%` → CEX TWAP alone; else median(CEX, AMM, last oracle); no CEX price in 30 ticks → AMM TWAP with `stale` flag set but usable; both stale for 60 ticks → `stale = true`, price = last. Stablecoins use their market price (not 1.00) (edge 13). Port: `oraclePrice` for 06B perps; `otherVenueLnP` = ln(deepest pool price) for 05.
2. Indices per block: `U = totalBorrowed/max(totalSupplied, 1)`; `r_b` kinked; `r_s = r_b·U·(1 − reserveFactor)`; `borrowIndex *= 1 + r_b/262,080`, `supplyIndex *= 1 + r_s/262,080` each tick (block); reserves grow by the reserve-factor share. Balances are scaled (`borrowedScaled`), so interest needs no per-account postings until repay/withdraw, where the interest part posts `DEFI_LEND_INTEREST`.
3. HF = `Σ_c coll_c·oracle_c·LT_c / Σ_d debt_d·oracle_d`; the borrow and withdraw gate uses collateral factors (`cf`) instead of thresholds and requires the result ≥ 1.0; MEME and LP tokens have cf 0; caps `supplyCap`/`borrowCap`; borrowing against an asset with a stale oracle is paused (edge 12) — liquidations continue at the last price.
4. Liquidation (keepers, X11): `mkt.defi.liquidate` by any LIC2 account; validated at execution in the block: target HF < 1 **at block start** (computed before any op in the block), `repay ≤ closeFactor (0.5) × that debt`; the liquidator pays `repay` of the debt asset and receives `repay·oracle_debt·(1 + bonus)/oracle_coll` of the collateral asset (bonus of the collateral asset's row) — exactly, rounded down in micro-units. If the account's collateral is insufficient, the liquidator receives all of it and the remaining debt becomes bad debt: `settleBadDebt` covers it from reserves, then writes down `supplyIndex` of that market pro rata (socialized), `mkt.defi.badDebt` PUBLIC; `mkt.defi.liquidation` PUBLIC without identity. Postings `DEFI_LIQUIDATION`, `DEFI_REPAY`.
5. NPC keepers (§6 item 6): before each block, for every account with HF < 1 at the current oracle, if `bonus value − gas cost > $50`, enqueue a liquidation op with an `intentId`-equivalent id derived from `(tick, accountId, marketId)` so it sorts by the same hash rule as player ops (no priority).
6. No atomic composition (§3.12.11, X9): every DeFi intent is exactly one op; there is no bundle intent kind and no callback; a borrow and its repay are two intents, which execute in different blocks at the earliest (an account's own repay in the same block as its borrow is rejected `AST_SAME_BLOCK` for the same market — guarantees "no intent can borrow and repay within one op" and a two-tick minimum for such strategies).

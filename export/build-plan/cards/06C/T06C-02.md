## Goal
The launch DeFi protocols of 06 §3.12.1 (all NPC-operated: Tidepool, Ferro, Vesta, Quill on Lattice; Cascade, Harbor, Mirage on Meridian; Arc Bridge; Keystone) as protocol rows with their treasuries, and the Tidepool pool engine of §3.12.2: constant-product swaps with fee 0.30% (protocol share 1/6 to the treasury, rest stays in reserves), stableswap pools (n = 2, A = 100, fee 0.04%) solved by deterministic Newton (≤ 64 iterations), proportional add/remove liquidity with LP tokens, the LP token mark and the LP_TOKEN `ModelValuer`, the impermanent loss formula for the LP screen, the per-block TWAP accumulator, and the intents `mkt.defi.swap`, `mkt.defi.addLiquidity`, `mkt.defi.removeLiquidity` as block ops (range pools: T06C-04 shares these intents). Genesis seeds the 24 launch pools of 22 §4.7.

## Implementation steps
1. Protocols: rows from 22 (`npc_TIDEPOOL` treasury etc.); fee revenue recorded per protocol per day (`ast.cry_pool` fee counters) for the GOV fundamentals.
2. Constant product (§3.12.2): in a block op, `in' = in·(1 − feeBps/10,000)`, `out = floor(rB·in'/(rA + in'))` in micro-units; reject if `out < minOut` (no state change except gas, T06C-01). Protocol fee `in·fee·(1/6)` is moved to the protocol treasury; the rest of the fee stays in reserves (so `rA·rB` never decreases). Postings: `moveQty(wallet → pool, tokenIn, in − protocolFee, 'DEFI_AMM_SWAP')`, `moveQty(wallet → treasury, tokenIn, protocolFee, 'DEFI_LP_FEE')`, `moveQty(pool → wallet, tokenOut, out, 'DEFI_AMM_SWAP')`. Pool reserves = the pool account's positions (03), mirrored in the `AmmPool` row and asserted equal in P13.
3. Liquidity: add proportional `minted = min(dA/rA, dB/rB)·lpSupply` (integer, floor), excess returned in the same op; first deposit `sqrt(dA·dB)` minus 1,000 micro-units locked forever (standard minimum liquidity; documented decision to prevent share inflation); LP token is a crypto asset `ast_CR_LP_<poolId>` minted/burned (`DEFI_LP_DEPOSIT`/`DEFI_LP_WITHDRAW`, quantity templates with `mint`/`burn`); remove pro rata.
4. Stableswap (n = 2): invariant `A·n^n·Σx + D = A·D·n^n + D^{n+1}/(n^n·Πx)`; `stableD` Newton from `D0 = Σx`, `D_{k+1} = (A·n^n·S + n·D_P)·D/((A·n^n − 1)·D + (n + 1)·D_P)` with `D_P = D^{n+1}/(n^n Πx)`, stop when |ΔD| ≤ 1 micro-unit or 64 iterations (deterministic bigint math); `stableY` similar. Fee 0.04% on the input, protocol share 1/6.
5. LP mark: `(rA·mark_A + rB·mark_B)/lpSupply` with 06A `markPrice` of each token; registered as the LP_TOKEN `ModelValuer`. IL for the screen: `IL(r) = 2√r/(1 + r) − 1`, r = price ratio since deposit.
6. TWAP: at the first op touching a pool in a block, `cumulative += price·(tick − lastTick)`, `lastTick = tick`; `twap(30)` = Δcumulative/Δticks over the last 30 ticks (ring of cumulative snapshots per tick touched).
7. Genesis: the 24 launch pools of 22 (pair, curve, fee tier, seed reserves equal value at p0) owned by NPC LPs (§6 item 6: NPC LPs keep 80% of seed liquidity and withdraw 30% when fear > 0.6 — implemented in T06C-07 with the other NPC crypto behaviour).

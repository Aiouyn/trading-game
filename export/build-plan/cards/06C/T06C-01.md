## Goal
The foundation of unit 06C: the crypto and DeFi data model of 06 §2.5 as 06 tables, the 06C events, genesis of the 20 launch tokens plus rule-created wrappers from 22 §4.7, the three chains (AURUM transfers only, LATTICE, MERIDIAN) with one block per chain per tick executed in P6 after the batch auctions, the no-MEV ordering rule `hash(tickEntropy, intentId)` with capacity and an order-preserving queue, the EIP-1559-style base fee burned in the native token, self-custody and exchange-custody wallets, AUC issuance with halving at each chapter boundary under the 18,000,000 cap, LTX/MRD staking issuance (`CRY_TOKEN_MINT`), and Vault Exchange custody with pro-rata recovery on insolvency. Every other 06C task registers its op executor with this block engine.

## Implementation steps
1. Schema: copy §2.5 verbatim; amounts are `bigint` micro-units of the token (6 decimals) except prices (`number`) inside pool math; conversions with `toMoney` half-even only for postings in fiat. Tables `ast.cry_*` (module '06', partition by chain so each chain's state lives in one partition, 19).
2. Genesis (22 §4.7): one `CryptoToken` per row (`ast_CR_<CODE>`, circulating supply, max supply, kind, chain, `v0`, `p0` → initial anchor `ln p0`), licenses LIC1 long / no short (06 §3.2), session CRYPTO, venue `ven_VAULTX` lines vs FDL; rule-created wAUC (supply 120,000, Keystone custodian), `aLTX`, `aMRD`, `aGUSD` (supply 0); NPC entities of 22 (`npc_GUARDIAN` … `npc_STAKERS`) registered as protocol accounts with 03 when 22 marks them 06-owned (protocol treasuries, issuers' token vaults); NPC miners hold block rewards; NPC stakers stake 60% of LTX/MRD supply (§6 item 6). Chains rows: `opsPerBlock` 500/2,000/3,000, `targetOps = 0.5·opsPerBlock`, `baseFee` from 22 (default 0.001 native).
3. Wallets: `mkt.wallet.create` (≤ 10 per chain per owner) registers a WALLET sub-account (`acc_<owner>_W<chain><n>`) with 03 owned by the actor (positions of tokens live in 03, the `Wallet` row is metadata + label); `custody: 'SELF'`. Exchange custody = the owner's main account positions in crypto lines on `ven_VAULTX` (a claim on Vault Exchange, `custody: 'EXCHANGE'`, implicit wallet per chain). Withdraw from exchange to self-custody = `mkt.wallet.transfer` from the EXCHANGE wallet (blocked while Vault Exchange withdrawals are frozen).
4. Block engine (§3.11.5): DeFi and transfer intents validate statically at P1 (schema, ownership, license, wallet on the right chain) and are enqueued with `orderKey = fnv1a64(tickEntropy ‖ intentId)` where `tickEntropy` is 02's per-tick entropy of the submit tick; at P6 (after 05 matching) for each chain in fixed order: block = previous queue (kept in its original order) followed by this tick's ops sorted by `orderKey` (ties impossible; fallback intentId); execute the first `opsPerBlock` ops one at a time through the registered `OpExecutor` (each op atomic, all-or-nothing; balance and `minOut` checks happen at execution); the rest stays queued in the same relative order (06#25). Each executed op is charged gas first (`chargeGas`; insufficient native → op rejected `AST_GAS`, nothing else happens); a failing op (e.g. `minOut`) is rejected without moving funds but gas is still charged (edge 14). No priority fees exist (X9, X10: intents are sealed until P0 commit). AURUM accepts only `mkt.wallet.transfer`.
5. Gas: `baseFee_next = baseFee·(1 + 0.125·(opsUsed/targetOps − 1))`, floor 0.0001 native units; gas is burned: `ctx.ledger.burn(walletAccount, nativeToken, fee, 'DEFI_NETWORK_FEE')` (token supply sink; no FDL flow; reason template gap reported). Emit `mkt.defi.block` PUBLIC aggregate `{chain, ops, baseFee, queueLength}`.
6. Issuance (`ast_auc_supply` row): AUC per-day issuance in chapter c = `i0 / 2^c` with `i0 = (18,000,000 − genesis supply)/(2 × 1,344)` (decision: geometric series filling the cap; 22 authors no per-day figure), minted at DAY_CLOSE to NPC miners pro rata 45/35/20 (`allocate`) with `CRY_TOKEN_MINT` (quantity only, no FDL), each mint clamped to `18,000,000 − totalSupply`; halving applied at 02's CHAPTER boundary. LTX 4%/yr and MRD 5%/yr: daily `supply·rate/364` to stakers pro rata by staked amount (NPC stakers and players). `mkt.defi.stake` / `unstake` (block ops on the token's chain; unstake released after 1 game day). MUSD staking savings rate is T06C-06's.
7. Custody (§3.11.5): `custodyStep` hourly: Vault Exchange solvency from `WorldPort06.vaultExchangeSolvency` (16 owns its balance sheet; default 1.0); if < 1, withdrawals freeze (`AST_EXCHANGE_FROZEN` for transfers out of EXCHANGE wallets and crypto sell orders still trade on the book only while 16 keeps it open), and customer claims are written down pro rata to the recovery ratio when 16 resolves the insolvency (positions scaled by `moveQty` to the estate account). Self-custody wallets carry 12's attack risk (12 resolves abstract outcomes; 06 applies the resulting balance changes it receives through `ConflictPort06`).
8. Capital controls (§3.9.4): `mkt.wallet.transfer` of a token held by a resident of a CONTROLS bloc to a wallet of a non-resident is blocked by `controlsBlock` (crypto is not an exemption route).

## Test plan

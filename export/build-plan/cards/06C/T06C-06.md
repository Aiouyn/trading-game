## Goal
The three stablecoin mechanisms of 06 §3.12.6–§3.12.8 with statuses that change only for a cause (06#31; no fixed depeg probability, replacing 01's): GUSD/EUNX fiat-reserve tokens (reserve mix, mint/redeem at 1.00 ± 0.10% for LIC2+ processed at day close, 10%/day redemption cap with a queue, solvency ratio ρ including the reserve bank's uninsured-deposit haircut, PEGGED/STRESSED/DEPEGGED/DEFUNCT), Vesta's VSD CDP (vaults on LTX, AUC/wAUC, ORE; MCR 1.50; stability fee set daily by NPC governance; 60-tick Dutch auctions with 13% penalty; bad debt covered by surplus then VSG dilution; the GUSD↔VSD Peg Stability Module), and Mirage's MUSD/MSH (rate-limited mint/redeem against MSH at the oracle, 0.5% fee, 12%/yr savings rate from MSH issuance) where a death spiral can emerge but is never scripted (06#32). Stablecoin fundamentals `V` for T06C-03.

## Implementation steps
1. Fiat (§3.12.6): issuer entities `npc_GUARDIAN` (GUSD, reserve bank Lakeshore Bancorp) and `npc_EUNX_ISSUER` (EUNX, Banca Valcourt) hold reserves as 03 positions: 60% government bills of the peg currency (06A bills), 30% deposits at the named bank (07), 10% overnight repo (07) (`ast_gusd_reserve_mix`, DEFAULT). `ρ = (bills marked + deposits × (1 − depositHaircut) + repo) / supply`. Mint/redeem intents (LIC2+) queue to DAY_CLOSE: mint = pay fiat to the issuer (`DEFI_STABLE_MINT`) and `mint` the token, fee 0.10%; redeem = `burn` tokens and the issuer pays fiat at 1.00 − 0.10% (`DEFI_STABLE_REDEEM`), capped at 10% of supply per game day, the rest stays queued FIFO by (submit tick, intent order key). Reserve rebalancing: the issuer buys bills with mint proceeds (06A bond market order at the next opening, NPC pool) and sells bills for redemptions beyond deposits.
2. Status (each tick, `stableStatusStep`): PEGGED if ρ ≥ 0.995 and the secondary price (CEX last, else deepest pool) within 0.5% of peg; DEPEGGED if secondary < 0.97 for 30 consecutive ticks; DEFUNCT if `issuerWindDown`; STRESSED otherwise, and explicitly when the redemption queue overflows the daily cap (06#31's two causes). No RNG is drawn for status (`ast.depeg` stays registered and undrawn: documented decision; 16's attestation-doubt shocks raise redemption demand through `attestationShock`, consumed by NPC holders' redemptions). `mkt.stable.status` PUBLIC `{token, status, rho}` on change. Edge 13: every protocol uses the oracle price of GUSD (not 1.00) from the next block (T06C-05 oracle reads markets).
3. CDP (§3.12.7): vault ops as block ops on Lattice: open with LTX, AUC/wAUC or ORE; mint VSD up to `collateral·oracle/MCR` (`DEFI_STABLE_MINT`, quantity); repay burns VSD; add/withdraw collateral (withdraw requires ratio ≥ MCR after). Stability fee daily by Vesta governance (NPC): `fee = clamp(0.04 + 2·(1 − VSD_price), 0.02, 0.08)`, accrued into debt daily (to protocol surplus). Liquidation when ratio < MCR: status LIQUIDATING, Dutch auction of the collateral over 60 ticks from `1.2·oracle` linearly to `0.8·oracle`; `mkt.defi.auctionBid` buys collateral at the current price paying VSD (burned against debt); penalty 13% of debt to surplus; unsold collateral at the end → bad debt; bad debt covered by surplus, then by minting VSG and selling it (Dutch auction for VSD, dilution of governance holders).
4. PSM: GUSD ↔ VSD 1:1 with 0.1% fee up to a cap of 25% of VSD supply held as GUSD in the PSM; a GUSD depeg therefore transmits into VSD (contagion by rule).
5. Mirage (§3.12.8): redeem 1 MUSD for $1 of MSH minted at the MSH oracle price; mint 1 MUSD by burning $1 of MSH; both rate-limited at `PARAM.mirage_redeem_cap_per_tick` (0.2%) of MUSD supply per tick (shared cap per direction), fee 0.5%; postings `mint`/`burn` with `DEFI_STABLE_MINT`/`DEFI_STABLE_REDEEM`; no external reserves. `V_MUSD = min(1, MSH market cap / MUSD supply)·peg`. Savings: 12%/yr on staked MUSD (T06C-01 staking table) paid daily in newly minted MSH (`CRY_TOKEN_MINT`). MSH demand comes from H (T06C-03) and the savings yield; the death spiral (MUSD < 1 → redemptions → MSH supply up → MSH price down → ratio down) is emergent only; NPC arbitrageurs redeem when MUSD secondary < 1 − fee (T06C-07 arbitrage capacity).
6. Fundamentals for T06C-03: `stableValue` per mechanism, anchor half-life 1 game day.

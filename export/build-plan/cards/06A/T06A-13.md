## Goal
The physical layer of commodities: 5 hubs with benchmark assignments and basis (06 §3.10.4), warehouse receipts created by physical spot purchases, daily storage fees (NPC public warehouse at a markup, player storage businesses at their rate), spoilage of perishables, finite hub capacity (purchases at a full hub rejected, fee ×2 above 95%), shipping between hubs with transit time, freight cost, FRX index and insurable loss risk (§3.10.5). Intents `mkt.commodity.withdraw` and `mkt.commodity.ship`. Futures delivery elections are 06B (T06B-06) and reuse the receipts here.

## Implementation steps
1. Hubs (§3.10.4): 5 hubs (one per bloc, 22 assigns the city); benchmark per commodity as in Exports; `P_hub = P_bench + basis_hub`; `basis_hub = shipCost(bench → hub)·(importer ? +1 : −1) + 0.5·shipCost·clamp(localImbalance, −1, 1)` with `localImbalance` from `ports.companies.regionalImbalance` (09/16); importer = hub demand > hub supply. Hub lines exist only where 22 lists activity (T06A-01). Native commodities (COMPUTE, DATA, POWER_*) have no hubs or receipts.
2. Receipts: in P6 after 05 has matched, scan `fillsOfTick` for physical commodity lines: buyer → `createReceipt` at the line's hub in the NPC public warehouse (`facilityId: null`) unless the buyer already holds a receipt at the hub in its own facility (then that one grows); seller → reduce receipts FIFO by `createdTick` then `receiptId`. P1 capacity check: the instrument-port extension (T06A-17) calls `physicalCapacityCheck` for buy orders; at 100% hub utilization → `AST_HUB_FULL` (06#21).
3. Storage fees (DAY_CLOSE): `fee = storageCostPerYearFrac·value/364` with value = qty × hub mark; NPC public warehouse charges `× cmd_public_storage_markup` and posts `CMDTY_STORAGE_FEE_NPC` (SINK, `ctx.ledger.elasticOut(owner, frm_<bloc>, …)`); a player facility (09 storage business) charges its posted `ratePerYearFrac·value/364` and posts `CMDTY_STORAGE_FEE_B2B` (`pay`). Above 95% hub utilization every fee ×2. Spoilage: `lossQty = qty·perishDecayPerYear/364` per day, receipt qty reduced and the asset qty removed via `ctx.ledger.moveQty(owner, 'sys_SPOILAGE', asset, lossQty, book, ccy, 'CMDTY_SPOILAGE')` (see `_unit.md` registry note on qty-destroying templates).
4. Withdraw (`mkt.commodity.withdraw`): owner, facility at the same hub with free capacity for the commodity; moves the receipt (or splits it) to `toFacilityId`.
5. Shipping: `mkt.commodity.ship` validates LIC2, owner, route exists (22 §4.6), freight capacity available (fleetUtil < 1), cash for freight available, capital controls (`controlsBlock(ccy,'WITHDRAW_FROM_BLOC')` when leaving a controlled bloc). Effect: freight `cost = qty·unitWeight·FRX·distanceFactor` posted `CMDTY_FREIGHT_NPC` (SINK, `elasticOut` to `frm_<from bloc>`) or `CMDTY_FREIGHT_B2B` to a player shipping line chosen by 09's capacity order; receipt qty removed; `Shipment {departTick: tick, arriveTick: tick + routeDays·720, status: IN_TRANSIT}`; emit `mkt.ship.departed` PARTIES. FRX: `FRX_base·(fleetUtil/0.80)²` clamped to [0.4, 6] × base, updated hourly.
6. Arrival (P2 at `arriveTick`), shipments in ascending `shipmentId`: one draw `ctx.rng('ast.commodity:' + commodityId).bernoulli(PARAM.ship_loss_prob_per_trip)` (the 02 registry stream; 06's text `rng('ship', …)` is not in the registry): loss → status LOST, qty written off (`CMDTY_SPOILAGE` to `sys_SPOILAGE`), emit `mkt.ship.lost` PARTIES; 08 pays the insured value if `cargoInsured` (edge 10; CDS-like contracts unaffected). Otherwise receipt at the destination hub (rejected? if the hub is full the cargo waits in transit one more game day and retries) and `mkt.ship.arrived`.
7. Marks: in-transit cargo = destination spot − remaining freight (`shipmentMark`).

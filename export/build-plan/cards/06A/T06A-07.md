## Goal
Own the reference indices of 06 §3.5.1 / 22 §4.8 (GX100, the five regional 30s, 12 sector indices, Dividend 30, REIT, bond aggregate, IG, HY, treasury indices, CMX, gold spot, CRX10): construction from universe rules, per-tick levels from mark prices, divisor continuity across rebalances and corporate actions, total-return variants, and the PUBLIC rebalance events that drive index-fund flows (X20). Also the instrument side of ETFs: NAV with marks, PUBLIC iNAV with last prices, daily expense accrual to the sponsor (06 §3.6). GXV (§3.5.3) is 06B's (needs options); GX Credit 40 is 06B's (CDS).

## Implementation steps
1. Genesis: for each 22 §4.8 index row with method FFMC_CAPPED/EQUAL/PRICE_WEIGHTED/RULE_BOND/RULE_COMMODITY/RULE_CRYPTO, compute constituents at genesis prices; `level = 1000`, `divisor = raw/1000`. Methods: FFMC_CAPPED weight ∝ free-float cap in FDL with cap (GX100/regional 10%, sector/REIT 20%, Dividend 30 6%, CRX10 25%); iterative capping: cap overweight names, redistribute the excess pro rata to the uncapped, repeat until none exceed (max 50 iterations). RULE_BOND: face-weighted (`amountOutstanding × price/100`), ≥ 1y to maturity, GXIG/GXHY by `FundsPort06.isInvestmentGrade`, 3% per issuer cap. RULE_COMMODITY: CMX uses 22 §4.6 production weights on front-month futures marks (rolling over days 5–9 of each game month, 20% of weight per roll day; futures come from 06B rows; when 06B is absent the CMX index uses benchmark spot marks and records `rollPending`). RULE_CRYPTO: CRX10 = 10 largest non-stable tokens by free-float cap (tokens from 06C; absent → index not created). Universe rules from §3.5.1 (GX100 100 largest by free-float cap in FDL; regional 30 largest of the bloc; sectors all listed; Dividend 30 = 30 highest trailing yields with payout ≤ 90%).
2. `stepIndexLevels` in P6 after 05's auctions: `level = Σ units_k · mark_k · FX_k→FDL / divisor` using `markPrice` (so a crashed print cannot move an index outside the band). Halted constituent: stays at its mark (edge 2), units recalculated at the next scheduled rebalance only.
3. Rebalance at the closing auction t = 540 of the last trading day of the period (quarterly: quarter-end; semiannual: quarters 2 and 4; monthly: last trading day of the month): compute new weights from that auction's official closes, `unitsFromWeights` at the old level, `adjustDivisor` so the level at the same prices is unchanged; emit `mkt.index.rebalanced` PUBLIC `{indexId, adds, drops, newUnits}` and publish the preview 5 trading days before (stored on the row; the preview is PUBLIC through the index view, X20: front-running is legal and competitive).
4. Corporate events (`applyIndexCorporateEvents`, P2): split → units × ratio (raw unchanged, no divisor change); spin-off → add the new equity with units = parent units × distribution ratio and adjust divisor so level is unchanged; bankruptcy (DISTRESSED) or delist → remove at the next tick with divisor adjustment at the removed name's last mark; total-return variants (`returnType: 'TOTAL'`) reinvest the dividend on ex-date: units_k ×= `1/(1 − d/P)`, divisor unchanged.
5. ETFs: `nav = (Σ basket qtyPerCreationUnit/creationUnitShares × mark × FX + cashPerShare − accruedExpensesPerShare)`; `inav` the same with `lastPrice`; both written each tick in P6 after indices (PUBLIC through the view). Expense accrual at DAY_CLOSE: `amount = expenseRatioBps/10000/364 × NAV × unitsOutstanding` posted `ctx.ledger.pay(etfFundEntity, sponsorId, toMoney(amount), ccy, 'FND_MGMT_FEE')` (D114: 08's code). Creation/redemption is 08's; 06 only updates `unitsOutstanding` when 08's `ETF_CREATE/ETF_REDEEM` postings occur (read from `ctx.ledger.position` of the sponsor's outstanding account at DAY_CLOSE).

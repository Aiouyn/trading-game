## Goal
The OTC layer 06 owns in P6 (06 §3.19.1): request-for-quote with up to 5 named dealers or ALL, NPC dealer quotes in the same tick, player dealer quotes from N+1 to N+3 seeing only a rotating requester label and credit tier (L11, D109, X19), acceptance and execution at the next P6, novation/CSA booking hand-off to 07 in P7, and post-trade transparency 30 ticks later without identities. A product registry lets 06B (swaps, CDS, FX forwards), 07 (term repo) and this task (blocks of listed assets, bilateral FX) plug their pricers in. Also the LIC4 block desk (§3.19.2): propose/accept a block crossed at the closing-auction price, filed PUBLIC 720 ticks later (X24).

## Implementation steps
1. Request (§3.19.1 step 1): `mkt.rfq.request` validates the product exists, `tradeGate` with the product's act (license for the product), at most `rfqMaxOpen` (5) OPEN RFQs per requester, ≤ 5 named dealers or 'ALL'; a block of a listed asset must be ≥ 5 × its average trade size (`modelAdv.advUnits / tradesCount` from 05 daily bar; if no trades, ≥ 5 lots); collectible private sales are not RFQ (rejected). Creates `RfqRequest{requesterLabel: rotating label per (requester, rfqId), createdTick: N, expiresTick: N + 3}`; emit `mkt.rfq.requested` PARTIES to the requester only.
2. NPC dealers (step 2) in P6 of tick N: dealers = named NPC dealers or all NPC dealers in 22 §5.2 that make this product; each computes `price = modelMid ± (baseSpread + 0.8·σ·√(size/ADV_equiv))·(1 + 2·fear)·(1 + CREDIT_TIER_WIDEN[tier])` (+ for BUY/PAY/BUY_PROTECTION, − for SELL/RECEIVE/SELL_PROTECTION) with tier from `ports.banks.creditTier(requester)`; declines if `size > dealerCapacity`. Quotes valid 3 ticks (`validUntilTick = N + 3`). Emit `mkt.rfq.quoted` PARTIES to the requester (dealer id visible, the requester is never visible to dealers).
3. Player dealers (step 3): see the request from N+1 through `dealerView` (rotating label and credit tier only, 06#42) and may submit `mkt.rfq.quote` until N+3 (LIC4 and `ports.social.isDealer`), `validTicks ≤ 3`, size ≤ requested.
4. Accept (step 4): `mkt.rfq.accept` validates the quote is live, margin/collateral available (07 via the product's execute; with 07 absent, cash hold of the notional for cash products); status ACCEPTED; execution at P6 of the next tick through `product.execute`; cleared products are novated by 07 in P7 (07 reads the created trade), bilateral ones are booked under the CSA; postings commit P13. Emit `mkt.rfq.filled` PARTIES. Cancel: own and OPEN only.
5. Prints (step 5): 30 ticks after a fill of a cleared product, emit `mkt.otc.print` PUBLIC `{productKind, spec summary without parties, price, size}`.
6. Surveillance X19: if a dealer's beneficial-owner group trades the same asset or product within 30 ticks after an RFQ it saw was filled, emit `mkt.surveillance.signal` SEALED `{pattern: 'RFQ_FRONT_RUN', actors, evidence}`.
7. Block desk (§3.19.2): both parties LIC4 (`tradeGate(...,'BLOCK')`), equities or ETFs only, propose before t = 530, accept before t = 539, holdings checked (seller `placeQty`, buyer `placeCash` of qty × last price × 1.10). On the closing-auction tick, in 06's P6 handler (after 05's closing auction has produced the official close), cross at `officialCloseOf(asset, today)` outside the auction (no participation in price formation, no impact): `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(buyer, seller, asset, qty, notional, ccy, 'MKT_BLOCK_TRADE')`. Filing `mkt.block.filed` PUBLIC at crossTick + 720 with asset, size, price; identities only when `ports.social.filingThresholdCrossed` (e.g. crossing 5%). X24: no other price is possible. Ownership note: 05 §3.20 (D091) also runs a generic `mkt.block.cross`; the 06 desk is the LIC4 negotiated variant and posts with the same code (reported to the reconciler).

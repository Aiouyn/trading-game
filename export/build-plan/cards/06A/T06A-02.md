## Goal
Implement the cross-class rules every 06 intent and every 05 order on a 06 instrument relies on: who may trade what (06 §3.2 license table and the extra P1 gates), when each session profile trades (§3.3.1), the T+0 settlement contract (§3.3.2), the clamped mark price used for net worth, margin, NWI and ladders (§3.3.3), and the beneficial-owner position and order limits (§3.3.4). Edge cases 22, 23, 25 and exploit counters X1, X2, X23 of §8/§9 are owned here.

## Implementation steps
1. `requiredLicense` encodes the 06 §3.2 table row by row (R15: 06 §3.2 is authoritative): collectibles LIC0; equities/ETFs/government bills LIC1 long, LIC2 short; spot launch crypto on the CEX LIC1, meme coins and every AMM/LP/lending/CDP/bridge act LIC2 (D115); bonds, FX spot, commodity spot and receipts LIC2 both sides; primary non-competitive LIC2, competitive LIC4; option buy LIC3, covered/cash-secured write LIC3, naked write LIC4; futures and perps LIC3 (leverage ≤ 10), perps 20× at LIC4; OIS/FX forwards and swaps/ZCIS/commodity swaps LIC3; CDS buy LIC3, CDS sell/TRS/XCCY/bilateral OTC/block desk LIC4; token launch LIC4 (+ R4 checked by 06C). `licenseShort === null` + act SHORT → `AST_NOT_SHORTABLE`.
2. `tradeGate` order of checks (first failure wins, all at P1): (a) Held (`isHeld`) → `AST_HELD` unless `opts.intentKind ∈ HELD_ALLOWED_INTENTS`; (b) license → `AST_NO_LICENSE` with params `{required}`; (c) restricted cash: if `isRestrictedBlocked(a.cls)` or act ∈ {BUY_PROTECTION, SELL_PROTECTION} or the action posts margin, and the actor's spendable cash net of restricted buckets (`ctx.ledger.available − restrictedCashOf`) is below the required amount, or `fundedFromRestrictedOnly` → `AST_RESTRICTED_CASH` (D113, 03 §3.5.6); (d) leverage: `maxLeverageFor` = base (10 LIC3, 20 LIC4 for perps) halved when `activeCaseSeverity ≥ 3`, then `min(·, PARAM.rookie_max_leverage)` during new-player protection; requested leverage above → `AST_LEVERAGE_CAP`. Return `maxLeverage` for 07.
3. `sessions.ts`: EQUITY open Mon–Fri with opening auction t=60, continuous 61–539, closing t=540; FX open from Monday t=0 to Saturday t=0 and Sunday from t=660 (06 §3.3.1; note 05 closes FX books all weekend per its own ruling, so 06 uses this only for 06-run FX forward/RFQ acceptance windows); CRYPTO every tick; FUT24 every tick Mon–Fri plus Sunday from t=660; OTC any tick Mon–Fri; AUCTION per-lot (always "open" for the auction engine); DISTRESSED one call at t=300 on trading days; NONE never. Coupon/maturity dates on weekends move to Monday t=719 via `nextBusinessDayTick`.
4. Settlement (§3.3.2, D095): document in `gates.ts` header and enforce by construction: every 06 trade is posted through `ctx.ledger.swapAsset`/`pay`/`moveQty` in the tick it executes (postings commit in P13); cross-partition legs use `ctx.holds` placed at P1 and released at P13 (edge 23). No settlement-fail state exists in any 06 table. X23: shorts require a locate, which is 07's (`BankPort06` is not consulted here; 05's order path calls 07's locate), and `tradeGate` refuses SHORT on `licenseShort === null`.
5. `marks.ts` implements the pseudocode of §3.3.3 exactly: model-valued classes call the registered `ModelValuer` (06B registers OPTION, SWAP, CDS; 06C LP_TOKEN); otherwise if `getPriceState(...).tick ≥ tick − PARAM.mark_stale_ticks` and a last batch price exists, clamp `lastPrice` into `[vOrMid·(1−b), vOrMid·(1+b)]` with `σ_tick = sigmaDailyOf(asset)/sqrt(720)`; else NPC mid (05 `lnB` via `getPriceState`); COLLECTIBLE → `collectibleMark`; `forLadder` on a player-company equity → `min(m, 3-game-day VWAP from dailyBars)`; DEFAULTED/DISTRESSED → last DISTRESSED auction price (T06A-09 stores it in `ast.bond`/`ast.asset.tags`), else 09 recovery estimate × face. Edge 22: on weekends equity marks are the Friday close (no batch price within the stale window and NPC mid frozen by 05). X1/X2: marks never use an unclamped last print.
6. `limits.ts`: `beneficialOwnerGroup` = `ultimateOwnerOf` → all `controlledEntities` of that owner ∪ members of `SocialPort06.linkedGroups(ownerGroupOf(owner))`, sorted. Long cap 25% of `freeFloatShares` of NPC companies; 49% of a player company for any group other than its controller unless `viaTender`; short-interest cap 50% of free float; perp per-owner ≤ `perp_max_oi_share` of open interest; CDS net protection sold ≤ 25% of the reference entity's debt outstanding. Reject codes `AST_POSITION_CAP` / `AST_SHORT_CAP`. Futures limits are T06B-06's.
7. Edge 25: every conversion to Money in 06 uses `toMoney` (half-even); every pro-rata uses `allocate`/`proRata` with remainders in hash order. Add a lint-style unit test that greps nothing; instead `limits.test.ts` checks `allocate` sums.

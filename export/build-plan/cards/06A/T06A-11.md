## Goal
Define the 10 FX pairs and quoting (06 §3.9.1), the leg fundamental and expected drift with 0.5 UIP pass-through (D098, R29; 04 owns `macro_fx_uip_share` and computes the value 05 uses as `lnF`, see step 2), the cross-from-legs identity, exact covered-interest-parity forwards with the stress basis (§3.9.3), the dynamic SRL jump intensity from carry crowding, and the P1 enforcement of 04's capital controls for every 06 intent that converts, withdraws or moves a controlled currency (§3.9.4, edge 8).

## Implementation steps
1. Pairs (§3.9.1): base priority EUN > FDL > NKR > PYN > SRL; price = quote units per 1 base; ids `ast_FX_<BASE><QUOTE>`; legs are the four pairs containing FDL; display 4 decimals, 2 when PYN is the quote.
2. Leg fundamentals (§3.9.2): implement the formula with `θ = ln 2 / 1.5`, `u = PARAM.macro_fx_uip_share` (0.5; R29 replaces 06's 0.30 text), `dt = 1/262080` per tick, β_risk per currency with the quote-side sign convention (for FDLxxx pairs). Ownership note: 05 §3.3.6 and 04 §3.13 make 04's `FxState.fairValueLn` the `lnF` that 05 pulls the legs toward (κ is not used for FX legs); 06 therefore does not write `ast.anchor` for FX legs. 06's `legExpectedDrift` and `legFundamental` drive the carry table and the asset page "fundamental pull" panel, and the test below asserts they agree with 04's drift to 1e-12 when both use the same inputs (04's extra terms `κ_ca·CA` and policy surprises set to 0 in the comparison).
3. Crosses: `V_cross = V_leg1 · V_leg2` (or ratio) computed from legs; 05 uses a 1-tick κ for crosses and NPC triangular arbitrage (05 T05-12). 06 writes `ast.anchor` for crosses from `crossFromLegs` each tick in P6 after 05's FX books (6a) so 05 sees it next tick.
4. SRL jump intensity: `λ = 2 + 6·crowding_SRL` per year, crowding = net long SRL OI in FX futures and forwards ÷ its 1-year average, clamp [0, 3]; the position source is registered by 06B (absent → crowding 0 → λ = 2). `defaultPriceParams` for FDLSRL uses `jumpLambdaPerYear = srlJumpIntensity(...)` (recomputed daily at DAY_CLOSE, down mean 8%, up 4%).
5. Forwards (§3.9.3): `F(T) = S·exp((z_quote(T) − z_base(T) + basis(T))·T)` with z = 04 `ois` zero rates; `basis = −xccy_basis_stress_bps·fear²/10,000` when FDL is the quote currency of the pair, else 0; fear = F/100 of the previous game hour.
6. Controls (§3.9.4): `controlsBlock` returns `AST_CAPITAL_CONTROLS {ccy, untilTick}` for acts on a currency whose 04 regime is CONTROLS and `tick < untilTick`; 06 intents that call it: RFQ FX forwards/swaps on the controlled currency (06B), `mkt.wallet.transfer` to a foreign-bloc wallet and stablecoin mint/redeem in the currency (06C), `mkt.commodity.ship` of receipts out of the bloc's hub paid in the controlled currency (T06A-13). 05 rejects spot conversion orders itself (`MKT_CAPITAL_CONTROLS`). Peg defence is 04's orders; 06 never clamps prices. Edge 8: a forward that settles during controls settles contractually, and the controlled-currency proceeds are frozen by `freezeProceedsUntilControlsEnd` (a cash hold expiring at `untilTick`).

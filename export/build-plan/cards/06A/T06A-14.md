## Goal
Collectibles as an asset class: daily category indices driven by top-1% wealth growth, Prestige spending and a fashion cycle (06 §3.18.1); appraisals that only the index, condition, provenance and age can move (06#40, X16); the Halberd & Vane weekly auctions with consignment, held bids, 5% increments, soft close, fees as SINK, NPC collectors and rotating labels (06#41, X17, D109, D110); instant dealer sales; storage, condition decay and pledgeability. Intents `mkt.collect.consign`, `mkt.collect.bid`, `mkt.collect.withdrawLot`, `mkt.collect.sellToDealer`, `mkt.collect.store`.

## Implementation steps
1. Categories (R39: 22 §4.14 wins on names and counts): WATCH, CAR, ART, WINE, YACHT, JET are indexed (`ast.coll_category` rows with `indexLevel` 1,000); SNEAKER has no index. Parameters: 06 §3.18.1 defaults (a, b, σ) WATCH (0.6, 0.4, 0.12), ART (0.8, 0.3, 0.10), CAR = 06's CLASSIC_CAR (0.5, 0.3, 0.12), WINE (0.3, 0.2, 0.08); YACHT and JET have no 06 row and use the CAR row (decision recorded in `_unit.md`). 06's JEWEL, RARE_BOOK, MEMORABILIA do not exist in 22 and are not created.
2. Index (DAY_CLOSE, P2): `g_top1 = ports.social.top1RealNwGrowth(day)`, `g_prestige = ports.status.prestigeSpendGrowth(day)` (trailing 28 days, 14), fashion OU with half-life 182 days and stationary sd 0.15; draw order per category in sorted id order: `z_fashion = rng.normal()`, then `ε = rng.normal()` from `ctx.rng('ast.collectible:' + categoryId)`. Record `indexAtIssue` for each LIMITED/UNIQUE catalog item the first time it is created.
3. Appraisal (§3.18.2): MASS `V = price_new·(1 − d0)·e^{−0.08·ageYears}·cond(c)` (d0 per item from 22/14 in [0.10, 0.40]); LIMITED `V = baseValue·rarity·(I_c(t)/I_c(issue))·cond(c)·prov`; UNIQUE same with rarity ≥ 3 and heritage `× min(1 + 0.02·ageYears, 1.5)`; SNEAKER (no index, 22): `V = (last auction or dealer sale price of the same catalogItemId, else baseValue·rarity)·cond(c)·prov`. ageYears = game years since `acquiredTick` of the first provenance entry. Owners have no intent that writes `appraisal` (06#40); consecutive private sales between linked accounts (15) are excluded as inputs (X16: SNEAKER last-sale input ignores sales where buyer and seller share a beneficial-owner group).
4. Condition decay per year (§3.18.2): ART 0.5, WINE 0 if VAULT else 4, CAR 3 unless `ports.status.serviced(item)`, SNEAKER 5, others 1; HOME storage adds +1/yr (raises 12 exposure, which 12 reads). Daily decrement `decay/364`, floor 0.
5. Sales: one per category per game week (Saturday for ART/WATCH/CAR, Sunday for others; 22's categories), lots close in sequence `t = 120 + 10·i`. Consign (`mkt.collect.consign`): owner, item not PLEDGED/STOLEN/UNVERIFIED-at-reputable-house, up to 1 game day before the sale, `reserve ≤ estimateHigh`; estimates = appraisal × (0.85, 1.15); item flag ON_AUCTION. Withdraw (`mkt.collect.withdrawLot`): before 1 game day to the sale, fee 2% of low estimate (`COLL_AUCTION_FEE`, SINK `burn`, sub 'WITHDRAW'); seizure-ordered items are withdrawn without fee (edge 18, `ports.law.seizureOrder`).
6. Bid (`mkt.collect.bid`, P1): LIC0; reject consignor and anyone in its beneficial-owner group or linked groups (X17, 06#41); `amount ≥ validBidFloor`; hold `ctx.holds.placeCash(bidder, ccy, amount·1.15, closeTick + 1, lotId)`; the previous high bidder's hold is released when outbid. In P6 each tick: the highest valid bid stands, ties by earlier seal order (orderKey) then `fnv1a32(bidderId + lotId)`. Soft close: a new high bid when `closeTick − tick ≤ 30` extends `closeTick += 30`, at most 10 extensions. NPC collectors (06 §6 item 7): when a lot opens, `n = 2 + roundHalfEven(4·I/I_0·(1 − fear))` NPC bidders, valuations `appraisal·(0.85 + 0.35·u)` (one `uOpen` each from `ast.collectible:<categoryId>`), capped by `ports.world.npcWealth(category)`/n; they bid the minimum valid increment each tick while below their valuation.
7. Close: SOLD if high bid ≥ reserve, else PASSED. Fees on SOLD: buyer premium 15% and seller fee 12%; NPC house (`HV`) → `COLL_AUCTION_FEE` SINK (`burn`) from buyer and seller; player houses (09) → `pay` to the house entity (TRANSFER). Settlement: player buyer ↔ player seller `COLL_AUCTION_SETTLE` (swap of the item: item `ownerId` change + cash `pay`); NPC buyer of a player's lot → `COLL_SALE_TO_NPC` (FAUCET, `elasticIn` from `hh` of the seller's home city); player buyer of an NPC-consigned lot → `COLL_BUY_FROM_NPC` (SINK, `elasticOut`). Emit `mkt.collect.result` PUBLIC `{lotId, itemId, price, buyerLabel, sellerLabel}` with rotating labels unless the parties opted in (L11, D109). Edge 16: if a court order seizes held cash, `voidBidOnSeizure` and the next-highest valid bid wins at its own amount.
8. Dealer (`mkt.collect.sellToDealer`): owner, unencumbered; LIMITED/UNIQUE at 70% of appraisal, MASS at 90% of V; `COLL_SALE_TO_NPC`. Storage (`mkt.collect.store`, allowed under Held): VAULT fee per year ART 0.5%, WINE 1%, CAR 2%, others 0.5% of appraisal at DAY_CLOSE posted with `LIFE_UPKEEP` (14's storage code) to the vault operator; HOME free. Pledge: LIMITED/UNIQUE pledgeable at 50% LTV (07 reads `appraisal`, flag PLEDGED set by 07). Edge 17: stolen/destroyed → flag, mark 0 or 08's insured value.

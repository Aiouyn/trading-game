## Goal
Execute 09's corporate events at the security level: tender offers with proration (`mkt.corp.tender`), merger completion (cash, stock, mixed), spin-offs, bankruptcy filings and plan confirmations, player-company delisting review and take-private floor, plus the DISTRESSED venue: one uniform-price call auction per trading day at t = 300 for defaulted bonds, suspended or bankrupt equities and claims. Short-selling rules of §3.4.7 are wired as hooks (locates and buy-ins are 07's).

## Implementation steps
1. Tender (§3.4.6): 09 announces `TENDER` with `offerPrice`, `maxShares`, close tick. `tenderHandler.validate`: actor holds ≥ qty available (`ctx.ledger.availableQty`), `tick < close`, gate LIC1; place `ctx.holds.placeQty(actor, asset, qty, closeTick, actionId)`; store an `ast.tender` row. `closeTender` at the close tick (P2): total tendered T; if `T ≤ maxShares` all accepted, else each holder gets `floor(qty·maxShares/T)` and the remaining lots go one by one to holders in ascending `fnv1a32(holderId + actionId)` order; release holds, then `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(acquirerId, holderId, asset, acceptedQty, notional, ccy, 'EQ_MERGER_CASH')`. A take-private tender of a player company is rejected at announcement unless `takePrivateFloorOk`. Tenders let a buyer exceed the 49% player-company cap (`checkLongCap(viaTender: true)`).
2. Merger completion at P2 of the effective tick (postings commit P13 = "P13 of the effective tick"): for each holder of the target: cash leg `swapAsset(acquirer, holder, target, qty, qty·cashPerShare, ccy, 'EQ_MERGER_CASH')`; stock leg `moveQty(acquirer, holder, acquirerShareAsset, floor(qty·stockPerShare to lot), bookValue, ccy, 'EQ_MERGER_STOCK')` with the fractional remainder paid in cash at the acquirer's official close (`EQ_MERGER_CASH`); target asset → DELISTED, `mkt.asset.delisted`; emit `mkt.corp.effective` (06B adjusts options to deliver the consideration, futures cash-settle at the consideration value: edge 4).
3. Spin-off: `ctx` creates `EquityAsset` (`createdBy:'ISSUANCE'`, status PENDING, id from 09's new company ticker `ast_EQ_<TICKER>`), shares distributed `EQ_SPINOFF` pro rata via `proRata`; listed with an opening auction on the effective day (05 LISTING row with offerPrice = 09 valuation per share); parent drop = 09's spin-off value per parent share handed to 05 as a DIVIDEND-kind row (reference drop) and the parent anchor lowered by the same ln; T06A-07 adds the spun entity to indices with a divisor adjustment (zero index impact).
4. Bankruptcy filing (09): equity status DISTRESSED, `venue = 'ven_DISTRESSED'`, `session = 'DISTRESSED'`; 05 gets a BANKRUPTCY row (05 suspends its book and cancels orders); emit `mkt.asset.status`; T06A-07 removes it from indices next tick; 06B settles options and futures at the first DISTRESSED auction price (edge 5). Plan confirmation: per 09's plan each share converts to claims (new `CLAIM` asset `ast_CL_<issuer>_<seq>`, `createdBy:'ISSUANCE'`), new equity, or zero (`cancelShares`), then DELISTED.
5. Delisting review: player company with `financials.ev < $10M` (FDL-equivalent) at 5 consecutive DAY_CLOSE boundaries → emit `mkt.asset.status {reason:'DELIST_REVIEW'}`; 09 decides.
6. Short selling (§3.4.7): no 06 intent; `shortSqueezeFlowFlag` is exported for 05/07 integration tests and the asset page (price up over the day and short interest > 30% of free float).
7. DISTRESSED auction (`ven_DISTRESSED`): `mkt.distressed.order` validates gate (bonds LIC2, equities LIC1, claims LIC2), asset status DEFAULTED/DISTRESSED/SUSPENDED or class CLAIM, buy → `placeCash(qty·limit)`, sell → `placeQty`; orders live for the current game day only (released at DAY_CLOSE). `clearDistressedAuction`: candidate prices = all limit prices; choose the price maximizing executed quantity; ties → smallest |imbalance|; then closest to `refPrice` (last distressed price else recovery estimate × face); then lower price. Fill priority: better limit first; at the marginal price pro rata by qty with lot remainders in ascending `sealKey` (the intent's orderKey). Each fill posts `swapAsset` with code `MKT_TRADE`; for defaulted bonds the price is flat (no accrued interest). Store the clearing price as the asset's last DISTRESSED price and the 30-tick VWAP window data (prints at t = 270–299 come from previous days' auctions only; within one day there is one print at t = 300).

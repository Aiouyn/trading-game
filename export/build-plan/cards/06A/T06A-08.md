## Implementation steps
1. Day count (06 §3.7.2): year fraction = game days / 364; periods 182 (semi), 91 (quarterly), 364 (annual); bill tenor 91 days; payment ticks are t = 719 of the scheduled day, weekend → next Monday t = 719 with no extra interest.
2. Curves (06 §3.7.1): 06 never invents a curve; `z(τ) = nsZero(govt_bloc, τ)` for government and corporate (corporate adds `s` from T06A-10 §3.8.2 as `spread_bps/10000`), `real` for linkers, `ois` only where stated. Negative rates are accepted (edge 1).
3. Pricing (§3.7.3): clean = dirty − accrued, per 100 face; dirty value of a holding `qty·face/100·(clean + accrued)`. YTM: the spec does not fix the compounding; use continuous compounding matching the curve formula (`dirtyFromYield = Σ CF·e^{−yτ}`) so a flat curve z gives y = z exactly. Newton from `y0 = couponRate`, ≤ 20 iterations, stop when the absolute change in y is < 1e-10; fall back to bisection on [−0.05, 1.00] (60 iterations) if Newton diverges or leaves the bracket. ModDur = Σ τ·CF·e^{−yτ}/P, Convexity = Σ τ²·CF·e^{−yτ}/P, DV01 = ModDur·dirty·0.0001.
4. Linkers (§3.7.4): indexed principal factor `CPI_{t − 28 game days}/inflationIndexBase` (one game month lag = 28 days, months 28/28/35 use the actual previous month length from `calendarOf`); real coupon on indexed principal; priced with `real(τ)`; breakeven = nominal yield of the same-maturity bloc 10y fixed − real yield (displayed via `bondAnalytics.breakeven`). Principal repaid = `face · max(1, factor)` (deflation floor).
5. FRN (§3.7.5): coupon = compounded ONR over the period (`Π(1 + ONR_d/364) − 1` from `BankPort06.onrFixing`; if 07 absent, 04 policy rate) + spread `couponRate`; price = par + annuity·(spread − current market spread). Bills: `100·exp(−govt(τ)·τ)`.
6. Anchor: `bondAnchorStep` hourly (t mod 30 == 0, curves are hourly) writes `lnF = ln(clean)` (05 pulls with the 2-day half-life of 06 §3.21). Defaulted bonds skip (DISTRESSED marks, T06A-10).
7. Servicing at DAY_CLOSE (P2, t = 719): for each bond with a payment due (`bondPaymentsDue`): for each holder (qty > 0, sorted) amount `couponAmount = toMoney(qty·face·per100/100)`. Government bonds post `ctx.ledger.pay(gov, holder, amt, ccy, 'SOV_COUPON' | 'SOV_REDEMPTION')` (04's codes for sovereign flows); corporate/bank bonds `BOND_COUPON` / `BOND_REDEMPTION`. Before posting, check the issuer's `ctx.ledger.available(issuer, ccy) ≥ Σ amounts`; if not, post nothing for that bond and call `onMissed` (T06A-10 starts the grace clock; edge 6). Maturity: after principal, status MATURED, emit `mkt.bond.matured` PUBLIC; each coupon emits `mkt.bond.coupon` PARTIES. Secondary trades carry accrued interest: T06A-17 registers a 05 fill hook that posts `BOND_ACCRUED_INTEREST` buyer → seller = `qty·face/100·accrued`.

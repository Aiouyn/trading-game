## Goal
Peer competition every week and a chapter Finals that late joiners can still reach, with no cash rewards and no sandbagging into weak groups.

## Imports

## Implementation steps
1. Group sizes 50 (TITAN 30, APEX 100). Shadow-pool characters (§3.12.6) group only with each other.
2. Finals: $10M identical portfolios, scenario seed, LIC3 asset set, no cyber, score = mean NAV of last 3 closes; 800 PP to winner [Quant: PP is 14's].
3. Rival highlight: closest player above in group, else nearest NPC reference if > 3× score basis.

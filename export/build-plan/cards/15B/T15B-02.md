One shared world (partitions invisible), an activity weight that sizes the NPC economy, country boards that open only when a country is big enough, and offline progression that freezes ops without wiping empires. Fresh Start stays in 14.

## Implementation steps
1. Recompute N_a at day close; publish to 05/09/13/14/16/10.
2. Event-clock bands 14:00 / 22:00 / 06:00 UTC rotate by `k mod 3` (schedulers in 04/09/16 consume).
3. INACTIVE (14 d): ops blocked, sessions freeze (12). DORMANT (30 d): leave boards except RICHEST all-time. ESTATE (90 d): UNIQUE → estate auction, proceeds to owner.
4. Returning dormant → ACTIVE next tick; league at next week start.

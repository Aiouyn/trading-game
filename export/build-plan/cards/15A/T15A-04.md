## Goal
Contracts that cannot breach on delivery: whatever is promised at activation is held first, and both sides settle in the same tick or neither does. The anchor partition makes this deterministic across partitions.

## Imports
- T03-08 `HoldApi` (escrow is a 03 hold with `purpose: 'ESCROW'`, per the 19 row 15.A scope; 07's escrow sub-ledger is later, open question 2). T03-09 postings `ESCROW_DEPOSIT`/`ESCROW_RELEASE`/`ESCROW_REFUND`. T02-14 partitions and homes, T02-15 cross-partition settlement.

## Implementation steps
0. **Escrow API (E-25, stable).** `escrow.hold(entityId, bundle, contractId) → holdId`, `escrow.release(holdId, toEntityId)`, `escrow.returnToOwner(holdId)` exactly as 15 §3.6.4 names them. They are implemented on 03 holds (`purpose: 'ESCROW'`, postings `ESCROW_*`). 07 may later re-implement the same three functions over its escrow sub-ledger without any 15A caller changing; the API is frozen by a type test.
1. At the last signature, the next tick's P1: each party's home places holds for its activation bundles (TRANSFER ON_ACTIVATION, LEASE deposit, OPTION collateral, LOAN principal, BOUNTY, engine PENALTY) and sends `HoldPlaced` to the anchor.
2. The anchor waits for all holds within the tick's barrier. If any hold fails, it is FAILED_TO_FUND and every hold is released. If all succeed, the contract goes ACTIVE.
3. TRANSFER settles via T02-15 two-phase commit: both legs or neither.
4. Escrowed assets still count in NW (03), but cannot be used.
5. Expiry: OFFERED past offerExpiresTick → EXPIRED_UNSIGNED.
6. Termination by clause (notice, then fee), by mutual agreement, or by court order.
7. Party loss (§3.6.9): the TERMINATION clause applies, else termination with escrow returned and pro-rata settlement. LOAN obligations survive into the estate.
8. VOIDED: unwind what is reversible (holds released, executed transfers reversed by compensating postings when the assets still exist; otherwise a 13 claim). Fees are not refunded.

## Test plan
  - **15#19** property test with random hold and settlement failures: a TRANSFER contract never ends with one side settled.
  - **AC-15A-04-1** one failed hold → FAILED_TO_FUND, and all other holds are released in the same tick.
  - **AC-15A-04-2** parties on two partitions: the anchor is the home of the lowest entity id, and replay gives an identical state hash.
  - **AC-15A-04-3** a link established after activation → VOIDED, holds released, fees kept.
  - **AC-15A-04-4** a party becomes ESTATE with no TERMINATION clause → TERMINATED, escrow returned.

## Goal
Only engine-executable, legal contracts get drafted. Every party sees the counterparty card before signing. Suspicious terms are flagged, and linked parties are voided.

## Imports
- T15A-02 `linkedAccounts`, `suspectPair`. T14A-08 read API (age, rank, guest). T13A-07 `services.law.gate` (Held), public record for the card. Ports for fair value and policy rate.

## Implementation steps
1. Common validation: parties 2..8; signer controls the party (owner chain) or holds an alliance APPROVE_DEALS role; no guests; Held signers refused (13A gate).
2. Per-clause drafting rules from the 15 §3.6.6 table:
   - TRANSFER: assets owned and unencumbered.
   - LOAN: rate ≥ 0, term ≤ 2,912 GD.
   - REVENUE_SHARE: 1–90%.
   - OPTION: premium > 0, expiry ≤ max term.
   - NON_COMPETE: ≤ 2 GY, non-empty scope.
   - GUARANTEE: guarantor ≠ guaranteed party, cap > 0.
   - …and so on for every kind.
3. Illegal content (13 list): paying for an op, flagged goods, price-fixing, or covenants to drop criminal cases → `SOC_CONTRACT_ILLEGAL` plus a 13 S5 flag message.
4. Flags: price > 10% from fair value, loan rate < policy − 2 pp, value to an account < 7 real days old, a suspect pair → flags set; signers must acknowledge them.
5. A linked pair → VOIDED at drafting (15#18 part 1).
6. Counterparty card fields: exactly the 15 §3.6.7 list. Never NW beyond the bucket, holdings or link scores.
7. Offer expiry defaults to 720 ticks, max 7 real days. Amending an ACTIVE contract creates version + 1, needs all signatures, and keeps the old version in history.

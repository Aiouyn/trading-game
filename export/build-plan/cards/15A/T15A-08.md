## Goal
Filings are the only legal way positions become public (L11). Obligations open automatically, and late filers are auto-filed and pay.

## Imports
- T03-16 ownership graph and look-through (beneficial ownership incl. option delta and concert contracts). T03-09 postings. 14B Reputation port (`FILING_LATE` −2).

## Implementation steps
1. Beneficial ownership = direct + controlled + option/swap delta + parties of engine-visible concert contracts (EQUITY_STAKE, COVENANT VOTE_WITH).
2. Trigger and due times from the 15 §3.8 table (720 or 1,440 ticks; quarterly + 21 GD).
3. At dueTick, an unsubmitted obligation is auto-filed as 'filed by the registrar', with fee $5,000 × MSI (`FILING_REGISTRAR_FEE`, a catalog gap; open question 1) and a `soc.filing.late` event → 14B −2 Rep.
4. The payload is built from `FILING_FIELDS` only; any extra key throws.
5. Voluntary statements carry evidence refs; a statement contradicting engine facts → 13 flag message.

## Implementation steps
1. Tables keyed by contract id, plus indexes by party entity, by due tick (clause events) and by anchor partition.
2. `CODES_15A`, mapping each 15 §3.15 name to its catalog code (alias), as was done for 14:
   - `CTR_REGISTRATION_FEE` → `SOC_CONTRACT_REG_FEE`
   - `CTR_TRANSFER`, `CTR_SERVICE_FEE`, `CTR_LICENSE_FEE`, `CTR_SUPPLY_PAYMENT`, `CTR_GUARANTEE`, `CTR_TERMINATION_FEE`, `CTR_MANDATE_FEE` → `SOC_CONTRACT_PAYMENT`
   - `CTR_LOAN_PRINCIPAL` → `SOC_LOAN_P2P_ADVANCE`/`_REPAY`
   - `CTR_LOAN_INTEREST` → `SOC_LOAN_P2P_INTEREST`
   - `CTR_LEASE_RENT` → `SOC_LEASE_PAYMENT`
   - `CTR_REVSHARE` → `SOC_REVENUE_SHARE`
   - `CTR_OPTION_PREMIUM` → `SOC_OPTION_PREMIUM_P2P`
   - `CTR_PENALTY` → `SOC_CONTRACT_PENALTY`
   - `CTR_BOUNTY` → `LAW_BOUNTY_PAYOUT`
   - escrow legs → `ESCROW_DEPOSIT`/`ESCROW_RELEASE`/`ESCROW_REFUND`
   - `CTR_P2P_TAX` → `TAX_P2P_TRANSFER` (TRANSFER to the tax authority, E-9/E-25)
   - `CTR_OPTION_SETTLE`, `CTR_COLLATERAL_SEIZE`, `FILING_REGISTRAR_FEE` → their own rows (E-25)
3. `CATALOG_GAPS_15A = []` (E-25). `WIRE_POST_FEE` and `INTEGRITY_CLAWBACK` are 15B/21 names and are not bound by 15A.
4. Events of 15 §5: `soc.contract.state`, `soc.contract.breach`, `soc.mandate.breaker`, `soc.filing.filed`, `soc.filing.late`, `soc.integrity.link` (internal).

## Goal
Provide the one predicate every module uses (15 §3.12.2): `linkedAccounts(a, b)`. It replaces the INERT `NO_LINKS` default in 14A (Fresh Start cooldown), 14B (editions, Rep events, hosting) and 13B (voiding, fraud). Signal collection (device, IP, payment, behaviour, the value-flow graph), reviews and enforcement are 15B; this card only combines the signals and answers the predicate.

## Imports
- T03-16 ownership graph (`controllingCharacterOf`, beneficial owner). T14A-02 `CharacterPorts`. `ports.linkSignals` (15B and 21; default: no signals).

## Implementation steps
1. Resolve both entity ids to their controlling characters (03 ownership). If they are the same character, the pair is linked.
2. Linked = score ≥ link_hard (0.7) OR admin-confirmed OR declared household OR same beneficial owner (03). Suspect = 0.4 ≤ score < 0.7.
3. Pairs are stored once (a < b, `cmpStr`); `linkedCharactersOf` is the sorted set of characters directly linked. Not transitive (open question 3); clusters are 15B.
4. A household is linked for every rule, and opens no review or sanction.
5. When a pair becomes linked, emit `soc.integrity.link` (internal) and void its contracts (T15A-04 `voidOnLink`).
6. Link scores and signals are never in any player view.
7. Wiring: the world composition passes `LINK_PORTS_FOR_14A` to 14A's `resolvePorts` and the 13B port, so the INERT ACs of 14A, 14B and 13B become live tests. Their `linkedPair` fixtures stay as unit fixtures.

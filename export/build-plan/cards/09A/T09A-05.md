## Goal
Distinct economics (09 §1 guarantee 2): give each archetype its own hourly formula behind one interface the hourly loop calls. This task builds the framework and the archetypes whose sales come from the market allocation or from physical output: FOOTFALL_RETAIL (incl. the FX booth), LOCAL_SERVICE, B2B_CONTRACT (spot part), TOURISM, TRANSPORT_CAPACITY, COMMODITY_PRODUCER and PROCESSOR, plus input costing (FIFO lots, energy, residual transaction fees), inventory, stockouts and the day-close reorder with deliveries.

## Implementation steps
1. Framework: an archetype's `hour()` is pure given its input and an `rng` factory that returns the business's `biz.ops:<bizId>` stream only when called (draw counts documented per archetype). All amounts are float64 in the business's currency at today's price level; the hourly loop (T09A-09) converts with `toMoney` and posts. Every quantity is multiplied by `x.scale` (first operation rule, R09).
2. Variable cost per unit (09 §3.4.1 step 3, §3.7.1): inputs from `type.inputs` with `qtyPerUnit × 0.96^efficiency × unitCostMult` (efficiency axis: −4% COGS and energy per level; product line cost multiplier from `ports.company.unitCostMult`); energy MWh from ENERGY_MWH lines priced at `ports.assets.powerPrice(bloc)`; COMPUTE_GPUH and DATA_DQU lines priced by 11 through `ports.ai.computePrice` (data at 22 reference cost); a residual `feePerUnit = max(0, κ_seg × refPrice − Σ ref input cost − ref energy cost) × 0.96^efficiency`, computed once from `calib` and 22 reference costs (κ is "inputs + energy + transaction"). Inputs consumed come from FIFO lots (`consumeFifo`); a just-in-time input with no lot is bought at spot that hour (cost recorded, see step 6).
3. FOOTFALL_RETAIL and LOCAL_SERVICE: `units = allocation.sold`, revenue `units × price` (source HOUSEHOLD, or the market's source); goods types are limited by finished inventory (already applied in the allocation) and stop selling a product on stockout until the next delivery. FX booth (twist `TOURIST_FLOW`): `price` is the spread fraction; `revenue = units × ticketSize(22) × price × fxMid(pair)/fxMid0`, demand source TOURISM. LOCAL_SERVICE κ small and φ mostly wages come from 22 data (no special code).
4. B2B_CONTRACT: contracted units are delivered first from capacity (T09A-15 delivers them daily at t = 0 and reserves `contractedPerHour = Σ active contract qtyPerDay / |hoursOpen|`); spot `units = allocation.sold` where the allocation used `cap − contractedPerHour`. Receivables on payment terms are created by T09A-15 for contract sales; spot B2B sales to NPC customers pay immediately.
5. TOURISM: room-nights `= allocation.sold` capped by rooms (capacity); `revenue = rooms × price`; occupancy `= sold / cap` stored in `dayAcc` via `demandSeen`. TRANSPORT_CAPACITY: capacity units × utilization where utilization comes from the freight market allocation (logit share of freight demand); `revenue = sold × price × freightIndex` (06 freight index, spikes in supply shocks); fuel cost per unit at the 06 crude/fuel price (`FUEL` line). Certified variants serve certified tenders (eligibility in T09A-14).
6. COMMODITY_PRODUCER: `output_h = outputRate0 × (1 + 0.12 × capLevel) × uptime × resourceFactor × weatherFactor`; `resourceFactor = (remaining/initial)^0.3` for mines (twist `RESERVES`; `reservesInitial = reserveYears(22) × 364 × 24 × outputRate0`, decremented by output; exploration upgrades add to both by the 22 amount), 1 otherwise; `weatherFactor = ports.world.weather(cityId, day)` for farms and wind (twist `WEATHER`), 1 otherwise. Output first serves internal allocations and supply contracts (T09A-13/15), the rest is sold to the NPC offtaker at `commoditySpot` (`COMMODITY_SALE_NPC` FAUCET), costs: lifting/AISC per unit = `κ_seg × refPrice × 0.96^eff` (`LIFTING`). Energy producers (MWh) price at the bloc power price.
7. PROCESSOR: `units = min(capacity, inputsAvailable/qtyPerUnit (binding input), demand)` where demand = `allocation.demandShare + secondPass`; finished goods produced and sold in the same hour; revenue at the business price, or for 22 types with `outputBasket` (refinery crack spread) at Σ product spot × yield; full bill of materials consumed FIFO; chip fab output may be allocated internally (T09A-13). Real-estate developer (PROCESSOR projects) and data broker use the same framework with 22 inputs.
8. Spot purchasing of inputs (09 §3.7.1, §3.4.3). Contradiction resolved here: 09 §3.7.1 says exchange-traded inputs are bought by orders on 05, while 06 §3.10.2 (owner of commodity price formation) already folds business input demand into its commodity model through `CompanyPort06.businessDemand`. Sending the same demand again as 05 orders would double-count price impact, so 09 buys spot inputs at the current `commoditySpot` (exchange-traded) or `wholesalePrice` (intermediate goods: `refCost × priceLevel × 1.03 × shortageMult`, shortageMult 1.25 during a SUPPLY_SHORTAGE event on that input) as `BIZ_COGS_FRM` (SINK to frm), aggregated per owner per game hour, and reports the aggregated quantities to 06 through `businessInputDemand` (and producer output through `producerOutput`). Listed in `_unit.md` for the reconciler.
9. Reorder (09 §3.4.3) at day close: for each input and finished good with trailing usage (last 7 days, from `dayAcc`/books), if `daysOfCover < reorderPolicy.daysOfCover` (default 3) order `2 × daysOfCover × usage` minus what is in transit, from (1) internal suppliers by allocation, (2) supply contracts (their schedule), (3) spot, respecting `reorderPolicy.maxPrice` (skip spot above it). Deliveries: spot at the next game-hour boundary, contracts on their schedule, cross-bloc after 1 game day. `receiveDeliveries` turns arrived rows into FIFO lots. Stockouts stop sales of the affected product until the next delivery.
10. Data by-product (09 §3.4.1 step 4, §3.7.5): `dataDqu = dataRateDquPerUnit × units` returned; the hourly loop forwards it to 11.

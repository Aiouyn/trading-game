## Implementation steps
1. PLATFORM_COMMISSION: `revenue_h = takeRate × observedFlow_h × platformShare`; `platformShare` = the business's smoothed share in its own platform market (platforms compete by logit in their category and scope, T09A-04); `observedFlow_h` = yesterday's value / 24 of: delivery app → Σ `revenueToday` (rolled to yesterday) of café and restaurant markets in the city (players and NPC field); marketplace → the bloc's retail markets; brokerage → `ports.market.npcRetailFlowNotional(bloc, day−1)` (never player trades); port terminal → port throughput value; exchange concession → `ports.market.blocTradedValue(bloc, day−1)`. Fee caps (09 §3.8.3 rule 5): exchange fee rate ≤ `PARAM.exchange_fee_cap_bps` (0.5 bp = 0.00005) of traded notional and total exchange concession revenue per game day ≤ `PARAM.exchange_rev_cap` × the bloc's daily traded value (tracked in `ArchState.earnedToday`, reset at t = 0); port fees ≤ 0.3% of throughput value. Revenue source FLOW_TAKE (`BIZ_SALES_FRM`).
2. SUBSCRIPTION per game hour: `N' = N × (1 − churn_h) + acquisitions_h`, `acquisitions_h = D_hour × s_j` (= `allocation.demandShare`), `churn_h = churnBase × (refPrice/price)^(−c_s/2) × (2 − qEff)`; `revenue_h = N × price / 24` (price per subscriber per game day); ad-funded types (social network): `revenue_h = N × ARPU × adIndex / 24` (`ports.world.adIndex`). Content or R&D spend lowers churn through `qEff` (product quality). `N` lives in `ArchState.subscribers`.
3. HIT_DRIVEN: at the business's first operation of each game week (Monday, first stagger hour) release `k = titlesPerWeek(capacityLevel)` titles; each title's lifetime revenue `= baseTitle × Pareto(α = 1.1, xm) × qEff` with `xm = 0.2 × (1 + b_s × ln(1 + mkt_j/M_ref))` ("marketing raises xm"), `baseTitle = 7 × rStar / 2.2` (2.2 = Pareto mean α·xm/(α−1) at xm 0.2, so expected revenue equals the calibrated target). Draws: one `uOpen()` per title from stream `biz.demand:<cityId>:<sector>` in title order. Earnings: exponential decay normalized to finish within 14 game days (336 hours, τ = 112 hours): hour i earns `lifetime × titleEarningsShare(i, i+1)`. Revenue source HOUSEHOLD (`HIT_REVENUE_NPC`). Data by-product as usual.
4. REGULATED_UTILITY: `revenue_h = allowedReturn × rateBase / (364 × 24) + passThrough_h` where `rateBase` = book value of assets (`c0 + upgradesSpent` indexed), `passThrough_h` = that hour's variable costs (energy, inputs); `allowedReturn = y10(bloc) + PARAM.utility_spread`, reset at each YEAR_CLOSE (13 regulator: `ports.law.regulatorReturnReset(bizId)` overrides when non-null). Power utility sells energy to all data centers in the city (11 pays; volume small risk).
5. COMPUTE_PROVIDER: `GPUh = min(capacity GPUh, ports.ai.computeDemandShare(bizId))`, revenue `GPUh × ports.ai.computePrice(bloc)`; energy MWh per GPUh at the bloc power price; internal allocation to an own lab at cost is T09A-13.
6. TRIAL_BINARY (twist `TRIALS`): one trial at a time; a new trial starts at the first operation of the game day after the previous ends; duration `28 + rng.int(64)` game days (one draw), cost per game day `trialCostPerDay` (posted at day close as `RD_SPEND_NPC`); at the end `P(pass) = PARAM.biotech_pass × qualityMult` (qualityMult = qEff), one `uOpen()` draw; pass pays `(10 + 20 × u2) × trialCostTotal` (`TRIAL_LICENSE_NPC` FAUCET) and sets `ArchState.lastTrialPassTick` (09B relaxes IPO history, 09 §3.10.1). Draw order per trial: duration, pass, multiple. Stream `biz.ops:<bizId>`. No revenue between trials.
7. TROPHY: ticket and sponsorship revenue from a small TOURISM-like market pool (allocation), heavy upkeep `PARAM.trophy_upkeep × value` per game day (value = indexed c0) posted at day close as `TROPHY_UPKEEP`; Prestige is 14's (emit nothing extra; 14 reads ownership).
8. FLIP_TRADING (twist `DROPS`): each game day `ports.world.hypeDrops(cityId, day)` offers 0 to 3 drops; `biz.flip.buy` (owner, OPEN, qty ≤ remaining daily capacity, cash) buys at `dropPrice` (`BIZ_COGS_FRM`); items keep `hype` that steps each game hour by `hypeStep` with 22's `decay`, `sigma` and one `normal()` draw per item per hour from `biz.demand:<cityId>:<sector>`; `biz.flip.sell` sells at `dropPrice × hype` to NPC buyers (`BIZ_SALES_HH` FAUCET) in P9; selling to players goes through 15 contracts. Rejects: `BIZ_NOT_OWNER`, `BIZ_NOT_FOUND`, `BIZ_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `BIZ_BAD_PAYLOAD` (qty > drop or capacity).
9. FINANCIAL_INSTITUTION: no hourly loop (09 §3.4.2); the institution runs in 07 or 08 (T09A-11 founds it).
10. Twist rules (09 §3.4.6), where each is implemented: `MOVE_DAILY` → T09A-12 `biz.move`; `CLUSTER` → T09A-13; `CHAIN_NATIVE` → T09A-11 (restaurant chain opens 3 sites); `VERTICAL_INPUT` → T09A-13 allocation; `CRITIC` → T09A-10 events; `PLAYER_SERVICE`, `TAX_REDUCER` → T09A-15; `NEWS_PUBLISHER` → the business may publish through 16's publishing intent (16 validates facts; false statements are 13 offences): 09 only exposes `canPublish(store, bizId)` = OPEN news outlet (export from twists.ts); `HACK_RATE` → `twistDemandMult` × `ports.conflict.worldHackRate`; `LITIGATION` → × 13 court caseload index (`ports.law.caseloadIndex`); `TOURIST_FLOW` → T09A-05 FX booth; `LIMITED_EDITIONS` → each finished unit of a watch manufacture is reported as `ctx.send(ownerId, 'life.collectible.mint', { bizId, serial, typeId }, 'NEXT')` for 14 to create a numbered collectible; `LONG_ORDERS` → yacht builder sales become orders with delivery `91 + rng.int(92)` game days, 30% deposit paid at order (`BIZ_SALES_HH` on the deposit), balance at delivery, cancellation per game day with `longOrderCancelProb(outputGap)` (stream `biz.supply:<bizId>`; the deposit is kept); `DROPS` → step 8; `REGULATORY_EVENTS` → 13 port reset/closures; `EMBARGO` → 16 `demandShock` returns 0 for embargoed bloc sales; `RESERVES`, `WEATHER` → T09A-05; `TRIALS` → step 6. The `LONG_ORDERS` cancellation rate and `titlesPerWeek` are decisions recorded in `_unit.md` (the spec states the mechanism, not the number).
11. Register all archetypes of this task with `registerArchetype`.

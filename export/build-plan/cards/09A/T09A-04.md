## Goal
The heart of real shared competition (09 §1 guarantee 1): every business, named NPC firm and the aggregate NPC field in a market gets an attractiveness `u`, the logit gives target shares, customers move toward them slowly (θ per game hour), each hour's demand is served up to capacity with a single overflow pass, and owners get a free pricing assistant that finds the profit-maximizing price by golden-section search. Pure rules plus the hourly per-market recompute step.

## Implementation steps
1. `SEGMENT_COEF` = 09 §3.3.1 table. `qualityIndex`, `qEff`, `staffFactor`, `mkt_j`, `M_ref = 0.05 × rStar` (indexed by price level), `location_j` = 10's location term (DISTRICT scope; 0 otherwise), `brand_j` per 09 §3.3.1 (bounded) where `boosts` = Σ active campaign `brandBoost` decayed (half-life 14 game days) + event boosts (viral, celebrity, critic) + conglomerate +0.05 + product LAUNCH +0.2 (`ports.company.launchBrand`). Features: `+0.3 × ln(1 + features)` in PREMIUM and B2B only (`ports.company.featuresTerm`). `productQualityRel` from `ports.company.productQualityRel` (1 for non-product types).
2. Promise bonus (09 §3.3.1): PRICE multiplies `c_s` by 1.3 on the price term only when `price < refPrice`; QUALITY multiplies `a_s` by 1.3; RELIABILITY multiplies `r_s` by 1.3; SPEED adds +0.2 if last-hour headroom `1 − units/cap ≥ 0.30`; CERTIFIED adds +0.3 in B2B and INSTITUTIONAL segments if any valid certification is held; CONFIDENTIAL adds +0.3 there if the posture is held (`ports.conflict.dpEff(company) ≥` the category's `minConfidentiality` from 22, or `> 0` if the category has none).
3. Promise cost: the spec says a promise "costs +5% to fixed costs". Decision written down here: PRICE is the default promise of every new business (09 §3.1.5 sets defaults; reference calibration in T09A-02 assumes no bonus at ref price) and carries no surcharge; every other promise multiplies fixed costs by 1.05 (`promiseFixedCostMult`). This keeps calibration (09-AT-01) exact for default businesses.
4. Target shares (09 §3.3.2): participants are player businesses whose status is OPEN, IDLE (weight × 0.5), STRIKE, UNDER_REPAIR or CLOSED_EVENT (they keep their weight but cannot sell, so their demand overflows), and named NPC firms from `ports.world.namedFirms(marketId)` (their own `u`). SETTING_UP businesses are not participants; FAILED, SOLD, SEIZED are removed (weight 0, their smoothed share decays at θ). `W_j = exp(u_j) × weightMult`, `W_npc = npcField.weight × exp(npcFieldU)`. Use `dmath.exp` and `dmath.ln`.
5. Smoothing: `s_j(h) = s_j(h−1) + θ (s*_j − s_j(h−1))`, θ = `PARAM.biz_share_adjust_rate`; new businesses start at 0; the NPC field gets `1 − Σ s_j` (renormalize). Keys iterate in sorted order (SortedMap) for determinism. `recomputeMarketShares` runs in P9 each game hour at ticks with `(tick − market.hourOffset) mod 30 = 0`, computes `u` for all participants from committed state, writes `Market.shares`, `hhi` (by owner group via `ports.social.ownerGroupOf`), `topOwnerShare/topOwnerId`, `avgPrice` (share-weighted, NPC at ref), `lastShareTick`, and copies each business's smoothed share into `Business.share`. Emits `biz.marketShares` PUBLIC once per game day (at the first recompute after t = 0) with HHI and the share bands of the top 3 business names (never owner identity).
6. Sales (09 §3.3.3): `demandShare_j = D_hour × s_j`; `cap_j = capacityPerHour0 × (1 + 0.12 × capacityLevel) × uptime × staffCapFactor` where uptime is 1 only for OPEN, `staffCapFactor = min(1, OPERATIONS staffed / required)`, pending placement multiplies by 0.9 (10 §3.15 rule 2); `sold_j = min(demandShare_j, cap_j, finishedInventory_j)` for goods. Overflow = Σ (demandShare − sold) over capacity- or inventory-limited sellers; second pass offers it to unconstrained sellers (players with spare capacity and the NPC field, unlimited) pro rata to their `s_j` normalized over the unconstrained set; each sells up to its remaining capacity; the remainder is lost. One pass only, no iteration (09-AT-05). Units sold never exceed demand. `lostById` attributes each seller's unserved own demand (shown as "unserved demand"). Fractional units are kept as float64 (they become money only at the posting).
7. Pricing (09 §3.3.4): `clampPrice` clamps to `[0.7, 1.3] × refPrice_segment` and reports `clamped` (the intent task sends a `biz.notice`; exactly 0.7 or 1.3 is allowed, edge 4). `pricingAssistant`: competitors fixed, `π(p) = min(D × s*(p), cap) × (p − mc) − fixed`, golden-section search on `[0.7, 1.3] × ref` with exactly 20 iterations (keep the bracket, return the midpoint), where `D` = the business's average open-hour demand (`demandDay / |hoursOpen|`), `mc` = `κ_seg × refPrice × 0.96^efficiency × unitCostMult` (variable cost per unit), `fixed` = daily fixed costs / |hoursOpen|. Free for everyone; AI operators and managers use the same function (09 §3.6.3).
8. Edge cases owned: §8 #2 two businesses of one owner in one market are separate participants (cannibalization real; cluster bonus is T09A-13); §8 #3 zero participants → NPC field 100%, demand still computed; §8 #4 price at a boundary allowed, outside clamped with a notice.

## Implementation steps
1. Accumulation check registered in 05 P1; tender is the only path past caps.
2. Tender lifecycle OPEN → (RAISED)* → CLOSED|LAPSED; holders withdraw until close; competing tenders allowed.
3. NPC holders tender if price ≥ reservation (premium draw per block on `biz.ma:<tenderId>`), follow board within 5%.
4. Payment to NPC holders: `EQ_TENDER_OFFER_NPC` (alias `TENDER_OFFER_PAY_NPC`), TRANSFER to the NPC holder sector (E-44); player holders `EQ_MERGER_CASH`.
5. Absorbed NPC company: legacy unit priced via 05's sector P/E for the 09 §3.1.4 sector it was listed in.

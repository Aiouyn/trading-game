## Implementation steps
1. Eligibility per §3.10.1 table, all params; biotech after a trial pass needs 1 quarter.
2. Float 15–49%, secondary ≤ `ipo_max_secondary`. Fee `ipo_underwriting_fee` × gross proceeds (`CMP_UNDERWRITING_FEE`, TRANSFER to the NPC underwriter, Producer ruling E-44).
3. Clearing: highest price in range with demand ≥ offering; below range → FAILED, fee 1% of target (`CMP_IPO_FAIL_FEE`, TRANSFER to the NPC underwriter; amount [Quant] confirm, E-44), refile after 91 days.
4. Allocation pro rata at the price, cap `ipo_max_alloc_pct × float`; excess re-spread pro rata among uncapped bidders, then largest remainder by bid id.
5. Proceeds primary to the treasury (`EQ_ISSUE_PRIMARY`); NPC buyers pay through `EQ_IPO_PROCEEDS_NPC` (TRANSFER from the NPC buyer sector, not FAUCET; E-44).

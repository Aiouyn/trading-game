## Goal
Company building means products that must keep up. Each category's frontier drifts up every game quarter, the NPC field sits on it, and a product line that stops investing slides in relative quality and loses share. R&D projects turn budget, researchers and AI assistance into quality, features, lower unit cost, new products or faster certification, with a seeded failure chance.

## Imports
- T09A-01 / T09A-04: business rows, attractiveness inputs (09A reads `CompanyHook09`). T11-??: `researchAssist(cmpId)` ∈ [0,1]. T16A-??: product news for launches. 22 via 09A: `techDrift(cat)`.

## Implementation steps
1. Durations 7..182 days; RESEARCH staff ≥ researchers (09A staff); budget spent evenly per day (`RD_SPEND_NPC` SINK or TRANSFER to a contracted player studio).
2. Completion draws: `U` for failure, then `z ~ N(0,1)`, in that order on `biz.rd:<projectId>`. Outcomes per §3.5.5; PROCESS_COST floors unitCostMult at 0.6; PARTIAL if gainRaw < 0.02.
3. Results PRIVATE (L11) until launch; launch PUBLIC news only for companies with revenue ≥ $1M/day.

A private market with real venture odds: most startups fail, a few return 20–200×. Fate is fixed at creation and money cannot change it; research only sharpens your view of it.

## Implementation steps
1. Fate on `biz:startup:<cmpId>` before any investment exists; investment code paths never read or write that stream.
2. Rounds every 91–182 days; milestone p = 0.7 + 0.1·[multiple ≥ 3]; up `U(2,4)`, down `U(0.5,0.9)`.
3. Exits: IPO if ≥ 10×, else trade sale at outcome × invested; failure → 5% of last post-money by preference.

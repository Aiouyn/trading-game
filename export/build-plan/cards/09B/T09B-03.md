## Goal
Owning the supply chain pays: inputs at cost, cheaper builds, shared services. It must not be a way to strip value from minority holders, so COST transfer pricing is only allowed inside wholly owned groups, and consolidation removes intra-group profit.

## Imports
- T09B-05: `hasMinorityHolders`. T09A-04 / 09A-05: capacity, inputs, hourly loop. T03-15: consolidation elimination.

## Implementation steps
1. COST across a minority boundary → reject `BIZ_ALLOC_MINORITY_COST` (09A code) and `law13.surveillance(+30)`.
2. Internal units post `INTERNAL_TRANSFER` (TRANSFER), eliminated by 03 consolidation; opportunity cost shown before commit.
3. The conglomerate count is of business sectors (`BizSector`, the 13 sector rows of 09 §3.1.4), not of the market sectors that §3.1.4 maps them to.
- **E-47 NPC rivals.** When the friction owner group's (T03-17) share of a market exceeds `biz_rival_share_trigger`, NPC entry (09 §3.3.6 `npc_entry_rate`) is multiplied by `1 + k·w` and NPC prices fall by `cut·w`, both [Quant]; product-line prices of T09B-02 face the cut. Cause string `why.topend.rivals`.
- **E-59 rival gate (E-59; gate final per E-60).** `groupShare` is the cluster-aggregated sector share (linked alts and shells, the same aggregation as T03-17 `frictionNW`), summed over all sibling companies of the group in the sector. A peak-share high-water mark is kept; once triggered, the response stays on and releases only after share has stayed ≥ `biz_rival_release_pp` (0.05) below the peak for `biz_rival_release_gd` (56) consecutive GD. Coefficients entry_k 1.5 and price_cut 0.07 stay PROVISIONAL [Quant] (E-60), applied with the gate; AC-09B-03-RIVALS keeps its E-47 vector until Quant finalizes them.

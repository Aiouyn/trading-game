## Implementation steps
1. Super-voting (≤ 10 votes) only before listing; converts 1:1 to common at IPO (T09B-06 calls `convertSuperVoting`).
2. Bonds: 1-game-day book on 06, uniform yield; unrated bonds only to players and HY funds. Convertibles convertible after 91 days. Interest daily `rate/364`, posted at day close (07 codes for bank loans; bond coupons via 06).
3. Covenants: breach → CURE_PERIOD `covenant_cure_days` (28); waiver draw from `biz.fin:<cmpId>`; uncured → event of default; NPC lender accelerates when `fear > 0.5` or breach > 25%, else reprices +200 bps; unpaid acceleration → T09B-12 distress.
4. Dividends: ex-date next game day, paid at that day close (`EQ_DIVIDEND` to players; `EQ_DIVIDEND_NPC` TRANSFER to the NPC holder sector, E-44). Buybacks ≤ 1% float per day through 05 company orders. Listed secondaries: 7 days notice, discount ≤ 10% to 5-day VWAP, or rights issue.
5. Related party: outside ±`related_party_band` of NPC appraisal → block, +30 surveillance; inside → listed in next filing.
6. `INV_BS_005`: per company and class, Σ cap-table shares = sharesOutstanding (no over-issuance).

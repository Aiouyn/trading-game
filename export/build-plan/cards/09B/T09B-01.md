## Goal
The stable contract of unit 09B (company building, corporate finance, IPOs, M&A, LBOs, restructuring). It transcribes the 09 §2.5 entities, defines tables and ids, binds every 09B money flow of 09 §3.17 to a 03 catalog row, declares ports to 05, 06, 07, 08, 13, 14, 15 and 16, and implements the shell of 09A's `CompanyHook09` (filled by later 09B cards). Every later 09B card imports from here.

## Implementation steps
1. Transcribe §2.5; Money fields bigint micro; percentages `number`; share counts bigint.
2. Ids: `cmp_` (companies, 03 kind, already in use). New kinds pass the E-20 regex (`^(?:[a-z]{3}|hh|prop)_…`, ≤ 64) and are on the E-26 table and lint allow-list (E-44); created only through `ID09B`.
3. `CODES09B` bindings (test asserts row and class):
   - `DIVIDEND` → `EQ_DIVIDEND`; buyback → `EQ_BUYBACK`; issuance and IPO buyer proceeds → `EQ_ISSUE_PRIMARY`; tender and merger cash → `EQ_MERGER_CASH`; stock mergers → `EQ_MERGER_STOCK`; squeeze-out / take-private → `EQ_DELIST_CASHOUT`; spin-off → `EQ_SPINOFF`; split → `EQ_SPLIT` (REVALUE).
   - Venture rounds → `CMP_STARTUP_ROUND`; liquidation preferences → `CMP_LIQ_PREF_PAYOUT`; restructuring swaps → `CMP_DEBT_EQUITY_SWAP`; absorbed NPC legacy operations → `CMP_NPC_REVENUE` (FAUCET) and `CMP_NPC_COSTS`; deal costs → `CMP_DEAL_COSTS_NPC`.
   - Underwriting fee → `CMP_UNDERWRITING_FEE` (TRANSFER to the NPC underwriter, E-44). NPC sale → 09A `CODE.SALE_NPC`. Intra-group → `INTERNAL_TRANSFER`.
   - E-44 rows, all TRANSFER (NPC money closed): `DIVIDEND_NPC` → `EQ_DIVIDEND_NPC`, `TENDER_OFFER_PAY_NPC` → `EQ_TENDER_OFFER_NPC`, `IPO_PROCEEDS_NPC` → `EQ_IPO_PROCEEDS_NPC`, `WAIVER_FEE` → `CMP_WAIVER_FEE`, IPO failure fee → `CMP_IPO_FAIL_FEE`, NPC golden parachute → `CMP_GOLDEN_PARACHUTE_NPC`. No null bindings remain.
4. No behaviour beyond construction and validation.

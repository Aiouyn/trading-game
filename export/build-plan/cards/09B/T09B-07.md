## Goal
Once listed, a player company lives under rules that make pumping your own stock unprofitable: lockups, capped and pre-announced founder sales, blackouts, rank marks at min(V, 3-day VWAP), media flags and fast disclosure. It reports quarterly earnings against a consensus this card computes, and governs through a board.

## Implementation steps
1. `insiderOrderCheck` registered as a 05 P1 validator for player-listed equities; blackout = earnings day − 2 to + 1 (06 schedule).
2. Founder cap: Σ sells today ≤ `founder_sell_cap × outstanding`, each covered by a filing made the day before.
3. Guidance miss > 20% two quarters running → guidance ignored 2 quarters.
4. Disclosure late by > 1 game day → 13 offence report (S1–S5 mapping from 03 §12 #10).

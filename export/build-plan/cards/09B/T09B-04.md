## Goal
One valuation rule ties together NPC sale prices, IPO pricing, takeover prices, restructuring and private-stake marks. EV is profit-based on a trailing 7-day mean so upgrades only count once they earn, and the NPC buyer pays 85% of it. For a listed player company the fundamental value V is a sum of the parts supplied to 05 each day close, tagged with the market sector from 09 §3.1.4.

## Imports
- T09A-02: `multipleDays(tier)`, `MARKET_SECTOR_OF`, catalog `c0`, `sectorAdj` (22). T09A-06: daily profit history.
- T10A-05: property appraisals. T06A-02: marks of financial assets. T15A-??: P2P transfer contracts and exchange.

## Implementation steps
1. `EV_biz = max(floor, profit7Avg·multipleDays·sectorAdj·growthAdj)`; IDLE days excluded; fewer than 3 open days → profit term 0; `floor = 0.25·(c0 + upgradesSpent)·condition/100`.
2. Sector map (09 §3.1.4): FOOD_HOSPITALITY 9, RETAIL_CONSUMER 8, REAL_ESTATE_SERVICES 10, LOGISTICS_TRANSPORT 5, ENERGY 3, MINING_AGRICULTURE 4, MANUFACTURING 5 (chip fab 2), TECH_AI 1 (data center, cloud 2), FINANCE 6, MEDIA_ENTERTAINMENT 11, HEALTH 7, LUXURY 9, SECURITY_LEGAL_INTEL 12, via 09A's `MARKET_SECTOR_OF`. A multi-business company lists in the sector of its largest EV contributor, ties by lower sector number (decision; open question).
3. NPC legacy operations use 05's sector P/E band midpoint for that sector.
4. NPC sale: instant at `npc_sale_haircut × EV_biz` (`BIZ_SALE_NPC` via 09A's code map, FAUCET); the market's NPC field weight += W_j. Player sale via 15 contract (2% tax; > 10% from EV flagged); exchange listing via 15.
5. All in number, one `toMoney` half-even at the end; `fundamentalValue` divides by diluted shares in bigint with half-even.

## Implementation steps
1. FAIR.path_parity: each family's (trading A02, business A06, property A08, AI A09, institution A10-A12, founder A07) median chapter NW multiple / cross-family median in 0.5-2.0; path_sharpe best/median ≤ 1.5; late_joiner A27 at world age 365 median NW at account age 56 / launch cohort at age 56 in 0.5-1.5 (G5, 20 seeds); bottleneck_tenure: no owner > 40% of a critical input (compute, energy, port, exchange concession, chip fab) for > 1 chapter without substitute entry; first_winner_lock correlation of day-7 and day-365 rank among top 100 ≤ 0.8; league_mobility ≥ 25% promoted in their first 4 league weeks; newcomer_protection 0 hostile ops in the window (ACCOUNTING).
2. Delta audit: for every delta delivered in the run, every field path must be entitled for its recipient at that tick (owner, party, public; 19.C channel table and 02 visibility rules); violations counted per seed; band PRV.delta_audit = 0 (ACCOUNTING, G2).
3. Inference: A24 predictions vs truth from the `holdings.truth` probe; baseline uses only public filings and aggregate flow; lift = accuracy difference ≤ 0.05 (EXPERIENCE). Whale Watch identity: aggregate items that name or uniquely identify an account without an allowed disclosure (24 R04) = 0.
4. SOC: A16 rings detected by 15's anti-cheat within 7 real days ≥ 90%; HONEST agents on a synthetic shared household network flagged ≤ 2%.
5. UX e2e bands (Playwright, `tests/e2e/ux-bands.spec.ts`): first trade < 90 s wall time on the scripted newcomer path; zero rendered strings containing "you should", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "buy now", "can't lose" or the French list (G0); pushes ≤ 5 per character per real day and 0 in quiet hours.

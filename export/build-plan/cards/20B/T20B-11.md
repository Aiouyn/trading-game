## Goal
Provide the machinery every band card uses and the market bands. The band runner turns a `BalanceBand` (T20A-05) into harness runs (preset, population template, horizon, seed count and set), computes its metric through the metric registry (`apps/harness/metrics/<id>.ts`, 20 Rule 3.7.2), evaluates it with `evaluateBand` (undefined-metric handling of 20 §8 edge 7), writes BandResults, and on failure files the worst seed as a RegressionCase. Common metric definitions are implemented once: NW at day close, multiple, beat share vs the BH basket (equal-weight stock basket, whole shares, market orders, same fees, same start tick), Sharpe with `tm_trading_days_per_year` (260), engaged agents (`tm_engaged_active_hours_per_real_day` 1.5), real-time conversions per 00 §5.1. Then every band of 20 §3.7.3 is registered and computable, with the four acceptance tests that are pure market bands.

## Implementation steps
1. Band runner: seeds from DEV for local runs, VALIDATION for G2, VALIDATION and WITHHELD for G3 (WITHHELD drawn and retired through `WithheldLedger`, results reported aggregate-only); each seed runs the band's preset, population template and horizon; metric undefined on a seed -> excluded; if fewer than 80% of seeds define it, extend with more seeds until 80% or 3× the seed count, then INSUFFICIENT_DATA (fails at G3 only for ACCOUNTING). A failing band's worst seeds become RegressionCases (source BAND). With `adaptiveShare` 0.1, 10% of the population is replaced by A47 (Rule 3.5.5).
2. Register all §3.7.3 bands with their metric, range, class, seeds and gate exactly as the table: MKT.kurtosis, vol_cluster, vol_volume, autocorr, reversal_ic, momentum_ic, vol_ratio, index_cagr, max_strategy_beats_bh, learn_value_gap_beat (0.40-0.60; 0.50-0.70 when the ecology publish-price feature flag is on), learn_value_gap_edge (≤ +0.05; ≤ +0.15 with ecology), learn_regime_split, crowd_decay, stacked_signals, self_impact_clip_ratio, self_mark_peak_pump, ladder_roundtrip_ev, parity_residual, halt_rate, news_drift.
3. Tape families, registered as separate bands with ids `MKT.rival_*`, `MKT.rumor_*`, `MKT.event_*` using the criteria 20 §3.7.3 lists: rival (single impact path ACCOUNTING; ADV cap ACCOUNTING; median multiple 0.4-2.5x; mirror beat ≤ 0.70 DOMINANCE; front-run median P&L ≤ 0 DOMINANCE; no look-ahead ACCOUNTING; ignores player ACCOUNTING), rumor at 200 seeds (true share 0.47-0.63; per-seed max ≤ 0.75; blind EV ≤ 0; insider flag rate 1.00; no truth leak), event at 200 seeds × 5 years (counts; overlap 0; majors 80-180 per 200 × 5 y; crash farm ≤ BH; flash dip EV ≤ 0; bubble timer ≤ 0.70; no end leak). The band values are 20's (20 owns bands; 16 §3.6.7's "[0.50, 0.60] sign accuracy" is the owner's design target inside 20's band).
4. Crowd-exit valuation for peaks and rank (H08): metrics read 05's `crowdExitValue` (exit against the asset's whole crowd), never one account's last print.
5. Crowd gate: crowd of 100 agents × $250k (and a single $25M agent) following one public signal; signed flow > 10% of ADV over the prior 21 game days defines "crowded"; compare beat share crowded vs uncrowded.

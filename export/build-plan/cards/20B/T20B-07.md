## Goal

## Implementation steps
1. A16 Alt ring: 2-6 linked accounts; alts funnel value to the main through the channel mix (P2P, contracts, off-market trades, staged lawsuits) coordinated over `ringChannel`. A17 Collusion pair: two "rivals" who trade, sue, insure and run ops against each other to move value. A18 Wash ring: linked accounts trade with each other in 1-720 clips to lift marks, volume or peaks.
2. A19 Greedy optimizer: each game day enumerates candidate intents over its observations (search width parameter), scores one-day expected NW gain with a simple model from its own observations, submits the best feasible set within the rate limit.
3. A24 Inference probe: from everything its character can see (public filings, aggregate flow, boards, Wire), predicts other agents' holdings and strategies (model class parameter: frequency baseline, logistic, k-NN); records predictions for the probe metric; never sees truth during the run.
4. A31 Tournament overfitter: tunes a strategy graph on public practice seeds to maximize tournament score (search budget), then enters the tournament (secret seeds).
5. A32 Exit-scam manager: runs a fund and tries every allowed intent sequence to extract investor value (fees changes, trades against own accounts, redemption gating, closing); 10,000 random action sequences are generated by the policy over the fund intent space.
6. A26 Newcomer: follows 18's onboarding path, small and cautious. A27 Late joiner: joins at world age 30, 180 or 365 game-day equivalents and plays A06 or A02. A28 Dormant returner: plays 2 weeks, goes dormant 14, 30 or 90 real days, returns. A29 Broke player: keeps NW under $1,000 by spending, watches the maximum ads, takes fresh starts. A30 Bust farmer (`yolo_bust`): all-in on the highest-volatility licensed asset; when NW < $1,000 it declares a fresh start, takes the best perk and repeats.

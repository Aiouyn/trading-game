## Goal
Implement the exploit hunts of 20 §3.6: the oracles O-MINT, O-RISKFREE, O-CAP, O-LEAK, O-PEAK, O-SHIELD, O-GRIEF over the hunting agents' accounts (Rule 3.6.2); the hunt catalogue H01-H35 with agents, oracles, designed counters and owning modules (Rule 3.6.3), each active from the first G1 pass of its target system; the finding lifecycle (Rule 3.6.4: candidate, reproduction on 3 VALIDATION seeds with ≥ 2 of 3, minimized RegressionCase, severity, live exposure check for CRITICAL and HIGH within one real hour, MITIGATED and CLOSED transitions, ACCEPTED_EDGE only with a 24 decision); the live exposure query handed to 21; and the continuous hunt farm of Rule 3.6.5. The NPC constraints of 20 §6 are checked through H21, H28 and A46 (an NPC rule a test agent profits from risk-free is a finding against 05 or 16).

## Implementation steps
1. Oracles exactly as Rule 3.6.2. O-MINT: Σ NW gains of hunters − Σ faucet postings they received − Σ NW losses imposed on identifiable counterparties (TRANSFER postings to them) − market P&L explained by the 05 cause vector; residual > `tm_oracle_mint_tolerance` (0.001) × traded notional crosses (ACCOUNTING). O-RISKFREE (DOMINANCE) with `tm_oracle_excess_min` (0.5 pp per game day). O-CAP (ACCOUNTING) over the caps the hunt names. O-LEAK (EXPERIENCE). O-PEAK (ACCOUNTING) against frictionless counterfactual mids. O-SHIELD (EXPERIENCE; intended value zero). O-GRIEF with `tm_oracle_grief_ratio` (20) on > 1% of attempts.
2. HUNTS table verbatim (35 rows with targets, agents, oracles, designed counters and owners). A hunt is active from the first G1 pass of its target system and runs on every G3 at 60 seeds and in the farm. A crime that pays only with its intended detection and sanction risk is not an exploit (Rule 3.6.1): H25 measures profit net of the expected sanction at the band's rate.
3. Finding lifecycle: candidate on a seed -> replay on 3 VALIDATION seeds, open when ≥ 2 reproduce; create the minimized RegressionCase (source HUNT) via T20B-03; severity CRITICAL if O-MINT residual > $1M per agent per game year at STANDARD or any money-cap O-CAP violation, HIGH if O-RISKFREE excess > 2 pp per game day, MEDIUM for O-LEAK lift > 0.05 or O-GRIEF, LOW otherwise; CRITICAL and HIGH schedule the exposure check within one real hour; MITIGATED when its case passes, CLOSED after one chapter of farm runs without recurrence; ACCEPTED_EDGE needs a 24 decision naming the capacity bound.
4. Exposure: the finding carries its signature as an event query; `liveExposure` scans the last chapter of the production event store read-only and applies the oracle to suspects' accounts; the list goes to 21's queues (no automatic punishment).
5. Farm: `tm_hunt_farm_worlds` (32) worlds on rotating DEV and VALIDATION seeds running POP_EXPLOIT, hunts of systems changed in the last 14 real days weighted ×3; findings follow step 3.

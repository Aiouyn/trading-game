## Goal
Implement the shared agent toolkit (observation helpers over the character's own views, order helpers that build intents exactly as a client would, simple public signals) and the 15 HONEST archetypes of 20 Rule 3.5.3 as `AgentPolicy` objects registered in the `AgentPolicyRegistry` of T20B-02. Each policy decides only from its `AgentApi` observations (Rule 3.5.2), draws its parameters per agent from the listed ranges on stream `tm:agentparams`, and uses only player intent kinds. NPCs are not agents (20 §6); HONEST agents play as intended and provide the baseline populations.

## Implementation steps
1. Toolkit: observations come only from the character's views; order helpers produce the same payloads as the client (05 order schema); public signals use only published daily bars and fair-value bands the client shows.
2. Policies (parameter ranges exactly as Rule 3.5.3; cadences in ticks; all money in micro-units):
   - A01 Passive investor: monthly buy of a diversified equity basket (equity share 0.4-0.9, rest bonds/cash), hold, quarterly rebalance at a MONTH_OPEN/QUARTER_OPEN day.
   - A02 Active trader: 5-20 names, momentum or value signal with lookback 5-63 days, stop 3-12%, cadence 30 ticks.
   - A03 Quant: builds an 11 strategy graph from factor signals, validates on withheld practice seeds through 11's validation view before deploying, capital 10-60%, DU budget by tier.
   - A04 Macro trader: rate, FX and curve positions from 04 releases with leverage 1-3x.
   - A05 Credit investor: buys bonds, CDS and tranches within a maximum rating-drop tolerance; buys distressed paper after defaults.
   - A06 Business builder: buys, upgrades and expands businesses (09), hires, reinvest share 0.5-0.95.
   - A07 Founder: founds a company, raises, files an IPO, does M&A and LBOs at 3-6x EBITDA leverage.
   - A08 Property investor: buys, mortgages at LTV 0.5-0.8 and develops property (10).
   - A09 AI lab: runs the 11 pipeline with training share of income 0.1-0.5; licenses models; installs defenses.
   - A10 Banker: charters a bank (07) once licensed, sets lending policy to a target capital ratio and loan growth, manages liquidity.
   - A11 Fund manager: runs a fund (08) with a strategy class and fees between 1/10 and 2/20, raises from other agents through in-game subscription.
   - A12 Insurer: charters an insurer, writes cover, buys reinsurance with a retention share.
   - A13 Market maker: registers (05), quotes two-sided with a quote width and inventory limit, flattens at day close.
   - A14 Lifestyle spender: spends 0.05-0.4 of income on 14's catalogue and hosts events.
   - A15 Whale defender: high-wealth start, spends a defense share of income on 12 defenses.
3. Every policy progresses legitimately to licenses it needs (20 §8 edge 10): when a license gate rejects, it buys the license or waits like a player.
4. Determinism: all agent randomness comes from `api.rng()`; no Date, Math.random or shared state between agents.

## Test plan
honest.test.ts (MICRO or SMALL harness worlds, 3-14 game days):
- each A01-A15 policy, alone with NPCs, submits at least one accepted intent of its family's kinds within 7 game days and never an intent kind outside its declared list (driver log).
- parameter draws fall inside the ranges and are reproducible from the seed.
- A01 rebalances on the quarter boundary and its basket weights match within one lot; A02 exits on a stop hit; A13 keeps inventory within its limit.
- no policy reads anything outside AgentApi (static check: policy files import only toolkit and protocol types).

## Implementation steps
1. G0 (≤ 20 min): typecheck, lint with determinism rules, INV-STR-001..010 (`runStructural` plus 19A/21 checkers), all E1 unit tests, E2 properties at `tm_property_runs_pr` (200), protocol contract tests, FAST regression cases, a 30-game-day MICRO world with invariants on, 2-partition equivalence on that world, bands UX.no_advice_words and FAU.ad_fixed_reward.
2. G1 per unit: its acceptance tests green in their categories, E2 properties at `tm_property_runs_nightly` (10,000), a passing negative test for every invariant it registers, its FAST and NIGHTLY cases; `certifiable` refuses passes from runs that stubbed the module.
3. G2 nightly and on toolchain upgrades: G0 + golden replay of recorded logs + every invariant over a 30-game-day world with all built units (STANDARD from S2 on, SMALL before) + equivalence {1, 4, 16} + random map + host profiles + toolchain comparison + browser check + all NIGHTLY cases + all G2 bands at 20 seeds + e2e at 1440 and 390 px + delta audit.
4. G3: G2 green on the same commit; G3 bands at their seed counts on VALIDATION and WITHHELD (WITHHELD drawn then retired after the run; aggregate-only report; the worst WITHHELD seeds of a failing band are copied re-salted into VALIDATION); DOMINANCE and CALIBRATION bands re-run with 10% A47; every active hunt at 60 seeds with no open HIGH or CRITICAL Finding in the systems under test; TAIL bands; adaptation runs; for a module turned on for the first time a 1-chapter STANDARD POP_EXPLOIT run and the specialist playtest record (T20B-20). Systems built but flagged off in production run with the flag on and block only the release that turns the flag on (20 §8 edge 8).
5. G4: G3 green; load, chaos and deploy drills (T20B-19); restore drill; version replay of the last production day; French coverage 100% (INV-STR-008); accessibility audit zero serious; playtest records for changed onboarding or new screen families; canary shadow verifier for `tm_canary_ticks` (720) with zero unintended divergence (T20B-17); rollback artefact present.
6. G5 (7 real days before a chapter boundary): G5 bands, population experiments, a diff run per parameter change in the boundary bundle (old vs new params, same seeds, no band leaves its range), the integrity report chapter summary (T20B-18).
7. Waivers via `requestWaiver` only at G3/G4 with two signers (TM_LEAD + module owner) and a 24 decision; never for ACCOUNTING, FATAL invariant tests, equivalence or INV-STR; a waiver lasts until the next G3.
8. Hotfix (INC-1/INC-2): G0 + the incident's case + version replay of the last game day + touched module bands at 20 seeds + canary of `tm_hotfix_canary_ticks` (60); the full G3 and G4 follow within 48 real hours or the next normal release is blocked.
9. Stage gates (Rule 3.9.5): S0 needs the invariant framework and INV-LED/INV-ENG; S2 adds INV-MKT and market bands; S4 adds INV-CCP/BNK/FND/INS and hunts H09-H13, H30-H32; S5 adds INV-CON/LAW and conflict bands; S7 adds INV-SOC/PRG and hunts H01-H04; S9 adds INV-STR-002..004 and FAU.*; S10 is the full G4.
10. 20 §9 counters as checks: E1 WITHHELD judging (G3 refuses a run where per-seed WITHHELD rows are visible); E2 A47 re-runs present; E3 agent permission test (T20B-02) in G2; E4 sampling forbidden (any invariant runner configured with sampling fails G0); E5 equivalence at 1/4/16 plus random map; E6 evidence grouping with `unimplementedDependencies`; E7 band definitions hashed and compared with the 24-approved registry hash; E8-E13 are covered by T20B-18, T02-16, T20B-20, T20B-18 (verify parity), T20B-02 (mirror) and T20B-05 respectively and appear as named checks.
11. tm events: every run writes the internal events to `tm_events` through `TmEventSink`; dashboard read models are registered with 21's console as data sources.

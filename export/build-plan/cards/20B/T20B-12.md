## Implementation steps
1. Wealth metrics use engaged HONEST agents (Rule 3.7.2) and account age in real days; ranges are target × [0.5, 2.0] (× [0.33, 3.0] for top-0.01% at STANDARD with ≥ 10k agents); growth_decel compares median daily growth on day 1 (≥ 40%) and in week 8 (≤ 8%).
2. whale_riskfree: any agent above $100B NW with > 2%/day for ≥ 7 game days and max drawdown < 5% counts as an occurrence (DOMINANCE, 0 allowed, treated as an exploit: opens a Finding through T20B-10).
3. Stabilizer bands: error |M/M_target − 1| at day close, P95 over a chapter ≤ 0.10; lever per-day change ≤ its 23 step clamp (ACCOUNTING); net_mint_share median 0-0.5%; legal_sink_share 0.5-1.5% per game year; nwi_consistency 0 mismatches (board order equals NW ÷ money-supply index); reward_faucet $0 (INV-SOC-001 cross-check).
4. Macro: inflation mean per bloc 1.0-4.0% (SRL 2.0-8.0%); recessions = 2 consecutive negative quarters; major crisis share of chapters 0.25-0.45 at 200 seeds; crisis_telegraph: every major has ≥ 8 real hours (5,760 ticks) of visible precursor events before start; no_major_final_48h: no major starting in the last 48 real hours (34,560 ticks) of the chapter; sovereign default 0.02-0.10; policy rates inside each bank's corridor and above the floor (ACCOUNTING).
5. Finance: NPC bank failures 0-3 per chapter; runs reaching the facility 0.1-1.5; CCP mutualized layer touched in ≤ 1 of 3 chapters; procyclical margin drawdown deepening 5-40% vs a fixed-margin counterfactual (paired runs with `SC_CRASH_FIXED_MARGIN`); credit cycle correlation 0.3-0.8; player bank ROE 4-18%; fund_manager_extraction $0 beyond fees (ACCOUNTING); tranche loss ratio 1.5-6 in the correlation shock; insurer loss ratio 60-80%; ETF discount 1-10% in the liquidity scenario.

## Goal
Implement the CONFLICT family (A20 opportunist, A21 serial pressure, A22 alliance raider, A23 litigator, A25 market-conduct probe) using only the abstract game verbs of 12 and 13 (plan, launch, cancel an op; file, sell, buy a case; market-conduct campaigns from 12's verb list), and the ADAPTIVE family (A46 NPC exploiter, A47 generic adaptive) with the cross-entropy learner of 20 Rule 3.5.5. Adaptive agents are what lets G3 show that frozen bots do not hide a broken market (every DOMINANCE and CALIBRATION band re-run with 10% A47, wired in T20B-21).

## Implementation steps
1. A20 Opportunist: runs 12's ops when the EV the game shows is positive given its risk appetite 0.2-0.9; launders per 12 rules. A21 Serial pressure: targets one victim repeatedly within the rules' limits (target selection rule parameter). A22 Alliance raider: in an alliance of 3-20, coordinates joint ops through alliance intents. A23 Litigator: files cases above a filing threshold, including weak ones, and sells and buys cases. A25 Market-conduct probe: squeezes, false stories, short campaigns and ramping with player-available verbs only, sized by campaign size.
2. CEM learner: every `tm_adapt_epoch_days` (21) game days sample M = `tm_adapt_population` (32) parameter vectors; each drives one sub-account (a separate character created through the normal flow); reward = ln(NW_end/NW_start) − λ·maxDrawdown with λ = `tm_adapt_dd_penalty` (0.5) using only the sub-account's own observations; update with the top ⌈0.2·M⌉. Hunts replace the reward with the hunt oracle score.
3. A46 NPC exploiter: learns the response of named NPC actors and NPC liquidity from public observations (fills, quotes, actor announcements) and trades against it; θ parametrizes the response model and the trading rule.
4. A47 Generic adaptive: θ weights the templates of `intentSpace` and their payload builders; each tick samples intents by weight within the rate limit.

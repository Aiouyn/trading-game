## Implementation steps
1. Factorial: 54 cells (3 × 2 × 3 × 3), 20 seeds each, STANDARD preset, 1 chapter; wealth "log-normal around $5,000 (σ = 1) for the 20% who join with a fresh-start history" draws from `tm:agentparams`; reports are E4 evidence, gating only through bands; failing cells open Findings against the owning module.
2. Adaptation: (a) A03 capital deployed into one public signal doubles every game quarter from $250k to $256M (11 stages), measuring beat share and execution cost per stage (expected monotone decay; MKT.crowd_decay at the $25M stage); (b) add 10 A47 every 21 game days to a world with a successful A03 and report the incumbent's edge half-life; (c) force a monetary regime shift at day 30 and report recovery time adaptive vs frozen.
3. GEV: from 200 NPC-plus-baseline seeds × 5 game years take quarterly block maxima of (a) worst one-day index loss, (b) intra-quarter peak-to-trough drawdown, (c) NPC banking quarterly loan-loss rate, (d) NPC insurance quarterly catastrophe loss ratio; fit by maximum likelihood (harness numeric code, plain JS Math is allowed here because it is not engine code); register TAIL bands with ξ ranges (0.05-0.40, 0.00-0.35, 0.05-0.50, 0.20-0.70) and 1-in-10-year return-level ranges (7-15%, 30-55%, 3-10% of loans, 120-300% of quarterly premium), class CALIBRATION, gate G3.
4. Crisis scenarios are harness Scenarios with scheduled shocks; they are also exported for 15's SCENARIO challenges and 18's practice worlds; no code path schedules them in the live world (the scenario loader refuses a production world id).
5. Agent intelligence: a cell of 20 seeds with model-driven actors replaced by rule-based actors must show no band regressions and per-tick cost within 16's budget before any live use.
6. Prose independence: run a 30-game-day world twice, once with the generated-prose provider and once with template text; compare logicalStateHash at every tick.

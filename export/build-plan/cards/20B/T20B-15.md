## Implementation steps
1. Bust farming: FAU.bust_farm_mean_ratio = mean NW(A30) / mean NW(A01 solvent) at 8 and 24 weeks ≤ 1.00 (200 seeds, DOMINANCE); bust_option_value = mean NW(A30) − mean NW(yolo without fresh start) ≤ $5,000 per 8-week window (ACCOUNTING); fresh_start_per_window ≤ 1 per 8 weeks; rank_from_fresh_cash = 0 (counterfactual run without the FRESH_START lot).
2. Ads: every grant exactly $250 × MSI at the grant tick and tagged AD_GRANT; ≤ 10 grants per character per local day including timezone changes, device clock changes, reconnects and multiple devices; zero grants on a local day whose start-of-day rank-basis NW was ≥ $1,000 real, or to accounts younger than 24 h, guests, Held or suspended characters; ad_gate_on_networth: $900 cash + $5,000 positions at local midnight -> ineligible all day; restricted_use: zero AD_GRANT or FRESH_START lot spent on ops, insider actions, margin, derivatives or P2P inside the window; ad_faucet_share alert above 0.0001 of daily net mint (INFO); ad_bust_stack: fresh start offered before ads, each at most once per bust episode; ad_fixed_reward: no random amount, multiplier, spin or chest in ad or purchase paths (G0, cross-checks INV-STR-010).
3. Local days are computed from each character's timezone history with TZ_FIXTURES transitions; 23- and 25-hour local days are included.

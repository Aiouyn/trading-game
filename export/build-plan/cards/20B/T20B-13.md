## Implementation steps
1. Business and property: BIZ.t1_payback median 4-9 game days net of all opex at reference crowding (09 owns the base of 6 days); hustler_time 1-2 real days to R1 Hustler ($20k) for a median engaged newcomer; upgrade_saturation ≤ 0.35 marginal income ratio top tier vs first; npc_entry correlation ≥ 0.3 with sector margin; antitrust: share > 60% sustained > 1 game quarter without an antitrust event = 0 (ACCOUNTING); PROP.gross_yield ranges per tier (working 6-9%, mid 5-7%, prime 3.5-5.5%, trophy 1-3.5%); cap_rate_rate_beta +30 to +90 bp per 100 bp.
2. AI: LAB.training_roi per run −20% to +60% with ≥ 15% negative (INTENDED_RISK); compute_share 10-20%; ai_vs_manual 0.8-1.6 at equal capital; du_cap ≤ 3.0M per partition per tick and ≤ 2,000 per player (PERFORMANCE); tournament_overfit ≤ 0.8 median secret/practice score for A31.
3. Conflict (POP_CONFLICT): crime_ev_ratio 0.8-1.2 and crime_variance 2-4× (INTENDED_RISK, 20 seeds); breach rates vs top-1% (solo 1-8%, coordinated 35-60%); conviction 40-65%; never attributed 30-50%; wrongful conviction < 3% at 200 seeds (ACCOUNTING); hit ≥ 3 times per real day < 1% of active; rookies attacked in first 7 days < 5%; top-1% falling ≥ 20% by non-hacking takedown routes 5-15%; bankruptcies by crime or court below the floor = 0 (ACCOUNTING); Most Wanted top-10 conviction ≥ 50%; session lifetime 6-18 victim-online hours; P at disconnect 0.15-0.35; sessions reaching P ≥ 0.6 < 10%; legitimate-entry disconnects < 15%; overnight loss ≥ 0.08 P = 0% (ACCOUNTING); club edge EV after dues ≤ 0; player-caused systemic triggers ≤ 0.5 per chapter.
4. Law: LAW.held_max ≤ 24 real hours online and never in sandbox or practice worlds (ACCOUNTING; the sandbox half is also asserted in T20B-20); restitution collected within 2 chapters ≥ 70%.

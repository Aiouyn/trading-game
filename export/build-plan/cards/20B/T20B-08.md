## Implementation steps
1. A33 Mark pumper: splits a buy of fixed notional into K clips, K ∈ {1, 30, 720}, to lift its own mark and peak (Tape §6); records its peak and the clip schedule for the oracle.
2. A34 Ad timer: changes its client timezone (using TZ_FIXTURES, including 23- and 25-hour local days), device clock, reconnects and devices, and moves NW around local midnight to try for more than 10 ads a day or ads while ineligible.
3. A35 Restricted-cash probe: inside the restricted window, tries every forbidden use of `AD_GRANT` and `FRESH_START` cash: ops, insider actions, margin, derivatives, P2P.
4. A36 Protection probe: tries to target protected, Rookie-zone, dormant and offline players with every hostile verb available.
5. A37 Collateral juggler: pledges the same asset across loan, repo, margin and contract escrow in one tick and across ticks.
6. A38 Appraisal inflater: related-party sales of property and collectibles at inflated prices, then borrows against them.
7. A39 Consolidation gamer: holding structures with intercompany loans, cross-holdings and circular ownership to inflate group NW.
8. A40 Insurance stager: insures, then arranges losses through linked accounts or its own ops.
9. A41 Rival front-runner: computes NPC rival funds' next picks from public data and trades ahead (K names, N ticks).
10. A42 Event timer: crash buyer, flash-dip and bubble-timer strategies around world-event phases (hold horizon).
11. A43 Rumor follower: trades every rumor blind, and separately only tracked sources above a threshold.
12. A44 Concession squatter: tries to keep a unique concession or bottleneck forever (re-bids, blocks entrants with legal verbs).
13. A45 Stabilizer gamer: times activity around stabilizer lever changes and NWI rebases published at day close.

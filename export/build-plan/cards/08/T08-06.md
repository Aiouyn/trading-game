## Goal
Pay what 08 §3.1.7 says, when it says: management fees and custody accrued daily by the strike (T08-03) are paid at each game-month end; performance fees crystallize at each game-quarter end (or year end for ANNUAL) and on redemption, are paid in cash to the manager by cancelling the investor's units so that `G` is unchanged, and paying series are re-based and merged. There are no per-trade manager fees (08 §9 row 6).

## Implementation steps
1. `feeRecipient`: the manager entity when it is a `chr` or `cmp` whose root is the sponsor's root (the manager is the sponsor or one of its companies); for a hired NPC manager or an AI model operator, the sponsor (who pays the NPC salary under 09 and owns the model). NPC funds pay their NPC sponsor.
2. `payMonthlyFees` (P8 at t = 719 when `cal.boundaries` has `MONTH_CLOSE`, after the strike and dealing of the day): `m = fundState.accruedMgmt`; `npc = npcShare(fundId)`; `mNpc = allocate(m, [{id:'NPC', w: round(npc×1e9)}, {id:'PLAYER', w: 1e9 − that}])` (largest remainder, parts sum exactly); post `ctx.ledger.pay(fundId, recipient, mPlayer, ccy, FLOW.MGMT_FEE, { sub: 'PLAYER_SHARE' })` and `…(mNpc, … { sub: 'NPC_SHARE' })` (zero parts skipped). Custody: `ctx.ledger.burn(fundId, accruedCustody, ccy, FLOW.CUSTODY_FEE)` (SINK, to the CCP custodian per 07 §3.20). If the fund's available cash is short, pay what is available and keep the rest accrued (it remains a NAV liability); never overdraw. Reset the paid accruals.
3. Class note (binding, see T08-02 step 3): the "NPC share" fee is the same 03 TRANSFER code with `sub: 'NPC_SHARE'`; the FAUCET side of NPC capital is the elastic subscription. 08#15 asserts this split (T08-07).
4. `crystallizeSeries`: for each series of the fund (id order) with `a_s = perfAccrualPerUnit(G, H_s, hurdle_s, perfFeeRate, hurdleType) > 0`, for each position in it (id order): `fee = a_s × units/1e6`; `cancel = toQty(fee / G, UNIT_SCALE)`; core holder: `ctx.ledger.moveQty(investor, fundId, fundId, cancel, toMoney(fee), ccy, FLOW.UNITS_CANCEL)`; elastic holder: no ledger qty (08 holds the unit record). Then `ctx.ledger.pay(fundId, recipient, toMoney(fee), ccy, FLOW.PERF_FEE, { sub: holderIsElastic ? 'NPC_SHARE' : 'PLAYER_SHARE', refs: [positionId] })`; `position.units −= cancel`, `series.units −= cancel`, `Fund.unitsOutstanding −= cancel` (manager-stake positions pay no performance fee to themselves: skip positions with `isManagerStake`). After all positions: `H_s = G`, `crystallizedTick = tick`. Return total fee.
5. Why `G` is unchanged: the fund pays `fee` cash and cancels `fee/G` units, so `(nav − fee)/(units − fee/G) = G`. Rounding: `toQty` half-even on units; the residual (< 1e-6 unit × G) is accepted and covered by INV-FND-001's one-micro tolerance per unit.
6. `crystallizeOnRedemption(pos, unitsRedeemed, Gdeal)` (08 §3.1.6 step 3, §3.1.7): `a = perfAccrualPerUnit(Gdeal, H_s, …)`; `fee = a × unitsRedeemed/1e6`, paid in cash `fund → recipient` with `FLOW.PERF_FEE`; the investor receives `Gdeal − a` per unit (T08-07 posts that), so no unit cancellation is needed for redeemed units; returns `{ fee, unitsCancelled: 0n }`.
7. `mergeSeries`: after crystallization, the lead series is `leadSeriesOf(series, G)`; every other series with `H_s ≥ lead.H_s` (within 1e-9) is merged: its positions move to the lead series id (position rows of the same investor are summed: `units`, `costBasis`; `lockupUntilTick` = max; `subscribedTick` = min), the series row is deleted, the lead series units increase.
8. `quarterlyCrystallization`: if `fees.crystallization === 'QUARTERLY'` and `QUARTER_CLOSE ∈ cal.boundaries`, or `'ANNUAL'` and `YEAR_CLOSE`, run `crystallizeSeries` with today's strike `G`, then `mergeSeries`. Side-pocket units never crystallize here (fees on side pockets only on realization, T08-09). Convicted manager (edge 6): when `law.convictedManagers` lists the manager, accrued uncrystallized performance fees are forfeited: skip crystallization from that tick on (the fund winds down per T08-09).
9. No fee is ever taken per trade; `fund.trade` has no fee hook (exploit 6).

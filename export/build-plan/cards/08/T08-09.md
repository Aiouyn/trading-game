## Implementation steps
1. `setStatus` emits by target status: FROZEN → `fund.frozen`, GATED → `fund.gated`, SUSPENDED → `fund.suspended`, WINDING_DOWN → `fund.windingDown`, CLOSED → `fund.closed`, back to ACTIVE → `fund.unfrozen`; all PUBLIC `{ fundId, reason, until? }`; each also appended to `fundState.incidents` and `trackRecord.publicIncidents` when the reason is BREAKER, DRIFT, PAYABLE, REGULATOR or an abrupt closure.
2. Effects (binding): FROZEN: manager orders rejected by T08-08's check, engine liquidation continues, redemptions gate-free (`gateFreeUntilTick` = end of freeze or +∞ while open-ended), subscriptions rejected at strike (not ACTIVE). GATED: subscriptions rejected at strike; redemptions follow the gate; ends (→ ACTIVE) when `redemptionPayable = 0`. SUSPENDED: no dealing (T08-07 rolls requests). WINDING_DOWN: no subscriptions, all redemptions gate-free.
3. Drawdown breaker (§3.1.9 row 3, 08#12): after each strike, `breakerFires(history, day, PARAM.fund_breaker_dd)` with the lead-series net NAV series: fires when `net_d ≤ (1 − 0.30) × max(net_{d−6..d})` (the HWM inside any 7-game-day window). Effect: FROZEN for `PARAM.fund_breaker_freeze_days` game day with gate-free redemptions that day; `lastBreakerTick = tick`; also emits `fund.breaker` PUBLIC. Timer end → ACTIVE.
4. Absent manager (rows 1–2): `last = social.lastLoginTick(principal) ?? lastActiveTick(principal)` of the human principal of the manager (for an AI/NPC operator: the sponsor's root character). `tick − last ≥ PARAM.fund_absent_freeze_days × 720` → FROZEN (`ABSENT`), redemptions gate-free; on return (login tick > freeze tick) → ACTIVE at the next tick. `≥ PARAM.fund_absent_winddown_days × 720` → `startWindDown(..., 'ABSENT')`.
5. Level 3 (08 §3.1.3 rules, §3.1.9 row 4): `level3Pct > PARAM.fund_max_level3_pct` (open-ended) → `level3SubsPaused = true` until back below; `> PARAM.fund_level3_suspend_pct` → `sidePocketDueTick = tick + 3 × 720`; at the due tick without a side pocket bringing it ≤ 0.25 → FROZEN (`LEVEL3`) until one is created.
6. Halts (row 5): Σ value of holdings whose `tradingStateOf` is HALTED > 50% of NAV → SUSPENDED (`HALT`); ends when the halt share ≤ 50% or after 3 game days (`haltSuspendSinceTick`), after which dealing resumes and halted holdings use Level-2 marks (`markPrice`) via `classifyLevel` (no trade today).
7. Regulator orders (row 6): `law.regulatorOrders(store, tick)` rows targeting a fund: SUSPEND → SUSPENDED, FREEZE → FROZEN, LIFT → ACTIVE (`flags.regulatorHold` set/cleared).
8. Side pockets (`fund.sidePocket` P1: actor operator; each asset is Level 3 for the fund (`FUND_NOT_LEVEL3`); Σ side pockets after ≤ 25% of NAV (`FUND_SIDE_POCKET_CAP`)). `createSidePocket`: `SidePocket { id: ctx.newId('spk'), fundId, assetIds, createdTick, units: investor → fund units held at this tick (copy of every position, including elastic holders), valuation }`; the assets stay on the fund's ledger but are excluded from the main NAV (T08-03). New investors never participate. Side-pocket units cannot be redeemed. `realizeSidePocket` when a side-pocket asset is sold (fill or 09/10 sale proceeds credited to the fund): proceeds are paid pro rata to SP unit holders (`FLOW.DISTRIBUTION`, `sub: 'SIDE_POCKET'`; elastic holders `FLOW.DISTRIBUTION_ELASTIC`); the performance fee on the realized gain over the SP's creation valuation is charged then (rate as the fund, paid to the fee recipient with `FLOW.PERF_FEE`, `sub: 'SIDE_POCKET'`).
9. Wind-down (`fund.close` by an operator, or triggers: ABSENT, BAN, ZERO_UNITS (edge 1), NEGATIVE_EQUITY (T08-07 step 9), ETF termination (T08-16), closed-end term end (T08-12)): status WINDING_DOWN, notice to investors (`fund.windingDown` PUBLIC), `cancelAllRequests` for subscriptions, `gateFreeUntilTick = +∞`. Engine sells all Level-1 positions over at most `PARAM.fund_winddown_days` game days: per asset per day `min(remaining / daysLeft, PARAM.fund_liquidation_adv_cap × ADV)` through `planLiquidation(..., 'WINDDOWN')` (if the ADV cap leaves stock after 5 days, selling continues at the cap; documented); derivatives are closed with offsetting engine orders; PB debt repaid from cash (`T07A-?? pbRepay`, through T08-07's import). Level-3 assets: create a forced side pocket (`createSidePocket(..., true)`), then list private stakes (`companies.listPrivateStake`) and properties (`listPropertyForSale`). When Level-1 and derivative positions are flat and PB debt is zero: final distribution of cash pro rata to units at the final NAV (`FLOW.DISTRIBUTION`; elastic `FLOW.DISTRIBUTION_ELASTIC`), pay final fees (`payMonthlyFees`), cancel all units (`Fund.unitsOutstanding = 0`), status CLOSED, `fund.closed`. Side pockets keep distributing as their assets sell. The track record stays public forever. Abrupt closure (manager `fund.close`) within 91 game days after `lastFeeIncreaseTick` or after any breaker (`lastBreakerTick` set) adds an incident to the fund page.
10. Edge 1: `unitsOutstanding = 0` on an ACTIVE fund at the strike → `startWindDown('ZERO_UNITS')`; it closes when positions are flat. Edge 6 / §3.1.12 ban: `law.convictedManagers` listing the manager → accrued uncrystallized performance fees forfeited (T08-06 skips them; the accrual is simply never paid), `flags.banEndsTick` set, every fund of that manager `startWindDown('BAN')`. Edge 7 is valuation (T08-03); the forced side pocket is step 5.
11. `applyRestitution` (§3.1.10): manager-stake units worth `amount` at the last NAV are cancelled first (`ctx.ledger.moveQty(manager, fundId, fundId, units, value, ccy, FLOW.FIRST_LOSS)`; `managerStakeUnits` and positions reduced, units outstanding reduced, so the value accrues to the other investors pro rata); the uncovered remainder is returned to 13 (`fund.incident` PUBLIC with `{ kind: 'RESTITUTION', covered, remainder }`).

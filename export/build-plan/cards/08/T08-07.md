## Goal
Investors leave open-ended funds at a price fixed at the strike (08 §3.1.6, ruling D144): the gate fills a crowded day pro rata and carries the rest forward, swing pricing charges the cost of large flows to those who cause them, early exits pay a fee into the fund, and cash shortfalls become a payable that the engine settles by selling Level-1 positions through normal 05 orders, which is the real first-mover incentive that makes fund runs emergent. This task also owns the daily dealing orchestrator `dealDay` that every fund runs at the strike (strike → gate → swing → redemptions → subscriptions).

## Implementation steps
1. `fund.redeem` P1: Held does not block redemptions of a bankrupt estate (14 submits as SYSTEM channel); otherwise a Held actor is rejected (`FUND_HELD`). Exactly one of `units`/`amount`. Fund OPEN_ENDED and not CLOSED (`FUND_NOT_OPEN_ENDED`); dealing not SUSPENDED (requests may be queued while SUSPENDED: they wait, see step 3). `pickRedeemablePositions`: positions of the investor in this fund ordered by `(subscribedTick, seriesId)`, excluding units under lockup (`lockupUntilTick > tick`); insufficient unlocked units → `FUND_LOCKUP`; no units → `FUND_NO_UNITS`. Manager-stake units: the post-redemption stake value must stay ≥ max(`PARAM.fund_manager_stake_min_abs`, `PARAM.fund_manager_stake_min_pct × AUM`) unless the fund is WINDING_DOWN (`FUND_STAKE_FLOOR`, edge 2). In-kind only when the investor holds ≥ 10% of units outstanding (`FUND_IN_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`). Core holders: `ctx.holds.placeQty(investor, fundId, units, dealingTick + 2, intentId)`. `amount` requests are converted to units at the strike (`unitsForAmount` with the series net NAV at `G_deal`), capped at the investor's unlocked units. Create `FundRequest` kind `REDEEM` with `dealingTick = dealingTickFor(tick, cutoff)`.
2. `dealDay(ctx, fundId)` at t = 719, P8, for every non-CLOSED fund in id order (ETF and MMF funds use their own dealing in T08-14 and T08-13; this function returns early for them): (a) `strike = strikeNav(ctx, fundId)`; if null and the fund is FUNDRAISING → `dealSubscriptions(ctx, fundId, PARAM.fund_initial_nav, 0)` and stop. (b) If status SUSPENDED: move every PENDING request's `dealingTick` to the next day and stop. (c) Redemption requests dealing today, including carried-forward remainders, sorted by `(submittedTick, reqId)`; value each at `G` (`units × net_s`). (d) `f = gateFraction(Σ value, nav, gate.maxRedeemPctNavPerDay, gate.enabled, isGateFree)`: `f = min(1, pct × nav / Σvalue)` when enabled and not gate-free. Every request fills `floor_to_1e-6(units × f)`; the remainder stays PENDING with `dealingTick` = next day and `carriedForward` = remainder units value; next day it is pro rata again with new requests (no priority). Emit `fund.gated` PUBLIC `{ fundId, reason: 'GATE', until }` when f < 1. (e) Swing: `netFlow = Σ pending subscription cash − Σ filled redemption value`; `s = swingFactor(netFlow, nav, estimatedLiquidationCost(strike.valuation.lines, |netFlow|/nav, cost), PARAM.fund_swing_threshold, PARAM.fund_swing_cap)`; `G_deal = G × (1 + s)`. (f) Redemptions pay at `G_deal` (`payRedemption`). (g) `aumAfter = nav − Σ redemption value at G_deal`; `dealSubscriptions(ctx, fundId, G_deal, aumAfter)`. (h) `checkPayableAge`.
3. `isGateFree`: true while `tick ≤ fundState.gateFreeUntilTick` (set by mandate/fee notice windows, breaker days, absent-manager freeze and wind-down notices; T08-08, T08-09).
4. `payRedemption` per request: for each picked position: `a = perfAccrualPerUnit(G_deal, H_s, hurdle_s, …)`; `payPerUnit = G_deal − a`; `gross = units × payPerUnit`; `crystallizeOnRedemption` pays `a × units` to the manager (08 §3.1.6 step 3); `fee = earlyExitFee(...)` = `earlyExitFeeRate × gross` when `tick − pos.subscribedTick < PARAM.fund_early_exit_days × 720` and not in a mandate/fee-notice window, else 0. Cash available = fund cash + `pbUndrawn` (draw from the PB only when cash is short: `pbDraw` for the gap). Core holder: `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(fundId, investor, fundId, units, toMoney(min(gross, available)), ccy, FLOW.REDEEM_SWAP, { holdId })` (the fund buys back and cancels its units), plus `ctx.ledger.pay(investor, fundId, toMoney(fee), ccy, FLOW.ANTI_DILUTION)` when fee > 0. Elastic hh holder: `ctx.ledger.elasticOut(fundId, hhId, toMoney(gross − fee), ccy, FLOW.REDEEM_ELASTIC)`. The units are cancelled at the strike even when cash is short: `Fund.unitsOutstanding −= units`, positions and series reduced. Shortfall `owed = gross − fee − paid` is added to `fundState.redemptionPayable` and recorded on the request (`owed` field: an addition to `FundRequest` like `holdId`), status PARTIAL, `payableSinceTick = tick` if unset; then `planLiquidation(ctx, fundId, owed, 'REDEMPTION_LIQUIDATION')`. Emit `fund.redemptionFilled` (PARTIES) `{ reqId, units, cash, gatedRemainder }`.
5. In-kind (step 6 of §3.1.6): the slice is `units / unitsOutstanding` of every Level-1 position, rounded down to lots (`getMarketSpec(...).lotSize`), delivered with `ctx.ledger.moveQty(fundId, investor, assetId, qty, bookValue, ccy, FLOW.IN_KIND)`; the rest of the redemption value is paid in cash per step 4.
6. `planLiquidation` (P8, consumed by 05 from the next tick through `FundsPort.forcedSales`): Level-1 long positions pro rata by value; for each asset `dailyCap = PARAM.fund_liquidation_adv_cap × advUnits`; per game hour the order quantity is `dailyCap × 30 / sessionTicks` (480 for exchange sessions, 720 for 24h products), rounded down to lots, until the asset's pro-rata share of the shortfall at the last price is covered; one `EngineOrder` row per (asset, game hour) with `pool 'LIQUIDATION'`, `accountId = fundId`, `tif 'TICK'`, `side 'SELL'`, `purpose`. Orders carry no limit (05 applies its market-order protection). Fills credit the fund through 05's normal settlement.
7. `settlePayables` every tick in P8: while the fund's available cash > 0 and owed requests exist, pay the oldest (`submittedTick`, `reqId`) first. The units were already bought back at the strike, so the deferred cash leg is a plain payment of an amount owed to a former holder: `ctx.ledger.pay(fundId, investor, amt, ccy, FLOW.DISTRIBUTION, { sub: 'REDEMPTION_PAYABLE', refs: [reqId] })` (03 `FND_DISTRIBUTION`, TRANSFER PAY; elastic holders: `elasticOut(..., FLOW.REDEEM_ELASTIC)`). Reduce `owed` and `redemptionPayable`; when a request's owed reaches 0 its status becomes FILLED. When the payable is fully paid, cancel the fund's remaining liquidation orders for that purpose.
8. `checkPayableAge`: if `redemptionPayable > 0` and `tick − payableSinceTick > PARAM.fund_redemption_max_delay_days × 720`: status `GATED`, emit `fund.gated` with reason `PAYABLE`; T08-09 applies the GATED effects. The amount owed was fixed at the strike: later price moves while selling are borne by remaining investors (no re-pricing).
9. Limited liability (§3.1.22): if `navBeforeFees ≤ 0` at the strike, `G` is 0, redemptions pay 0, the fund is put into WINDING_DOWN by T08-09 and any PB deficit is 07's default-waterfall loss: no posting ever debits an investor or the manager beyond their units.
10. Edge 28 (fund of funds redeeming from a gated underlying): the FoF's own payable simply waits for cash from the underlying (no special case); it may gate itself through step 8.

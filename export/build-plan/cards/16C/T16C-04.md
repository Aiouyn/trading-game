## Goal
Secrets are true, discoverable by at least one legal path, and never made from a player's private state.

## Imports
- T16B-06 dynasty structures (ownership, family, political ties), T16B-04 trust (CONVERSATION), T16A-09 Research Desk (RESEARCH_MAP); 09 registry, 15 filings, 12 dossiers and espionage, 13 leads (ports); 22 authored secrets (port).

## Implementation steps
1. Fact generator reads only engine state that is public, owned by NPCs, or legally filed; a dependency test forbids player private tables.
2. Path defaults per §3.11.2 table (ARCHIVE_STUDY $5,000 p 0.6, CONVERSATION p = 0.3 + 0.5·trust/100 − 0.3·integrity, …); ESPIONAGE paths refused here (12's intents).
3. Paid paths post `SECRET_PATH_FEE`, which resolves to `WLD_ARCHIVE_PURCHASE` (E-33).
4. Edge §8.10: a fact made public mid-investigation ends it at no further cost, recorded as PUBLIC_MOSAIC. Edge §8.19: anonymized character's records are deleted.
5. Counterplay: CONVERSATION reveals interest with p 0.2; holders may self-disclose, restructure (09/15), deny ambiguous facts, or sue for NON_PUBLIC acquisition (13).
6. Evidence pricing (E-35 §3, PROVISIONAL [Quant]): a REPORTed fact adds its path's expected cost per success to the 13 investigation's cumulative spend S on E-24's curve P(S) = min(1, 0.25·√(S/$50k)) (archive +$8.3k ≈ 10 points; research map 3 layers +$94k ≈ 34 points; free paths add $0 and only open the lead).
7. Dynasty discovery (§3.4.4) paths come from this card and switch on 16#15 for T16B-06.

What a player can lawfully do with what they know, and the counters to abuse.

## Implementation steps
1. Sell: only non-material or PUBLIC_MOSAIC facts, not already public (edge §8.11 `ALREADY_PUBLIC`); one sale per fact world-wide; seller rank ≥ `fact_sale_min_rank` = R2; base `fact_sale_base_real` = $5k; cap `fact_sale_cap_real_per_chapter` = $100k real per character per chapter, linked accounts pooled (E-35 §2, PROVISIONAL [Quant]); posts `FACT_SALE_NPC` as a TRANSFER from the NPC firm sector (E-33). Per the E-35 amendment (PROVISIONAL [Quant]), the drained npcFirm cash is refilled at the next day close by `ROW_NPC_INCOME` (FAUCET into frm) through 04's nominal-GDP closure, so the sale is new money one day close later, bounded at $100k real per R2+ character per chapter.
2. Share: recipients copy legal basis; NON_PUBLIC material shares logged for 13 as possible tipping.
3. Report: 13 lead, Rep +3 (14), award if sanctioned. Publish: public via a 15 statement; holders' trust −30 toward the publisher.
4. Whistle: a disgruntled NPC (integrity ≥ 0.7, trust ≤ −50 with employer) contacts a player with Rep ≥ 60.

## Test plan
  - **16#50** `soc.world.fact.sell` for a material NON_PUBLIC fact is refused; total FACT_SALE_NPC per character per chapter never exceeds $100k real (E-35, PROVISIONAL [Quant]).
  - **16#52** a REPORT that leads to a 13 sanction grants Rep +3 and the 13 award.
  - **AC-16C-05-CLOSED** (E-33) a fact sale posts `FACT_SALE_NPC` as a TRANSFER: legA debits the NPC firm sector account (5000), legB credits the seller's revenue (4000); no FAUCET or MINT entry is created. Test: sell a $5k (× MSI) fact; the frm sector balance falls by exactly the amount the player gains, total money (player + NPC sectors) is unchanged, and an RNG/ledger scan finds zero FAUCET-class entries for the tick.
  - **AC-16C-05-ONCE** (E-35) a second `soc.world.fact.sell` of a fact that is already public is rejected `ALREADY_PUBLIC`; each fact sells once world-wide.
  - **AC-16C-05-RANK** (E-35, PROVISIONAL [Quant]) a seller below R2 is rejected.
  - **AC-16C-05-NOLOOP** (E-35 exploit, PROVISIONAL [Quant]) over every discovery path × outlet audience × sensitivity grid, a scripted acquire→sell cycle repeated for a chapter nets the player ≤ $100k real (the cap) after path costs and Desk fees; with the cap removed, the test reports the per-cycle margin (expected archive ≈ +$19k) so any retune that makes the loop unbounded fails. Money stays closed (AC-16C-05-CLOSED).
  - **AC-16C-05-REFILL** (E-35 amendment, PROVISIONAL [Quant]) over a seeded chapter with N R2+ sellers at the cap, the `ROW_NPC_INCOME` refill attributable to `FACT_SALE_NPC` drains is ≤ $100k real × N per chapter and lands on the day close after each sale; it is booked to npcFirm F_p, never as a human FAUCET.
  - **AC-16C-05-X5** a create-sell-recreate loop sells each fact once; linked accounts share the cap (§9 X5, X6).

## Goal
Technology advances from real R&D spend, and eras change the world on a slow, predictable cadence.

## Imports
- T16A-03 `rndGameDay`; 09/11 player R&D (ports); 11 AIRaceIndex (port); T16A-06 for diffusion effects and C13; 21 LiveOps flag (port).

## Exports

## Implementation steps
1. `baselineRnd` fixed at world creation; real dollars = nominal / MSI.
2. Effects ramp linearly over `diffusionGameDays` through owner hooks (06, 09, 10, 11, 05) and update `automationFactor` for T16A-03; levels > 5 are incremental at 0.5× level-5 effects.
3. Eras: change at the day close after crossing, ≤ 1 per 2 chapters, never in a finish window (edge §8.15 → chapter boundary); shift consumption shares (T16A-04 hook), unlock 22 content, move voterMedian.tech +0.05 (T16B-08), write a PERMANENT history entry and an ERA news item.
4. Edge §8.14: one breakthrough per domain per 14 real days; progress caps at 1.99.
5. Pace (E-36, PROVISIONAL [Quant]): `tech_base_cost_chapters` = 1.3 (was 1.6), `era_thresholds` = [0, 8, 18, 30, 40] (was 45). E-35 §4 era timings (1.6 / 4.3 / 8 / 15 at base 1.6) are SUPERSEDED by E-36.
6. Era foreshadowing (E-36, PROVISIONAL [Quant]): at each day close project eraScore 7 GD ahead (level + 1 where progress_d + 7·rnd_d/cost_d ≥ 1); the era day T is the first allowed crossing after `era_min_chapters` and the finish-window rule. When the projection first shows T within [5, 9] GD, schedule on the `world.tech` seeded stream, each item CausalLinked to the gating breakthrough (N1/N2): `tech.patent_filing` 2–3 items at T − U(7, 9) GD, nw 0.053–0.075 (candidate); `tech.late_stage_trial` 1 item at T − U(3, 7) GD, nw 0.24–0.40 (candidate); `world.era.imminent` 1 item at T − U(1, 2) GD, nw 0.5, alwaysPublish; the ERA item gets a ×1.5 telegraph boost. If T moves, unpublished items are re-anchored to the new T; if T slips by more than 14 GD after a precursor has published, a `tech.era.pushed_back` follow-up item fires. No item states an era date. New NewsKind rows go to 22; T16A-08 ranks them.

## Implementation steps
1. Product gates re-checked every game hour; falling below 80% of the gate pauses the deployment.
2. Kill switch is never rate-limited and runs in P1 of the submission tick.
3. Staged capital: STAGE_10 → 25 → 50 → FULL; demotion on drawdown breach is automatic.

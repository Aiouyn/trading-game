## Goal
Models do not see the future; they see committed truth through noise that shrinks with capability. Shared training data means shared mistakes (crowding); stolen students share their teacher's noise.

## Imports
- T04-03 regimes; T02-06 streams `ai/common/<key>/<tick>` and `ai/lineage/<root>/<key>/<tick>`. Owning modules register signals (05, 04, 06, 07, 09, 10, 16) through `registerSignal`.

## Implementation steps
1. Registered signals never include player holdings, orders, P&L or strategy (L11).
2. `z` draws from `ai/common` and `ai/lineage` streams; stolen students share the teacher's lineage root.
3. Ensemble mult applied at the deployment (T11-11); poison only on trading/analyst use.

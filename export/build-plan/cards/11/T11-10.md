## Goal
Agents trade through the same order intents as humans, on committed state only, with a validated graph whose DU cost the deployment can afford.

## Imports
- T11-02 tier universe and cadence; T11-04 effective DU; T11-08 estimates. T05-?? `mkt.*` intents and basket. T02-12 P5 / P6 order.

## Implementation steps
1. Observation rule: no block reads P0–P4 of the current tick or uncommitted entropy (static check on block defs).
2. Cadence staggered by `hash(deploymentId) mod cadence`; never faster than the tier cadence.
3. Templates: Trend Follower, Mean Reverter, Pairs, Market Maker, Sentiment Rider, Earnings Drift, Macro Carry, Crypto Basis, Vol-Target Overlay, Defensive Rebalancer, Hedger, Execution Assistant.

## Implementation steps
1. Node completes when both unlock time and PoC compute are done; nodes are permanent (transfer only with the company).
2. Pool refresh every 7 GD; stars listed 1 GD; highest total offer wins (NPC labs bid).
3. Loyalty uses 12 §3.9.1; quit when loyalty < 15 for 7 GD.

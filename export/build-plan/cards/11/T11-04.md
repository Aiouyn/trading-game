## Goal
When compute is short, defense is served first and training waits; decision capacity (DU) is shared fairly so nobody can crowd a partition.

## Imports
- T11-02 DU_cap; T11-03 supply; T06A-12 spot; T02-14 partition rounds. 12 hostile drain via its session table (read-only, T12-04).

## Implementation steps
1. Zero allocation for 24 game hours → PAUSED_NO_COMPUTE. Idle sold at spot − 5% when enabled.
2. Hostile drain read from 12 is capped at 0.20 in the formula (matches 12 `victim_drain_cap`).

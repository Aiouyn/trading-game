## Goal
One read-only, typed and deterministic view over the shipped content catalogs (22 §0.1), handed to every module as `ctx.content`. The first consumers are units 04 (T04-01, T04-10, T04-14 and T04-03's `peNorm0`), 02 (runtime and genesis), 03 (T03-08 entity registration), 05 (sector factor ids), 09A (business types, typed here only as a pass-through) and 20B (presets, through T22B). Presets, including MICRO, are owned by T22B `contentForPreset('MICRO'|'SMALL'|'STANDARD')`; T22A-01 loads only the full content. Rules:
- **Pure.** No I/O after load and no `Date`. Every call returns the same frozen value for the same content hash.
- **Canonical blocs.** Bloc ids are the canonical currency codes FDL, EUN, PYN, NKR and SRL (24 R39, 22 §0.3). 04's aliases FU, EC, PL, NA and SC resolve through one alias table.
- **Replays.** Content is versioned (`catalogVersion` per file) and hashed into `contentHash` (22 §0.1.5), so a replay can refuse a content mismatch.
- **Money.** Content money columns are nominal genesis amounts in whole FDL units (22 §0.1 rule 2), loaded with `toMoney()`. They are not real. `realFields` lists only columns that 22 explicitly labels real (for example 22 l.2418). Converting real to nominal belongs only to T14A-02 `realToNominal`, which 13A, 14B, 15A and 16A also use.

## Imports
- T02-01: `sortBy`, `cmpStr`, `sha256`, `canon` (canonical JSON).
- T23-01: `ParamsView` (core/params.ts on main); the sector `PE_base` params (e.g. `PARAM.eq_sector_tech_pe_base`) (06 §3.4.1) is read by `derived.ts`.
- T19A-01: the package layout (`packages/content/data/*.json`).
- T02-04: `toMoney()`, used for every money column.
- T14A-02 (types only): `Money`. Converting real to nominal stays with T14A-02 `realToNominal`. ContentView never converts.

## Implementation steps
1. **Load.** Read every `packages/content/data/*.json` once and validate it with the 22 §18 loader checks. Deep-freeze the result. Each file must carry an integer `catalogVersion`.
2. **Hash.** `contentHash = sha256(canon({ [fileName]: fileJson }))`, with keys sorted by `cmpStr`. Overrides are part of the hashed object, so a preset world never shares a hash with full content. The engine stores the hash at genesis, and a replay with a different hash fails with `CONTENT_HASH_MISMATCH` (02 replay rule).
3. **Bloc aliases.** There is one table, `BLOC_ALIAS_04`. `canonicalBloc` accepts both canonical ids and 04 aliases. `canonicalEntityId` rewrites only the 22 §0.3 institution prefixes (`cbk_`, `exc_`, `reg_`, `gov_`, `crt_`) when they are followed by a 04 alias. ContentView getters always return canonical ids.
4. **Derived lists:**
   - `primaryDealers(b)` (22 §5.2 l.1240): 8 per bloc, 40 seats in total. The list is b's SIB, b's commercial bank A (`commercialBankA`: FDL bnk_lksb, EUN bnk_bvlc, PYN bnk_tscb, NKR bnk_sksb, SRL bnk_bavd, per 22 §5.1), the four other blocs' SIBs, `npc_dlr_GRAY` and `npc_dlr_OSTP`, sorted. `npc_dlr_*` ids are valid under the `npc` kind with holder class npcFirm.
   - `tchMembers()`: the 5 SIBs, the 5 commercial banks A, `npc_dlr_GRAY`, `npc_dlr_OSTP`, `npc_VAULTCLR` and `bnk_serena`. That is 14 at launch.
   - `npcBanks()`: the 26 rows of 22 §5.1. "Assets (% bloc GDP)" is kept as a ratio (`assetsPctGdp`); the loader converts it to money at genesis with 04's GDP. The one fixed row (bnk_serena "$40B fixed") goes to `fixedAssets` via `toMoney()`. Roles are split on `,` and `;`. "—" and "no" become null or empty lists.
   - `swfEntityId(b)` = `fnd_SWF_${b}`: holder class npcFirm, `ownerId` = `gov_${b}`. Its flows (SWF_TRANSFER) are TRANSFERs, never MINT or BURN.
   - `fxAssetId`: the 22 §4.5 legs `ast_FX_EUNFDL`, `ast_FX_FDLNKR`, `ast_FX_FDLPYN` and `ast_FX_FDLSRL`. Quote order follows the base priority EUN > FDL > NKR > PYN > SRL, so only EUN is the base against FDL. CB pools never trade crosses.
   - `releases(b).statisticsOfficeId` = `npc_STATS_${b}` (E-58): kind `npc` (03 §2.1, 22 §1.4), holder class npcFirm, `ownerId` = `gov_${b}`. It publishes data and declares cycles only; it never holds money and no reason code posts to it.
   - Cities: 3 per bloc with ids `cty_<slug>`, 15 in total. `cty_isla_serena` belongs to no bloc: `serena()` returns homeBloc `SERENA` and reportingCcy `FDL`, and the city is excluded from `cities()`.
   - `canonicalEntityId` stores canonical ids only. T03-08 `registerEntityRt` passes ids through it, so 04-alias ids never reach storage.
   - `sectors()`: the 12 06 §3.4.1 rows. 22 §4.1 has no standalone sector table, so names come from the `idx_GXS_*` names in 22 §4.8.
5. **peNorm0 (source for T04-03 `peNorm` genesis; the T04-01 card's UNSOURCED flag).**
   - `peNorm0_b` is the genesis aggregate P/E of the bloc's 30-stock index at neutral valuation:
     `peNorm0_b = Σ_{i∈top30_b} cap_i / Σ_{i∈top30_b} (cap_i / PE_base_{sector(i)})`.
   - `top30_b` is the 30 largest b-listed equities by `cap0 × freeFloat`, with ties broken by assetId. Free float (22 §4.1 l.521; dynasty set from 22 §10.4 l.2570–2581): `freeFloat = 0.90` for a listed REIT; else `0.60` if the ticker appears in any §10.4 "Listed stakes" cell (dynasty-linked); else `0.75`.
   - Free float only selects the 30 constituents. Index weights are full `cap0`, not float-adjusted.
   - `PE_base` comes from params the sector `PE_base` params (e.g. `PARAM.eq_sector_tech_pe_base`) (06 §3.4.1). Defaults: TECH 32, SEMI 26, ENERGY 11, MATERIALS 12, INDUSTRIALS 16, FINANCIALS 10, HEALTH 20, STAPLES 18, DISCRETIONARY 20, REAL_ESTATE 16, MEDIA 18, SECURITY 24.
   - Aggregate P/E is total cap over total earnings, so the weighting is harmonic, which is how an index P/E is defined.
   - "Neutral" means the 06 §3.4.2 rate term `exp(−d·(y10 − r*))` = 1 and risk appetite RA = 0 (F = 50). That makes peNorm0 the long-run normal valuation, which `peNorm` (the 5-year trailing mean of peAgg) converges to. It is not a snapshot of genesis rates.
   - Values with the default content and params:

     | Bloc | peNorm0 |
     |---|---|
     | FDL | 16.79 |
     | EUN | 16.94 |
     | PYN | 17.77 |
     | NKR | 14.63 |
     | SRL | 15.45 |

   - The ordering follows the sector tilts: PYN and FDL are tech- and semis-heavy, NKR and SRL are energy- and materials-heavy (04 §2.2).
   - Money units do not affect it. P/E is cap divided by earnings, and both are in the same units (nominal genesis FDL via `toMoney`), so the money correction leaves every peNorm0 value unchanged.
   - Storage: the value is written into `content/indices.json` as field `peNorm0` on the five regional index rows. It is recomputed by the loader and must match to 0.01, or the loader fails with `CONTENT_PENORM0_STALE`. Range is [8, 40], the 06 sector P/E span [10, 32] widened by about 20%.
   - Retuning the sector `PE_base` params (e.g. `PARAM.eq_sector_tech_pe_base`) within the registry's ±50% re-derives the value through `peNorm0Of`. It is not a separate tuning knob.
   - Consumer: T04-03, which seeds `peNorm` at genesis and serves as the fallback while `peAgg` history is shorter than 5 game years. T20B presets read the same field.
   - Expected genesis offset (consistent with the spec, not a bug): if genesis has y10 > r*, the starting peAgg is below peNorm0 and the T04-03 valuation imbalance starts negative ("rates high, valuations below normal"). Engine Dev reports the genesis valuation z per bloc in the T04-03 test log.
6. **Money.** Every money column is nominal genesis whole-unit FDL (22 §0.1 rule 2) and is loaded with `toMoney()`. `realFields` lists only columns that 22 explicitly labels real. Nothing in ContentView multiplies or divides by MSI; T14A-02 `realToNominal` is the single converter.

## Test plan
`view.test.ts`. Every AC names the consumer it serves.
- **AC-22A-01-PURE** (all consumers): two `loadContent('full')` calls give an equal `contentHash` and deep-equal getter outputs. Getter results are frozen, and mutating one throws in strict mode. `Date` and `Math.random` are not referenced (lint).
- **AC-22A-01-HASH** (02 T02-12/T02-21 replay): changing one byte in any data file changes `contentHash`. Changing only key order does not change it. An override patch changes it.
- **AC-22A-01-VERSION** (02): a file without an integer `catalogVersion` fails the load.
- **AC-22A-01-ALIAS** (04 T04-01, 03 T03-08). Only these 5 prefixes are rewritten (`nfd_NA` is unchanged), and `registerEntityRt` stores canonical ids only:
  - `canonicalBloc('FU') = 'FDL'`, and likewise for all five aliases.
  - `canonicalEntityId('cbk_NA') = 'cbk_NKR'`.
  - `canonicalEntityId('cmp_halc')` is unchanged.
  - An unknown bloc string throws `CONTENT_UNKNOWN_BLOC`.
- **AC-22A-01-BLOCS** (T04-01 `blocDef`): `blocs()` returns 5 rows in order FDL, EUN, PYN, NKR, SRL. SRL has politicalCapital Alta Verde and financialCentre Sundara Coast. Isla Serena is not a bloc row.
- **AC-22A-01-RELEASES** (T04-01, T04-02): `releases('NKR').statisticsOfficeName = 'Statistics Nordic (SN)'` and its cycle committee is `'SN Cycle Committee'`. `releases(b).statisticsOfficeId` is `npc_STATS_<B>` for every bloc (e.g. `npc_STATS_NKR`), passes `assertEntityId`, and no reason code or posting ever names it (E-58).
- **AC-22A-01-CITIES** (T04-01, 10): `cities()` has 15 rows, 3 per bloc, every id matching `^cty_[a-z_]+$`. In every bloc the `cityGdpShare` values sum to 1 ± 1e-9, and every `cycleBeta` is in [0.6, 1.8]. `serena()` returns cityId `cty_isla_serena`, bloc null, homeBloc `SERENA` and reportingCcy `FDL`.
- **AC-22A-01-INDEX** (T04-01, T04-03): `blocEquityIndex('FDL').indexId = 'idx_FED30'` and each of the other four blocs maps to its own 30-stock index.
- **AC-22A-01-PENORM0** (T04-03, T20B):
  - With default params, `peNorm0` is FDL 16.79, EUN 16.94, PYN 17.77, NKR 14.63, SRL 15.45 (±0.01).
  - With `PARAM.eq_sector_tech_pe_base` 32 → 16, FDL's value drops and NKR's changes by less than FDL's.
  - A stale stored field fails with `CONTENT_PENORM0_STALE`.
  - Every value is in [8, 40].
  - Recomputing from `cap0` as Money (bigint) or as a float gives the same value to 1e-9, so units do not matter.
- **AC-22A-01-DEALERS** (T04-01, T04-09/11 auctions): `primaryDealers(b)` has length 8 for every b and 40 seats in total. Every list contains `npc_dlr_GRAY`, `npc_dlr_OSTP`, all 5 SIBs and `commercialBankA(b)`. `commercialBankA` is FDL bnk_lksb, EUN bnk_bvlc, PYN bnk_tscb, NKR bnk_sksb, SRL bnk_bavd.
- **AC-22A-01-BANKS** (07A):
  - `npcBanks()` has 26 rows: per bloc 1 NPC_SIB, 2 NPC_COMMERCIAL and 2 NPC_REGIONAL, plus `bnk_serena` (NPC_PRIVATE, bloc null, `fixedAssets` = toMoney(40e9), `assetsPctGdp` null).
  - Every other row has `assetsPctGdp` set and `fixedAssets` null.
  - 8 rows have a dynasty.
  - Every bloc city appears in some row's `defaultRetailFor`.
- **AC-22A-01-TCH** (07A, 07B): `tchMembers()` has length 14 and contains `npc_VAULTCLR` and `bnk_serena`.
- **AC-22A-01-SECTORS** (T04-10, 05 T05-01 `factorIds`): 12 ids in 06 §3.4.1 order, each with a name, and every 22 §4.1 sector value is among them.
- **AC-22A-01-SWF** (T04-10, 03): `swfEntityId('NKR') = 'fnd_SWF_NKR'`. Its registration spec has holder class npcFirm and `ownerId` `gov_NKR`, and the SWF_TRANSFER catalog class is TRANSFER.
- **AC-22A-01-FX** (T04-14): `fxAssetId` returns `ast_FX_EUNFDL`, `ast_FX_FDLNKR`, `ast_FX_FDLPYN` and `ast_FX_FDLSRL`. Any cross (non-FDL pair) is refused.
- **AC-22A-01-FXSIDE** (T04-14; corrected by E-58): a CB selling its own currency for FDL trades the authored leg in that leg's own quote (22 §4.5: EUNFDL is FDL per EUN; FDLxxx is xxx per FDL). Input p = FDL per unit of the CB's currency.
  - EUN is the base of `ast_FX_EUNFDL`: the CB **sells** EUNFDL at p, **not inverted**. Example: EUN CB at 1.10 FDL per EUN → SELL `ast_FX_EUNFDL` @1.1000.
  - FDL is the base of `ast_FX_FDLNKR`, `ast_FX_FDLPYN`, `ast_FX_FDLSRL`: the CB **buys** the pair (it buys FDL, the base) at **1/p**, rounded half-even to the pair's display decimals. Examples: NKR CB at 0.095 → BUY `ast_FX_FDLNKR` @10.5263; PYN CB at 0.15 → BUY `ast_FX_FDLPYN` @6.67; SRL CB at 0.20 → BUY `ast_FX_FDLSRL` @5.0000.
- **AC-22A-01-MONEY** (13A, 14B, 15A, 16A):
  - `equities()[i].cap0` equals `toMoney(cap0 × 1e9)` from the authored column (nominal genesis FDL).
  - `realFields('instruments')` is empty.
  - `realFields` returns only columns labelled real in 22.
  - Changing MSI changes no getter output.
- **AC-22A-01-PARAMS** (02 T02-11 `ctx.params`): `view.params` is the same object identity as the ParamsView passed to `loadContent`.
- **AC-22A-01-FREEFLOAT** (22 l.521, §10.4): `freeFloat` is 0.90 for listed REITs, 0.60 for every ticker in a §10.4 listed-stakes cell, and 0.75 otherwise. With default content, DNBR is excluded from the EUN 30 index and CLAR is included; CMAL is excluded from the SRL 30 index and BCMP is included.
- **AC-22A-01-FULLONLY**: `loadContent` accepts only `'full'` or a `ContentOverrides` with base `'full'`; any other source (e.g. `'minimal'`) fails to typecheck and is rejected at runtime. Preset subsets (MICRO, SMALL, STANDARD) come from T22B `contentForPreset`, not from this card.

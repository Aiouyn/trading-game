## Invariants (20 §3.1, added by ledger errata E-22)
INV-CHR-016 No intent or rule moves an estate or a House to another account; the character id is unchanged across succession.

## Goal
A House is an identity layer over the one character (§3.15.2), never a balance sheet. Succession ("Pass the Torch") is voluntary, once per chapter, and nothing escapes it: same entity, contracts, debts and court history (§9 #5, #6). This task also implements the 14A ports `inProbate` and `hasHouse`.

- Ports: 13 `openCaseOrInvestigation(chr)`, `unpaidJudgmentOrGarnishment(chr)`, `lastConvictionTick(chr)`, `isHeld`; 12 `plantedHiddenSession(chr)`; 07 `treasuryInstallmentLoan(chr, amount, bloc)`; 16 `TAX_INHERITANCE` scale (default 1.0).

## Implementation steps
1. Founding: R4+ (`ACCT_RANK_GATE`), no House, fee `house_founding_fee_real × MSI` (`ACCT_HOUSE_FEE`, gap), unique moderated name; emits `acct.house.founded`, milestone `M_FIRST_HOUSE`. Rename once per chapter with the fee again.
2. Heirlooms ≤ `house_heirlooms_max` LIMITED/UNIQUE items, changeable once per chapter per item; selling removes the flag; §8 #21 divestiture removes it.
3. Begin: requirements of §3.15.4 (each failing blocker has its own reject code `ACCT_SUCC_<BLOCKER>`); state PROBATE for `probate_ticks` (17,280). During PROBATE 15 refuses outbound P2P and new payer contracts (reads `inProbate`), and 14A refuses Fresh Start and handle change (ports).
4. Completion: recheck blockers; a new case blocks and the succession waits up to one chapter, then auto-aborts (§8 #12). `estateValueReal = rankBasisReal` at completion (§8 #11). Tax via `inheritanceTax`; pay in cash (`ACCT_INHERITANCE_TAX`, gap); shortfall → 07 installment loan over one chapter. Persona name and generation +1; Generation entry; Reputation `50 + f(R − 50)` with f 0.5 (0.8 if a conviction in the last chapter) as one `SUCCESSION_RESET` event; Notoriety × 0.25; previous titles → House titles (count at 50%); heir trait for one chapter; Legacy +25 (message to T14B-10); milestone `M_FIRST_GENERATION`; event `acct.house.succession.completed` PUBLIC if `showHouse`.
5. Heirloom heritage (§3.15.3, 14#48): heirloom `heritageStartTick` never resets; non-heirloom items keep theirs too (same owner); gifted items restart at the gift (15 contract, T14B-03 sets `heritageStartTick = tick`).
6. §3.15.5 / 14#49: no intent kind in module 14 has a payload field naming another account as estate receiver (test enumerates the schemas).

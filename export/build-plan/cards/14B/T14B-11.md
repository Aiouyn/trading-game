Everything other players can see about a character, built only from the publication contract (LC16) and never per tick: the estate render spec (rebuilt at day close and on display changes), the profile card (§3.16.2), the quarterly Gilt List (global and per country, deterministic cover star), and the opt-in verified track record (05 owns the math; classes only, never instruments).

## Implementation steps
1. Payload builders read only allowlisted fields; one shared `canonEncode` so identical public inputs give identical bytes (14#53).
2. Gilt (§3.16.3): top 100 Richest at that close (bucketed), estate spec ids only with `showEstate`, climbers by `ln(NW_end / max(NW_start, $100k))`, Most Flamboyant top 10 by PP, Most Generous top 10 by real philanthropy this quarter; cover star = max `0.4·rankDeltaPct + 0.3·ppPct + 0.3·newMilestone` among `showEstate`, ties by higher NWI then id; nobody twice per chapter per edition scope (§8 #20); no country edition for too-small boards (§8 #19). Cover star adds Legacy GILT_COVER (T14B-10) and title. Emits `life.gilt.published`.
3. Undisplaying an item removes it from the estate payload at the next day close (14#19 with T14B-06's PP removal).

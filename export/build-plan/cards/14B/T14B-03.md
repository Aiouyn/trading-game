## Goal
Buying, owning, storing and showing things. A MASS purchase is a sink that recognizes a smaller asset (the purchase hit). Only items in a residence can be displayed; paid storage never displays (§9 #10). Hiding everything is allowed (quiet money, §9 #14). Dealer buyback always loses money (§9 #8). The starter gift cannot be farmed (§9 #15).

## Implementation steps
1. `life.item.buy` (P1): MASS only (LIMITED goes through drops, T14B-04); `rank >= minRank` (`LIFE_RANK`); storage free in the chosen home or `toPaidStorage` (`LIFE_NO_STORAGE`); cash via `placeCashFor(..., 'LIFE_PURCHASE')` (restricted cash allowed, §3.13.5); HOME category defs are bought through 10 (`LIFE_HOME_VIA_PROPERTY`).
2. Apply P12: one `elasticOut(chr, 'hh_LUX', price, 'FDL', 'LIFE_PURCHASE', { valueLegA: <item asset account>, valueLegB: price × (1−hit) })` so the sink and the asset recognition are one balanced entry (03 alias of LIFE_BOUTIQUE_PURCHASE + LIFE_ITEM_RECOGNIZE). Item row with condition 1e6, `pricePaidReal = nominalToReal(price)`, `displayed = storage in a residence`. Wire: if displayed, real price ≥ `wire_purchase_threshold_real` and `showLifestyleOnWire`, emit `life.item.acquired` PUBLIC with `priceBucket`; else PRIVATE.
3. Built-to-order (`buildRealDays > 0`, §8 #9): `life.item.order` charges at order, `buildCompletesTick = tick + buildRealDays × 17,280`, gives no PP until complete; `perChapterBuildCap` reached → `LIFE_BUILD_CAP` with `params.nextChapter`. With `builderCmpId` (a player yacht builder, 09) the payment is a TRANSFER to the company (code from `CODES_14B`, gap listed) and build time comes from 09 port.
4. Starter gift (E-22 addendum): a non-cash item, posted with the ACCRUE template: debit `1860:lid_kessler_field`, credit `4110`, `realToNominal(840_000_000n, msiPpm)` micro (E-23; Quant signed off; fixed at grant tick, no MSI revalue), code `ACCT_STARTER_GIFT` (no cash moves, so INV-LED-003 holds); `sellLockUntilTick = tick + starter_gift_lock_real_days × 17,280`; guests cannot sell (14A `isGuest`).
5. `life.item.sell DEALER` (§3.6.10): MASS, not pledged, not STOLEN, sell lock passed; proceeds `value × (1 − dealer_buyback_haircut)` via `elasticIn('hh_LUX', chr, ..., 'LIFE_RESALE_TO_NPC')`; item removed; `life.item.disposed SOLD`.
6. Display (§3.6.5): `true` needs the item stored in a residence home (`LIFE_DISPLAY_NEEDS_RESIDENCE`; paid storage → same code, 14#20). `false` sets `displayPrivate`. PP updates at the next day close (T14B-06).
7. Storage (§3.6.6): capacity = Σ homes' `storageProvided` + paid units. Paid storage charges `paid_storage_rate` of value per game year at day close (`LIFE_STORAGE`, gap code). Berth/hangar rentals: $30,000 and $120,000 real per game year, `LIFE_BERTH_RENT`/`LIFE_HANGAR_RENT`; `LIFE_SLOT_RENT_P2P` when 10 reports a player-owned marina.
8. `onHomeRemoved` (§8 #5, #6): items move to paid storage that tick, displayed false; staff lines stop; the last home → rented room. Rented-room rent (10 §3.14 market rent) charged at day close only while unrestricted cash ≥ `rent_min_cash_real`.

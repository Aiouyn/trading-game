## Invariants (20 §3.1, added by ledger errata E-22)
INV-CHR-014 Prestige counts only displayed, non-cosmetic items (with INV-PRG-002); an item counts in at most one set.

## Goal
Prestige is public status from what you own and show. Computed once per day close: per-category sums with breadth weights 0.5^(k−1) (§9 #9), +20% set bonuses, title PP, active experience bursts. This task also owns the title store for all of module 14 (it extends 14A's `acct.title` table, where T14A-09 already writes `T_PHOENIX`) and the milestone awards of §3.5.1, and it exports the small capped uses of PP read by 12, 15, 09 and 11.

## Imports
- T14B-02: `ppItem`. T14A-05: `acct.rank.up`, rank rows. T14A-09: `titleAwardTable` rows (Phoenix). Event sources for milestones: 09, 05, 10, 12, 13, 16 (consumed as event schemas with fixtures).

## Implementation steps
1. `computePrestige` (§3.8.2): displayed items only; EXPERIENCE and PHILANTHROPY excluded from categories; `setBonus = 0.2 × Σ PP of items used`; `titlesPP` fresh in the earning chapter and the next, then heritage; previous persona titles × `house_title_pp_share`; bursts active at tick; `pp = roundHalfEven(total)` (round half to even, stated as an AC: **AC-14B-06-ROUND** every PP value, per item and per character, uses `roundHalfEven`; `Math.round` is a lint error).
2. Sets (§3.9): requirements checked against owned and displayed items, 10 homes per bloc (port), districts (port). Completing a set awards its title once (set titles have 0 PP).
3. Milestones (§3.5.1): one-time per character, reward decor item (no cash, L7), title, `acct.milestone` PUBLIC when `wire`, else PRIVATE. `M_100K`, `M_MILLIONAIRE`, `M_BILLIONAIRE` read rank-basis NW at day close (14A `rankBasisReal`). `M_FIRST_HOUSE` and `M_FIRST_GENERATION` come from T14B-09.
4. 14A handoff: `T_PHOENIX` rows written by T14A-09 are read by `titlePP` like any other title (class "Milestone titles", 5/5).

## Goal
All loan mathematics of 07 §3.3 as pure functions shared by every lender (NPC banks, player banks, 09/10 originations, the aggregate allocator): the origination eligibility and limits, the price `rate = fundingIndex + EL + capitalCharge + opexCharge + margin + standardsAddOn`, the four amortization schedules that repay exactly the principal, the fixed-rate break cost, the delinquency/default state machine with penalty interest, covenant testing with cure periods and the four breach outcomes, and the IFRS-9-style stage and provision of each loan. T07A-08 wires them into intents and the daily loop.

## Implementation steps
1. Eligibility (07 §3.3.1 step 2 and 4), checked in this order, first failure returns: lender `RESTRICTED` (unless `cureLoan`) or in resolution/resolved/liquidated → `BNK_RESTRICTED_BANK`; WATCH lender beyond 50% of normal supply (`watchSupplyUsedFrac ≥ 0.5`) → `BNK_RESTRICTED_BANK`; product disabled `BNK_PRODUCT_DISABLED`; borrower Held `BNK_HELD`; NPC lender and borrower in the 14-real-day post-bankruptcy lockout `BNK_LOCKOUT`; grade below `minGradeFor(S)` `BNK_GRADE_CUTOFF`; amount > min(productLimit, borrowerLimit) `BNK_LIMIT`; large exposure `BNK_LARGE_EXPOSURE`; related party `BNK_RELATED_PARTY`; LTV > `effectiveMaxLtv` `BNK_LTV`.
2. `PRODUCT_LGD` (DEFAULT where 07 gives none; aligned with §3.5.4 and 06 LGDs): PERSONAL_LINE 0.80, AGG_HH_CONSUMER 0.80, LOMBARD 0.45, MARGIN_DEBIT 0.45, COLLECTIBLE 0.50, BUSINESS_TERM 0.50, REVOLVER 0.50, BRIDGE 0.50, AGG_FRM 0.50, MORTGAGE_RESI 0.25, AGG_HH_MORTGAGE 0.25, MORTGAGE_COMM 0.35, CONSTRUCTION 0.45, LBO_SENIOR 0.50, LBO_MEZZ 0.75, INTERBANK 0.55, CB_FACILITY 0.10.
3. `loanRate` (07 §3.3.1 step 5): `EL = PD_1y · LGD_eff`; `capitalCharge = RW · cet1Target · bank_cost_of_equity`; `opexCharge` per class; `margin = policy.loanSpreadGrid[grade]` (NPC default `NPC_SPREAD_GRID`, linear from 0.25% AAA to 4.0% CCC); `standardsAddOn = 0.03 · S²`; fundingIndex = ONR for FLOAT, the OIS par rate for the term for FIXED (`up.macro.oisPar`, 06 §3.16.3). Higher S strictly raises the rate (∂/∂S = 0.06 S > 0 for S > 0; at S = 0 the cutoff and LTV still tighten) and lowers max LTV (07#9).
4. Schedules (07 §3.3.2): `n = termDays / paymentFreqDays` (integer; termDays is a multiple of the frequency by validation). ANNUITY: `A = P·i/(1 − (1+i)^−n)` with `dmath.pow`; interest of each period = the accrued daily interest (`dailyInterest` per day on the outstanding), principal = A − interest; LINEAR: principal `P/n` (`allocate` P into n equal parts, remainder to the last) + interest; BULLET: interest each period, principal at maturity; IO_THEN_ANNUITY: interest during `ioPeriodDays`, then annuity over the remaining periods; REVOLVING: interest monthly on drawn, principal at maturity. The final payment equals the whole remaining outstanding plus accrued (absorbs rounding, 07#10). Floating loans re-fix the rate daily from ONR (+ spread, + penalty while DELINQUENT, + `rateStepBps`) and recompute the annuity on the remaining schedule at each reset.
5. Payment application order: fees → penalty interest → accrued interest → principal (`applyPayment`). Principal posts CREDIT_DESTROY (`BNK_LOAN_REPAY`), interest TRANSFER (`BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`) in T07A-08.
6. Break cost (D134): FIXED loans: remaining contract payments discounted at the current OIS rate for the remaining term, `PV = Σ CF_k · exp(−r_ois · τ_k)` (τ in game years, `dmath.exp`), cost = `max(0, PV − outstanding)` (toMoney); FLOAT → 0.
7. State machine (07 §3.3.3, evaluated at each day close after the payment attempt): missed payment → overdue += unpaid; DPD = game days since `firstMissedTick`; DPD ≥ 1 → DELINQUENT (penalty `+5%`/yr on the overdue amount each day, credit late count +1 once, event); DPD ≥ `loan_default_dpd` (3) → DEFAULTED, `accelerate` (whole outstanding due), `startEnforcement` (enforcement runs in P8 of the next tick, 07#11); paying all overdue before then → PERFORMING with `watchUntilTick = tick + 7×720` (status WATCH until then).
8. Covenants (07 §3.3.4): due every `testEveryDays` at t = 719; MAX_* breached when value > threshold, MIN_* when value < threshold, CHANGE_OF_CONTROL when the borrower's controller changed since origination, NEGATIVE_PLEDGE when the borrower pledged assets to another secured party after origination; first breach → `START_CURE` (`breachedSinceTick = tick`); value back within → `CURED`; still breached after `cureDays` → `APPLY` the `onBreach`: `RATE_STEP_100` (+100 bps until cured), `CASH_SWEEP_50` (50% of daily net income to principal), `ACCELERATE` (treated as default), `WAIVER_FEE_1PCT` (NPC lenders waive when `npcWaives`, charging 1% of outstanding as `BNK_LOAN_FEE`; player lenders decide by policy, default waive = false).
9. ECL (07 §3.3.7): stage 3 if DEFAULTED; stage 2 if WATCH, DPD ≥ 1, `notchesDown(originGrade, currentGrade) ≥ 3`, or any covenant breached; else 1. Provision: stage 1 `PD·LGD·EAD`; stage 2 `PD_life·LGD·EAD` with `PD_life = 1 − (1 − PD)^years remaining`; stage 3 `EAD − expected recovery` (collateral after haircut minus 1% enforcement costs). `eclInputFor` maps these to 03's `EclInput` so 03 posts `LED_ECL_PROVISION` (stage 2: `pd12m = PD_life`; stage 3: `defaulted = true`, `lgd = 1 − recovery/EAD`).

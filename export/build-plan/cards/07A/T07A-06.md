## Goal
Banks fund each other overnight through an hourly per-bloc match in which lenders price and ration borrowers by PD and fear, so weak banks are squeezed out to the central bank and a freeze emerges without a script (07 §3.7, D127). The day's interbank loans fix the ONR, the floating index of swaps, FRNs and floating loans (06). Banks draw the marginal lending facility against eligible collateral; ELA goes through 04's decision with 07's solvency test; heavy MLF use and any ELA become public with a 7-game-day lag and seed true rumors (stigma).

## Implementation steps
1. Reserve gap (07 §3.7.1), at `t mod 30 == 29`: `G_b = reserves_now + scheduled inflows − scheduled outflows − (rr · (sight + savings) + reserve_buffer_frac · deposits)`; scheduled flows until day close = interbank and CB facility maturities due at this close and loan payments due at this close from borrowers at other banks (DEFAULT, the spec leaves "scheduled" implicit; deposit flows are not scheduled). Lenders G > 0, borrowers G < 0.
2. `matchHour` (07 §3.7.2): for every lender L and borrower B (B ≠ L): skip if `refuses(PD_B, tol_L, fear)`; `limit_LB = lenderLimit(...)` with existing L→B interbank exposure (open INTERBANK loans); `ask_LB = askRate(DFR, PD_B, fear)`, for MANUAL lenders `ask_LB = limitRate` and limit = `min(counterpartyLimits[B] ?? limit_LB, limit_LB)`. Borrowers are processed in ascending `orderKey = hex(sha256(canonEncode([bankId, hourTick])))`; each takes asks in ascending rate (ties by lender id) while `ask ≤ MLFR − 0.0001` (and ≤ its own `limitRate` if MANUAL), its need is unfilled, the lender's remaining supply (its `G_L`, or the MANUAL order amount) and `limit_LB` allow. Amount per fill = min of the three (money rounded with `toMoney` at booking). Unfilled borrowers rely on the end-of-day facility.
3. Booking each fill (P8, postings P13): `Loan` row kind `INTERBANK`, rate fixed, maturity next day close (t = 719 of the same day if the hour is before 719, else the next); postings `BNK_INTERBANK_LEND` (reserves L → B, 1304 at L, 2110 at B). `settleOvernightInterbank` at t = 719 repays principal `BNK_INTERBANK_REPAY` and interest `amount · rate / 364` `BNK_INTERBANK_INTEREST`. If B fails overnight, the claim joins class 6 (T07A-13, 07 §8 edge 5).
4. `InterbankHourResult` per bloc (`volume`, `vwapRate`, `refusedBorrowers` = borrower ids with unfilled need, stored engine-side only) and event `bank.interbank.hour` PUBLIC `{ bloc, hourTick, volume, vwap, refusedCount }` (count only, L11).
5. Player banks in MANUAL (`policy.interbankMode`) submit `bank.interbank.order` (validation: owner or mandated operator, bank ACTIVE or WATCH, MANUAL mode `BNK_NOT_MANUAL`, side/amount/limitRate valid); orders stored in `bnk.ib_order` for the next `t mod 30 == 29` and expire after it. AUTO player banks and NPC banks use their computed gap and `interbankPdTolerance`.
6. Freeze (07 §3.7.3) is emergent: higher fear and PD shrink limits and raise asks; no extra rule. The stress indicator `(ONR − policyRate)`, volume / 30-day average volume and refused count come from `interbankStress` (T07A-19 publishes it).
7. ONR (07 §3.7.4), t = 719: VWAP of the day's interbank loans of the bloc; if volume < `onr_min_volume_frac` × bloc bank deposits → `policy + (prevONR − prevPolicy)`, `fallback = true`; first fixing after genesis uses prevONR = policy. Write `bnk.onr`, emit `bank.onr.fixing` PUBLIC.
8. MLF intent `bank.cb.facility` (07 §3.7.5, D136): P1: bank owner or operator, collateral list eligible at venue CB (T07A-02 `eligible(cls,'CB')`), unencumbered, value after CB haircut ≥ amount (`BNK_COLLATERAL`); apply P8: `createPledge` to `cbk_<bloc>` for each item, then `ctx.send(cbk, 'cb.facility.draw', { bankId, kind: 'MLF', amount, pledgeIds }, 'B1')`; 04 books the loan at MLFR. The automatic day-close draw for negative reserves is 03's conversion (T07A-05). Deposit-facility moves and ELA requests use 04's own intents (`bank.cb.deposit`, `bank.cb.depositWithdraw`, `bank.cb.elaRequest`); 07 only provides `solvencyTest` (exported for 04's `MacroCreditPort.solvencyOk`, T07A-10 wires it) and `requestEla` for the run playbook (T07A-12), which sends the same request on behalf of the bank.
9. Stigma: `trackStigma` at day close: MLF outstanding (04 facility loans kind REPO/standing for the bank) > 1% of total assets on 2 consecutive day closes → one record (kind MLF) per episode; every new ELA loan → a record (kind ELA). `publishTick = usedTick + 7 × 720`. Event `bank.cb.facilityUsed` PRIVATE (to the bank owner) on each use. `publishCbReports` at WEEK_CLOSE emits `bank.cb.weeklyReport` PUBLIC listing `{ bankId, kind, amountBand }` of records with `publishTick ≤ tick` not yet published, then marks them published. Rumor: from use until publication + 28 game days, each day close draw `rng('bank.run:<bankId>').bernoulli(0.3)` (one draw per bank per day, banks in id order); true → `bank.stigma.rumorSeed` (PRIVATE subject bank, payload `{ bankId, kind, truthful: true }`) for 16's rumor generator. `stigmaLive` = published and within 28 game days (the confidence `stigma` term, T07A-12).
10. Edge 5: interbank lender's claim on a failed borrower is class 6 (handled in T07A-13 creditor hierarchy). Y17 (interbank arbitrage) is allowed; nothing blocks it.

  - **07#19 Hourly interbank match: no lender exceeds its limit or its gap; borrowers with PD above lender tolerance receive nothing from that lender; ONR equals the volume-weighted rate of the day's loans or the fallback when volume is below threshold**: fast-check 2,000 random hours (3–10 lenders and borrowers, random PD, tol, fear, gaps): Σ fills per lender ≤ its supply, per pair ≤ limit_LB, no fill where `PD_B > tol_L·(1 − 0.5 fear)`, every fill rate ≤ MLFR − 0.0001.

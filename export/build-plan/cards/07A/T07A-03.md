## Goal
Compute, from a bank's 03 balance sheet and its 07 records, every prudential number of 07 §3.2: the exposure list with risk weights, RWA (credit + market + derivatives), CET1, Tier 1, total capital, the combined requirement, the leverage ratio, LCR, NSFR, the reserve requirement and its penalty, large-exposure and related-party headroom, the insured/uninsured split of deposits per beneficial-owner group, the daily P&L decomposition, and the status table (ACTIVE, WATCH with MDA fraction, RESTRICTED, failure trigger). All pure over `StoreRead` so the daily loop (T07A-11), runs (T07A-12), resolution (T07A-13), charters (T07A-14) and the views reuse one implementation. INV-BNK-001 checks that every published metric equals this recomputation and that no bank below its minimum is ACTIVE.

## Implementation steps
1. `buildBalanceSheet` reads the bank's 03 accounts (`up.ledger.balance` of `accountKey(bankId, code, ccy, sub)` for every account of the bank, translated to the reporting currency with the FX mark) and its 07 rows. Mapping (03 §3.2 chart, balances debit-positive):
   - 1002 reserves and 04 deposit-facility balance → `RESERVES` (0%), HQLA L1.
   - 1520 bonds by issuer: own-bloc government `OWN_GOV`; other governments by rating AA− or better `OTHER_GOV_AA`, A `OTHER_GOV_A`, BBB `OTHER_GOV_BBB`, below `OTHER_GOV_BELOW`; corporate and bank bonds by grade AAA–A `CORP_AAA_A`, BBB `CORP_BBB`, BB `CORP_BB`, B and below `CORP_B_BELOW`. HQLA: own-bloc and other AA government L1, IG corporate L2A. RSF: L1 5%, L2A 15%, other 85%.
   - 1304 interbank loans `INTERBANK` 20%; 1305 reverse repo: with `ccp_TCH` `REVREPO_CCP` 20%, with a bank 20% (`INTERBANK`), with others by the counterparty grade.
   - Loans (07 `bnk.loan` rows where lenderId = bank, exposure = outstanding + accruedInterest − provision; DEFAULTED → `DEFAULTED` 150%): MORTGAGE_RESI and AGG_HH_MORTGAGE by current LTV ≤ 0.80 `RES_MORT_LE80` else `RES_MORT_GT80` (current LTV from 10's appraisal of the pledged property, or `segmentLtv` for pools; absent → origination LTV); MORTGAGE_COMM `CRE`; CONSTRUCTION `CONSTRUCTION`; BUSINESS_TERM, REVOLVER, BRIDGE, AGG_FRM by borrower grade (corporate rows); PERSONAL_LINE, AGG_HH_CONSUMER `CONSUMER`; LOMBARD and MARGIN_DEBIT (1302) `LOMBARD`; COLLECTIBLE `COLLECTIBLE_LOAN`; LBO_SENIOR, LBO_MEZZ their rows; INTERBANK `INTERBANK`. RSF: mortgages 65%, other loans 85%, interbank ≤ 30 days 15% (treated as Level 2A-like short claim; DEFAULT).
   - 1500, 1510, 1530 `EQUITY` 250% (GX100 equities HQLA L2B); 1540 tokens `CRYPTO` 1250%; 1560 tranches by grade `SEC_*`; 1600/2600 and every `ExposureSource` row → derivatives.
   - Encumbered (`pledgedQty` > 0, repo'd out) parts are excluded from HQLA (07 §3.2.3).
   - Funding lines from 2100 deposits per holder (classify by holder kind and `DepositAccount.kind`: natural persons `chr` and `hh` → retail, split insured/uninsured with `insuredSplit`; companies controlled by a player root → `PLAYER_CORP`; NPC companies and `frm` SIGHT → `NPC_OP_CORP`, their SAVINGS/TERM → `NONOP_CORP`; `bnk fnd ins spv ccp` and 08 funds → `FINANCIAL`), 2110 interbank, 2120 CB borrowing, 2130 repo (Level 1 collateral → `REPO_L1`), 2300 bonds by residual maturity, TERM deposits with residual maturity > 91 days → `TERM_GT91`, equity → `CAPITAL`.
   - Equity: paidIn = −(3100 + 3110 + 3150), retained = −3200, currentResult = −3300, revaluation = −3400, intangibles = 1700 + 1710 + 1720 + 1730 + 1740, dta = 1900, at1 = bonds issued with seniority AT1, tier2 = subordinated bonds issued.
2. `computeCapital`: `CET1 = paidIn + retained + currentResult + revaluation − intangibles − dta` (provisions are already booked by 03's ECL step). `Tier1 = CET1 + at1`, `totalCapital = Tier1 + tier2`. `RWA_credit = Σ RW × exposure` with RW from params `rw_<class>`; derivatives: exposure = max(0, MTM) + `rw_DERIV_ADDON` × notional, weighted by the counterparty's RW row (bank → INTERBANK, corporate by grade) or `rw_DERIV_CLEARED` (2%) for TCH-cleared exposures. `RWA_mkt = rw_MKT_MULT × tradingEs10d` (12.5 × 97.5% ES over 10 game days of the trading book: 1500, 1510, 1540 positions and derivatives; the ES number is computed by T07A-17 and passed in). Requirement `= cet1Min + conservationBuffer + ccyb + sibSurcharge` from `bank_capital_requirement`. Leverage exposure `= totalAssets + 0.10 × Σ undrawn commitments + derivativeExposure`; minimum 3% (SIB 3.5%).
3. `statusFromMetrics` (07 §3.2.2 table, checked in this order): `cet1Ratio < bank_failure_cet1` → failureTrigger true (status RESTRICTED until T07A-13 opens resolution); `cet1Ratio < 0.045` or `leverageRatio < leverageMin` → RESTRICTED (mda 0); `cet1Ratio < requirement` (and ≥ 4.5% + CCyB/2) or `lcr < 1.0` → WATCH; else ACTIVE. Note the WATCH band's lower edge `4.5% + CCyB/2`: a CET1 between 4.5% and that edge is RESTRICTED-free but below the WATCH band, so it is WATCH with mda 0 (DEFAULT: bottom quartile). MDA: buffer span `[minWithHalfCcyb, requirement)` split into 4 equal quartiles → 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 of earnings; ≥ requirement → 1.
4. Liquidity per 07 §3.2.3: HQLA = `L1 + min(L2A' + L2B'', (2/3)·L1)`, where `L2A' = 0.85 × L2A`, `L2B' = 0.50 × L2B`, `L2B'' = min(L2B', (15/85)·(L1 + L2A'))` (L2A ≤ 40% and L2B ≤ 15% of HQLA). Outflows: insured retail 5%, uninsured retail 10%, PLAYER_CORP 25%, NPC_OP_CORP 25%, NONOP_CORP 40%, FINANCIAL 100%, undrawn commitments 10% (financial 40%), interbank/CB/repo maturing ≤ 30 game days 100% (REPO_L1 0%). Inflows = 0.5 × contractual performing loan inflows in 30 days + 1.0 × maturing interbank lending, capped at 0.75 × outflows. `lcr = hqla / max(outflows − inflows, 1)`. NSFR ASF: capital 100%, TERM_GT91 and BOND_GT1Y 100%, retail sight (insured + uninsured retail SIGHT/SAVINGS) 90%, corporate 50%, financial 0%; RSF per step 1; NSFR is report-only (WATCH below 1.0 per the table's LCR rule does not apply; WATCH is set by NSFR only through `nsfrReportOnly` = false).
5. Reserve requirement: `required = rr × (sight + savings deposits)`; shortfall at day close posts `(MLFR + 0.02) × shortfall / 364` (T07A-05 posts it: `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_P` burn for player banks, `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_W` pay to the bloc cbk for NPC banks).
6. Large exposures: per beneficial-owner group (`ports.social.ownerGroupOf`) Σ exposures ≤ 25% Tier1 (both banks SIB: 15%); related parties (the bank's own owner group) ≤ `min(policy.relatedPartyLimitFrac, 0.05)` × Tier1. Headroom functions return the remaining room (may be negative).
7. `insuredSplit` (07 §3.8 cap, §8 edge 2, Y4): group the bank's deposit accounts by beneficial-owner group; convert each balance to FDL at the FX mark; the cap `dgf_cap` (FDL) applies once per group across all currencies; fill insured amounts in this order: accounts in the bloc currency first, then other currencies, within each by largest balance first, ties by accountId; amounts converted back with `toMoney`. `blanket = true` (04 blanket guarantee) → everything insured. `bnk`, `cbk`, `gov` holders are never insured.
8. `dailyPnl` (07 §3.2.4) from the day's committed postings of the bank by reason code: NII = loan interest + coupons + reserve interest + reverse repo − deposit interest − interbank − CB − repo − bond interest; Fees = BNK_LOAN_FEE + BNK_FX_MARGIN + PB_FEE + PB_MARGIN_INTEREST spread + PB_CUSTODY_FEE + CCP_FEE received + SECLEND agent share; Trading = Δ marks of trading book (03 LED_MTM entries of the bank); Opex = branches × `branch_cost_per_day.baseUsd` × `up.macro.wageIndex` + 0.006 × totalAssets / 364 + DGF premiums + regulatory fees; Credit = −Δ provisions − write-offs + recoveries; Tax from 03. Opex is posted by T07A-11 (`BNK_OPEX_ELASTIC` to the bloc `frm` sector), not here.
9. `metricsOf` fills `BankMetrics` (money fields via `toMoney` in the bank's reporting currency; `nim30d`, `roe30d`, `costIncome30d` from the last 30 `bnk.daily` rows; `largestExposureFrac` = max group exposure / Tier1; `durationGapYears`).
10. INV-BNK-001 (DAY_CLOSE FATAL): for every bank row written at this day close, recompute `buildBalanceSheet` + `computeCapital` and compare to `bank.metrics` (`cet1Ratio`, `leverageRatio`, `lcr` equal within 1e-9 relative; money fields exact); and no bank with `cet1Ratio < cet1Min` (4.5%) has status ACTIVE. Negative tests in this task.
11. Edge 6 (07 §8): negative DFR flows through the reserve-interest term with sign; formulas accept negatives.
- **E-47 large exposures.** The beneficial-owner group of the large-exposure cap is the friction owner group (T03-17 `frictionNW` membership: owner group, 15-linked characters, their shells). The 25% / 15% fractions are [Quant]. A refused loan shows `why.topend.large_exposure`.

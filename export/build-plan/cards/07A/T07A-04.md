## Goal
The one shared credit bureau of 07 §3.4: every lender (NPC banks, player banks, 15 P2P contracts) reports to it; each day close it recomputes every character's and company's `CreditProfile` (score, grade, PD) from leverage, liquidity, debt service, income volatility and payment history, never from Reputation or Prestige (D125). It also holds the player credit product limits of §3.4.3 (grade caps, rookie limit, Lombard formula, R11 business sizing), the global DSR cap, and the **ClearingDebt registry** (07 §3.16.5): debts created by 07B deficits, accrued at ONR + 2%, repaid with 30% of daily net income, blocking leverage, P2P outflows, ops funding and lifestyle purchases above $1,000 until repaid, and surviving bankruptcy. The bankruptcy interplay of §3.19 is handled here.

## Implementation steps
1. Score inputs (07 §3.4.2), consolidated for companies (03 groups): `lev = totalDebt / max(NW, $1)`; `liq = liquid / max(totalDebt, $1)` capped 3 (liquid = cash and deposits + marks of LIC1-tradeable holdings, restricted cash excluded); `dsr = debtService30d / max(income30d, $1)`; `vol = coefVar(last 28 daily net incomes)` capped 2; `hist = onTime / (onTime + 3·late + 1)`, and 0.5 when the entity has no reported payments (new file). `z` exactly as the formula with indicator terms; `score = round(1000·σ(z))`; PD and grade bands exactly as written (D when any own loan is DEFAULTED). Reputation and Prestige are not inputs anywhere (07#14, D125).
2. Thin file (spec contradiction resolved here): the formula with `hist = 0.5` gives a fresh $5,000 character `z = 1.8 + 0.6·3 + 1.2·0.5 + 0.15·ln(0.1) = 3.854612` (score 979, AAA), while 07 §3.4.2 states "about 560 (BB)" and 07#15 requires 540–580. Both texts are in the owning module; the specific statement and the acceptance test govern the no-history case: while `thinFile` (no payment ever reported, no defaults, no judgments) `z += THIN_FILE_Z = logit(0.56) − 3.854612 = −3.613450`, so the reference character scores exactly 560 and wealthier fresh characters still score higher (NW $20k → 585, $100k → 643). The flag clears on the first reported payment. Recorded in `_unit.md`.
3. Bureau storage: `bnk.profile` row per entity (id = entityId), `bnk.bureau_hist` keeps 28 daily net incomes (`up` T03-19 `netIncomeOfDay`) and 30 days of debt service (scheduled payments of all loans of the borrower + 15 contract repayments). `updateProfiles` (DAY_CLOSE, P8, before lending decisions) iterates entities in id order: chr and cmp that have a profile, a loan, a deposit or a pending application. `totalDebt` = Σ outstanding of loans where the entity is borrower + P2P loans + ClearingDebt. Emits `bank.credit.profileUpdated` (PRIVATE, grade change only). `activeJudgments` from 03 claims register; `bankruptcyTick` from 03's fresh-start event.
4. Product limits (07 §3.4.3): grade caps between AAA $50M and B $50k are geometric (each notch × (0.001)^(1/5)), personal-line and Lombard rates linear from AAA to B (DEFAULT, the spec gives only the ends); CCC, CC, D limit 0. Rookie window (72 real hours, `up.players.inRookieProtection`): personal line beyond $1,000 unavailable (07 §8 edge 10). Lombard limit `Σ collateral × (1 − 1.5 × haircut)` using the base haircut of T07A-02 at the Lombard venue. Business term `≤ 3.0 × EBITDA` with DSCR ≥ 1.25, revolver `≤ 1.0 × EBITDA`, together ≤ 4 × EBITDA, no EV cap (R11). LBO senior ≤ 5×, total ≤ 7×. Mortgage and construction limits are 10's (T07A-08 calls the 10 port).
5. DSR cap: new credit refused with `BNK_DSR` if post-loan `dsr > PARAM.max_dsr` (0.50); a loan secured by liquid collateral (Lombard, cash, securities) is exempt up to the collateral value after haircut (07 §3.4.3, Y14 stacking: debt service counts every lender).
6. P2P loans (07 §3.4.4): `ingestP2pReports` reads `ports.social.p2pLoanReports` each day close and reports payments/defaults; they do not create money (15 moves the deposit as TRANSFER), they only enter the bureau.
7. ClearingDebt: `createClearingDebt` writes the row with `rate = ONR_bloc + ccp_clearing_debt_terms.spreadOverOnr` (re-fixed daily), `repaymentShare = ccp_clearing_debt_terms.repaymentShareOfDailyNetIncome`, registers a persistent 03 obligation (persistentKind `CCP_DEFICIT`) so it survives the fresh start, sets the bureau `clearingDebt`. `accrueAndAutoRepay` (DAY_CLOSE): interest `outstanding · rate / 364` added (toMoney per debt, half-even), then if the day's net income > 0, pay `min(outstanding, 0.30 × netIncome)` from the debtor to the creditor with `CLR_DEBT_REPAY` (TRANSFER); voluntary repay via the intent (P8, validation: own debt, amount ≤ outstanding, available cash). Fully repaid → `bank.clearingDebt.repaid` PRIVATE, row closed (outstanding 0).
8. `clearingDebtBlocks`: true for MARGIN, DERIVATIVES, P2P_OUTFLOW, OPS_FUNDING, LIFESTYLE when `amountUsd > 1000`, false for SPOT_OWN_CASH, BUSINESS_T1_T2, UPKEEP (07 §3.16.5 point 2, 07#43 uses it). Other modules call it (14B lifestyle, 12 ops, 15 P2P, 07B margin, 06 derivatives gates).
9. `onFreshStart` (07 §3.19): for the character and its consolidated group: NPC-bank loans and lines are discharged (lender writes off through T07A-08's charge-off path: `BNK_LOAN_CHARGEOFF`, losses hit the NPC bank's CET1); player-lender loans, 15 contracts, ClearingDebt and court judgments are untouched; deposits are left to 03's estate rules; the profile records `bankruptcyTick`; margin and NPC credit are blocked until `up.players.freshStartUntil` (14 real days), checked by every credit intent (`BNK_LOCKOUT`) and by 07B margin.
10. Exploits: Y14 loan stacking (shared bureau, global DSR); Y19 bureau gaming (hist is a ratio; leverage and income dominate); Y20 (0% P2P flagged by 15, reported here as ordinary debt).

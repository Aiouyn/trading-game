## Goal
One liquidation engine for every forced sale or purchase of 07 (margin accounts, repo defaults, securities-lending buy-ins, Lombard and loan collateral, PB, CCP hedging, bank fire sales, sweeps): it slices each line to at most 20% of the expected tick volume, prices it inside a fair-value band that widens 1% per unfilled tick up to 10%, waits for the opening auction in closed markets and for reopening when halted, and charges the 1% penalty where the source says so. Liquidation never trades at a crashed print (07 §1 law 3, D131, Y8). On top of it, collateral enforcement of defaulted loans (07 §3.3.5) realises each pledge in lien order, applies proceeds fees → penalty → interest → principal, returns any surplus, and turns a shortfall into a judgment debt that an NPC lender's bankruptcy fresh start discharges and a player lender's does not.

## Implementation steps
1. Engine state: one `LiquidationJob` row per job (`bnk.liq`), lines per asset; status ACTIVE / HALTED_WAIT / DONE. `stepJobs` runs in **P7** each tick in jobId order: (a) read `fillsOfTick(tick − 1)` filtered to `sourceId = jobId` (slices written in P7 of tick N are read by 05 in P6 of N+1, and their fills are visible from P7 of N+1, 07#42) and record them in `bnk.liq_fill`, decrementing `qtyRemaining`; a line whose slice did not fill fully increments `unfilledTicks`, a fully filled slice resets it to 0; (b) if a planner is registered for the source, replace the lines with its plan (07B margin: close in `closeOrder` order until `IM(remaining) ≤ equity / 1.1`, or everything if equity ≤ 0); (c) for each line with remaining qty: trading state from 05: HALTED → status HALTED_WAIT and no order (07 §8 edge 14, grace and fees continue for buy-ins); CLOSED (session closed) → an `auctionOnly` order for the next opening auction with `openingSlice`; open → `sliceQty` with `liq_max_participation`; (d) fair value = registered `FairValueFn` or `markPrice` (06A band-clamped mark, never the last print); band = `bandAt(band0(σ_tick), unfilledTicks)` with `σ_tick = sigmaDailyOf / √720`; limit = `limitPrice`, converted with `toPrice`; (e) write the slice into the job (`pendingTick = tick`) so `liquidationOrders(s, tick)` returns `ExternalOrder { sourceId: jobId, pool: 'LIQUIDATION', assetId, side, qty, limitPrice, tif: 'TICK', auctionOnly?, accountId: ownerId }` sorted by (jobId, assetId).
2. Penalty: for jobs with `penaltyTo` set, each tick's filled notional × `ccp_liquidation_penalty.rate` (1%) is paid owner → penaltyTo with `CCP_LIQUIDATION_PENALTY` (TRANSFER; retail → TCH retail insurance fund, client → its clearing member, CRY → product insurance fund; 07B sets `penaltyTo`). 07A sources (ENFORCEMENT, REPO, SL_BUYIN, LOMBARD, BANK_SALE, SWEEP, PB) set `penaltyTo` null except PB (the PB is the member: penalty to the PB).
3. Proceeds: fills settle in the owner's account (05); the job's `proceedsTo` callback is dispatched by source: ENFORCEMENT → `onCollateralProceeds`; REPO → T07A-15; SL_BUYIN → T07A-16; MARGIN/CCP_* → 07B. Event `bank.liquidation.order` PARTIES per job per tick with `{ jobId, assetId, side, qty, limit, fair, band }`; when a job finishes, an aggregate count goes to the systemic snapshot (T07A-19/07B).
4. Multiple simultaneous liquidations are independent jobs; together they may still move prices (intended cascade, 07 §3.16.4 last paragraph).
5. Enforcement (07 §3.3.5), `startEnforcement` in P8 of the tick after DEFAULTED (T07A-09 calls it): for each active pledge of the loan sorted by `lienRank` then pledgeId:
   - SECURITY: `openJob({ source: 'ENFORCEMENT', ownerId: pledgor, lines: [{ assetId, side: 'SELL', qtyRemaining: qty }], closeAll: true, penaltyTo: null, proceedsTo: lender })`.
   - PROPERTY: `ctx.send(propId, 'prop.forcedSale', { propId, discount: 0.10, beneficiaryId: lender, loanId, pledgeId }, 'NEXT')` (10 sells instantly to an NPC fund at −10% of appraisal and reports `bank.collateral.proceeds` back); absent 10 → no properties exist.
   - BUSINESS: if the loan contract (`contractId` clause from 15) says transfer → `ctx.send(bizId, 'biz.transferToLender', …)`, else `ctx.send(bizId, 'biz.forcedSale', { priceFrac: 0.85, beneficiaryId: lender, … })` (09 sells to an NPC buyer at 85% of EV).
   - COLLECTIBLE: if the next sale of the item's category starts within 2 game days → `ast.collect.forcedConsign` with reserve 60% of appraisal; else `ast.collect.forcedDealerSale` at 70% (06 §3.18.4).
   - CASH: set off immediately (pay the pledged amount pledgor → lender, `BNK_COLLATERAL_SEIZE`).
   - RECEIVABLES: each day close `sweepReceivables` pays `frac × max(0, incomeDaily)` of the pledgor to the lender until repaid.
   Edge 12: a court seizure (13) of a pledged asset does not cancel the lien; the secured party is paid first and the court gets the surplus (route the surplus to `ctx.send(courtId, 'law.seizure.surplus', …)` when a 13 seizure fact exists for the asset).
6. `onCollateralProceeds`: `applyPayment` order with the 1% enforcement fee first (`BNK_ENFORCEMENT_FEE`, borrower → lender, TRANSFER), then penalty and interest (`BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`), then principal (`BNK_COLLATERAL_PROCEEDS_APPLY`, CREDIT_DESTROY); release the pledge; emit `bank.collateral.enforced` PARTIES `{ asset, proceeds, shortfall }`.
7. `finishEnforcement` when every pledge is realised: surplus (proceeds beyond everything owed) stays with the borrower (no posting needed when proceeds were paid only up to the debt); remaining shortfall → unsecured claim: lender NPC → judgment row with `survivesBankruptcy = false`; lender a player bank or a 15 P2P contract → `true`; both register a 03 obligation kind `JUDGMENT` (03 applies its 30%-of-daily-income garnishment plan). The unrecovered principal is written off: `BNK_LOAN_CHARGEOFF` (provision → loan, CREDIT_DESTROY, 07 §3.3.7), loan status WRITTEN_OFF; `reportDefault` to the bureau.
8. `dischargeOnFreshStart`: marks the character's judgments with `survivesBankruptcy = false` discharged and cancels their obligations; others stay (07#12; ROADMAP 2.6 via 07 §3.3.5).
9. Edge 3 (07 §8): when the lender bank fails, the loan and its enforcement move with the loan to the acquirer or liquidator (T07A-13 reassigns `lenderId`; jobs keep running with the new `proceedsTo`). Edge 11: a delisted or defaulted pledged security is revalued at its new mark (06) and enforcement sells at that mark's band.

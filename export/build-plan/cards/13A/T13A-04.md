## Goal
Money owed under a sanction is collected without ever pushing a character below the floor (no permanent ruin). Anything that cannot be collected becomes an interest-free garnishment of 30% of daily income. Victims are not left waiting: the Victim Compensation Fund advances up to half of an unpaid award at once.

## Imports
- T13A-03 obligations. T03-09 postings and holds. T03-17 liquid valuation. T03-26 `protectedSet`, `GARNISH_TAGS`, `PROTECTED_FLOOR_REAL`, `liquidMoney`.

## Exports
```ts
export function collect(ctx: PhaseCtx, sanctionId: Id): { collected: Money; planId: Id | null };   // P11 at application
export function runGarnishment(ctx: PhaseCtx): number;     // DAY_CLOSE, after 03 income totals
export function floorRoom(read: StoreRead, chrId: Id, tick: Tick): { cash: Money; liquidPositions: Money; businesses: Money };

```

## Implementation steps
1. Priority: restitution and victim disgorgement first, then the fine, then no-victim disgorgement (13 §3.9).
2. Sources in order (13 §3.12): unrestricted cash; then liquid positions sold at fair value by a sell request to the owning module (05/06; the result arrives next tick); then businesses at −15% (`LAW_ASSET_SEIZURE` / divestiture to 09). The protected set from T03-26 (`$5,000 liquid + one T1 business`) is never touched, and restricted cash lots (T03-12) are excluded.
3. Remainder → `GarnishmentPlanRow`; `runGarnishment` takes `garnishment_rate` × the day's garnishable income (T03-26 `GARNISH_TAGS`) and posts `LAW_GARNISHMENT`. No interest ever. 13A plans come before 03 persistent claims, and the combined take is ≤ max(garnishment_rate, comeback_garnish_rate) (open question 2).
4. VCF: when a victim's restitution goes into a plan, advance `vcf_advance_pct` × unpaid (bounded by the VCF balance) by `LAW_VICTIM_FUND_PAYOUT`. Later garnishment for that plan repays the fund first, up to the advance, then the victim.
5. A Fresh Start (14A) or personal insolvency (03) does not discharge plans; 03 moves them to persistent claims (T03-26) and 13A marks the plan `transferredTo03`.

## Test plan
  - **13#9** property test (5k random characters and judgments): after collection, liquid ≥ `protected_floor_cash_real` = $7,300 × MSI (E-45; was $5,000) and one T1 business remains when the character had one; any remainder becomes a plan at 30% with interest 0.
  - **13#10** the advance is ≤ 50% of the unpaid award and ≤ the VCF balance; garnishment repays the VCF before the victim, and the victim + VCF totals equal the award when the plan is done.
  - **AC-13A-04-1** cash is used before positions, and positions before businesses; the business sale haircut is 15% (SINK part) at fair value.
  - **AC-13A-04-2** a character with $4,000 cash and no business pays nothing now; the full amount becomes a plan.
  - **AC-13A-04-DIVEST** (E-45, E-31c) a business seized at fair value 1,000,000 sells at 850,000; the 150,000 haircut is a TRANSFER to the buyer (NPC sector when the buyer is NPC), not a SINK; Σ money unchanged.
  - **AC-13A-04-3** (INV-LAW-002) after every P11 and DAY_CLOSE, no character with an active 13A obligation is below the floor because of a 13A posting.
  - **AC-13A-04-4** the garnish on a day with zero income is 0, not a balance draw.
  - **Exploit notes** §14 #3 (spending to the floor before trial): the fine stays large (T13A-02); collection stops at the floor and the rest garnishes. Moving cash to an alt is the 14A Fresh Start clawback / 15 linking concern and is not undone here (flagged).
  - **AC-13A-04-OVERLAP** (E-23): a character with a 13A plan (owed $10,000) and a 03 persistent claim (owed $10,000) earns $1,000 garnishable income in a day. With garnishment_rate 0.30 and comeback_garnish_rate 0.30, the total take is $300 (not $600), and all of it goes to the 13A plan. Once the plan is paid, the claim gets the rest of the $300. With rates 0.30 and 0.40, the take is $400, with $300 to the plan first.

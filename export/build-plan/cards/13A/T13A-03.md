## Goal
The single entry point by which a sanction comes into existence. It records rows, queues postings, updates chapter counters and dispatches non-money effects to their owners. Every fine is split 85% destroyed, 10% Victim Compensation Fund and 5% whistle pool, and the split always sums to the fine.

## Imports
- T13A-01 tables and codes; T13A-02 `computeFine`. T03-09 `LedgerApi.post`. T03-17 `nwAt`. T14A-07 `onSanction` (licence SUSPEND/REVOKE/LIFT).

## Exports
```ts
export interface SanctionOrder { caseId: Id; chrId: Id; severity: Severity; kinds: SanctionKind[]; offenseTick: Tick;
  victimId?: Id; provenLoss?: Money; gains?: Money; quantum: boolean; plea: boolean; targetRefId?: Id; source: '13B'|'ADM' }
```

## Implementation steps
1. Validate: chrId exists and is not ANONYMIZED; severity allowed for each kind (13 §3.9: LICENSE_SUSPEND S5; OP_BAN S3+ criminal; HELD S5–S6 or third S4); if `o.victimId` is linked to `o.chrId` (ports) → `LAW_LINKED_VOID` (13B also voids; this is defence in depth).
2. Amounts: restitution = 1.5×/2× proven loss by tier (S3 1.5, S4 2, S5 1.5, S6 2), or `statutoryMinimumRestitution` when `!quantum`. Disgorgement = gains (S4 and above). Fine from `computeFine` with `nwAt(offenseTick)`, `nwAt(tick)` and the chapter row.
3. Order (13 §3.9 "paid before fines"): restitution and disgorgement obligations rank ahead of the fine in T13A-04 collection.
4. `splitFine`: whistle = floor(amount × 5%), vcf = floor(amount × 10%), sink = amount − vcf − whistle (the remainder goes to the sink, so the sum is exact).
5. Postings are created as obligations (rows), not immediate debits. T13A-04 collects and posts with codes: fine → `LAW_FINE_SINK` / `LAW_FINE_VICTIM_FUND` / `LAW_FINE_WHISTLE_POOL`; restitution → `LAW_RESTITUTION` (TRANSFER to victim); disgorgement → `LAW_DISGORGEMENT_VICTIM` if a victim exists, else `LAW_DISGORGEMENT_NO_VICTIM` (SINK).
6. Non-money kinds: LICENSE_SUSPEND → T14A-07 `onSanction` SUSPEND for 7 real days; OP_BAN → `opBanTable.untilTick = max(existing, tick + OP_BAN_TICKS[sev])`; HELD → T13A-05 `scheduleHeld`; DIVESTITURE and MODEL_DESTRUCTION → message to the owning module (open question 8); PUBLIC_RECORD → `life.reputation.event { kind: 'CONVICTION', severity }` to 14B.
7. Chapter row: convictions += 1, s4Count += 1 if S4, finesTotal += fine. A third S4 in the chapter adds HELD with reason THIRD_S4.
8. Emit `law.sanction_applied` per row.

## Test plan
  - **13#4** for 1,000 random fines, sink + vcf + whistle == fine exactly, with each part within 1 micro-unit of 85/10/5; posted codes are the three fine codes.
  - **13#5** restitution and identifiable-victim disgorgement post TRANSFER (`LAW_RESTITUTION`, `LAW_DISGORGEMENT_VICTIM`); disgorgement with no victim posts SINK `LAW_DISGORGEMENT_NO_VICTIM`. Court and NPC legal fees are 13B's; this test asserts that their codes are SINK in the catalog.
  - **AC-13A-03-1** a second S4 in a chapter does not add HELD; the third does (reason THIRD_S4).
  - **AC-13A-03-2** LICENSE_SUSPEND reaches T14A-07 and the licence is SUSPENDED for 7 × 17,280 ticks.
  - **AC-13A-03-3** OP_BAN never shortens an existing ban.
  - **AC-13A-03-4** `liftSanction` (appeal won) decrements the chapter counters, ends Held/op ban/suspension early, and refunds collected fine parts by reversal postings with the same codes (open question 4).
  - **AC-13A-03-5** an order whose victim is linked to the defendant is refused `LAW_LINKED_VOID` and writes nothing.
  - **Exploit notes** §15 #3 fake suits between friends to move money: linked victim refused here too. Split rounding cannot leak money because the remainder always goes to the sink.
  - **AC-13A-03-REVERSE** (E-24; Designer cleared, E-33): `liftSanction` refunds all collected fine parts (sink, VCF and whistle shares reversed with the same codes; restitution already paid to the victim is returned only if the sanction was restitution). It leaves served Held ticks counted in `heldTicks`, and decrements convictions and s4Count. Test: the chapter counters before the conviction equal the counters after the reversal, except `heldTicks`.

The human side of conflict: staff who are paid and treated well are hard to turn, moles must win a real job, and defenders can bait thieves with honeypots and canaries that turn theft into evidence without ever baiting an honest licensee.

## Implementation steps
1. Loyalty `clamp(40 + 40·(w/mw − 1) + 10·perks + 0.5·tenure + 10·[rep ≥ 60] − 15·[convicted], 0, 100)` [Quant].
2. Counter-intel costs [Quant]: vetting $3k/hire, loyalty program 5% payroll (+10), compartmentalization −5% efficiency, exit clause $1k/departure, canary briefing $10k (+40 evidence if traded on).
3. Honeypots $30k/$120k/$400k with poison 0.20/0.40/0.60; at most 1 canary per 3 datasets; honeypots are visible only to C01 survey and 15 refuses to license them (`HONEYPOT_LICENSE`).
4. NPC employers raise wages and perks when loyalty < 40 (§6.4, via 16 decision port).

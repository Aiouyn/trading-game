## Goal
Theft moves money, it never creates it: every character-to-character take is a TRANSFER capped by the victim's liquidity and net worth, punching down is taxed, and NPC-crew theft and the fence haircut leave the economy as SINKs.

## Imports
- T03-06 payment rail; T03-17 net worth (r computed at loot time). T12-04 payloads. T11-?? compute runs (leeched compute usable only on the attacker's own runs).

## Implementation steps
1. Cap before r-scaling (INV-CON-004 checks the pre-scale value); r > 10: loot ×(10/r)^0.5, noise +10·log10(r/10), damages ×min(3, 1 + log10(r/10)); r < 0.1: cost −20%, Climber board credit. [Quant: 10, 0.1, 0.10, 0.02 spec constants]
2. Non-cash loot (data, weights, compute) is not r-scaled.
3. All math in number, one `toMoney` half-even per posting.
4. Fence (E-46, Producer): `OPS_FENCE_SALE_NPC` SWAP_ASSET TRANSFER: the npcFirm sector pays roundHalfEven(0.40 × value) and receives the item; no haircut posting, no SINK, no FAUCET.

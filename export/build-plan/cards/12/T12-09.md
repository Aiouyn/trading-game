## Goal
One gate that every op passes at plan, launch and resolve, so nobody can be farmed, dogpiled, ambushed as a rookie or hit while protected, and every attempt, success or failure, leaves evidence. This card also carries the G15–G22 suite: the anti-grief and anti-exploit checks of 12 §10 tests 15–22, re-run here end to end through the gate.

## Imports
- T13A-06 op ban and accuser lock; T14A-08 protection, Rookie zone, dormancy, presence; T15A-02 linked accounts. T12-04 caps, T12-05 disconnect fee, T12-06 loot caps.

## Implementation steps
1. Gate order (stable reject precedence): `OP_BANNED` → `ACCUSER_LOCK` → `TARGET_DORMANT` → `TARGET_PROTECTED` (72 h window) → `TARGET_ROOKIE` (Rookie zone unless r ≤ 10) → `PAIR_COOLDOWN` / `PAIR_ATTEMPTS` → `DOGPILE_CAP` → session caps (T12-04).
2. Same gate at RESOLVE (INV-CON-005 fires if any op resolves against a protected state).
3. Third success in 24 h → automatic 12 h shield; any detected success → shield.
4. Every op writes evidence: failures U{5..15} on `conflict` stream, successes the §3.9 noise, disconnects the §3.7.7 table.
5. An attacker inside their own 72 h window loses protection on their first op (and on a non-contract lawsuit, via 13).

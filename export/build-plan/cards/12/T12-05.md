The victim's view is a fair puzzle: every account sees decoys, the list never marks hostility, and the tell is the same entry named repeatedly. Scans by defense tier are the honest way to find sessions, and they never flag a legitimate entry.

## Implementation steps
1. Clue and decoy draws on `conflict` stream keyed by owner; OFF_HOURS_HANDSHAKE names the hostile entry if real, a fresh random legit entry if a decoy; SLOWDOWN and ODD_USAGE name nothing.
2. Serialization: one serializer for both sources; timestamps at game-hour precision; `real` and `sessionId` stay server-side.
3. Disconnecting a hostile session writes evidence +20/+28/+35 (+10 if flagged) [Quant]; a legit disconnect fires the contract's termination clause.
4. Lockdown shield blocks new plants for 12 real hours = 8,640 ticks.

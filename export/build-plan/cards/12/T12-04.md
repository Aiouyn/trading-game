## Implementation steps
1. Linked attacker–victim (or any joint participant linked, §8 #7): session VOIDED on plant, P stays 0, S4 laundering flag via the 13 evidence port; residue still ties any student.
2. Offline stretch: `offlineStretchGain` reset on presence; once it reaches 0.08 further offline accrual adds nothing to P (h_eff keeps counting for audit only).
3. Victim INACTIVE (14 real days) → FROZEN; dead or bankrupt → VOIDED (§8 #5).
4. Tier caps T0–T1 1, T2 2, T3 3, T4 4, T5 5; pair cooldown 24 real hours = 17,280 ticks after disconnect.
5. COMPUTE drain counts toward the 20% victim drain cap; an over-cap plant is refused with `SESSION_CAP_VICTIM`.

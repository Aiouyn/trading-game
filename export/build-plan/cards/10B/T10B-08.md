## Goal
Run a project from SITE_PREP to COMPLETE. The budget comes from base cost, the city multiplier and an input index that tracks wages, CPI and commodities; the schedule from type and size; each phase draws an overrun and maybe a delay by contractor grade; money flows equity first, then construction loan, then limited presale deposits through a project escrow; at completion the building, units, appraisal and term-loan conversion appear.

## Implementation steps
1. Budget lines per §3.11.4: hard, soft 10%, contractor fee, contingency 0–15% (unused returns at completion), financing estimate, projected value.
2. Schedule: `dev_days_<type>` × size factor; CONVERSION 0.5×, RENOVATION_MAJOR 0.25×; 1 GD = 720 ticks; LEASE_UP until stabilized or 91 GD.
3. Overrun and delay at each phase start from stream `re.dev:<projectId>:<phase>` in that order (overrun draw first, delay Bernoulli second, delay size third). Commodity spike mid-phase (§8 edge 5) applies only at the next phase start. Realized overruns SEALED until phase end, then PARTIES.
4. Financing order: equity, construction loan (LTC ≤ 0.60, interest capitalised monthly), presale deposits (only STRUCTURE and FIT_OUT, ≤ 50% of deposits). Phase cost into project escrow at phase start, paid per game hour (`PROP_DEVELOPMENT_CAPEX` SINK to NPC; `_B` TRANSFER to player contractor, 60% materials share SINK unless it owns supply businesses). A player contractor without free m² capacity cannot start.
5. Presales: strata only, ≤ 70% of units, price ≤ 0.95 × projected unit value, 10% deposit in escrow. NPC buyers fail financing in a crunch → deposit forfeit to developer, unit back to inventory.
6. Completion: building and units created, SYSTEM appraisal, presales close, construction loan converts or must be repaid within 28 GD, district supply and `capexMomentum91` rise by the hard cost, `dev.completed` PUBLIC transition.
7. Dormant owner (§8 edge 19): funded phases continue; unfunded next phase pauses 28 GD then stalls (T10B-09).

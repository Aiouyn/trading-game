## Goal
Everything before a shovel goes in. Who may build what size, where the land comes from (weekly sealed-bid land auctions, buying buildings, assembling up to 6 contiguous parcels), and whether the city approves it: by-right permits are near certain and fast, variances and rezonings are uncertain and slower, neighbours may object within limits, and rejections may be appealed. Heritage districts lock demolition and height.

## Implementation steps
1. Land auction proceeds: `PROP_LAND_AUCTION`, TRANSFER to the land authority treasury (E-9; Producer ruling E-39). Bids held in escrow until close. §8 edge 16: no bid → relist next week at 10% lower reserve, floor 50% of genesis; land appraises at ≥ 5% of genesis.
2. Assembly ≤ 6 contiguous parcels in one block; lowest parcel id is the primary address.
3. Permits: by right `permit_days_by_right` (2 GD ≤ 5,000 m², else 5), fee 0.4% SINK, p 0.98 (ACCESS only, deterministic, shown before submission); variance 7 GD, 1%; rezoning 14 GD, 2%; pApprove formulas per §3.11.3 shown before submission. Decision draw from `re.permit:<permitId>`. Decision clock starts when the application leaves the FIFO queue.
4. Objections: objector must own a prop within 150 m, fee $5,000 (`PROP_OBJECTION_FEE`, TRANSFER to the city government, E-39 [Quant] confirm), one per project, −0.05 each, total ≥ −0.20.
5. Appeal (`prop.dev.appeal`, 10 §4, E-39): $25k + 0.2% (`PROP_PLANNING_APPEAL_FEE`, SINK, E-39 [Quant] confirm), 7 GD, p 0.30 for variance-math rejections, 0 for heritage.
6. Heritage (§3.11.7): no demolition of buildings older than 50 GY at genesis; height capped; no variance or rezoning above caps; facade from heritage archetype set.

## Goal
One atomic settlement for every property sale: buyer cash and mortgage proceeds in, title out, leases and their deposits transferred, any existing mortgage paid from proceeds first, costs and duty posted, seller paid the rest. P2P exchange trades add 15's fees. Only SYSTEM appraisals count for net worth and LTV, and players cannot appraise or broker inside their own owner chain.

## Implementation steps
1. Order inside P13 (one batch, all-or-nothing): buyer pays price (from cash plus `BankPort10.originate` proceeds); buyer pays `transaction_cost` 3% (`RE_TXN_COST` → `PROP_TXN_COST_NPC`, TRANSFER to the NPC frm sector account, E-39 amendment; or TRANSFER to a hired player brokerage) and stamp duty 1% (`TAX_STAMP_DUTY` TRANSFER to bloc `gov`); P2P adds 2% tax and 0.5% fee via 15; existing liens paid in rank order (unless `mortgage_assumable` and the lender allows); seller receives the rest; title, leases and deposit escrow move.
2. Seller and buyer kinds choose the code: NPC pool → player `PROP_PURCHASE_FROM_ELASTIC`; player → NPC buyer `PROP_SALE_TO_ELASTIC`; player → player `PROP_SALE_P`.
3. Cross-bloc: cash held in escrow for the §3.2.4 delay; price fixed at intent time in local currency (§9 exploit 11). Capital controls (§8 edge 6): proceeds cannot leave the bloc.
4. Appraisal integrity: only SYSTEM appraisals feed NW and LTV; player appraisals are stored with the appraiser id (13 evidence).

## Goal
How properties come to market and how fast they sell. NPC landlords list a small daily share of pooled stock, players list on the open market, NPC buyers arrive with a hazard that falls steeply as the ask rises over appraisal, and an owner in a hurry can sell instantly to the city's NPC fund at a discount within a daily capacity.

## Imports
- T10A-04: `carve` (a pooled listing becomes a real prop only when bought), pool stock per district.
- T10A-05: `appraise`, district `stress`, tier.
- T10A-07: `registerProp`.
- T10B-01: tables, `BankPort10.cB` (credit freeze).

## Implementation steps
1. NPC listing count: `floor(rate·stock + carry)`, carry keeps the fraction per district (deterministic). Rate ×2 for 28 GD in a new city, ×0.5 when `stress > 0.6`. Ask uniform draw from stream `re.list:<cityId>` (one draw per listing, in district then sequence order).
2. Hazard: `liquidityMult = (1 + 2·stress)·(1 + max(0, −c_b))·tierMult` (working 0.8, mid 1.0, prime 1.3, trophy 2.0, unique 4.0). Draw per listing per game hour from `re.buyer:<cityId>`. Credit freeze `c_b < −0.6`: a second draw fails half the arrivals (counted as a viewing, shown to the seller). An arriving buyer pays the ask.
3. §8 edges 1 and 2: first valid `buyListing` by P0 sequence wins, later ones `LISTING_SOLD`; an accepted player offer in P1 cancels that hour's NPC arrival (hazard runs in P9).
4. Offers: `expiresInTicks ≤ 2,160`; NPC sellers decide at the next game hour with `npcAutoAccept`.
5. Instant sale: quote, `minPrice ≤ quote` else `PRICE_BELOW_MIN`; capacity `instant_sale_fund_capacity_share × NPC pooled value` per day; excess queues to the next day; forced sales (T10B-05) jump the queue. Instant and forced sales are excluded from the comps index (§9 exploit 6; flag passed to T10A-05).

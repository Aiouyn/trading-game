## Goal
Borrowers can repay early and refinance, but cash-out refinancing cannot be looped on inflated marks. The card also supplies the daily risk fields 08 needs to pool mortgages, the REIT NAV anchor 05 needs, and the per-property exposure 08 needs to insure.

## Imports

## Implementation steps
1. Penalty to lender (`BNK_LOAN_FEE`). Refinance: new loan proceeds pay the old loan in the same P13 batch.
2. Cash-out: rejected `SEASONING` within 28 GD (20,160 ticks) of purchase or last cash-out; appraisal capped at last × 1.10.
3. Risk feed fields per §3.10.8; household mortgage defaults are 07/16's, from 10's price index.
4. REIT NAV at day close supplied to 05; dividends 90% of attributable pooled NOI (05 pays).

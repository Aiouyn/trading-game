## Goal
The stable contract of unit 10B: the property-specific mortgage extension on 07's `Loan`, development projects with budgets, permits and presales, listings and offers, land auctions, and the city demand inputs. It fixes ids, binds every 10B reason code to a 03 catalog row and declares the ports to 07, 08, 09, 13, 15 and 16. Every later 10B card imports from here.

## Imports
- T10A-01: `Id`, `Money`, `Ccy`, `Tick`, `PropertyRow`, `ParcelRow`, `BuildingRow`, `UnitRow`, `PropertyType`, `UseClass`, `assertEntityId`, id helpers (`propId`, `parcelId`, `buildingId`, `unitId`, `leaseId`).
- T07A-01: `Loan` (07's record; the loan id is `lon_` per E-26), `LoanKind`.
- T03-01 / T03-05: `Money` bigint micro, `toMoney` (half-even), catalog lookup.

## Implementation steps
1. Transcribe §2.5–§2.7 and §2.9. Money fields are bigint micro; rates and probabilities are numbers.
2. Ids: loans keep 07's `lon_`; properties `prop_`, parcels `pcl_`, buildings `bld_`, units `unt_`, leases `lse_` (approved E-26). The spec's `dev_` and `lst_` plus new `ofr_`, `prm_`, `psl_`, `lau_`, `mqt_`, `obj_` all pass the E-20 regex and are on the approved E-26 list and the lint allow-list (E-39). Ids are created only through `ID10B`.
3. `CODES10B` (each test asserts the catalog row and class):
   - `RE_LAND_SALE` → `PROP_LAND_AUCTION` (TRANSFER to the land authority treasury, E-9; Producer ruling E-39).
   - `RE_PERMIT_FEE` → `PROP_PERMIT_FEE` (SINK). `RE_CONSTRUCTION_PAY` → `PROP_DEVELOPMENT_CAPEX` (SINK, NPC contractor) or `PROP_DEVELOPMENT_CAPEX_B` (player contractor).
   - Purchases from NPC pools → `PROP_PURCHASE_FROM_ELASTIC` (SINK); sales to NPC buyers or the NPC fund → `PROP_SALE_TO_ELASTIC` (FAUCET, catalog row); player-to-player → `PROP_SALE_P`.
   - `RE_STAMP_DUTY` → `TAX_STAMP_DUTY` (E-26 alias).
   - `RE_MORTGAGE_INTEREST` → 07 `BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`; `RE_MORTGAGE_FEE`, `RE_PREPAY_FEE`, late fee → `BNK_LOAN_FEE`; principal → `BNK_MORTGAGE_REPAY` (CREDIT_DESTROY); origination → `BNK_MORTGAGE_DRAWDOWN` (CREDIT_CREATE).
   - E-39 rows (class and rate [Quant] confirm): `RE_TXN_COST` → `PROP_TXN_COST_NPC` (TRANSFER to frm, 3%, NPC brokerage); foreclosure cost → `PROP_FORECLOSURE_COST_NPC` (TRANSFER to frm, 2%); pause site security → `PROP_SITE_SECURITY_NPC` (TRANSFER to frm, 0.1% of hard cost per GD); objection fee → `PROP_OBJECTION_FEE` (TRANSFER to the city government, $5,000); planning appeal fee → `PROP_PLANNING_APPEAL_FEE` (SINK, $25k + 0.2%). No null bindings remain.
4. No logic beyond construction and validation.

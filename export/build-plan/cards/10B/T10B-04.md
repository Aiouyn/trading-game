## Implementation steps
1. Max LTV per `max_ltv_<product>` (RES 0.80, COMM 0.65, CONSTRUCTION 0.60 LTC, BRIDGE 0.70, SECOND_LIEN 0.85 combined, RES only); multiply by `1 + 0.25·min(0, c_b)`. 07's large-exposure cap per lender applies (07 check).
2. Spread = base + `0.02·max(0, LTV − 0.60)/0.20` + `0.01·max(0, 700 − score)/100` + `0.005·[working tier]` + lender adjustment from 07. Coefficients are spec values, not tuned here.
3. Schedule: `r = rate·7/364`, `n = termGD/7`; annuity via 07's `annuityPayment`, converted with `toMoney` half-even once; final payment absorbs rounding. First payment 7 GD after funding. Origination fee 0.5% (`BNK_LOAN_FEE`).
4. Interest accrues at day close `balance·rate/364`. Principal is CREDIT_DESTROY (07 posts).

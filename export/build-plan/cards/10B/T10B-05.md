## Goal
When property values fall, mortgages must bite without starting a fire sale. Weekly SYSTEM appraisals and payment dates test LTV and DSCR; a breach starts a 3-GD cure; an uncured loan forecloses with a lender listing and then a sale to the NPC fund at a fair-value band; a city's forced-sale volume above 1% per GD goes to a 07 bad bank so a cascade cannot exhaust buyers. Recourse rules decide who carries any shortfall.

## Implementation steps
1. Covenant: `LTV_now = all liens / SYSTEM appraisal`; breach above origination max + `ltv_covenant_buffer` (RES 0.90, COMM 0.75). DSCR trailing 91 GD < `dscr_covenant` for income props. Construction loans test LTC at each phase start (§8 edge 4: breach blocks the next phase, no foreclosure mid-phase unless payments are missed).
2. Cure: deadline `now + cure_period_ticks` (2,160); each cure event PARTIES; Held owner (§8 edge 3) may still pay and the deadline extends by the Held duration up to 24 h.
3. Foreclosure: frozen; lender listing at 0.95 × appraisal for `foreclosure_auction_ticks`; unsold → `forcedSale` at the band price (never the last distressed print). Court freeze (§8 edge 12) suspends the clock, interest continues.
4. Proceeds: 2% cost (`PROP_FORECLOSURE_COST_NPC`, TRANSFER to frm, E-39 [Quant] confirm), lien 1, lien 2, borrower. Each lender receives through 07A `onCollateralProceeds`.
5. Recourse per §3.10.6; collection respects 13's floor ($5,000 + one T1 business). Events: `mortgage.foreclosure` PARTIES and anonymous PUBLIC; 13 S1 civil default record; 14 Reputation −3 if the borrower could have cured, else −1.

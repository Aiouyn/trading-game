## Implementation steps
1. `capacity = floor(activeChars/200)` per partition; priority = harm × evidence; tips jump the queue; when full, a higher-priority item preempts the lowest, which re-queues (§14 #9).
2. ECA: score ≥ 60 for 3 game days → inquiry (10–20 evidence); ≥ 80, or ≥ 60 plus another source → +5/day; ≥ 55 evidence → tribunal or consent decree.
3. Revenue: `p_audit = 0.02·(1+min(3, spend/income))·(1+0.5·tips)` per game quarter; a clean audit gives +2 Rep; unexplained wealth adds 20–40 evidence to triples funded by illicit codes.
4. CMA: city-sector share > 60% → case; remedy fine S5 or divestiture.
5. Whistle: reward = min(5% fine, $50M × MSI); 3 false reports in a chapter → 7-day lock.
6. NPC regulators take over S5–S6 cyber files (§11).
- **E-47 regulator scrutiny.** Each regulator computes `scrutinyScore` per friction owner group from its 5-game-day share of market volume (05). It only lengthens approvals and reviews (antitrust, merger, licence, listing) via `scrutinyDelay` [Quant]. It is never an input to fines, freezes, evidence, case priority or verdicts. Cause string `why.topend.scrutiny`.

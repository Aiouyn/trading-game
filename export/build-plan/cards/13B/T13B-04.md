## Goal
The fee and filing decisions: who may file where, at what bar and deadline, and how every legal fee is coded (SINK to NPCs, TRANSFER to players).

## Imports
- T13B-02 `evidenceAt`. T03-09 postings with `LAW_COURT_FEE`, `LAW_NPC_LEGAL_FEE`, `LAW_LEGAL_FEE_B2B`, `LAW_ARBITRATION_FEE_B2B`.

## Exports
```ts
export function fileCase(ctx, i: FileCaseIntent): Id | Reject;      // LAW_BELOW_BAR, LAW_DEADLINE_PASSED
export function counselFee(grade: Grade, claim: Money, retainer: boolean): Money;
export function feeCode(firmId: Id | 'NPC'): 'LAW_NPC_LEGAL_FEE'|'LAW_LEGAL_FEE_B2B';
```

## Implementation steps
1. Bars: civil 40, tribunal 55, criminal 70 (a victim needs 70; regulators file for free); arbitration 40.
2. Deadlines (§3.13): civil 28 real days after detection, regulator 42 after the op, criminal any time in the chapter.
3. Court fee = max($500 × MSI, 1% claim), SINK.
4. Counsel fee = $5,000 × grade² + 0.5% claim; −20% with a 12 retainer; NPC firm SINK, player firm TRANSFER.
5. A criminal conviction on the same facts injects a 70-point source into the civil case; double restitution netted (§14 #4).
6. Evidence decaying below the bar disables filing again (§14 #1).

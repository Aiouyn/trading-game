## Goal
Discovering evidence costs money and time; the board is honest but imperfect; a poor victim can still sell the case; and abusing courts against innocents costs the filer.

## Implementation steps
1. Priors only from legitimate signals (§2.4); the real attacker is hidden until attributed.
2. Action costs and times come from the §4 table. Progress is set by Quant's formula (E-24; replaces the placeholder, no longer PENDING):
   - Each investigation tracks cumulative spend `S_i` (bigint micro, real dollars).
   - `B = INV_SQRT_BASE × MSI` at the posting tick.
   - `P_ppm(S) = min(CAP_ppm, GAIN_ppm × isqrt(S × 10^12 / B) / 10^6)`, using bigint floor `isqrt` and integer division, with no floats.
   - A new spend `s` gives progress `ΔP = P(S_i + s) − P(S_i)`, then `S_i += s`. Spend-only progress counts as evidence points against a goal of 100: `points = P_ppm / 10,000` (E-25).
   - A spend below `INV_SPEND_MIN × MSI` (default 1% of base) is rejected `LAW_SPEND_BELOW_MIN`, never rounded.
   - Keys (23 §4.12, E-25; all **PROVISIONAL** pending the sim gate: conviction rate on attributed ops 40–65%, detected-never-attributed 30–50%):
     - `INV_SQRT_BASE` = 50_000_000_000 micro × MSI
     - `INV_SQRT_GAIN_PPM` = 250_000
     - `INV_SQRT_CAP_PPM` = 1_000_000
     - `INV_SPEND_MIN` = 500_000_000 micro × MSI
   - Postings: Quant's default is a TRANSFER into the investigating authority's treasury. However, 13 §3.3/§11 classes fees to NPC law and PI firms as SINK (`LAW_NPC_LEGAL_FEE`) and fees to player firms as TRANSFER (`LAW_LEGAL_FEE_B2B`), so the V3 classing applies.
3. Revenge discount: −30% on investigation costs against an attacker convicted of hitting you, for 7 real days (§13).
4. The replay subpoena requires a FILED civil case.
5. Bounty: `law.set_bounty` escrows the amount; on conviction it is paid pro rata by points from sealed submissions.
6. Case selling: share clamped to [0.30, 0.50]; a linked assignee is voided; the victim cannot then file the same triple and venue (§14 #6).
7. Malicious prosecution (lost case and true attacker later convicted, or evidence < 25 at trial): filer pays the defendant's fees + 0.25% NW (SINK) and −5 Rep (14B event); 3 in a chapter → 7-day non-contract filing lock. Never applied when evidence ≥ 40 at filing.

## Goal
Fix the 13B shapes and the `law.*` intent surface (13 §9) once. Every intent goes through one common validator: rate limit, actor is self or under a mandate, Held gate (T13A-06), and linked-account voiding. Inputs from 12, 05, 08 and 15 arrive only through ports whose defaults are 'module not present'.

## Imports
- T13A-01 tables, `Severity`, `DefendantCaseFlagRow`; T13A-07 `services.law.gate`. T02-09 tables, T02-11 intent handler types. T03-01 ids.

## Implementation steps
1. Tables keyed for O(1) lookups by triple, case and defendant; case rows also write the 13A `defendantFlagTable` (op lock and leverage cap).
2. Intent kinds of 13 §9, each with a zod schema in `packages/protocol`; the rate limit is 20/tick (19 §3.9).
3. `commonLawValidate`: if any two parties of the intent are linked (ports) → the intent is accepted but VOIDED and flagged (`law.void_flag` to 15). Fees already due are still charged (§14 #7). Held → `LAW_HELD`, except `law.post_bond`, `law.offer_settlement`, `law.accept_settlement` and `law.appeal` (defensive acts; open question 1).
4. Defaults: with 12 absent there are no evidence sources, so nothing becomes fileable (INERT, see AC).

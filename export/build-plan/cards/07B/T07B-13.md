## Goal
Uncleared trades (TRS, XCCY, bilateral FX crosses, bilateral repo, PB OTC) carry real counterparty risk. This task makes a CSA mandatory for them, exchanges netted variation margin daily, holds bilateral IM segregated at TCH ESC when both parties are LIC4 or institutions, and on a default computes the close-out amount, nets it, applies collateral and records the remainder as an unsecured claim for 13 or bank resolution.

## Imports
- T07B-04: `imHvar` (10-day 99% option); T07B-14: `escrowHold`, `escrowRelease`.
- T07A-02: `eligible(cls, 'BILATERAL')`, `haircutFor`.
- T06B-??: `pvOf(s, tradeId)`, `rfqHalfSpread(s, tradeId)`, bilateral trade rows.

## Implementation steps
1. P1: uncleared trade without a CSA → `CCP_NO_CSA`; NPC dealers sign standard terms automatically.
2. VM at t = 719: net PV of all trades under the CSA; `transfer = (netPv − threshold) − held` when its absolute value ≥ MTA, else 0; collateral moves as pledges (T07A-02) with bilateral haircuts.
3. Bilateral IM when both LIC4/institution: `HVaR 99% × √10` (T07B-04 PB path with 10-day MPOR) or the schedule; posted into ESC, segregated, not re-usable (T07A-02 reuse forbidden).
4. Close-out (§3.18): for each trade, value = mid PV ± half the 06 RFQ spread (replacement cost to the non-defaulter: asset +half spread, liability −half spread); net across the CSA; subtract collateral held (VM pledges + released bilateral IM); collateral applied and any settled close-out amount post `CCP_BILATERAL_CLOSEOUT` (TRANSFER PAY, E-38); a positive remainder is a `T07B.claim` row (`source: 'CSA'`) handed to 13 (`up.law`) or bank resolution (07A).

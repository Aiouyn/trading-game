## Goal
Turn a close-out loss into exact postings, layer by layer, in the order 07 fixes, so no one pays while an earlier layer still has capacity (INV-CCP-002) and no loss ever reaches a non-member. Worst auction bidders lose their DF first (juniorization), assessments are capped per event and per 30-game-day window, and after the funded layers the service uses VM gains haircutting, partial tear-up and finally closure. Using steps 7 to 9 puts TCH into recovery.

- T03-06: `LedgerApi.post`.

## Implementation steps
1. Layers for DF services in order: DEFAULTER_IM (all its margin incl. client collateral of unported clients only up to their own losses), DEFAULTER_DF, SITG1 (TCH capital, `CCP_SKIN_IN_GAME_LOSS` REVALUE), SURVIVOR_DF (junior set first, each up to its full contribution, then the rest pro rata to contributions), SITG2, ASSESSMENT (pro rata to contributions, capped by `assessmentCap`), VMGH, TEAR_UP, then service closure.
2. Postings: survivor DF and assessments `CCP_WATERFALL_ALLOCATION` / `CCP_ASSESSMENT` (TRANSFER member → service account); defaulter layers consume pledged collateral via `CCP_LIQUIDATION_PROCEEDS`. All amounts bigint micro; pro rata uses floor plus largest remainder so every layer sums exactly.
3. VMGH: for up to 5 game days, members with net VM gains in the service receive them reduced pro rata to cover the remaining loss (applied inside `postVariationMargin` via a haircut factor per member). Tear-up: unauctioned defaulter positions cancelled against members' opposite positions at the last settlement price, pro rata by position. Closure: all positions terminated at last settlement, margin returned, service CLOSED until 13 and TCH reopen.
4. Recovery (§3.17.11): if VMGH, tear-up or closure is used, emit `bank.ccp.serviceStatus` RECOVERY; owner banks recapitalize by NPC decision (16 port; default: pro rata to ownership); the service may stay closed up to 3 game days; a permanent history record is written; if TCH capital is negative after all steps, owner banks absorb it pro rata (TRANSFER from their capital). No layer is public money: no FAUCET, no 04 capital (04 may lend liquidity only).
5. Replenishment (Producer ruling E-38; E-37 PROVISIONAL [Quant]): at tick d+720 the replenishment CALL is issued (new DF contributions through the T07B-09 path) and assessments are called; each member has 7 GD (5,040 ticks) from the call to pay in full (07 §3.17.5). DefaultEvent CLOSED when every call is paid or has passed its deadline. The 30-GD assessment cooling-off and 5-GD VMGH stay in game days.
6. `INV_CCP_002`: for every DefaultEvent's allocation record, no layer has amount > 0 while an earlier layer has unused capacity; no payer outside the service's members and TCH appears.

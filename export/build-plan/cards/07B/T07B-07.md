## Goal
When an account still has negative equity after every position is closed, someone must eat the loss now and the owner must owe it. The clearing member pays immediately (reimbursed by the TCH retail insurance fund for NPC brokers), and the owner gets a ClearingDebt in 07A's registry that blocks leverage but leaves a comeback path. The retail insurance fund is capped and swept weekly to SINK so penalties do not pile up as hoarded money.

## Implementation steps
1. `deficit = −equity` (micro). Retail or client account: the clearing member pays the deficit into the account at once (member → account, TRANSFER `CCP_DEFICIT_ABSORB`, E-38), bringing the account to 0. An owner who is itself a member has no member above it: the hole goes to the service default path (T07B-10).
2. NPC broker member: the retail insurance fund reimburses the member at once, `RETAIL_INS_FUND` → member, `CCP_DEFICIT_ABSORB` (E-38). If the fund is short, it pays what it has and the member keeps the rest (no FAUCET).
3. `createClearingDebt({ debtorId: owner, creditorId: ccp_TCH when the retail insurance fund reimbursed an NPC broker (one debt per creditor when the reimbursement was partial: TCH for the reimbursed part, the member for the rest), else the member, principal: deficit, ccy, refs: [accountId, jobId] })`. Rate ONR + 2% and repayment share 30% come from `ccp_clearing_debt_terms` and are 07A's.
4. Weekly sweep: `cap = round_half_even(retail_ins_fund_cap × retailMarginBalances)`; amount above cap → SINK with `CCP_INS_FUND_SWEEP` (the only SINK in 07B).
5. `canCloseAccount`: rejects `CCP_CLR_DEBT` when the owner has outstanding ClearingDebt unless the balance is moved to another own account (§8 edge 23).
6. Emit `bank.margin.deficit` PARTIES `{ accountId, deficit, debtId }` and add 1 to the PUBLIC count.

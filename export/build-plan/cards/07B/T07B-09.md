## Goal
Each Monday at t = 0 (P7) every DF service resizes its default fund so it covers the two worst member losses beyond their IM under stress (Cover-2), at least the service minimum, and splits it across members pro rata to IM with a $10M floor. TCH's own skin in the game (SITG1 and SITG2) follows the fund. Members short of their new contribution can only reduce risk until they post it.

## Implementation steps
1. Group members with affiliates (06/15 owner group) and include client accounts in the member's IM and loss.
2. `S_m = max(0, max_s (loss_s − IM_m))`; `DF = max(S_(1) + S_(2), DF_min × N_a)` with `n` from `ccp_default_fund_cover` (Cover-2 default); DF_min LD $2B, OTC $3B, CDS $1B, REPO $1B (from `ccp_<service>_*`). CRY has no DF (skip).
3. `c_m = max($10M, DF·IM_m/ΣIM)` in bigint: `DF × IM_m / ΣIM` with half-even rounding on the remainder. The floor makes Σc ≥ DF; that is intended (spec).
4. `SITG1 = ccp_sitg1_frac × DF`, `SITG2 = 0.10 × DF`; TCH capital earmarks them (no posting; they are layers of TCH's own capital, losses post `CCP_SKIN_IN_GAME_LOSS` REVALUE in T07B-11).
5. Delta of each member's contribution posts `CCP_DEFAULT_FUND` (top-up member → service account; excess returned). Players have 7 game days; while short, pre-trade allows only risk-reducing orders (`CCP_DF_SHORT`). NPC members (§6.5) pay at once from capital.
6. Event `bank.ccp.dfResized` PUBLIC `{ service, df, sitg1, sitg2, members: count }`. Store a `dfSize` row per week.

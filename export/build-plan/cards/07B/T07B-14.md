## Goal
The Clearing House's non-risk service holds what must be safe whatever fails: 15 contract escrow, 13 judgment escrow during appeals, 08 fund custody, 09 IPO books, bilateral IM, collectible-loan items and large auction bid holds. Escrowed assets are never lent, re-used, pulled into a waterfall, a bank resolution or a bankruptcy estate before release, and escrow cash sits as TCH reserves at the central bank so no commercial bank failure can touch it.

## Imports
- T07A-01 / T07A-05: reserve accounts at `cbk_<bloc>` (payment system).
- T03-06: `LedgerApi.post`; T03-25: estate builder hook (`excludeFromEstate(holdIds)`).
- T07A-02: `openReuse` must reject escrowed units (reuse forbidden).

## Implementation steps
1. Cash holds: depositor → `ccp_ESC` (`ESCROW_DEPOSIT`), then TCH moves the same amount into its reserve account at the central bank of the currency in the same P13. Security holds encumber the units through a pledge to `ccp_ESC` with `reuseAllowed = false`.
2. Exclusions: waterfall capacity (T07B-11) never reads ESC; 07A resolution creditor hierarchy skips ESC; the 03 estate builder skips holds until released.
3. Courts may attach a beneficiary's interest; the hold stays, and release pays the court instead of the beneficiary (§9 Y22).
4. Fees: retail custody free; LIC4: daily fee = rate × max(0, securities − `custody_fee_threshold_real`)/(1e6 × 364) with `custody_fee_ppm_gy` = 200 and threshold $1B × MSI (excess only, no cliff; E-37 PROVISIONAL [Quant]), posted as TRANSFER PAY `CCP_CUSTODY_FEE` 5080 → 4000 to `ccp_TCH` (Producer ruling E-38, final, per E-9; was SINK). Collateral in any TCH MarginAccount is excluded from the custody base. Daily amount rounded with roundHalfEven, matching the ledger (Producer ruling E-40; not floor). 08 fund custody fee (0.25%/yr) is 08's posting through this port.

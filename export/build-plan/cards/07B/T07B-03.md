## Goal
The SCAN margin model used by CCP LD and CRY, by retail derivatives at the broker and inside portfolio margin. It revalues each underlying group under 16 scenarios with 06 pricing and returns the group IM, then applies inter-group credits and calendar spread charges. Getting this right is what makes derivative leverage cost what its risk is.

## Implementation steps
1. `R_g = 2.33 · σ_IM,tick · sqrt(MPOR) · price`, MPOR from `ccp_<service>_mpor` (LD 720, CRY 60; BROKER uses the service of the product).
2. Scenarios in fixed order: m ∈ {0, +1/3, −1/3, +2/3, −2/3, +1, −1} × v ∈ {+0.25, −0.25} (14, weight 1), then ±2 with v = 0 (weight 0.35). `Loss_s = −(PV_after − PV_now) · weight`.
3. `IM_g = max(max_s Loss_s, SOM_g)`, `SOM_g = 0.02 × underlying notional × number of short option contracts` in the group.
4. Credits: candidate pairs with ρ > 0.7 and opposite delta signs, sorted by ρ descending then (g, h); each group used in at most one pair; `credit = 0.5·ρ·min(IM_g, IM_h)`.
5. Calendar: futures on the same underlying in different expiries: each spread leg pays 0.003 × notional; legs = min(long qty, short qty) matched nearest expiry first.
6. Result converted once with `toMoney` half-even. Groups sorted by key.

## Goal
The per-tick heart of margin. Each P7 it marks every account with a changed mark, picks the IM model by account type, moves the account between OK, CALL, LIQUIDATING and DEFICIT, issues and expires calls, and opens margin liquidation jobs in the 07A engine with a planner that closes the largest IM contributor first until IM ≤ equity / 1.1. At P13 variation margin on cleared positions moves as cash between accounts and the service account, so derivative P&L is realised every tick and nets to zero (INV-CCP-003).

## Implementation steps
1. Model dispatch (`ACCOUNT_RULES`): CASH none; RETAIL_MARGIN `retailIm`; PORTFOLIO `max(SCAN, HVaR-PB)` × the PB multiplier in [1.0, 1.5] (`up` PB tier, default 1.0) then add-ons; MEMBER_* the service model (LD/CRY SCAN, OTC/CDS HVaR, REPO haircut sum) with MM = IM. Between recomputes use `scaleIm`.
2. Equity per §3.16.1. Collateral counts at `valueAfterHaircut` with the CCP venue for MEMBER, PB venue for PORTFOLIO, BILATERAL for broker retail; ineligible or wrong-way collateral counts 0. Each posted unit is a pledge (T07A-02) so it cannot be sold or re-pledged (§9 Y11).
3. Transitions (§3.16.2): RETAIL `equity < MM` → LIQUIDATING now; `MM ≤ equity < IM` → CALL (pre-trade accepts only risk-reducing orders, `CCP_CALL_RISK_REDUCING`); `≥ IM` → OK and any open call MET. PORTFOLIO / PB / MEMBER: `equity < IM` with no open call → `MarginCall(IM − equity, due = tick + 30)`; `equity < 0.6·IM` → LIQUIDATING (MEMBER: return `memberDefault` for T07B-10); call unmet at due → FAILED and LIQUIDATING (member default for MEMBER).
4. Liquidation: `openJob({ source: 'MARGIN', ownerId, lines: all positions, penaltyTo: ownerIsClient ? memberId : RETAIL_INS_FUND (retail at an NPC broker) | cryInsFundId(product) (CRY), proceedsTo: accountId })`. `marginPlanner` re-plans each tick with `closeOrder` on marginal IM per unit expected cost until `IM(remaining) ≤ equity/1.1`, or everything when equity ≤ 0. Orders made in P7 of N reach 05 in P6 of N+1 (07A engine). Held owners liquidate exactly like everyone, always at the fair-value band. When nothing is left and equity < 0 → DEFICIT and call T07B-07 `settleDeficit`.
5. VM at P13: for every cleared position `ΔPV = PV_now − lastPv`; post `CCP_VARIATION_MARGIN` between the account and `SERVICE_ACCOUNT[service]` (gain: service pays; loss: account pays), update `lastPv`. Post in (service, assetId, accountId) order. Rounding: each ΔPV is `toMoney` half-even; because the CCP holds the mirror leg of every position (INV-CCP-001), the service account's sum is Σ of the same rounded amounts with opposite sign, so it nets to exactly zero.
6. Intents: deposit arrives at P13 of the submission tick and counts from the next P7 (§3.16.3); Held characters may deposit. Withdraw rejects with `CCP_WITHDRAW_BUFFER` when `equity − amount < 1.1·IM`. `setType` requires LIC4 (`CCP_NOT_LIC4`).
7. `INV_CCP_003`: for each service, Σ of last tick's `CCP_VARIATION_MARGIN` amounts over all member accounts + the service account = 0.

## Goal
When a clearing member fails, TCH must take over its book without the market noticing more than it must. This task declares the default, ports clients to their backup members, hedges the remaining directional and vega risk through the 07A liquidation engine and 06 RFQs, auctions the hedged book in lots to members, and produces the close-out cost that T07B-11 runs through the waterfall. Every step has a fixed tick window measured from the declaration tick d.

## Implementation steps
1. Triggers (§3.17.6) evaluated in P7 after marking; declaration posts nothing, emits `bank.ccp.memberDefault` PUBLIC `{ member, service }` and `bank.ccp.serviceStatus` DEFAULT_MANAGEMENT. Each service is managed separately (losses never cross services).
2. Porting (ticks d+1…d+120, E-37 PROVISIONAL [Quant]; evaluated each tick): ISA clients first (clientId order): an NPC backup decides at d+1 and accepts if `equity ≥ 1.1 × IM`; a player backup decides by intent any time up to d+120 (no answer = refuse). Omnibus: as a block only if the backup accepts all. Accepted → positions and pledged collateral move to a new client account at the backup (`TCH_PORT` record, no market trades). Unported clients join the house portfolio; after close-out each receives its collateral surplus within 1 game day (§8 edge 17). Event `bank.ccp.porting`.
3. Hedging (ticks d+1…; E-37 PROVISIONAL [Quant]): hedge each risk factor with its most liquid instrument first (index futures, then liquid names, then RFQ); the VaR cut is measured at d+120 and the auction starts at d+120 whether or not it is reached. Directional delta per underlying hedged with `openJob({ source: 'CCP_HEDGE', penaltyTo: null })` at ≤ `liq_max_participation`; option vega with one RFQ per underlying per tick while vega remains. Stop when VaR(portfolio + hedges) ≤ 0.2 × VaR at d.
4. Auction (ticks d+120…d+480, E-37 PROVISIONAL [Quant]): three 120-tick rounds (d+120, d+240, d+360); one lot per product in round 0; a lot needs ≥ 3 sealed bids including ≥ 2 NPC, otherwise it is re-offered at half size in the next round; sealed bids; members holding positions in the product must bid (no bid = worst score); others may. NPC members bid `model − 2·IM_lot·(0.2 + fear)` if within CET1 headroom (§6.5). Winner = highest bid ≥ reservation (`model − 2·IM_lot`), ties by memberId. No winner (or fewer than 3 bids) → re-offer twice with lots halved (rounds 1, 2), then the remainder goes to the tear-up list at d+480. Bid holds for large lots go to ESC (T07B-14). Winners take the positions at their price (`CCP_LIQUIDATION_PROCEEDS` SWAP_ASSET); scores appended to `auctionScores` (keep last 10).
5. §8 edge 16: a second default during management is processed in parallel; lots of the same product may be bundled into one auction; assessment caps are shared (T07B-11).
6. After the last lot: `closeOutCost`, then T07B-11 `runWaterfall`.

## Goal
Crypto derivatives clear without a mutualized fund: each perp or future has an insurance fund fed by liquidation penalties and 10% of CRY fees, and a deficit beyond it is covered by auto-deleveraging the most profitable, most leveraged opposite positions at the defaulter's bankruptcy price. The task also lets an R6 player group sponsor a new CCP using these rules within 13's regulatory bounds.

## Implementation steps
1. Deficit of a CRY account after liquidation: pay from the product's insurance fund (`CCP_DEFICIT_ABSORB` fund → service account, E-38). Remaining > 0 → `runAdl`: walk `adlQueue`; for each candidate close `min(candidate qty, qty needed)` against the defaulter at the bankruptcy price; the candidate's forgone profit versus mark covers the deficit; stop when covered. Emit `bank.ccp.adl` PARTIES to each deleveraged account and a PUBLIC count.
2. §8 edge 18: no candidates or still short → the CRY member's capital bears the rest (`CCP_DEFICIT_ABSORB` member → service, E-38) and the product halts until rebalanced (05 halt flag through `up`).
3. Insurance funds receive 1% liquidation penalties (07A engine `penaltyTo`) and 10% of CRY fees (T07B-08). No CRY DF exists; `layerCapacities` for CRY is DEFAULTER_IM, INSURANCE_FUND, ADL.
4. Sponsored CCPs (§3.17.10): requires a UNIVERSAL bank, rank R6 (14) and $10B capital (`CCP_NOT_MEMBER` otherwise); bounds confidence ≥ 0.99, MPOR ≥ service default, Cover-2 or more, SITG ≥ 10% of DF, checked at P1 of creation and of every change (§9 Y23). Its services reuse every 07B function keyed by its own `ccp_<sponsorCode>` id. Registry gap: `bank.ccp.sponsor` is not in 07 §4; Open question 2.

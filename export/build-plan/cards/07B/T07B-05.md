## Goal
Retail (LIC2+) margin is per-position and rule-based so it is simple to read and impossible to game with offsets. This task implements the account-type table (which model, MM fraction, call style) and the retail IM formula with the license leverage table and all modifiers. It also exposes the pre-trade check every trading screen and 05 order entry use.

## Implementation steps
1. Table exactly as §3.15.2: SPOT_LONG and SPOT_SHORT 50% at LIC2–4; FUTURE LIC3 `max(SCAN·1.25, 10%)`, LIC4 portfolio (route to RISK model when the account is PORTFOLIO; a RETAIL_MARGIN LIC4 account uses the LIC3 rule); PERP LIC3 10%, LIC4 5%; OPTION_BOUGHT premium in full; covered 0 extra; cash-secured put holds strike cash; naked LIC4 only: `max(SCAN·1.25, 10% of underlying notional)` (calls) or 10% of strike notional (puts). Missing cells → `CCP_NOT_ALLOWED` reject in pre-trade.
2. `IM = Σ max(notional·imRate, SCAN_i·1.25)` with SCAN only for derivatives; `imRate = 1/maxLeverage` after modifiers. MM = 0.5 × IM (`ccp_retail_im_rule`).
3. Modifiers in order: S3+ case halves leverage; the 72-hour new-player window caps leverage at 3; both apply → min. Fresh start: `marginGate` rejects any margin account opening or new margin position for 14 real days (07A records the lockout). Restricted cash counts 0 in equity. Held: existing positions stay margined; new risk rejected.
4. All per-position IM in number, summed, then one `toMoney` half-even.

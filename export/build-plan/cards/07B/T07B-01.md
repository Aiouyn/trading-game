## Goal
The single stable contract of unit 07B (margin, the Clearing House TCH, novation, default fund, default management, waterfall, CRY and ADL, bilateral CSAs, escrow and custody). It transcribes the 07 §2.7 entities, defines tables and ids, creates the institution `ccp_TCH` and its five risk-service accounts plus the ESC service, exposes the cleared-product registry that 06B calls, and fixes the zod schemas of every 07B intent (07 §4, `bank.margin.*`, `bank.ccp.*`, `bank.csa.sign`) and event (07 §5, `bank.margin.*`, `bank.ccp.*`). Every later 07B task imports from here; nothing here has behaviour beyond construction and validation.

- T07A-01: `Id`, `Ccy`, `Tick`, `CODES` (07A codes), `BNK_REJECT`, `pad`, `LiquidationJob`, `ClearingDebt` (the record type; 07A owns the `bnk.clr_debt` table), `up` (upstream adapter), `MarginPort07`.

## Implementation steps
1. Transcribe §2.7 field by field. Money fields are `bigint` micro-units of the account currency (03). Rates and σ are `number`. The three added `MarginAccount` fields (positions, segregation, backupMemberId, sigmaAtCompute) are 07B storage choices documented in JSDoc.
2. Ids: `ctx.newId(kind)` with the `ID_KINDS` 3-letter kinds; they match the E-20 regex `^(?:[a-z]{3}|hh|prop)_[A-Za-z0-9_]+$` and the 64-character limit. Fixed ids: `ccp_TCH`, `ccp_LD`, `ccp_OTC`, `ccp_CDS`, `ccp_REPO`, `ccp_CRY`, `ccp_ESC`, `ccp_TCH_RETAIL_IF`. Members keep their own `bnk_`/`cmp_` ids. A schema test asserts every fixed id and every generated id kind passes the regex.
3. Service rows are seeded at genesis from the §3.17.2 table through `ctx.params` (`ccp_<service>_*`); fee and MPOR values are read, never inlined. DF services start with `defaultFund = DF_min × N_a` (T07B-09 resizes), CRY with `defaultFund = null` and one insurance fund per registered CRY product at `cryInsFundInit × N_a`, funded by a genesis TRANSFER from TCH capital (no FAUCET).
4. `CCP_REJECT`: `CCP_NOT_MEMBER`, `CCP_MIN_CAPITAL`, `CCP_DF_SHORT` (risk-reducing only), `CCP_HAS_POSITIONS`, `CCP_NOTICE`, `CCP_NOT_LIC4`, `CCP_WITHDRAW_BUFFER`, `CCP_CALL_RISK_REDUCING`, `CCP_LOCKOUT` (fresh start), `CCP_CLR_DEBT`, `CCP_HELD`, `CCP_LOT_CLOSED`, `CCP_BID_BELOW_ZERO`, `CCP_INELIGIBLE_COLLATERAL`, `CCP_SERVICE_CLOSED`, `CCP_ESCROW_LOCKED`, `CCP_PARAM_BOUNDS`, `CCP_NO_CSA`, `CCP_NOT_ALLOWED`.
5. Codes: a test asserts each `CCP_CODES` entry exists in 03's catalog with the class 07 §5 states (all TRANSFER except `CCP_INS_FUND_SWEEP` SINK and `CCP_SKIN_IN_GAME_LOSS` REVALUE). E-38 adds `CCP_DEFICIT_ABSORB`, `CCP_ISA_FEE`, `CCP_CUSTODY_FEE`, `CCP_BILATERAL_CLOSEOUT`, all TRANSFER PAY; this list (`CCP_CODE_CLASS`) is the single place the 07B ledger class of each code is fixed (fees: TRANSFER to ccp_TCH per E-9, Producer ruling, formerly SINK).
6. Intents (07 §4): `bank.margin.deposit`, `bank.margin.withdraw`, `bank.margin.setType`, `bank.ccp.join`, `bank.ccp.leave`, `bank.ccp.auctionBid`, `bank.ccp.segregation`, `bank.ccp.backupMember`, `bank.csa.sign`, `bank.ccp.sponsor` (E-38). Amounts travel as decimal strings parsed to bigint micro. `bank.clearingDebt.repay` stays 07A's.
7. Events (07 §5) with the stated visibility: `bank.margin.call`, `bank.margin.liquidation`, `bank.margin.deficit` (PARTIES, plus a PUBLIC aggregate count variant `bank.margin.counts`), `bank.ccp.dfResized`, `bank.ccp.memberDefault`, `bank.ccp.serviceStatus` (PUBLIC), `bank.ccp.porting`, `bank.ccp.auction`, `bank.ccp.waterfall` (PUBLIC body with layer and total; PARTIES body with member shares), `bank.ccp.adl` (PARTIES, PUBLIC count). No PUBLIC schema has an `accountId`, `ownerId` or position field (07#50 is checked by the 07A dashboard card; the schema test here asserts the field absence).
8. 07B draws no RNG: NPC bids, porting acceptance and ADL ranking are deterministic. No stream is registered (02 §3.10.4 lists none for 07B).

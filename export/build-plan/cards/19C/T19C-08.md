## Goal
Implement 19 Rules 3.14.1, 3.14.3 and 3.14.4: no personal data ever enters batches, events, snapshots or engine state (proved by a scanner), a player can export everything about their account and character, and a deletion request, after its grace period, removes personal data, renames the character to a generated label through `sys.account_deleted`, and leaves world history (and replay hashes) intact.

## Implementation steps
1. No PII in the engine (Rule 3.14.1): the engine knows `chrId` and handle only; country and age band enter only through `sys.profile_change`. The scanner walks every decoded batch (intents, system inputs), event row and snapshot chunk; any string or key matching a PII pattern, or equal to a known device id, is a finding (masked sample: first 2 characters plus `***`). It runs in the CI `contract` job on a 1-game-day headless world (19#44) and nightly on staging.
2. Export (Rule 3.14.3): at most one open request (409); the job collects account data (`account_ref`, email via `readEmail` with an audit row `data.export`), the character's public and private history (postings of its entities, its sealed intents, events whose `party_ids` contain the character), chat messages it authored (15's source), purchases (21's source); writes a zip of JSON files to object storage; link valid 7 days; completes within `export_sla_days` (30); the job is scheduled immediately and retried on failure.
3. Deletion (Rule 3.14.4): request needs MFA or a fresh sign-in; status `DELETION_PENDING` for `deletion_grace_days` (14), cancellable. When due: delete `pii.account_pii` row and OAuth links, status `ANONYMIZED`, revoke sessions and keys, submit `sys.account_deleted {characterId, newLabel}` (JOBS key), chat messages deleted or replaced by "[removed]" via 15's hook, audit row `data.deletion`. The engine keeps the pseudonymous `chrId` and the label; positions and debts follow the estate process (14, §8 edge 26); world history is untouched so replay is unchanged.

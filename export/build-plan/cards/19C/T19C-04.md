## Goal
Build the WebSocket gateway (uWebSockets.js) that terminates client sockets: the connection lifecycle of 19 Rule 3.8.1, the `hello`/`welcome` handshake with protocol version enforcement (Rule 3.8.10), the intake checks of Rule 3.4.1 in their exact order with forwarding to `INTAKE` and seal acknowledgements only after the coordinator's reply, the shared rate buckets of Rule 3.8.6, socket caps, heartbeats, presence computation (Rule 3.8.7), revocation within one tick, and halt and queue behaviour. Delta fan-out and subscriptions are T19C-05.

## Implementation steps
1. Lifecycle (Rule 3.8.1): accept only connections forwarded by the CDN (header check, Rule 3.9.10); shed new connections first when `gateway_max_sockets` is reached; `hello` within 10 s or close. `handleHello`: `protoVersion < protoMinVersion(PROTO_VERSION)` -> `bye UPGRADE_REQUIRED` (19#35; current and current−1 accepted, Rule 3.8.10); redeem the ticket (single use, device bound); load session (revoked -> `bye SESSION_REVOKED`) and character; send `welcome {serverTick, tickStartMs, tickMs: 5000, protoVersion, sessionId, features}`. Max `max_sockets_per_account` (4) sockets: the oldest is closed `bye TOO_MANY_SOCKETS`.
2. Heartbeat: answer `ping` with `pong {clientMs, serverMs}`; close after `ws_idle_timeout_s` (45) without any frame; frame limit `max_frames_per_s` (50): over the limit continuously for 5 s -> `bye RATE_LIMITED`.
3. Intake (Rule 3.4.1 item 2, first failure answers `intentAck` with `sealTick: null`): SIZE frame <= `max_intent_bytes` (BAD_SCHEMA); SESSION bound to the socket (else close); ACTOR = character or an operator in the session's cached actor list, refreshed every tick from the `me` channel (ack BAD_SCHEMA plus `notice AUTH_NOT_PERMITTED`); KEY belongs to the session and is not revoked (BAD_SIGNATURE); SIGNATURE (BAD_SIGNATURE); NONCE window and ULID skew (BAD_SIGNATURE for nonce, BAD_SCHEMA for skew, §8 edge 2); KIND known (BAD_SCHEMA); SCHEMA payload (BAD_SCHEMA); RATE `takeIntentToken` (RATE_LIMITED; UI, API and managers share the bucket, E6); WORLD not halted: status `HALTED_EXCHANGE` or `MAINTENANCE` -> WORLD_HALTED (02 §3.20 item 2: intents during a halt are refused, not queued). Additionally `clientTick < currentTick − intent_max_age_ticks` -> REJECT_STALE early (the coordinator decides authoritatively).
4. Forwarding: `IntakeEnvelope` to `intake.<partitionId of the actor's home>` from the cached map; the client sees "sending" until the coordinator's seal reply, then `intentAck {status: 'SEALED'|'DUPLICATE'|'LATE_EXACT'|'REJECT_STALE', sealTick}`. Gateways never reorder, never drop after ack and never inspect payload semantics (Rule 3.4.1 item 4). On SEAL(N) the forwarder publishes its `ClosedMarker`.
5. Queue (19 §8 edge 20): when INTAKE publish fails (bus without quorum) intents wait in an in-memory FIFO up to `gateway_queue_max` (200,000) and are forwarded in order when the bus returns; beyond the limit -> WORLD_HALTED. Queued intents keep their ids (dedupe makes resends safe, edges 4-5).
6. Presence (Rule 3.8.7): present if any authenticated socket of the account is open or it sealed an intent in the last `presence_intent_window_ticks` (180); transitions are submitted as `sys.presence` system inputs; presence is never sent to any client (E12).
7. Revocation (Rule 3.8.1 item 6, 19#31): subscribe to `REVOCATION_CHANNEL`; on SESSION or ACCOUNT revocation close the sockets with `bye SESSION_REVOKED` at the latest at the next tick boundary (immediately on message); KEY revocation invalidates the key cache entry so the next intent fails BAD_SIGNATURE.

## Test plan
`gateway.test.ts` (gateway in-process with a memory bus and Valkey test double; a ws client library in tests):
- `19#35 a client at proto_min_version − 1 gets UPGRADE_REQUIRED; current−1 keeps working`: PROTO_VERSION = 3 in the test: hello with 1 -> `bye UPGRADE_REQUIRED`; 2 and 3 -> welcome.
- `19#31 revoking a session closes its sockets within one tick and its signing key rejects the next intent`: two sockets of the session; `revokeSession`; both closed with SESSION_REVOKED within 5,000 ms (one tick) of the call; a new socket with a fresh ticket of another session of the same account stays open; an intent signed with the revoked session's key and sent on another open socket is rejected BAD_SIGNATURE.
- account socket cap: the 5th socket closes the oldest with TOO_MANY_SOCKETS; idle 46 s closes; frames at 60/s for 6 s close with RATE_LIMITED.
`intake.test.ts`:
- `INTAKE_CHECK_ORDER` equals Rule 3.4.1 item 2; a frame failing both SIZE and SIGNATURE reports SIZE.
- 25 intents of one actor in one tick: 20 forwarded, 5 RATE_LIMITED; an API connection of the same actor shares the bucket.
- halted world: WORLD_HALTED, nothing forwarded; bus without quorum: queued, forwarded in order after recovery; queue at max: WORLD_HALTED.
- seal reply maps statuses to the right sockets; DUPLICATE carries the original sealTick.
- presence: open socket -> one `present: true` change; close and 180 ticks without intents -> `present: false`.

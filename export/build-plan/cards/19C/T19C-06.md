## Goal
Add to the `sys` engine module the system inputs of 19 §4.3 that concern accounts and need 14.A's character: `sys.account_status` and `adm.freeze_character` (character frozen or released; 15 defines what freezing means in play), `sys.account_deleted` (generated label, removal from boards and the estate process, 19 Rule 3.14.4), `sys.profile_change` (game-relevant handle, country, timezone and age band under 14/15's limits), `sys.guest_upgrade` and `sys.wallclock_mark` (the engine's only wall clock, R26). Single-writer rule: the sys module records these facts in its own tables and emits events; 14 and 15 apply them to their own entities.

## Implementation steps
1. `sys.account_status {characterId, status, enforcementId}` (source ADMIN, with an enforcement record of 15/21): character must exist; write status; SUSPENDED or BANNED sets `frozenUntilTick = null` (indefinite) and emits `sys.account_status_changed` PRIVATE; ACTIVE releases. `adm.freeze_character {characterId, until?}` (2 approvals): same as SUSPENDED with `frozenUntilTick = until` (auto-release at that tick at P1).
2. `sys.account_deleted {characterId, newLabel}` (source JOBS after the grace period, 19 Rule 3.14.4): set `deleted`, `label`; emit `sys.account_deleted_internal` so 14 renames the character to the label and starts the estate process, and 15 removes it from boards; history keeps the pseudonymous chrId (replay unchanged; liabilities to other players stay, §8 edge 26). The engine never receives personal data (Rule 3.14.1): newLabel is generated (`Investor-` + 6 base32 chars).
3. `sys.profile_change` (source WEB): handle must match 14's grammar and at most one change per chapter (14 §3.1.3; the paid handle-change intent of 14 charges the fee, this input only carries the result) -> `SYS_HANDLE_LIMIT`; country at most once per `country_change_real_days` (180, 15) measured in ticks (x 17,280) -> `SYS_COUNTRY_COOLDOWN`; timezone at most once per `tz_change_cooldown_days` (30) real days measured by wall-clock marks -> `SYS_TZ_COOLDOWN`, unknown zone -> `SYS_UNKNOWN_TZ`; a timezone change takes effect at the next local-day start (§8 edge 25, E20): `tzEffectiveFromUtcMs = nextLocalDayStartUtcMs(now, newTz)`, before that the old zone stays effective; ageBand may only go from 16_17 to 18_PLUS (`SYS_AGEBAND_DOWNGRADE`). Values are what boards, country rules (15) and chat rules use.
4. `sys.guest_upgrade {characterId}` (source WEB): `guest = false`; 15 lifts guest limits by reading the view.
5. `sys.wallclock_mark {utcIso}` (source COORD, every `ad_clock_mark_minutes` = 15 real minutes, R26; broadcast to every partition): parse ISO UTC; must be later than the last mark (else `SYS_CLOCK_BACKWARDS`); write `T_CLOCK` on each partition. It is the only wall-clock source for account-level rules (local-day starts of 21's ad eligibility, login recency of 15 §3.13); replay reads the recorded mark.
6. All handlers validate at P1 on COORD and send the effect to the character's home with `when: 'NEXT'` (applied at P1 of N+1 before intents), except the broadcast wall-clock mark (applied locally at P1 of N).

## Test plan
`account-inputs.test.ts` (TestWorld with 14.A fixtures for characters):
- status: SUSPENDED -> `sys.account_status_changed` PRIVATE to the character; `sys.account_of` shows SUSPENDED from N+1; ACTIVE releases; `adm.freeze_character` with until N+100 auto-releases at N+100; with one approval rejected.
- deletion: label written, internal event emitted once, the character id unchanged; replaying the same batches gives identical hashes.
- profile: country change at day 0 then again at day 179 (in real days of ticks) -> SYS_COUNTRY_COOLDOWN, at day 180 accepted; timezone change effective only from the next local midnight (worked example: mark 2026-03-01T22:30Z, new tz Europe/Paris (UTC+1) -> effective from 2026-03-01T23:00Z); a second tz change 29 real days later -> SYS_TZ_COOLDOWN; ageBand 18_PLUS -> 16_17 rejected.
- wall clock: a mark earlier than the last -> SYS_CLOCK_BACKWARDS; marks appear on every partition in a 4-partition reference run.

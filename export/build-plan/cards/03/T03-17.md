## Goal
Net worth is always derived from the balance sheet at a tick and never stored as truth (INV-BS-001; V3 2.1). This task computes:
- **Solo marked NW** of any entity, per currency and translated to its reporting currency (03 §3.3.6, §3.10.1).
- **Consolidated NW** of a group parent (the look-through NAV of T03-16).
- **NWrank** of characters and alliances in FDL: consolidated look-through NW with the three board adjustments of §3.10.3:
  - the square-root impact haircut;
  - the control cap on controlled listed companies;
  - the extra 10% stale haircut.

It applies the §3.10.2 inclusion table, keeps the FX translation rates (intraday latest mid, official day close, and period averages for T03-18), runs the §3.10.4 cadence, and installs `ctx.ledger.netWorth` (GLOBAL G7) and T03-16's board value function.

## Implementation steps
0. In T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`, add two kinds:
   - `FX_CLOSE_E6`: subject `${from}>${to}`, value bigint string, the official day-close FX spot mark from 05;
   - `CHALLENGE_BOOK`: subject entityId, value boolean, from 15. Challenge-event portfolios are a separate book and never count.
   05 writes `FX_MARK_E6` every tick with the latest clearing mid. That fact already exists.
1. **FX.**
   - `fxRateE6` resolves the rate in this order:
     - same currency → 1,000,000n;
     - the direct pair fact;
     - the inverse fact, as `1e12 / rate` half-even;
     - a cross through FDL: `rate(from>FDL) × rate(FDL>to) / 1e6`, half-even.
   - A missing rate is `LEDGER_BUG` in engine code. In views it degrades to "n/a" and is never shown as 0.
   - `translate` uses T02-04 `fxConvert` (half-even).
   - `recordFxCloses` stores every `FX_CLOSE_E6` fact at DAY_CLOSE and prunes days older than 364.
   - `averageRateE6` is the integer mean of the stored daily closes over the inclusive day range, rounded half-even. With no stored closes it falls back to the closing rate.
   - An FX crash shows up at once: NWrank and flash NW use the intraday `FX_MARK_E6` (03 §8 edge 3). Tax bases keep acquisition-day rates (lots, T03-20).
2. **What counts** (`countsForNw`, 03 §3.10.2):
   - MEMO accounts → no;
   - 1221 restricted compute → counted at 0 (valueAccount already returns 0);
   - restricted cash subs of 1001 → yes, at face;
   - 1050, 1060 and 1070 (margin, default fund, escrow deposited) → yes, for the depositor until release;
   - assets under `COURT_FREEZE` → yes, because ownership is unchanged;
   - seized assets → excluded naturally: 13 moves them to the court escrow entity, and they count for the victim only after the award transfers them;
   - judgments, fines and restitution → liabilities posted by 13 from the verdict tick; pending lawsuits are never posted;
   - 2990 persistent claims → a liability;
   - entities with `CHALLENGE_BOOK` true → excluded from every consolidation and from NWrank. `nwConsolidated` drops them from `lookThroughSet` before solving.
3. **Solo NW** (`nwSolo`).
   - Per currency: `Σ valueAccount(...)` over counted ASSET accounts, minus `Σ (−balance)` over LIABILITY accounts, plus `−balance` of the 3800 currency trading accounts. 3800 is treated as equity, so the per-currency books balance (03 §3.3.6).
   - Translate every currency into the reporting currency with `mode` (default LATEST). `byCcy` and `translated` are returned.
   - The translated 3800 sum is the cumulative FX result. T03-19 shows it as FX gains/losses.
4. **Consolidated NW** = `lookThroughNav(entity)` (T03-16). For an entity with no 1570/1580 holdings of members, it equals `nwSolo`. `markedNetWorth(read, id, tick)` returns `nwConsolidated` for chr, ali and any group parent, and `nwSolo` for everyone else. This is what G7 `ctx.ledger.netWorth` serves.
5. **Board value** (`boardValueOfHolding(read, holderId, a, tick)`), for exchange-traded holdings, meaning accounts whose asset has a `MARKET_STATS` fact with `adv20 > 0`:
   - **Mark.** Use the volume-weighted mean of the hour's mark history (`hourMarkSeries`, 03 §9 "board NW uses the hour's mark history, not one print"), or the effective mark if the hour has no history. `mv = qty × mark`, using `notional` half-even.
   - **Impact haircut.** `ratio = groupQtyOf(root(holder), asset) / adv20`, aggregated over the holder's whole group so that splitting a stake across subsidiaries does not reduce the haircut. If `ratio > 0.05`, `h = min(0.5, params.num('nw_impact_y') × sigmaDaily × sqrt(ratio))`; else `h = 0`. Value = `mv × (1 − h)`.
   - **Stale haircut.** If `effectiveMark(...).stale`, value × (1 − 0.10). This covers a halted asset, or an equity on its fundamental-value fallback after 5 game days without trades (03 §8 edge 14).
   - **Control cap.** If the issuer is controlled by the holder's group (`controls(root(holder), issuer)` or root(issuer) == root(holder)): `value = min(value, economicShare(holder, issuer) × lookThroughNav(issuer) × params.num('nw_control_listed_cap'))`. Summed over the group's holdings through the look-through, this equals `effectiveOwnership × NAV × cap` (03 §3.10.3 rule 2).
   - Each reduction is recorded into the breakdown accumulator of the current `computeNwRank` call.
   - Holdings that are not exchange-traded return `valueAccount(...).value`.
6. **NWrank** (`computeNwRank(read, id, tick, params)`, chr or ali):
   - `solveLookThrough(read, root = id, …)` with `BoardValueFn = boardValueOfHolding`;
   - `nwRank = translate(nav(id), reportingCcy, 'FDL', 'LATEST')`;
   - `nwConsolidated` = the same solve with the default `valueAccount` board function, so the breakdown shows what the adjustments removed.
   The breakdown has concentration, controlCap and stale, each translated to FDL. NWrank is used by ranks (14), boards (15), the Stabilizer (T03-24) and NWI (T03-24).
7. **Cadence** (`runNwRank`). It runs in P13 when `isNwRankTick(ctx.cal.tickOfDay)` (once per game hour at t mod 30 == 29, and at t = 719 for the Stabilizer and MSI), after T03-11's money update.
   - For every chr and ali entity of the partition (index byKind, sorted), compute the NWrank and write the `led.nwrank` row with `cadence = tickOfDay === 719 ? 'DAY_CLOSE' : 'HOUR'`.
   - Flash NW is not stored. Views call `flashNw` every tick (17).
8. **INV-BS-001** (TICK FATAL, 20 §3.1).
   - For every `led.nwrank` row in `ctx.writeSet` of this tick: `computeNwRank(ctx.post, id, tick)` must equal the stored row exactly (all fields).
   - No other 03 table stores a net-worth value. A grep test in `networth.test` asserts that no 03 TableDef other than `led.nwrank` has a field named `nw*` or `netWorth*`.
   - A failure is `ctx.fail('INV-BS-001', { entityId })`.

## Test plan
- `03#14 A character holding 30% of a stock whose ADV20 is 1% of shares outstanding with σ_daily = 2% gets a board haircut of min(0.5, 0.5·0.02·sqrt(30)) ≈ 5.5%`:
  - Setup: issuer outstanding 1,000,000 shares, so ADV20 = 10,000; chr holds 300,000 at mark $10 (one-print hour history).
  - Assert:
    - `impactHaircut(300000, 10000, 0.02, 0.5)` equals `0.5 × 0.02 × sqrt(30)` = 0.0547722557…;
    - the board value is `toMoney(3,000,000 × (1 − 0.0547722557))` = $2,835,683.23… (exact micro per half-even);
    - the NWrank breakdown `concentration` equals mv − board value;
    - the same stake split 150k/150k between the chr and its 100% cmp gets the same total haircut (group qty).
- `03#15 A character who controls a listed company trading at 5× its look-through NAV is counted on the board at 2× NAV × effective ownership`:
  - Setup: chr holds 60% of listed cmp_L. L's NAV is $10M and its market cap $50M, so mv of the stake is $30M; ADV is high enough that there is no impact haircut.
  - Assert: the board value is `0.6 × 10M × 2` = $12M; the breakdown `controlCap` = $18M.
  - Variant (03 §8 edge 14): L untraded for 5 game days → the fundamental mark applies, the stale 10% applies, and the cap still applies.
- `fx.test.ts`:

  - an inverse pair;
  - the average of 3 daily closes;
  - after a 50% intraday crash of a foreign ccy, flash NW drops in the same tick.

  - restricted cash counts at face; 1221 counts at 0; escrow 1070 counts; a challenge-book entity is excluded;

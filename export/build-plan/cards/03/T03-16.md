## Goal
This task derives who owns and who controls every entity from the share quantities the ledger holds. It builds:
- the ownership graph (03 §3.11.1);
- control: majority, de facto and contract rules, at most one controller per entity, evaluated to a fixed point in sorted order (§3.11.2);
- effective ownership (§3.11.3);
- the look-through NAV, the anti-duplication algorithm that stops value being counted twice through holding structures (§3.11.4, Bible I06; 03 §9 "Duplicating NW through holding structures").

It also provides 09's acquisition guard `LED_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP` (product of the cycle's shares ≥ `cycle_max_product`), and an hourly revaluation of every 1580 investment to its look-through or 09 value. When control changes, it updates `ownerId`, `rootOwnerId` and `perimeter`, with the matching money-aggregate adjustment. INV-BS-004 (03 I20) guards the result.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kind.** Add `'SPV_EQUITY_TRANCHE'` to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`: subject spvId, value trancheId, written by 08. Also add `markOwnershipDirty` to T03-09's commit step c, which is a one-line call for qty postings whose account code is in `OWNERSHIP_ACCOUNTS` or is 3100/2320. Both edits stay inside unit 03.
1. **Graph** (03 §3.11.1, §2.7).
   - `issuerOfAsset`: in `accountsOfAsset(assetId)`, the account with code 3100 (shares, units), or 2320 for an SPV tranche. Its entity is the issuer. `shareClass` is the issuance account's sub (03 §3.2: 3100 sub shareClass; 2320 sub trancheId); holders' 1570 subs are `<cmpId>:<shareClass>`.
   - Edges come from holder accounts 1500 and 1570 (votes), 1560 tranches (votes only for the SPV equity tranche, step 2c) and 1530 (economic only, `votingPct = 0`).
   - `votingPct = qty × votesPerShare / Σ_classes outstanding × votesPerShare`.
   - `economicPct = qty × economicWeight / Σ_classes outstanding × economicWeight`. Both are float, computed with `Number(qty)` over `Number(outstanding)`; `economicWeight` comes from 09 and is "after preferences".
   - Preferred shares have 0 votes unless 09 converts them; dual class follows 09's `votesPerShare`.
   - Holdings of an issuer's own shares (treasury, held by the issuer itself) are excluded from both numerators and denominators.
   - `biz` and `prop` are leaves: one edge `{ holderId: ownerId, votingPct: 1, economicPct: 1 }`.
2. **Control** (`computeControl`, 03 §3.11.2). Iterate to a fixed point, at most `n = entities + 1` passes, in sorted issuer order. In each pass, for each issuer X:
   - **Aggregated votes.** `votes(H, X) = Σ votingPct(h, X)` over holders h with `h === H` or `controls(H, h)` under the current assignment.
   - **Suspension rule.** Votes held in X by an entity that X itself controls (directly or recursively) are suspended. This is the treasury-share rule for cross-holdings (03 §3.11.4 last paragraph), and it is what keeps control acyclic in 03 §8 edge 7.
   - **Majority.** Candidates are holders H with `votes > 0.5`. The direct controller is the qualifying candidate that does not control another qualifying candidate (the lowest link of the chain); ties go to the smaller id.
   - **De facto.** Otherwise, H qualifies when `votes(H, X) >= params.num('control_defacto_min')`, `votes(H, X) >= 2 ×` the largest other holder group's votes (holder groups = holders aggregated by their current root), and fact `BOARD_CONTROL` `${H}:${X}` is true (09 governance).
   - **Contract** (c):
     - `fnd`: the manager never controls its fund for consolidation (custody rule, 08). The fund's `controllerId` is undefined, but its `ownerId`/`rootOwnerId` keep the registered manager, so a player fund stays in PLAYER (03 §8 edge 16: "the fund is in P").
     - `spv`: the sponsor controls only when it holds > 50% of the qty of the tranche named by `SPV_EQUITY_TRANCHE`.
     - biz/prop: LEAF control by `ownerId`.
   - No controller → `displayOwnerId` = the largest holder group's root (ties → smaller id).
   - **Result per entity.** `ownerId = controllerId ?? displayOwnerId ?? id`. `rootOwnerId` follows `controllerId` to the end; an uncontrolled entity is its own root (03 §3.11.2 last sentence). `controlPath` runs from the root down to the direct controller.
   - `groupOf(root)`: every entity whose root is `root`, with `effectiveOwnership = effectiveOwnership(root, e)`, `controlPath`, and `nci[e] = 1 − eff`.
3. **Look-through set and NAV** (03 §3.11.4).
   - `lookThroughSet(root)`: BFS from `root` over edges whose holder account is 1570 or 1580, or biz/prop leaves, i.e. holdings not valued at market. It includes the root and is sorted in canonical order (`ENTITY_KIND_ORDER`, then id).
   - For each member e, `a_e` (float, in e's reporting ccy, via `fxConvert` and `FX_MARK_E6`) is computed as follows:
     - `a_e = Σ valueAccount` over e's ASSET accounts, excluding holdings of shares or 1580 of other members of G, minus Σ of its LIABILITY balances;
     - listed holdings (1500/1510) in issuers outside G are valued with `BoardValueFn` (board value, 03 §3.10.3);
     - intercompany loans, receivables and payables with members are **kept** on both sides. The worked paragraph of §3.11.4 says the loan "appears as an asset in a_A and a liability in a_B ... nets correctly (minority holders of B bear their share)". This is a spec inconsistency with the "excludes intercompany receivables/payables" bullet; resolved for the explicit worked rule.
   - `S[e][j] = economicShare(e, j)` for members j held through 1570/1580/leaf edges.
   - Gauss–Seidel solves `x_e = a_e + Σ_j S[e][j] · x_j` in canonical order for **exactly** `params.num('lookthrough_iterations')` sweeps, starting from `x = a`. The float arithmetic order is fixed, so the result is deterministic.
   - `residual` is the max `|x − (a + S x)|` after the last sweep. If `residual > 1e-6 × max(1, max|x|)`, emit a `ledger.invariant.failed` ops alert `LOOKTHROUGH_NOT_CONVERGED` (non-fatal: the cycle limit should make it impossible).
   - `lookThroughNav(e) = toMoney(x_e)` in e's reporting ccy.
   - Results are memoized per `(root, tick)` in the runtime; the memo is dropped when any member's accounts change.
4. **Effective ownership.** `effectiveOwnership(H, X)` solves `y_j = S[j][X] + Σ_k S[j][k] · y_k` over `lookThroughSet(rootOf(H))` with the same sweep count, and returns `y_H` (the sum over all paths of the products of economic shares, 03 §3.11.3).
5. **1580 values.**
   - `lookThroughValueOfHolding(read, holderId, entityId, tick)`:
     - a `biz`: the 09 valuation model mark (`effectiveMark(read, entityId)`, source MODEL_FUND/APPRAISAL published by 09 hourly, the "upgrades only 70% reflected" rule is 09's), falling back to `lookThroughNav`;
     - otherwise: `economicShare(holder, entity) × lookThroughNav(entity)`.
   - The `LookThroughFn` is installed into T03-13 by T03-28, so `valueAccount` of 1580 uses it.
   - `revalueInvestments` (P2 at HOUR, plus DAY_CLOSE): for each 1580 account in sorted order, call `postRevaluation(ctx, a, value, 'LED_APPRAISAL', 'PNL')` (T03-14). 1580 is a financial investment, so it goes through P&L (03 §3.9.4).
6. **Circular-ownership guard** (`checkAcquisition`, 09 calls it in P1 on every share acquisition). Let `s = economicShare` the buyer would have after the acquisition. Compute `maxCycleProduct`:
   - the maximum, over directed paths issuer → … → buyer in the current graph, of the product of economic shares;
   - found by max-product Dijkstra (weights `−log(share)`, all shares in (0, 1]), in sorted order with deterministic tie-break;
   - multiplied by `s`.
   If that product is `>= params.num('cycle_max_product')`, return `{ ok: false, code: 'LED_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP' }`; 09 rejects with this G9 code (03 calls it `REJECT_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP`). The limit keeps `S` strictly contractive (spectral radius < 1). Example: B owns 70% of A and A buys 60% of B: the product is 0.6 × 0.7 = 0.42 < 0.95, which is allowed, and the look-through converges (03 §8 edge 7).
7. **Recompute** (`recomputeOwnership`, P13 after `commitTick`):
   - Read the `led.owndirty` issuers, plus entities registered this tick. Expand to the issuers whose holders' control may change (descendants of the touched issuers' holders' roots), then run `computeControl` on that set and on what depends on it.
   - For each entity whose `ownerId`, `rootOwnerId` or `perimeter` changed, update the entity row and the whole controlled subtree's `rootOwnerId`. When the perimeter changed, call `adjustPemForPerimeterChange`: for each ccy, compute `m` = the entity's PEM-counted balances (1001 all subs, 1010, 1050, 1070); `led.money.PEM += m` (entering PLAYER) or `−= m` (leaving). This is not a flow (03 §3.5.5 counts crossings by entries only), so T03-11's full day-close recompute still matches.
   - Clear `led.owndirty`.
   - Perimeter and control changes take effect for entries from the next tick. The money effect of this tick's entries was computed with the perimeters at commit.
8. **INV-BS-004** (`INV_BS_004`, TICK FATAL, 20 §3.1; covers 03 I20). The checks are incremental over the dirty issuers and the entities changed this tick, and run in full at day close:
   - (a) following `controllerId` from any entity ends within `n` steps at an entity without a controller: no cycle;
   - (b) `rootOwnerId` equals that terminal, and `ownerId === controllerId ?? displayOwnerId ?? id`;
   - (c) at most one controller per entity: `computeControl` on the issuer reproduces the stored row;
   - (d) `perimeter === perimeterFor(entity)`, which matches the root;
   - (e) for every share asset, Σ holder qty (1500/1510/1570/1560 holders, excluding the issuer's treasury) equals `−qty(issuer 3100 or 2320)`, and equals `outstanding` of the `SHARE_CLASS` fact when that fact exists (voting shares sum to shares outstanding);
   - (f) the terminal's kind is one of chr, ali, npc, gov, sys, or an uncontrolled NPC-core entity (cmp, bnk, ins, fnd, spv, exc, ccp, rat, cbk, reg, crt, hh, frm, row), which 03 §3.11.2 makes its own root. 20's wording ("ending at a chr, npc, gov or sys") is narrower than 03 §3.11.2. The module rule wins, and the gap is reported.
   Each failure is `ctx.fail('INV-BS-004', { entityId, sub: 'a'..'f' })`.

## Test plan
- `03#17 Look-through NAV with A owning 60% of B and B owning 30% of A converges within 64 sweeps to the analytic solution within 1e-9 relative, and the group's NAV equals external net assets attributable (no duplication)`:
  - Setup: cmp_A with external net assets $1,000,000 and cmp_B with $500,000. A holds 60% of B's shares (1570), B holds 30% of A's, and outside chr holders own the rest.
  - Assert:
    - `x_A = (1,000,000 + 0.6·500,000) / (1 − 0.18)` and `x_B = 500,000 + 0.3·x_A`, each within 1e-9 relative;
    - the outside holders' attributable value `0.7·x_A + 0.4·x_B` equals $1,500,000 within 1e-9 relative (no duplication);
    - `sweeps === 64`;
    - an A→B intercompany loan of $100k leaves the outside total unchanged.
- `03#18 An acquisition creating a cycle with product ≥ 0.95 is rejected REJECT_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP`:
  - A owns 100% of B; B tries to buy 95% of A: product 0.95 → `{ ok: false, code: 'LED_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP' }`. Buying 94% → ok.
  - A 3-entity cycle (A 99% of B, B 99% of C, C buying 97% of A: product ≈ 0.9507) → rejected.
- `control.test.ts`:
  - majority via a controlled subsidiary: chr holds 60% of cmp_P, and P holds 55% of cmp_S → S's controller is P and its root is chr;

## Goal
This card assembles unit 03 into the `ModuleDef` that 02 loads (GLOBAL G6/G7, T02-11), with:
- the ledger provider handed over as `services.ledger` (T02-11's port: `createPartition`, `globalCheck`, `testHooks`);
- every table, intent, event, invariant and view of the unit;
- the phase and boundary wiring in the fixed order that T03-09 and T03-15 specify;
- the installation of the cross-card hook functions;
- genesis.

It also verifies two things:
- 03 §6: NPC entities follow the same rules as players.
- 03 §9: every exploit counter, as a regression suite that points at the card enforcing each counter.

## Implementation steps
1. **`installLedger`**, called at the top of `index.ts` and idempotent. It:
   - calls `setLookThroughFn(lookThroughValueOfHolding)` (T03-16 into T03-13);
   - calls `setEquityMethodFn(equityMethodValue)` (T03-18 into T03-13, the 1570 branch for unlisted associates);
   - calls `setBoardValueFn(boardValueOfHolding)` (T03-17 into T03-16);
   - calls `setNetWorthFn(markedNetWorth)` (T03-17 into T03-09, G7 `ctx.ledger.netWorth`);
   - freezes `registerCloseStep` (T03-15) and the reason-code extension list (T03-05).
   It throws `LEDGER_BUG` if `catalogErrors()` is non-empty.
2. **Tables.** `LEDGER_TABLES` is the concatenation of every `TableDef` exported by T03-01 to T03-26, sorted by name. The test asserts:
   - every `led.*` name appears exactly once;
   - every exported `*Table` of the unit is included (a static scan of the exports).
   The COORD tables (`led.stab`, `led.stablever`, `led.msi`) are marked COORD-homed and replicated read-only (reconciler item for 02).
3. **Phases** (fixed order; T03-09 step 4 and T03-15 step 8):
   - `P2` every tick:
     - `collectGarnish` (T03-26);
     - expired-hold processing is done by T03-08 inside commit, so it is not called here.
   - `P12` at t = 719 on PLAYER and COORD partitions: `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P12')`.
   - `P13` → `partitionOf(ctx).runP13(ctx)` with the pre and post lists below.
     - pre:
       - restricted releases (`queueReleases`, T03-12);
       - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_PRE')`;
       - at DAY_CLOSE, `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P13_LOCAL')`.
     - commit.
     - post, in this order:
       - T03-11 money update;
       - `updateLots` (T03-20);
       - `recomputeOwnership` (T03-16);
       - `updatePeriodTotals`, `updateIntercompany`, `checkRelatedParties` (T03-18);
       - `applyRailChanges` (T03-19);
       - `runDefaults` (T03-25);
       - `personalTrigger` (T03-26);
       - `accumulateGarnish` (T03-26);
       - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_POST')`, which includes at DAY_CLOSE: `recordFxCloses`, `runBalanceSheetTest`, `runAutoOpen`, `materializeStatements`, and step 10;
       - `publishDue` (T03-19);
       - when `isNwRankTick`, `runNwRank` (T03-17);
       - at t = 719, `buildSample` + `sendSample` (T03-24).
   - Messages: every handler in `MESSAGE_HANDLERS` is dispatched in 03's P2 from the inbox (02 delivers NEXT messages), sorted by (kind, sender, id).
4. **Boundaries** (run in P2 when the boundary is in `cal.boundaries`; 02 calls them in boundary order):
   - every close boundary: `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P2')` (steps 1–5, the cash part of step 7, `runDistributions`, `runPersonalCases`, `remitPropertyTax`, `remitVat`);
   - `DAY_OPEN`, in this order:
     - `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'DAY_OPEN_CATCHUP')`;
     - `collectDue`;
     - `runLateInterest`;
     - `refundOverpayments`;
     - `settleAudit` for audits due;
     - on COORD only, `runStabilizer`;
   - `HOUR`: `revalueInvestments` (T03-16) through its close step;
   - `MONTH_OPEN`: `ensurePropertyTaxAccruals`.
5. **Intents** (03 §4), with each handler's id exactly as in §4:
   - `acct.payment.setAccounts`, `acct.statement.request`, `acct.ledger.export` (T03-19);
   - `acct.tax.election`, `acct.lots.select` (T03-20);
   - `acct.tax.changeResidency` (T03-22);
   - `acct.tax.payEarly`, `acct.tax.dispute` (T03-23);
   - `acct.insolvency.file`, `acct.insolvency.claim.sell` (T03-25).
   A test asserts that the set equals the §4 table.
6. **Events.** `LEDGER_EVENT_SCHEMAS` = T03-01 `LEDGER_EVENTS` (03 §5), plus the kinds added by later cards: `ledger.insolvency.freshstart` and `ledger.persistent.paid` (T03-26). Every `ctx.emit` kind used under `packages/engine/ledger` must be a key; a static scan test enforces this.
7. **Invariants.**
   - `LEDGER_INVARIANTS`:
     - INV-LED-001 – 009 and 011 – 014;
     - INV-LED-010 as `makeInvLed010([I22_SUBCHECK])`;
     - INV-BS-001, 002, 004, 008, 011 and 012.
   - Each is a 20A `InvariantImpl` with the id, cadence and severity of the 20A catalogue.
   - A test asserts the id set equals the unit's ownership list (INV-LED-001..014; INV-BS-001, 002, 004, 008, 011, 012).
8. **Genesis** (`ledgerGenesis`, world creation; 03 §3.1):
   - `registerEntity` for `sys_SYSTEM`;
   - `seedTaxRegimes` (T03-20);
   - initialize `led.stab` (mode OBSERVE, neutral levers, `launchDay` = 0) and `led.msi` (msi 1.0) on COORD;
   - initialize `led.money` rows per ccy.
   Other modules register their own entities (cbk 04, gov 04, hh/frm/row 16A, npc banks 07) through `registerEntity` in their genesis, which runs after 03's.
9. **`params.ts`** lists every PARAM key the unit reads and gives one typed accessor per key. Display units are converted per GLOBAL-CONTEXT: money via `toMoney`, real days × 17,280, game days × 720. A static test greps `params.(num|money|…)('…')` under `packages/engine/ledger` and asserts that each key is in `LEDGER_PARAM_KEYS` and in the 23 registry. The keys without a registry entry are the unit's registry gaps: `led_code_alarm_mult`, `led_code_alarm_floor`, `stab_lever_ranges`, the protected floor, and the 72-hour restricted window (written as `RESTRICTED_WINDOW_TICKS` 51,840 per D036). They stay named constants until 23 adds them.
10. **NPC rules** (03 §6), `npc.test.ts`. NPC core entities use the same accounts, templates, taxes and insolvency rules as players:
    - an NPC cmp posting `CMP_NPC_REVENUE` (FAUCET) and `CMP_NPC_COSTS` (SINK) hourly through a test module numbered 05 has a real income statement, is taxed by `corporateYear` and can enter review and a case like a player company;
    - an NPC bank lends with `tplLend` (deposits and loan created);
    - LP rebalancing codes `LP_REFILL_FROM_ROW`, `LP_SWEEP_TO_ROW` and `LP_INVENTORY_*` post against `row_WORLD`;
    - an elastic sector's flows are booked from the model module's numbers only; 03 has no elastic decision code;
    - a named NPC actor (test module 16) is rejected by holds when `ledger.available` is short;
    - NPC entities are audit-eligible (`runAuditSelection` includes npc cmp) and can default (`ledger.default` emitted).
11. **Exploit counters** (03 §9), `exploits.test.ts`. One `describe` per §9 row, asserting the counter through the module, with a short scenario each:
    - related-party marks ignored (T03-13) / look-through NAV and cycle limit (T03-16);
    - concentration haircut and control cap (T03-17);
    - thin-print guard (T03-13);
    - P2P tax and related flags (T03-21, T03-18);
    - arm's-length tax prices, CFC and substance (T03-22);
    - no Serena residency (T03-22);
    - wash sale including related parties (T03-20);
    - exit tax (T03-22);
    - protected floor only for crime/court debts (T03-26);
    - persistent claims (T03-26);
    - fresh-start cooldown (T03-26);
    - restricted cash for ops (T03-12);
    - a player bank creates no NW (T03-07 LEND: Δ NW of bank and borrower = 0);
    - Stabilizer robustness: 3 outlier hoarders do not move the weighted median (T03-24);
    - installments on YTD (T03-23);
    - engine-set reason codes: an intent cannot choose a reason (G7 `post` from a module only; T03-05 owner check);
    - private statements (T03-19 access);
    - a money-creating bug aborts the tick (INV-LED-001/003 negative test) and is corrected only by `ADM_CORRECTION_*` with requestedBy the admin module.
12. **`module.test.ts`**: `createTestWorld({ modules: [CORE_MODULE, LEDGER_MODULE], partitions: 1 })` and `partitions: 4` both run 30 game days on an empty world (S0 gate: ledger invariants green, equal state hashes); `services.ledger === ledgerProvider`; `testHooks.balance` agrees with `w.balance`.

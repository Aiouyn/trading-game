## Goal
The Stabilizer Board of the Exchange is the only definition of the Stabilizer (ruling R04; 04 §3.17 references it). It is a deterministic PI controller that runs once per game day. Its input is the log of the activity-weighted median of engaged characters' NWrank divided by the designed wealth curve `W_target(age)` (03 §3.7.2). It moves four levers that act only on FAUCET and SINK flows:
- L1 NPC demand;
- L2 lifestyle and NPC-service price index;
- L3 high-volume transaction levy;
- L4 upkeep index.

Each lever has a range and a daily step. The controller has OBSERVE and ACTIVE modes, guards and alerts, and publishes the public Money Supply Report every game day plus the Stabilizer and Exchange Report every chapter. It never names a player and never touches prices, rates, taxes, fines, start cash, ads, grants, compute spot or any individual.

The same aggregation computes the Money Supply Index and NWI = NWrank / MSI (03 §3.8).

## Implementation steps
1. **Target curve** (03 §3.7.2).
   - `wTarget` interpolates `ln W` linearly in age between the table points: 0 → 5,000; 1 → 15,000; 7 → 500,000; 21 → 20,000,000; 56 → 300,000,000; 180 → 2,000,000,000; 365 → 6,000,000,000. Past 365 real days it is `6e9 × (1 + stab_target_growth_after_year)^(age − 365)`.
   - Age is in world-time real days (ticks / 17,280) since the character's `createdTick`.
   - All math uses dmath, so it is deterministic.
2. **Sample** (each partition, P13 post-commit at t = 719, after T03-17's NWrank).
   - For each chr:
     - `w = activityWeight(daysSinceActive)`, from `LAST_ACTIVE_TICK`;
     - the chr is engaged when `isEngaged` holds (`ACTIVE_DAYS` has ≥ `stab_engaged_min_active_days` distinct days in the last 168) and its age is ≥ 1 real day;
     - `q = NWrank_FDL / wTarget(age)`.
   - Also collect:
     - `designWeightedAll = Σ_all w × wTarget(age)`, over every character, for MSI;
     - `designWeighted`, the same sum over the engaged set, for the inflation guard;
     - the PEM in FDL (Σ ccy translated, T03-17);
     - `M0_sys` in FDL;
     - `N_a` (T03-11);
     - the per-chr NWrank map, used only for MSI and NWI and never published.
   - Send the sample to `STAB_HOME_ID` with `'NEXT'`. It arrives at COORD in R1 of t = 0 of the next day.
3. **Controller** (`runStabilizer`, COORD, P2 of DAY_OPEN). The spec's run "at t = 719 with effect from t = 0" becomes a run at t = 0 on the samples from t = 719, which have the same effect timing. The run:
   - Merge the samples sorted by `(partitionId)`, then entries by id. `e = ln(weightedMedian(engaged))`. An empty sample gives `e = 0`.
   - **Mode.** OBSERVE if `dayIndex − launchDay < stab_observe_game_days`, or if the engaged count is < `stab_min_sample`. Otherwise ACTIVE.
   - **Deadband** with `db = stab_deadband`: `eEff = |e| ≤ db ? 0 : e − sign(e) × db`.
   - **Saturation** means every lever is at the bound it would move toward for the sign of `eEff`. When `u > 0`, tightening moves L1 down and L2, L3 and L4 up; when `u < 0`, the reverse.
   - **Integral.** If not saturated, or if `sign(eEff) ≠ sign(I)`: `I = clamp(I + eEff, −stab_integral_max, +stab_integral_max)`. In OBSERVE, `I` stays 0.
   - **Output.** `u = clamp(stab_kp × eEff + stab_ki × I, −1, 1)`.
   - **Lever targets** (`LEVER_SPEC`, ranges and steps from `stab_lever_ranges`):
     - L1 = `1 − 0.10u`, range [0.90, 1.10], step 0.005;
     - L2 = `1 + 0.25u`, range [0.90, 1.25], step 0.005;
     - L3 = `0.5 + 1.5u` bp, range [0, 3.0], step 0.1;
     - L4 = `1 + 0.15u`, range [0.95, 1.15], step 0.003.
   - **Lever moves.** OBSERVE: levers held at neutral (L1 1.00, L2 1.00, L3 0.5 bp, L4 1.00); targets are still published. ACTIVE: `stepLevers` moves each lever toward its target by at most its step, then clamps it to its range. Values are rounded to 1e-9 to keep float state canonical.
   - Write `led.stab` (with history trimmed to 364 days) and `led.stablever` (`fromTick = tick`), and emit `stab.lever.changed` `{ lever, from, to }` PUBLIC for each lever that changed.
4. **Lever application by owners.** The levers are read with `leverValue`, and 03 itself applies none of them:
   - L1: 09 city demand pools; 10 elastic rent at half strength (`rentDemandMultiplier`). Listed NPC companies' modelled revenue is not affected, so share prices are never steered.
   - L2: 14 lifestyle catalog, NPC club dues and hosting; 16 Terminal/data tiers; 06 NPC auction fees; 09 NPC service prices and the catalog `c0` of new businesses; 10 NPC property services. Shown to players as the "Luxury CPI".
   - L3: 05 charges `MKT_TRANSACTION_LEVY` on a consolidated group's exchange notional above `levy_free_daily_notional` per game day.
   - L4: upkeep in 09, 10 and 14.
   `LEVER_READERS` documents these readers, and T03-28 exports them.
5. **Guards** (03 §3.7.6), as `stab.alert`, internal (ops):
   - `|e| > 0.5` for 28 consecutive days → `STRUCTURAL_IMBALANCE`. The Stabilizer keeps acting within its ranges.
   - Over the current chapter, the growth of `M0_sys / N_a` above 2× the growth of `designWeighted / N_a` → `INFLATION`.
   - ρ (T03-11 `rho` over the rolling game year of FlowDays, merged from the samples' partitions) below 0.75 or above 1.0 for 28 consecutive days → `RHO_BAND`. The §3.6 family bands go out of range (`familyBandCheck`) → `FAMILY_BAND`, reported in the same alert.
   - Lever values are state, not params. Changes to gains or ranges follow 02 §3.18 (param versioning).
6. **MSI** (03 §3.8, COORD at the same run):
   - `raw = PEM_FDL / (msi_money_share × designWeightedAll)`;
   - `ema += MSI_ALPHA × (raw − ema)`, with `ema` starting at `raw` on the launch day;
   - while `dayIndex − launchDay < stab_observe_game_days` (168): `MSI = 1.000` and `emaBaseSum += ema`, `emaBaseN++`;
   - at the end of that period, `emaBase = emaBaseSum / emaBaseN`;
   - afterwards, `MSI = clamp(ema / emaBase, MSI_prev × 0.995, MSI_prev × 1.005)`;
   - the published value is rounded to 4 decimals and emitted as `msi.updated` `{ msi, raw }` PUBLIC.
   - `nwi(id) = NWrank / MSI`, using the 4-decimal MSI as an E4 integer, half-even. Boards (15) show it with the label `real`.
7. **Reports** (03 §3.7.7).
   - **Daily** `stab.daily.report` PUBLIC with `MoneySupplyReport`: M0, M0_sys and M2 per currency; PEM and PEW (Σ NWrank of players in FDL); N_a; e; u; levers and their change; families for the day and the rolling year; ρ; MSI; mode.
   - **Chapter**, at CHAPTER_CLOSE: the Stabilizer and Exchange Report, built from history, `led.flowday`, and facts from 07 (CCP default-fund usage, systemic indicators), 08 and 21 (exploit-fix history).
   - Individual players are never named: no ids are present in any report payload, and a test asserts this.

## Test plan
- `03#33 The Stabilizer in OBSERVE mode publishes e and lever targets but levers stay neutral; in ACTIVE mode with e = +0.25 for 10 game days, L1 decreases by exactly 0.005 per day until its target, and no lever ever moves more than its daily step or leaves its range`:
  - A unit-level controller test with a synthetic sample of 300 engaged characters with q = exp(0.25):
    - OBSERVE (day 10 < 168) → the report has e = 0.25 and targets, the levers equal neutral, and I = 0;
    - ACTIVE (days 200–209) → e_eff = 0.20 and I grows by 0.2/day. L1 goes 1.000 → 0.995 → 0.990 → … each day exactly −0.005 until it reaches `1 − 0.1u`, after which it tracks the target;
    - each lever's |Δ| ≤ its step and its value stays within range on every day.
  - A sample of 150 → OBSERVE.
- `03#34 The Stabilizer never changes taxes, market prices, interest rates, compute spot price, start cash or ad values (static check of its outputs)`:
  - Static test: the stabilizer folder imports nothing from `tax/`, `marks/` or `catalog/`, and calls no `ctx.ledger` write method.
  - Its `TableDef`s are exactly `led.stab`, `led.stablever` and `led.msi`; its event kinds are exactly the four `stab.*` and `msi.updated`.
  - A run over 30 days leaves every tax regime, mark, POLICY_RATE fact and 14/18 start-cash and ad param unchanged (hash compare).
- `03#35 MSI stays 1.000 for the first 168 game days; afterwards it changes by at most 0.5% per game day; NWI = NWrank / MSI on every board row`:
  - A synthetic raw series that jumps 50%: MSI is exactly 1.0000 on days 0–167, and |ΔMSI| ≤ 0.5% on every later day;
  - for 50 characters, `nwi(id) === NWrank / MSI` (half-even), and the 03 boards view rows (T03-27) carry the same value.

## Goal
Measure the money of the world exactly, per currency, every tick: base money (M0) split into outside money created by faucets (M0_sys) and central-bank credit (M0_cb), broad money M2, player-economy money PEM and the activity-weighted player count N_a; accumulate the day's flows by class, by reason code and by faucet/sink family including the player-perimeter crossing view; and assert the money identities (I4, I17, I18) and the daily faucet/sink alarm. These numbers feed the Stabilizer and MSI (T03-24), the Money Supply Report and 20's balance gates.

## Implementation steps
1. Perimeters (03 §3.5.1, D035): `led.entity.perimeter` set by `registerEntity` (T03-08) and recomputed on control changes by T03-16. "Core" = PLAYER ∪ NPC. Elastic sectors (03 §3.5.2) hold real claims like any entity and spend virtual 1009 money; 1009 never counts in M0, M2 or PEM.
2. Aggregates (03 §3.5.3) as exported. M2 counts deposits of elastic sectors (they are holders of 2100 subs) and excludes 1001 of banks, cbks and treasuries. PEM counts every 1001 sub (restricted lots included, 03 §3.10.2 counts restricted cash at face), 1010, 1050 cash margin (sub ccp/bnk, money part only) and 1070.
3. `entryMoneyEffect`: sum the entry's postings' effects: M0 component accounts (cbk 2001/2002/2003, sign −), M0_sys (cbk 1910), M2 components, PEM components (by the entity's perimeter). `ownAccount` = Σ deltas of `B:2100:*` where bank B is the headline payer or payee of the entry and the 2100 delta is matched by B's own 4xxx/5xxx/3xxx posting in the same entry (interest, fees, opex, dividends paid into or out of deposits); `cbkOwn` = Σ M0 deltas of entries where the cbk is a party and the class is TRANSFER (interest on reserves, profit remittance) plus REVALUE of cbk reserves in foreign currency.
4. `updateMoneyAfterCommit` (P13, after `commitTick`): for each committed valid entry and each currency it touches: add `amount` (headline) to `flowsToday[cls]` and `flowsByCode[reason]`; add ownAccount, cbkOwn; P-crossing (03 §3.5.5): if `pem > 0n` add to `pCrossInToday[family]` and `grossInToday`, if `< 0n` add `−pem` to `pCrossOutToday[family]` and `grossOutToday`. Then recompute and store M0, M0_sys, M0_cb = M0 − M0_sys, M2, PEM on `led.money` (O(touched): maintain them incrementally by adding the entry effects; a full recompute runs at day close and must match, else `ctx.fail('INV-LED-003', ...)`).
5. `rollDay` at t = 719 after the day's last commit: write `FlowDay` (amounts as decimal strings) with N_a; delete FlowDay rows older than 364 days; reset the "today" fields; `dayIndex` = `cal.gameDay`.
6. Faucet/sink families and ρ (03 §3.6, D037): `familyShares` = family inflow (or outflow for S families) / Σ gross inflows to P over the window; bands from the §3.6 table (F1 60–80%, F2 4–12%, F3 −5–12%, F4 2–10%, F5 0–15%, F6 0–1% after the first chapter, S1 35–60%, S2 8–25%, S3 4–15%, S4 2–10%, S5 8–20%, S6 1–4%, S7 0.3–2% of PEM per game year (computed against average PEM, not gross inflows), S8 1–5%, S9 1–8%, S10 0–15%); ρ band 0.80–0.95, alert below 0.75 or above 1.0 for 28 consecutive game days (the alert is raised by T03-24 with `stab.alert` `RHO_BAND`). These are the bands 20's balance harness asserts (03#42 is run by T03-28).
7. Invariants (03 §3.16 I4, I17, I18; 24 D034 is in force: central-bank credit changes M0_cb, so 20's wording "no other class changes M0" is implemented as "no other class changes M0_sys", owning-module precedence 00 §3.5):
   - `INV_LED_003` sub-checks: I4 M0_sys: Δ cbk:1910 over the tick (ctx.pre vs ctx.post) = Σ FAUCET headline amounts − Σ SINK headline amounts of the tick's valid entries, per ccy, exactly; and no entry of another class has `m0sys ≠ 0n`. I17: `netIssuance(sys, ccy) === −balance(sys:2900:ccy)` and Σ cbk 1910 = −sys:2900 per ccy. I18 (day close only): `led.money.flowsToday` by class equals the sum recomputed from the day's `led.entry` rows by class.
   - `INV_LED_004`: I4 M0_cb: Δ M0_cb = Σ `CBK_CREDIT_CODES` CREDIT_CREATE − CREDIT_DESTROY headline amounts + cbkOwn of the tick; I4 M2: for each entry: REVALUE `m2 = 0n`; TRANSFER `m2 = ownAccount + (M0 moved between banks' reserves and non-banks' 1001 inside the entry)`; bank CREDIT_CREATE (`BNK_*`, `REPO_BANK_*`, `PB_*`) `m2 = +amount + ownAccount` when the borrower is a non-bank (03 §3.5.4 table); CREDIT_DESTROY mirror; FAUCET `m2 ≥ 0n`, SINK `m2 ≤ 0n`; and Σ entry m2 = Δ computeM2 over the tick.
   - `INV_LED_011` (DAY_CLOSE, ALERT): for every FAUCET and SINK code, the day's total ≤ its alarm ceiling = `alarmMult × max(trailing 28-day daily mean of that code, floor)`, with `alarmMult = ctx.num('led_code_alarm_mult')` (registry gap, DEFAULT 10) and `floor = ctx.num('led_code_alarm_floor')` dollars (registry gap, DEFAULT 1,000,000) ; and `ACCT_REWARDED_AD` day total ≤ 0.0001 × the day's net mint (Σ FAUCET − Σ SINK; when net mint ≤ 0 the ad check compares against gross FAUCET) (20 INV-LED-011, 21 §3.14.9). Failure is ALERT (tick commits).
8. Edge case 16 (03 §8): QE buying bonds from a player fund: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` → M0_cb up, PEM up, M0_sys unchanged (tested in 03#7).

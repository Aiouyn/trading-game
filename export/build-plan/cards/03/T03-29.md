## Goal
These are the three acceptance tests of 03 that need the whole game: every unit loaded, the 20B harness, and the agent archetypes. They run in stage S10, under the `TYCOON_LONG=1` flag in the NIGHTLY tier and at gates G3 and G5. The default `pnpm test` skips them with `describe.skipIf(!process.env.TYCOON_LONG)`.

They are 03's ACCOUNTING checks, so waivers are never allowed (STAGES-AND-GATES).

## Implementation steps
1. **The 03#36 / 03#37 run.**
   - `buildHarnessWorld` with:
     - preset STANDARD;
     - population `POP_EXPLOIT(1000)`: the template already includes every RULE_PROBE and OPTIMIZER family at its share;
     - partitions 4;
     - 20 chapters = 20 × 56 real days = 20 × 967,680 ticks.
   - For runtime, the run executes at headless speed. The invariant set is on every tick (G2 rule). The test does not sample: it reads each `TickRecord`.
   - After each tick, assert:
     - `committed === true` and `failedInvariants` is empty for INV-LED-001 (every tick), INV-LED-005 (I13 cross-partition suspense) and, at day close, INV-LED-010 (I3 full);
     - I3 itself, every tick: the `ledger.totals` probe (Σ of every account balance per ccy across all partitions, i.e. Σ debits − Σ credits) is 0 for every ccy. The engine checks I3 fully only at day close, so this probe is the per-tick check.
   - **03#37**: every tick, for every asset, `holdings.truth` Σ qty over all accounts of the asset (including elastic-sector holdings 1909 subs, issuer accounts 3100/2320 and cancelled-share moves) equals the asset's issued total. That is: I8 full, from INV-LED-010, with no failure, plus the probe recomputation.
   - The test fails on the first violating tick and prints the repro bundle that T20B-03 captures.
2. **The 03#42 run.**
   - Preset STANDARD, `POP_BASELINE(1000)`, one game year (364 game days = 262,080 ticks), seeds from `SEED_SETS` VALIDATION (20 seeds through `runBand`).
   - `familyYearReport` sums family inflows and outflows from the run's postings using the catalog's `stabilizerFamily`. The shares are each family's flow over Σ gross inflows to the player economy P (03 §3.6). Each share is checked against the §3.6 band: F1 60–80%, F2 4–12%, F3 −5–12%, F4 2–10%, F5 0–15% and F6 per §3.6, plus the sink families S1–S9.
   - ρ = sinks/faucets over the rolling window (T03-11 `rho`), and the band test reuses `familyBandCheck`. Every day after the first chapter (day > 56 real days in ticks) must have ρ within 0.80–0.95.
   - Pass criterion: in every seed, every family is in band and `rhoOkAfterChapter1` is true. A failure is an ACCOUNTING band failure (20 balance gate), and no waiver is allowed.
   - The report also cross-checks: the per-day family totals equal T03-11's `led.flowday` rows.

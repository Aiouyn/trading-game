## Goal
This task lays the base of the tax engine. Every tax is computed exactly from postings: each reason code carries a `taxTag`, there is no self-assessment, and nothing can be under-declared (03 §3.12.1). It provides:
- the six regimes of 03 §3.12.3 (five blocs plus the Isla Serena free zone) as 03-owned state, which 04 may move;
- residency of every entity kind (§3.12.2);
- the `TaxAccount` per (entity, jurisdiction, tax type, year) (§2.9);
- tax lots and their consumption, FIFO by default or by specific identification (§2.5; `acct.lots.select`);
- realized capital gains with FX at the acquisition-day and disposal-day rates, the short/long split, the main-home exemption, the lifestyle-loss rule, netting and loss carry-forward (§3.12.5);
- the wash-sale rule, including related parties (§3.12.6; 03 §8 edge 6);
- stock splits on lots (03 §8 edge 5);
- the `acct.tax.election` intent with its type registry (LOT_METHOD here; GROUP_FORM and GROUP_LEAVE in T03-21; EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS in T03-22).

Taxes are TRANSFERs to the jurisdiction's treasury (`gov_<BLOC>`). They are never a Stabilizer lever.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kinds.** Add to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`:
   - `OWNER_OCCUPIED_SINCE`: subject propId, value the Tick since which the owner has occupied it, or null (10);
   - `ASSESSED_VALUE`: subject propId, value Money string, the assessed value for property tax (10: "10 supplies the rate and the assessed value").
   Taxes are TRANSFER codes owned by 03 (`TAX_*`), paid by the taxpayer to `gov_<BLOC>`. Serena franchise fees (`CMP_FRANCHISE_FEE_SERENA`, SINK) are T03-21's.
1. **`DEFAULT_TAX_REGIMES`** (03 §3.12.3, DEFAULT; amounts in each bloc's currency at nominal; the thresholds are not indexed and MSI never applies to taxes):

   | | FDL | EUN | PYN | NKR | SRL | SERENA |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | ccy | FDL | EUN | PYN | NKR | SRL | FDL |
   | 0% band up to | 40,000 | 30,000 | 50,000 | 30,000 | 20,000 | n/a (`personalTaxAuthority false`, no brackets) |
   | rate up to 1,000,000 | 12% | 18% | 10% | 20% | 8% | |
   | rate up to 50,000,000 | 20% | 28% | 15% | 30% | 15% | |
   | rate up to 1,000,000,000 | 28% | 36% | 22% | 38% | 20% | |
   | rate above (top bracket) | 35% | 45% | 30% | 45% | 25% | |
   | CGT short | 20% | 28% | 15% | 30% | 15% | 0 |
   | CGT long | 10% | 25% | 8% | 25% | 10% | 0 |
   | Corporate | 21% | 27% | 20% | 24% | 30% | 0% |
   | Property tax/yr | 1.0% | 0.8% | 0.6% | 1.2% | 1.5% | 0.5% |
   | VAT | 7% | 20% | 10% | 25% | 16% | 0% |
   | Securities tx tax | 0 | 0.10% | 0.05% | 0 | 0.20% | 0 |
   | Stamp duty | 1.0% | 4.0% | 3.0% | 2.5% | 5.0% | 8.0% |
   | Interest cap (EBITDA) | 30% | 30% | 40% | 30% | 25% | n/a (`interestCapPctEbitda = 1`) |
   | Loss carry-forward | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 0 |

   - The non-treaty withholding rates (`wht`) are the "every pair" treaty defaults (10% dividends / 0% interest / 5% royalties). The full matrix, including the SRL–NKR and Serena rows, lives in T03-22.
   - `longHoldTicks = 91 × 720`.
   - **Rates owned by a 23 PARAM key.** Two rates have a PARAM key owned by 10, and GLOBAL-CONTEXT forbids hard-coding them:
     - property tax: `property_tax_rate_<city>`; its defaults are FDL .010, EUN .012, PYN .008, NKR .009, SRL .015, Serena .004;
     - stamp duty: `stamp_duty_rate`, 0.01.
     The regime fields `propertyTaxAnnualE6` and `stampDutyPropertyE6` are display defaults only. T03-21 reads the city param for property tax and the stamp param for stamp duty. This contradiction (03 §3.12.3 vs 10 §2.1 / 23) is reported.
   - `setRegimeRate` (04 fiscal policy) accepts:
     - top personal bracket within 25–45% (01 §14.2);
     - every other rate within [0, 1e6].
     The per-rate ranges 03 §3.12.3 attributes to 23 are missing from the registry (registry gap).
2. **Residency** (`taxStatus`, 03 §3.12.2):
   - chr → RESIDENT at `homeBloc`. Never SERENA: registration and residency change reject it (T03-22).
   - cmp, bnk, ins, exc, rat → RESIDENT at `homeBloc`, the place of incorporation (09). SERENA is allowed for cmp and uses ccy FDL.
   - fnd, spv → TRANSPARENT (HOLDERS): holders are taxed on distributions (`FND_DISTRIBUTION` DIV) and on gains on units (lots of 1530).
   - ali → TRANSPARENT (MEMBERS_BY_DUES): income is attributed pro rata to dues paid in the year (dues by member from T03-18 period totals of `SOC_ALLIANCE_DUES` on the alliance's money accounts).
   - npc kinds and NPC companies → MODELLED: `TAX_CORPORATE` = corporate rate × modelled profit, posted by 05/09 (the catalog allows 05 and 09).
   - NPC banks, insurers and funds → RESIDENT, like player ones.
   - gov, cbk, sys, reg, crt, ccp, hh, frm, row → EXEMPT.
   - biz and prop are not taxpayers: their results belong to their owner (personal income for a chr owner, corporate profit for a company owner). T03-21 attributes them through `ownerId`.
3. **Tax accounts.** `getOrOpenTaxAccount` creates the row with status OPEN and amounts at 0. `addToBase` adds to `base`. One row exists per (entity, jurisdiction, type, year), with the year from `ctx.cal.year`.
4. **Lots** (`updateLots`, P13 after commit). For each committed valid entry and each posting with `qty` on an account whose code is in `LOTTED_CODES`, for a taxable entity (RESIDENT, or TRANSPARENT holders on their own units):
   - **Acquisition** (debit qty into E's asset account from another entity):
     - open a lot with `qty`;
     - `costBasis` = the price paid plus fees composed into the entry with fee or tax tags (`TAX_SECURITIES_TX`, `TAX_STAMP_DUTY`, exchange or broker fee codes of the same entry for E), translated into the residency ccy at `fxRateE6(LATEST)` at that tick (the acquisition-day rate);
     - value-only assets (`unitLot`) open a lot with qty 1;
     - a gift-like move with no price (MOVE_QTY) uses `bookValue`, or `taxPriceFor(entry)` when the transfer is flagged;
     - then call `applyWashSaleOnAcquisition`.
   - **Disposal** (credit qty out of E to another entity):
     - `selection = led.lotsel[entry.requestedBy.intentId]` when `taxPrefs.lotMethod === 'SPECIFIC'`;
     - `consumeLots` takes the selected lots first, then FIFO by `(acquiredTick, lotId)`, and splits a lot partially (basis pro rata half-even, with the remainder kept on the lot);
     - `proceeds` = E's cash leg of the entry (net of E's fee postings in the entry), or `taxPriceFor(entry)` when the related-party flag is set (`relatedPartyAdjusted`), allocated over the consumed lots by qty with `allocate`. Proceeds are translated at the disposal-day rate, so FX gains on foreign assets are part of the gain (03 §3.12.5);
     - write one `RealizedGain` per consumed lot, with `kind`:
       - DERIVATIVE: `GAIN_SHORT` tag, or codes 1600/2600, perps, FX; always SHORT;
       - HOME: a 1800/1580-prop disposal where `OWNER_OCCUPIED_SINCE ≤ tick − 91 × 720`; `exempt = min(gain, $10,000,000)` per sale;
       - LIFESTYLE: 1860; a loss here is `exempt = −gain`, i.e. not deductible;
       - NORMAL otherwise.
     - If the gain is negative and the code is in `WASH_SALE_CODES`, call `applyWashSaleOnLoss`.
   - **Same-entity moves.** Moves between an entity's own accounts, such as collateral pledges inside the same entity, consume nothing.
   - **No-consideration disposals.** Seizures, insolvency realizations and forced divestitures dispose at the entry amount, or at `taxPriceFor` when it exists.
   - **Gains taxed elsewhere.** `GAIN_SHORT`-tagged cash flows without qty (VM, option premia, perp funding, FX settlements) write a DERIVATIVE gain record of the signed P&L amount of E's posting, with no lots.
5. **Splits.** On `EQ_SPLIT` (03 §8 edge 5), `splitLots(asset, new, old)` scales every lot's qty by new/old with floor. The remainder goes to the lot with the latest acquisition so the total qty matches the account. Basis is unchanged, so basis per share is divided. 05 adjusts open orders.
6. **Wash sale** (03 §3.12.6; `W = params.num('wash_sale_window_game_days') × 720`).
   - `applyWashSaleOnLoss(g)`, look-back: take the lots of the same asset acquired in `[g.tick − W, g.tick]` by E or by any entity `isRelated` to E, that were not consumed by this sale, ordered by `(acquiredTick, lotId)`. Disallow `|loss| × min(1, Σ replacementQty / soldQty)`, allocated across the replacement lots by qty. Add it to their `washSaleAdjust` and `costBasis`, and record `g.washDisallowed`. Any loss left, with the remaining qty, opens a `WashWindow` until `g.tick + W`.
   - `applyWashSaleOnAcquisition(lot)`, look-forward: for each open window of the same asset whose entity is E or related to E (edge 6: a character and its own company), with `tick ≤ untilTick`, disallow `lossRemaining × min(1, lot.qty / qtyRemaining)`. Add it to the lot's basis and `washSaleAdjust`, increase the original gain record's `washDisallowed`, and shrink the window.
   - Expired windows are deleted at day close.
   - A disallowed loss is removed from the year's capital loss: `gain + washDisallowed` is the counted amount.
7. **Capital gains year** (`capitalGainsYear`, characters):
   - per record, `counted = gain + washDisallowed − exempt`;
   - net within SHORT and within LONG;
   - a net short loss offsets a net long gain, and the reverse;
   - the incoming `lossCFIn`, from up to `lossCarryForwardYears` previous years (oldest first), is used against the remaining net gains only;
   - `tax = shortRate × taxable.short + longRate × taxable.long`, half-even;
   - a remaining net loss becomes the new carry-forward. It is stored on the CAPITAL_GAINS TaxAccount `lossCF` by T03-21's loss roll at YEAR_CLOSE.
   Companies do not use this. `realizedGainsTotal` feeds corporate taxable profit, where realized gains are measured against lot cost basis (T03-21).
8. **Elections and lot selection.**
   - **`acct.tax.election`**, applyPhase P1. `validate`: the actor controls the entity named in `params.entityId` (or is the character itself), then dispatch to the registered type; an unknown type → `ACCT_BAD_ELECTION`.
   - **LOT_METHOD**, registered here:
     - params `{ entityId, method: 'FIFO' | 'SPECIFIC' }`;
     - SPECIFIC requires `LICENSE` ≥ LIC2 for the actor → else `ACCT_LICENSE_REQUIRED`;
     - apply: write `taxPrefs.lotMethod` with `lotMethodFrom = tick + 1`.
   - **`acct.lots.select`** `{ saleIntentId, lotIds }`, applyPhase P1:
     - validate: actor LIC2+ (`ACCT_LICENSE_REQUIRED`); every lot exists and belongs to an entity the actor controls (`ACCT_LOT_NOT_FOUND`); all lots are of one asset; Σ qty ≥ the sale intent's qty when that is readable, else ≥ 1 (`ACCT_LOTS_DO_NOT_COVER`);
     - apply: write `led.lotsel`, keyed by `saleIntentId`.
     - The sale entry carries `requestedBy.intentId = saleIntentId`, so step 4 consumes those lots first.

## Test plan
- `03#21 A capital gain on a stock held 100 game days is taxed at the long rate; held 50 game days at the short rate`:
  - Setup: chr (FDL) buys 100 shares at $10 (lot), sells at $15 after 100 game days → one record with term LONG; `capitalGainsYear` tax = 10% × $500 = $50.
  - A second asset held 50 days → SHORT, 20%.
  - Exactly 91 days is LONG; 90 days 719 ticks is SHORT.
- `03#22 Selling a stock at a loss and buying it back 3 game days later disallows the loss and adds it to the new lot's basis; buying it back 6 game days later does not`:
  - Buy 100 at $10, sell 100 at $8 (loss $200), rebuy 100 at $8.10 on day +3: the loss is disallowed ($200); the new lot's basis is $810 + $200 = $1,010 and `washSaleAdjust = $200`.
  - Rebuy on day +6 instead: no adjustment, and the $200 loss counts.
  - Edge 6 variant: the rebuy is made by the chr's 100% company on day +2 → disallowed, and the basis is adjusted on the company's lot.
  - Look-back variant: a buy on day −2 and a sale of the older lot at a loss on day 0 → disallowed against the day −2 lot.
- `lots.test.ts`:
  - FIFO across 3 lots with a partial lot;
  - SPECIFIC with `acct.lots.select` (LIC1 → `ACCT_LICENSE_REQUIRED`);
  - a 2:1 split keeps total basis and doubles qty;
  - a foreign asset bought at FX 1.10 and sold at 1.20 includes the FX gain;
  - a related-party sale at 150% uses the band-edge proceeds.
- `gains.test.ts`:
  - netting short loss against long gain;
  - a carry-forward used only against gains;
  - a home owner-occupied for 100 days with a $12M gain → $10M exempt;
  - a lifestyle loss is not deductible;
  - a derivative gain is always short;
  - `progressiveTax(2,000,000 FDL)` = $315,200 (shared with 03#20 in T03-21);
  - Serena has no personal brackets.

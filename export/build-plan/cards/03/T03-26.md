## Goal
Personal insolvency of a character (03 §3.14.5, L13 "bankruptcy comeback"). T03-25 provides the generic case machinery (`openCase`, claims, `waterfall`, `closeCase`). This card adds what is specific to a chr:
- **Trigger**: a due obligation is past grace and available money is exhausted, or `reviewEquity` < 0 at day close; also voluntary filing and 14's Fresh Start declaration.
- **Protected floor**: against crime and court debts only (fines, restitution, judgments), the chr keeps $5,000 of liquid money (real $ × MSI) and one T1 business. It never protects against trading or borrowing losses.
- **Liquidation**: requests to the venue owners for every non-protected asset (exchange orders 05, estate sales 09/10/11, auctions 06, close-outs 07, redemptions 08).
- **Two-pass waterfall**: the non-protected estate pays every claim; the protected assets pay only claims that are not crime or court debts.
- **Discharge, except persistent claims**: CCP deficits, restitution, fines, judgments and taxes move to `2990:<creditorId>`. They are collected by garnishing `PARAM.comeback_garnish_rate` (0.30, E-21) of every later income inflow, without interest.
- **Fresh start**: if money after discharge is below `fresh_start_cash_real` × MSI, 03 asks 14 to post `ACCT_FRESH_START` for the difference. The cash lands as restricted cash (T03-12, 51,840 ticks). At most once per rolling `fresh_start_cooldown_real_days` = 56 real days; 14 owns the cooldown and sets it through a fact.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kinds.** Add to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`:
   - `BIZ_TIER`: subject bizId, value 'T1' | 'T2' | … (09);
   - `FRESH_START_COOLDOWN_UNTIL`: subject chrId, value Tick (14 pushes it when it posts `ACCT_FRESH_START`: grantTick + `fresh_start_cooldown_real_days` × 17,280);
   - `CRIME_COURT_DEBT`: subject the liability key (2550/2560 sub), value 'FINE' | 'RESTITUTION' | 'JUDGMENT' (13 pushes it when it books the payable).
1. **Trigger** (rule 1). `personalTrigger` handles chr debtors only (T03-25 step 2 hands them over):
   - **payment default**: at P13_POST every tick, for each chr that gets a DEFAULTED obligation this tick (from `runDefaults`), or has one still open, check exhaustion. Money is exhausted when `liquidMoney(chr, reportingCcy) ≤ floorFor(chr)`. Here `floorFor` is `PROTECTED_FLOOR_REAL × MSI`, translated, when every defaulted obligation is a crime/court debt (`CRIME_COURT_DEBT` fact on its liability, or kind `JUDGMENT`), and 0 otherwise. If exhausted → `openPersonalCase(chr, 'PAYMENT_DEFAULT')`.
   - **balance sheet**: at DAY_CLOSE P13_POST, after `runBalanceSheetTest`, a chr with `reviewEquity < 0` → `openPersonalCase(chr, 'BALANCE_SHEET')`. Persistent 2990 balances are added back, so a discharged chr is not reopened for claims a case cannot discharge.
   - **voluntary**: `acct.insolvency.file` PERSONAL (T03-25 step 4) and 14's Fresh Start declaration (14 §3.13.1; message `acct.insolvency.personal.open`, validated by 14) both call `openPersonalCase(chr, 'VOLUNTARY')`. Reject `ACCT_ALREADY_IN_CASE` when a case is open.
   - `openPersonalCase` calls T03-25's `openCase(chr, trigger, 'PERSONAL')`, which freezes the chr and builds and tags the claims. It then computes `protectedSet` and writes the `PersonalCaseRow` with stage SELLING. Finally it calls `requestSales(…, false)`. All of this is state only, in P13_POST.
   - Status changes for the chr: ACTIVE → IN_INSOLVENCY. Events are PRIVATE to the chr (T03-25).
2. **Protected floor** (rule 2; 03-conflict §13.5).
   - `floorApplies` = at least one admitted claim is a crime/court debt. Crime/court debts are claims on 2550 or 2560 whose liability carries `CRIME_COURT_DEBT`; their `persistentKind` is FINE, RESTITUTION or JUDGMENT.
   - `protectedCash` = `min(PROTECTED_FLOOR_REAL × msiValue, liquidMoney)`, in the chr's reporting ccy (translated from FDL with `translate`, CLOSE). Restricted lots are not liquid money and never count toward it.
   - `protectedBizId`: among biz entities with `ownerId = chr` and `BIZ_TIER` = 'T1', the one with the highest `valueAccount` of its 1580 account; ties go to the smallest id. If there is none, it is undefined.
   - When `floorApplies` is false, nothing is protected (rule 2: the floor does not protect against trading or borrowing losses).
3. **Liquidation requests** (rule 3). `requestSales(caseId, includeProtected)`:
   - For every asset account of the chr with a non-zero balance or quantity whose code is in `VENUE_OF`, send one message `ctx.send(chrId, 'acct.insolvency.estate.sell', { caseId, assetKey, assetId, qty, venue, sellerId: chrId }, 'NEXT')`. The venue module (`VENUE_OF[code].module`) subscribes to that message kind. Skip the 1580 account of `protectedBizId`, unless `includeProtected` is true.
   - `VENUE_OF`:
     - 1500, 1510, 1520, 1540, 1560, 1210 → 05 EXCHANGE_ORDER (normal orders inside fair-value bands, 05);
     - 1530 → 08 REDEMPTION;
     - 1541, 1542 → 06 EXCHANGE_ORDER (pool exits);
     - 1600, 1050 → 07 CLOSE_OUT;
     - 1570, 1580 (biz and cmp), 1200, 1830, 1850 → 09 ESTATE_SALE;
     - 1580 (prop), 1800, 1810, 1740 → 10 ESTATE_SALE. 1580 is routed by the kind of the controlled entity.
     - 1700, 1710, 1720, 1840, 1220 → 11 ESTATE_SALE;
     - 1860, 1870 → 06 AUCTION (auction house).
   - Receivables (1100–1306, 1160) are not sold. They stay estate assets and are collected as they fall due. Escrow 1070 follows T03-25 step 6.
   - Each request is recorded in `requests`. The venue answers `acct.insolvency.estate.done { caseId, assetKey, sold }`, and `onSaleDone` sets `done` and `sold`. Proceeds arrive through the venue's normal codes, with the chr as seller (the ESTATE_CODES exemption of the freeze).
   - `sold: false` means there was no bid at any price. The asset then stays in the estate and returns with the chr at close.
4. **Stage machine** (`runPersonalCases`, DAY_CLOSE P2; its distributions are cash PAYs, so they run before B3). T03-25's `runDistributions` skips PERSONAL cases.
   - SELLING → DIST_A once every request is done. DIST_A runs pass A:
     - `estateA` = `liquidMoney(chr)` − `protectedCash` (if `floorApplies`) − the admin reserve;
     - `personalWaterfall` pays every claim in `RANK_ORDER` (secured from its own collateral first, as T03-25 `waterfall`);
     - post one `INSOLV_DISTRIBUTION` PAY per paid claim (legs as in T03-25 step 7).
   - After pass A:
     - if some claims that are not crime/court remain unpaid, and protected value exists (`protectedCash > 0` or `protectedBizId`), go to SELLING_PROTECTED: `requestSales(…, true)` for the protected biz;
     - otherwise go to CLOSING.
   - SELLING_PROTECTED → DIST_B once the request is done. DIST_B runs pass B: `protectedB = protectedCash` plus the biz proceeds, which pay only claims with `crimeCourt = false`, in rank order and pro rata (`allocate`). Anything left stays with the chr.
   - CLOSING: call T03-25 `closeCase(caseId)`. It discharges the unpaid non-persistent claims (`INSOLV_DEBT_DISCHARGE`) and moves the persistent claims (CCP_DEFICIT, RESTITUTION, FINE, JUDGMENT, TAX) to `2990:<creditorId>`. The chr returns to ACTIVE.
   - Then call `recordPersistent` and `freshStartCheck`, and set the stage to CLOSED.
   - The protected biz and protected cash are never offered to a crime/court creditor in any pass. The part of a crime/court debt that they would have covered becomes a persistent 2990 claim (rule 2, "anything beyond becomes garnishment").
5. **`personalWaterfall`** (pure):
   - `paidA` = T03-25 `waterfall({ estateCash: estateA, claims })`;
   - `paidB` = `waterfall({ estateCash: protectedB, claims: unpaid-after-A filtered to !crimeCourt })`;
   - `unpaid` = outstanding − paidA − paidB.
   Every split uses `allocate` (largest remainder, ties by claim id), so Σ paid equals the cash used exactly.
6. **Persistent claims** (rule 4).
   - `recordPersistent` writes one `PersistentRow` per (chr, creditor), with the kind from the claim.
   - The 2990 balance is the truth. `collected` is a statistic.
   - No interest: `registerAccrual` rejects 2990 keys (`LED_PERSISTENT_NO_INTEREST`, T03-15). A later personal case carries 2990 forward intact (T03-25 `buildClaims` excludes it).
7. **Garnishment** (rule 4, 30%, E-21).
   - `accumulateGarnish` runs at P13_POST every tick, after commit. For each chr of the partition with Σ 2990 > 0:
     - `base` per ccy = (a) money received on the chr's unrestricted money accounts from committed valid entries whose primary reason has a tax tag in `GARNISH_TAGS`, plus `LED_INTRAGROUP_SWEEP` from the chr's businesses (business sweeps: dividends, interest, rent, royalties, business, service and other income); plus (b) for every `RealizedGain` row of the chr with `tick = now` and `gain > 0`, the gain (realized trading gains; not the sale proceeds);
     - add `floor(base × comeback_garnish_rate)` to `led.garnpend[chr].byCcy`.
     - Inflows from EXEMPT, P2P, faucet and refund codes are not income and are not garnished. Restricted lots are never garnished.
   - `collectGarnish` runs at P2 of the next tick, before any intent of that tick, so the chr cannot spend the money first. For each pending row:
     - `amt = min(pending, availableFor(chr, ccy, 'LAW_GARNISHMENT'), Σ outstanding in ccy)`. Outstanding in another ccy is converted with `translate` CLOSE at collection;
     - split `amt` across the chr's 2990 creditors pro rata to their outstanding (`allocate`, ties by creditor id), each part capped at its outstanding;
     - for CCP_DEFICIT, RESTITUTION, JUDGMENT and TAX, post `LAW_GARNISHMENT` PAY with chr → creditor and `requestedBy { module: '13', phase: 'P2', entityId: chr }`. 13 owns the code, and 03 posts it as the owner of 2990, the same pattern as `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY`. The value legs are `valueLegA = 2990:<creditorId>` and `valueLegB` = the creditor's receivable from the claim (`receivableKey`), or `1160:<caseId>`;
     - for FINE, place `placeCashFor(chr, ccy, part, tick + 720, 'garn:<chr>:<tick>', 'LAW_FINE_SINK')` and send `ctx.send(<court of the chr's bloc>, 'law.garnishment.persistent', { debtorId, creditorId, kind: 'FINE', amount: part, holdId, liabilityKey }, 'NEXT')`. 13 posts its fine split (`LAW_FINE_SINK`/`LAW_FINE_VICTIM_FUND`/`LAW_FINE_WHISTLE_POOL`) consuming the hold, with `valueLegA = liabilityKey`;
     - the unpaid pending amount carries over; delete the row when it reaches zero;
     - when a creditor's 2990 balance reaches 0, delete its `PersistentRow` and emit `ledger.persistent.paid` PRIVATE.
   - Garnishment never uses restricted cash: `LAW_GARNISHMENT` and the LAW_FINE_* codes are not `restrictedOk`.
8. **Fresh start** (rules 5–6; R17; 14 §3.13).
   - `freshStartCheck` after close:
     - `money = liquidMoney(chr, reportingCcy)` plus the chr's restricted lots;
     - `target = translate(fresh_start_cash_real × msiValue, FDL → reportingCcy, CLOSE)`;
     - if `money ≥ target` → NOT_NEEDED;
     - else if `getFact(FRESH_START_COOLDOWN_UNTIL, chr) > tick` → COOLDOWN (a second insolvency inside the 56 real days gets no top-up);
     - else GRANTED: send `ctx.send(chr, 'life.freshStart.topup', { chrId, caseId, amount: target − money, ccy: reportingCcy }, 'NEXT')`.
   - 14 then posts `ACCT_FRESH_START` (MINT, owner 14). The MINT credits `1001:RESTRICTED:<tick+51840>:FRESH_START` (T03-07 / T03-12 `RESTRICTED_FAUCETS`), and 14 pushes the cooldown fact. Reputation, the perk, the leverage and credit blocks and the Legacy record belong to 14; licences are kept.
   - Emit `ledger.insolvency.freshstart` `{ chrId, caseId, topUp, reason }` PRIVATE.
   - The restricted lot cannot fund ops (12), margin, derivatives, insider actions or P2P for 51,840 ticks. It can fund spot trades, B1–B2 businesses, upgrades and lifestyle (T03-12 `restrictedOk`).
9. **Edge 12** (03 §8): a fresh start and a fine in the same tick. The fine cannot be paid from the new restricted lot (`LAW_FINE_*` codes are not `restrictedOk`, T03-12). 13 books it as payable 2550 (with `CRIME_COURT_DEBT`), and it is garnished later once it becomes persistent.
10. **Registration** (T03-28 wires these):
    - close steps: `personalTrigger` (P13_POST, every tick and DAY_CLOSE), `runPersonalCases` (P2, DAY_CLOSE), `accumulateGarnish` (P13_POST every tick), `collectGarnish` (P2 every tick);
    - message handlers: `acct.insolvency.personal.open` and `acct.insolvency.estate.done`.

## Goal
Pure functions that turn "A pays B amount X in currency c" into exact double-entry postings through the right money accounts (settlement cash at the central bank, reserves, bank deposits), following the payment-rail table of 03 §3.3.4 including the central bank's holder sub-accounts, the draw order when the default account lacks funds, restricted-cash draw order, and the posting well-formedness rules of 03 §3.3.2. Every template (T03-07) uses these cash legs, so correctness here is what keeps I5 and I6 true.

## Implementation steps
1. Sign convention (03 §3.3.1): balances are signed; a debit adds, a credit subtracts; displayed sign flips for credit-normal classes (display is 17's; store the signed value).
2. `normalizeDraft` and `validateDraft` (03 §3.3.2): rule 1 `amount > 0n` (except QTY_ONLY codes, T03-05), `debitAccount !== creditAccount`, both keys' ccy equal `ccy`; rule 2 if `qty` present then `assetId` present and both accounts' `sub` (or the account's asset) refer to the same `assetId`: accounts holding quantities are keyed with `sub = assetId` (or `cmpId:shareClass` for 1570, where the asset id is `cmpId:shareClass`). Messages: `AMOUNT_NOT_POSITIVE`, `SAME_ACCOUNT`, `CCY_MISMATCH`, `QTY_ASSET_MISMATCH`, `QTY_WITHOUT_ASSET`.
3. `netByEntityCcy`: for each posting add `+amount` to the debit account's entity and `-amount` to the credit account's entity in that ccy, skipping 9xxx codes; a balanced entry has every value 0n (I2, "including 3800 accounts").
4. Money codes per kind (`defaultMoneyCode`): `bnk` and `ccp` pay and receive through `1002` (reserves; 03 §3.3.4 last row); `gov` through `1003` sub `GENERAL`; `cbk` pays by issuing (kind `CB_ISSUE`, no own money account); elastic kinds never use the rail (their virtual 1009 is handled by ELASTIC templates); every other kind `1001`.
5. `receivingAccount(payee)`: if `payee.receiveInto` is `{ kind: 'BANK', bankId }`, the bank is not frozen and `st.tick >= railChangeTick` → `payee:1010:<bankId>:<ccy>`; else the default money account of its kind. A frozen bank's deposits receive nothing new from tick N+1 (03 §8 edge 2): fall back to 1001.
6. `payingSources(payer, ccy, amount, opts)`:
   - If `opts.fixedSources` (the hold's reserved parts, T03-08): take from them in order up to each part's `amount`; any remainder continues with the normal order below.
   - Else ordered candidates: (a) if `opts.restrictedOk`, restricted lots of 1001 in `restrictedLots` order (03 §3.5.6 "spending draws restricted cash first for allowed purposes"); (b) the default paying account (`payFrom`, rail change rule as step 5; a frozen bank deposit is skipped); (c) unrestricted `1001`; (d) deposits in bank-id order (03 §3.3.4: "default paying account, then 1001, then deposits in order of bank id"); duplicates removed.
   - Take `min(spendable, remaining)` from each in order; if amount remains after all candidates, add the remainder to the first unrestricted candidate (the default paying account) so the shortfall surfaces as a negative balance and fails I10 at P13 (03 §3.3.2 rule 5: modules must hold; the ledger never silently skips).
   - `cbk` payer: one source `CB_ISSUE`.
7. `cashLeg(payer, payee, amount)` emits, per source and in this order, the postings of the 03 §3.3.4 table (holder sub-accounts on the cbk of `ccy`: `cbk:2001:<holder>` for settlement cash, `cbk:2002:<bankId>` for reserves (banks and the CCP), `cbk:2003:<govId>` for treasury accounts):
   - CASH → CASH: `Dr B:1001 / Cr A:1001` and `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2001:B`.
   - DEPOSIT at X → CASH: `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr B:1001 / Cr X:1002`; `Dr cb:2002:X / Cr cb:2001:B`.
   - CASH → DEPOSIT at Y: `Dr Y:1002 / Cr A:1001`; `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2002:Y`; `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B`.
   - DEPOSIT at X → DEPOSIT at Y, X ≠ Y: `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr Y:1002 / Cr X:1002`; `Dr cb:2002:X / Cr cb:2002:Y`; `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B`.
   - DEPOSIT at X → DEPOSIT at X: `Dr X:2100:A / Cr X:2100:B`; `Dr B:1010:X / Cr A:1010:X` (no reserves move).
   - RESERVES (bank A) and TREASURY (gov A) sources behave as CASH with codes 1002 / 1003:GENERAL and holder subs 2002 / 2003; a bank paying into its own customers' deposits (payee deposit at A itself): `Dr B:1010:A / Cr A:2100:B` and the bank's own side `Dr <value leg> ...` is the template's; reserves do not move (bank own-account flow, 03 §3.5.3).
   - Payer pays from its DEPOSIT at X and the payee is the bank X itself: `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X` only (the deposit is extinguished; X's side is completed by the template's value leg; a "bank own-account flow", 03 §3.5.3). Mirror: bank X pays a payee that receives into a deposit at X: `Dr B:1010:X / Cr X:2100:B`.
   - CB_ISSUE (payer is the cbk): `Dr B:<money> / Cr cb:<2001|2002|2003>:B`; if the payee receives into a deposit at Y, `Dr Y:1002 / Cr cb:2002:Y` and `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B`.
   - Payee is the cbk: mirror (`Dr cb:<holder sub of A> / Cr A:<money>`), destroying base money.
   - Sub-accounts of 1001 (RESTRICTED lots) are distinct accounts of the same entity; the holder sub on the cbk is the same `2001:A` for all of them.
   - Same payer and payee and same account: no postings (a sweep between sub-accounts is `MAC_TREASURY_SWEEP` with explicit subs, `opts.payeeSub`).
8. Multi-currency (03 §3.3.6): the rail is single-currency; cross-currency payments are always the `FX` template (T03-07). `NW_R` translation lives in T03-17.
9. Edge cases owned here (03 §8): 1 (draw order, holds on actual source: `fixedSources`), 2 (frozen bank deposits receive/pay nothing from N+1), 17 (fee rounding by `mulRate` half-even may give 0n: a 0n draft is dropped by `normalizeDraft`, never an error), 18 (negative accrual amounts swap sides).

## Goal
This task covers the life cycle of a tax liability, following the 03 §3.12.14 table:
- the running estimate (state, kept by T03-21);
- quarterly installments, `max(0, YTD estimate × q/4 − paid so far)` for personal income, CGT and corporate, payable within 7 game days;
- the annual assessment at year end, with the balance due within 7 game days and any overpayment refunded;
- late interest, daily at the bloc policy rate + 3% (Act/364). It is suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (03 §8 edge 10). After 28 game days unpaid, the treasury files a garnishment with 13.

It also implements:
- **Audits.** Personal odds per quarter and corporate odds per year, drawn as Bernoulli trials from stream `ledger.audit_sample:<bloc>`. Findings are deterministic: (a) unexplained wealth from illicit-coded inflows, taxed at the top rate + 20% penalty and passed to 13 as evidence; (b) a false Serena substance claim, which triggers CFC reassessment + 20%; (c) nothing else. A clean audit is free and gives +2 Reputation (14).
- **Intents** `acct.tax.payEarly` and `acct.tax.dispute`.
- **The I22 sub-check** of INV-LED-010: tax accounts reconcile to the 2400/2410/2420 balances.

Audit content stays abstract: the engine reads reason codes and amounts only.

## Implementation steps
1. **Due rows and payments.**
   - Every amount that becomes due creates a `led.taxdue` row with `dueTick = tick + PAYMENT_WINDOW_TICKS` (7 game days) and emits `ledger.tax.due` `{ taxAccountId, amount, dueTick }`, PRIVATE to the taxpayer.
   - `payDue` posts `ctx.ledger.pay(entity, gov_<j>, amt, ccy, TAX_<type>, { valueLegA: 2400, valueLegB: 1130, sub: '<j>:<type>:<year>' })`. The code depends on the type:

     | Type | Code |
     |---|---|
     | PERSONAL_INCOME | `TAX_PERSONAL_INCOME` |
     | CAPITAL_GAINS | `TAX_CAPITAL_GAINS` |
     | CORPORATE | `TAX_CORPORATE` |
     | EXIT | `TAX_EXIT` |
     | AUDIT | `TAX_AUDIT_ASSESSMENT` |

   - It then updates `paid` on the row and the TaxAccount, and emits `ledger.tax.paid`.
   - Restricted cash can never pay tax: `TAX_*` codes are not `restrictedOk`.
   - Payments are TRANSFERs to the treasury, which lives on FINANCE. They are therefore queued in P1 (intent) or P2 (DAY_OPEN `collectDue`), never in P13.
   - `collectDue` (DAY_OPEN, P2): for each unpaid row with `tick ≥ dueTick − PAYMENT_WINDOW_TICKS`, pay `min(outstanding, available(entity, ccy))`. This automatic pay-down keeps payments on time when cash exists.
2. **Installments** (`runInstallments`, close step 8, QUARTER_CLOSE, P13_PRE; state only). For each taxpayer and each type in {PERSONAL_INCOME, CAPITAL_GAINS, CORPORATE}:
   - `ytd` = the T03-21 estimate;
   - `q = ctx.cal.quarter` within the year (1–4);
   - `amt = installmentAmount(ytd, q, paidSoFar)`;
   - if `amt > 0`, create a TaxDue of kind INSTALLMENT.
3. **Annual assessment** (`runAnnualAssessment`, close step 9 order 10, YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE; state only). For each taxpayer and each jurisdiction segment:
   - Compute the final liabilities by type:
     - personal income with residency segments (T03-22), CFC attributions and transfer-pricing adjustments;
     - CGT (`capitalGainsYear`);
     - corporate (`corporateYear`, grouped when in a tax group).
   - Subtract credits (accounting firm, foreign tax via `foreignTaxCredit`, CFC credits) and `withheld`.
   - `balance = total − credits − paid − withheld`:
     - a positive balance creates a TaxDue of kind ASSESSMENT;
     - a negative balance schedules a refund for the next DAY_OPEN (`refundOverpayments`: `TAX_REFUND` PAY from gov to the taxpayer, legs 1130/2400).
   - Write `led.assessment`, set the TaxAccount status to ASSESSED, and move the foreign tax lost to 6020 (`LED_TAX_PROVISION` with `valueLegA` 6020).
   - It runs before `rollLossCarryForwards` (order 20) and `LED_PERIOD_CLOSE` (order 50).
4. **Late interest** (`runLateInterest`, DAY_OPEN, P2). For each row with `paid < amount` and `tick > dueTick`:
   - **First late day.** Set `lateSinceTick` and emit `ledger.tax.late`.
   - **Accrual.** Register an accrual (T03-15, kind INTEREST, `principalKey` = the row's payable key, rate `{ fact: 'POLICY_RATE_E6', subject: bloc, spreadE6: 30000 }`), so interest accrues daily on Act/364 as `LED_ACCRUAL`. It is settled with `TAX_LATE_INTEREST` (legs 5090/4020) when the row is paid.
   - **Court freeze.** While `COURT_FREEZE` is true for the entity, end the accrual; it is re-opened when the freeze lifts. Tax still accrues; only the late interest is suspended (03 §8 edge 10).
   - **Garnishment.** When `tick − dueTick ≥ GARNISH_AFTER_TICKS` and `!garnishmentFiled`, set the flag and `ctx.send(<court of the bloc>, 'law.garnishment.file', { creditorId: gov_<j>, debtorId, amount: outstanding, ref: dueId }, 'NEXT')`. 13 owns the case.
   - Fines (§8 edge 12) are not tax. They remain 13's payables.
5. **Audits** (`runAuditSelection`, close step 8 order 20, QUARTER_CLOSE).
   - **Personal**, every quarter, for each chr resident in bloc b:
     - `p = personalAuditOdds(LIFESTYLE_SPEND_4Q, declaredIncome4Q, TIPS_4Q)`;
     - draw `ctx.rng('ledger.audit_sample:' + b).bernoulli(p)` in sorted chr id order (02 §3.10.4: one stream per bloc, so draws are independent of other modules).
   - **Corporate**, at the quarter that closes the year, for each group root company in bloc b:
     - `p = corporateAuditOdds(serenaShare, relatedPartyShare, illicit)`;
     - `serenaShare` = the group's assets in Serena companies / its total assets;
     - `relatedPartyShare` = revenue from related parties / revenue (from T03-18 `intraGroup` and the rpflags);
     - `illicit` = any ILLICIT-coded posting touched the group in the year;
     - the same stream is drawn after the personal draws.
   - For each selected entity:
     - create an `AuditCase` with TaxAccount status UNDER_AUDIT;
     - emit `ledger.tax.audit.selected` `{ entityId, scope }`, PRIVATE to the taxpayer;
     - compute the findings at once, with `ledger.tax.audit.finding` SEALED until 13 discloses it.
   - **Findings** (`computeFindings`, deterministic from the ledger):
     - **(a) UNEXPLAINED_WEALTH.** `amount` = Σ inflows to the entity (and its controlled group) in the audit window (4 quarters personal, the year corporate) on entries whose code is in `ILLICIT_CODES`. `tax = topPersonalRate(bloc) × amount` and `penalty = 0.20 × tax`. `entryIds` are passed to 13 as evidence: `ctx.send(<court/regulator of the bloc>, 'law.evidence.ledger', { entityId, entryIds, kind: 'UNEXPLAINED_WEALTH' }, 'NEXT')`. 13 assigns 20–40 points per open triple (03-conflict §10.3).
     - **(b) FALSE_SUBSTANCE.** For a Serena company with `SERENA_ACTIVE_CLAIM` whose CFC rows have `substance false` in any claimed year: `tax` = the CFC reassessment of every such year (`computeCfc` with active status denied) and `penalty = 0.20 × tax`.
     - **(c)** Nothing else.
   - `settleAudit` (next DAY_OPEN, P2):
     - creates the AUDIT TaxDue rows: `TAX_AUDIT_ASSESSMENT` for the tax, and `TAX_PENALTY` (legs 5210/4190) for the penalty;
     - closes the case;
     - restores the account status;
     - a clean audit (no findings) costs nothing: `ctx.send(chr, 'life.reputation.adjust', { delta: +2, reason: 'audit_clean' }, 'NEXT')` to 14.
6. **Intents** (G9 codes):
   - **`acct.tax.payEarly`**, applyPhase P1.
     - validate: the actor controls the entity → else `ACCT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`; `amount ≤ outstanding estimate − paid` → else `ACCT_EXCEEDS_ESTIMATE`; `amount ≤ available(entity, ccy)` → else `ACCT_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, and a hold is placed.
     - apply: `payDue` against the open year's TaxAccount (creates a prepayment row), so later late interest is avoided.
   - **`acct.tax.dispute`**, applyPhase P11.
     - validate: the assessment exists, is ≤ 28 game days old (`tick − assessment.tick ≤ 28 × 720`) and belongs to an entity the actor controls → else `ACCT_DISPUTE_INVALID`.
     - apply: mark `disputed` and `ctx.send(<tax tribunal of the bloc>, 'law.case.tax_dispute.open', { assessmentId, groundsKey, entityId }, 'NEXT')`.
     - Payment is suspended only if 13 grants it, via fact `COURT_FREEZE` or a 13 message `acct.tax.suspend` handled as a fact.
7. **I22** (`I22_SUBCHECK`, DAY_CLOSE; reported under INV-LED-010). For each (entity, jurisdiction):
   - `Σ (liability − paid − withheld)` over the open and assessed TaxAccounts of types written to 2400 must equal `−balance(2400:<j>:*)`;
   - the same holds for VAT against 2410 and for withholding agents against 2420.
   A mismatch → `ctx.fail('INV-LED-010', [entity], 'I22 ...')`.

## Test plan
- `03#27 An unpaid quarterly installment accrues late interest daily at policy rate + 3% and triggers a garnishment filing at 28 game days`:
  - FDL policy rate 4%. An installment of $100,000 is due on day D+7 and left unpaid; the chr has no cash.
  - Assert:
    - each day after D+7, one `LED_ACCRUAL` of `accrue(100,000, 0.07, 1)` = $19.230769 (half-even);
    - on day D+7+28 a `law.garnishment.file` message is sent to the bloc court, exactly once;
    - with `COURT_FREEZE` true on days 10–12, no late interest accrues on those days.
- `03#28 An audit of a character with OPS_RANSOM inflows produces an unexplained-wealth finding, tax at the top rate + 20% penalty, and an evidence event for 13; an audit of a clean character produces no finding`:
  - Force selection with a fixed seed, or a test param making p = 1.
  - A chr with a $1M `OPS_RANSOM` inflow (FDL top rate 35%) gets a finding of tax $350,000 and penalty $70,000, a `law.evidence.ledger` message listing the entry, and the finding event SEALED.
  - A clean chr gets no finding and a +2 reputation message.
  - The draws for the same seed are identical between runs, and independent of other modules' RNG use.
- `installments.test.ts`:
  - `installmentAmount(400k, 2, 100k) = 100k`, and with 250k already paid it is 0;
  - an annual assessment with overpayment → `TAX_REFUND` the next day;
  - payEarly beyond the estimate → `ACCT_EXCEEDS_ESTIMATE`;
  - a dispute on day 29 → `ACCT_DISPUTE_INVALID`.
- `taxAccounts.test.ts` (I22 negative test): a test hook adds $1 to 2400 without a TaxAccount change → INV-LED-010 fails with I22; a clean year passes.

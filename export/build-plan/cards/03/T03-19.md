## Goal
Every entity's statements are a projection of its accounts and postings. Nothing is entered by hand (03 §3.13.1). This task builds the `FinancialStatement` of 03 §2.10 for any entity, scope (SOLO or CONSOLIDATED) and period (FLASH, DAY, MONTH, QUARTER, YEAR, CHAPTER). The statement contains:
- the income statement, on the exact 03 §3.13.2 account mapping, with a "Personal statement" variant for characters;
- the balance sheet, current/non-current, with a liquidity-order variant for banks and insurers;
- the direct-method cash-flow statement by reason-code `cf` class;
- equity changes;
- the KPIs of §3.13.5.

Statements are materialized at period close right after the P13 commit (close stage `P13_POST`, state written by 03 in P13; 03 §3.13.1 says P14, but 02 runs no module work after commit except event packaging) and kept per the §3.13.6 cadence. `ledger.statement.ready` is emitted for each. Statements are published only for listed and chartered entities: on the earnings date, or to the regulator. Characters' statements are never visible to anyone else (L11).

This task also implements three intents: `acct.statement.request`, `acct.ledger.export` and `acct.payment.setAccounts`.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kinds.** Add to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`:
   - `LISTED`: subject entityId, boolean (05);
   - `PUBLIC_FUND`: subject fndId, boolean (08);
   - `CHARTERED`: subject entityId, boolean (13);
   - `EARNINGS_DATE`: subject `${entityId}:${kind}:${index}`, the Tick (09);
   - `LOAN_MATURITY`: subject loanId, the Tick (07).
   If T03-01 lacks the 03 §2.10 `FinancialStatement` and `StatementLine` interfaces, add them verbatim (they are 03-owned types).
1. **Income statement** (`INCOME_LINES`, 03 §3.13.2). The lines are, in order:

   | Line | Accounts |
   |---|---|
   | Revenue | 4000, 4010, 4040, 4050, 4140 |
   | Cost of sales | 5000 |
   | **Gross profit** | subtotal |
   | Operating expenses | 5010, 5020, 5030, 5040, 5050, 5060, 5070, 5080, 5100, 5110, 5230, 5240, 5250, 5260, 5270, 5280 |
   | **EBITDA** | subtotal |
   | D&A | 5120, 5130 |
   | **EBIT** | subtotal |
   | Net interest | 4020 − 5090 |
   | Investment result | 4030, 4060 − 5160, 4070 − 5170, 4150, plus FX = 4080 − 5180 + the translated 3800 movement of the period |
   | Other items | 4090 − 5190, 4100, 4110, 4120, 4130, 4190, −5140, −5150, −5200, −5210, −5220 |
   | **Profit before tax** | subtotal |
   | Income tax | 6000, 6010, 6020 |
   | **Net income** | for CONSOLIDATED: split into attributable to owners / to NCI (T03-18 `nciResult`) |

   - Amounts are `−periodNet` for income accounts (credit-normal) and `periodNet` for expenses, summed over subs.
   - The line `children` are the account codes with their labels (`acct.<code>`), so the Books screen can drill down to the Ledger explorer.
   - For characters: `labelKey 'stmt.personal'`, and lifestyle consumption (5200) is its own line taken out of Other items.
   - FLASH uses the current period index of DAY.
2. **Balance sheet** (03 §3.13.3).
   - Groups:
     - current assets: 1001–1230 except long-dated items, loans with `LOAN_MATURITY − tick < 364 × 720`, and marketable securities 1500–1560;
     - non-current assets: other loans, 1570–1880, 1900;
     - current liabilities: 2001–2160, 2400–2590, and loans due < 364 game days;
     - non-current liabilities: the other 2xxx;
     - equity: 3100–3990, plus 3300 as the period result so far, with NCI separately in CONSOLIDATED.
   - Contra accounts (1309, 1839, 3150) are shown netted under their parents, with the gross amount in `children`.
   - Banks (bnk) and insurers (ins) use `LIQUIDITY_ORDER` with no current/non-current split (07/08 presentation).
   - The balance sheet of the requested period is the balances at the period's last tick. For a closed period, build it at close time and store it; history is not re-derived.
3. **Cash flow** (direct method, 03 §3.13.4).
   - From `periodTotal(...).byCode` of every money account of the entity (1001 all subs, 1002, 1003, 1010; 1050 cash margin shown as "cash pledged" under FIN). Group by the code's `cf` (OP, INV, FIN), and within each heading by catalog family (`stabilizerFamily` or the code prefix), with inflows and outflows separate.
   - NC codes never appear.
   - The total equals Σ Δ money accounts over the period. Internal moves between the entity's own money accounts (`MAC_TREASURY_SWEEP`, deposit in/out between 1001 and 1010) cancel inside a heading and are shown in a "transfers between own accounts" memo line that nets to 0.
4. **Equity changes**: opening equity; net income; dividends declared (postings to 3200 with dividend codes); capital issued and redeemed (3100/3110/3150 movements); OCI (3400 Δ); FX (3800, and 3500 for CONSOLIDATED); period close reclass (`LED_PERIOD_CLOSE`, netting to 0 within equity); closing equity.
5. **KPIs** (03 §3.13.5), using `safeDiv` with fallback NaN, shown as "n/a" (never 0):
   - all entities: gross, EBITDA and net margin; ROE; ROA; debt/equity; net debt/EBITDA; interest cover (EBIT / net interest); current ratio; free cash flow (OP − capex in INV);
   - banks: capital ratio and liquidity coverage (read facts `BANK_CAPITAL_RATIO` / `BANK_LCR` from 07 when present, else n/a), net interest margin, cost/income;
   - insurers: loss, expense and combined ratios, solvency ratio (fact from 08);
   - funds: NAV per unit, return, drawdown, gross/net leverage (facts from 08);
   - characters: savings rate, lifestyle share of income, NW change and NWI change (T03-17/T03-24 values).
   KPI facts the owning modules do not supply show as n/a. These four KPI fact names (`BANK_CAPITAL_RATIO`, `BANK_LCR`, `INS_SOLVENCY_RATIO`, `FUND_KPIS`) are added to `LedgerFactKind` in step 0 as well.
6. **Materialize** (`materializeStatements`, registered as close step 10 in P13_POST for DAY_CLOSE, MONTH_CLOSE, QUARTER_CLOSE, YEAR_CLOSE and CHAPTER_CLOSE):
   - For each closing boundary, build SOLO for every entity of the partition (DAY: every entity; CHAPTER: characters and group parents only) and CONSOLIDATED for every chr and every group parent.
   - Write `led.stmt` with visibility:
     - OWNER by default;
     - REGULATOR for MONTH of chartered institutions;
     - QUARTER/YEAR of publishers are written as OWNER and queued for publication.
   - YEAR statements set `audited = true` for listed companies and chartered institutions when an audit-fee entry (`CMP_AUDIT_FEE_NPC` or `CMP_AUDIT_FEE_B2B`) exists for the entity in that year (from `byCode`).
   - Prune DAY rows older than 28 game days.
   - Emit `ledger.statement.ready` `{ entityId, period: { kind, index }, scope }`: PRIVATE to the owner (root), or PUBLIC when published.
7. **Publication** (`queuePublications` as close step 8, order 50, P13_PRE at QUARTER_CLOSE, and also YEAR via the same step at YEAR_CLOSE; `publishDue` every tick after the P13 commit):
   - When `tick >= EARNINGS_DATE` for that statement, set `visibility = 'PUBLIC'` and `publishedTick`, then emit `ledger.statement.ready` with PUBLIC visibility.
   - NPC listed companies publish too.
   - Characters' statements are never PUBLIC (L11). `isPublisher` returns false for chr and ali whatever the facts say.
   - Published statements are engine-generated and therefore true. Commentary is 15/13's concern.
8. **Access** (`canSeeStatement`):
   - the owner chain (`actorControls(viewer, entityId)`) sees everything;
   - REGULATOR rows are visible to the owner chain through this view; the regulator (13, an engine module) reads them directly from `led.stmt` in its own phases, never through a player view;
   - PUBLIC rows are visible to anyone;
   - FLASH is owner only;
   - `viewer.isAdmin` sees nothing extra here; ops reads go through 21's audited tools.
   Every 03 view that returns statement or posting data goes through this check (T03-27 reuses it).
9. **Intents** (00 §5.5 envelope; rejects in G9 `ACCT_*` form):
   - **`acct.statement.request`**, applyPhase P1.
     - validate: `actorControls(actor, entityId)` or (`isPublisher` and the period is published) → else `ACCT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. An unknown period index → `ACCT_NO_STATEMENT`.
     - apply: nothing is written. The data is read through `statementView` with the same check: a stored row, or for FLASH `buildStatement` at the current tick. It has no rate-limit cost beyond the API quota.
   - **`acct.ledger.export`**, applyPhase P1.
     - validate: `actorControls` → else `ACCT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`; `toTick − fromTick ≤ MAX_EXPORT_TICKS` and `fromTick ≤ toTick` → else `ACCT_RANGE_TOO_LONG`.
     - apply: nothing is written. `ledgerExportView` returns CSV or JSON of the entity's postings in the range from `led.entry` (the in-engine window of `ENTRY_RETENTION_TICKS`). For the older part it returns `archivedBefore`, and 19's export job fills that part from the postings archive with the same `ExportRow` format.
     - `counterpartyLabel` follows T03-09: never another player's id (L11).
   - **`acct.payment.setAccounts`**, applyPhase P1.
     - validate: `actorControls` → else `ACCT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Each `{ bank }` must be a `bnk` entity, not frozen (`BANK_FROZEN`), with an existing deposit account `E:1010:<bank>:<reportingCcy>` (opened by 07) → else `ACCT_NO_BANK_ACCOUNT`.
     - apply: write a `led.railchg` row with `effectiveTick = tick + 1`.
     - `applyRailChanges` (P13 after commit) copies due rows into `LedgerEntity.payFrom/receiveInto` and deletes them, so the change applies from the next tick (03 §4). Holds placed this tick keep their sources.
10. Register close steps with T03-15: `{ no: 10, name: 'statements', stage: 'P13_POST' }` for each close boundary, and `{ no: 8, order: 50, name: 'statementsQueued', stage: 'P13_PRE', when: 'QUARTER_CLOSE' }` plus the YEAR_CLOSE twin `statementsQueuedYear`.

## Test plan
- `03#40 Statements for every entity reconcile: Δmoney accounts = cash flow statement total; balance sheet balances; net income = Δretained earnings + dividends at year end`:
  - A seeded 2-game-year driver posts random valid entries of every class across 30 entities (chr, cmp, bnk, fnd, biz, prop, hh). After each DAY and YEAR close, for every stored statement assert:
    - `cashOk`: the CF total equals Σ Δ money accounts;
    - `balanceOk`: assets = liabilities + equity, including 3300;
    - at YEAR close, `retainedOk`: net income = Δ3200 (Δ3900 for chr) + dividends declared to 3200 in the year;
    - CONSOLIDATED statements also balance.

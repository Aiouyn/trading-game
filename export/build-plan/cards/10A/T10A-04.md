Make the map and the economy agree at tick 0, and keep the 1.5M units cheap by pooling unbound stock.

## Implementation steps
1. Develop parcels in priority order until RES units = households × 1.05 and the other uses match §3.19.7 within ±1%.
2. A pool key is `<districtId>|<use>|<grade>`; bound items (player-owned, listed, HQ, storefront in use) carry full simulation.
3. Carve and re-pool conserve area and occupied area to the m².
4. INV-BS-009: every property has exactly one holder (owner) at all times; pools are held by NPC owner entities.

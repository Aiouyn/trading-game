## Goal
Property earns and costs money every day: rent from tenants, upkeep and tax out. Players can house businesses and homes in the city.

## Imports
- T03-09 postings (rent, upkeep, management fees, property tax). T15A-03 lease contracts for player tenants; T15A-05 P2P caps and tax on player-to-player rent. T13A-06 gate (Held, freezes).

## Exports
```ts
export function unitRent(read, unitId): Money;   // area × R_{d,u} × gradeMult × conditionMult × amenityMult
export function propOps(ctx, propId): void;     // P9, hourly offset hash(propId) mod 30
```

## Implementation steps
1. Rent formula and examples of §3.6 (studio and ROOM vectors).
2. Leases: term, CPI or fixed indexation at anniversaries, deposit held in escrow (T15A-04 API), arrears and the EVICTING flow.
3. A related-party lease outside ±10% of market is refused at validation; within the band it is eliminated by 03 consolidation.
4. Player-to-player leases go through a 15A contract (±25% band) and `chargeP2P`.
5. Opex and condition decay (§3.7); management policy and NPC agency fees (§3.13).
6. Home mode switching is limited to once per 5,040 ticks (`COOLDOWN`).
7. Premises: a business with no free storefront enters pending placement in queue order (§3.15).

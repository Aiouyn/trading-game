## Goal
Close the remaining platform guarantees of 19 with checks over everything that leaves the servers: (a) hostile and legitimate `me.connections` entries are indistinguishable on the wire (19#28, 12 §3.7, threat E11); (b) presence never leaks except through the opt-in friends and alliance indicator (19#29, Rule 3.8.7, threat E12); (c) before reveal, no API, channel or log readable by non-world services exposes `S_e` or `entropy(N)` (19#38, Rule 3.12.3, threats E4, E21); (d) the world halt drill: `adm.halt_world` stops sealing after the current tick, clients show the diegetic halt overlay within one tick, and `adm.resume_world` resumes with no lost intents (19#59, §8 edge 20); (e) the rewarded-ad grant path: the platform submits `sys.ad_grant` and the engine rejects it at P1 when the character was at or above $1,000 NW (× MSI) at local-day start or for an 11th grant in a local day, even with a valid receipt (19#63, L12, threat E19).

## Implementation steps
1. Tap: a debug-only hook (refused in `prod` by config) mirrors every outbound byte stream with service, tick and viewer to a sink; scans run on staging captures and in CI on headless runs.
2. Connections (19#28): generate 1,000 `me.connections` payloads (12's generator, mixed legitimate and hostile entries per 12's rates), serialize through the real channel encoder, train the classifier on wire bytes only; pass when `|accuracy − baseRate| <= 0.02`. The guarantee comes from 12's identical serialization and rotating labels; this card provides the check.
3. Presence (19#29, Rule 3.8.7): presence enters the engine only as `sys.presence` and is never emitted (19 §5); the scan walks decoded frames, HTTP bodies and projection rows over 1 game day of a swarm session and flags any presence-like field about another character unless `allowedIndicator` (15's opt-in friends/alliance indicator) allows it for that viewer.
4. Foresight (19#38, Rule 3.12.3): the harness knows each epoch's `S_e` and every `entropy(N)`; the scan searches every outbound record and every service log line of non-world services (gateway, web, jobs, projector, harness-role replays) for any encoding of them; a hit timestamped before the epoch's reveal tick fails. After reveal, `sys.entropy_revealed` is expected and allowed.
5. Halt (19#59): during a swarm, staff submit `adm.halt_world` (2 approvals); the tick in which it is sealed completes and no further tick is sealed; `sys.world_status` reaches clients and `haltOverlay` turns visible within one tick; new intents during the halt are refused `WORLD_HALTED` (02 §3.20 precedence, 02 owns intake states) and counted; intents already forwarded but unsealed when the halt took effect are sealed in the first tick after `adm.resume_world`; nothing accepted is lost. A bus outage instead queues at gateways up to `gateway_queue_max` (200,000) then refuses (§8 edge 20), checked in the same drill by pausing the BATCH stream.
6. Ad grant (19#63): the platform path of 21 (receipt verified, session unique, age <= 30 min) submits `sys.ad_grant {characterId, receiptId, amount}` with a valid receipt; the engine handler (21) recomputes eligibility at P1 against `AdDayState` at local-day start: NW >= $1,000 × MSI -> `AD_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; the 11th grant (`ad_max_per_local_day` = 10) -> `AD_DAILY_CAP`; successful grants post exactly `ad_grant_real` ($250) × MSI into restricted cash. No other `sys.*` input carries money (§9 E19).

## Test plan
`scan.test.ts`: `scanSecrets` finds a secret embedded as base64url in a log line and in a JSON field, and ignores it after the reveal tick; `scanPresence` flags `{ friends: [{ id: other, online: true }] }` when the indicator is not opted in and passes when it is.
`scan.e2e.ts` (headless world with gateway and HTTP doubles, fixture modules, swarm of 200 SDK bots, 1 game day = 720 ticks; staging capture when `STAGING=1`):
- `19#28 For 1,000 generated me.connections payloads mixing legitimate and hostile entries, a classifier given only the wire bytes does no better than the base rate (accuracy within +-2 percentage points), matching 12's test 13`: accuracy within 0.02 of the base rate.
- `19#29 No event, channel body or HTTP response other than the opt-in friends/alliance indicator contains another character's presence; a scan of 1 game day of all outbound payloads finds none`: zero leaks.
- `19#38 Before reveal, no API, channel or log readable by non-world services exposes S_e or entropy(N); checked by secret scanning of all outbound payloads and service logs`: zero hits before reveal; a deliberately injected debug log of `entropy(500)` is caught (negative control).
`halt-drill.e2e.ts`:
- `19#59 adm.halt_world stops sealing after the current tick, clients show the diegetic halt overlay within one tick, and adm.resume_world resumes with no lost intents (queued intents are sealed on resume or refused if over the gateway queue limit)`: halt sealed at tick 300: last TickRecord 300 until resume; `overlayLagTicks <= 1` for all bots; `lostIntents === 0`; intents forwarded before the halt appear sealed at the first post-resume tick; intents sent during the halt are refused WORLD_HALTED; bus-pause variant: the 200,001st queued intent is refused.
`ad-grant.e2e.ts`:
- `19#63 A sys.ad_grant for a character at or above $1,000 NW at local-day start, or an 11th grant in a local day, is rejected by the engine at P1 even with a valid receipt (L12)`: character at $1,000.00 NW at local-day start with MSI 1.0 -> AD_NOT_ELIGIBLE; at $999.99 -> 10 grants succeed (each $250 restricted cash), the 11th -> AD_DAILY_CAP; a reused receipt -> AD_DUPLICATE_RECEIPT.

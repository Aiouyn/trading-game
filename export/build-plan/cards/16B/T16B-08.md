3. Mood, collapse and snap elections per §3.5.3. 4. Pipeline: INTRODUCED → COMMITTEE 14 GD → FLOOR next day close → PASSED/FAILED → ENACTED after lag → IN_FORCE; failed bills blocked 91 GD; tie (150 YES) fails; edge §8.4 re-clamp and NO_OP.
5. Values clamp to registry bounds and move ≤ maxChangePerBill; taxes take exactly the legislated value (no Stabilizer offset).
6. Central bank appointments and `h` per §3.5.10; players cannot lobby the central bank.

1. Field-for-field from §2.2–§2.4; money bigint micro; ids `npc_`, `dyn_`, `pty_`, `cau_`, `bil_`, `ele_`, `lob_` (in the E-26 id table and the lint allowed list, E-30).
2. `POLICY_LEVERS` is the §3.5.9a table verbatim (min, max, default, max change, lag, direction).
3. Reason codes (§3.14): `LOBBY_SPEND`→`WLD_LOBBYING_SPEND`, `POL_CONTRIBUTION`→`WLD_CAMPAIGN_DONATION` (aliases, E-30); `ISSUE_CAMPAIGN`, `PETITION_COMMIT` have rows (SINK ELASTIC_OUT 5240→4000 frm, amounts 'Quant', E-30). `CATALOG_GAPS_16B = []`.
4. Every §5 event of 16B scope (`world.activity` routing, `world.actor.*`, `world.dynasty.*`, `world.bill.stage`, `world.policy.inForce`, `world.election.result`, `world.lobby.*`) gets its schema and visibility.

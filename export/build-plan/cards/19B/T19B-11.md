## Goal
Write the partition snapshots of 19 Rule 3.7.3 without stalling ticks: every partition writes a `DAILY` snapshot of the state after the day-close tick (`t = 719`) at the same tick world-wide, a `CHAPTER` snapshot at every chapter boundary, `MOVE` snapshots on rebalance and `MANUAL` ones on `adm.snapshot`, as zstd-compressed canonical chunks with the per-entity hash list, recorded in `world.snapshot`, finished within `snapshot_max_ticks` or alerted. This also provides the S3-compatible object store client used by the archiver and recovery.

## Implementation steps
1. Due snapshots: after the commit of tick N, `snapshotKindsDueAfterCommit` (T02-10) gives the engine kinds; map with `SNAPSHOT_KIND_FROM_ENGINE`. DAILY when `tickOfDay(N) === 719` for every partition (aligned world-wide on the same tick), CHAPTER at the chapter boundary tick, MOVE for partitions in a rebalance (T19D-01), MANUAL after an accepted `adm.snapshot {partitionId?}` (all partitions when omitted).
2. Pinning (copy-on-write via structural sharing, 19 Rule 3.7.3): `pinSnapshot` is called in the partition worker right after `commit` of N, before any write of N+1: it copies, per table, the sorted array of committed row references (O(rows) pointers, no deep copy). Rows are never mutated after commit (the 02 store replaces rows on `put`; a test asserts a pinned row object is not changed by a later tick).
3. Encoding: the worker advances the cursor in slices of `maxRows` (default 20,000 rows) whenever it is idle between control messages; each finished chunk (canonical encoding, at most `MAX_CHUNK_BYTES`) is zstd-compressed (Node `zlib.zstdCompressSync`, level 3) and sent to the main thread, which `put`s it under `KEYS.snapshot`. When done: the per-entity hash list (sorted `id`, entityHash pairs, canonical) under `KEYS.entityHashes`, the manifest (T02-10 shape with chunk sha256 of the compressed bytes and the stateHash recorded at commit), then the `world.snapshot` row.
4. Deadline: a job not finished `snapshot_max_ticks` (60) ticks after its tick raises `SNAPSHOT_SLOW` (it keeps going).
5. Failure (19 §8 edge 22): a failed put is retried 3 times; then the job is abandoned with `SNAPSHOT_FAILED`; ticks continue; recovery uses the previous snapshot plus a longer replay; two consecutive failed DAILY snapshots of one partition raise `SNAPSHOT_FAILED_TWICE`.
6. Retention of hot/warm/cold snapshots is applied by the retention job (T19B-15): CHAPTER forever, last 48 DAILY per partition, 1 per real day for 90 real days (19 Rule 3.7.6), consistent with 02's `snapshot_daily_retention_days` thinning.

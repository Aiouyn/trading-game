## Goal
Host 02's commit-reveal entropy buffer in the coordinator (R28: 02 owns the guarantees, 19's epoch scheme is the implementation): obtain each epoch secret `S_e` from a KMS-backed generator, publish its commitment `C_e` as a `sys.entropy_commit` system input in the batch of the last tick of epoch `e−2`, reveal `S_e` with `sys.entropy_reveal` 1,000 ticks after the epoch ends, retry reveals every tick during a KMS outage (19 §8 edge 28), persist commitments and reveals in `world.entropy_epoch`, and replicate the unrevealed secrets to the warm standby so a coordinator failover never loses entropy.

## Implementation steps
1. `forTick(N)`: `buffer.ensureCommitted(N)` creates `S_e` for every epoch up to `epochOf(N) + 2` from the prefetched KMS secrets (prefetch keeps at least 3 ahead; if the prefetch queue is empty the call awaits the KMS before sealing: the seal waits, it never uses a weaker source). A commitment for epoch `e` is emitted as input `sys.entropy_commit {epoch: e, commitment}` exactly in the batch of the last tick of epoch `e−2` (tick `(e−1)·epochTicks − 1`), so it is public at least one full epoch (720 ticks) before `e` starts (19 Rule 3.12.1; `entropy_commit_lead_ticks` = `entropy_epoch_ticks`, 23 §5.6).
2. `eN = buffer.eOf(N)` = HMAC-SHA-256(S_e, 'tick' ‖ N) (19 Rule 3.12.2). Missing secret (only after a corrupt restore) -> `fallback = true`; T19B-07 then uses 02's `fallbackEntropy` and flags the header.
3. Reveals (19 Rule 3.12.3): for each epoch `e` with `N >= (e+1)·epochTicks + revealLagTicks − 1` and not confirmed, add `sys.entropy_reveal {epoch, secret}`. If unwrapping fails (KMS outage), the reveal is omitted for this tick and retried at every next tick (edge 28: late, never skipped); `pendingReveals` exposes the backlog and a metric `entropy_reveal_backlog` is set. Ticks never wait for reveals.
4. Commitments for future epochs are always two ahead, so a KMS outage shorter than one epoch never stops sealing (edge 28). A longer outage stops sealing at the first tick whose epoch has no secret: the world shows CATCHING_UP (T19B-09) and pages.
5. Persistence: `confirmSealed` writes commits (with the wrapped secret in `entropy_secret_sealed`) and reveals (secret into `entropy_epoch.secret`, `revealed_tick`) after the batch is durable (T19B-07), in one transaction.
6. Standby: the active coordinator sends `replicationState()` to the standby with every heartbeat (T19B-11); secrets are only ever stored wrapped outside the world host's memory (19 Rule 3.9.6, no foresight 19#38).
7. Never log a secret or eN (logger redacts `secret`); unit test greps captured logs.

## Test plan
`entropy-service.test.ts` (memory store, dev KMS):
- `19#36 every epoch's commitment is published at least 720 ticks before the epoch starts and every reveal matches its commitment, over a chapter-length headless run`: drive `forTick` for every tick of one chapter (1,344 epochs x 720 = 967,680 ticks; the service alone, no world, runs in seconds) and record the inputs: for every epoch e >= 2 the commit appears at tick `(e−1)·720 − 1` = `e·720 − 721`, i.e. 721 ticks before epoch start (>= 720); each reveal appears at tick `(e+1)·720 + 999` and `verifyReveal` is true; no epoch is revealed twice; no reveal before its due tick.
- Worked example: epoch 5 starts at tick 3,600; its commit is in the batch of tick 2,879 (last tick of epoch 3); its reveal is due at tick 4,320 + 1,000 − 1 = 5,319 (first tick at which 1,000 ticks have passed since the epoch ended at 4,319).
- KMS outage: unwrap fails for ticks 5,319..5,340: no reveal input; at 5,341 the reveal appears; sealing continued throughout (eN available).
- Replication: a standby built from `replicationState()` at tick 3,000 produces identical eN and inputs for ticks 3,001..6,000.
- `setSecret` after commit throws `EntropyChangeRefused` (02's guarantee surfaced).

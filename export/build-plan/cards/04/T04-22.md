## Goal
03 §3.1 item 1 says authored institutions are created at genesis with opening balance sheets. No card did this for the regulators, the courts, `sys_EXA` or `sys_STAB`, and no card posted the treasuries' or central banks' opening money (T04-21 Open point 4). This card does both. Every opening amount comes from a Quant-owned ratio param (E-93 (6)) times the genesis nominal GDP, so the amounts scale with `S0`. E-81 keeps `regId`, `crtId` and `STAB_ID` in `macro/ids.ts`, so 04 owns this card. T04-21's genesis calls it.

## Imports
- T04-01: `BLOC_ORDER`, `BLOC_DEFAULTS` (`ccy`), `govId`, `cbkId`, `regId`, `crtId`, `STAB_ID`.
- T03-08 / T03-28: `ctx.ledger.registerEntity` (idempotent) and `ctx.ledger.mint`.
- T03-05: seed row `MAC_GENESIS_ENDOWMENT` (FAUCET, owner 04, MINT template, leg 3100).
- T04-20: `reservesFdl` genesis state, which must equal the minted reserves.

## Implementation steps
1. **`registerMacroInstitutions`.** All calls are idempotent (T03-28), so 13 may later register the courts again with the same spec. For each `b` in `BLOC_ORDER`:
   - `regId(b)`: kind reg, `reportingCcy = BLOC_DEFAULTS[b].ccy`, `ownerId null`, `homeBloc b` (22 §2.1: FMA, CFA, LFSA, NFSA, CSBC);
   - `crtId(b)`: kind crt, same ccy, `ownerId null`, `homeBloc b` (22 §2.1 circuits).

   Then `sys_EXA`, `STAB_ID` (`sys_STAB`), `crt_MCC`, `crt_GCB` and `crt_TCHA`, each with kind from prefix, `reportingCcy 'FDL'`, `ownerId null` and no home bloc (22 §2.2). That makes 15 entities.
   - Out of scope: `exc_<B>` (05, T05-23), `ccp_TCH` (07), the 22 §2.1 NPC funds (07, 08, 10, 13), and `crt_COURT` (already registered by `LedgerWorld`).
2. **`postOpeningBalanceSheets`.** Postings go in `BLOC_ORDER`. A param of 0 posts nothing. A value outside its range throws `MAC_PARAM_RANGE` before any posting. There are no other postings.
   - Params (E-93 (6)), read with `p.num`:

     | Key | Unit | Range | FDL | EUN | PYN | NKR | SRL |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | `mac_gov_cash0_gdp_<B>` | share of annual nominal GDP, in ccy_b | [0, 0.25] | 0.0246 | 0.0331 | 0.0200 | 0.0285 | 0.0219 |
     | `mac_cbk_reserve_cover0_<B>` | months of imports (04 §3.13.4) | [0, 36] | none | 4 | 10 | 4 | 11 |
     | `mac_gov_fdlcash0_gdp_SRL` | share of SRL annual nominal GDP, paid in FDL (E-94 (c), E-95 (1)) | [0, 0.05] | none | none | none | none | 0.007392 |

   - `nominalGdp0_b = genesisNominalGdp(b, S0, p)` (T04-20).
   - `openingTreasuryCash(b) = toMoney(mac_gov_cash0_gdp_b × fromMoney(nominalGdp0_b), ccy_b)`. The ratio is 4 game weeks of genesis primary spending, `(revBase_b − primaryBalance0_b) / 13`, rounded to 4 dp, so it equals T04-11's `cashTarget` at quarter 0 when no sovereign lines exist. E-103 (1) keeps these values: the cash only has to carry the treasury to the first auction settlement (day 7), and the largest drawdown before it (spending days 0 to 6, revenue days 0 to 5, worst daily order) is 0.00121, 0.00135, 0.00088, 0.00102 and 0.00078 of GDP (FDL, EUN, PYN, NKR, SRL), so each ratio covers it 20 to 28 times; a lower ratio would only move the gap to `cashTarget` into the first need. Quant's model of AC-04-11-CASHFLOOR with these values: lowest treasury cash FDL 149.704e9 (day 6), EUN 127.015e9 (day 6), PYN 611.868e9 (day 6), NKR 390.635e9 (day 98), SRL 316.755e9 (day 0) at `S0 = 0.2`; 5 times that at `S0 = 1`.
   - `openingReservesFdl(b) = toMoney(cover_b × openness_b × fromMoney(nominalGdp0_b) × fx0_b / 13, 'FDL')`, evaluated left to right, so genesis `reserveCoverMonths = cover_b`. E-103 (1): EUN and NKR float freely (no current-account absorption, no FDL debt), and 4 months sits above the 3-month floor of 04 §3.13.2 and §3.13.5. PYN 10 sits inside the realignment band (devalue below 6, revalue above 15, 04 §3.13.3); with CA0 +0.03 absorbed it drifts toward 10.83 (10.049 at day 364). SRL 11 (E-103, was 10): with CA0 −0.02 absorbed and nominal FDL GDP growth 0.065, cover falls about 0.63 months a year, and 10 crossed the CURRENCY_CRISIS line (cover < 9, 04 §3.16) on day 288 by drift alone; 11 crosses it on day 561 (9.678 at day 364). FX debt service is not the binding case: SRL's FX_SERVICE test (T04-13) needs reserves above 1 month of imports plus 21 days of FDL service, about 1.2 months.
   - Worked example 1, `S0 = 0.2`: FDL cash `0.0246 × 6.4e12 = 1.5744e11` FDL, which is 157,440,000,000,000,000 micro. PYN cash `0.02 × 3.2e13 = 6.4e11` PYN (640,000,000,000,000,000 micro). PYN reserves `10 × 0.6 × 3.2e13 × 0.15 / 13 = 2.2153846e12` FDL.
   - Worked example 2, `S0 = 1`: FDL cash `0.0246 × 3.2e13 = 7.872e11` FDL (787,200,000,000,000,000 micro). SRL reserves `11 × 0.4 × 7.5e13 × 0.2 / 13 = 5.0769231e12` FDL (E-103).
   - `openingSrlFdlCash = toMoney(mac_gov_fdlcash0_gdp_SRL × fromMoney(nominalGdp0_SRL) × fx0_SRL, 'FDL')`, evaluated left to right. It is the first semiannual coupon (game day 182) on SRL's FDL debt at genesis (E-95 (1), Producer ruling: launch sovereign bonds pay semiannual coupons): `fxDebtShare0 0.30 × initialDebtGdp 0.70 × 0.064 / 2 = 0.00672` (coupon 6.40% of `ast_GB_SRL_G10Y_L0`, 22 §4.3; the line matures at 3,640 game days, so no principal falls due). Worked example 1, `S0 = 0.2`: face `0.30 × 1.05e13 × 0.2 = 6.3e11` FDL, coupon `6.3e11 × 0.064 / 2 = 2.016e10` FDL, and `0.00672 × 1.5e13 × 0.2 = 2.016e10` FDL (20,160,000,000,000,000 micro). Worked example 2, `S0 = 1`: face `3.15e12` FDL, `0.00672 × 7.5e13 × 0.2 = 1.008e11` FDL (100,800,000,000,000,000 micro). Both products are exact in float, so `toMoney` does no rounding. E-103 (2), Producer ruling: the value is that coupon plus 10%, `0.00672 × 1.10 = 0.007392`, used exactly (not rounded to 0.00739). The 10% is left after the day-182 coupon even if no N10Y_FDL syndication settles first. Worked example 1, `S0 = 0.2`: `0.007392 × 1.5e13 × 0.2 = 2.2176e10` FDL (22,176,000,000,000,000 micro), and `2.2176e10 − 2.016e10 = 2.016e9` FDL is left after the coupon. Worked example 2, `S0 = 1`: `1.1088e11` FDL (110,880,000,000,000,000 micro), and 1.008e10 FDL is left. Both products are exact in float.
   - Treasury cash: `ctx.ledger.mint(govId(b), openingTreasuryCash(b, S0, p), ccy_b, 'MAC_GENESIS_ENDOWMENT')`. SRL also gets `ctx.ledger.mint(govId('SRL'), openingSrlFdlCash(S0, p), 'FDL', 'MAC_GENESIS_ENDOWMENT')`.
   - Central-bank FDL reserves (b ≠ FDL): `ctx.ledger.mint(cbkId(b), openingReservesFdl(b, S0, p), 'FDL', 'MAC_GENESIS_ENDOWMENT')`.
   - The MINT template balances each posting (Dr E:1001 / Cr cbk:2001:E; Dr cbk:1910 / Cr sys:2900; Dr sys:5900 / Cr E:3100), so I17 holds with no extra leg.
   - `reg_*`, `crt_*`, `sys_EXA` and `sys_STAB` open at zero.
3. **Consistency with T04-20.** After `macroGenesis`, `mac.bloc[b].reservesFdl` equals the minted `cbk_<b>` FDL cash for every b ≠ FDL. T04-20 genesis step 8 calls `openingReservesFdl(b, S0, p)` with the same `S0` (E-93 (6)).

## Open points
1. **Amounts.** Set by Quant in E-93 (6); derived and amended in E-103 (1), (2) (SRL cover 11, SRL FDL cash 0.007392). No spec gives opening treasury cash or central-bank reserves; the cash defaults follow T04-11's `cashTarget` and the cover defaults sit inside the spec's reserve thresholds (04 §3.13.3, §3.13.5, §3.16 CURRENCY_CRISIS).
2. **Sovereign bonds.** T04-20 `FiscalState.debt` stays macro state until 06 cards a bondholder side. There is no ledger posting for debt outstanding (Quant, Producer). `fxDebt` stays 0 in T04-20 and `mac_gov_fdlcash0_gdp_SRL` is derived from the 30% share. Once 06 stores sovereign debt, the SRL FDL face is read from the stored FDL-denominated `gov_SRL` bond face at genesis and the param is replaced by `face × coupon / 2` (E-95 (2), T06A-01 06#1-SRLFDL). E-97: 06 now stores the launch face (T06A-01 step 8 through T04-20 `launchDebtSplit`); the G10Y face is 630,000,000,000 FDL at `S0 = 0.2` and 3,150,000,000,000 FDL at `S0 = 1`, so the param value 0.007392 (E-103 (2)) gives exactly one coupon plus 10%. Replacing the param by `fdlUnits × 1,000 × 0.064 / 2 × 1.10` is a Producer call; the bondholder side is still open.
3. **cbk owner.** `LedgerWorld` registers `cbk_<ccy>` with no owner before 04 runs, so the `ownerId govId(b)` set in T04-21 step 5 is lost. Engine Dev decides the fix in 03 code (E-92 (5)).

## Goal
The curves every bond, future and swap is priced off (06 uses them as value anchors, 05 adds flow and noise on top) and the sovereign credit model. The market believes a hawkishness `ĥ` learned from decisions, blends the published dots with its own model path by credibility, integrates the forward policy path into an OIS curve, adds a term premium, sovereign spread, liquidity premium and convenience yield to get government zero rates at seven nodes, fits Nelson-Siegel for any maturity, and derives breakevens and the cross-currency basis. Sovereign credit: an annual default hazard from eight fundamental z-scores (smoothed, with caps and modifiers), the spread and PD term structure, the market-implied PD from traded yields and CDS (players move that one, never the model one, exploit 8), rating inputs for 08 and the rating cliff effects (investment-grade index exclusion, collateral grade).

## Implementation steps
1. **Recompute triggers (§3.7.1).** T04-20 calls `recomputeCurve(ctx, b, reason)` at: every release tick of bloc `b` (after T04-16 publishes, same P2), the guidance tick, the decision tick (after `runMeeting`), the minutes tick, any 08 sovereign rating action for `govId(b)` (08 event read by T04-20), any `macro.fx.regime` event of `b`, and at day close (after steps 5 and 9 of the macro day step). Between recomputes `mac.curve` is constant. Recomputes in one tick for several blocs run in `BLOC_ORDER`. No randomness anywhere in this card.
2. **Market belief ĥ (§3.7.2 step 1).** Genesis `hHat = 0.5`; reset to 0.5 by T04-06 `appointGovernor`. After each decision (scheduled or emergency) T04-06 calls `impliedHawk` then `stepHHat`. `impliedHawk`: `g(h) = iPrev + ρ·(i*(h) − iPrev) − iNew` where `i*(h)` = `ruleRate` with `coeffsFromHawk(b, h, mandate)` on the published data at the decision tick (`publishedData`), ρ = `macro_cb_smoothing`. If `g(0)` and `g(1)` have opposite signs (or one is 0), bisect exactly 40 times and return the midpoint of the final interval; otherwise return whichever endpoint has the smaller `|g|` (0 on ties). Hidden: `hHat` is in `HIDDEN_FIELD_NAMES` and never published.
3. **Expected path (§3.7.2 steps 2, 3).** `dotsPath` = the CB's published dots (T04-06, latest guidance); `modelPathRates` = T04-06 `dots(...)` called with `coeffsFromHawk(b, ĥ, mandate)` and the same published data and `r*_mkt = r*_cb` (DEFAULT simplification). `E_h = cred·dots_h + (1 − cred)·model_h` for h ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8}. `nInf = r*_cb + π_e5`.
4. **OIS (§3.7.3).** `f(s)` piecewise linear through `(0, i)`, `(0.25, E1)`, `(0.5, E2)`, `(1, E4)`, `(2, E8)`; for `s > 2`: `nInf + (E8 − nInf)·2^(−(s − 2)/2)`. `OIS(τ) = (1/τ)·Σ trapezoid` on the grid `k/52`, `k = 0..52τ` (every node is a multiple of 1/52, so the trapezoid is exact on the linear part). `i` = current policy rate.
5. **Term premium (§3.7.4).** `TP10 = tpBase + 0.25·|π_e5 − π*|/0.01·0.01 + 0.15·(debtGdp − d*)/0.5·0.01 − 0.00025·(qeHoldingsGdp/0.01) + tpFear·(F − 50)/10 + shockSum`, clamped to [−0.01, 0.04]. `tpBase`, `tpFear` from `BLOC_DEFAULTS`. `qeHoldingsGdp` = CB QE holdings (T04-09 rows, FDL-free: local currency face at model value ÷ nominal GDP; 0 before T04-09 has run). `shockSum` = Σ active shock values with `target = 'tp10'` for the bloc (storm RATE_SCARE +40 bp, crisis rows; shapes per T04-18 `shockValue`; this card defines the target key, `_unit.md` lists it). `TP(τ) = TP10·(1 − e^(−τ/4))/(1 − e^(−2.5))`.
6. **Government curve (§3.7.5).** For each node τ in `CURVE_NODES`: `y_gov(τ) = OIS(τ) + TP(τ) + s_sov(τ) + liq − conv` (continuous zero rates). Published `CurvePoint`s are these nodes (`nodes`, and `ois` for the OIS nodes). Fit `ns = nsFit(nodes, macro_ns_lambda)`, `nsOis = nsFit(ois, λ)`, `nsReal = nsFit(nodes − breakeven at each node, λ)`. `nsFit`: rows `nsBasis(τ_k)`, solve `(AᵀA)x = Aᵀy` with partial pivoting; all arithmetic in plain float64 with fixed loop order (G8). The 3m and 10y nodes feed `DailyHistoryRow.y3m/y10` and T04-03's `nextInversionDays`.
7. **Inflation curve (§3.7.6).** `be10 = 0.6·π_e5 + 0.4·π_e1 + macro_irp`; `breakevenAt(τ) = π_e1` for τ ≤ 1, `π_e1 + (be10 − π_e1)(τ − 1)/9` for 1 < τ ≤ 10, `be10` beyond 10 (unit decision: 04 defines the blend only up to 10 years). Real curve for 06 linkers = `y_gov − breakeven` (on CPI-indexed principal, `cpiLevel`).
8. **Basis (§3.7.7).** EUN, PYN, NKR: `−(0.0005 + 0.006·max(0, FSI − 0.3))`; SRL `−(0.003 + 0.015·FSI)`; FDL 0. With an active swap line to that bloc (`swapLineActive` from T04-09's state, passed by T04-20): `max(b, −0.0025)`.
9. **Sovereign hazard (§3.11.1), day close, per bloc in `BLOC_ORDER`, after fiscal (T04-10) and FX (T04-14) steps of the day:**
   - z1 `(debtGdp − d*)/0.40`; z2 `pbGap/0.03` with `pbGap = (r_eff − g_nom)·debtGdp − primaryBalanceGdp`, `r_eff` = face-weighted average coupon of the government's outstanding lines (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`, fallback the genesis policy rate when no lines), `g_nom = g_pot + π*`; z3 `fxDebtShare·max(0, dep_vs_FDL_yoy)/0.10`; z4 `max(0, (6 − reserveCoverMonths)/3)` only for `MANAGED_FLOAT`, `MANAGED_BAND` and `HARD_PEG` (else 0); z5 `(F − 50)/25`; z6 `(0.5 − politicalStability)/0.25` (16 via inputs, held last value); z7 `failedAuctions21d` (T04-11 counts); z8 `max(0, π_core − π*)/0.05`.
   - `λ_raw = clamp(λ0·exp(Σ w_k·clamp(z_k, −3, 4)), 1e−5, 0.60)` (`λ0` = `macro_sovereign_base_hazard` per bloc), then FU cap 0.02 and NA cap 0.03 (edge 17: the z clamp bounds a fast world shrink).
   - Modifiers in order: active Concord program (T04-14 `mac.csf` row ACTIVE for the bloc) `× 0.6`; status `POST_RESTRUCTURING`: `λ_raw = max(λ_raw, 0.08·2^(−daysSince/182))`; crisis shocks with `target = 'lambda_raw_mult'` multiply `λ_raw` (SOVEREIGN_DEBT_CRISIS ×3 for 63 days), applied last, then re-clamped to [1e−5, 0.60].
   - `λ ← λ + (λ_raw − λ)(1 − 2^(−1/5))`; on the trigger day of such a shock `λ = λ_raw` (bypass smoothing).
   - `stressIndex = clamp(ln(λ/λ0)/ln 50, 0, 1)`.
10. **Spreads and PDs (§3.11.2).** `h(τ)` normal or distress (λ > 0.08); `s_sov(τ) = LGD·λ·h(τ)` with `macro_sov_lgd`; `spread10y = s_sov(10)`; `pd1y = pdAt(1)`, `pd5y = pdAt(5)`. `liquidityPremium`: FU `0 + breach`; others `0.001·(1 + 2·(1 − depthIndex))` + breach, `depthIndex` = `ports.markets.bondDepthIndex(store, b, false)` (missing: last value, genesis 1), breach = fiscal rule-breach premium from T04-10's `TreasuryState` (0 when none). `convenienceYield` = `BLOC_DEFAULTS.conv` (FU 0.0020, NA 0.0005, others 0).
11. **Market PD (§3.11.3).** `y10Mkt` = `ports.markets.lineYield` of the on-the-run 10y line (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`, `onTheRun && tenor = 10Y`); `λ_mkt = max(0, y10Mkt − OIS(10) − TP(10) − liq + conv)/(LGD·h(10))`, computed first with the normal shape; if the result exceeds 0.08 recompute with the distress shape. CDS where listed (`ports.assets.sovCds`) takes precedence when `cds.adv ≥ lineAdv(on-the-run 10y)`: `λ_mkt = spread5y/(LGD·h(5))` with the same two-pass shape rule. No print and no CDS → `null`, rating input `marketPd1y` = model PD (documented fallback). Exploit 8 counter: `λ_mkt` never feeds `λ`, triggers (§3.12.1) or the curve; only 08 reads it.
12. **Rating inputs (§2.8, §6.7).** Every day close fill `ratingInputs` (debtGdp, interestRevenue = interest cost ÷ revenue from T04-10, primaryBalanceGdp, realGrowthTrend = `g_pot`, inflationVol = stdev of the last 364 daily headline yoy values from history, reserveCoverMonths, fxDebtShare, cbCredibility, politicalStability, marketPd1y, modelPd1y). Genesis `politicalStability` is 0.7 (T04-20 step 1.9, E-107 (1)); with the 16 port null, z6 reads the held `ratingInputs.politicalStability`. Stored on `SovereignCreditState`; 08 reads it through T04-20's api `sovereignRatingInputs(store, govId)`. 04 reacts to grades only through step 13 and the §3.7.1 recompute.
13. **Cliff effects (§3.11.4).** Grades via `ports.funds.sovereignGrades(store, govId)` (three agencies). `isInvestmentGrade`: at least two of three at index ≤ `GRADES.indexOf('BBB-')`. Each day close compare with yesterday's flag stored on `mac.bloc`: on true → false, `igExclusionCheck` writes a `HookCall { owner: '05', hook: 'MANDATE_SELL', args: { govId, assetIds, qtyPerDay[], tradingDays: 10, startDay } }` (E-105: through T04-18 `writeHook`, worldEventId `igx_<B>_<⌊tick / 720⌋>`) with qty per line = 25% of NPC holdings at that close (Σ `committedQty` of the asset accounts (`accountsOfAsset`) owned by the elastic sectors `hh`, `frm`, `row`), split into 10 equal daily lot amounts (remainder lots on the first day), starting on the next trading day; T04-20's `scheduledMandateFlows` expands it into 05 `ExternalOrder`s for the closing auction of each of the 10 trading days. `lowestOfTwoBest` (grade of the second-best agency) is the collateral grade T04-08 uses for §3.8.3 haircuts. 07's capital risk weights read 08 grades directly (no 04 action). Edge 16: no special rule for a defaulted bloc whose bonds are CCP collateral.
14. All `exp`, `ln` through `dmath`. Curve state and sovereign credit state are COORD-homed rows of `mac.curve` and `mac.bloc`; `hHat`, `hazard` internals, `stressIndex` and `ratingInputs` are not in `PublicMacroView` except the fields §2.4 lists (spread10y, pd, curve points).

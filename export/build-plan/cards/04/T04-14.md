## Goal
The FX layer. 04 computes each currency's fair value against FDL (the value layer 05 trades around: carry earns about half the rate differential and blows up in crises), PPP, the real exchange gap and the current account. It runs the regimes: free floats with rare coordinated intervention, PYN's managed band with edge orders and realignments at meetings, SRL's managed float leaning against the wind, an optional hard peg after hyperinflation. Reserves move with interventions, current account, Concord money and gold. Exchange market pressure drives the speculative attack and the break rule (Concord program or a float with a fair-value drop, level-2 controls and an emergency hike). The Concord Stability Facility lends with conditions. Capital controls (SRL and PYN) restrict conversions; 05 enforces them at P1 using 04's rule.

## Implementation steps
1. **Fair value (§3.13.1)**, per non-FDL currency in order EUN, PYN, NKR, SRL, at day close (full step) and at release and CB decision ticks (the surprise terms only): `ln F += [−υ(i_X − i_FDL) + θ_ppp(ln PPP − ln F) + κ_ca·CA]Δ + β_risk·ΔRA + β_oil·Δln E + 0.03·surprise_X − 0.03·surprise_FDL + shocks`, `υ = macro_fx_uip_share` 0.5, `θ_ppp = ln2/1.5`, `κ_ca = 0.5`, β from `BLOC_DEFAULTS` (risk SRL 0.12, PYN 0.03, EUN 0.01, NKR 0.04; oil NKR 0.25, SRL 0.10), `ΔRA` = change of T04-15's risk appetite since the previous step, `shocks` = active shocks with target `fx_fair_ln` for the currency (T04-18). `policySurprise` = (decision rate − T04-07's `E1` stored before the decision) in percentage points. PPP relative to genesis spot; `q̃`; `CA` uses yesterday's gaps. `fairValueLn`, `pppLn`, `realExchangeGap`, `currentAccountGdp` written to `FxState`; 05 reads `fairValueLn` (T04-20 `getFxState`). FDL's fair value is 0 by definition. `crossRate` = `S_A/S_B`; 04 publishes no cross; 05 quotes crosses as products of two FDL pairs (04#28).
2. **Regimes (§3.13.2), orders built each tick in P4 and handed to T04-09's `writePoolOrders` (pool `CB:<bloc>`, asset `ContentView.fxAssetId(ccy)`):**
   - FREE_FLOAT: only during an active major world event (T04-18) and when `|S/F − 1| ≥ 0.25`: IOC orders against the move up to 1% of reserves per trading day split over session ticks (`FX_COORD`).
   - MANAGED_BAND (PYN): half-width 0.03 around `c = bandCenter(...)` (c0 = genesis ln spot). Resting SELL PYN at `exp(c + hw)` and BUY at `exp(c − hw)`, each `2 × ADV` per tick, the BUY side bounded by FDL reserves (`FX_BAND`); in the outer 20% of the band (`|ln S − c| > 0.8·hw`) an IOC lean of `0.05 × ADV × (distance into the zone / (0.2·hw))` (`FX_LEAN`).
   - MANAGED_FLOAT (SRL): when `|ln S_t − ln S_{t−5 trading days}| > 0.04`, IOC orders against the move up to 0.02 × reserves per trading day spread over session ticks; stops when cover < 3 months unless a Concord program is ACTIVE (`FX_LEAN`).
   - HARD_PEG: resting two-sided at `p(1 ± 0.001)`, BUY of X sized by FDL reserves, SELL of X at 10 × ADV per tick (`FX_PEG`); the policy rate is `pegPolicyRate` (T04-06 uses it instead of the rule); break when reserves < 0.8 × `computeM0(ccy)` in FDL → FREE_FLOAT via `executeBreak`.
3. **Band realignment (§3.13.3)** at PYN's scheduled meetings only (04#29): within 0.5% of the weak edge for ≥ 21 trading days and cover < 6 → `c0 += ln 0.97`; within 0.5% of the strong edge ≥ 21 trading days and cover > 15 → `c0 += ln 1.02`; reserves < 3 months → band ±0.06 at an emergency meeting (T04-06 trigger CURRENCY). Each emits `macro.fx.regime` PUBLIC (old, new, band, devaluation size).
4. **Reserves (§3.13.4).** `reservesFdl` = value of `cb.reserves` in FDL (FDL cash, FDL bond buckets at fair value, gold at 06 spot via `ports.assets.commodityClose('GOLD')`, other FX at spot). Changes: CB pool FX fills (T04-09 `applyPoolFills`), current account for managed regimes (`CA × nominalGdp_FDL × Δ`, booked as an FDL cash change of the CB through `ctx.ledger.fx(cbk, 'row', ...)` with code `MAC_FX_CA_ABSORB` TRANSFER, registered here), Concord disbursements, gold revaluation (REVALUE by 03 marks). `importsMonthFdl = openness × nominalGdp_FDL / 13`; `reserveCoverMonths = reserves / imports`. Daily `macro.fx.intervention.daily` PUBLIC published the next trading day (net intervention FDL, reserves).
5. **EMP and the break rule (§3.13.5).** `σ_5d` = stdev of 5-trading-day log changes over the last 364 days; `EMP = −Δ5 lnS/σ_5d + (−Δ5 R/R)/0.05 + Δ5 i/0.01`. Counter `empDays` of consecutive trading days with cover < 3 and EMP > 2 (SRL always; PYN only when the band is exhausted, i.e. at the weak edge with BUY orders unfilled for lack of reserves). At 5, arm the rule; at the next `t = 45` of a trading day `breakRuleCheck`: `canAgreeConcord` → `openConcord(cause 'BREAK_PENDING')` and band tolerance widened (PYN ±0.06); otherwise `executeBreak`: regime FREE_FLOAT, `ln F −= 0.15 + 0.25 × severity`, `severity = 0.6 + 0.8 × u` (one draw on `S.fx(ccy)`), capital controls level 2 for 3 game days then level 1 for 28 game days (`setCapitalControls`, starting at the next tick, exploit 14), emergency defence hike (T04-06 `runMeeting(..., true, ['CURRENCY'])`), `credibilityOneOff('PEG_BREAK')` for band or peg breaks, `adjustRr(SRL, 'DEFENCE')`, `macro.fx.regime` PUBLIC. Edge 6: breaks happen at the next `t = 45` of a trading day, except SRL capital controls, which may start at a weekend day close when a weekend crisis trigger (T04-18) calls `setCapitalControls` directly. Hard peg: `π_core > 0.25` for 91 game days after a break → at the next meeting a currency board at the then-spot (edge 3).
6. **Concord (§3.13.6, §2.13, §6.6).** `npc_CSF` (registered by T04-21, funded at genesis with `MAC_GENESIS_ENDOWMENT` of Σ quotas × 3 in FDL). Qualifies: (break pending, FX_SERVICE pending (T04-13), or `s10 > 0.08`) and `λ × 0.6 < 0.15` and capacity ≥ size. Size `min(3 × quota_b, capacity)`; four tranches at start and every 91 game days: `ctx.ledger.pay(CSF_ID, govId, tranche, 'FDL', 'CSF_DISBURSE')` (reserves +). Conditions: an AUSTERITY program of 1.5% GDP per year (`addProgram`, source CONCORD_CONDITIONALITY), CB independence (04 has no monetary financing path, always met), capital controls phased out within 91 game days (`setCapitalControls(..., 0)` scheduled). Effects read elsewhere: λ × 0.6 (T04-07), SRL swap line (T04-09), BB collateral waiver (T04-08), EUN backstop allowed (T04-11), SRL procyclical rule replaced (T04-10). Rate `oisZero_FDL(5) + 0.02`, interest daily accrued, paid quarterly `ctx.ledger.pay(govId, CSF_ID, interest, 'FDL', 'CSF_INTEREST')`; principal repaid in 8 quarterly installments starting 364 game days after the last tranche (`CSF_REPAY`) (unit decisions; 04 gives no repayment schedule). `compliance = clamp(achieved pb improvement / required, 0, 1) × (controls phased out on time ? 1 : 0.5)`; below 0.7 at a tranche date suspends the next tranche and writes a Wire hook. Emits `macro.csf.program` PUBLIC (size, conditions, tranche dates, compliance). The managing director is a 16 actor (speeches are text only).
7. **Capital controls (§3.13.7), SRL and PYN only.** Levels: 1 levy 1% on conversions of X into another currency (`CAPCON_LEVY` SINK, posted by 05, registered here); 2 conversion hold (orders selling X for another currency rejected `CAPCON_BLOCKED`, except reduce-only closes of existing FX derivative positions; trading X assets within X continues); 3 = 2 plus dividends, coupons and rents in X stay in X (09, 10, 06 read the level). Default durations: level 2 for 3 game days, level 3 for 72 game days, stepping down one level at expiry (`stepCapitalControls`). `capconDecision` is the pure rule 05 calls; `FxState.capitalControls`, `capitalControlsUntilTick` are public; `macro.fx.capcontrols` PUBLIC at start and expiry. Edge 13: margin calls in another currency are 07's problem (SRL cannot be converted at level ≥ 2).
8. **Codes registered by this card:** `CAPCON_LEVY` SINK (BURN, requester 05), `CSF_DISBURSE`, `CSF_REPAY`, `CSF_INTEREST`, `MAC_FX_CA_ABSORB`, all TRANSFER.

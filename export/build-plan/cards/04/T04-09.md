## Goal
The central bank's balance-sheet tools beyond the corridor. QE starts at the lower bound or in market dysfunction, buys bonds through 05 as real orders under holdings caps, tapers to HOLD and runs off in QT; its stock lowers the term premium. PYN's yield curve control defends a 10y cap with a real resting bid that breaks visibly when capacity runs out. FDL provides swap lines that floor the cross-currency basis. An LTRO window opens in emergencies. Macroprudential settings (countercyclical buffer, LTV and DTI caps) that 07 and 10 apply. All CB, treasury and Concord orders go to 05 as `NpcPoolOrder` rows each tick (§6.3); foreign reserve managers rebalance quarterly (§6.4); CB profits are remitted to the treasury each quarter.

## Implementation steps
1. **QE start (§3.8.5)**, evaluated in `runMeeting` (scheduled and emergency): ELB mode when `i = elb` and `i* < elb − 0.005`; DYSFUNCTION mode when `FSI > 0.6` and `cb.lowDepthDays ≥ 3` (`lowDepthDays` = consecutive trading days with `ports.markets.bondDepthIndex(store, b, false) < 0.5`, updated at day close). Size `purchasesSize`; eligible mix `{ govBonds: 1 }`, or `{ govBonds: 0.8, corpIG: 0.2 }` when FSI > 0.5, and during an active SECURITIZATION_CRISIS (T04-18 active major kind) `abs: 0.1` taken from corpIG. Emit `macro.cb.tool` `QE_START` with the size. Edge 1: ELB plus a rule asking for more easing means QE; the rate stays at `elb`.
2. **Taper, hold, QT.** At a meeting with state QE and `i* > elb + 0.0025` → HOLD (`QE_HOLD`): maturing face is reinvested, no net purchases. QT when `i ≥ r*_cb + π* − 0.005` and holdings > 0 (`QT_START`): maturing face runs off up to `qtRunoffCapPerMonth = 0.015` of holdings (excess maturing face reinvested); active sales only for PYN, up to 0.005 of holdings per month (FDL, EUN, NKR, SRL none).
3. **Holdings and caps.** Holdings per line = `ctx.ledger.position(cbkId(b), line)` (authoritative; 06 pays coupons and principal to the CB as holder). Value at 05 fair price (`fairPriceOf`, fallback 03 mark). `gdpShare = total / nominalGdp`, feeds T04-07's `qeHoldingsGdp`. Caps: total ≤ `holdingsCapGdp = 0.35` of GDP; per line ≤ 50% of outstanding face (`lineCapRoom`). `dailyProgram`: gov lines = `ports.assets.sovereignLines(govId)` with remaining maturity 2 to 30 years, pro rata to outstanding face; corporate and ABS lines from `ports.assets.qeEligibleLines`; daily amount = (monthly target − bought so far this month) ÷ trading days left in the game month (FDL-free, local currency face converted with the line price). Clip each line to its cap room and the total cap. 
4. **Pool orders (§6.3), P4 every tick.** `writePoolOrders` writes `mac.pool_order` rows for this tick (05 reads them with T04-20 `npcPoolOrders(store, tick)`): QE/QT slices per line on each continuous session tick (`sessionState` of the bond family for the bloc; t = 61..539 on trading days), `qty = sliceOfDay(remaining face of the day, session ticks left)`, also ≤ 10% of the line's ADV per tick (`ports.markets.lineAdv`), `tif: 'TICK'` (IOC), `limitPrice = iocLimit(fair, makerHalfSpread, side)`; unfilled face rolls into the next tick's remaining and into the month's remaining (never chases price). Rows are deleted the next tick after `applyPoolFills` has read the fills. Purposes `QE`, `QT`, `YCC`, plus FX and CSF orders T04-14 passes in (`FX_BAND`, `FX_LEAN`, `FX_COORD`, `FX_PEG`, `BACKSTOP`) and `RESERVE_REBAL`. Fills post through 05 as `MKT_TRADE` (TRANSFER); 04 posts nothing for QE trades.
5. **YCC (§3.8.6),** bloc `macro_ycc_bloc` (default PYN, the 04 alias PL). Start at day close of a trading day when `i = elb` and `y10 − i > 0.01` for 10 consecutive trading days (`cb.ycc10Streak`): `capYield = i + 0.005`, `YCC_START`. Each tick while active: `yccBid` = resting BUY on the on-the-run 10y at `priceFromYield(line, capYield)`, size `2 × lineAdv` replenished each tick, bounded by `capacityFace` = min(total-cap room, line-cap room). Edge 20: when capacity is below the size, the bid shrinks to it; at zero capacity no bid is posted, the yield can exceed the cap, and 04 writes a `HookCall { owner: '16', hook: 'WIRE_ITEM', args: { key: 'ycc_under_pressure', bloc } }` once per episode. Exit: `π_core > π* + 0.005` for 63 trading days (`yccHighInflDays`) → at each following meeting raise the cap 0.0025 (`YCC_RAISE`); after two raises remove it (`YCC_END`); if removed while holdings are at a cap, `credibilityOneOff('YCC_ABANDON')` (−0.10).
6. **Swap lines (§3.8.7).** `cb_FDL.swapLinesOut` to EUN, NKR, PYN with limit `0.03 × nominalGdp_X × spot_X` (FDL); SRL only while a Concord program is ACTIVE. Activation at day close when `basis_X < −0.004` or `FSI > 0.6` (`SWAPLINE_ON`); deactivation (unit decision, 04 gives none) when neither holds for 10 consecutive trading days and `drawn = 0` (`SWAPLINE_OFF`). Rate = FDL `oisZero(1/364)` + 0.0025; banks pay +0.0010 when 07 on-lends. 07 sends `mac.swap.draw { bloc, amount }` / `mac.swap.repay` to `cbkId(X)` (NEXT); `handleSwapDraws` checks the limit and posts `ledger.fx(cbk_FDL, cbk_X, 'FDL', amt, ccy_X, amt × spot, 'CBK_SWAP_LINE_DRAW')` (TRANSFER, registered here; repay `CBK_SWAP_LINE_REPAY`), and interest daily `CBK_SWAP_LINE_INTEREST` (TRANSFER) X → FDL. While active, T04-07's basis is floored at −0.0025.
7. **LTRO window.** `openLtro` from an emergency meeting (T04-06, 28 game days, unit decision) or for the length of a major crisis POLICY phase (T04-18), sets `cb.ltroOpenUntilTick`; emits `LTRO_OPEN`. T04-08's `bank.cb.repo` accepts 91-day terms only while open.
8. **Macroprudential (§3.8.8).** At scheduled meetings: `ccyb` target; an increase is stored as `ccybPending { value, effectiveTick = now + 91 × 720 }` and applies at that tick; decreases at meetings apply immediately. Releases to 0 at any day close when the bloc's regime is CONTRACTION or `FSI > 0.5` (`macroprudentialRelease`), cancelling a pending increase. LTV: residential 0.80, −0.05 when housePriceGap > 0.15, −0.10 when > 0.30; commercial 0.65, −0.05 when creditGap > 0.06; DTI 0.40. Emits `CCYB_CHANGE`, `LTV_CHANGE` (PUBLIC). 07 and 10 read them through T04-20 (`ccyb(store, bloc)`, `macroprudential(store, bloc)`). Crisis lending standards (CREDIT_CRUNCH −20 LTV points) are 07's, not here.
9. **Reserve managers (§6.4).** Each CB's FX reserves (`cb.reserves`) target: FDL bonds 0.60, EUN 0.20, gold 0.10, NKR 0.05, PYN 0.05 of reserve value; the bucket of the CB's own currency is dropped and the rest renormalized (FDL's own CB therefore holds 0.5 EUN, 0.25 gold, 0.125 NKR, 0.125 PYN). The FDL bucket holds FDL cash and FDL government bonds of 2 to 10 years; FX intervention (T04-14) spends the FDL cash first. At the first trading day of each quarter `reserveRebalanceOrders` computes the gap per bucket at fair value and writes `RESERVE_REBAL` orders spread evenly over 5 trading days (IOC at the §6.3 limit). These are what give FDL its reserve-currency bid; they are public in aggregate through `macro.fx.intervention.daily` (T04-14).
10. **Profit remittance (§3.8.5 last bullet).** At QUARTER close, `remitProfit`: CB net income = −Σ `periodNet` over the cbk's income (4xxx) and expense (5xxx) accounts for the quarter (coupons received, facility interest, IOR paid, deposit interest paid); if positive, `ledger.pay(cbkId, govId, amount, ccy, 'CBK_PROFIT_REMITTANCE')` (03 canonical; 04 §3.18's `CBK_PROFIT_REMIT` is its alias); losses are retained.
11. **Codes registered by this card:** `CBK_SWAP_LINE_DRAW`, `CBK_SWAP_LINE_REPAY`, `CBK_SWAP_LINE_INTEREST`, all TRANSFER (world-internal, cbk to cbk), template FX for draw/repay and PAY for interest.
12. `testing/marketStub.ts`: a deterministic stub of 05 for 04's tests: one line, an exogenous NPC sell flow per tick, a single-price clearing of the resting and IOC pool orders against it, yield from the clearing price via a supplied price/yield function; exposes `poolFills` and `lineYield` for the ports.

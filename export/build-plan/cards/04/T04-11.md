## Goal
How each treasury funds itself. At each quarter start the gross financing need is split by tenor per the issuance mix; the calendar of T04-02 turns it into auctions, each announced 3 trading days ahead with a when-issued yield and (for a new line) a coupon. Players bid through `mkt.sov.bid` (license, face steps, yield range, owner-group caps, cash hold) and cancel through `mkt.sov.bidCancel`; an NPC dealer ladder always bids (exploit 4 counter). Clearing is uniform price and fully deterministic: noncompetitive first, competitive sorted by yield with hash ties, pro rata at the stop, remainders in hash order, awards never above offered (edge 11), no group above 35% (exploits 4, 5). Results publish only aggregates. Failed and weak auctions raise sovereign stress and can trigger the CB backstop. 04 owns sovereign primary auctions (04 §1.1, D059); 06 creates the bond instrument from 04's issue row (see `_unit.md`, overlap with T06A-09).

## Implementation steps
1. **GFN (§3.10.1)** at trading day 1 of each quarter (`planQuarter`, per bloc in BLOC_ORDER): `GFN = annualDeficit/4 + maturities in the quarter (face of lines with maturityTick inside it, from ports.assets.sovereignLines) + (cashTarget − cashBuffer)`, `cashTarget = 28/364 × annual spending`, `cashBuffer = ctx.ledger.cash(govId, ccy)`; floor 0. Mix `macro_issue_mix_*` (keys `macro_issue_mix_<default|srl>_<tenor>`): DEFAULT B3M 0.12, B6M 0.12, B1Y 0.06, N2Y 0.15, N5Y 0.20, N10Y 0.22, N30Y 0.08, L10Y 0.05; SRL B3M 0.16, B6M 0.16, B1Y 0.08, N2Y 0.15, N5Y 0.15, N10Y 0.10, N10Y_FDL 0.20 (syndicated by T04-12, FDL-denominated), N30Y 0, L10Y 0. N30Y new lines are syndicated (T04-12) once per year; its auctions are reopenings. Size per auction = `floor(share × GFN / auctionsCountInQuarter(tenor) / 1e6) × 1e6` face units. Carried shortfalls (step 9) add to the next B3M.
2. **Calendar and announcement (§3.10.2).** Auction ticks from T04-02's `auctionsInQuarter` (t = `macro_auction_tick` = 330). At t = 420 three trading days before, `announceDue`: `y_WI` = `ports.markets.lineYield` of the same line (reopening) or `nsZero(curve.ns, maturityYears)` (new line); new line coupon `newLineCoupon(y_WI)` so price ≤ 100, maturity = auction day + tenor (B3M 91, B6M 182, B1Y 364 days; Nk years 364k days), `assetId = sovereignAssetId(...)`, a `SovereignIssue` row in `mac.issue` (`cac { threshold: 0.75, aggregated: true }`, onTheRun true and the previous line of that tenor set off-the-run at settlement); `bidOpenTick` = announce tick, bids accepted until `auctionTick − 1`. Emit `macro.sov.auction.announced` PUBLIC (auction fields, offered face, y_WI, coupon). T04-20's `issuanceCalendar(store, tick)` exposes the rows to 06, which creates the instrument with exactly that id before the auction tick.
3. **`mkt.sov.bid` validation (§4, §3.10.3), P1 in fair order:** auction exists and `bidOpenTick ≤ tick ≤ auctionTick − 1` else `SOV_AUCTION_CLOSED`; actor Held (13) → `SOV_LICENSE` (Held characters cannot place new bids; standing bids stay); noncompetitive needs LIC2+, competitive needs LIC4 or `CH_BANK`/`CH_FUND` (`ports.players.hasCharter`) or ACTIVE primary dealer of the bloc, else `SOV_LICENSE`; `face` multiple of 1,000 and ≥ 10,000 else `SOV_FACE_STEP`; competitive `yieldBp` present, a multiple of 0.1 bp, within `y_WI ± 200 bp` else `SOV_YIELD_RANGE`; noncompetitive group total (existing + new) ≤ `macro_auction_noncomp_max_face` = 10,000,000 else `SOV_CAP_EXCEEDED`; capital controls (T04-14) level ≥ 2 on SRL and the bidder's reporting ccy is SRL while the auction's ccy is not SRL → `CAPCON_BLOCKED`; cash hold `face × price(y − 50 bp)/100` (competitive) or at `y_WI − 50 bp` (noncompetitive) in the auction ccy via `ctx.holds.placeCash` (expires auctionTick + 1) else `SOV_HOLD_FAILED`. On success store `AuctionBid` (sealed, `bids` never published), emit `macro.sov.bid.accepted` PRIVATE to the bidder. `yieldBp` stored as integer bp × 100 (half-even).
4. **`mkt.sov.bidCancel`:** own bid, `tick ≤ auctionTick − 2` (strictly before auction tick − 1) else `SOV_AUCTION_CLOSED`; release the hold, delete the bid.
5. **NPC ladder (§3.10.4 step 1).** `BTC = 2.4 × (1 − 0.5·stress) × (1 − 0.3·max(0, (F − 50)/50))`; FDL: `× (1 + 0.2·max(0, (F − 50)/50))` instead of the fear cut. `μ = 0.5 bp + 10 bp·stress + 3 bp·max(0, size/avgSize − 1)`, `s = 1.5 bp + 8 bp·stress`; `stress` = T04-07 `stressIndex` plus active shocks with target `auction_stress` (failed +0.15, weak +0.05, crisis rows), clamped to [0, 1]; `avgSize` = mean size of that tenor's last 4 auctions (itself if none). Levels `y_k = y_WI − 3s + k·(μ + 7s)/40`, k = 0..40; cumulative `C_k = floor(offered × BTC × Φ((y_k − y_WI − μ)/s) / 1,000) × 1,000`; level bid face `C_k − C_{k−1}` (C_{−1} = 0), zero-face levels skipped; bidder `DEALER_POOL_ID(b)`, `primaryDealer: true`, bid ids `npc:<auctionId>:<k>`. T04-12 adds primary-dealer obligation bids to this list.
6. **Clearing (`clearAuction`), P6 of the auction tick.** `cancelHeldBidders` first (P1, edge 12). Group cap: per owner group (`ports.players.ownerGroupOf`; NPC ladder is one group exempt from the cap) total face above `0.35 × offered` is cut from that group's highest-yield bids first (noncompetitive counted first). Noncompetitive: total ≤ `0.20 × offered`, pro rata if above (floor to 1,000, remainders in hash order); `Q_c = offered − noncompetitive filled`. Sort competitive by yield ascending, then face descending, then `fnv1a32(bidId + auctionId)` ascending. Walk to the first bid where the cumulative reaches `Q_c`: stop yield `y*` = its yield; bids below fill fully; bids at `y*` share the remainder pro rata to face, floored to 1,000, remainder units of 1,000 one at a time in hash order. Invariant (edge 11, INV-MAC-003 in T04-21): Σ awards ≤ offered. Total bids < offered → everything bid is awarded, `failed = true`, `y*` = highest bid yield. `bidToCover = total bid face (noncompetitive + competitive incl. NPC)/offered`; `tailBp = (y* − y_WI) × 10,000`; `weak = !failed && (tailBp > 3 || bidToCover < 1.5)`; shares by face of NPC vs others.
7. **Settlement (§3.10.2 T+0, §3.10.4 step 5)** at the auction tick, postings committed in P13: price `P* = ports.assets.priceFromYield(assetId, y*, tick)` (06), falling back to `priceFromYieldLocal` (semiannual coupons for notes, zero for bills) if 06 is absent; each non-NPC winner: `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(winner, govId, assetId, face, cash = face × P*/100, ccy, 'SOV_BOND_ISSUE')` (03 canonical; 04 §3.18's `GOV_AUCTION_SETTLE` maps to it under R23); NPC ladder awards: `ctx.ledger.elasticIn('row', govId, cash, ccy, 'SOV_DEBT_ELASTIC_PLACEMENT', { refs: [assetId] })` with the bond quantity leg as 03's template defines for that code. Release every hold of the auction. Owner-group check again at settlement (exploit 5: a group flagged by 15 since submission is reduced to the cap, its excess reallocated down the sorted list). Emit `macro.sov.auction.result` PUBLIC (stopYield, bidToCover, tailBp, allotmentAtStop, NPC/player shares) and one `macro.sov.bid.result` PRIVATE per player bid (own awarded face, price). Edge 10: no player bids → ladder alone clears, results still published. Market-wide halt does not move the auction.
8. **Accounting of debt:** awarded face increases the line's `faceOutstanding` in `mac.issue`; cash raised enters the treasury cash buffer through the postings.
9. **Failed or weak (§3.10.4 step 7).** Failed: add `ActiveShock { target: 'auction_stress', value: 0.15, 21 days, STEP }`, increment the counter read by `failedAuctions21d` (auction rows with `failed` in the last 21 game days), add the shortfall (offered − awarded) to the next B3M auction's size. If `cashBuffer < maturities due in the next 21 game days`: backstop by bloc: FDL, PYN, NKR may buy up to 100% of the shortfall in the secondary market over the next 5 trading days; SRL up to 50%; EUN forbidden unless a Concord program is ACTIVE for EUN (Compact rule): as `BACKSTOP` pool orders handed to T04-09's `writePoolOrders` (IOC at the §6.3 limit, split over 5 trading days). Weak: `auction_stress` +0.05 for 21 days and `HookCall { owner: '16', hook: 'WIRE_ITEM', args: { key: 'weak_auction', auctionId } }`.

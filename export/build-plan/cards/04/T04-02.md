## Goal
Pure functions that place every scheduled macro happening on the tick grid: each bloc's quarterly CB meeting (staggered, D056), its forward-guidance and minutes ticks, every data release of 04 §3.15.1 with its bloc offset, and every sovereign auction of 04 §3.10.2 with its announcement tick. Later tasks (releases, CB policy, auctions, MKT-02 calendars) read these schedules; nothing here mutates state except the precomputed schedule rows written at genesis and at each quarter start.

## Implementation steps
1. Period helpers from 02 constants: quarter = 91 game days; months inside a quarter are 28/28/35 (`MONTH_LENGTHS`). Trading days per bloc come only from 02 `isTradingDay(bloc, day)` (weekends and 02 holidays). 04 §3.6.1 says "65 trading days" per quarter; with holidays the count can be lower, so every "trading day n" is resolved by `nthTradingDay`, clamping to the last trading day of the period.
2. Meetings (§3.6.1, D056): `macro_cb_meetings_per_year` must equal 4 (assert; the param is fixed); meeting day = `nthTradingDay(b, qStart, 91, BLOC_DEFAULTS[b].meetingTradingDayOfQuarter)` (NKR 15, SRL 27, FDL 39, EUN 51, PYN 63), tick = day × 720 + `macro_cb_decision_tick` (420). Guidance: meeting tick − 24 × 720, i.e. day − 24 at t = 420; if that day is not a trading day of the bloc, use `previousTradingDay`. Minutes: day + 14 at t = 420; if not a trading day use `nextTradingDay`. Old 01 §5.4 "~6 real days" cadence is never used (04#44).
3. Releases (§3.15.1): monthly kinds use `nthTradingDay(b, monthStart, monthLen, day + offset_b)`: PMI 1 @45, JOBS 2 @45, RESERVES 4 @300, CPI 6 @45, RETAIL 9 @45, TRADE 12 @45, HOUSING 14 @45, CREDIT 16 @300; SENTIMENT weekly on the bloc's Friday (weekday 4) at t = 300, or the last trading day of that week if Friday is a holiday; GDP_ADV trading day 10 (+offset) of the next quarter @45 for reference quarter q−1; GDP_REV1 / GDP_REV2 = 20 / 40 bloc trading days after the ADV day @45 (`nthTradingDay` counting from the ADV day + 1); FISCAL trading day 15 (+offset) of the next quarter @300. `consensusTick` = the previous trading day of the bloc at t = 420 (§3.15.2 "1 game day before"). `referencePeriod.index` = the month/quarter/week counter since genesis of the period measured.
4. Auctions (§3.10.2): B3M and B6M every Monday that is a bloc trading day (else next trading day), B1Y every fourth Monday (weeks of year ≡ 0 mod 4); N2Y, N5Y on trading day 8 (+offset) of each month; N10Y trading day 10 (+offset) each month, `newLine` in the first month of the quarter; N30Y: reopening on trading day 12 of the quarter's second month, plus one syndicated new line per game year in the first quarter on trading day 20 (DEFAULT chosen here: 04 says "once per game year" without a day); L10Y trading day 12 of the third month; SRL additionally a syndicated "SRL Global FDL 10Y" (`fxDenominated`) each quarter on trading day 20 of the first month. Auction tick = day × 720 + `macro_auction_tick` (330). Announcement = 3 bloc trading days before at t = 420; `bidOpenTick` = announceTick; bids accepted until auctionTick − 1. Mix shares per tenor (zero share ⇒ no auction: N30Y and L10Y are absent for SRL) come from T04-11.
5. `utcHourOfTick`: bigint math `((sealUtcMs + BigInt(k − tick) * 5000n) % 86_400_000n) / 3_600_000n`, converted with `Number`; never uses `Date`.
6. `writeQuarterSchedule`: writes one `mac.schedule` row per (bloc, kind, tick) with id `${bloc}:${kind}:${tick}`, idempotent (skips existing ids). Called by T04-20 at genesis (current and next quarter) and at each QUARTER_OPEN for the quarter after next, so calendars are visible ≥ 1 game day ahead (04#13 checked in T04-16).

## Test plan
- `calendar.test.ts`:
  - **04#44 meetings are quarterly per bloc and staggered; the old "~6 real days" cadence is not used**: over 10 game years, each bloc has exactly 4 meetings per game year, one per quarter; the gap between consecutive meetings of one bloc is between 77 and 105 game days (never ~144 = 6 real days); across blocs, no two meetings share a game day; within a quarter the order is NKR, SRL, FDL, EUN, PYN.
  - Worked meeting example (day 0 is a Monday, weekday = dayIndex mod 7; use a fixture holiday table with no holiday in quarter 0): the n-th trading day of a quarter starting on a Monday `q` is `q + 7·floor((n−1)/5) + (n−1) mod 5`. NKR n = 15 → day 18, meetingTick = 18 × 720 + 420 = 13,380; its guidance day 18 − 24 = −6 is before genesis, so the first NKR meeting has no guidance (T04-06 skips guidance ticks < genesis). FDL n = 39 → day 52, meetingTick 37,860; guidance day 28 (Monday) → tick 20,580; minutes day 66 (Thursday) → tick 47,940.
  - `nthTradingDay` clamps: n = 70 in a 65-trading-day quarter returns the last trading day.
  - Releases never fall on weekends or bloc holidays; CPI for EUN is one trading day after FDL's CPI in a holiday-free month.
  - Auctions: SRL quarter has no N30Y and no L10Y; FDL first month has a new N10Y line, months 2 and 3 reopenings; every announceTick ≤ auctionTick − 3 trading days.
  - `utcHourOfTick`: sealUtcMs 0, tick 0, k = 720 → 3,600,000 ms → hour 1; k = 17,280 → hour 0 (24 h wrap).

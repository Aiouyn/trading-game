## Goal
Each treasury's budget, the cash it actually moves, and the political programs it runs. Revenue rises with the cycle and with commodity prices; spending carries automatic stabilizers and programs; the five fiscal rules (FLEXIBLE, DEFICIT_CAP, BALANCED_CYCLE, SURPLUS_SWF, PROCYCLICAL) create stimulus or austerity at fixed check days; 16's proposals are validated against the rule and pass as a public "rule breach" with a 25 bp liquidity premium for 182 game days (04#45). Programs carry sector demand multipliers for 09 and budgeted incentives for 09, 10 and 11. The fiscal impulse feeds the output gap (T04-04) and the debt path feeds sovereign credit (T04-07). Treasuries never trade in secondary markets except buybacks (§6.2).

## Implementation steps
1. **Revenue (§3.9.1).** `revenue = revBase_b × nominalGdp × (1 + 1.2x) + commodityRevenue` (`macro_rev_base_<bloc>`: 0.30, 0.42, 0.25, 0.40, 0.27; keys `macro_rev_base_fdl/eun/pyn/nkr/srl`); `commodityRevenue = nominalGdp × max(0, commodityExporter) × 0.06 × energyIndex/100` for NKR (0.9) and SRL (0.6). Active TAX_CUT programs reduce revenue by `sizeGdpPerYear × GDP`. Player taxes (03) are not added (they are SINKs/TRANSFERs to the treasury already; the NPC model contains the base).
2. **Spending (§3.9.2).** `spending = spendBase × GDP + autoStab + Σ programs + interest`, `spendBase = revBase − primaryBalance0` (genesis matches §2.2), `autoStab = 0.4 × max(0, u − u*) × GDP`, interest = Σ coupon × face over the government's lines (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`) plus bill discount accretion (bills: face × discount yield). AUSTERITY programs enter with negative size.
3. **Day close (§3.9.6), macro step 9.** `stepFiscalDay`: annual flows × Δ (1/364); `primary = revenue − (spending − interest)`; `deficit = −(primary − interest)`; `debt ← debt + (spending − revenue) × Δ` (toMoney half-even); SRL's `fxDebt` revalued at spot for ratios; `debtGdp = (debt + fxDebt/spot)/GDP`. Ratios over GDP on an annual basis. `averageCoupon` and `averageMaturityYears` face-weighted over the lines (no lines: genesis policy rate and 5 years). History keeps `deficitPrimaryGdp` daily; `fiscalImpulse = deficitPrimary_t − mean364` (shorter at genesis) → `BlocMacroState.fiscalImpulse` for T04-04. Edge 17: debts are never rescaled when the world scale shrinks.
4. **Cash (§3.9.6, §3.18).** `postFiscalCash`: revenue `ctx.ledger.elasticIn(sector, govId, amt, ccy, 'MAC_TAX_FROM_ELASTIC')` split 0.6 `hh`, 0.4 `frm`; non-interest spending `ctx.ledger.elasticOut(govId, sector, amt, ccy, 'MAC_GOV_SPENDING_ELASTIC')`: HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER and autoStab to `hh`, all other spending 0.5 `hh` / 0.5 `frm` (unit decision; 04 gives no split). Interest is not posted here: 06 pays coupons from the treasury (`SOV_COUPON`, `SOV_DEBT_ELASTIC_SERVICE`, 06A schedule). The accrual-cash difference is the treasury cash buffer `ctx.ledger.cash(govId, ccy)`, financed by auctions (T04-11).
5. **Fiscal rules (§3.9.3)** at trading days 1 and 131 of the game year (`tradingDayIndexInYear`), per bloc, in BLOC_ORDER:
   - FLEXIBLE (FDL): when the Cycle Committee declares CONTRACTION (T04-03 event, checked at that day close, once per declaration): stimulus 1.5% GDP for 364 days as two programs (INFRASTRUCTURE 0.75%, HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER 0.75%). At check days: `debtGdp > 1.30` and `s10 > 0.015` → AUSTERITY 0.5%/yr.
   - DEFICIT_CAP (EUN): `deficitGdp > 0.03` → AUSTERITY 0.5%/yr; escape clause: suspended while true regime CONTRACTION and `FSI > 0.5`, then an INFRASTRUCTURE 1% program is allowed.
   - BALANCED_CYCLE (PYN): `cab = pb − 0.4x` outside [−0.015, +0.015] → program of size `min(0.01, distance to band)` in the correcting direction (AUSTERITY if above spending need, HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER if below); CONTRACTION → INDUSTRIAL_SUBSIDY 1%.
   - SURPLUS_SWF (NKR): `swfStep` daily: `pb > 0.02` → `(pb − 0.02) × GDP × Δ` paid `ctx.ledger.pay(govId, swfId, amt, ccy, 'SWF_TRANSFER')`; in CONTRACTION draws up to 1% GDP per year from the SWF (`swf → gov`, same code) funding GREEN_ENERGY or HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER (half each); `swfAssets` mirrors the SWF's cash.
   - PROCYCLICAL (SRL): EXPANSION/OVERHEATING: HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER of 60% of revenue above trend (trend = revBase × trend nominal GDP); CONTRACTION with `s10 > 0.05`: forced AUSTERITY 1.5%; with an ACTIVE Concord program its conditionality (T04-14) replaces this rule.
   Automatic programs carry `source: 'AUTOMATIC'` and are emitted as `macro.fiscal.program` (PUBLIC, no sponsors).
6. **Programs from politics (§3.9.4, §6.8).** Daily `acceptProposals` reads `ports.world.fiscalProposals(store, tick)` (16), sorted by id. `wouldBreach`: AUSTERITY never; EUN: `deficitGdp + size > 0.03` unless the escape clause holds; FDL: `debtGdp > 1.30 && s10 > 0.015`; PYN: resulting `cab` outside the band; NKR: resulting pb below +0.02; SRL: any expansionary program while a Concord program is ACTIVE. Breach → stored anyway with a `breachUntilTick = tick + 182 × 720`, `macro.fiscal.rule_breach` (PUBLIC: bloc, rule, programId); `ruleBreachPremium` adds 0.0025 per active window to T04-07's liquidity premium. The program is public after passage with `sponsorActorIds` (16 decides who is named). Exploit 11: the program applies to every firm in the sector; incentives have budgets.
7. **Demand and incentives.** `SECTOR_DEMAND_DEFAULTS`: INFRASTRUCTURE Industrials +0.06, Materials +0.04, Real estate services +0.03; GREEN_ENERGY Energy renewables +0.08; AI_COMPUTE Semis +0.05, Tech +0.03; HOUSING Real estate +0.05; DEFENSE_SECURITY Security and Defense +0.08; others none. Sector names resolve to indices through `ContentView.sectors()`. `sectorDemandAdd` = Σ over active programs `mult × sizeGdpPerYear/0.01`. `activeIncentives` for 09 (CAPEX_SECTOR, HIRING), 11 (COMPUTE_SPEND) and 10 (MORTGAGE_RATE); `remaining = budget − spent`, none when `≤ 0`. The paying module posts `GOV_SUBSIDY_P` (payer `govId`, registered here as TRANSFER under 03's boundary rule since the treasury's money already exists; 04 §3.18 lists FAUCET; R23 class disputes follow 03) and sends `mac.program.spent { programId, amount }` to `govId` (NEXT); `handleProgramSpend` adds to `spent`. Overshoot within one tick is allowed and recorded.
8. **Codes registered by this card:** `GOV_SUBSIDY_P` TRANSFER (PAY, requesters 09, 10, 11), `SWF_TRANSFER` TRANSFER (PAY).
9. Quarter close: `quarterFiscalEvent` emits `macro.fiscal.quarter` (deficit, primary, debtGdp), the same values T04-16's FISCAL release prints.

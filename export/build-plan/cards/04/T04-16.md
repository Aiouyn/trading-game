## Goal
The statistics offices of the five blocs. Every scheduled release appears in the calendar ahead of time, gets an NPC consensus one game day before, and is published at its tick as the true value plus measurement error, rounded; GDP is revised twice. The surprise against consensus drives a news jump on equity and FX factors (rates move only through the curve recompute in the same P2). Prints feed the public macro view, the central bank's rule (published data only), the Cycle Committee and 16's news. Research nowcasts give paying players a less noisy estimate, the same number on every query.

## Implementation steps
1. **Calendar (§3.15.1, 04#13).** T04-02 writes `mac.schedule` rows for the next quarter at each quarter start (so every release is listed ≥ 1 game day ahead, far more in practice): PMI day 1 t 45, JOBS 2 t 45, RESERVES 4 t 300, CPI 6 t 45, RETAIL 9 t 45, TRADE 12 t 45, HOUSING 14 t 45, CREDIT 16 t 300 (trading-day index of the game month plus bloc offset FDL 0, EUN 1, PYN 2, NKR 3, SRL 4; beyond the month's trading days → last trading day), SENTIMENT every Friday t 300 (previous trading day on a Friday holiday), GDP_ADV trading day 10 of the next quarter t 45, REV1 and REV2 at +20 and +40 trading days, FISCAL trading day 15 of the next quarter t 300; CB items from T04-06's meeting plan. Never on a weekend or bloc holiday (edge 6). Edge 5: a market-wide halt does not move a release.
2. **Consensus (§3.15.2).** At t = 420 of the trading day before the release, `postConsensus`: compute `trueValues` (drawing PMI's N(0,1) and RETAIL's N(0, 0.004) truth terms now, stored hidden on the row so the release uses the same truth), then one normal per published field on `S.shock(b)` (fixed field order per kind): `consensus = true + σ_cons·ε`, `dispersion = 0.6·σ_cons`. Emit `macro.release.consensus` PUBLIC (bloc, kind, scheduledTick, consensus, dispersion). The Terminal-tier calendar view (16) shows it.
3. **True values by kind** (`trueValues`): PMI `50 + 2.5·(g − g_pot)/0.01 + N(0,1)` with g = annualized real growth over the last 91 days; JOBS headline unemployment u, extra `payrollIndex = 100·(1 − u)/(1 − u_28d ago)`; RESERVES reserves FDL and cover months (PYN, SRL also intervention total of the month), measurement σ 0; CPI headline yoy (headline field), core yoy and mom; RETAIL `g + 0.5·Δ(real wage growth) + N(0, 0.004)`; TRADE trade balance and current account, % GDP, trailing quarter; HOUSING house price index from 10 (`ports.housing.housePriceIndex`; missing → last value, genesis 100) and transactions index (100 when 10 absent); CREDIT credit growth yoy and a lending-standards index = `−100·creditConditions`; SENTIMENT `100 + 300x − 100(π_e1 − π*) − 0.5(F − 30)`; GDP_ADV/REV1/REV2 q/q annualized real growth of the reference quarter (same truth, decreasing σ); FISCAL deficit, primary balance, debt % GDP of the quarter (σ 0).
4. **Publication (§3.15.2)** at the scheduled tick in P2 (`publishRelease`): `actual = round(true + σ_meas·ε, decimals)` per field on `S.shock(b)` (fixed order, after any draws already consumed by the day step that tick: releases at t = 45 or 300 come before the day close step); `prior` = previous actual of that kind; for REV1, REV2 `revisedPrior` replaces the prior print in the public view. `surprise = (actual − consensus)/σ_surp`. Write the `DataRelease` row (true value hidden), update `mac.public_view.prints[kind]` (actual, consensus, surprise, tick), emit `macro.release.published` PUBLIC (bloc, kind, actual, consensus, prior, revisedPrior, surprise, riskReaction), then `recomputeCurve(ctx, b, 'RELEASE')` in the same P2 so the expectation update is visible at this tick's P14 together with fills (exploit 1: orders sealed in P0 of this tick were placed without the print; 04#16 is tested in T04-20). Every release is PUBLIC to everyone at its tick; no tiered early access.
5. **Risk reaction.** CPI: `equityJump = −0.003·s`, `fxValueJump = +0.003·s`; GDP_*, JOBS (with `s` negated, since a higher unemployment print is bad), PMI, RETAIL: `equityJump = +0.004·s·(1 − 1.5·clamp((π_core − π* − 0.01)/0.02, 0, 1))`, `fxValueJump = +0.003·s`; others 0. Stored on the release; T04-20's `factorJumps(store, tick)` hands them to 05 (EQ_bloc and FX factors; 16 names the news). Rates: none here (curve recompute).
6. **GDP print error for the CB.** `gdpPrintError` = `actual − true` of the latest GDP print (any of ADV, REV1, REV2); T04-06's `x_gdp_pub = x(at print tick) + error`.
7. **Nowcasts.** `nowcast(ctx, chrId, releaseId, quality)`: if a `mac.nowcast` row exists return it; else `value = true + σ_cons·(1 − 0.8·quality)·ε` with ε the first normal of `S.nowcast(chrId, releaseId)` (a stream per pair, registry gap `macro.nowcast:<chrId>:<releaseId>` listed in `_unit.md`), store the row, return it. `quality ∈ [0,1]` is supplied by the caller (11 data and model quality, 16 or 11 sells the access). Only before the release tick; afterwards return the actual.
8. **Named economists (§6.5).** 16 owns naming and the fixed bias draw; `economistForecast` = consensus + bias, a pure helper so the track record is consistent with consensus.
9. Draw-count rule: every release consumes exactly one normal per published noisy field at consensus and one at publication, plus the PMI/RETAIL truth term; σ 0 kinds consume nothing (fixed by kind).

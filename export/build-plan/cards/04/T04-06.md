## Goal
Each bloc's central bank: a hidden hawkishness drawn at genesis and at each governor appointment (D325: from 16's ideology formula when 16 supplies it), the extended Taylor rule on published data only, quarterly decisions in 25 bp steps with smoothing and step caps, a 9-member board whose votes and dissent directions are the public clue to hawkishness, forward guidance one real day ahead with noise inversely related to communication clarity, dots, emergency meetings, and the credibility gauge. Emits `macro.cb.guidance`, `macro.cb.decision`, `macro.cb.minutes`, `macro.governor.appointed`.

## Implementation steps
1. Personality (§3.6.2): at genesis `appointGovernor(…, 'GENESIS')` draws, on `S.cbPersonality(b)` in this order: `hawk = U(hawkMin, hawkMax)`; 9 member offsets `o_j = 0.15·normal()`; `rStarBias = U(−0.005, +0.005)` (§3.4.4). At later appointments (`TERM_END` every `macro_governor_term_days` = 2,912 game days; `POLITICAL` only when 16 requests it and unemployment > NAIRU + 0.03 for 182 days, or `PEG_BREAK` after a peg break): if `ports.world.governorAppointment(b, tick)` returns `{hawk0}` use it (D325, 16 §3.5.10) and still draw offsets and bias; else draw hawk from the range. The market belief resets halfway: `ĥ ← ĥ + 0.5·(0.5 − ĥ)` (T04-07 state). Credibility moves 0.05 toward `credibility0`. Emit `macro.governor.appointed` (bloc, governor npc id from 16 or null, term end).
   Daily drift between appointments: `hawk += 0.05·√Δ·normal()` on `S.cbPersonality(b)`, clamped to `[hawkMin, hawkMax]`; when 16 supplies `governorHawk(b)` (16 moves `h` at most 0.05 per strategic epoch), use 16's value instead of the random walk (no draw is consumed then? No: always consume the draw for a fixed count, ignore it).
   Coefficients: `φ_π = 0.5 + 0.6(hawk − 0.5)`; `φ_x = 0.5 − 0.5(hawk − 0.5)`, × 0.7 for PRICE_STABILITY mandates (EUN, NKR, SRL); `bias = 0.005(hawk − 0.5)`; `φ_fs = macro_cb_phi_fs` 1.5; `φ_fx` from `BLOC_DEFAULTS` (SRL 0.30, PYN 0.20); `ρ = macro_cb_smoothing` 0.5.
2. Rule (§3.6.3) on published data only: `π_cb` = latest published CPI core yoy; `x_gdp_pub` = the true gap at the tick of the latest GDP print plus that print's measurement error expressed as a gap (T04-16 stores `gapAtPrint` and `errGap = measurementError/4/... `: DEFAULT conversion: error in q/q annualized growth pp × 0.25 → gap units), `u_pub` latest JOBS; `FSI_ex = max(0, FSI − 0.3)`; `i* = r*_cb + π_cb + φ_π(π_cb − π*) + φ_x·x_cb − φ_fs·FSI_ex + φ_fx·fxTerm + bias`, `r*_cb = r* + rStarBias`. Store all seven `ruleTerms`.
3. Scheduled decision (§3.6.4) at each `meetingPlan` tick (P2, t = 420): `Δraw = ρ(i* − i)`; hold if `|Δraw| < 0.00125`; else `Δ = 0.0025·roundHalfEven(Δraw/0.0025)` clamped ±`macro_cb_max_step` (0.005), or ±`macro_cb_max_step_wide` (0.0075) when `|i* − i| ≥ 0.02`; `i_new = max(elb, i + Δ)` (edge 1: at the ELB the rate stays; QE takes over in T04-09; guidance score is clamped to ≥ −1). Corridor rates updated by T04-08's `corridor`. QE/QT/YCC decisions run in the same tick (T04-09 hook `onMeeting`). Votes: each member's preference = `scheduledStep(i, i*_j, elb, ρ)` where `i*_j` uses `hawk + o_j`; `tally`. `statementKey` by direction, size, guidance match (decision direction equals guidance band direction), dominant `ruleTerms` component by |value| excluding `neutral`. Emit `macro.cb.decision` (PUBLIC; ruleTerms rounded per T04-01).
4. Guidance and dots (§3.6.5) at the guidance tick: `i*_proj` = rule rate now; `impliedMove = clamp(roundHalfEven(ρ(i*_proj − i)/0.0025), −2, 2)`; `score = clamp(impliedMove/2 + (1 − clarity)·0.3·normal(), −1, 1)` on `S.cbDecision(b)`; text band: < −0.6 VERY_DOVISH, < −0.2 DOVISH, > 0.6 VERY_HAWKISH, > 0.2 HAWKISH, else NEUTRAL. Dots: iterate quarterly (4 steps of 0.25 years per year up to 8 quarters) with `π_cb → π*` (half-life 0.75 y), `x_cb → 0` (0.5 y), `FSI → 0.2` (0.25 y) and `scheduledStep` each quarter; publish horizons 1, 2, 4, 8.
5. Credibility (§3.6.7), daily: +0.10(1 − cred)Δ when `|π_e5 − π*| < 0.005` and `|π_core − π*| < 0.01`; −0.30·cred·Δ when `offTargetDays > 182` (counter of consecutive days with `|π_core − π*| > 0.02`); −0.10·cred·Δ when QE active and `π_core > π* + 0.02`. One-offs: decision against guidance (score ≥ +0.25 then a cut, or ≤ −0.25 then a hike) `−macro_cred_guidance_break` (0.05); a move differing from `impliedMove` by ≥ 2 steps with no release since guidance having |surprise| ≥ 2: −0.03; decisive emergency FX-defence hike +0.05; peg break or YCC abandonment −0.10 (called by T04-14, T04-09); new governor ±0.05 toward credibility0. Clamp [0.05, 0.98].
6. Emergency meetings (§3.6.6), checked at day close and at the hourly FSI update (T04-15 calls `emergencyTriggers`): any trigger fires and `emergencyCountQuarter < 2` ⇒ schedule the meeting at `t = 45` of the bloc's next trading day (record `pendingEmergency` in the CB row; at most one pending). Size: cuts `Δ = clamp(0.0025·roundHalfEven((i* − i)/0.0025), −macro_cb_emergency_step (0.01), 0)` with ρ = 1; FX-defence hike (SRL or PYN with the CURRENCY trigger): `Δ = clamp(0.0025·roundHalfEven((i* − i)/0.0025), +0.01, +0.05)` (unit decision: an emergency defence moves at least 100 bp, the spec's "up to +0.0500" is the cap), credibility +0.05. `emergency = true` on the decision; the tools (LTRO, swap lines, QE at ELB, CCyB release) are triggered by T04-08/T04-09 reading `lastDecisions[0].emergency`. The count resets each quarter (edge 7: a scheduled meeting still occurs).
7. Minutes (§3.6.1) at minutesTick: `macro.cb.minutes` with `dissentHigher`/`dissentLower` counts and risk keys. Speeches (16) never contain information beyond guidance (§6.1, B09).
8. Events: `macro.cb.guidance`, `macro.cb.decision`, `macro.cb.minutes`, `macro.governor.appointed`, all PUBLIC; the hidden `hawkishness` never appears in a payload.

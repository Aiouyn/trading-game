## Goal
The rest of the primary market. Primary dealers: 8 NPC dealers per bloc (one pool) and players who qualify; they must bid in auctions, are revoked after three misses in a quarter, and gain competitive bidding without LIC4, facility access and syndication lead eligibility. Syndications for new N30Y lines (yearly) and SRL's FDL bonds (quarterly): a one-day book with guidance, tightening on demand, pro rata allocation and lead fees. Buybacks: a reverse auction for off-the-run lines when the treasury has excess cash or off-the-run markets are dysfunctional. Intents `bank.primaryDealer.apply`, `bank.primaryDealer.resign`, `mkt.sov.syndicationOrder`, `mkt.sov.buybackOffer`.

## Implementation steps
1. **NPC dealers (§3.10.5).** One NPC dealer pool per bloc (`DEALER_POOL_ID(b)`, registered at genesis by T04-21) bids the T04-11 ladder; the 8 dealer names from 22 are used only for league-table and lead-manager display (each dealer = 1/8 of the pool's awards).
2. **Application.** `bank.primaryDealer.apply` P1: `CH_BANK` chartered in the bloc (`ports.players.hasCharter(bankId, 'CH_BANK', b)`) or the SWF co-investment privilege (`ports.players.swfPrivilege`), else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; equity ≥ 0.0005 × the bloc's debt stock (`ports.credit.bankEquity`, or `ctx.ledger.netWorth` for an SWF entity, converted to the bloc currency at spot) else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `ports.players.reputationLowestBand` true → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; revoked within its 2-quarter ban → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Store status APPLIED; ACTIVE from the next game quarter start. `resign` → RESIGNING, removed at quarter end.
3. **Obligations.** For every auction of the bloc in the quarter, an ACTIVE player dealer must have at least one bid with `face ≥ offered/(2 × dealerCount)` (floored to 1,000) and yield ≤ `y_WI + 5 bp`. At quarter end `quarterEndDealerReview` counts misses; `misses ≥ 3` (consistent with the 90% participation obligation at about 30 auctions per quarter) → REVOKED for 2 game quarters (`revokedUntilTick`). Privileges are read by T04-11 (competitive without LIC4) and T04-08 (`isCounterparty`).
4. **League table.** Quarterly aggregate share of auction face won per dealer (NPC dealers by name, players by name only when `nameInLeague` is true, otherwise `null`), ranks only; served by T04-20's view `macro.dealerLeague`.
5. **Syndication (§3.10.6).** Scheduled by T04-02 (N30Y new line once per game year, SRL FDL 10Y quarterly). `openSyndication` at t = 420 of the day before pricing: `bookOpenTick` now, `pricingTick` = next trading day t = 420 (1 game day); size = the tenor's GFN share for the year (N30Y: 0.08 × annual GFN estimate = 4 × the quarter GFN × 0.08 minus the reopenings planned) or the quarter (N10Y_FDL: 0.20 × GFN_q), floored to 1,000,000; `guidanceSpreadBp = 5` over the curve yield at the maturity (`nsZero`); leads = `leadManagers` (top 3 entity keys by `dealerShares` over the last two quarters, players qualify; NPC names fill the rest); `feeRate = 0.0015`. Emit `macro.sov.auction.announced` with `syndication: true`.
6. **Orders.** `mkt.sov.syndicationOrder` P1: book open (`bookOpenTick ≤ tick < pricingTick`) else `SOV_AUCTION_CLOSED`; competitive-bidder eligibility of T04-11 (LIC4, CH_BANK, CH_FUND or ACTIVE dealer) else `SOV_LICENSE`; face step as auctions; cash hold `face × price at (guidance − 50 bp)` else `SOV_HOLD_FAILED`; group cap 35% of size (`SOV_CAP_EXCEEDED`). NPC book (unit decision; 04 gives no NPC syndication demand): `npcFace = size × ladderParams(...).btc` with no limit.
7. **Pricing.** `book = Σ player orders + npcFace`; `finalSpreadBp = guidance − min(5, floor(max(0, book/size − 2)/0.5))`. Eligible orders: no limit, or `limitSpreadBp ≥ finalSpreadBp`. Leads get up to 15% of size each first (pro rata within their own eligible orders); the rest pro rata to eligible face, floored to 1,000, remainders in `fnv1a32(orderId + syndicationId)` order; Σ ≤ size. Yield = curve + final spread; coupon `newLineCoupon(yield)`; price from yield (06 port, local fallback). Settlement at the pricing tick: players `SOV_BOND_ISSUE`, NPC `SOV_DEBT_ELASTIC_PLACEMENT` (sector `row`); SRL FDL bonds in FDL (`fxDenominated`, adds to `fxDebt`). Fee `0.0015 × size × price/100` split equally among the 3 leads, `ctx.ledger.pay(govId, lead, amt, ccy, 'GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE')` (TRANSFER, registered here; 04 §3.18 lists a FAUCET `_P` variant, but the treasury's money already exists so 03's boundary rule makes it a TRANSFER, R23); fees to NPC leads (the dealer pool) are booked to the pool the same way. Emit `macro.sov.syndication.priced` PUBLIC (size, final spread, coupon, book cover; no orders).
8. **Buybacks (§3.10.7).** At t = 420 of the first trading day of each game month, `buybackTrigger`: (a) `cashBuffer > 2 × cashTarget` and stress < 0.2 → excess = `cashBuffer − 2 × cashTarget`; (b) `ports.markets.bondDepthIndex(store, b, true) < 0.5` and FSI > 0.5 → excess = `max(0, cashBuffer − cashTarget)`; `maxFace = min(excess at par, 0.02 × debt)`. Lines: off-the-run lines of the government. Offers until t = 330 of the next trading day (buyback tick). `mkt.sov.buybackOffer` P1: buyback open, asset in the list, `availableQty − ports.credit.pledgedQty ≥ face` else `SOV_HOLD_FAILED`; qty hold placed. Settlement: accept offers with `minPrice ≤ fairPrice + 0.10 per 100` in ascending `minPrice`, ties by `fnv1a32(offerId + buybackId)`, up to `maxFace` (partial last offer floored to 1,000); each accepted pays its own price: `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(govId, holder, assetId, face, cash, ccy, 'GOV_BUYBACK')` with the asset leg retiring the bond (template SWAP_ASSET, accounts as `SOV_REDEMPTION`); line `faceOutstanding` reduced; holds released; `macro.sov.buyback.result` PUBLIC (aggregate face, average price, cover). Treasuries never trade in secondary markets otherwise (§6.2).
9. **Codes registered by this card:** `GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE` TRANSFER (PAY), `GOV_BUYBACK` TRANSFER (SWAP_ASSET).

## Test plan
- `dealers.test.ts`: eligibility rejects (no charter, equity 0.0004 × debt, lowest reputation band, within ban); APPLIED becomes ACTIVE exactly at the next quarter start; `obligationMinFace(10,000,000,000, 10)` = 500,000,000; a bid at y_WI + 5.1 bp does not qualify; three misses → REVOKED and `revokedUntilTick` = start of the quarter after next; league table hides a non-opted player's name; `dealerCount` with 2 active players = 10.
- `syndication.test.ts`: book 3.2× size, guidance 5 bp → final 3 bp; book 5× → final 0 bp (cap −5); book 1.8× → 5 bp. Size 5,000,000,000: three leads each with orders of 1,000,000,000 receive 750,000,000 first (15%), the remaining 2,750,000,000 pro rata to the other eligible orders; an order with limit 2 bp (below the final 3 bp) gets nothing; Σ allocations ≤ size. Fee 0.0015 × 5e9 at price 100 = 7,500,000 → 2,500,000 per lead posted as `GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE`. SRL FDL syndication settles in FDL and raises `fxDebt`.
- `buyback.test.ts`: cash 3× target, stress 0.1, debt 1e12 → open, maxFace = min(cash − 2 × target, 20e9); dysfunction path opens at FSI 0.6 with depth 0.4; offers at 99.80, 100.05, 100.20 with fair 100.00 → cap 100.10: the first two accepted in that order, the third rejected; each paid its own price; holds released; pledged bonds cannot be offered.

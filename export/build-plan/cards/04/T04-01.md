## Goal
Lay down every type, table and constant that the rest of unit 04 builds on: the enums and entity interfaces of 04 §2 verbatim, deterministic id helpers, the default bloc table of 04 §2.2 as typed constants, the dependency-inversion ports through which 04 reads modules built after it (G6.1), the zod schemas of every `macro.*` event of 04 §5 with its visibility, and the mapping of 04's stream names onto the binding 02 §3.10.4 registry. No rules run here; later tasks import these names verbatim.

## Implementation steps
1. `schema.ts`: transcribe every interface of 04 §2.1 to §2.13 field for field (comments kept short).

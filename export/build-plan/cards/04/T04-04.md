## Goal
Steps 2 (scale part) and 4 of the macro day step: the world scale `S` that sizes the NPC economy from activity-weighted players (D053), bloc nominal GDP and city activity that 09 and 10 consume, the output gap pulled toward its regime attractor through the named transmission channels (monetary, fiscal, credit, external, real exchange rate, energy, fear, wealth, trade, cyber, crisis shocks), potential growth with the AI-race effect, Okun unemployment, wages and the hidden neutral rate.

## Implementation steps
1. Scale (§3.2, macro step 2, global, before the blocs): `N_a` = `computeNa(store, tick)` (03). `S ← stepScale(S, scaleTarget(N_a))`; `S` lives in `GlobalMacroState.worldScale`; genesis S = `scaleTarget(N_a at genesis)` clamped to the min (0.2) when there are no players. Edge 17: shrink ≤ 0.5%/day, floor 0.2; debts are never rescaled (only flow and pool quantities derive from S).
2. Bloc nominal GDP (§3.2 step 4) after the level update of step 4 below: `nominalGdp_b = toMoney(100e12 × S × gdpShare0_b × (realGdpIndex/100) × (cpiLevel/100) / fx0_b, ccy_b)` with `macro_world_gdp_ref` in display dollars (G3). World nominal GDP in FDL uses 05 last FX prints via `ports.markets.fxSpot` (FDL itself 1.0; missing print → previous `spotVsFdl` from history). City activity and city nominal GDP per §3.2 step 6 with `cityGdpShare`, `cycleBeta` from 22 content; `islaSerenaIndex` for 10. The monitor ratio (§3.2 step 7) is written to `StabilizerState.diagnostics.npcToPlayerWealthRatio`; outside `[20, 200]` (`npc_to_player_wealth_band`) it emits nothing but is reported by 20.
3. Output gap (§3.4.1), per bloc in `BLOC_ORDER`:
   - `R = i − π_e1 − r*` (true r*), `R̃ ← R̃ + (R − R̃)(1 − 2^(−1/120))`.
   - `x_reg` by regime: +0.005, +0.025, −0.04, −0.01 (`macro_xreg_exp/ovh/con/rec`).
   - Channels (coefficients `macro_x_a_r` 0.5, `macro_x_m` 0.8, `macro_x_a_c` 0.012, `macro_x_a_e` 0.4, `macro_x_a_q` 0.10, `macro_x_a_o` 0.03, `macro_x_a_f` 0.010, `macro_x_a_w` 0.05, `macro_x_a_h` 0.08, `macro_x_a_t` 0.2, `macro_x_a_k` 0.010; the family row `macro_x_*` in 23 covers them, key names fixed here): monetary `−a_r·R̃`; fiscal `m·FI` with `m = 0.8·(1 − 0.5·stress)`, × 1.25 when `i ≤ elb`; credit `a_c·creditConditions`; external `a_e·openness·x_others` (GDP-weighted gap of the other four blocs, using yesterday's gaps so the bloc order does not matter); real FX `−a_q·q̃`; energy `−a_o·clamp(e_yoy, −1.5, 1.5)·(1 − 1.5·commodityExporter)` with `e_yoy = ln(E/E_{−364})`; fear `−a_f·(F − 30)/20`; wealth `a_w·ln(EQ/mean364(EQ)) + a_h·housePriceGap` (EQ missing → 0); trade `a_t·openness·ln(WTI/mean364(WTI))`; cyber `−a_k·max(0, hack − 70)/30`; shocks = Σ value of active shocks with `target = 'x_target'` and this bloc (shape STEP/RAMP/DECAY evaluated by T04-18's `shockValue`).
   - `x_target = clamp(x_reg + Σ, −0.12, 0.08)` stored in `outputGapTarget`; `x ← x + θ_x(x_target − x)Δ + σ_x√Δ ε`, `θ_x = ln2/0.30`, `σ_x = 0.008`, `ε = rng.normal()` on `S.shock(b)` (draw 1 of the bloc's day draws).
4. Levels (§3.4.2): `g_pot = potentialGrowth0 + 0.005 × (aiRaceIndex − 50)/50 × techShare_b` (aiRaceIndex missing → 50); `potentialGdpIndex ×= exp(g_pot/364)`; `realGdpIndex = potentialGdpIndex × (1 + x)`; `gdpGrowthQoQAnn` from history 91 days back (before day 91: from genesis 100).
5. Unemployment and wages (§3.4.3): `u_target = u* − 0.5x`; `u ← u + (u_target − u)(1 − 2^(−Δ/0.2))`, clamp [0.015, 0.30]; `w = π_e1 + (g_pot − laborForceGrowth) − 0.5(u − u*)`; `wageIndex ×= exp(wΔ)`; `wageGrowth = w`.
6. Neutral rate (§3.4.4): `RW ← RW + 0.001·√Δ·ε` (`ε` = draw 2 on `S.shock(b)`), then clamp RW so that `r*` stays in `r*_0 ± 0.01`; `r* = r*_0 + 0.5(g_pot − g0) + RW`. The CB estimate `r*_cb = r* + bias_rstar` is handled in T04-06 (bias drawn at each governor appointment).
7. All exp/ln through `dmath`. NaN guards: `safeDiv` for ratios; inputs missing hold their last value (edge 23) via T04-20's input snapshot.

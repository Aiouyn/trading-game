## Goal
The central bank's balance-sheet operations with banks. The corridor around the policy rate; reserve requirements per bloc and their SRL changes; daily interest on reserves with the 3× tier (exploit 6 counter); the standing lending facility (1 to 7 game days at the corridor ceiling, 91-day LTRO at the policy rate while open) against haircut collateral (exploit 7 counter); ELA for solvent banks only, with a sealed notice to the regulator; the deposit facility; the repayment and interest of 03's automatic day-close overdraft conversion; and the daily public aggregates that never name a user (L11). Intents `bank.cb.repo`, `bank.cb.repoRepay`, `bank.cb.deposit`, `bank.cb.depositWithdraw`, `bank.cb.elaRequest`.

## Implementation steps
1. **Corridor (§3.8.1).** `lendingRate = i + w`, `depositRate = i − w`, `w = macro_corridor_halfwidth`; if `negativeRatesAllowed = false` then `depositRate = max(0, i − w)`. `setCorridor` runs at genesis and after every decision (T04-06), updates `cb.facilities.lendingRate/depositRate`, `elaPenaltySpread = 0.015`, `maxTermDays = 7`, and calls `putFact(ctx, 'FACILITY_RATE_E6', ccy, roundHalfEven(lendingRate × 1e6))` (03 uses it for the automatic conversion). 07 keeps its interbank rate inside the corridor; 04 only publishes the rates (`macro.cb.decision.corridor`).
2. **Reserve requirement (§3.8.2).** `cb.reserveRequirement` from `macro_rr_<bloc>` (FU 1%, EC 1%, PL 5%, NA 1%, SC 10%; key names `macro_rr_fdl`, `macro_rr_eun`, `macro_rr_pyn`, `macro_rr_nkr`, `macro_rr_srl` of the `macro_rr_*` family). `adjustRr`: SRL only; `DEFENCE` (called by T04-14 in a currency defence) `+0.02`, cap 0.20; `EASING` (called by T04-06 after a cut) `−0.02` if no EASING change yet this quarter, floor at the default. Each change emits `macro.cb.tool` `RR_CHANGE` (PUBLIC). 07 measures eligible deposits and reads `rr` through T04-20's api (`rr(store, bloc)`).
3. **Interest on reserves (§3.8.2), day close (P8), per bank in sorted id order.** `ports.credit.bankReserves(store, bankId)` gives `balance` (current account, excluding the deposit facility earmark of step 8), `required`. `tierBase = min(balance, 3 × required)` (`iorTierMultiple = 3`), `excess = balance − tierBase`. `tierInterest = half-even(tierBase × i / 364)`, `excessInterest = half-even(excess × depositRate / 364)`. Each positive part: player-economy bank (`ports.players.ownerGroupOf` chain ends at a `chr`, i.e. 03 perimeter PLAYER) → `ledger.mint(bankId, amt, ccy, 'CBK_IOR_P')` (FAUCET, 24 §8); world bank → `ledger.pay(cbkId, bankId, amt, ccy, 'CBK_IOR_W')`. Each negative part (negative policy or deposit rate, EUN and NKR only, edge 2): player bank → `ledger.burn(bankId, |amt|, ccy, 'CBK_NEGATIVE_RATE_CHARGE_P')` (SINK, registered here), world bank → `ledger.pay(bankId, cbkId, |amt|, ccy, 'CBK_NEGATIVE_RATE_CHARGE_W')` (TRANSFER). `ports.credit = null` → nothing (no banks exist, edge 18). The FAUCET is counted by 03's flow accounting, which the Stabilizer sees (exploit 6).
4. **Reserve penalty.** 07 applies and posts it (04 §3.8.2 and 07 both say so): `reservePenalty = half-even(shortfall × (lendingRate + 0.02) / 364)` with codes `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_P` (SINK) and `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_W` (TRANSFER) registered here; 04 exports `reservePenaltyRate`. 04 posts no penalty itself.
5. **Collateral (§3.8.3).** `classifyCollateral`: `ports.assets.assetInfo` → `GOV` with `issuerBloc = b` → OWN_GOV, other bloc → OTHER_GOV, `CORP` → CORP, `ABS_SENIOR`/`COVERED` → ABS_COVERED, `EQUITY` → EQUITY; `ports.credit.isLoanClaim` non-null → CREDIT_CLAIM; else OTHER (ineligible). Grade: sovereigns `lowestOfTwoBest(ports.funds.sovereignGrades(govId))`, others `ports.funds.gradeOf(assetId)`; `gradeBucket`: AAA..AA- → AAA_AA, A+..A- → A, BBB+..BBB- → BBB, BB+..BB- → BB, lower → BELOW, null → unrated (ineligible except CREDIT_CLAIM/EQUITY under ELA). Haircuts (`macro_cb_haircut_*` family; keys `macro_cb_haircut_<kind>_<bucket>`, defaults the table): OWN_GOV 0.01/0.02/0.04/0.15 (BB only with crisis waiver = a CSF program ACTIVE for the bloc, T04-14 row), OTHER_GOV 0.03/0.05/0.10, CORP 0.05/0.07/0.12, ABS_COVERED 0.08/0.12/0.20; ELA adds CREDIT_CLAIM 0.30 and EQUITY 0.40 (ELA mode only). Add `0.005 × max(0, yearsToMaturity − 5)` where maturity is known. Value = `ports.markets.fairPriceOf` × qty, falling back to `effectiveMark` (03); a missing price makes the line ineligible. `postHaircut = Σ value × (1 − haircut)` (half-even per line).
6. **`bank.cb.repo` (§4).** Validate in P1: schema; `ports.credit` null → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (edge 18); actor is Held (13, `ports.players.isHeld`) → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `isCounterparty` false → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `termDays ∈ 1..7`, or 91 only while `cb.ltroOpenUntilTick > tick` (T04-09 opens LTRO in an emergency or a crisis POLICY phase), else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; any line ineligible → `CBK_COLLATERAL_INELIGIBLE`; qty above `availableQty − ports.credit.pledgedQty` → `CBK_COLLATERAL_INELIGIBLE`; `postHaircut < amount` → `CBK_HAIRCUT_SHORT`. On success place one `holds.placeQty` per line (expires at maturity + 1 day, ref = new facility id) so the collateral cannot be sold; 07 reads `mac.facility` for its pledge view and handles top-up calls (edge 15). Apply in P6: `openFacility` writes `FacilityLoan` (`kind` REPO, or LTRO for 91 days, `rate` = lendingRate, LTRO at `i`), `ledger.cbCredit(cbkId, bankId, amount, ccy, 'CBK_STANDING_FACILITY', { sub: loanId })` (03 canonical code; 04 §3.18's `CBK_REPO_OPEN` maps to it under R23), emits `macro.cb.repo` PARTIES (subject bankId; bank, amount, term, collateral summary).
7. **Accrual, maturity, repay.** `accrueFacilities` at day close: `accrued += half-even(amount × rate / 364)`. At the maturity day close (or `bank.cb.repoRepay`: own facility, `available(bankId) ≥ amount + accrued`, else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; early repayment pays interest to date), `closeFacility`: `ledger.cbCreditUnwind(bankId, cbkId, amount, ccy, 'CBK_STANDING_FACILITY_REPAY' | 'CBK_EMERGENCY_LIQUIDITY_REPAY', { sub: loanId })`, `ledger.pay(bankId, cbkId, accrued, ccy, 'CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST')` (03 TRANSFER, R23 over 04 §3.18's SINK/TRANSFER split), release holds, status REPAID. A bank short of reserves at maturity goes negative and 03 converts it to an overnight facility at the close (T03-15); no special path.
8. **Deposit facility.** `bank.cb.deposit`: chartered in the bloc (`ports.credit.isChartered`), amount ≤ `available`; apply P8: `holds.placeCash(bankId, ccy, amount, MAX_TICK, depositRowId)` (the earmark), `DepositFacilityBalance.amount += amount`. `bank.cb.depositWithdraw`: amount ≤ balance; `holds.reduce`. Day close `postDepositFacilityInterest`: `half-even(amount × depositRate / 364)`; positive → `CBK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST_P` (mint, FAUCET per 24 §8) or `CBK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST_W` (pay); negative → the two charge codes of step 3 (edge 2).
9. **Automatic facilities (03 §3.3.4).** At DAY_OPEN (P2) `repayAutomaticFacilities`: for each account of `cbkId(b)` with chart code 1307 and sub starting `sf:` and non-zero balance dated the previous game day, `ledger.cbCreditUnwind(bank, cbk, amt, ccy, 'CBK_STANDING_FACILITY_REPAY', { sub })` and `ledger.pay(bank, cbk, half-even(amt × lendingRate / 364), ccy, 'CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST')`.
10. **ELA (§3.8.4).** `bank.cb.elaRequest` P1: `ports.credit` non-null, chartered bank, `ports.credit.solvencyOk` (capital ratio ≥ minimum) else `CBK_ELA_INSOLVENT`; collateral in ELA mode, short → `CBK_HAIRCUT_SHORT`; holds placed. Apply: `ctx.send(cbkId(b), 'mac.ela.request', {...}, 'NEXT')`. Next tick P8: re-check solvency; refused → release holds and emit `macro.cb.ela` PARTIES with `{ refused: 'CBK_ELA_INSOLVENT' }` (07's resolution procedure runs from its own solvency checks); granted → `FacilityLoan` kind ELA, rate `lendingRate + 0.015`, term 90 game days, `ledger.cbCredit(..., 'CBK_EMERGENCY_LIQUIDITY', { sub })`, emit `macro.cb.ela` PARTIES and a SEALED copy with subject `regId(b)` (bloc regulator, 13). ELA balance increases `cb.balanceSheet.elaOutstanding`.
11. **Disclosure (L11).** `facilityDaily` at day close emits `macro.cb.facility.daily` PUBLIC with bloc aggregates only: repo outstanding, LTRO outstanding, ELA outstanding, deposit facility total, count of counterparties bucketed (0, 1–3, 4+); no ids, no names. Repo and ELA events are PARTIES (never PUBLIC).
12. **Codes registered by this card (rows for T03-05 `registerReasonCodes`, called from T04-21):** `CBK_NEGATIVE_RATE_CHARGE_P` SINK (BURN), `CBK_NEGATIVE_RATE_CHARGE_W` TRANSFER (PAY), `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_P` SINK (BURN), `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_W` TRANSFER (PAY). Owner module 04 (07 may request the penalty codes; the rows list `mayRequest: [4, 7]`).

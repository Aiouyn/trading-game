## Goal
Combine every 06B piece into the unit's `ModuleDef` (`id '06B'`, module `'06'`, hard dependencies `'05'` and `'06A'`), wire the phase and boundary handlers in a fixed documented order (06B is registered after 06A, so in each shared phase it runs after 06A), register the five 06B intents, the 06B events, views, the stream `ast.credit_event`, the tables, the model valuers (OPTION, SWAP, CDS), the RFQ products and the instrument-port extension; read every 06B parameter through `params.ts`; hand derivative trades to 07B for novation (CCP_LD, CCP_CRY, CCP_OTC, CCP_CDS); run the derivative integration scenarios and the numbered replay-determinism test 06#2.

## Implementation steps
1. `createDerivativesModule(ports)`: `resolveAssetPorts(ports)`; at module load `registerOptionValuer(sigmaOf)`, `registerModelValuer('SWAP'|'CDS', …)`, `registerSwapRfqProducts()`, `registerCdsRfqProducts()`, `registerPortExtension('06B', {...})` (dealerNetGamma, packageQuote, hasListedOptions, underlyingOf, fairBasisLn, preTradeGate branches for OPTION/FUTURE/PERP/CDS), `registerSrlPositionSource(srlPositionSource)`; `defineModule({ id: '06B', module: '06', dependsOn: ['05', '06A'], tables, streams: [...STREAMS_06B], phases, boundaries, intents: DERIV_INTENTS, events: DERIV_EVENTS, views: DERIV_VIEWS, genesis })`.
2. Genesis: `listPerps`; dealer books for every optionable underlying (`ast.drv_dealer`, zero inventory); first surfaces; no option or future series rows (lazy, T06B-01); GX Credit 40 series 1.
3. Boundaries (02 runs them in P2): DAY_OPEN: `dailyGridMaintenance`, `hedgerOrders` queued for the opening flow; DAY_CLOSE (t = 719, after 06A's DAY_CLOSE handlers): `processEarlyExercises`, crypto option expiries (`expireSeries` at t = 719), FUT24/CRYPTO `publishDailySettlements`, `settleExpiries` for t = 719 expiries, `swapPaymentStep`, `premiumStep` (CDS), `determinationStep`, `limitBreachStep`; QUARTER_CLOSE: top-40 option underlying reselection (via `dailyGridMaintenance` quarter flag), CDS roll and every second quarter a new GX Credit 40 series.
4. P4 (NPC orders, after 06A's P4 if any): `dealerQuoteOrders`, `dealerHedgeOrders`, `parityArbOrders`, `basisArbOrders`; returned as `ExternalOrder`s through 05's port (`flows`), in that order, each list sorted by (assetId, side, price).
5. P6 (after 05's matching and after 06A's P6): (1) `updateDealerInventory`; (2) `perpTickStep`; funding at t ∈ {0, 240, 480}: `fundingStep`; (3) `surfaceStep`, `gxvStep`; (4) t = 540: EQUITY-session `publishDailySettlements`, `expireSeries` for t = 540 expiries, `settleExpiries`, `runPhysicalDeliveries` at commodity last trades; (5) t = 300 on auction days: `settlementAuction` (after 06A's `runDistressedAuctions` this tick); (6) `netClearedOffsets`; hourly `t mod 30 == 0`: `valuationStep`, `cdsMarkStep`, `freezeOnControl`; (7) `computeCapacityDeliveries` at compute expiries; (8) `pinningSignal` on expiry days at t = 540.
6. P14: `publishGxv`.
7. Settlement contract (06 §3.3.2, D095): every posting commits in P13 of its tick; no pending-settlement status table (asserted).
8. Clearing hand-off: listed series carry `clearing` (CCP_LD/CCP_CRY); swaps and CDS rows carry `clearing` (CCP_OTC/CCP_CDS/BILATERAL); 07B novates in P7 of the trade tick; 06B never computes margin.
9. Intents: assert no kind collides with `SPOT_INTENTS` or 05's kinds (registration test).

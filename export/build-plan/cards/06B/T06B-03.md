## Goal
The per-underlying implied volatility surface of 06 §3.13.3 (GARCH term structure, variance risk premium, fear-scaled skew, curvature, dealer-inventory term, clamps, 04's ATM-vol floor), the two arbitrage guards applied on the strike grid at every evaluation (calendar total variance, call convexity/monotonicity), the NPC options dealers of §6 item 1 who quote every listed series (materialized or not) from the model with the specified half-spread, carry vega inventory up to capacity and delta-hedge each tick in P4 (§3.13.6), the put-call parity arbitrageurs of §6 item 2 (X3), and the expiry-pinning concentration signal (X13). Also the 06B members of the instrument port that 05 needs for options: `dealerNetGamma`, `packageQuote`, `hasListedOptions`, `underlyingOf`.

## Implementation steps
1. Inputs per underlying: `σ_now` = 05's GARCH vol annualized (`sqrt(h)·sigmaDailyOf·√364`, as 05 exposes); `σ_LR = sigmaAnnual` of the underlying's PriceParams × factor-implied ratio (05's `getPriceState().factorVolRatio`, 1 if absent); `α, β` from 05's GARCH params (`garchStable` for stablecoins); F from the ATM forward (BS: `S e^{(r−q)T}`; futures options: F; FX: CIP forward via T06A-11 `forwardRate`).
2. `k_mr = −ln(α + β) · TICKS_PER_GAME_YEAR` exactly as written in §3.13.3 (the owning module's formula; see `_unit.md` note on per-day GARCH), `σ_atm(T) = sqrt(σ_LR² + (σ_now² − σ_LR²)(1 − e^{−k T})/(k T))·(1 + vrp)`; `vrp` by underlying class (`fear = F/100` of the previous game hour). 04 anchor: for GX100 the 30-game-day ATM vol (interpolated in total variance between the bracketing expiries) is floored at `0.10 + 0.004·F`; for others the floor is that anchor × `σ_LR,u/σ_LR,GX`; apply as a multiplicative lift of the whole ATM term structure when the floor binds.
3. Smile: `x = ln(K/F)`, `σ = σ_atm(1 + skew(T)·x + 1.5x²)(1 + dealerTerm)`; dealer term `χ = PARAM.opt_dealer_vega_chi` (0.3) × `dealerNetVega/vegaCapacity`, clamped ±30% (dealers short vega when players net-buy → positive term → vol up); total σ clamped `[0.3σ_atm, 4σ_atm]`.
4. Guards at every evaluation (§3.13.3): (1) for each grid x, if `σ²(T_i)T_i < σ²(T_{i−1})T_{i−1}` raise the shorter-tenor σ to restore equality at that x (iterate from the longest tenor down to keep it single-pass deterministic); (2) for each expiry compute call prices on the strike grid; if any `C(K_i) − C(K_{i+1}) < 0` or convexity `C(K_{i−1}) − 2C(K_i) + C(K_{i+1}) < 0` (scaled for uneven steps), multiply skew by 0.9 and recompute, up to 30 steps (then set skew 0, which always passes). Results cached in `ast.drv_surface` per underlying per tick of evaluation; re-evaluated when S moves > 0.25 σ_tick, at each game hour (fear update), or when dealer vega changes > 5% of capacity.
5. Dealer quote (§3.13.3): mid = model price (T06B-02 with `sigmaOf`), `half = max(tickSize, vega·(0.005 + 0.02·σ))` (0.5 vol pt = 0.005 in σ units; vega per 1.00 vol), × (1 + 2·fear), × 2 when T < 1 game day; NPC dealers (3 desks of 22 §5.2 / 07 §6 NPC banks) widen ×2 and halve capacity when F > 40 (§6 item 1). Unmaterialized series are quoted through the model on request (view, RFQ, order touching it → `materialize`) — the quote exists for every grid strike (needed by GXV, T06B-04).
6. Dealer orders in P4: NPC_MAKER-pool `ExternalOrder`s at bid/ask with size = remaining vega capacity / series vega, split across the 3 desks by `allocate`; a desk at capacity on one side quotes only the reducing side.
7. Inventory: P6 after 05 matched, `fillsOfTick` filtered on option series where an NPC desk was a party updates `DealerBook` (net delta/gamma/vega via `greeksOf`, cached 30 ticks).
8. Delta hedge (§3.13.6) in P4: order on the underlying `= −(Δ_dealer − 0)` × multiplier, capped at `PARAM.opt_dealer_hedge_participation` (0.10) × the tick's expected volume (05 `modelAdv` × intraday profile); short gamma (players long calls) → dealers buy as price rises. Pinning and squeezes are not scripted.
9. Parity arbitrage (§6 item 2, X3): for each European (call, put) pair of the same strike/expiry with both books quoted, if `|parityGap| >` total fees both legs + underlying (05 fee schedule) + 0.10% of S, buy the cheap side and sell the rich side plus the offsetting underlying/forward, size capped at `PARAM.npc_arb_cap_frac` (0.02) × ADV of the series per tick; arbitrageurs carry no directional risk beyond 1 tick (they flatten the underlying leg next tick).
10. X13 pinning: on expiry days, if a beneficial-owner group holds ≥ 10% of open interest at strikes within 1% of the underlying's close and traded the underlying in the last 60 ticks before the closing auction, emit `mkt.surveillance.signal` SEALED `{pattern: 'EXPIRY_PINNING', actors, evidence}` for 05's score; trading stays allowed.
11. Port extension: `registerPortExtension('06B', {dealerNetGamma, packageQuote, hasListedOptions: id → optionUnderlyings contains id, underlyingOf: seriesId → s.underlyingId})`.

  - `06#34 Option NPC quotes satisfy calendar and butterfly no-arbitrage on the strike grid at every evaluation; put-call parity holds within spread + fees for European series`: property test over 500 seeded surfaces (random σ_now, σ_LR, fear, dealer vega, spot): after guards, total variance is non-decreasing in T at every grid x and calls are decreasing and convex in K; at every evaluation in a 5-game-day TestWorld run (03+04+05+06A+06B) the same checks hold on GX100 and two single stocks; for every European series pair `|C_mid − P_mid − (S e^{−qT} − K e^{−rT})| ≤ half_C + half_P + fees` with NPC parity arbitrage on.
  - clamp: an extreme skew input never yields σ outside `[0.3σ_atm, 4σ_atm]`.
- `dealers.test.ts`:
  - quote: vega 0.4, σ 0.2, fear 0, T 0.1 → half = max(tick, 0.4·(0.005 + 0.004)) = 0.0036; fear 0.5 → ×2; T < 1 day → ×2 again.
  - players net-buy 5,000 GX100 calls: desks' net vega negative, dealer term > 0, ATM σ up; F = 45 → spreads ×2 and capacity halved.
  - hedge: dealer Δ = +3,000 underlying units, expected tick volume 10,000 → sell order 1,000 (cap 10%); short gamma: a +1% move produces a buy hedge.
  - parity arb: a call quoted 0.5 above parity with fees 0.1 → arbitrage orders within the cap; gap below fees + 0.10% → no orders.
  - pinning: a group with 15% of an expiry's near-the-money OI trading the underlying before the close emits one SEALED signal; no signal without the option position.

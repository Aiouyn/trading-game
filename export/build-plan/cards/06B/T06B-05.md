## Goal
The option lifecycle after the trade: early exercise of American series (`mkt.option.exercise`, processed at t = 719 of the request day), automatic exercise at expiry of series in the money by ≥ 1 tick at the settlement price unless `mkt.option.doNotExercise` was submitted before t = 540, deterministic pro-rata assignment to writers, cash settlement at the SOQ (closing auction price; index from closing auction prices; crypto 30-tick TWAP t = 690–719; halted underlying: last mark), and corporate-action adjustments (split, special dividend > 10%, merger consideration, spin-off). Edge cases 4 (delisted underlying), 5 (bankrupt underlying), 20 (split crossing an exercise request) and 24 (unmaterialized series) are handled here.

## Implementation steps
1. `mkt.option.exercise` (P1): AMERICAN series, before expiry tick, holder long ≥ qty (net of earlier same-day requests), sufficient cash for calls (strike × multiplier × qty) or shares for puts, or margin capacity (07; with 07 absent: cash or shares only), license gate as for trading; else reject with `AST_REJECTS` codes. Writes an `ast.drv_exercise_req` row; HELD actors may submit it (T06A-02 `HELD_ALLOWED_INTENTS`).
2. DAY_CLOSE t = 719: `processEarlyExercises` in fixed order (seriesId, holderId, req id). Edge 20: a split effective the next t = 0 does not affect today's processing; a split already effective earlier today has been applied to the series and the request (qty × ratio) before processing. Physical delivery: calls `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(holder, ccp, underlying, qty·mult, strike·qty·mult, ccy, 'DRV_OPTION_EXERCISE')` then the CCP passes the same to assigned writers (step 5); puts the reverse. Cash series: CCP pays `(S − K)⁺·mult·qty` (call) or `(K − S)⁺` (put) with `DRV_OPTION_EXERCISE`. Positions in the option are closed with `moveQty` to the CCP (exercised contracts cease). Emits `mkt.option.exercised` (holder) and `mkt.option.assigned` (each writer), PARTIES.
3. Expiry (`expireSeries`): only materialized series (edge 24: unmaterialized series produce no state, events or postings). Settlement price `settlementPriceForOption`: equity/ETF/futures-option underlyings → `officialCloseOf(underlying, expiryDay)` (closing auction t = 540); index → index level computed from closing auction prices (06A index engine via `indexLevelOf`); crypto → 30-tick TWAP of the CRX/CEX price over t = 690–719 from 05 fills (no fills: the 05 mid TWAP); halted underlying → `markPrice` (06 §3.3.3); bankrupt (edge 5) → first DISTRESSED auction price; delisted by merger (edge 4) → the consideration value on the effective tick (series adjusted, then settled in cash at consideration value if the deliverable has no listed market).
4. Auto-exercise: ITM by ≥ 1 tick of the series (`tickSize`) at the settlement price and no DNE (DNE accepted only before t = 540 of expiry day); OTM and DNE series expire (`mkt.option.expired`, positions removed with `moveQty` to the CCP, no cash). Physical-settled ITM exercises deliver like step 2 at P13 of the expiry tick (shortfall of shares or cash: 07 handles as margin default; the engine never blocks the expiry).
5. Assignment (§3.13.4): for each series and tick, `exercisedQty` is allocated across writers (accounts with a short position, sorted by accountId) pro rata `floor(exercised · short_i / ΣShort)`; the integer remainder goes one contract each to accounts ordered by `fnv1a64(tickEntropy ‖ accountId)` ascending (02's `tickEntropy`), stopping when exhausted; `Σ assigned = exercised` exactly.
6. Adjustments (§3.13.5): on split effective (T06A-05 at t = 0): every series on that underlying gets strike ÷ ratio, multiplier × ratio (`num/den`), `adjusted = true`, id kept; resting option orders are adjusted by 05 from the `SPLIT` corporate action with the series multiplier change reported through the port (`corporateActions` includes a `SERIES_ADJUST` note for options; 05's quantity rule leaves option contract counts unchanged). Special dividend > 10% of the cum price: strike − D. Merger: deliverable = consideration package per contract (cash and/or shares × 100). Spin-off: deliverable = 100 parent + (100 × ratio) spin-off shares. No new strikes are listed on adjusted series (T06B-01 grid skips `adjusted`); new unadjusted series continue on the normal grid.
7. Value invariance (06#8): for a 5:1 split, positions in the underlying × 5 and prices ÷ 5 (T06A-05 + 05), option strikes ÷ 5 and multiplier × 5; the option model value per contract is unchanged, so every account's marked value is unchanged.

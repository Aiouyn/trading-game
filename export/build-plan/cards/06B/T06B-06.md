## Goal
Everything after the futures grid (T06B-01): fair value `F` per product (§3.14.2) handed to 05 as the series anchor with κ half-life 5 game days (rate and FX: 1 game day), daily settlement prices (§3.14.3), last-trade and expiry rules with final cash settlement (§3.14.4, `DRV_FUTURES_FINAL_SETTLE`), physical delivery elections for commodity futures with the delivery-failure rule (§3.10.5), compute capacity delivery records for 11, position limits against corners (§3.14.5, X4, X5), NPC basis arbitrageurs (§6 item 2) and commodity producers/consumers (§6 item 3). Variation margin is 07B's (it marks every tick); 06 fixes the daily settlement and final settlement.

## Implementation steps
1. Fair value (§3.14.2): index `F = S·e^{(z_ois − q)T}` (q = index dividend yield from projected dividends); rate "ONR-3M" `F = 100 − 100·fwdAvg(ois, quarter)` where `fwdAvg` = simple annualized forward `(DF(t1)/DF(t2) − 1)·364/91` over the 91-game-day reference quarter from `ois` zeros, inside the quarter mixing realized fixings so far and the forward for the rest; bond `F` = notional 6% 10-year bond price (per 100 face) from the `govt` forward curve at expiry (no cheapest-to-deliver basket, `DEFAULT`); commodity `futuresFair` (T06A-12, §3.10.3) at the benchmark hub; FX = `forwardRate` (§3.9.3 CIP with basis); crypto `F = S·e^{(z_ois,FDL + 0.5·H)T}` (H = 06C hype via the port extension `hype`, 0 when 06C absent); compute: compute forward = expected spot for the delivery month (`computeSpot` at the utilization path; flat at current spot when 11 gives no forecast); power: expected average hourly price for the month (current hourly anchor × seasonal factor 1). Each series' anchor `lnF = ln F_fair` with `anchorHalfLifeDays` 5 (RATE and FX 1) written to `ast.anchor` hourly and on underlying jumps.
2. Daily settlement (§3.14.3): EQUITY-session products (index futures) at the closing auction t = 540 (`officialCloseOf(series)`); FUT24/CRYPTO at the 30-tick VWAP over t = 690–719 from `fillsOfTick` (no trades → fair value). Emits `mkt.future.settlement` PUBLIC `{seriesId, price, day}`; 05's `settlementPriceOf` and 07B read this value.
3. Last trade and expiry (§3.14.4): index/FX/bond/crypto last trade = expiry tick (third-Friday-of-quarter rule of T06B-01 at t = 540, crypto t = 719); monthly commodities last trade = close of the 3rd trading day before the delivery month, delivery elections allowed during the last 3 trading days up to last trade; rate futures last trade = reference-quarter start + 91 game days, final = `100 − 100·compounded realized ONR` (07 §3.7.4 fixings); open positions after last trade settle in cash at the benchmark spot close (commodity) or the final settlement price (others): `ctx.ledger.pay(ccp ↔ holder, qty·mult·(Final − lastDailySettle), 'DRV_FUTURES_FINAL_SETTLE')` with VM to the last settle being 07's; positions closed by `moveQty` to the CCP; `mkt.future.settlement` emitted with `final: true`. Edge 4: an index future whose constituent merges just changes index; a single-name delisting is not applicable (no single-stock futures).
4. Delivery election (§3.10.5): `mkt.future.electDelivery` validation: in the last 3 trading days, long ≥ qty, hub is the contract's delivery hub, `physicalCapacityCheck(hub, commodity, qty·size)` passes — else reject (edge 9: storage full → rejected, the position cash-settles). At last trade (`runPhysicalDeliveries`): electing longs (sorted by electing tick, id) are matched to shorts holding receipts at the hub (largest receipt holders first, ties by id); remaining electing quantity is assigned to the other shorts pro rata by short size (remainders by `fnv1a64(tickEntropy ‖ accountId)`), who must deliver: the engine buys hub spot for them at `lastPrice` of the hub line, charging the cost plus a delivery-failure penalty of 5% of value (`CMDTY_PHYSICAL_DELIVERY` sub `FAILURE_PENALTY`, paid to the long), unless they hold a receipt. Delivery: the short's receipt quantity moves via `moveQty`, the long gets a receipt (`createReceipt`), cash = qty × final settlement (`CMDTY_PHYSICAL_DELIVERY`), emitted `mkt.future.delivery` PARTIES. Non-electing positions settle in cash (step 3).
5. Compute futures (PHYSICAL_CAPACITY): at expiry each long's contracts become an allocation row `{holderId, gpuh, month}` in `ast.drv_delivery` for 11 to provision; shorts that are not data-center owners pay the compute spot × qty (engine buys NPC cloud capacity: elastic supply, 01 §3.4.1); with 11 absent the row is still written (11 consumes later).
6. Limits (§3.14.5) as an extension of `preTradeGate` (T06A-16) for FUTURE orders increasing a position: spot month (last 10 trading days before last trade) net group position ≤ `PARAM.fut_spot_month_limit_frac` (0.10) × deliverable supply (inventory at the benchmark hub at spot-month start, snapshotted); all months ≤ max(25% of product OI, $50M notional / (F × mult)); compute ≤ 10% of `ports.ai.computeSupplyNextMonth`. Rejected `AST_POSITION_LIMIT` at P1. Breaches from OI falling: flagged at DAY_CLOSE; still in breach after 1 game day → `liquidateExcess` request to 07B (with 07B absent: an engine reduction order of the excess at the next opening auction, NPC pool).
7. Basis arbitrage (§6 item 2, X3): each tick in P4, for each listed future with a spot line (index via ETF/basket proxy GX100 ETF; commodities; FX; crypto), if `|F_mkt − F_fair|/F_fair >` fees of both legs + 0.10% → buy cheap/sell rich and offset in spot, capacity `npc_arb_cap_frac` × ADV per tick; flat within 1 tick (spot leg unwound when the future converges or at expiry).
8. Producers and consumers (§6 item 3, 22 roster, 16 models): at DAY_OPEN, producers sell futures to hedge 50% of next 6 months' expected output when the curve average ≥ 1.05 × `marginalCost` (T06A-12); consumers (airlines, refiners, food firms) buy 30% of next quarter needs; orders go to the 05 opening flow as `ExternalOrder` pool HEDGER, spread across the months they cover.
9. SRL position source: `registerSrlPositionSource(srlPositionSource)` = net long SRL from FDLSRL futures OI (players' net) + FX forwards (T06B-08 rows), 1-year average from a daily ring.
10. Port extension: `fairBasisLn`, anchors for FUTURE series via the anchor table, `underlyingOf(seriesId)`, `preTradeGate` FUTURE branch (`futuresLimitGate`).

## Test plan
- `futures.test.ts`:
  - fair: index S 1,000, z 0.04, q 0.02, T 0.25 → 1,000·e^{0.005} = 1,005.0125208594010; rate future with flat ois 3.5% continuous → 100 − 100·(e^{0.035·91/364} − 1)·364/91 = 96.48464274067354 (1e-12); crypto with H 0.5 adds 0.25 to the carry rate.
  - daily settlement: FUT24 product with fills at t = 690–719 → VWAP; none → fair; index future → closing auction price.
  - final settlement: rate future final uses realized ONR fixings; commodity monthly last trade is the 3rd trading day before the delivery month.
  - limits: a group at 9% of deliverable supply buying 2% more in the spot month → `AST_POSITION_LIMIT`; outside the spot month allowed up to 25% OI; compute order above 10% of next-month supply rejected (X5); a breach from OI falling persists 1 game day → excess reduction requested.
  - basis arb closes a 1% gap toward fees + 0.10% within the capacity cap and never holds directional risk > 1 tick.
- `delivery.test.ts`:
  - `06#22 A commodity futures holder who elects delivery with storage capacity receives a receipt at the hub; a holder without capacity is cash-settled; spot-month positions above 10% of deliverable supply are rejected`: TestWorld 03+05+06A+06B with CRUDE at its benchmark hub: long A elects 3 contracts with capacity → receipt of 3,000 bbl at the hub, pays 3 × 1,000 × final; long B's election with a full hub rejected (edge 9) → cash-settled at the benchmark spot close; an order that would put a group above 10% of the snapshotted deliverable supply in the spot month rejected at P1.
  - delivery failure: assigned short without receipt and insufficient spot at the hub → engine purchase at hub `lastPrice` and 5% penalty paid to the long (`FAILURE_PENALTY` sub-code).
  - X4: a corner attempt (one group long 30% OI into delivery) is blocked by the spot-month limit before the spot month starts reducing.

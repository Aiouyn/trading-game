## Goal
The ViewHandlers the 17.B screens of 06 §7 need for derivatives: the options chain (expiries × strikes, smile and term structure, greeks on demand, payoff builder preview whose multi-leg ticket submits legs as separate orders in the same tick), the futures and perps panel (curve, OI, funding history and countdown, mark vs index, liquidation line read from 07B), the swap PVs and cashflow schedules for the RFQ blotter, the credit desk's CDS curves, GX Credit 40, credit events and recovery auctions, plus the 06B parts of the corporate calendar (CDS rolls) and the compute screen (reserved curve). Privacy (L11, D109): a viewer sees only its own positions, trades and exercise requests.

## Implementation steps
1. Pure functions over `StoreRead` with zod args; no writes (greeks use the cached path without writing the cache when called from a view — compute on demand and return; P-phase dealer hedging owns cache writes).
2. Options chain: series from `seriesDefinitionsAt` (all grid strikes), quotes from the model and dealer half-spread (T06B-03); `oi` from `ast.drv_series_state`; IV from the surface. Payoff preview returns order drafts, never submits.
3. Licensing: options/futures/perps chain data is public (market data); the order tickets are gated by 05; the views return `licenseHint` for the viewer (`requiredLicense`), not a lock, except the credit desk (LIC3) and swap blotter (own only).
4. Privacy (L11, D109): `ast.swapBlotter`, `ast.myDerivatives` filter by `viewer.entityIds`; no view exposes other holders' positions; OI and funding are aggregates.
5. Composition with 06A views via `registerViewExtension`: add `cdsRolls` to `ast.corpCalendar`, CDS curves to `ast.creditIssuer`, reserved curve to `ast.computePower`, and OPTION/FUTURE/PERP/SWAP/CDS class panels to `ast.asset`; the derivative ids above stand alone.

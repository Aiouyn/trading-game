## Goal
The data model of unit 06B: option series, vol surfaces, futures series, perps, swap trades, CDS contracts and credit events (06 §2.6) as 06 tables, the 06B event schemas, the deterministic listing grids for options (underlying sets, weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual and crypto expiries, nice-number strike grids extended each day) and futures (06 §3.14.1), and lazy series: definitions exist inside the grid but state is materialized only when an order or position first touches a series (06 §3.1.3; edge 24). Derivatives are LIC3/LIC4-gated (L10; gates are T06A-02's).

## Implementation steps
1. Types: copy 06 §2.6 verbatim. Each listed series is an `Asset` row with `cls OPTION/FUTURE/PERP`, `createdBy: 'ENGINE_SERIES'`, `licenseLong 'LIC3'`, `licenseShort 'LIC3'` (naked option writes need LIC4: enforced by `requiredLicense` with act WRITE_NAKED), `clearing` from the grid rule (CCP_LD, CCP_CRY), `marginClass` 'OPT_IDX'|'OPT_EQ'|'OPT_FUT'|'OPT_FX'|'OPT_CRY'|'FUT_IDX'|'FUT_RATE'|'FUT_BOND'|'FUT_CMD'|'FUT_FX'|'FUT_CRY'|'PERP_CRY'.
2. Option grid (§3.13.1): underlyings GX100 + 5 regional indices (European, cash, 10 × index, CCP_LD); top 40 equities by free-float cap re-selected each QUARTER_CLOSE (American, physical, 100 shares); ETFs GX100, gold, HY bond, CRX10 (European, cash, 100); futures CRUDE, NATGAS, GOLD, COPPER, CORN front 3 (American on the future, physical into the future, 1); FX legs EUNFDL, FDLPYN, FDLSRL, FDLNKR (European, cash, 100,000 base); AUC, LTX (European, cash TWAP, 1 token, CCP_CRY). Expiries at t = 540 of game Fridays (crypto t = 719): next 4 Fridays (weekly), last Friday of next 3 game months, next 4 quarter-end Fridays, next 2 year-end Fridays; crypto: next 3 game days at t = 719 plus the weeklies. Weekends cannot be expiries (edge 3).
3. Strikes: `step = niceStep(spot · {1%, 2.5%, 5%})`, strikes = multiples of step within ±10% / ±25% / ±50% of spot at listing; each DAY_OPEN extend so ≥ 5 strikes exist on each side of spot; strikes with open interest are never removed. Strike in Price micro-units; id via `optionSeriesId`.
4. Futures grid (§3.14.1): index futures GX100 + 5 regional (next 4 quarterly, 50 × index, cash at expiry closing auction, session EQUITY + FUT24 overnight); rate futures "ONR-3M" per bloc (next 8 quarterly; `underlyingId null`, `refRateBloc`); 10Y notional bond futures per bloc (next 2 quarterly, 100,000 face, 6% notional coupon); 13 physical commodity futures (energy and metals monthly next 12; AGRI months 3, 5, 7, 9, 12 for 2 years; contract sizes from 22 §4.6); compute (monthly next 6, 10,000 GPUh, physical capacity via 11); power per bloc (monthly next 6, 100 MWh over the month's game hours, cash vs average hourly price); FX futures on 4 legs (next 4 quarterly, 100,000 base); crypto AUC, LTX (next 3 quarterly, 1 token, cash TWAP, session CRYPTO). Expiry and last-trade ticks per §3.14.4 (computed in T06B-06; the grid calls `futureExpiries`).
5. Lazy series (§3.1.3): `seriesDefinitionsAt` builds the grid deterministically on demand; nothing is stored until `materialize` is called (by the 05 order-acceptance hook through the port extension, by an RFQ, by an exercise or by position creation). Quotes for unmaterialized series come from the model (T06B-03). Edge 24: at expiry, unmaterialized series produce no events and no state.
6. Perps: genesis creates the 11 perp assets (06 §3.15 underlyings, `fundingIntervalTicks 240`, `maxLeverageByLicense {LIC3: 10, LIC4: 20}`, `insuranceFundId` from 07B CRY service).
7. Events: zod schemas for every 06B kind; `mkt.perp.funding` PUBLIC for the rate with a PARTIES twin `mkt.perp.funding` payload variant for individual payments (two emissions, two visibilities; the kind key is shared, the payload discriminates `scope: 'RATE'|'PAYMENT'`).

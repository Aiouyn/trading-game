## Goal
The OTC swap book of 06 §3.16: the ONR floating index and its compounding (07 §3.7.4 publishes ONR), the seven products (OIS, ZCIS, FX_FWD, FX_SWAP, XCCY, TRS, COMMODITY_SWAP) with their standard tenors and clearing (CCP_OTC or BILATERAL under a CSA), each registered as an RFQ product with T06A-15 (pricer, sigma/ADV-equivalent, dealer capacity, execute), fair rates (§3.16.3), hourly valuation with DV01 projection between (§3.16.4), periodic payments and settlements (`DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`, `FX_FORWARD_SETTLE`, `FX_SWAP_LEG`), termination by unwind RFQ and close-out by offsetting cleared trades (§3.16.5, `mkt.swap.terminate`), and the SWAP `ModelValuer` for marks. Edge 8: FX controls during a pending forward settlement.

## Implementation steps
1. Floating index (§3.16.1): ONR per bloc from 07 at t = 719; compounding over a period `Π(1 + ONR_d/364) − 1` over the game days of the period (weekends included at the Friday fixing, 07's convention). With 07B absent the fixing is 04's policy rate (`policyRate`) — same rule, empty input.
2. Products (§3.16.2) and RFQ registration with T06A-15: each `RfqProduct` defines a zod spec (tenor, notional, ccy or pair, fixed rate side, reference asset for TRS, commodity for COMMODITY_SWAP), `act` (LIC3 for OIS/ZCIS/FX/COMMODITY; LIC4 for XCCY and TRS per T06A-02's `requiredLicense` table), `modelMid` (par rate, forward points, or spread), `sigmaAndAdvEquiv` (rate products: σ = daily vol of the par rate from the last 21 hourly curves × √(size), ADV equivalent 22 §5.2 dealer table; FX: `sigmaDailyOf(pair)`; TRS: underlying's σ), `dealerCapacity` (NPC bank dealer risk capacity from 16; default `SPEC_CONSTANTS_06.rfqDealerCapacity`), `execute` = `bookSwap`, `cleared` per `SWAP_CLEARING`.
3. Fair rates (§3.16.3): OIS `K = (1 − DF(T))/Σ τ_k DF(t_k)`, `τ_k = 91/364`, `DF = e^{−ois(t)t}`; swap spread vs government = `K − govt par yield` (computed the same way off `govt`, shown in views). ZCIS: `(1 + K)^T = E[CPI_T/CPI_0]·(1 + 0.002·√T)` — the inflation risk premium `0.002·T^0.5` is added to `K` (`K = E^{1/T} − 1 + 0.002√T`; documented decision on how the premium is combined). TRS spread = `ONR + PARAM.trs_dealer_spread_bps (50)·(1 + 2·fear)` plus the borrow fee when the dealer hedges a short. Commodity swap fixed = average of the futures curve (`futuresFair`) over the game months of the period. FX_FWD rate = `forwardRate` (CIP + basis); FX_SWAP = near spot + far forward; XCCY basis = `xccyBasis` on the non-FDL leg.
4. Valuation (§3.16.4): full PV every game hour (`t mod 30 == 0`) and on demand (views, RFQ unwind); between hours `projectedPv = PV_h + DV01 × Δcurve(bp)` for 07's margin. Discount at `ois` of the payment currency. OIS PV (receive fixed) = `K_trade·Σ τ DF − (1 − DF(T))` per unit notional for a fresh trade, generalized with accrued compounded ONR of the current period. `registerModelValuer('SWAP', …)` returns PV per unit notional.
5. Payments (DAY_CLOSE t = 719 after ONR): OIS quarterly net = notional × (fixed·τ − compoundedOnr(period)) → `DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`; ZCIS at maturity `CPI_T/CPI_0 − 1` vs `(1 + K)^T − 1`; FX_FWD at the settlement date gross exchange of both currencies (`FX_FORWARD_SETTLE`); FX_SWAP near and far legs (`FX_SWAP_LEG`); XCCY notional exchange at both ends and quarterly floating vs floating + basis (`DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`); TRS monthly reset: total return (price change + dividends) vs ONR + spread, net (`DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`); COMMODITY_SWAP monthly fixed vs average daily spot of the month. Cleared trades pay through `ccp_OTC` (payer → CCP → receiver), bilateral trades party to party. Emits `mkt.swap.payment` PARTIES.
6. Edge 8 (FX controls): an FX forward/swap settling while 04's controls are active on a currency settles contractually; the controlled-currency proceeds are frozen by `freezeProceedsUntilControlsEnd` until controls end; the forward is never cancelled.
7. Termination (§3.16.5): `mkt.swap.terminate` (party, LIVE trade) opens an UNWIND RFQ (`spec: {kind: 'UNWIND', tradeId}`) where dealers quote `PV ± spread` (spread from the product's sigma formula); accept → `executeUnwind` pays the PV-based amount (`DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`, sub 'UNWIND'), status TERMINATED, `mkt.swap.terminated` PARTIES. Cleared trades can also be closed by an offsetting cleared trade with identical terms: `netClearedOffsets` closes both (CCP nets). Novation of bilateral trades: see T06B-09 (`mkt.cds.notifyNovation`, which accepts bilateral swap trade ids too, with the remaining party's consent).

## Test plan
- `swaps.test.ts`:
  - OIS par: 2y quarterly, flat continuous zero 3.5% → K = 0.0351535725932649 (1e-12).
  - `06#38 OIS par rate from §3.16.3 makes the swap's PV zero at inception to within $1 per $100M notional`: for random Nelson-Siegel ois curves (500 seeds) and tenors 1, 2, 5, 10, 30: PV at K_par of a $100M notional ≤ $1 in absolute value (Money units).
  - compounded ONR: fixings 3.64% for 91 days → (1 + 0.0001)^91 − 1 = 0.0091410717527316; quarterly payment = notional × (K·0.25 − that).
  - ZCIS, TRS (fear 0.5 → ONR + 100 bp), commodity swap (average of a known curve) formulas.
  - payments: cleared OIS pays via `ccp_OTC` with `DRV_SWAP_PAYMENT`; FX forward settles both currencies with `FX_FORWARD_SETTLE`; FX swap near/far legs `FX_SWAP_LEG`.
  - edge 8: forward on FDLSRL settling under controls: settlement posted, SRL proceeds held until the controls' `untilTick`, then released.
  - valuation: between hours projected PV within 1e-6 of full PV for a 1 bp parallel move; hourly recompute at t mod 30 == 0 only.
  - termination: unwind RFQ quoted at PV ± spread; accept → TERMINATED and the payment; two offsetting cleared trades net to closed.

## Goal
Two programs: players with LIC4 and the `CH_EXCHANGE_MEMBER` charter can register as market makers (quote both sides atomically, meet presence and size obligations, earn a rebate, crowd the NPC maker up to half on their levels, lose rebates and registration when absent); and two parties can agree a block that executes at the official close outside the auction, with no impact and a deferred print.

## Implementation steps
1. Registration (05 §3.19.1, D090): `mkt.mm.register {entityId, assets}` requires LIC4 and `CH_EXCHANGE_MEMBER` held by the principal/entity (reject `NO_LICENSE` when LIC4 is missing and `NOT_MM` when the charter is missing), ≤ `PARAM.mkt_mm_max_assets` assets; effective at the next trading day's open (`since` = that tick). Re-application blocked 28 game days after a revocation (`NOT_MM`).
2. `mkt.mm.quote {assetId, bid:{price,qty}, ask:{price,qty}}` (P1, from a registered entity, else `NOT_MM`): atomically replaces both sides as two resting LIMIT DAY orders tagged as quote orders (one message for the message fee, 05 §3.15.1; exempt up to 2,000 per day). `mkt.mm.resign {entityId, assets?}`: effective next open.
3. Obligations (05 §3.19.2): per asset per trading day, presence = share of continuous ticks with both sides quoted at half-spread ≤ `PARAM.mkt_mm_spread_mult × s` and size ≥ `PARAM.mkt_mm_min_qty_adv` ADV per side; required ≥ `PARAM.mkt_mm_presence` (50% while F > 60). Edge 23: tiny quotes inside the NPC spread are allowed but do not count for presence.
4. Benefits and sanctions (05 §3.19.3): quote fills pay `PARAM.mkt_mm_rebate` (negative fee, `MKT_MAKER_REBATE` from `exc_<bloc>`) through T05-10 (`isRegisteredMmQuote`). Missing presence on ≥ 2 days in a game week → `REBATE_SUSPENDED` for the next week; 3 consecutive suspended weeks → `REVOKED` (re-application after 28 game days). Emit `mkt.mm.status` (PRIVATE) daily and on change.
5. Crowding (05 §3.19.4): for levels within 2s of `lnRef` where registered makers display depth, the NPC maker's depth on those levels is multiplied by `mmCrowding = max(0.5, 1 − 0.5 × mmDepth/makerDepth)`; T05-04's `depthMult` receives `mmCrowdingFn`. The NPC maker never leaves (exploit #12: pulling quotes in a crash cannot break the market).
6. Block crosses (05 §3.20, D091): `mkt.block.cross {crossId?, accountId, counterpartyEntityId, assetId, side, qty}`; the first side gets `crossId = ctx.newId('blk')` (shared out of band); the second must match asset, quantity and opposite side and name the first as counterparty; both before `t = 535` (`SPEC_CONSTANTS.blockCutoffTick`). Minimum size max(0.25 × ADV, $1M notional at the last price) (`BLOCK_TOO_SMALL`). `mkt.block.cancel {crossId}` before t = 535. Execution in 6c of the closing tick at the **official closing price** (edge 21: when no closing auction prints, the official close rule of T05-02 applies), outside the auction (no impact, no tally, no maker interaction); holds checked at 6c: a side failing buying power fails the cross (`FAILED`, `mkt.block.failed` PARTIES). Settlement `MKT_BLOCK_TRADE` via T05-10 with fee = half the taker rate. On success emit `mkt.block.done` (PARTIES). Linked parties allowed and monitored (F14, F13; exploit #13).
7. Deferred print: published on the tape with `block = true, deferred = true` at the next day's opening tick (24-hour assets: 180 ticks after execution) and `mkt.block.print` PUBLIC.

## Test plan
- `marketMakers.test.ts`:
  - Registration: LIC3 entity → `MKT_NO_LICENSE`; LIC4 without charter → `MKT_NOT_MM`; valid → ACTIVE from next open; 51 assets rejected.
  - **05#45 registered MM missing presence twice in a week loses its rebate the next week**: presence 85%, 40%, 85%, 30%, 90% → status REBATE_SUSPENDED next week (quote fills pay the normal maker fee); three consecutive suspended weeks → REVOKED.
  - **05#46 NPC maker depth never falls below half its uncrowded value due to MM crowding**: mmDepth/makerDepth 0.4 → 0.8; 1.0 → 0.5; 5.0 → 0.5; property over random ratios ≥ 0.5; levels beyond 2s → 1.
  - Presence edge 23: quote of 0.001 ADV inside the spread does not count.
  - F > 60: presence requirement 50%.
- `blockCross.test.ts`:
  - **05#47 block crosses execute at the official close with no tally change and print deferred at the next open**: two players agree 0.3 ADV ($2M) at t = 500; closing auction prints $10.00; at 6c both legs fill at $10.00, maker `Qc` unchanged, `mkt.block.done` emitted to both; the tape of the closing tick has no block print; the next day's opening tick has a print with `block: true, deferred: true`.
  - Too small (0.2 ADV and $0.5M) → `MKT_BLOCK_TOO_SMALL`; second side at t = 536 → rejected; buyer without cash at 6c → FAILED for both.
  - Fee: $2M notional EQ → half of 3 bp = $300.

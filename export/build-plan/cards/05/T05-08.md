## Goal
Compute each asset's model average daily volume (ADV) from fundamentals (size × turnover × fear × attention × regime), never from realized volume, so wash trading cannot inflate liquidity, impact or fee tiers; generate background volume so the tape totals about ADV per day; and provide the U-shaped intraday profile `U(t)` used by VWAP algos, pool pacing and background volume. ADV is consumed by 03 (thin-print guard), 07 (liquidity haircuts) and 08 (basket cost) through the `mkt.adv` view (T05-20).

## Implementation steps
1. `ADV_units = turnover × size × (1 + PARAM.mkt_adv_fear_k × max(0, F − 20)) × attention × regimeTurnover` (05 §3.14.1). `size` from `ports.instruments.sizeOf` (free float for equities, outstanding face for bonds, 22's authored daily notional for FX legs where `turnover = 'AUTHORED'` means size already is the daily notional and turnover = 1, supply float for crypto, open interest for futures); ETFs with turnover 'CONSTITUENTS' use the weighted ADV share of constituents (Σ w_i × ADV_i notional / ETF price). `attention = 1 + min(1, Σ newsworthiness of today's items)` (16 via `ports.world.newsworthinessToday`). `advNotionalFdl = advUnits × lastPrice` converted to FDL with 04's FX fair value of the asset currency.
2. Edge 18: until 5 trading days of model inputs exist (`aux.daysOfInputs < 5`), use 22's genesis turnover through the same formula with `F = 20`, attention 1, regime factor 1 (no realized inputs at all).
3. Recompute at P8 t=719 (emit `mkt.adv.updated {assetId, advUnits, advNotionalFdl}` PUBLIC) and intraday at each game hour if the Fear Index moved more than 10 points since the last computation.
4. ADV never reads fills, `dayVolume`, or background volume (D087). Exploit #2 counter: there is no code path from realized volume to ADV, impact or fee tiers.
5. Intraday profile (05 §3.14.3): EXCHANGE_SESSION opening auction 8%, closing auction 12%, continuous ticks t ∈ 61..539 with `w(t) ∝ 1 + 1.5 e^{−(t−61)/40} + 1.0 e^{−(539−t)/40}` normalized to 80%; pre-market and after-hours 0. 24-hour profiles: fix/settlement auction 6%; other matching ticks `w ∝ 1 + 0.6 × [60 ≤ t ≤ 540]` normalized to 94% over the matching ticks of the day (FUT excludes 710..719). Crypto has no auction: weights normalized to 100%. Precompute tables once per profile (720 floats) at module construction.
6. Background volume (05 §3.14.2) per matching tick: `bg = max(0, ADV × U(t) × e^{0.3 z} − modeled)`, `z` from `mkt.npc_flow:<assetId>` (this stream replaces 05's `mkt/bg`, see T05-01), `modeled` = matched quantity of this tick (orders and maker). It prints at p* with aggressor NONE, no impact, no postings; it is added to the tape and day volume only. It is excluded from POV's `vol_{N−1}` (`aux.prevVolume` stores matched + maker only; exploit #19) and from surveillance denominators.
7. Determinism: the bg draw is the first draw of `mkt.npc_flow:<assetId>` in P6 of each matching tick (exactly one draw even when ADV is 0).

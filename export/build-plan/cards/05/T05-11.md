## Goal
Every `mkt.*` order intent passes the same ordered P1 checks for humans, player AIs and delegated operators (fairness rule): schema and grid, open-order limits, license and Held, session, capital controls, onboarding cap, position and short limits, buying power and holds. Accepted orders become `Order` rows with holds; oversized non-algo orders become POV parents; modifications and cancels act before the tick's auction. Ownership-threshold crossings are emitted after fills.

## Implementation steps
1. Run the checks of 05 §3.8 strictly in this order; the first failure rejects with its code (`MKT_<code>`):
   1. Schema (zod), ids exist, `qty` a positive multiple of `lotSize` (`BAD_LOT`), prices on the grid (`gridTickAt`/`onGrid`, `BAD_PRICE_GRID`), price within `[minPrice, 10 × prevClose]` (`BAD_PRICE_GRID`), GTD ≤ 91 game days ahead (`BAD_SCHEMA`), iceberg via `validateIceberg` (`BAD_LOT`), OCO groups ≤ 3 members (`BAD_SCHEMA`), type allowed for the class (`SESSION_CLOSED_FOR_TYPE`).
   2. Rate and open-order limits: the 20-per-tick intent limit is enforced by 02/19 intake (G9) which returns `MKT_RATE_LIMIT` before P1; 05 enforces ≤ `PARAM.mkt_max_open_orders` open orders per actor and ≤ `PARAM.mkt_max_open_orders_asset` per asset (`TOO_MANY_ORDERS`). AI agents and operators count against their principal's character (exploit #17; operators also have their own 20 at intake).
   3. License: `MarketSpec.licenseRequired` via `hasActiveLicense(store, principal, lic)` (`NO_LICENSE`); LIC2 for algos (T05-12). Held (13 §3.10, owner of the Held rule): a Held principal may submit only cancels and reducing orders through the UI/API channels (`HELD`); orders from channel `AI` or `MANAGER` acting under a mandate stay allowed, and standing orders stay live (13 §3.10 "AIs keep running within their mandate, standing orders stay live"). This resolves the contradiction with 05 §8 edge 13 by precedence (13 owns Held): at Held start 05 does not cancel standing orders; manually started algo parents (channel UI/API) stop (status PAUSED until Held ends), matching 05 edge 13's "existing algos stop". Reason `HELD_FORCED` is used when 13 sends a forced reduce (handled through the LIQUIDATION path of 07).
   4. Session: order type allowed in the current effective state (`effectiveState`, `auctionAllowsType`, `mocCutoffCheck`); halted assets accept orders and cancels but not IOC/FOK (`HALTED_IOC`); suspended assets reject everything except cancels (`ASSET_SUSPENDED`).
   5. Capital controls: implemented in T05-12 (`fxChecks`), called here at this position.
   6. Onboarding cap (18): `ports.onboarding.firstTradeCap(principal)`; buys only: if buy notional (qty × limit or band hi) exceeds the cap, reject `ONBOARDING_CAP` (05 §10 test 38 "enforced on buys only"; sells never checked).
   7. Limits (05 §3.15.2): `positionCapCheck` (`POSITION_CAP`: group holding + qty > `PARAM.mkt_cap_npc_float` × float for NPC companies, `PARAM.mkt_cap_player_float` for player companies per `ports.companies.isPlayerCompany`), `shortCapCheck` (`SHORT_CAP`: new short sales rejected while aggregate short interest ≥ `PARAM.mkt_short_cap` × float), `computeOiCapCheck` (`OI_CAP`: compute futures OI per group ≤ `PARAM.mkt_compute_oi_cap` of total).
   8. Buying power and holds: cash accounts hold `qty × (limit, or band hi for market buys) × (1 + taker fee rate)` via `ctx.holds.placeCash` (on `LED_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` → `INSUFFICIENT_BP`); sells hold the asset via `placeQty`; a sell beyond the position is a short sale: requires `ports.credit` and a locate (`borrowLocate`, else `NO_LOCATE`); margin accounts (`ports.credit.isMarginAccount`) call `bpCheck` (`INSUFFICIENT_BP` if not ok; hold = `initialMarginHold`). Defendant restrictions are inside 07's check (R12); 05 only passes the result. `postOnly` marketable vs N−1 best → `POST_ONLY_WOULD_CROSS`; `reduceOnly` without a position → `REDUCE_ONLY_NO_POSITION`.
2. Ordering inside P1: intents are processed in the sealed `orderKey` order (02); order ids come from `ctx.newId('ord')` in that order, so ids and priority depend only on orderKey (INV-MKT-006).
3. Auto-split (05 §3.6.8, D081): a non-algo order with `qty > PARAM.mkt_autosplit_adv × ADV` becomes a POV parent at `PARAM.mkt_autosplit_pov` with the order's limit (or the band for market orders), DAY TIF, no license needed; emit `mkt.order.accepted` with `{autosplit: true, algoId}`; the preview (T05-21) tells the player in advance.
4. `mkt.order.place` apply (P1): create the `Order` (status WORKING, or PENDING_TRIGGER for stop types; brackets create the entry plus two children with `bracketParentId`, status PENDING_TRIGGER until `activateBracket`), store, emit `mkt.order.accepted` (PRIVATE). Rejections emit `mkt.order.rejected` (PRIVATE) with the code.
5. `mkt.order.modify` (P1, before the auction): `ORDER_NOT_FOUND` for unknown/foreign/closed orders; re-run steps 1, 4, 7, 8 for the delta (raising qty increases the hold; lowering reduces it); `applyModify` sets priority; emit `mkt.order.modified`.
6. `mkt.order.cancel` / `cancelAll {assetId?, accountId?}` (one intent): cancel reason USER, release holds (`releaseOrderHold`), emit `mkt.order.cancelled`. Held principals may always cancel.
7. Every accepted, modified or cancelled order counts one message in `recordGroupActivity` for the message fee (T05-10).
8. Ownership thresholds (05 §3.15.2): after P7 settlement, for every equity fill, compare the group's holding before and after against 5/10/15/20/25% of shares outstanding; emit `mkt.ownership.thresholdCrossed {ownerGroupId, assetId, threshold, direction, tick}` PARTIES to 15 and 09.
9. Edge 11 (asset suspended with algos working) and edge 13 (Held mid-day) are wired here as handlers `onSuspend(assetId)` (cancel all orders `ASSET_SUSPENDED`, release holds; T05-12 cancels algos) and `onHeldStart(principal)` (per step 1.3). Exploit #18: open-order caps.
- **E-47 participation cap.** In P1, the friction owner group's (T03-17 `frictionNW`) filled-plus-working quantity today in one instrument ≤ `mkt_topend_part_base / w(N)` × ADV [Quant]; excess refused with `MKT_TOPEND_PARTICIPATION` and the cause `why.topend.participation`. Cost of size uses T05-04 δ(q) unchanged (no new impact math). Applies after auto-split (a POV parent counts its children).
- **E-59 participation anchor (E-59; final per E-60).** The cap binds only on net new exposure above the anchor = max(`mkt_topend_anchor_avg_gd` = 20-GD average position, `mkt_topend_anchor_ema_gd` = 120-GD EMA of position). The anchor may rise at most `msi_drift_per_gd` + `mkt_topend_anchor_rise` (0.0029 + 0.0025) per GD, compounded. Positions are per friction owner group (T03-17).

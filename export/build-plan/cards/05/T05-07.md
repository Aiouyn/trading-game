## Goal
Bound how fast prices can move and stop trading when they move too far: per-tick bands with clamping and volatility interruptions, LULD-style single-asset halts at ±15% / ±30%, bond halts, pre-news halts, per-bloc market-wide circuit breakers L1/L2/L3 on the bloc's main index, futures limit-up/down with lock and widening, and the overlay that turns the scheduled session slot (T05-02) into the effective trading state for every asset each tick.

## Implementation steps
1. Per-tick band (05 §3.9.1): `|ln p* − lnP_{N−1}| ≤ w`, `w = max(tickBandMin, PARAM.mkt_band_sigma_k × σ_tick)`. Auction bands per the export signature, measured from the last price. `bandPrices` rounds `lo` up and `hi` down to the price grid, never below `minPrice` (edge 17: at the floor sells rest unfilled; T05-19 flags the asset), and inside futures `limitUp/limitDown`.
2. Clamping: if the unconstrained clearing price would lie outside (use `unconstrainedWouldExceed` with a band widened to the asset's 10× prevClose validation range), `p*` is clamped to the edge and executes what crosses there; `bandClamped = true`. Three consecutive clamped ticks in the same direction start a VOL_INTERRUPTION of `PARAM.mkt_vol_interrupt_ticks` ticks (no matching, entry and cancels allowed, indicative every tick) ending with a REOPEN auction using the opening band. The streak resets on the first unclamped tick or a direction change (edge 2). Crypto and FX: bands every tick, no interruptions (05 §3.9.1; 05#26).
3. Single-asset halts (`EQUITY_LULD`: equities, ETFs, listed compute and data credits): reference = previous official close; move ≥ `PARAM.mkt_halt_l1` → HALTED `PARAM.mkt_halt_l1_ticks`; ≥ `PARAM.mkt_halt_l2` → `PARAM.mkt_halt_l2_ticks`; each level at most once per day (`aux.l1Today`, `aux.l2Today`); afterwards bands only. A halt that would end after t=530 ends at the closing auction instead (`clampHaltEnd`). Every halt ends with a REOPEN auction. Corporate bonds (`BOND` rule, class CORP_BOND): ±10% → 60 ticks (`SPEC_CONSTANTS.bondHaltPct/bondHaltTicks`); government bonds bands only. Company-event halts (D084): for each `ports.companies.scheduledMaterialNews` item, HALTED from `publishTick − PARAM.mkt_news_halt_ticks` to `publishTick`, ending in a REOPEN.
4. Market-wide circuit breakers (05 §3.9.3) per bloc on its main index `<BLOC>50` (level supplied by T05-19): decline vs previous close ≥ 7% → L1 halt of 120 ticks of every EXCHANGE_SESSION instrument of that bloc's exchange (equities, ETFs, equity options); ≥ 13% → L2 halt 120 ticks; ≥ 20% → L3: trading ends for the session, the close is the last price, the closing auction is skipped. L1 and L2 once per day each and never at or after `t = PARAM.mkt_mwcb_cutoff_tick`; L3 any time. Bonds, FX, futures and crypto do not halt (except index futures of the bloc, step 5). Emit `mkt.mwcb.triggered {bloc, level, tick}` (PUBLIC; 04 labels an emergent FLASH_CRASH from it).
5. Futures (`FUT_LIMITS`): limits ±`PARAM.mkt_fut_limit` of prevSettle. Locked = clearing at the limit with the limit side's imbalance persisting; after 10 consecutive locked ticks → LIMIT_LOCKED for 20 ticks, then widen once to ±`PARAM.mkt_fut_limit_wide` and run a REOPEN. Index futures of a bloc also follow L1/L2/L3. Options: `OPTION_FOLLOW` takes the underlying's state; ETFs with `ETF_FOLLOW` take their index's market-wide state plus their own LULD.
6. Overlap (edge 3): when a single-asset halt and a market-wide halt overlap, the later end tick wins and one REOPEN auction runs. Edge 20: a scheduled daily close tick ends a VOL_INTERRUPTION or halt early and the closing auction runs (unless L3 stopped trading). Edge 4: L3 before the close → no closing auction, official close = last price (T05-02 `officialClose` with `l3Stopped`), MOC orders expire unfilled.
7. Halt behaviour (05 §3.9.5) is enforced by consumers using `effectiveState`: entry, modify and cancel work; stops not evaluated (T05-10); algos pause (T05-12); market-order countdowns pause (T05-10); the value layer keeps stepping (T05-03); indicative published for the last 10 ticks before reopening (`indicative: true`); pools submit only `auctionOnly` orders (T05-14); 07 does not liquidate into a halted book and queues for the reopen (edge 14: liquidation orders join the reopen as `auctionOnly` market orders; T05-14 marks them).
8. `mkt.state.changed {assetId, tradingState, untilTick?}` is emitted PUBLIC on every change of effective state (T05-22 calls it).
9. Exploit #20 (triggering a market-wide halt to force a CB cut): no special code; L1/L2 are rare with world-scale depth and F5 scoring applies (T05-17).

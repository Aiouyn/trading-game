## Goal
Keep the market consistent through an instrument's life: listing with an IPO auction, splits and consolidations adjusting every resting order, stop, trailing peak, pool position and history, dividends dropping value and maker base on the ex-date, suspensions and delistings cancelling orders, and index membership changes. Compute every tick the index levels used across the game (GX100, `<BLOC>50` used by circuit breakers, sector, government bond, IG/HY credit, CX10) and, daily per bloc, the bond market depth index that 04 reads for its QE dysfunction rule.

## Imports
- T05-01: `MarketSpec`, `PriceState`, `MarketAux`, `IndexState`, `BlocMarketState`, `CorporateAction`, `FactorState`, `T`, `MarketPorts`, `resolveMarketSpec`.
- T05-03: `FactorState.refSigma` maintenance. T05-04: maker depth multiplier `D` of the tick (for bond depth). T05-07: `auctionBand('IPO')`, `checkMarketWide` consumes `<BLOC>50` levels.
- T05-09: order adjustment helpers (`applyModify` not used; direct field adjustment). T05-11: `onSuspend`.

## Implementation steps
1. Listing (05 §3.21): 06 or 09 (IPO) provides the instrument via `ports.instruments.listedSpecs` and a `LISTING` corporate action with an offer price: create MarketSpec (resolved), PriceState (`lnV = lnF = lnB = ln(offer)`, h 1, pools zero) and aux; the first matching tick is an OPEN auction with reference = offer price and band ±50% (`auctionBand('IPO')`); `aux.daysOfInputs = 0` so ADV uses genesis turnover (edge 18). Listing fees `MKT_LISTING_FEE` are posted by the lister's module (09), not here.
2. Splits and consolidations at P2 of the effective tick (edge 10): ratio r (new shares per old). Every resting order: `qty × r`, prices (limit, stop, trailing peak, ABS trail value) ÷ r rounded to the grid (buy limits down, sell limits up), quantities rounded down to whole lots with the fractional remainder cancelled (`CORPORATE_ACTION`; an order below one lot is cancelled entirely). Pool positions are in ADV units (unchanged); ADV units × r; price history (closes, highs, lows, last prices, prevClose, dayOpen/High/Low, VWAP numerators) ÷ r; maker `lnB`, `lnV`, `lnF`, `lnP` −= ln r. Holds are adjusted by 03's corporate-action handling (05 only adjusts its own rows).
3. Dividends on the ex-date open: `lnV`, `lnF` (06 also moves its anchor) and `lnB` drop by `ln(1 − d/P)` with P the previous close; resting buy limits are not adjusted (D092), GTC sell stops are not adjusted.
4. Delisting, suspension, bankruptcy (09): state SUSPENDED; all orders cancelled `ASSET_SUSPENDED`; algos cancelled and holds released (edge 11); positions remain (03 marks them).
5. Index changes: announced 5 trading days before the effective day (`IndexState.pendingChange`, PUBLIC event `mkt.index.level` with `pendingChange`); the MANDATE pool trades in the effective day's closing auction (T05-14 reads it); the change applies after that close.
6. Indices (05 §3.22) every tick after 6b from last prices: GX100 (world, free-float cap weighted, 100 constituents), one `<BLOC>50` per bloc (circuit breakers, T05-07), sector indices, a government bond index per bloc, IG and HY credit indices (yield spread; spreads from 06 via `priceParams`-adjacent marks: use `(yield − govt yield)` from `ports.instruments.anchor` model prices converted by duration: spread = −(lnP − lnF_gov)/duration), the crypto index `CX10`, and the bond market depth index per bloc. Weights capped at 10% per constituent with excess redistributed pro rata; quarterly review every 91 game days. Divisors keep levels continuous across changes and corporate actions. Names and constituents come from 22.
7. Bond depth index: each matching tick accumulate the maker depth multiplier `D` of every government bond line of the bloc; at P8 compute today's average ÷ the mean of the last 364 daily values (genesis: 1.0 until history exists) and emit `mkt.bondDepthIndex {bloc, value, gameDay}` (04 §3.8.5 QE "market dysfunction" reads it).
8. Edge 17 (price grid collapse at very low prices): when the last price is at `minPrice` and sells rest unfilled below it, emit `mkt.flag.belowMinPrice` once per day (09 penny-stock or delisting rules consume it).
9. `updateFactorRefSigma`: daily EWMA (λ 0.94) of each factor's reference index log return, annualized, stored in `FactorState.refSigma` (GX → GX100, EQ_<bloc> → <BLOC>50, SEC_* → sector index, RATES_<bloc> → bloc government bond index, FX_<ccy> → the FX leg, CREDIT → IG index, CRYPTO → CX10, commodity groups → their 22 reference instruments, AI_RACE → 04's aiRaceIndex series).

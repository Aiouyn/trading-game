## Goal
Compute, per beneficial-owner group per game day, the abstract market-conduct features F1–F15 of 05 §3.16 with their evidence references, seal them to 13 (never to the player), and maintain `surveillanceScore ∈ [0, 100]` with daily decay, the value 12 and 13 read. 05 only computes features and the score; 13 decides and prosecutes, 12 defines the market-conduct verbs. NPC named funds are scored the same way. Everything is game-mechanical: order, fill, timing and holding patterns only.

- T05-06: `AuctionOutcome.linkedMatches` (F3, F14 inputs). T05-10: `OwnerGroupStats` (messages, trades). T05-11: `groupHolding`, `shortInterest`. T05-15: claim rows (F15).

## Implementation steps
1. Collection: during P6/P7 record per tick the entries, cancels and fills of every owner group (players, AIs, operators, registered MMs and NPC named funds of 16; pools and the maker are excluded) with: grid distance to the N−1 best, the median displayed size at that level, size as a fraction of ADV, tick matched volume (background volume excluded from denominators, 05 §3.14.2), ΔlnP and σ_tick. Keep a rolling window of 60 ticks per asset in memory-free form: write compact rows to a sealed working table (`mkt.surv` rows with `id = ${group}:${day}:work`), deleted after the daily run.
2. Detectors (05 §3.16), each producing `EvidenceRef { kind, assetId, ticks:[from,to], orderIds, fillIds, newsIds?, points }`:
   - F1 spoofing: order ≥ 0.5% ADV and ≥ 5× the level median, within 3 grid ticks of the best, cancelled within 2 ticks without fills, while the same group fills on the other side within that window → 8 points per episode.
   - F2 layering: three or more such orders at different levels on one side in the same window → 10 (an episode counted as F2 is not also counted as F1).
   - F3 wash/circular: fills between linked owner groups (`linkedMatches`, `ports.social.linkedGroups`) that leave the combined positions of the linked set within 10% of their start-of-day positions → 12 per asset-day.
   - F4 marking the close: group share of closing-auction volume > 20%, close moved > 2σ_d in its favour vs the t=530 indicative, and reversed ≥ 50% by the next open (evaluated at the next day's open, attributed to the close's day) → 15, × 1.5 on quarter-end days, NAV strike days (`ports.funds.navStrikeDay`), derivative expiries, and when the group holds derivatives or collateral marked off that close.
   - F5 ramping: aggressive buying (selling) ≥ 30% of tick volume for ≥ 10 consecutive ticks moving price ≥ 3σ_tick per tick, then exiting into the TREND pool's response within 30 ticks → 12.
   - F6 promote and dump: holding + a paid promotion or Wire post about the asset (`ports.social.promotions`) + a sale within 2 game days → 20.
   - F7 benchmark distortion: trading the FX fix, futures settlement or an index close while holding positions that settle on it → 15.
   - F8 insider: trades in the 10 game days before a material non-public event when the group had access (`ports.social.knowledgeAccess`, board seats and employees via `ports.companies.employeesOf`, `ports.conflict.stolenDataAccess`): points `40 × size_f × timing_f × profit_f` with `size_f = min(1, notional/(0.1 × ADV notional))`, `timing_f = clamp(1 − daysBefore/10, 0, 1)`, `profit_f = min(1, |abnormal return captured|/(2σ_d))`; evaluated when the event publishes (lookback 10 days), booked on the publish day.
   - F9 front-running: trading ahead of orders the group could see privately: 12's C08 order-tap flags (`ports.conflict.orderTapFlags`), orders of clients it operates under a mandate (`ports.mandates.mandateClientsOf`), orders of funds it manages (`managedFundsOf`), group fill within 5 ticks before the victim order's fill on the same side → 25.
   - F10 stop pressure: concentrated aggression toward round-number levels (prices whose last two significant display digits are 00 or 50) followed by a reversal the group profits from, repeated ≥ 3 times in 5 days → 6.
   - F11 squeeze/corner: holding > 20% of float or > 30% of lendable supply (`ports.credit.lendableSupply`) while short interest > 30% and price ≥ 3σ_d above its 21-day mean → 10 per day.
   - F12 message abuse: order-to-trade ratio > 100 with measurable price effect (mean |ΔlnP| on the group's message ticks > 1σ_tick) → 5.
   - F13 off-market value transfer: P2P transfers (`ports.social.p2pTransfers`) or crosses between linked groups at prices > 5% from the market → 10.
   - F14 block-cross abuse: ≥ 2 block crosses between linked groups in 5 days → 8.
   - F15 disinformation overlap: a claim later resolved false (claim rows RELEASING_FALSE, `falseClaimFlags`) by the group or its linked accounts while holding a position that benefited → 25.
3. Score (05 §3.16): `score_d = min(100, PARAM.mkt_surv_decay × score_{d−1} + Σ points_d)`; store per group per day; `surveillanceScore(playerId, window)` = max over the player's owner groups over the last `window` days. 13 opens an ECA inquiry at ≥ 60 for 3 days and investigates at ≥ 80 (13's rule, not implemented here).
4. Emit `mkt.surveillance.daily` with the full `SurveillanceFeatures` as SEALED (court evidence, 13 discovery only). Never emit anything to the player. `mkt.surveillance_sample` stream is registered but not drawn (05's rules are deterministic).
5. Exploit counters wired: #2 (F3), #4 (F1, F2, message fee), #7 (F11), #8 (F4, F7), #11 (F10), #13 (F13, F14), #15 (F15), #16 (F8), #20 (F5).

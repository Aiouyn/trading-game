## Goal
Only the four XXX/FDL books exist (D077). This task implements everything that converts currency through them: the linked FX leg of an `autoFx` order (sub-phase 6a so the asset leg in 6b sees this tick's FX price), cross-currency orders executed as two all-or-none legs priced at the product of the two clearing prices (no triangular arbitrage), the P1 capital-control checks of 04 §3.13.7 and the `CAPCON_LEVY`, cross display quotes, and `mkt.order.placeMulti` packages against 06's NPC options maker in 6d.

## Implementation steps
1. FX books (05 §3.4.7): run in sub-phase 6a before every other book, in ascending fxAssetId, using the normal auction path (T05-06) with the FX leg's maker. `lnV` of an FX leg is 04's fair value (T05-03). CB edge orders and lean-against-wind orders are pool orders (T05-14), not special-cased here. A broken band (04 §3.13.5) simply removes the CB edge orders.
2. autoFx (05 §3.6.3): when the order's account cash is in another currency, P1 places a linked FX leg sized to the estimated notional × (1 + `PARAM.mkt_autofx_buffer`) (estimated at limit or band hi). In 6a the FX leg trades; if it fails (no fill, band clamp leaving it unfilled, or capital controls forbid it) the asset leg is cancelled in 6b with `FX_LEG_FAILED` (edge 12: capital controls imposed while an autoFx order works → the next FX leg fails and the asset leg cancels). Unused converted cash stays in the target currency.
3. Cross orders (EUN/PYN etc.): executed as two linked legs in 6a (sell EUN/FDL, buy PYN/FDL) under one intent, all-or-none: if either leg cannot fill fully at the tick's prices, neither fills (exclude both and re-run the FX books once without them; this counts against `mkt_max_auction_passes`). Price = product (ratio) of the two FDL clearing prices (exploit #21; 04 test 28). Edge 24: if one FX book is in VOL_INTERRUPTION the cross waits in 6a until its TIF ends, then expires.
4. Capital controls (04 §3.13.7, 05 §3.8 step 5): level 1 adds the 1% levy `CAPCON_LEVY` (SINK, `ctx.ledger.burn`) on any conversion of the controlled currency into another; levels 2 and 3 reject (`CAPITAL_CONTROLS`) orders selling the controlled currency for another currency, except reduce-only closes of existing FX derivative positions; trading X-denominated assets within X continues. Level 3 additionally keeps dividends, coupons and rents in X (not 05's flow; no code here).
5. Packages (05 §3.6.9): 2–4 legs on derivatives of one underlying, net limit; P1 validates every leg like an order (license, Held, holds for the worst case of the net limit). In 6d call `ports.instruments.packageQuote`; if a quote exists and its net price satisfies `netLimit` (buy: ≤, sell: ≥), all legs fill at the quoted leg prices (fills recorded on each instrument, settlement through T05-10 with derivative handoff to 07), else nothing fills (IOC: cancelled; DAY: retried each tick until close). Packages never enter the instrument's batch auction and have no impact on the underlying book. With no 06 provider the package is rejected `SESSION_CLOSED_FOR_TYPE` at P1.

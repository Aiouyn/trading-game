## Goal
The order lifecycle as pure state transitions over `Order` records: which types are allowed in which state, TIF expiry ticks, flag semantics, OCO and bracket linkage, stop/take-profit/trailing triggers evaluated in sub-phase 6.0 against tick N−1's LAST or MARK, market-order protection countdowns, iceberg display rules and priority reset on modification. Intents (T05-11) and the tick pipeline (T05-22) call these functions; nothing here touches the ledger.

## Implementation steps
1. Types (05 §3.6.1) and class support: MOO/LOO only with an opening (or REOPEN) auction; they expire if unfilled after that auction. MOC/LOC only with a closing, fix or settlement auction; entry cut-off `t = 535` for MOC and new LOC; after 535 only LOC orders that offset the published imbalance (opposite sign) are accepted, else `MOC_CUTOFF`.
2. TIF (05 §3.6.2, D080): DAY expires at the asset's daily close (`dailyCloseTickOf`: t=540 session, t=719 24-hour). DAY orders entered after the close (t ≥ 541 for session) are DAY orders for the next session's close. GTC expires `PARAM.mkt_gtc_max_days × 720` ticks after entry; one game day (720 ticks) before expiry emit PRIVATE `mkt.order.modified {orderId, expiryWarningTick}` as the owner warning, and at expiry PRIVATE `mkt.order.expired`. GTD ≤ 91 game days ahead (validated in T05-11). IOC fills what it can in its tick; the rest cancels (reason `EXPIRED`). FOK handled by T05-06.
3. Flags (05 §3.6.3): `postOnly` rejected in P1 (`POST_ONLY_WOULD_CROSS`) if marketable vs tick N−1's published best bid/ask; if it becomes marketable in the auction it still trades and is billed the maker rate (T05-13 reads `postOnly`). `reduceOnly`: accepted only with an existing position (`REDUCE_ONLY_NO_POSITION`), capped at the open position at execution. `autoFx` handled in T05-11. `auctionOnly`: participates only in OPEN/CLOSE/fix/settlement/REOPEN auctions.
4. OCO (05 §3.6.4): up to 3 orders per `ocoGroupId`; in 6.0 of tick N, every member with a fill in tick N−1 (even partial) cancels the other members' unfilled quantity (reason `OCO`). Two members filled in the same tick is possible; the excess is a normal position. Brackets: an entry with take-profit and stop-loss children; when the entry fills (any fill), the children become WORKING as an OCO pair sized to the entry's cumulative filled qty (resized on later entry fills).
5. Triggers (05 §3.6.5) in 6.0 against tick N−1's reference: LAST = `ps.lastPrice`; MARK = `ports.credit.mark` (07, 03 §3.9.1 thin-print guard), falling back to LAST when 07 is not loaded. Buy STOP triggers when ref ≥ stopPrice; sell STOP when ref ≤ stopPrice; TAKE_PROFIT the favourable side (sell TP when ref ≥ stopPrice; buy TP when ref ≤ stopPrice). TRAILING_STOP: sell trailing tracks the highest ref since entry (`peak`), stop = peak − value (ABS) or peak × (1 − value) (PCT); buy trailing mirrors with the lowest ref; the stop never moves against the holder. Triggered orders join this tick's auction with `priorityTick = N` (STOP → MARKET with protection; STOP_LIMIT/TP_LIMIT → LIMIT). Stops never trigger while the asset is HALTED or VOL_INTERRUPTION (or LIMIT_LOCKED); they are evaluated against the reopen price on the tick after the reopen. Stops are private, never in depth (T05-20), and the maker never sees them (exploit #11).
6. Edge 6: a stop triggered by a band-clamped print triggers on that LAST and joins the next tick as a market order with protection.
7. Market-order protection (05 §3.6.6): a market order not fully filled because the band clamped the price stays alive with `marketTicksLeft = PARAM.mkt_market_order_ticks` further ticks, keeping its entry `priorityTick`; when the countdown reaches 0 it is cancelled `PROTECTION_EXPIRED`. Never trades outside its tick's band. During halts the countdown pauses. A market order that is unfilled for another reason (no interest) follows the same countdown.
8. Icebergs and modification (05 §3.6.7): display ≥ max(1 lot, `PARAM.mkt_iceberg_min_display` × qty) else reject `BAD_LOT`. Lowering qty keeps priority; raising qty or changing price (limit or stop) resets `priorityTick` to the modification tick (exploit #22); modifying in the entry tick is allowed. Modifications and cancels take effect in P1 of the tick they are sealed in, before that tick's auction (edge 9: the modified order is what matches).
9. Expiry emits `mkt.order.expired` (PRIVATE, owner) and releases holds through the `onRelease` callback (T05-22 passes T05-13's `releaseOrderHold`; a callback avoids an import cycle).

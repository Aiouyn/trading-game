## Goal
Compose all 05 rules into the phase handlers of the module, in the exact order of 05 §3.1 and 00 §5.2, so each tick every asset steps its value layer, NPC flows are computed from N−1 state, every book clears once in its sub-phase, fills settle, state publishes, and daily boundary work runs at the right tick of day. Prove bit-for-bit replay and the 40 ms P6 budget.

## Implementation steps
1. Phase placement (05 §3.1), each handler iterating assets in ascending `assetId` (cmpStr):
   - **P1**: intents validated by the IntentHandlers of T05-11, T05-12, T05-13, T05-18 (registered in T05-23); nothing here.
   - **P2** (00's calendar phase; 05 §3.1 calls it "world", reading published inputs): `readLeversAtDayStart` at t=0 (T05-10); `ingestHookCalls` (T05-03); `applyCorporateActions` (T05-19); compute effective trading states (T05-07: `effectiveState`, news halts, MWCB using last tick's index levels) and emit `mkt.state.changed` on change; read 04 macro state and Fear Index of N−1, 06 anchors, 16 news with `publishTick = N` through the ports (cached in tick scratch).
   - **P3** (value): `drawFactorShocks` once; for each asset `stepValue` (T05-03) on active ticks (shocks also on weekends), then `followValue` (T05-04) and `ingestClaims`/`applyResolutions`/`stepBelief` (T05-15); opening print on OPEN/REOPEN ticks.
   - **P4** (npc): for each asset build the maker quote for tick N (`buildMakerQuote` with `mmCrowdingFn` from T05-18, depth event from hooks, auction flag), pool participants (`buildPoolParticipants`, from end-of-N−1 state) and external participants (`externalParticipants`: 04 CB/TSY/Concord orders, 07 liquidations, 08 ETF and fund flows, MANDATE, sell programs).
   - **P5**: AI agents (11) and operators submit intents that pass P1 validation in the same tick before P6 (02 handles the re-entry; 05 has no P5 handler).
   - **P6** in this fixed order: **6.0** OCO cancels (T05-09), bracket activation, triggers (`runTriggers`, against N−1 LAST/MARK, skipped while halted), trailing peaks, algo children (`runAlgos`), market-order carry-over (`protectionStep`); **6a** the four FX books (`runFxBooks`, so autoFx conversions see this tick's FX price); **6b** every other book independently per asset in ascending assetId (`clearAsset`: assemble `AuctionBook` from resting + new + triggered + generated orders, pools, externals and the maker; band from T05-07 (tick or auction band); `runAuction` (T05-06); clamp detection and streak; apply maker fill (`applyMakerFill`, `accumulateDayDepth`), pool fills, background volume (`drawBackground`, `backgroundVolume`), cause decomposition (`decomposeTick`, `accumulateHour`), surveillance recording, official-close accumulators (`accumulateLast10`, `accumulateMark540`), then `stepIndices`); **6c** block crosses at the closing tick only (`runBlockCrosses`); **6d** RFQ fills (06, not 05) and packages (`runPackages`). Halted or non-matching assets skip the auction but still publish the indicative (edge 1) and keep stepping value. The FINANCE partition's primary auctions (04) share no state with 6a–6d.
   - **P7** (settle): `settleFills` (T05-10), hold releases, message fees, `emitOwnershipCrossings` (T05-11), `mkt.fill` events, algo progress (`onAlgoChildFill`), MM presence (`recordPresence`).
   - **P8** (accrue) at t = 719: `expireOrders('P8')` for GTC/GTD (DAY orders expire at the asset's daily close tick through `expireOrders('DAY_CLOSE')` in P8 of that tick), `rollGroupDay` (fee tiers), `recomputeAdvDaily` (and intraday hourly trigger), `onDailyCloseGarch`, maker `foldDailyTally` and `layoff` at each asset's daily close tick (540 session, 719 24-hour), `runDailySurveillance`, `postDailyLevy`, `dailyClosePublish` (official close, stats, short interest), `publishBondDepth`, `updateFactorRefSigma`, `dailyObligationCheck`, `decayPoolPositions` (every tick, cheap), `pruneCauses`.
   - **P13**: 03 commits; no 05 handler.
   - **P14** (publish): `publishTick` per asset (book, tape, auction result, indicative, ticker), `closeHour` at each game-hour end, `closeDay` at the daily close, `publishDeferredBlocks` at the next open, `markoutUpdate`, `mkt.index.level`.
2. Determinism (05 §3.1, §3.23): inside P6 an asset's auction is a pure function of its book after P1, the maker ladder, pool orders and its own streams. Never iterate `Map`/`Set` unordered; tick scratch is a `SortedMap<assetId, AssetTickScratch>` discarded after P14 (not persisted). Every RNG draw keyed by its registered stream; fixed draw counts per tick per asset.
3. Performance (05 §3.23): ≤ 0.5 ms per asset per tick at p99 with 200 resting orders, ≤ 40 ms for 6a + 6b over 400 assets. The ladder is analytic (no materialized ladder orders); candidates bounded by the band; per-asset order lists via the `byAsset` index; avoid re-sorting resting orders each tick by keeping them in priority order in the scratch.
4. Edge 26 (replay with partitions): a replay of the MARKET partition from a snapshot reproduces every p*, fill and cause vector bit for bit: no state outside the store; scratch rebuilt deterministically each tick.

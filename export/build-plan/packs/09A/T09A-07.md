## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Upgrades (09 §3.5.1): level `n` = the new level 1..25 (`PARAM.biz_level_cap`); cost `c0_indexed × k × g^n` with `dmath.pow`; build time `ceil(n/5)` game days = `ceil(n/5) × 720` ticks; one build per axis at a time; effects apply when the build completes (`completeBuilds` in P9); during the build the business keeps operating; builds continue while IDLE (edge 11) and transfer with the business on sale (edge 5: the `biz.build` row keeps `bizId`, cost already paid stays sunk). Marketing is a flow (`marketingPerDay`), expansion is T09A-11 (`biz_expansion_cost` × c0). Automation levels ≥ 10 need an 11 operations model of MCS ≥ 50 (`ports.ai.modelMcs(modelId) ≥ 50` for the assigned AI operator; validation in T09A-12 returns `BIZ_NEEDS_MODEL`). Costs post as `BIZ_UPGRADE_CAPEX` (SINK) or `BIZ_UPGRADE_CAPEX_B2B` when a player construction company is contracted (T09A-15); `upgradesSpent += cost`; 03 books 30% impairment per its own rule (T03-02 note), not 09.
2. Condition (09 §3.5.3) at each day close: `required = biz_upkeep_ratio × (c0 + upgradesSpent) × priceLevel × L4`; `condition' = clamp(condition − biz_decay_per_day × (1 − min(1, budget/required)), 0, 100)`. The budget actually paid is `min(upkeepBudgetPerDay, ...)`: the full `upkeepBudgetPerDay` is paid (`BIZ_UPKEEP`); a business with `upkeepAuto` (new (+) field on Business, default true at purchase, false after `biz.setUpkeep`) or a manager pays exactly `required`. IDLE: condition does not decay (09 §3.4.4) and upkeep is still paid as set. `biz.repair` cost `(100 − condition) × required × 1.5`, immediate (`BIZ_REPAIR_NPC`), condition 100.
3. Headcount (09 §3.6.1): `required[role] = ceil(staffPerCapacity[role] × (1 + 0.12 × capacityLevel) × (role === 'OPERATIONS' ? 0.97^automation : 1))` (automation reduces operations headcount by 3% per level, applied multiplicatively like the other per-level effects). Wage per head per game day = `baseWage[role](22) × cityWageIndex(10/16) × wageLevel(04) × (0.8 + 0.1 × skillAvg) × w × 0.94^automation`; IDLE: furloughed, wages 0; STRIKE: wages not paid.
4. Morale and loyalty daily (formulas of 09 §3.6.1 verbatim); `wageLagOver3 = wageLag91(history) > 0.03` where history is the (+) `Business.wageHistory` ring of the last 91 daily `{cpi, wagePerHead}` pairs appended at each day close; `utilHighStreak` = utilization > 0.95 on each of the last 3 days (`utilizationLog`); `profitGrowthPos = books.growth28 > 0`. `avgTenureDays` updated daily (+1 per day, hires reset weighted).
5. Hiring (09 §3.6.2): `biz.hire` creates vacancies; each day close vacancies fill at `fillRatePerDay` heads/day (fractional accumulator in `StaffBlock`; whole heads hired when it passes 1); each hire draws skill with `drawHireSkill` from `biz.staff:<bizId>` (city mean and sd from 22); `skillAvg` becomes the headcount-weighted mean. `biz.fire` (T09A-12) is immediate with `severance` (7 days of that head's wages, `BIZ_WAGES`). Hiring at reference wages is instant at setup (T09A-11).
6. Managers (09 §3.6.3): candidates come from `ports.world.managerPool(cityId, day)`; salary per game day `managerSalary` (`BIZ_MANAGER_FEE_NPC`), posted at day close. A managed business (manager on it or on its company) gets: +8 h offline cap (T09A-09), daily price and marketing decisions at the assistant optimum with error `managerDecision` (price optimum from `pricingAssistant`; marketing optimum from the same golden-section search over `[0, 4 × M_ref]` holding price), one `uOpen()` each from `biz.staff:<bizId>` in the order price then marketing; auto-reorder (`reorderPolicy` default), auto-hire to required headcount, upkeep at required. AI operators (11) get the same +8 h but make their decisions through intents themselves (I05: no profit multiplier anywhere).
7. Key staff (B3+): hired from `ports.world.keyStaffPool`; each adds +0.05 to staffFactor (max +0.15, used by T09A-04) and raises research skill (09B reads `KEY_STAFF_TABLE` by role RESEARCH); `poachAccepted` implements the lawful counter-offer rule (`biz.poachOffer` in T09A-12: `offerWage = 2× current` by default or the given multiplier); 12's E3 poaching uses loyalty from `StaffBlock.loyalty`.
8. Strikes (09 §3.6.4): when the strike event fires (T09A-10) the business is STRIKE for its drawn duration; `strikeSettles(oldW, newW, lag)` is the `biz.settleStrike` validation (raise ≥ the inflation lag); settlement ends the strike at the next game hour. Fixed costs continue during a strike.
9. `dayCloseStaffAndCondition` returns the money amounts to post (the day-close task posts them) and the updated business; it never posts itself.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[09 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

Businesses are the steady engine of wealth (the tycoon half; markets are the volatile half, 01 §4). Companies are how a player turns businesses into an institution: found a company, build products, hire, borrow, compete for customers, publish earnings, list on the exchange, acquire rivals, survive distress.

What the module guarantees:
1. **Real shared competition.** Every business sells into a demand pool shared with every other player and NPC competitor in its market (city or district × product category × segment). A logit share model decides who wins customers (01 §4.1). Similar firms coexist; nothing consumes a unique worldwide slot except named unique infrastructure (C03).
2. **Distinct economics.** Each business belongs to one of 16 economic archetypes with its own formula (footfall retail, local service, B2B contract, commodity producer, processor, platform, subscription, hit-driven, tourism, transport capacity, regulated utility, compute provider, trial binary, financial institution, trophy, flip trading). A niche is a combination of archetype, category, segment, location, operating model and promise (C02), not just a new name (C04, R01).
3. **Customers with requirements.** NPC customers post tenders with price caps, reliability, certification, response time and confidentiality requirements; switching takes effort; concentration has risks (C05–C08).
4. **Company building in depth** (Mathis directive 2026-10-09, STONKS-9800 spirit): R&D, product lines, marketing, hiring, production and capacity, pricing, expansion, financing; real operating economics; filings and earnings that feed the market; IPO onto the same exchange.
5. **Corporate finance and control**: capital structure, covenants, dividends, buybacks, issuance, IPOs, takeovers and defences, activism, LBOs, restructuring with absolute priority. A bankrupt company is a market, not just a delisting (V3 3.7).
6. **Honest money.** Revenue from NPC customers is a faucet, costs paid to NPCs are sinks, everything between players is a transfer; diminishing returns (logit share, exponential upgrade costs, finite demand) and the offline cap keep business income from running away (01 §8.3).

#### §3.5.3 Condition

`condition' = clamp(condition − PARAM.biz_decay_per_day (1.0) × (1 − min(1, upkeepBudgetPerDay / upkeepRequired)), 0, 100)` at each day close, where `upkeepRequired = PARAM.biz_upkeep_ratio (0.0015) × (c0 + upgradesSpent)` per game day (01 §4.2: 1%/day decay without upkeep). Paying the required upkeep holds condition; paying half halves the decay. Upkeep is part of the fixed costs φ at reference. Condition feeds quality (§3.3.1), fire risk, inspections, catastrophe vulnerability (08) and EV (via profit). `biz.repair` restores condition at `(100 − condition) × upkeepRequired × 1.5` immediately (SINK).

#### §3.6.1 Headcount and wages

- Required headcount per role = `staffPerCapacity[role] × (1 + 0.12 × capacityLevel)` rounded up (22 per type; e.g. café: 4 operations, 1 management at level 0). Automation reduces operations headcount required by 3% per level (in addition to −6% wages).

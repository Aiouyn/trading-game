## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. PLATFORM_COMMISSION: `revenue_h = takeRate × observedFlow_h × platformShare`; `platformShare` = the business's smoothed share in its own platform market (platforms compete by logit in their category and scope, T09A-04); `observedFlow_h` = yesterday's value / 24 of: delivery app → Σ `revenueToday` (rolled to yesterday) of café and restaurant markets in the city (players and NPC field); marketplace → the bloc's retail markets; brokerage → `ports.market.npcRetailFlowNotional(bloc, day−1)` (never player trades); port terminal → port throughput value; exchange concession → `ports.market.blocTradedValue(bloc, day−1)`. Fee caps (09 §3.8.3 rule 5): exchange fee rate ≤ `PARAM.exchange_fee_cap_bps` (0.5 bp = 0.00005) of traded notional and total exchange concession revenue per game day ≤ `PARAM.exchange_rev_cap` × the bloc's daily traded value (tracked in `ArchState.earnedToday`, reset at t = 0); port fees ≤ 0.3% of throughput value. Revenue source FLOW_TAKE (`BIZ_SALES_FRM`).
2. SUBSCRIPTION per game hour: `N' = N × (1 − churn_h) + acquisitions_h`, `acquisitions_h = D_hour × s_j` (= `allocation.demandShare`), `churn_h = churnBase × (refPrice/price)^(−c_s/2) × (2 − qEff)`; `revenue_h = N × price / 24` (price per subscriber per game day); ad-funded types (social network): `revenue_h = N × ARPU × adIndex / 24` (`ports.world.adIndex`). Content or R&D spend lowers churn through `qEff` (product quality). `N` lives in `ArchState.subscribers`.
3. HIT_DRIVEN: at the business's first operation of each game week (Monday, first stagger hour) release `k = titlesPerWeek(capacityLevel)` titles; each title's lifetime revenue `= baseTitle × Pareto(α = 1.1, xm) × qEff` with `xm = 0.2 × (1 + b_s × ln(1 + mkt_j/M_ref))` ("marketing raises xm"), `baseTitle = 7 × rStar / 2.2` (2.2 = Pareto mean α·xm/(α−1) at xm 0.2, so expected revenue equals the calibrated target). Draws: one `uOpen()` per title from stream `biz.demand:<cityId>:<sector>` in title order. Earnings: exponential decay normalized to finish within 14 game days (336 hours, τ = 112 hours): hour i earns `lifetime × titleEarningsShare(i, i+1)`. Revenue source HOUSEHOLD (`HIT_REVENUE_NPC`). Data by-product as usual.
4. REGULATED_UTILITY: `revenue_h = allowedReturn × rateBase / (364 × 24) + passThrough_h` where `rateBase` = book value of assets (`c0 + upgradesSpent` indexed), `passThrough_h` = that hour's variable costs (energy, inputs); `allowedReturn = y10(bloc) + PARAM.utility_spread`, reset at each YEAR_CLOSE (13 regulator: `ports.law.regulatorReturnReset(bizId)` overrides when non-null). Power utility sells energy to all data centers in the city (11 pays; volume small risk).
5. COMPUTE_PROVIDER: `GPUh = min(capacity GPUh, ports.ai.computeDemandShare(bizId))`, revenue `GPUh × ports.ai.computePrice(bloc)`; energy MWh per GPUh at the bloc power price; internal allocation to an own lab at cost is T09A-13.
6. TRIAL_BINARY (twist `TRIALS`): one trial at a time; a new trial starts at the first operation of the game day after the previous ends; duration `28 + rng.int(64)` game days (one draw), cost per game day `trialCostPerDay` (posted at day close as `RD_SPEND_NPC`); at the end `P(pass) = PARAM.biotech_pass × qualityMult` (qualityMult = qEff), one `uOpen()` draw; pass pays `(10 + 20 × u2) × trialCostTotal` (`TRIAL_LICENSE_NPC` FAUCET) and sets `ArchState.lastTrialPassTick` (09B relaxes IPO history, 09 §3.10.1). Draw order per trial: duration, pass, multiple. Stream `biz.ops:<bizId>`. No revenue between trials.
7. TROPHY: ticket and sponsorship revenue from a small TOURISM-like market pool (allocation), heavy upkeep `PARAM.trophy_upkeep × value` per game day (value = indexed c0) posted at day close as `TROPHY_UPKEEP`; Prestige is 14's (emit nothing extra; 14 reads ownership).
8. FLIP_TRADING (twist `DROPS`): each game day `ports.world.hypeDrops(cityId, day)` offers 0 to 3 drops; `biz.flip.buy` (owner, OPEN, qty ≤ remaining daily capacity, cash) buys at `dropPrice` (`BIZ_COGS_FRM`); items keep `hype` that steps each game hour by `hypeStep` with 22's `decay`, `sigma` and one `normal()` draw per item per hour from `biz.demand:<cityId>:<sector>`; `biz.flip.sell` sells at `dropPrice × hype` to NPC buyers (`BIZ_SALES_HH` FAUCET) in P9; selling to players goes through 15 contracts. Rejects: `BIZ_NOT_OWNER`, `BIZ_NOT_FOUND`, `BIZ_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `BIZ_BAD_PAYLOAD` (qty > drop or capacity).
9. FINANCIAL_INSTITUTION: no hourly loop (09 §3.4.2); the institution runs in 07 or 08 (T09A-11 founds it).
10. Twist rules (09 §3.4.6), where each is implemented: `MOVE_DAILY` → T09A-12 `biz.move`; `CLUSTER` → T09A-13; `CHAIN_NATIVE` → T09A-11 (restaurant chain opens 3 sites); `VERTICAL_INPUT` → T09A-13 allocation; `CRITIC` → T09A-10 events; `PLAYER_SERVICE`, `TAX_REDUCER` → T09A-15; `NEWS_PUBLISHER` → the business may publish through 16's publishing intent (16 validates facts; false statements are 13 offences): 09 only exposes `canPublish(store, bizId)` = OPEN news outlet (export from twists.ts); `HACK_RATE` → `twistDemandMult` × `ports.conflict.worldHackRate`; `LITIGATION` → × 13 court caseload index (`ports.law.caseloadIndex`); `TOURIST_FLOW` → T09A-05 FX booth; `LIMITED_EDITIONS` → each finished unit of a watch manufacture is reported as `ctx.send(ownerId, 'life.collectible.mint', { bizId, serial, typeId }, 'NEXT')` for 14 to create a numbered collectible; `LONG_ORDERS` → yacht builder sales become orders with delivery `91 + rng.int(92)` game days, 30% deposit paid at order (`BIZ_SALES_HH` on the deposit), balance at delivery, cancellation per game day with `longOrderCancelProb(outputGap)` (stream `biz.supply:<bizId>`; the deposit is kept); `DROPS` → step 8; `REGULATORY_EVENTS` → 13 port reset/closures; `EMBARGO` → 16 `demandShock` returns 0 for embargoed bloc sales; `RESERVES`, `WEATHER` → T09A-05; `TRIALS` → step 6. # SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[09 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

Businesses are the steady engine of wealth (the tycoon half; markets are the volatile half, 01 §4). Companies are how a player turns businesses into an institution: found a company, build products, hire, borrow, compete for customers, publish earnings, list on the exchange, acquire rivals, survive distress.

What the module guarantees:
1. **Real shared competition.** Every business sells into a demand pool shared with every other player and NPC competitor in its market (city or district × product category × segment). A logit share model decides who wins customers (01 §4.1). Similar firms coexist; nothing consumes a unique worldwide slot except named unique infrastructure (C03).
2. **Distinct economics.** Each business belongs to one of 16 economic archetypes with its own formula (footfall retail, local service, B2B contract, commodity producer, processor, platform, subscription, hit-driven, tourism, transport capacity, regulated utility, compute provider, trial binary, financial institution, trophy, flip trading). A niche is a combination of archetype, category, segment, location, operating model and promise (C02), not just a new name (C04, R01).
3. **Customers with requirements.** NPC customers post tenders with price caps, reliability, certification, response time and confidentiality requirements; switching takes effort; concentration has risks (C05–C08).
4. **Company building in depth** (Mathis directive 2026-10-09, STONKS-9800 spirit): R&D, product lines, marketing, hiring, production and capacity, pricing, expansion, financing; real operating economics; filings and earnings that feed the market; IPO onto the same exchange.
5. **Corporate finance and control**: capital structure, covenants, dividends, buybacks, issuance, IPOs, takeovers and defences, activism, LBOs, restructuring with absolute priority. A bankrupt company is a market, not just a delisting (V3 3.7).
6. **Honest money.** Revenue from NPC customers is a faucet, costs paid to NPCs are sinks, everything between players is a transfer; diminishing returns (logit share, exponential upgrade costs, finite demand) and the offline cap keep business income from running away (01 §8.3).

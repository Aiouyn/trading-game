## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Seed (22 names): per bloc the 2 largest NPC banks, 2 NPC dealer firms, the Vault Exchange crypto member (CRY only), the Isla Serena private bank; each gets one MEMBER_HOUSE and one MEMBER_CLIENT_OMNIBUS account per service it clears.
2. `bank.ccp.join` (P1 checks): charter UNIVERSAL (any service) or COMMERCIAL (LD, REPO only), else `CCP_NOT_MEMBER`; `capital ≥ PARAM.cm_min_capital` else `CCP_MIN_CAPITAL`; DF collateral ≥ $10M per service, CCP-eligible after haircut, else `CCP_DF_SHORT`. Contribution posts `CCP_DEFAULT_FUND` member → service account and is a pledge. Joined members must offer client clearing and bid in auctions (T07B-10 reads `services`).
3. `bank.ccp.leave`: rejects `CCP_HAS_POSITIONS`; records notice; WITHDRAWN 28 game days later, DF returned with `CCP_DEFAULT_FUND`.
4. Client routing: default member = NPC broker subsidiary of the home bank; LIC4 may choose (`CCP_NOT_LIC4` otherwise); default backup = largest NPC member of the bloc; ISA creates a MEMBER_CLIENT_ISA account per client.
5. Novation (§3.17.4), P7 **first** in the 07B order (before `markAccounts`): for each fill in a cleared product (by fill id): buyer account += qty, seller account −= qty, and the service books the mirror legs in `ccpLeg` (CCP short to buyer, CCP long to seller). Positions net per account (one row per `(account, asset)`). OTC/CDS: new 06B rows are novated the same way at the P7 after booking.
6. Fees (§3.17.2, `ccp_<service>_fee`): LD $0.20 per contract per side, OTC 0.2 bp, CDS 0.5 bp of notional, REPO 0.05 bp per day, CRY 2 bp; TRANSFER `CCP_FEE` payer → `ccp_TCH`; 10% of CRY fees go on to the product insurance fund (T07B-12).
7. `INV_CCP_001`: for each service and asset, Σ ccpLeg qty = −Σ member-account qty and the CCP's own net (Σ legs + Σ accounts) is 0.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[07 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module is the **plumbing of money and leverage**: where every unit of cash actually sits, how banks create credit and destroy it, how they fund themselves, how confidence in them rises and collapses, how they fail and are resolved, how leverage is extended to traders (broker margin, prime brokerage, repo, securities lending), and how the Clearing House stands between counterparties, collects margin and absorbs defaults through a waterfall.

It gives the game its systemic layer (V3 §3.1, §3.3, §3.4):
- **Credit cycles are caused**: lending standards loosen in booms and tighten in busts as bank capital and losses move (§3.5).
- **Bank runs are feedback loops** fed by losses, rumors and other runs, stoppable by central-bank liquidity, deposit-insurance action, recapitalization or a rescue merger, and they leave a permanent record (§3.9).
- **Procyclical margin** turns a volatility spike into forced deleveraging, which is the cleanest cascade in the game (§3.15, §3.16).
- **The default waterfall** gives large players mutualized, "too big to fail" risk (§3.17).
- **Players can run banks** (§3.11): earn net interest margin, carry duration, credit and run risk, and fail for real.

Design laws:
1. Money is either **settlement cash** (a direct claim on a central bank, 03 account 1001: the default, risk-free money of every non-bank, earning nothing unless 04 pays on it), a **bank deposit** (03 account 1010, a claim on a bank: earns interest, carries the bank's risk), or a bank's **reserves** (1002). The payment rail between them is 03's (03 §3.3.4); this module owns everything about banks and deposits. In this module `fear = F / 100`, F = 04's Fear Index (04 §3.14.1).
2. A loan creates a deposit (`CREDIT_CREATE`); a repayment destroys one (`CREDIT_DESTROY`). Base money (M0) changes only through 03 faucets and sinks and 04 central-bank operations.
3. Liquidations and close-outs use fair-value price bands, never a crashed print (01 §9).
4. Nothing reveals an individual player's positions or strategy (L11). Systemic dashboards publish aggregates only.

**Status of numbers:** every `PARAM.*` value in this module, and every choice tagged `DEFAULT`, is `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`. Locked decisions L1–L15 (00) and the 00 time model are not defaults. The full list is in `_notes/07-notes.md`.

#### §3.17.4 Novation
In P7 every matched trade in a cleared product is novated: the CCP becomes buyer to every seller and seller to every buyer, member by member. Positions net within each account. From then on, each party's counterparty risk is to TCH only.

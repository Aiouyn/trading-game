## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

**[14 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

1. **Make money feel like something.** Rank, home, shell, possessions and titles turn net worth into a place and an identity. A player should feel the jump from $50k to $5M without reading a number (old 04 §0.1).
2. **Drain money in a way players want.** Lifestyle is the economy's most voluntary sink. Mass luxury loses value on purchase and fades in Prestige, so it must be refreshed; collectibles hold value but carry upkeep (old 04 §5.1).
3. **Give small, real, capped advantages** for spending (access, convenience, talent, collateral, reach, physical security) so spending is a decision, never a cash faucet (old 04 §0.4).
4. **Keep three separate social axes**: Prestige (status from what you own and display), Reputation (trust from clean conduct), Notoriety (fame from crime). A player can be high on all three (old 04 §7.1, bible decision 5).
5. **Remember.** The world never resets (L1), so Legacy, Houses and succession give a permanent public history: founding plaques, generations, heirlooms, chapter portraits (V3 Part 9).
6. **Respect the player.** No demotion, no punishing streaks, no real-money path to any of this (L7), quiet money stays a valid strategy (old 04 §7.8).

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Rank threshold crossed and lost within one game day.** Rank is evaluated at day close only, so intraday spikes never rank up; once reached at a close, it is kept.
2. **Two ranks crossed at one close** (big IPO): both ranks are awarded at the same tick; one combined ceremony shows the highest, the unlock cards list everything.
3. **R6 streak broken by a board recalculation.** The streak counts only closes where the published board row was top 50; corrections by 15 never retroactively revoke an awarded R6.
4. **MSI changes after an item purchase.** PP uses `pricePaidReal` fixed at purchase, so inflation never changes PP.
5. **Home sold with items displayed.** Items move to paid storage at that tick, display turns off, PP drops at next close. Staff lines stop at the sale tick.
6. **Last home seized by foreclosure (10/07).** Same as 5; the character becomes a rented-room resident.
7. **Item pledged and a Fresh Start occurs.** 07 liquidation sells it and pays the loan first.
8. **Drop with zero player requests.** NPC rule §6.3 applies; serials continue across drops ("Edition 12 of 99" in drop 2 is allowed: S is the per-drop size, the serial format is "drop D, N of S").
9. **Gigayacht build cap reached.** New orders are refused until the next chapter; the Boutique shows "next build slot opens at chapter N".
10. **Player-made edition maker company sold mid-edition.** Unsold units transfer with the company; minted serials keep the original maker name.
11. **Succession during a market crash.** Estate value is taken at the completion tick; the player may abort before completion.
12. **Succession completes while a new case is filed during probate.** Completion is blocked (the requirement is rechecked at completion); the succession stays in PROBATE until the case ends or the player aborts, max 1 chapter, then auto-aborts.
13. **Club eligibility lost because Prestige fell from novelty fade.** Grace applies (one game quarter).
14. **Hosting venue sold before the event.** Event is cancelled with a full refund (`LIFE_HOSTING_REFUND`) since the host did not choose to cancel.
15. **Attendee is Held during the event.** May stay in the chat read-only; no Rep bump.
16. **Restricted cash exactly covers a purchase that is disallowed** (e.g. margin): the intent fails with a reason naming the restriction and the release tick.
17. **Upkeep due while Held.** Upkeep posts normally (Held does not block automatic postings, 13).
18. **Deleted account owned a Hall of Patrons slot.** Slot becomes neutral civic until the next chapter.
19. **Country board too small for a Gilt edition.** No country edition; the player appears in the regional roll-up edition (15 §3.2).
20. **Same character qualifies as cover star for global and country editions.** Allowed; the "twice per chapter" rule applies per edition scope.
21. **A UNIQUE item is the subject of a court divestiture (13).** It is consigned by 13; heirloom flag is removed.
22. **Exam answers submitted for a license the character cannot afford.** Passing is recorded; buying later needs no retake for `PARAM.exam_valid_chapters = 2` chapters.

### §3.10 Net worth (NW) and ranking net worth (NWrank)

#### §3.10.1 Definitions

- **NW (book, marked)** of an entity in currency R: `Σ marked assets − Σ liabilities` of its own books (solo), translated to R.
- **NW (consolidated)** of a group parent: §3.11.4 look-through.
- **NWrank** of a character or alliance, in FDL, used by ranks (14), boards (15), the Stabilizer (§3.7) and NWI (§3.8): consolidated look-through NW with the board adjustments of §3.10.3.

#### §3.10.2 What counts

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Restricted cash (§3.5.6) | counts at face |
| Restricted compute (1221) | 0 |
| Escrow deposited | counts for the depositor until release |
| Assets under court freeze | count (ownership unchanged) |
| Seized assets (court escrow) | do not count for the former owner; count for the victim only after the award |
| Final judgments, fines, restitution owed | liabilities from the verdict tick |
| Pending lawsuits, investigations | not counted (no contingent liabilities on boards) |
| Persistent claims after discharge (2990) | liabilities |
| Shares in own listed company | see §3.10.3 control cap |
| Challenge-event portfolios (15) | never (separate book) |

#### §3.10.3 Board adjustments

1. **Concentration (impact) haircut** for any exchange-traded holding with `qty / ADV20 > 0.05`:
   `haircut = min(0.5, PARAM.nw_impact_y (DEFAULT 0.5) × σ_daily × sqrt(qty / ADV20))`, consistent with 05's square-root impact law. Value = mark × qty × (1 − haircut). A whale's 40% stake in a thin name cannot be counted at the screen price.
2. **Control cap**: a listed company controlled by the character's group is counted at `min(haircut market value of the stake, effectiveOwnership × NAV_lookthrough × PARAM.nw_control_listed_cap (DEFAULT 2.0))`. Pumping one's own listed company does not pump one's rank beyond twice its underlying value.
3. **Stale illiquid haircut**: STALE marks on exchange-traded assets take an extra 10% haircut until a valid mark returns.

#### §3.10.4 Cadence

NWrank is computed for every character and alliance in P13 of ticks with `t mod 30 == 29` (once per game hour, aligned with board refreshes, 15) and at day close (for the Stabilizer and MSI). A character's own screens show a flash NW recomputed every tick from the latest marks (17).

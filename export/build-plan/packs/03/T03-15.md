## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task implements the clock-driven side of the books.
- **Accruals (03 §3.3.7).** Daily Act/364 interest accrual and per-day accrual of rent, wages, upkeep and subscriptions, for every calendar day. Negative rates swap sides (03 §8 edge 18).
- **Period-close registry (03 §3.15).** Later tasks plug their period-close steps into this registry. It runs the ten steps in the exact 03 §3.15 order: steps 1–5 and the cash-moving part of step 7 in the P2 boundary hook; steps 6, 8 and 9 in 03's P13 handler just before `commitTick` (stage `P13_PRE`, so they see every entry queued in the closing tick); and step 10 right after `commitTick` (stage `P13_POST`). No postings can cross partitions after barrier B3 (T02-14 R3/B3), so `P13_PRE` steps may queue only single-entity or same-partition entries; `assertLocal` enforces this.
- **`LED_PERIOD_CLOSE` (year end).** Every 4xxx/5xxx/6xxx balance moves into 3200, or into 3900 for characters and alliances. The system entity's 5900/4900 move into 3990.
- **Standing-facility conversion (03 §3.3.4, last paragraph; 03#10).** Any bank reserve account (1002) still negative at day close becomes an overnight `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` loan. Central banks live on the FINANCE partition (T02-14 home rule), so the conversion runs in three places:
  - in P13 for banks homed on the same partition as their central bank;
  - in 03's P12 handler at t = 719 for banks on PLAYER partitions, whose legs go out at B3;
  - in the next tick's P2 (DAY_OPEN) as a catch-up for any reserve that became negative after the P12 pass.

## Implementation steps
0. In T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`, add `'FACILITY_RATE_E6'`. Its subject is a ccy and its value is a number. 04 writes it: the overnight standing-facility rate, which 04 also uses to charge `CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST`.
1. **Accruals.**
   - `registerAccrual` validates that both entities exist, that `a !== b`, that INTEREST has `principalKey` and `rate`, and that the other kinds have `perDay > 0n`. It rejects `LED_PERSISTENT_NO_INTEREST` when either value leg is a 2990 key: persistent claims carry no interest (03 §3.14.5 rule 4; T03-26). It fills `valueLegA/B` from `DEFAULT_LEGS` and sets `lastAccruedDay = (startDay ?? today) − 1`.
   - `dailyAccrualAmount` for INTEREST:
     - `p = committedBalance(read, principalKey)`, read as a magnitude: for a credit-normal principal (a deposit 2100 on the payer's side), use `−balance`;
     - `rateE6` is `fixedE6`, or the fact value plus `spreadE6`;
     - the amount is `accrue(p, BigInt(rateE6), days)`. The rate may be negative, giving a negative amount.
   - For the other kinds the amount is `perDay × BigInt(days)`.
   - `runAccruals` (step 1, DAY_CLOSE), for each ACTIVE row in id order:
     - `days = min(today, endDay ?? today) − lastAccruedDay`. Days lost to a halt are caught up in one posting: still Act/364, every calendar day, weekends included;
     - if `days > 0`, post `ctx.ledger.accruePair(a, b, amt, ccy, 'LED_ACCRUAL', { valueLegA, valueLegB, sub })`. A negative `amt` swaps the sides (T03-07; 03 §8 edge 18). A zero amount posts nothing;
     - set `lastAccruedDay = today`, and set ENDED once `endDay` is reached.
   - An entity that is DISSOLVED ends its accruals.
   - `settleAccrual`: `amt = min(amt ?? accruedBalance, accruedBalance)`. Post `ctx.ledger.pay(b, a, amt, ccy, code, { valueLegA: row.valueLegB, valueLegB: row.valueLegA, sub })`, which is Dr b:payable / Cr a:receivable plus the cash rail. `code` must be the owner's settlement code (for example `BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`, `CMP_INTERCOMPANY_INTEREST` or `SOC_LOAN_P2P_INTEREST`); the catalog decides whether the caller may request it.
   - Bonds traded between coupon dates carry accrued interest in the trade itself (`BOND_ACCRUED_INTEREST`, owned by 06). No accrual row is needed for that.
   - Hourly business operations settle cash immediately with hh/frm (09). Leases settle monthly (10) and bonds at coupon or maturity (06), each by its own module's schedule.
2. **Step registry.**
   - `registerCloseStep` stores the steps in a module-level sorted list; the list is frozen after `installLedger` (T03-28).
   - `runCloseStage(ctx, stage)` runs, for each step whose `stage` matches and whose `when` is in `ctx.cal.boundaries`, in order of `(no, order, name)`:
     - `id = `${when}:${periodIndexOf(ctx, when)}:${no}:${name}``; if a `led.close` row with that id exists, skip it;
     - otherwise call `run(ctx)` and write the row.
   - This makes period close exactly-once even when a halt delays a boundary: a year that ends inside a halt closes at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4; 03 §8 edge 11), and a replay after rollback re-creates the same rows.
   - `periodIndexOf` returns the index of the period that is closing (DAY_CLOSE → `gameDay`, YEAR_CLOSE → `year`, and so on).
3. **Steps this task registers.** These are the 03 §3.15 numbers. The `order` field breaks ties inside a step; later tasks register the rest.

4. **Projection.** `projectedBalance(ctx, key)` adds three things:
   - `committedBalance`;
   - for each queued entry of this tick without `invalid`, its postings on `key` (debit +, credit −);
   - for each leg in the partition's `acceptLegs` buffer of this tick on `key`, its signed amount.
   P13_PRE steps (tax provision, period close, local facility) use it so that they see every entry queued in P1–P12 of the closing tick. The postings they queue commit in the same `commitTick` (03 §3.15: "All postings of steps (1)–(9) commit in P13 of the closing tick").
5. **`runPeriodClose` (YEAR_CLOSE, step 9).** For every entity in this partition (sorted ids, not DISSOLVED) and every account with code 4000–6999 (sorted keys) whose `projectedBalance` is non-zero:
   - `t = closingTargetOf(e, code)`:
     - sys and code 5900 or 4900 → 3990, which keeps `netIssuance` (T03-11) unchanged;
     - kind `chr` or `ali` → 3900;
     - everything else, including sys's other P&L accounts, fnd, spv, hh, frm and row → 3200.
   - If the balance `b > 0n` (debit: expense or loss), post `ctx.ledger.reclass(E, b, ccy, 'LED_PERIOD_CLOSE', code, t, { sub: a.sub })`, which is Dr t / Cr code.
   - If `b < 0n`, post `reclass(E, −b, ccy, 'LED_PERIOD_CLOSE', t, code, { sub })`, which is Dr code / Cr t.
   - The `sub` is the P&L account's sub. The equity target ignores the sub (T03-07 keys equity accounts without sub).
   - After commit, every P&L account is 0, and 3300 (the derived parent of 4xxx–6xxx) is 0.
   - Legs from another partition that arrive after the closing tick belong to the next period. B3 delivery is tick-bounded (02), and the projection counts the legs that arrived this tick.
6. **Year-end ordering across partitions.** Each partition closes its own entities in its own P13. No cross-partition reads are needed, because P&L accounts are local to their entity.
7. **Standing-facility conversion** (`convertNegativeReserves(ctx, mode)`):
   - **Candidates.** For each `bnk` entity (and `exc`/`ccp` if they hold 1002) of the partition, and each ccy with an account key `B:1002::ccy`: `r = projectedBalance`. If `r < 0n`, the bank gets a facility:
     - `facilityId = `sf:${B}:${ccy}:${closingGameDay}``;
     - build the entry with `tplCbCredit(env04, cbkId(ccy), B, −r, ccy, 'CBK_STANDING_FACILITY', { sub: facilityId })` and push it with `runtimeOf(ctx).queue.push(entry)`. `env04` is the template env with `requestedBy = { module: '04', phase, entityId: B }`;
     - result: `Dr B:1002 / Cr cbk:2002:B` and `Dr cbk:1307:<facilityId> / Cr B:2120:<facilityId>`, after which reserves are exactly 0.
   - **Modes.** The cbk lives on FINANCE (T02-14 home rule), and no leg can cross partitions after B3. So:
     - `'P13_LOCAL'` (03's P13 handler at t = 719, last P13_PRE action, after steps 6–9): only banks whose cbk is on the same partition, which are the NPC banks on FINANCE. These are exact for the closing tick.
     - `'P12'` (03's `phases.P12` at t = 719 on PLAYER/COORD partitions): banks whose cbk is remote. The cbk legs leave at B3. The projection includes everything queued up to 03's P12 handler.
     - `'DAY_OPEN_CATCHUP'` (03's P2 DAY_OPEN hook of the next tick): any committed `1002 < 0` left on a remote-cbk bank, from payments queued after 03's P12 handler in the closing tick (modules 14 and 15 run later in P12). The loan uses the closing day's `facilityId` and is dated to the conversion tick.
   - **Ownership.** `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` is owned by 04, and its catalog description says "incl. automatic day-close conversion, §3.3.4". 03 performs the automatic part on 04's behalf, so the catalog ownership rule still holds (requestedBy module 04). `LEDGER_AUTOMATIC_CODES` is the closed list of codes for which 03 may do this.
   - 04 repays the facility the next day (`CBK_STANDING_FACILITY_REPAY`) and charges `CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST` at `FACILITY_RATE_E6`.
   - **I10 at day close** (T03-10):
     - at t = 719, banks with a local cbk must have every 1002 ≥ 0;
     - remote-cbk banks must have every 1002 ≥ −intraday limit at t = 719, and ≥ 0 after the DAY_OPEN catch-up of the next tick (checked in that tick).
8. **Wiring (done by T03-28's phases.ts).**
   - The P2 boundary hooks call `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P2')`, and DAY_OPEN also calls `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'DAY_OPEN_CATCHUP')`.
   - `phases.P12` at t = 719 calls `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P12')`.
   - `phases.P13` → T03-09 `runP13`, in this order:
     - restricted releases (T03-12);
     - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_PRE')`;
     - `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P13_LOCAL')` (DAY_CLOSE only);
     - `commitTick`;
     - the post-commit updates;
     - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_POST')`.

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

#### §3.3.4 The payment rail (cash legs)

Each entity has a **default paying account** and a **default receiving account**, both DEFAULT `1001 Settlement cash` in its reporting currency; an entity can set a bank deposit as default (`acct.payment.setAccounts`, §4). The rail turns "A pays B amount X in ccy c" into postings:

| A pays from | B receives into | Postings |
|---|---|---|
| A:1001 | B:1001 | `Dr B:1001 / Cr A:1001` (and cbk reassigns holder sub-accounts: `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2001:B`) |
| A:1010:X (bank X) | B:1001 | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr B:1001 / Cr X:1002`; `Dr cb:2002:X / Cr cb:2001:B` |
| A:1001 | B:1010:Y | `Dr Y:1002 / Cr A:1001`; `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2002:Y`; `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B` |
| A:1010:X | B:1010:Y, X ≠ Y | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr Y:1002 / Cr X:1002` (+ cbk holder reassignment); `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B` |
| A:1010:X | B:1010:X (same bank) | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr X:2100:B` and `Dr B:1010:X / Cr A:1010:X` (book transfer, no reserves move) |
| A is a bank paying from its reserves | — | treat A's paying account as A:1002 |

If A's paying account lacks funds but A has another money account with funds, the rail draws in this order: default paying account, then 1001, then deposits in order of bank id. Holds always reserve against the account the rail will use (02 §3.14.3).

Bank reserve constraint: a bank's 1002 may go negative intraday down to its **intraday credit limit** at the central bank (07 sets it, DEFAULT = 100% of eligible collateral pledged); at day close any negative 1002 is converted automatically into an overnight standing-facility loan (`CBK_STANDING_FACILITY`, CREDIT_CREATE) at the facility rate (04). This keeps I10 satisfiable.

#### §3.3.7 Accrual conventions

- Day count **Act/364** for every interest-bearing item (game year = 364 days). Daily accrual at day close via `accrue()` (02 §3.9.3), every calendar day.
- Rent, wages, upkeep and subscriptions accrue daily by their per-day rate; cash moves on the schedule the owning module defines (hourly operations for businesses settle cash immediately with hh/frm; monthly for leases; at maturity or coupon dates for bonds).
- Bonds trade with accrued interest: buyer pays seller clean price plus accrued (`BOND_ACCRUED_INTEREST`).

At the end hook of each period (02 §3.4), in this order: (1) accruals of the day; (2) mark-to-market; (3) ECL provisions; (4) appraisals due; (5) depreciation and amortization; (6) tax provisions; (7) for MONTH: property tax and VAT remittance due; (8) for QUARTER: tax installments due, published statements queued; (9) for YEAR: annual tax assessment, loss carry-forwards rolled, `LED_PERIOD_CLOSE` moving every 4xxx/5xxx/6xxx balance into 3200 (3900 for characters and alliances), group tax elections take effect; (10) statements materialized in P14. ## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

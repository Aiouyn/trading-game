## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task covers insolvency and bankruptcy accounting for every core entity except treasuries and central banks:
- **Detection** (03 §3.14.1):
  - payment defaults after a 720-tick grace, from an obligation registry that owning modules feed;
  - the day-close balance-sheet test (marked solo equity < 0 → `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW` in the same commit, I19);
  - automatic case opening after 5 uncured game days with an unpaid creditor;
  - voluntary filing, creditor petition (≥ $10,000) and regulator action.
- **Case model** (`InsolvencyCase`, `Claim`, 03 §2.10). Opening freezes the debtor. Claims are taken at face plus accrued interest and ranked SECURED … COMMON_EQUITY.
- **Absolute-priority waterfall** with `allocate`, run each time the estate realizes cash, at day close.
- **Discharge** at close, except persistent claims.
- **Procedure hooks** for 09 (liquidation, restructuring with cram-down respecting absolute priority), 07 (bank resolution) and T03-26 (personal).
- **Claim trading** (`acct.insolvency.claim.sell`, `INSOLV_CLAIM_TRADE`) and voluntary filing (`acct.insolvency.file`).
- **Sovereign, fund and SPV rules** (§3.14.6–7).
- **INV-BS-002**: a character's equity goes negative only with a recorded cause event in the tick. It also carries I19.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kinds.** Add to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`:
   - `COLLATERAL_OF`: subject the liability key (loanId), value the asset account keys pledged (07);
   - `SUBORDINATED_ASSET`: subject the assetId of a bond, value boolean (06/09).
   If T03-01 lacks the 03 §2.10 `InsolvencyCase`, `Claim` and `ClaimRank` types, add them verbatim.
1. **Obligations** (03 §3.14.1 rule 1). Owning modules register scheduled obligations (loan instalments 07, coupons 06, tax T03-23, wages and rent 09/10, contract payments 15, VM calls 07, judgments 13) and mark them paid. `runDefaults` runs every tick in P13_POST and is state only:
   - OPEN with `tick > dueTick` → GRACE;
   - GRACE with `tick > dueTick + 720` and `paid < amount` → DEFAULTED. This also:
     - emits `ledger.default` `{ debtorId, obligationRef, amount }` PARTIES (debtor and creditor);
     - sets the debtor's review row (reason PAYMENT_DEFAULT);
     - sets the status to `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW`.
     Creditor remedies are 07, 15 and 13's.
   - **biz and prop debtors.** Their default is the owner's default (03 §8 edge 8): the review is set on `rootOwnerId`. A biz never enters insolvency separately.
2. **Balance-sheet test** (rule 2; I19). Close step at DAY_CLOSE, P13_POST, after the commit of the closing tick, so the status lands in the same commit. For every core entity not in `EXEMPT_KINDS` and not a fnd or spv:
   - `eq = reviewEquity` (marked solo NW; for a chr the 2990 persistent claims are added back, because a new case cannot discharge them and they are collected by garnishment, T03-26);
   - if `eq < 0n` and the status is ACTIVE: set `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW`, write the review row, and emit `ledger.insolvency.review` `{ entityId, equity }` PRIVATE to the owner plus the regulator (partyIds include the bloc regulator `reg_<BLOC>`);
   - if `eq ≥ 0n` and the review reason is BALANCE_SHEET with no default open: cure (back to ACTIVE, delete the row);
   - otherwise `consecutiveDays++`.
   Characters go to T03-26's personal trigger instead of the automatic case.
3. **Auto-open** (`runAutoOpen`, DAY_CLOSE, P13_POST). A company (cmp, bnk, ins, exc, rat, npc company) with `consecutiveDays ≥ 5` and at least one DEFAULTED obligation, or a creditor unpaid after grace → `openCase(debtor, 'BALANCE_SHEET', procedure)`:
   - LIQUIDATION by default;
   - BANK_RESOLUTION for bnk;
   - 09 may switch to RESTRUCTURING within the case.
4. **Other triggers.**
   - `acct.insolvency.file` (VOLUNTARY): the actor controls the entity, and the entity is not already in a case (`ACCT_ALREADY_IN_CASE`). Not allowed for EXEMPT kinds or fnd/spv (`ACCT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`). PERSONAL is allowed only for the actor's own chr.
   - Creditor petition (13 message) needs an unpaid admitted claim ≥ $10,000 → `CREDITOR_PETITION`.
   - Regulator action (07 message) → `REGULATOR`.
5. **Case open** (`openCase`, P13_POST, state only).
   - Status becomes `IN_INSOLVENCY`. The debtor is frozen: the rail and templates reject new liabilities and payments from the debtor except `ESTATE_CODES` (T03-08's `LED_ENTITY_FROZEN`).
   - **Claims** (`buildClaims`): every LIABILITY account with a balance, at face, plus accrued interest to the opening tick (2520 subs matched to the loan), excluding 2990 persistent claims (which are carried forward intact) and the equity accounts. Ranks (`rankOf`):
     - SECURED to the extent of `valueAccount` of the `COLLATERAL_OF` assets; the deficiency becomes a separate SENIOR_UNSECURED claim;
     - ADMIN: court and estate costs after opening, admitted as they arise;
     - TAX: 2400, 2410, 2420;
     - WAGES: 2510, capped at the wages accrued over the last 28 game days (T03-18 DAY totals). The excess is SENIOR_UNSECURED;
     - DEPOSITS_INSURED (banks): 2100 per holder up to `DEPOSIT_INSURED_LIMIT`. The rest is SENIOR_UNSECURED;
     - SENIOR_UNSECURED: bonds 2300 not subordinated, 2110, 2130, 2200–2206, 2500, 2530–2590 (judgments 2550, restitution 2560), 2600, and uninsured deposits;
     - SUBORDINATED: bonds with `SUBORDINATED_ASSET`, and shareholder loans (loans from an entity that holds the debtor's shares, 2800 from related parties);
     - PREFERRED_EQUITY and COMMON_EQUITY: holders of the share classes (T03-16 `edgesOfIssuer`), entitled only to the residual.
   - Contingent claims are admitted when they crystallize (`admitContingent`).
   - Persistent kinds are tagged for personal cases (T03-26): CCP deficits (`CCP_DEFICIT` claims of ccp_TCH), restitution (2560), fines (2550 from LAW_FINE_*), judgments (2550 from law.judgment, crime/court debts beyond the protected floor, 03 §3.14.5 rule 2) and taxes.
   - Emit `ledger.insolvency.opened` `{ caseId, debtorId, procedure, recoveriesByRank: {} }`: PUBLIC for companies and institutions, PRIVATE for characters.
   - A bankrupt company is a market: its bonds keep trading (06), admitted claims can be sold, and shares trade until cancelled.
6. **Realizations** (03 §3.14.4).
   - Estate asset sales use normal codes (`MKT_TRADE`, `PROP_SALE_*`, `BIZ_SALE_*`) with the debtor as seller. Proceeds of a SECURED claim's collateral are attributed to that claim's `realized`.
   - Escrow deposited (1070) stays with the CCP or court; the contract decides release (15; 03 §8 edge 15). The estate's 1070 is not realizable until released.
7. **Waterfall** (`waterfall`, pure):
   - each secured claim takes `min(outstanding, securedProceeds)`; any excess goes to the estate; any deficiency joins SENIOR_UNSECURED;
   - the estate cash goes down the ranks in `RANK_ORDER`, excluding SECURED;
   - inside a rank, payment is pro rata with `allocate` (largest remainder, ties by claim id; 02 §3.9.3);
   - a rank is paid in full before any lower rank receives anything.
   - `runDistributions` (DAY_CLOSE, P2: these are cash PAYs, so before B3):
     - for each case with procedure ≠ PERSONAL (T03-26 runs PERSONAL cases with the protected floor) and estate cash > 0 (debtor money accounts above the admin reserve), compute the waterfall;
     - post one `INSOLV_DISTRIBUTION` PAY per paid claim, with legs = the debtor's liability account of the claim and the creditor's receivable account;
     - update `recovered`;
     - emit `ledger.insolvency.distribution` with `recoveriesByRank`;
     - set the status to DISTRIBUTING.
8. **Close** (`closeCase`), when the estate is exhausted or 09/07 completes:
   - every unpaid admitted non-persistent claim gets `INSOLV_DEBT_DISCHARGE`: the debtor gains 4130 (tax-exempt, tag EXEMPT) and the creditor's loss goes through 1309 or 5190;
   - persistent claims move to the debtor's 2990:<creditorId> (T03-26 collects them);
   - for a liquidated company: shares are cancelled with `MOVE_QTY` from the holders to the issuer, then `LED_DISSOLUTION` (T03-05 QTY_ONLY) and status DISSOLVED;
   - a PERSONAL case returns the chr to ACTIVE (never DISSOLVED) and deletes its review row;
   - emit `ledger.insolvency.closed`.
9. **Procedures.**
   - RESTRUCTURING (09 decides):
     - `proposePlan` validates the classes, and `checkAbsolutePriority` returns ok only if no class junior to a non-accepting, not-fully-paid senior class receives value (cram-down respects absolute priority);
     - votes are recorded per class;
     - `executePlan` books the discharge of the rest after 09 posts `CMP_DEBT_EQUITY_SWAP` and the plan cash.
   - BANK_RESOLUTION (07): 03 updates claim amounts after 07's `BNK_BAILIN_CONVERSION` and `BNK_DEPOSIT_INSURANCE_PAYOUT`. The insurer (gov treasury DEPOSIT_INSURANCE_FUND sub) subrogates into the DEPOSITS_INSURED claims.
10. **Sovereigns, central banks, funds and SPVs** (03 §3.14.6–7).
    - gov and cbk may run negative equity indefinitely: no review, no case. 04 handles sovereign restructuring (`SOV_RESTRUCTURING_EXCHANGE`) and recapitalization.
    - fnd: when assets < liabilities at day close → `openCase(fund, 'BALANCE_SHEET', 'LIQUIDATION')` at once. Unit holders rank as COMMON_EQUITY and never pay more than their units; lenders take the shortfall.
    - spv: never opened while 08's `SPV_LOSS_ALLOCATION` can allocate losses. 08 signals exhaustion with the message `acct.insolvency.regulator`.
11. **Claim trading** (`acct.insolvency.claim.sell`, applyPhase P9):
    - validate: the claim is admitted, its `creditorId` is controlled by the actor, the case is not CLOSED, and the buyer is a core entity → else `ACCT_CLAIM_INVALID`. Buyer acceptance comes through 15's contract flow, as a message `acct.insolvency.claim.accept` carrying the contract id.
    - apply: `INSOLV_CLAIM_TRADE` SWAP_ASSET (buyer cash → seller; value leg Dr buyer:1190:<claimId> / Cr seller:<receivable>, with the seller's book adjustment), then `creditorId = buyer`.
12. **INV-BS-002** (TICK FATAL, 20 §3.1):
    - for every chr touched this tick whose marked equity (`nwSolo`, consolidated not required) is < 0 in `ctx.post` and was ≥ 0 in `ctx.pre`, the tick's events must contain an event of `CAUSE_EVENT_KINDS` whose subjectIds include the chr → else fail with `{ entityId }`;
    - DAY_CLOSE sub-check **I19**: every core entity with `reviewEquity` < 0 has status INSOLVENCY_REVIEW or IN_INSOLVENCY, except gov and cbk.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

**[03 §3.14.1]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.14.1 Detection (day close, every core entity)

1. **Payment default**: any scheduled obligation (loan instalment, coupon, tax, wages, rent, contract payment, VM call, judgment) not paid at its due tick enters a grace of 720 ticks (1 game day); unpaid at grace end → `ledger.default` event, creditor remedies (07/15/13) and `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW`.
2. **Balance-sheet test**: marked solo equity < 0 at day close → `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW`. A company under review for 5 consecutive game days without curing (recapitalization, debt waiver) and with any creditor unpaid after grace → case opened automatically (`trigger = BALANCE_SHEET`). Characters: § 3.14.5.
3. **Voluntary filing** (`acct.insolvency.file`), **creditor petition** (13 filing by a creditor with an unpaid admitted claim ≥ $10,000), **regulator** action for chartered institutions (07 resolution triggers).
4. Treasuries and central banks are exempt (§3.14.6). Funds cannot have negative unit-holder equity: losses beyond fund capital fall on the fund's lenders (§3.14.7).

V3 Part 8 invariant "no negative equity without a recorded event" is implemented by step 2: negative equity always comes with an `INSOLVENCY_REVIEW` status and event at the same day close (I19).

#### §3.14.3 Claims register and absolute-priority waterfall

```
on case open: freeze the debtor (no new liabilities, no payments except by the estate);
  claims = all liabilities at face + accrued interest to the opening tick; contingent claims admitted when crystallized;
  rank each claim:
    SECURED            (to the extent of collateral value; deficiency becomes SENIOR_UNSECURED)
    ADMIN              (court fees, estate costs, post-petition wages)
    TAX                (taxes due)
    WAGES              (unpaid wages, capped at 28 game days)
    DEPOSITS_INSURED   (banks only: covered by deposit insurance, which then subrogates)
    SENIOR_UNSECURED   (bonds, loans, trade payables, uninsured deposits, judgments)
    SUBORDINATED       (subordinated bonds, shareholder loans)
    PREFERRED_EQUITY, COMMON_EQUITY
distribution (each time the estate realizes cash, at day close):
  1. secured creditors: proceeds of their own collateral, up to their claim; excess to the estate
  2. estate cash by rank in the order above; inside a rank pro rata with allocate() (02 §3.9.3)
  3. a rank is paid in full before any lower rank receives anything (absolute priority)
at case close: every unpaid admitted claim is discharged with INSOLV_DEBT_DISCHARGE
  (creditor: loss through the allowance or 5190; debtor: gain 4130, tax-exempt)
  except persistent claims (§3.14.5), which survive
```

#### §3.14.7 Funds and SPVs

A fund's unit holders cannot lose more than their units. If a fund's liabilities exceed its assets, the fund is liquidated; lenders (prime brokers, repo counterparties) take the shortfall as creditors. SPV losses are allocated bottom-up to tranches (`SPV_LOSS_ALLOCATION`, 08); an SPV never enters insolvency while its waterfall can allocate losses.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

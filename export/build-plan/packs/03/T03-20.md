## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task lays the base of the tax engine. Every tax is computed exactly from postings: each reason code carries a `taxTag`, there is no self-assessment, and nothing can be under-declared (03 §3.12.1). It provides:
- the six regimes of 03 §3.12.3 (five blocs plus the Isla Serena free zone) as 03-owned state, which 04 may move;
- residency of every entity kind (§3.12.2);
- the `TaxAccount` per (entity, jurisdiction, tax type, year) (§2.9);
- tax lots and their consumption, FIFO by default or by specific identification (§2.5; `acct.lots.select`);
- realized capital gains with FX at the acquisition-day and disposal-day rates, the short/long split, the main-home exemption, the lifestyle-loss rule, netting and loss carry-forward (§3.12.5);
- the wash-sale rule, including related parties (§3.12.6; 03 §8 edge 6);

- the `acct.tax.election` intent with its type registry (LOT_METHOD here; GROUP_FORM and GROUP_LEAVE in T03-21; EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS in T03-22).

Taxes are TRANSFERs to the jurisdiction's treasury (`gov_<BLOC>`). They are never a Stabilizer lever.

3. **Tax accounts.** `getOrOpenTaxAccount` creates the row with status OPEN and amounts at 0. `addToBase` adds to `base`. One row exists per (entity, jurisdiction, type, year), with the year from `ctx.cal.year`.
4. **Lots** (`updateLots`, P13 after commit). For each committed valid entry and each posting with `qty` on an account whose code is in `LOTTED_CODES`, for a taxable entity (RESIDENT, or TRANSPARENT holders on their own units):
   - **Acquisition** (debit qty into E's asset account from another entity):
     - open a lot with `qty`;
     - `costBasis` = the price paid plus fees composed into the entry with fee or tax tags (`TAX_SECURITIES_TX`, `TAX_STAMP_DUTY`, exchange or broker fee codes of the same entry for E), translated into the residency ccy at `fxRateE6(LATEST)` at that tick (the acquisition-day rate);
     - value-only assets (`unitLot`) open a lot with qty 1;
     - a gift-like move with no price (MOVE_QTY) uses `bookValue`, or `taxPriceFor(entry)` when the transfer is flagged;
     - then call `applyWashSaleOnAcquisition`.
   - **Disposal** (credit qty out of E to another entity):
     - `selection = led.lotsel[entry.requestedBy.intentId]` when `taxPrefs.lotMethod === 'SPECIFIC'`;
     - `consumeLots` takes the selected lots first, then FIFO by `(acquiredTick, lotId)`, and splits a lot partially (basis pro rata half-even, with the remainder kept on the lot);
     - `proceeds` = E's cash leg of the entry (net of E's fee postings in the entry), or `taxPriceFor(entry)` when the related-party flag is set (`relatedPartyAdjusted`), allocated over the consumed lots by qty with `allocate`. Proceeds are translated at the disposal-day rate, so FX gains on foreign assets are part of the gain (03 §3.12.5);
     - write one `RealizedGain` per consumed lot, with `kind`:
       - DERIVATIVE: `GAIN_SHORT` tag, or codes 1600/2600, perps, FX; always SHORT;
       - HOME: a 1800/1580-prop disposal where `OWNER_OCCUPIED_SINCE ≤ tick − 91 × 720`; `exempt = min(gain, $10,000,000)` per sale;
       - LIFESTYLE: 1860; a loss here is `exempt = −gain`, i.e. not deductible;
       - NORMAL otherwise.
     - If the gain is negative and the code is in `WASH_SALE_CODES`, call `applyWashSaleOnLoss`.
   - **Same-entity moves.** Moves between an entity's own accounts, such as collateral pledges inside the same entity, consume nothing.
   - **No-consideration disposals.** Seizures, insolvency realizations and forced divestitures dispose at the entry amount, or at `taxPriceFor` when it exists.
   - **Gains taxed elsewhere.** `GAIN_SHORT`-tagged cash flows without qty (VM, option premia, perp funding, FX settlements) write a DERIVATIVE gain record of the signed P&L amount of E's posting, with no lots.
5. **Splits.** On `EQ_SPLIT` (03 §8 edge 5), `splitLots(asset, new, old)` scales every lot's qty by new/old with floor. The remainder goes to the lot with the latest acquisition so the total qty matches the account. Basis is unchanged, so basis per share is divided. 05 adjusts open orders.
6. **Wash sale** (03 §3.12.6; `W = params.num('wash_sale_window_game_days') × 720`).
   - `applyWashSaleOnLoss(g)`, look-back: take the lots of the same asset acquired in `[g.tick − W, g.tick]` by E or by any entity `isRelated` to E, that were not consumed by this sale, ordered by `(acquiredTick, lotId)`. Disallow `|loss| × min(1, Σ replacementQty / soldQty)`, allocated across the replacement lots by qty. Add it to their `washSaleAdjust` and `costBasis`, and record `g.washDisallowed`. Any loss left, with the remaining qty, opens a `WashWindow` until `g.tick + W`.
   - `applyWashSaleOnAcquisition(lot)`, look-forward: for each open window of the same asset whose entity is E or related to E (edge 6: a character and its own company), with `tick ≤ untilTick`, disallow `lossRemaining × min(1, lot.qty / qtyRemaining)`. Add it to the lot's basis and `washSaleAdjust`, increase the original gain record's `washDisallowed`, and shrink the window.
   - Expired windows are deleted at day close.
   - A disallowed loss is removed from the year's capital loss: `gain + washDisallowed` is the counted amount.
7. **Capital gains year** (`capitalGainsYear`, characters):
   - per record, `counted = gain + washDisallowed − exempt`;
   - net within SHORT and within LONG;
   - a net short loss offsets a net long gain, and the reverse;
   - the incoming `lossCFIn`, from up to `lossCarryForwardYears` previous years (oldest first), is used against the remaining net gains only;
   - `tax = shortRate × taxable.short + longRate × taxable.long`, half-even;
   - a remaining net loss becomes the new carry-forward. It is stored on the CAPITAL_GAINS TaxAccount `lossCF` by T03-21's loss roll at YEAR_CLOSE.
   Companies do not use this. `realizedGainsTotal` feeds corporate taxable profit, where realized gains are measured against lot cost basis (T03-21).
8. **Elections and lot selection.**
   - **`acct.tax.election`**, applyPhase P1. `validate`: the actor controls the entity named in `params.entityId` (or is the character itself), then dispatch to the registered type; an unknown type → `ACCT_BAD_ELECTION`.
   - **LOT_METHOD**, registered here:
     - params `{ entityId, method: 'FIFO' | 'SPECIFIC' }`;
     - SPECIFIC requires `LICENSE` ≥ LIC2 for the actor → else `ACCT_LICENSE_REQUIRED`;
     - apply: write `taxPrefs.lotMethod` with `lotMethodFrom = tick + 1`.
   - **`acct.lots.select`** `{ saleIntentId, lotIds }`, applyPhase P1:
     - validate: actor LIC2+ (`ACCT_LICENSE_REQUIRED`); every lot exists and belongs to an entity the actor controls (`ACCT_LOT_NOT_FOUND`); all lots are of one asset; Σ qty ≥ the sale intent's qty when that is readable, else ≥ 1 (`ACCT_LOTS_DO_NOT_COVER`);
     - apply: write `led.lotsel`, keyed by `saleIntentId`.
     - The sale entry carries `requestedBy.intentId = saleIntentId`, so step 4 consumes those lots first.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

**[03 §3.12.1]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.1 Principles

1. Taxes are TRANSFERs from the taxpayer to the treasury (`gov`) of the jurisdiction; treasuries spend through 04's fiscal rules, mostly into the elastic economy (`MAC_GOV_SPENDING_ELASTIC`, SINK). Taxes are therefore a delayed sink for the player economy, but **not** a Stabilizer lever (§3.7.1).
2. The engine computes every tax exactly from postings (each reason code carries a `taxTag`). There is no self-assessment and no way to under-declare; "planning" means choosing structures, timing and jurisdictions, and "evasion" means moving money through illicit reason codes, which the Revenue Office can find (§3.12.14).
3. Tax year = game year. Amounts in the jurisdiction's currency (Serena: FDL).
4. Answers V3 Part 13 Q5 ("how deep should tax be?"): **five bloc regimes plus one free zone, a treaty matrix, CFC rules, transfer pricing, participation exemption, tax groups, exit tax and audits**: a planning game with real trade-offs, bounded so that every rule is computable each tick. DEFAULT (Mathis may override).

**[03 §3.12.2]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.2 Jurisdictions and residency

- **Characters** are tax-resident in the bloc of their home city (00 §5.9: start in an FDL city, so FDL). They may change residency to another bloc where they own or lease a home (10) with `acct.tax.changeResidency` (§4), at most once per 91 game days, paying exit tax (§3.12.13). Characters cannot be resident in Isla Serena (DEFAULT: Serena has no personal tax authority; this stops the top of the board from simply leaving the tax system).
- **Companies, banks, insurers** are resident where incorporated (09). Incorporation in the **Isla Serena Free Zone** is allowed for companies (`homeBloc = 'SERENA'`, currency FDL): 0% corporate tax, annual franchise fee `max($250,000, 0.1% of total assets)` (`CMP_FRANCHISE_FEE_SERENA`, SINK), no treaties, subject to the home blocs' CFC rules (§3.12.11).
- **Funds and SPVs** are tax-transparent (no entity-level tax; holders taxed on distributions and gains).
- **Alliances** are transparent: income is attributed to members pro rata to dues paid in the year.
- **NPC entities**: NPC companies' modelled tax is `corporate rate × modelled profit` posted with `TAX_CORPORATE` from their fundamentals (05/09); NPC banks, insurers and funds follow the same rules as player ones.

**[03 §3.12.5]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.5 Capital gains tax (characters; companies include gains in corporate profit)

- Realized gain per disposal = proceeds (net of fees) − cost basis of the lots consumed (FIFO or specific identification, §2.5), in the residency currency (translated at the disposal-day rate and the acquisition-day rate respectively, so FX gains on foreign assets are part of the gain).
- Assets: securities, ETFs, fund units (on redemption or sale), crypto and tokens, DeFi positions on withdrawal, collectibles, property, businesses sold, private stakes, AI models and datasets sold or licensed as assignment, concession rights. Derivatives, perps and FX trading positions: gains always taxed at the short rate.
- **Main home exemption:** gain on a home owner-occupied for ≥ 91 game days before sale is exempt up to $10,000,000 of gain per sale (DEFAULT).
- **Lifestyle durables**: gains taxable, losses not deductible.
- Netting: gains and losses of the same year net within short and within long; a net short loss offsets net long gain and vice versa; a remaining net loss carries forward 5 game years (only against gains).


<!-- excerpt 03§3.12.6 -->
**[03 §3.12.6]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.6 Wash-sale rule

A loss on the disposal of an asset is disallowed if the same entity or a related party (§3.11.6) acquires the same asset within `PARAM.wash_sale_window_game_days` (DEFAULT 5) before or after the disposal; the disallowed loss is added to the basis of the replacement lot (`washSaleAdjust`). Applies to exchange-traded assets, crypto and fund units.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

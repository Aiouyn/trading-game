## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
Net worth is always derived from the balance sheet at a tick and never stored as truth (INV-BS-001; V3 2.1). This task computes:

## Implementation steps
0. In T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`, add two kinds:
   - `FX_CLOSE_E6`: subject `${from}>${to}`, value bigint string, the official day-close FX spot mark from 05;
   - `CHALLENGE_BOOK`: subject entityId, value boolean, from 15. Challenge-event portfolios are a separate book and never count.
   05 writes `FX_MARK_E6` every tick with the latest clearing mid. That fact already exists.
1. **FX.**
   - `fxRateE6` resolves the rate in this order:
     - same currency → 1,000,000n;
     - the direct pair fact;
     - the inverse fact, as `1e12 / rate` half-even;
     - a cross through FDL: `rate(from>FDL) × rate(FDL>to) / 1e6`, half-even.
   - A missing rate is `LEDGER_BUG` in engine code. In views it degrades to "n/a" and is never shown as 0.
   - `translate` uses T02-04 `fxConvert` (half-even).
   - `recordFxCloses` stores every `FX_CLOSE_E6` fact at DAY_CLOSE and prunes days older than 364.
   - `averageRateE6` is the integer mean of the stored daily closes over the inclusive day range, rounded half-even. With no stored closes it falls back to the closing rate.
   - An FX crash shows up at once: NWrank and flash NW use the intraday `FX_MARK_E6` (03 §8 edge 3). Tax bases keep acquisition-day rates (lots, T03-20).
2. **What counts** (`countsForNw`, 03 §3.10.2):
   - MEMO accounts → no;
   - 1221 restricted compute → counted at 0 (valueAccount already returns 0);
   - restricted cash subs of 1001 → yes, at face;
   - 1050, 1060 and 1070 (margin, default fund, escrow deposited) → yes, for the depositor until release;
   - assets under `COURT_FREEZE` → yes, because ownership is unchanged;
   - seized assets → excluded naturally: 13 moves them to the court escrow entity, and they count for the victim only after the award transfers them;
   - judgments, fines and restitution → liabilities posted by 13 from the verdict tick; pending lawsuits are never posted;
   - 2990 persistent claims → a liability;
   - entities with `CHALLENGE_BOOK` true → excluded from every consolidation and from NWrank. `nwConsolidated` drops them from `lookThroughSet` before solving.
3. **Solo NW** (`nwSolo`).
   - Per currency: `Σ valueAccount(...)` over counted ASSET accounts, minus `Σ (−balance)` over LIABILITY accounts, plus `−balance` of the 3800 currency trading accounts. 3800 is treated as equity, so the per-currency books balance (03 §3.3.6).
   - Translate every currency into the reporting currency with `mode` (default LATEST). `byCcy` and `translated` are returned.
   - The translated 3800 sum is the cumulative FX result. T03-19 shows it as FX gains/losses.
4. **Consolidated NW** = `lookThroughNav(entity)` (T03-16). For an entity with no 1570/1580 holdings of members, it equals `nwSolo`. `markedNetWorth(read, id, tick)` returns `nwConsolidated` for chr, ali and any group parent, and `nwSolo` for everyone else. This is what G7 `ctx.ledger.netWorth` serves.
5. **Board value** (`boardValueOfHolding(read, holderId, a, tick)`), for exchange-traded holdings, meaning accounts whose asset has a `MARKET_STATS` fact with `adv20 > 0`:
   - **Mark.** Use the volume-weighted mean of the hour's mark history (`hourMarkSeries`, 03 §9 "board NW uses the hour's mark history, not one print"), or the effective mark if the hour has no history. `mv = qty × mark`, using `notional` half-even.
   - **Impact haircut.** `ratio = groupQtyOf(root(holder), asset) / adv20`, aggregated over the holder's whole group so that splitting a stake across subsidiaries does not reduce the haircut. If `ratio > 0.05`, `h = min(0.5, params.num('nw_impact_y') × sigmaDaily × sqrt(ratio))`; else `h = 0`. Value = `mv × (1 − h)`.
   - **Stale haircut.** If `effectiveMark(...).stale`, value × (1 − 0.10). This covers a halted asset, or an equity on its fundamental-value fallback after 5 game days without trades (03 §8 edge 14).
   - **Control cap.** If the issuer is controlled by the holder's group (`controls(root(holder), issuer)` or root(issuer) == root(holder)): `value = min(value, economicShare(holder, issuer) × lookThroughNav(issuer) × params.num('nw_control_listed_cap'))`. Summed over the group's holdings through the look-through, this equals `effectiveOwnership × NAV × cap` (03 §3.10.3 rule 2).
   - Each reduction is recorded into the breakdown accumulator of the current `computeNwRank` call.
   - Holdings that are not exchange-traded return `valueAccount(...).value`.
6. **NWrank** (`computeNwRank(read, id, tick, params)`, chr or ali):
   - `solveLookThrough(read, root = id, …)` with `BoardValueFn = boardValueOfHolding`;
   - `nwRank = translate(nav(id), reportingCcy, 'FDL', 'LATEST')`;
   - `nwConsolidated` = the same solve with the default `valueAccount` board function, so the breakdown shows what the adjustments removed.
   The breakdown has concentration, controlCap and stale, each translated to FDL. NWrank is used by ranks (14), boards (15), the Stabilizer (T03-24) and NWI (T03-24).
7. **Cadence** (`runNwRank`). It runs in P13 when `isNwRankTick(ctx.cal.tickOfDay)` (once per game hour at t mod 30 == 29, and at t = 719 for the Stabilizer and MSI), after T03-11's money update.
   - For every chr and ali entity of the partition (index byKind, sorted), compute the NWrank and write the `led.nwrank` row with `cadence = tickOfDay === 719 ? 'DAY_CLOSE' : 'HOUR'`.
   - Flash NW is not stored. Views call `flashNw` every tick (17).
8. **INV-BS-001** (TICK FATAL, 20 §3.1).
   - For every `led.nwrank` row in `ctx.writeSet` of this tick: `computeNwRank(ctx.post, id, tick)` must equal the stored row exactly (all fields).
   - No other 03 table stores a net-worth value. A grep test in `networth.test` asserts that no 03 TableDef other than `led.nwrank` has a field named `nw*` or `netWorth*`.
   - A failure is `ctx.fail('INV-BS-001', { entityId })`.
- **E-47 top-end friction ramp.** `frictionNW` aggregates the 03 owner group, every 15-linked character (`linkedCharactersOf`, 15 §3.12) and the shells any of them control, each counted once. `topEndRamp` is the single ramp that 05, 07A, 07B, 09B and 13 call; coefficients [Quant]. Pure number math, no posting.
- **E-59 (PROVISIONAL):** the same `frictionNW` cluster aggregation also defines 09B's rival-gate sector share and 05's participation anchor positions.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

<!-- excerpt 03§3.10.3 -->
**[03 §3.10.3]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.10.3 Board adjustments

1. **Concentration (impact) haircut** for any exchange-traded holding with `qty / ADV20 > 0.05`:
   `haircut = min(0.5, PARAM.nw_impact_y (DEFAULT 0.5) × σ_daily × sqrt(qty / ADV20))`, consistent with 05's square-root impact law. Value = mark × qty × (1 − haircut). A whale's 40% stake in a thin name cannot be counted at the screen price.
2. **Control cap**: a listed company controlled by the character's group is counted at `min(haircut market value of the stake, effectiveOwnership × NAV_lookthrough × PARAM.nw_control_listed_cap (DEFAULT 2.0))`. Pumping one's own listed company does not pump one's rank beyond twice its underlying value.
3. **Stale illiquid haircut**: STALE marks on exchange-traded assets take an extra 10% haircut until a valid mark returns.


<!-- excerpt 03§3.10.4 -->
**[03 §3.10.4]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.10.4 Cadence

NWrank is computed for every character and alliance in P13 of ticks with `t mod 30 == 29` (once per game hour, aligned with board refreshes, 15) and at day close (for the Stabilizer and MSI). A character's own screens show a flash NW recomputed every tick from the latest marks (17).


<!-- excerpt 03§3.3.6 -->
**[03 §3.3.6]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.3.6 Multi-currency

Each posting is in one currency. An entity's books balance **per currency** thanks to the currency trading accounts 3800 (template `FX`). Net worth in a reporting currency R:

```
NW_R(E) = Σ_ccy fx(ccy→R) · NW_ccy(E)     where NW_ccy(E) = Σ assets_ccy − Σ liabilities_ccy (including 3800 as equity)
```

The translated sum of the 3800 accounts is the entity's cumulative FX result; statements show it as FX gains/losses (§3.13.2). FX rates for translation are the official day-close marks of FX spot (05), intraday the latest clearing mid.


<!-- excerpt 03§3.9.1 -->
**[03 §3.9.1]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.9.1 General rules (mark governance)

1. Marks come only from engine sources: market clearing, engine models, engine appraisals, NAV strikes. **Never** from a price a player declares, from a P2P or related-party transaction price, or from a contract term.
2. **Thin-print guard** for exchange-traded assets: the intraday mark after a tick with clearing volume `V` at price `P` is `mark = (V·P + V_min·mark_prev) / (V + V_min)` where `V_min = PARAM.mark_min_volume_frac (DEFAULT 0.002) × ADV20` (05 provides ADV). A single tiny print cannot move the mark much (anti-ramping for NW).
3. **Stale marks**: a mark older than its `validUntilTick` is flagged `STALE`; NW still uses it, but boards apply the illiquidity haircut of §3.10.3; collateral systems (07) apply their own haircuts.
4. **Halted instruments** keep the last mark flagged `STALE` until the reopening auction.
5. All marks are in the asset's currency; translation per §3.3.6.

**[03 §8]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

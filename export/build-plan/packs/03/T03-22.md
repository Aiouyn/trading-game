## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task covers the tax rules that cross jurisdictions:
- **Withholding at source** on cross-border dividends, interest and royalties at the treaty-matrix rate. The recipient's residence gets a foreign tax credit, capped at its own tax on that income; the excess goes to 6020 (03 §3.12.11).
- **CFC attribution.** Passive income of a controlled low-tax company (always Serena) is attributed yearly to the controlling resident at that resident's rates, with credit for tax the company paid. The Serena substance test is applied, and a false substance claim is an audit finding (T03-23).
- **Transfer pricing.** Every related-party transaction outside ±`related_party_band` is assessed at the nearest band edge, shown line by line (§3.12.12).
- **Residency change** (`acct.tax.changeResidency`), with its rules:
  - a home must be owned or leased in the target bloc;
  - at most one change per 91 game days;
  - the character must not be Held or IN_INSOLVENCY;
  - never Serena.
- **Exit tax** (§3.12.13): a deemed disposal at marks, 10% of the excess over $1M at the departing bloc's long rate, paid at once or by the `EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS` election in 4 quarterly installments, with lot step-up.
- **Mid-year residency** (03 §8 edge 13): income is taxed by each bloc for its part of the year, with brackets prorated by days of residency.

## Implementation steps
1. **Withholding** (§3.12.11).
   - `withholdingFor` is called by the owning module that pays a DIV, INT or ROYALTY tagged code (06 coupons, 07 interest, 08 distributions, 09 dividends and licences, 15 contracts; the catalog allows 06, 07, 08, 09 and 15 on `TAX_WITHHOLDING`). The module composes the result into its payment entry.
   - When `taxStatus(payer).jurisdiction = A` and `taxStatus(recipient).jurisdiction = B` with `A ≠ B`:
     - `rate = whtRateE6(A, B, kind)`;
     - `wht = mulRate(gross, rate)`;
     - the supporting entry is a `TAX_WITHHOLDING` PAY of `wht` from the payer to `gov_<A>`, with legs 2420/1130. The recipient receives `gross − wht` through the owner's code;
     - record `withheld += wht` on the recipient's TaxAccount of the income type for year Y, in B.
   - Transparent recipients (funds, SPVs) look through to their holders: the withholding is computed at the fund's distribution, using the holder's residence.
   - The matrix (DEFAULT):
     - every bloc pair → `TREATY_DEFAULT`;
     - SRL↔NKR (either direction) → `NO_TREATY_SRL_NKR`;
     - payments to a Serena company → `TO_SERENA`;
     - payments from a Serena company → 0.
   - `foreignTaxCredit` (used by T03-21/T03-23 at assessment): for each income type, `credit = min(Σ withheld abroad, own tax attributable to that income)`, where the own tax is computed as the average rate × that income. `lost = withheld − credit`, posted at assessment as `LED_TAX_PROVISION` to 6020.
2. **CFC** (`computeCfc` / `applyCfc`, close step 9 order 5, YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE, state only).
   - For each company X and each controller H of X (`controls`, the direct or indirect controller), where H is resident in bloc A and X is low-tax for A (effective passive tax rate < 50% of A's corporate rate, which is always true for Serena):
     - `passiveIncome` = X's year totals of PASSIVE_TAGS, plus trading and capital gains (lot gains, T03-20 `realizedGainsTotal`), plus intra-group service fees (`CMP_INTRAGROUP_SERVICE` received);
     - for a Serena X, if `serenaSubstance(X).passes` (Serena opex ≥ `serena_substance_opex`, paid to the Serena city's hh/frm sectors, plus a Serena property, either the fact `SERENA_PROPERTY` or a 1580 prop in a Serena city) and X claims active status, only passive income is attributed. Active business income is never attributed;
     - attributed amount = `effectiveOwnership(H, X) × passiveIncome`, taxed at A's rates: personal brackets for a chr, the corporate rate for a company;
     - `credit` = H's share of tax X paid;
     - `attributedTax − credit` is added to H's TaxAccount (type PERSONAL_INCOME or CORPORATE) for the year, and the row is written to `led.cfc`.
   - A Serena company that claims active status (fact `SERENA_ACTIVE_CLAIM` from 09; add it to `LedgerFactKind`) but fails substance is recorded with `substance false`. T03-23 turns it into finding (b).
3. **Transfer pricing** (§3.12.12). `transferPricingAdjustments(E, year)`: for each `led.rpflag` row whose parties include E and whose tick falls in the year, `adjustment = (armsLength − price)` from E's side. It increases taxable income for the side that under-priced income or over-priced costs. T03-21's `corporateYear` and `personalIncomeYear` add Σ adjustments. Pricing inside the band is lawful planning and gets no adjustment. The Tax Center shows the adjustments line by line (T03-27).
4. **Exit tax** (§3.12.13). `exitTax(chr, fromBloc)`:
   - `unrealized = Σ max(0, gain)` over every lot at its mark (T03-20 `unrealizedGain`); losses are not netted;
   - `excess = max(0, unrealized − $1,000,000)`;
   - `tax = cgtLongRate(fromBloc) × excess`. For FDL with $5M gains: 10% × $4M = $400,000.
   - Payment is either at once (`TAX_EXIT` PAY to `gov_<fromBloc>`) or, when the `EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS` election is on file, 4 equal quarterly installments (`allocate`, remainder to the first). Installments are scheduled on the TaxAccount EXIT with due ticks at the next 4 quarter ends; late interest follows T03-23.
   - Then `stepUpLots`: every lot basis becomes its mark value.
   - `EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS` is registered with `registerElectionType`: params `{ chrId }`, recorded in `taxPrefs.elections`, and used by the next residency change.
5. **`acct.tax.changeResidency`** `{ toBloc }`, applyPhase P11. Validate, in order:
   - the actor is a chr → else `ACCT_NOT_CHARACTER`;
   - `toBloc !== 'SERENA'` → else `ACCT_SERENA_PERSON` (03's `REJECT_SERENA_PERSON`);
   - `toBloc !== current` → else `ACCT_SAME_BLOC`;
   - fact `HOME_IN_BLOC` `${chr}:${toBloc}` is true (owns or leases a home, 10) → else `ACCT_NO_HOME`;
   - `tick − taxPrefs.residencyChangedTick ≥ 91 × 720` → else `ACCT_COOLDOWN`;
   - not `HELD`, and status not IN_INSOLVENCY → else `ACCT_BLOCKED`.

   Apply:
   - compute and post or schedule the exit tax;
   - step up the lots;
   - record the residency segment;
   - set `taxPrefs.residencyChangedTick = tick`;
   - write `LedgerEntity.homeBloc = toBloc` with effect from the next tick, through a pending row applied in 03's P13 post-commit (like the rail change of T03-19). The reporting currency is unchanged.
6. **Mid-year residency** (03 §8 edge 13).
   - `residencySegments` splits the year at each change.
   - For personal income, each segment's income (period totals by day range) is taxed by that segment's bloc with `proratedTax`: every bracket threshold × `daysResident / 364`, half-even.
   - Capital gains are taxed by the bloc of residence on the disposal day.
   - T03-23's assessment sums the segments.

## Test plan
- `03#23 A Serena company controlled by an FDL resident with $10M interest income and no substance has the $10M attributed to the resident and taxed at FDL rates with credit for taxes paid; with substance ($2M Serena opex + a Serena property) and active business income, nothing is attributed`:
  - chr (FDL) controls cmp_S (SERENA) at 100%. S earns $10M of INT. `computeCfc` gives `passiveIncome` $10M, the attribution on chr's PERSONAL_INCOME, and the progressive FDL tax on chr's total. A credit equals the tax S paid (0, Serena rate 0%).

- `03#24 A dividend from an EUN company to an FDL resident is withheld at 10%; to a Serena company at 30%; between SRL and NKR at 25%`:
  - `withholdingFor` of $1,000 gross:
    - EUN cmp → FDL chr: $100 `TAX_WITHHOLDING` to `gov_EUN`, recipient nets $900;
    - EUN → Serena cmp: $300;
    - SRL cmp → NKR chr: $250;
    - Serena cmp → FDL: 0.
  - The FDL chr's foreign tax credit is capped at its own tax on that income.
- `03#25 A character cannot change residency to Serena, nor to a bloc without a home, nor twice within 91 game days`:

  - EUN without `HOME_IN_BLOC` → `ACCT_NO_HOME`;
  - with a home → accepted;
  - another change 90 days later → `ACCT_COOLDOWN`;
  - after 91 days → accepted.

  - with `EXIT_TAX_INSTALLMENTS`, 4 × $100,000 are scheduled at quarter ends;
  - $0.8M of gains → no exit tax.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

#### §3.12.11 Withholding, treaties and controlled foreign companies

- **Withholding** on cross-border dividends, interest and royalties paid by a resident of jurisdiction A to a resident of B ≠ A: withheld by the payer at source (`TAX_WITHHOLDING`, to A's treasury) at the treaty rate; the recipient's residence gives a foreign tax credit up to its own tax on that income (excess is lost, 6020).
- **Treaty matrix** (DEFAULT): every pair of blocs: dividends 10%, interest 0%, royalties 5%, **except** SRL–NKR (no treaty): 25% / 15% / 15%. Payments **to** Serena companies from any bloc: 30% / 30% / 30%. Payments **from** Serena companies: 0%.
- **CFC rule**: if a character or company resident in bloc A controls (§3.11.2) a company resident where the effective tax on its passive income is < 50% of A's corporate rate (always true for Serena), the company's **passive income** (interest, dividends, royalties, rents, trading and capital gains, intra-group service fees) is attributed each year to the controlling resident and taxed at A's rates, with credit for taxes the company paid. **Active business income** (operating businesses, properties operated with staff) is not attributed.
- **Serena substance test**: a Serena company is treated as having active income (not attributed) only if in the tax year it paid at least `PARAM.serena_substance_opex` (DEFAULT $2,000,000) of operating costs in Isla Serena (to the Serena city's hh/frm sectors) and holds or leases a property on Isla Serena (10). A Serena company failing the test while claiming active status is an audit finding (§3.12.14).


<!-- excerpt 03§3.12.12 -->
**[03 §3.12.12]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.12 Transfer pricing

For tax, every related-party transaction (§3.11.6) is valued at the engine's arm's-length price: if the transaction price lies outside ±10% of fair value, the assessment automatically uses the nearest band edge (the 10% band is the legal planning margin). Because the engine knows fair value, no transfer-pricing evasion is possible; aggressive pricing inside the band is lawful planning. The adjustment is shown in the Tax Center line by line.


<!-- excerpt 03§3.12.13 -->
**[03 §3.12.13]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.13 Exit tax

On a character's change of residency: deemed disposal of every asset at its mark; if the total unrealized gain exceeds $1,000,000, the excess is taxed at the departing bloc's long-term CGT rate (`TAX_EXIT`). Payable at once or, by election, in 4 equal quarterly installments with late interest. Lot bases are stepped up to the marks.

**[03 §3.12.2]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.2 Jurisdictions and residency

- **Characters** are tax-resident in the bloc of their home city (00 §5.9: start in an FDL city, so FDL). They may change residency to another bloc where they own or lease a home (10) with `acct.tax.changeResidency` (§4), at most once per 91 game days, paying exit tax (§3.12.13). Characters cannot be resident in Isla Serena (DEFAULT: Serena has no personal tax authority; this stops the top of the board from simply leaving the tax system).
- **Companies, banks, insurers** are resident where incorporated (09). Incorporation in the **Isla Serena Free Zone** is allowed for companies (`homeBloc = 'SERENA'`, currency FDL): 0% corporate tax, annual franchise fee `max($250,000, 0.1% of total assets)` (`CMP_FRANCHISE_FEE_SERENA`, SINK), no treaties, subject to the home blocs' CFC rules (§3.12.11).
- **Funds and SPVs** are tax-transparent (no entity-level tax; holders taxed on distributions and gains).
- **Alliances** are transparent: income is attributed to members pro rata to dues paid in the year.
- **NPC entities**: NPC companies' modelled tax is `corporate rate × modelled profit` posted with `TAX_CORPORATE` from their fundamentals (05/09); NPC banks, insurers and funds follow the same rules as player ones.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

## §9 Exploits and counters

| Exploit | Counter |
|---|---|
| Duplicating NW through holding structures (sell assets to own company at inflated price, circular holdings) | marks never come from related-party prices (§3.9.1); look-through NAV (§3.11.4); intercompany profit reserve in consolidation; cycle limit |
| Pumping own listed company to climb the board | concentration haircut and control cap (§3.10.3); thin-print guard; surveillance (05/13) |
| Ramping a thin market at the hourly board snapshot | thin-print guard (§3.9.1) weights marks by volume; board NW uses the hour's mark history, not one print |
| Alt-account funnelling via off-market P2P sales | P2P tax 2%, related-party flags, 15's caps and linked-account detection, marks unaffected |
| Transfer-pricing profits into Serena | engine applies arm's-length prices for tax (§3.12.12); CFC rules; substance test; audit odds rise with Serena share |
| Becoming a Serena tax exile | characters cannot be Serena residents (§3.12.2) |
| Harvesting losses and rebuying immediately | wash-sale rule including related parties (§3.12.6) |
| Exit before tax bill (change residency) | exit tax with step-up (§3.12.13) |
| Spending down before a fine | fines are % of NW at the higher of offense and verdict ticks (03-conflict §1); protected floor only for crime/court debts |
| Going bankrupt to escape the Clearing House or victims | persistent claims survive discharge and are garnished (§3.14.5) |
| Repeated fresh starts | once per 56 real days, restricted cash, reputation hit, 1× leverage and credit blocks (14 §3.13.5) |
| Using ad or fresh-start cash to fund ops | restricted cash (§3.5.6, I21) |
| Printing money via a player bank | bank lending creates deposits and an equal loan asset (no NW created); capital and liquidity rules (07); loans to alts are related-party and 15-flagged; defaults hit the bank's own equity |
| Exploiting the Stabilizer (e.g. hoarding cash to trigger loosening) | input is median wealth ratio of engaged players, robust to a few actors; levers act world-wide, never per player; deadband, step limits and ranges |
| Gaming tax timing at year end | installments based on YTD; deemed rules (exit tax); unrealized gains taxed only on realization for everyone equally |
| Hiding illicit inflows | reason codes are set by the engine from the actual mechanism, never by the player; Revenue Office audits read them |
| Reading other players' finances | statements private (L11); published only for listed/chartered entities; ledger events visible to parties only |
| Bug that creates money | I3/I4/I17 abort the tick; compensating entries via `ADM_CORRECTION_*` with two approvals; replay finds beneficiaries |

## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task computes every domestic tax exactly from postings and reason-code tax tags (03 §3.12.1). The taxes are:
- **Personal income tax** of characters (§3.12.4): tagged income, net rental income, sole-proprietor business profit, deductions, exemptions, and losses carried forward 5 game years.
- **Corporate income tax** (§3.12.7): profit before tax adjusted for unrealized fair-value results, lot-based realized gains, participation exemption, non-deductible items and the interest cap (excess carried forward without limit), less loss carry-forward. It also covers tax groups, the accounting-firm credit and deferred tax.
- **Property tax** (§3.12.8): daily accrual, paid at month end.
- **VAT on final consumption** (§3.12.9): the business accrues it and remits monthly; lifestyle purchases pay it at the till.
- **At-source transaction taxes** (§3.12.10): securities tax, stamp duty and the 2% P2P tax, built as supporting entries composed into the owner's trade entry.
- **The Isla Serena franchise fee** (§3.12.2).

Assessment, installments, late interest and audits are T03-23. Withholding, treaties, CFC, transfer pricing and exit tax are T03-22.

## Implementation steps
1. **Tag sums.**
   - Per entity and year, sum the period totals (T03-18 `periodNet`, kind YEAR) of the income accounts. Each committed posting is attributed to its code's `taxTag` (T03-05). The P13 `updatePeriodTotals` keeps a `byTag` split alongside `byCode` for P&L accounts; add that field to `PeriodTotalRow` (T03-18 schema, same unit).
   - For `ytdToDay`, use the running YEAR row (the running estimate).
   - Results of biz and prop entities are attributed to their `ownerId`: a chr owner's personal income, or a company owner's corporate profit.
   - Alliance income is attributed to members pro rata to dues paid in the year (`taxStatus` MEMBERS_BY_DUES).
2. **Personal income tax** (`personalIncomeYear`, 03 §3.12.4):

   | Component | Source |
   |---|---|
   | dividends | 4030 with tag DIV, non-exempt |
   | interest | 4020 with tag INT |
   | net rental | the chr's directly owned prop entities: 4010 − (5020 + 5040 + 5260 property costs) − 5090 mortgage interest of that property − 5120 depreciation |
   | sole-proprietor | for each biz owned directly: revenue − costs − depreciation. A loss offsets other income of the year |
   | royalties | 4140 |
   | other | tags SERVICE, OTHER and alliance attributions |
   | deductions | 5090 tagged INVESTMENT: interest on loans used to buy income-producing assets |

   - Not taxable: tag EXEMPT (4110 faucet income: start cash, grants, ads, quest rewards, compensation), 4120 restitution and damages, debt discharge gains, and gifts and inheritances received (the giver pays the P2P tax).
   - Capital gains are separate (T03-20).
   - `taxable = max(0, Σ − lossCFUsed)`, where `lossCFUsed` takes the oldest of up to 5 years first.
   - `tax = progressiveTax(taxable, regime.personalBrackets)` in the residency bloc's currency.
   - Characters have no Serena residency: `taxStatus` never returns it.
   - A net negative income becomes the year's loss carry-forward.
   - Credits:
     - the accounting-firm credit (step 6);
     - the foreign tax credits written by T03-22 into `TaxAccount.credits`.
3. **Corporate income tax** (`corporateYear`, 03 §3.12.7; companies, banks, insurers, exchanges, rating agencies; NPC companies are MODELLED and post their own `TAX_CORPORATE`):
   - **Profit before tax.** `pbt` = the solo income statement's PBT (T03-19 line mapping over `periodNet`).
   - **Unrealized fair-value results.** `unrealizedRemoved = −(4070 − 5170)`: unrealized gains and losses are taxed only on realization.
   - **Realized gains.** `realizedGainsAdj = realizedGainsTotal(entity, year)` (lot-based, T03-20) − (4060 − 5160 + 4090 − 5190): the book-based realized result is replaced by the lot-based one, so that earlier unrealized revaluations are taxed when realized. This is an interpretation of "taxed only on realization" together with the deferred-tax sentence. Without it, gains booked through 4070 would never be taxed.
   - **Participation exemption.** `participationExempt` = dividends (DIV) and lot gains on shares of companies where `isParticipation(holder, issuer)`: ≥ 10% economic share, held ≥ 91 game days by the oldest open lot.
   - **Non-deductible items.**
     - 5210 fines and penalties;
     - postings with codes `OPS_BRIBE_*`;
     - 5240 tagged DONATION;
     - 30% of 5230 tagged HOSTING (hosting and entertainment).
   - **Interest cap.**
     - `taxEbitda` = EBITDA of the tax computation;
     - `netInterest = 5090 − 4020`;
     - `cap = interestCapPctEbitda × max(0, taxEbitda)`;
     - the excess `max(0, netInterest − cap)` is added back, and `led.intcap.carriedForward` grows by the excess (no time limit);
     - unused cap room in a year releases carried-forward interest (`interestCFUsed`).
   - **Loss carry-forward.** `lossCFUsed` takes the oldest first, up to `lossCarryForwardYears`.
   - **Tax.** `taxableProfit = pbt + unrealizedRemoved + realizedGainsAdj − participationExempt + nonDeductible + interestAddBack − interestCFUsed − lossCFUsed`, and `tax = corporateRate × max(0, taxableProfit)`. SERENA residents have a 0% rate.
   - **Credit.** `credit = tax × accountingFirmCredit` and `taxAfterCredit = tax − credit`.
   - Related-party transactions use the arm's-length price for tax. T03-22's transfer-pricing adjustment adds `price − taxPriceFor` per flagged entry.
4. **Tax groups** (§3.12.7).
   - `GROUP_FORM` params `{ headId, memberIds }`:
     - eligible members are in the same bloc, and the head holds ≥ 75% of each member, directly or through members (`effectiveOwnership` over controlled chains) → else `ACCT_GROUP_INELIGIBLE`;
     - the election takes effect from the next game year (`effectiveYear = year + 1`).
   - `GROUP_LEAVE` params `{ headId, memberId }` takes effect at year end.
   - Both types are registered with `registerElectionType`.
   - In a grouped year, `corporateYear` is computed for the head over the pooled members: profits and losses are summed, and intra-group transactions are removed through the T03-18 `intraGroup` split of each P&L account. One CORPORATE TaxAccount is kept on the head, and members show their allocated share (by positive taxable profit, `allocate`).
   - `applyGroupElections` is close step 9 order 60, YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE: it applies the pending forms and leaves.
5. **Deferred tax.**
   - `deferredTaxTarget`: temporary differences are the unrealized fair-value gains (book above lot basis) and losses at the corporate rate. Gains give a liability in 2430; losses give an asset in 1900, sub = jurisdiction.
   - The daily provision (step 7) posts `LED_TAX_PROVISION` for the change in current tax (Dr 6000 / Cr 2400) and `LED_TAX_PROVISION` with `valueLegA 6010`, `valueLegB 2430/1900` for the change in deferred tax.
   - Characters have no deferred tax.
6. **Accounting-firm credit** (01 §4.4): the entity owns, or has a service contract with, an accounting-firm business, signalled by the fact `ACCOUNTING_FIRM_TIER` (09). The credit is `min(0.05, 0.01 × (tier − 1) + 0.02)` of the computed income tax (corporate or personal); no fact → 0.
7. **Daily provision** (`runTaxProvision`, close step 6, DAY_CLOSE, P13_PRE), for every taxpayer of the partition:
   - compute the YTD estimates by type: PERSONAL_INCOME and CAPITAL_GAINS for chr; CORPORATE for companies; PROPERTY, VAT, SEC_TX, STAMP and P2P from their accounts and totals;
   - write `led.taxest` and the TaxAccount `base`/`liability` (status OPEN);
   - emit `ledger.tax.estimate` `{ entityId, ytdByType }` PRIVATE (03 §5);
   - for companies, post `LED_TAX_PROVISION` for `Δ(YTD liability) − Δ(2400 accrued so far this year)`. A negative amount releases, and ACCRUE swaps the sides.
   - Projected balances (T03-15) make the estimate include entries queued earlier in the closing tick. `assertLocal(entity)` is called before each post.
8. **Property tax** (§3.12.8):
   - **Rate and base.** The rate is the city param `property_tax_rate_<city>` of the property's city (10 owns the param; 23); the base is `ASSESSED_VALUE` (10) at the start of the month. This is the 03 §3.12.3 vs 10/23 contradiction, resolved for the PARAM, as recorded in T03-20.
   - **Accrual.** `ensurePropertyTaxAccruals` (MONTH_OPEN, P2) registers or refreshes one `LED_ACCRUAL` (T03-15 `registerAccrual`, kind FEE) per prop entity: `a = gov_<bloc>`, `b` = the prop's owner, `valueLegA` 1130 (tax receivable at the treasury), `valueLegB` 2400, sub `<bloc>:PROPERTY:<year>`, `perDay = propertyTaxDaily`.
   - **Who pays.** Owner-occupied homes pay too. Isla Serena islands pay the Serena rate, collected by the FDL treasury (DEFAULT).
   - **Remittance.** `remitPropertyTax` (close step 7, MONTH_CLOSE, P2) calls `settleAccrual(id, 'TAX_PROPERTY')`, paying the treasury and clearing the payable/receivable. Its cash leg goes to `gov` on FINANCE, so it runs in P2, before B3.
   - 10's own `RE_PROPERTY_TAX` (SINK) is not used: the tax is a TRANSFER to the treasury (03 owns the tax engine, 03 §3.12.1). Reported as a contradiction.
9. **VAT** (§3.12.9).
   - **Business sales to households** (`BIZ_SALES_HH`): 09 composes `vatOnSale` into its sale entry. The supporting entry is an ACCRUE of `vatPart(gross, v)` with Dr 4000 (revenue reduced) / Cr 2410:<bloc>.
   - **Lifestyle purchases** (`LIFE_PURCHASE`): 14 composes `vatOnLifestyle`, a `TAX_VAT_REMIT` PAY of the VAT part from the buyer to the buyer's residency treasury. The remainder is the sink part (T03-02 note).
   - The lifestyle price shown is `lifestylePrice`.
   - B2B, B2G and export sales carry no VAT, and there is no input-credit bookkeeping (DEFAULT).
   - **Remittance.** `remitVat` (close step 7, MONTH_CLOSE, P2) pays every 2410 balance with `TAX_VAT_REMIT` (legs 2410/1130).
10. **At-source taxes** (§3.12.10). Each function returns a supporting entry that the owning module composes into its trade entry with `composeEntries`, so the trade and its tax commit together:
    - `securitiesTxTax`: buyer pays `rate(listedBloc) × notional`, for products EQUITY and ETF only. It uses `TAX_SECURITIES_TX` with requestable modules 05 and 06.
    - `stampDuty`: `stamp_duty_rate × price`, paid by the buyer of a property at completion (`TAX_STAMP_DUTY`, module 10). The single PARAM applies to every bloc, as recorded in T03-20.
    - `p2pTax`: `p2p_tax × value` (2%), paid by the payer to the payer's residency treasury, for the codes in `P2P_TAXED_CODES`. `SOC_REVENUE_SHARE` is taxed only when the recipient `isRelated` to the payer. It returns undefined for the exempt codes.
    - All three post to gov of the relevant bloc with value legs 5280/4190.
11. **Serena franchise fee** (§3.12.2): at YEAR_CLOSE (P2), each `homeBloc === 'SERENA'` company pays `max($250,000, 0.1% × total assets)` with `CMP_FRANCHISE_FEE_SERENA` (SINK, ELASTIC_OUT to frm_FDL, 5250).
12. **Loss carry-forwards** (`rollLossCarryForwards`, close step 9 order 20, YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE):
    - personal, capital and corporate losses of the closing year are stored on the TaxAccount `lossCF` of the next year;
    - losses older than the jurisdiction's limit expire;
    - interest carried forward stays without limit.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[03 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

#### §3.12.4 Personal income tax (characters)

```
taxable_income(year) =   dividends received (non-exempt)              (4030, taxTag DIV)
                       + interest received                            (4020, INT)
                       + net rental income of directly owned property (4010 − 5020/5040/5260 property costs − mortgage interest 5090 on that property − depreciation 5120)
                       + profit of directly owned businesses          (sole proprietorship: revenue − costs − depreciation of the biz; losses offset other income of the year)
                       + royalties and licence income                 (4140)
                       + other taxable income (fees for services, revenue shares, alliance attributions)
                       − allowable deductions (interest on loans used to buy income-producing assets, 5090 tagged INVESTMENT)
not taxable: faucet income 4110 (start cash, grants, ads, quest rewards, compensation), gifts and inheritances received (taxed at the giver via P2P tax), restitution and damages received (4120), gains on debt discharge in insolvency
tax = Σ over brackets of rate × income in bracket (progressive), in the residency bloc's currency
```

Losses: a net negative taxable income carries forward (5 game years) against future taxable income.

#### §3.12.7 Corporate income tax (companies, banks, insurers, exchanges, rating agencies, NPC companies)

```
taxable_profit = profit before tax (solo statement, §3.13)
               − unrealized fair-value gains (4070) + unrealized fair-value losses (5170)    (taxed only on realization)
               − exempt participation income (dividends and gains on shares of companies held ≥ 10% for ≥ 91 game days)
               + non-deductible items: fines and penalties (5210), bribes (OPS_BRIBE_*), lobbying donations (5240 tagged DONATION), 30% of hosting/entertainment
               + interest expense above the cap: net interest > cap% × tax-EBITDA → excess added back and carried forward indefinitely
               − loss carry-forward used (oldest first, up to the jurisdiction's limit)
tax = corporate rate × max(0, taxable_profit)
```

Deferred tax (1900/2430) records the timing difference of unrealized gains and losses at the corporate rate (`LED_TAX_PROVISION`).

**Tax groups**: companies resident in the same bloc where one holds ≥ 75% of the others (directly or through members) may elect (`acct.tax.election GROUP_FORM`) to be taxed as one: profits and losses pooled, intra-group transactions ignored. Election effective from the next game year; leaving takes effect at year end.

**Tax-planning credit** (01 §4.4 "Accounting firm reduces owner's tax by up to 5%"): an entity that owns, or has a service contract with, an Accounting-firm business (09) receives a credit = `min(5%, 1% × firm tier above T1 + 2%)` of its computed income tax (corporate or personal). DEFAULT.

#### §3.12.8 Property tax

Daily accrual `rate / 364 × appraised value at the start of the month` (`LED_ACCRUAL`), paid at month end (`TAX_PROPERTY`). Owner-occupied homes pay it (01 §13). Isla Serena islands pay the Serena rate (collected by the FDL treasury on behalf of the free-zone authority, DEFAULT).

#### §3.12.9 VAT

- Applies only to **final consumption**: business sales to households (`BIZ_SALES_HH`: the posted price includes VAT; the business accrues `price × v/(1+v)` in 2410 and remits at month end with `TAX_VAT_REMIT`) and lifestyle purchases by characters (`LIFE_PURCHASE`: price shown includes VAT; the VAT part goes to the purchaser's residency treasury at purchase, and the rest is the sink). The lifestyle price shown is `catalog price × L2 × (1 + v)`.
- B2B, B2G and exports: no VAT (DEFAULT simplification: no input-credit bookkeeping).

#### §3.12.2 Jurisdictions and residency

- **Characters** are tax-resident in the bloc of their home city (00 §5.9: start in an FDL city, so FDL). They may change residency to another bloc where they own or lease a home (10) with `acct.tax.changeResidency` (§4), at most once per 91 game days, paying exit tax (§3.12.13). Characters cannot be resident in Isla Serena (DEFAULT: Serena has no personal tax authority; this stops the top of the board from simply leaving the tax system).
- **Companies, banks, insurers** are resident where incorporated (09). Incorporation in the **Isla Serena Free Zone** is allowed for companies (`homeBloc = 'SERENA'`, currency FDL): 0% corporate tax, annual franchise fee `max($250,000, 0.1% of total assets)` (`CMP_FRANCHISE_FEE_SERENA`, SINK), no treaties, subject to the home blocs' CFC rules (§3.12.11).
- **Funds and SPVs** are tax-transparent (no entity-level tax; holders taxed on distributions and gains).
- **Alliances** are transparent: income is attributed to members pro rata to dues paid in the year.
- **NPC entities**: NPC companies' modelled tax is `corporate rate × modelled profit` posted with `TAX_CORPORATE` from their fundamentals (05/09); NPC banks, insurers and funds follow the same rules as player ones.

**[03 §3.12.1]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.12.1 Principles

1. Taxes are TRANSFERs from the taxpayer to the treasury (`gov`) of the jurisdiction; treasuries spend through 04's fiscal rules, mostly into the elastic economy (`MAC_GOV_SPENDING_ELASTIC`, SINK). Taxes are therefore a delayed sink for the player economy, but **not** a Stabilizer lever (§3.7.1).
2. The engine computes every tax exactly from postings (each reason code carries a `taxTag`). There is no self-assessment and no way to under-declare; "planning" means choosing structures, timing and jurisdictions, and "evasion" means moving money through illicit reason codes, which the Revenue Office can find (§3.12.14).
3. Tax year = game year. Amounts in the jurisdiction's currency (Serena: FDL).
4. Answers V3 Part 13 Q5 ("how deep should tax be?"): **five bloc regimes plus one free zone, a treaty matrix, CFC rules, transfer pricing, participation exemption, tax groups, exit tax and audits**: a planning game with real trade-offs, bounded so that every rule is computable each tick. DEFAULT (Mathis may override).

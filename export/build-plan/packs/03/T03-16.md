## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
This task derives who owns and who controls every entity from the share quantities the ledger holds. It builds:
- the ownership graph (03 §3.11.1);
- control: majority, de facto and contract rules, at most one controller per entity, evaluated to a fixed point in sorted order (§3.11.2);
- effective ownership (§3.11.3);
- the look-through NAV, the anti-duplication algorithm that stops value being counted twice through holding structures (§3.11.4, Bible I06; 03 §9 "Duplicating NW through holding structures").

It also provides 09's acquisition guard `LED_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP` (product of the cycle's shares ≥ `cycle_max_product`), and an hourly revaluation of every 1580 investment to its look-through or 09 value. When control changes, it updates `ownerId`, `rootOwnerId` and `perimeter`, with the matching money-aggregate adjustment. INV-BS-004 (03 I20) guards the result.

## Implementation steps
0. **New fact kind.** Add `'SPV_EQUITY_TRANCHE'` to T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`: subject spvId, value trancheId, written by 08. Also add `markOwnershipDirty` to T03-09's commit step c, which is a one-line call for qty postings whose account code is in `OWNERSHIP_ACCOUNTS` or is 3100/2320. Both edits stay inside unit 03.
1. **Graph** (03 §3.11.1, §2.7).
   - `issuerOfAsset`: in `accountsOfAsset(assetId)`, the account with code 3100 (shares, units), or 2320 for an SPV tranche. Its entity is the issuer. `shareClass` is the issuance account's sub (03 §3.2: 3100 sub shareClass; 2320 sub trancheId); holders' 1570 subs are `<cmpId>:<shareClass>`.
   - Edges come from holder accounts 1500 and 1570 (votes), 1560 tranches (votes only for the SPV equity tranche, step 2c) and 1530 (economic only, `votingPct = 0`).
   - `votingPct = qty × votesPerShare / Σ_classes outstanding × votesPerShare`.
   - `economicPct = qty × economicWeight / Σ_classes outstanding × economicWeight`. Both are float, computed with `Number(qty)` over `Number(outstanding)`; `economicWeight` comes from 09 and is "after preferences".
   - Preferred shares have 0 votes unless 09 converts them; dual class follows 09's `votesPerShare`.
   - Holdings of an issuer's own shares (treasury, held by the issuer itself) are excluded from both numerators and denominators.
   - `biz` and `prop` are leaves: one edge `{ holderId: ownerId, votingPct: 1, economicPct: 1 }`.
2. **Control** (`computeControl`, 03 §3.11.2). Iterate to a fixed point, at most `n = entities + 1` passes, in sorted issuer order. In each pass, for each issuer X:
   - **Aggregated votes.** `votes(H, X) = Σ votingPct(h, X)` over holders h with `h === H` or `controls(H, h)` under the current assignment.
   - **Suspension rule.** Votes held in X by an entity that X itself controls (directly or recursively) are suspended. This is the treasury-share rule for cross-holdings (03 §3.11.4 last paragraph), and it is what keeps control acyclic in 03 §8 edge 7.
   - **Majority.** Candidates are holders H with `votes > 0.5`. The direct controller is the qualifying candidate that does not control another qualifying candidate (the lowest link of the chain); ties go to the smaller id.
   - **De facto.** Otherwise, H qualifies when `votes(H, X) >= params.num('control_defacto_min')`, `votes(H, X) >= 2 ×` the largest other holder group's votes (holder groups = holders aggregated by their current root), and fact `BOARD_CONTROL` `${H}:${X}` is true (09 governance).
   - **Contract** (c):
     - `fnd`: the manager never controls its fund for consolidation (custody rule, 08). The fund's `controllerId` is undefined, but its `ownerId`/`rootOwnerId` keep the registered manager, so a player fund stays in PLAYER (03 §8 edge 16: "the fund is in P").
     - `spv`: the sponsor controls only when it holds > 50% of the qty of the tranche named by `SPV_EQUITY_TRANCHE`.
     - biz/prop: LEAF control by `ownerId`.
   - No controller → `displayOwnerId` = the largest holder group's root (ties → smaller id).
   - **Result per entity.** `ownerId = controllerId ?? displayOwnerId ?? id`. `rootOwnerId` follows `controllerId` to the end; an uncontrolled entity is its own root (03 §3.11.2 last sentence). `controlPath` runs from the root down to the direct controller.
   - `groupOf(root)`: every entity whose root is `root`, with `effectiveOwnership = effectiveOwnership(root, e)`, `controlPath`, and `nci[e] = 1 − eff`.
3. **Look-through set and NAV** (03 §3.11.4).
   - `lookThroughSet(root)`: BFS from `root` over edges whose holder account is 1570 or 1580, or biz/prop leaves, i.e. holdings not valued at market. It includes the root and is sorted in canonical order (`ENTITY_KIND_ORDER`, then id).
   - For each member e, `a_e` (float, in e's reporting ccy, via `fxConvert` and `FX_MARK_E6`) is computed as follows:
     - `a_e = Σ valueAccount` over e's ASSET accounts, excluding holdings of shares or 1580 of other members of G, minus Σ of its LIABILITY balances;
     - listed holdings (1500/1510) in issuers outside G are valued with `BoardValueFn` (board value, 03 §3.10.3);
     - intercompany loans, receivables and payables with members are **kept** on both sides. The worked paragraph of §3.11.4 says the loan "appears as an asset in a_A and a liability in a_B ... nets correctly (minority holders of B bear their share)". This is a spec inconsistency with the "excludes intercompany receivables/payables" bullet; resolved for the explicit worked rule.
   - `S[e][j] = economicShare(e, j)` for members j held through 1570/1580/leaf edges.
   - Gauss–Seidel solves `x_e = a_e + Σ_j S[e][j] · x_j` in canonical order for **exactly** `params.num('lookthrough_iterations')` sweeps, starting from `x = a`. The float arithmetic order is fixed, so the result is deterministic.
   - `residual` is the max `|x − (a + S x)|` after the last sweep. If `residual > 1e-6 × max(1, max|x|)`, emit a `ledger.invariant.failed` ops alert `LOOKTHROUGH_NOT_CONVERGED` (non-fatal: the cycle limit should make it impossible).
   - `lookThroughNav(e) = toMoney(x_e)` in e's reporting ccy.
   - Results are memoized per `(root, tick)` in the runtime; the memo is dropped when any member's accounts change.
4. **Effective ownership.** `effectiveOwnership(H, X)` solves `y_j = S[j][X] + Σ_k S[j][k] · y_k` over `lookThroughSet(rootOf(H))` with the same sweep count, and returns `y_H` (the sum over all paths of the products of economic shares, 03 §3.11.3).
5. **1580 values.**
   - `lookThroughValueOfHolding(read, holderId, entityId, tick)`:
     - a `biz`: the 09 valuation model mark (`effectiveMark(read, entityId)`, source MODEL_FUND/APPRAISAL published by 09 hourly, the "upgrades only 70% reflected" rule is 09's), falling back to `lookThroughNav`;
     - otherwise: `economicShare(holder, entity) × lookThroughNav(entity)`.
   - The `LookThroughFn` is installed into T03-13 by T03-28, so `valueAccount` of 1580 uses it.
   - `revalueInvestments` (P2 at HOUR, plus DAY_CLOSE): for each 1580 account in sorted order, call `postRevaluation(ctx, a, value, 'LED_APPRAISAL', 'PNL')` (T03-14). 1580 is a financial investment, so it goes through P&L (03 §3.9.4).
6. **Circular-ownership guard** (`checkAcquisition`, 09 calls it in P1 on every share acquisition). Let `s = economicShare` the buyer would have after the acquisition. Compute `maxCycleProduct`:
   - the maximum, over directed paths issuer → … → buyer in the current graph, of the product of economic shares;
   - found by max-product Dijkstra (weights `−log(share)`, all shares in (0, 1]), in sorted order with deterministic tie-break;
   - multiplied by `s`.
   If that product is `>= params.num('cycle_max_product')`, return `{ ok: false, code: 'LED_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP' }`; 09 rejects with this G9 code (03 calls it `REJECT_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP`). The limit keeps `S` strictly contractive (spectral radius < 1). Example: B owns 70% of A and A buys 60% of B: the product is 0.6 × 0.7 = 0.42 < 0.95, which is allowed, and the look-through converges (03 §8 edge 7).
7. **Recompute** (`recomputeOwnership`, P13 after `commitTick`):
   - Read the `led.owndirty` issuers, plus entities registered this tick. Expand to the issuers whose holders' control may change (descendants of the touched issuers' holders' roots), then run `computeControl` on that set and on what depends on it.
   - For each entity whose `ownerId`, `rootOwnerId` or `perimeter` changed, update the entity row and the whole controlled subtree's `rootOwnerId`. When the perimeter changed, call `adjustPemForPerimeterChange`: for each ccy, compute `m` = the entity's PEM-counted balances (1001 all subs, 1010, 1050, 1070); `led.money.PEM += m` (entering PLAYER) or `−= m` (leaving). This is not a flow (03 §3.5.5 counts crossings by entries only), so T03-11's full day-close recompute still matches.
   - Clear `led.owndirty`.
   - Perimeter and control changes take effect for entries from the next tick. The money effect of this tick's entries was computed with the perimeters at commit.
8. **INV-BS-004** (`INV_BS_004`, TICK FATAL, 20 §3.1; covers 03 I20). The checks are incremental over the dirty issuers and the entities changed this tick, and run in full at day close:
   - (a) following `controllerId` from any entity ends within `n` steps at an entity without a controller: no cycle;
   - (b) `rootOwnerId` equals that terminal, and `ownerId === controllerId ?? displayOwnerId ?? id`;
   - (c) at most one controller per entity: `computeControl` on the issuer reproduces the stored row;
   - (d) `perimeter === perimeterFor(entity)`, which matches the root;
   - (e) for every share asset, Σ holder qty (1500/1510/1570/1560 holders, excluding the issuer's treasury) equals `−qty(issuer 3100 or 2320)`, and equals `outstanding` of the `SHARE_CLASS` fact when that fact exists (voting shares sum to shares outstanding);
   - (f) the terminal's kind is one of chr, ali, npc, gov, sys, or an uncontrolled NPC-core entity (cmp, bnk, ins, fnd, spv, exc, ccp, rat, cbk, reg, crt, hh, frm, row), which 03 §3.11.2 makes its own root. 20's wording ("ending at a chr, npc, gov or sys") is narrower than 03 §3.11.2. The module rule wins, and the gap is reported.
   Each failure is `ctx.fail('INV-BS-004', { entityId, sub: 'a'..'f' })`.

## §1 Purpose

The ledger is the game's first law: **every unit of money and every unit of every asset exists on exactly one balance sheet, and moves only by a posting with a reason code**. This module defines:

1. the entity kinds and the balance sheet each one carries;
2. the full chart of accounts;
3. the double-entry posting model, its standard templates (pay, mint, burn, lend, revalue, FX) and the payment rail across settlement cash, bank deposits and partitions;
4. the complete reason-code catalog of the game, each code in exactly one class (FAUCET, SINK, TRANSFER, CREDIT_CREATE, CREDIT_DESTROY, REVALUE);
5. money aggregates (M0, M2, player-economy money and wealth), the faucet and sink inventory with target ratios, the **Stabilizer** PI controller, the **Money Supply Index (MSI)** and the **Net Worth Index (NWI)**;
6. valuation (marking) rules for every asset type, including illiquid ones;
7. consolidation of ownership chains, minority interests and intercompany elimination;
8. taxes in the five blocs and the Isla Serena free zone;
9. financial statements generated for every entity;
10. insolvency and bankruptcy accounting;
11. the invariants asserted every tick.

Design stance: real accounting where it creates gameplay (balance sheets, consolidation, taxes, credit creation, insolvency), simplification only where it would be invisible (no accrual-vs-cash tax timing nuances beyond those listed, no lease accounting standards). The ledger is also the evidence base for courts (13), the explanation base for "Why it moved" (18) and the data base for the Truth Machine (20).

#### §3.11.1 The ownership graph

The graph has an edge for every holding of voting shares (1500 of a listed cmp, 1570 of an unlisted cmp, 1580 controlled entities, fund units carry no votes, bank and insurer shares as cmp shares). `biz` and `prop` are always 100% owned by one entity (`ownerId`), so they are leaves. #### §3.11.2 Control

Entity H **controls** issuer X if, counting votes held directly by H plus votes held by entities H controls (recursively, evaluated to a fixed point in sorted order), H has:
1. more than 50% of votes; or
2. at least `PARAM.control_defacto_min` (DEFAULT 30%) of votes and at least twice the votes of the next largest holder group, and board control under 09's governance rules (DEFAULT de facto control rule, Mathis may override); or
3. control by contract: fund managers do **not** control their funds for consolidation (custody rule, 08); SPV sponsors control their SPV only if they hold the majority of the equity tranche.

Every entity has at most one controller. `ownerId` = direct controller (or the largest holder when no one controls, for display only); `rootOwnerId` = end of the control chain. An entity with no controller has itself as root and belongs to the NPC core unless it is a chr or ali.

#### §3.11.3 Effective ownership

`eff(H, X)` = sum over all ownership paths from H to X of the product of economic shares along the path (economic share = holder's fraction of the issuer's residual economics after preferences, 09). Computed with the same linear system as §3.11.4.


<!-- excerpt 03§3.11.4 -->
**[03 §3.11.4]** (from spec file 03-LEDGER-AND-ACCOUNTING.md)

#### §3.11.4 Look-through NAV (the anti-duplication algorithm)

For a set of entities `G` (all entities reachable from a root through any holdings that are not valued at market):

```
For each e in G (sorted by canonical order):
   a_e = Σ marked external assets of e  − Σ liabilities of e
         where "external" excludes holdings of shares in other members of G
         and excludes intercompany receivables/payables with members of G (they are included through the members' own books)
   listed holdings in entities outside G are included in a_e at board value (§3.10.3)
Solve  x = a + S · x   where S[e][j] = economic share of j held by e (unlisted holdings within G)
   by Gauss–Seidel in canonical order for exactly PARAM.lookthrough_iterations (DEFAULT 64) sweeps
NAV(e) = x_e
```

Intercompany balances: a loan from member A to member B appears as an asset in `a_A` and a liability in `a_B`; because B's NAV flows to A in proportion to ownership, the loan nets correctly (minority holders of B bear their share). This is why internal payments transfer money between owned entities but never create group value (Bible I06).

Validity rule (enforced by 09 on any share acquisition): an acquisition that would create an ownership cycle whose product of economic shares is ≥ `PARAM.cycle_max_product` (DEFAULT 0.95) is rejected (`REJECT_CIRCULAR_OWNERSHIP`). This keeps `S` strictly contractive so the iteration converges well inside 64 sweeps.

Shares of a member held by another member of the same group (cross-holdings within a group) are **treasury shares** for the group's consolidated statements; the look-through system handles their value without double counting.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment rail with insufficient default account but funds elsewhere**: rail order §3.3.4; holds were placed on the actual source in P1.
2. **Bank fails while a player's payment from its deposit is in flight**: the bank's status changes at day close (07); payments already held in P1 of tick N settle in N; from N+1 deposits are frozen until resolution (07) and holds on them are rejected.
3. **FX translation of a character's books when a currency crashes**: NWrank falls immediately with the intraday FX mark; tax bases use the acquisition-day rates.
4. **A dividend split among 1,000,001 holders**: `allocate` guarantees exact totals; holders with zero rounding receive nothing; the issuer pays exactly the declared total.
5. **Stock split during an open order**: 05 adjusts orders; ledger posts `EQ_SPLIT` changing qty, lots split proportionally (basis per share divided).
6. **Wash sale across a character and its own company**: related parties, rule applies.
7. **Ownership cycle attempt** (A buys 60% of B while B owns 70% of A): product 0.42 < 0.95, allowed; look-through converges. A attempt to reach 100%/100% is rejected by 09 using `cycle_max_product`.
8. **A biz whose owner cannot fund its cash shortfall**: biz accrues payables; if unpaid past grace it enters review; a biz itself cannot enter insolvency separately (it is part of its owner): its default is the owner's default.
9. **Elastic sector 1009 very negative**: allowed by design; 04 monitors sector budgets; no invariant on 1009.
10. **Tax due while assets are frozen by a court**: tax accrues; late interest suspended while a court freeze blocks payment (13 provides the freeze flag).
11. **Year end inside a halt**: the year ends at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4).
12. **Restricted cash and a fresh start in the same tick as a fine**: fines cannot be paid from restricted cash; they become payable (and garnishable later).
13. **Character changes residency mid-year**: income before the change taxed by the old bloc, after by the new one; brackets prorated by days of residency.
14. **NWrank of a character who controls a listed company with no trades for 5 game days**: mark falls back to fundamental value, STALE haircut, control cap still applies.
15. **Escrow held for a contract when the depositor goes bankrupt**: escrow remains with the CCP; contract terms decide release (15); the estate's claim on the escrow is subject to the contract.
16. **Central-bank QE buying bonds from a player fund**: `CBK_ASSET_PURCHASE` with the fund receiving settlement cash; M0_cb rises; PEM rises (the fund is in P) while M0_sys is unchanged.
17. **Rounding of percentage fees on tiny trades**: `mulRate` half-even; a fee may round to 0 on a $0.10 trade; minimum fees are 05's decision.
18. **Negative interest rates** (04 may set them): accrual codes handle negative amounts by swapping debit and credit (amounts are always positive in postings).

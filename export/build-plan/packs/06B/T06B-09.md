## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Reference entities (§3.17.1): every issuer (corporates, NPC banks, player companies that issued bonds, the 5 sovereigns) with ≥ $100M bonds outstanding; reference obligation = most liquid senior unsecured bond (by 05 ADV, ties by amount outstanding, then id); sovereign = its 10y. Standard coupon 100 bp if IG at listing (`FundsPort06.isInvestmentGrade`), else 500 bp. Maturities 1, 3, 5, 7, 10 game years from the latest quarter-end roll date; premium dates quarter-end Fridays at t = 719. CDS are OTC RFQ products (cleared at CCP_CDS), traded by notional; `CdsContract` rows in `ast.drv_cds`.
2. Pricing (§3.17.2): `s_fair` = T06A-10 spread for the issuer's senior unsecured without the liquidity premium (`10,000·h·LGD`) + basis `b_e` (bps), `b` updated hourly as a discrete OU with `dt = 1/(364·24)`: `b ← b·e^{−θdt} + 15·sqrt(1 − e^{−2θdt})·z`, `θ = 4/year` (decision: the spec gives only mean and scale), `z` from `rng('ast.credit_event', entityId, tick)` normal. `h = s_fair/(1 − R)`, `R = 0.40`. `PV_prem = c·Σ τ_k e^{−(r+h)t_k}` + accrued-on-default term `c·Σ ∫_{t_{k−1}}^{t_k} (t − t_{k−1}) h e^{−(r+h)t} dt` (closed form); `PV_prot = (1 − R)·h/(r + h)·(1 − e^{−(r+h)T})`; upfront = `PV_prot − PV_prem(c)` (paid by the buyer when positive, at trade execution via the RFQ product). `r` = flat ois of the currency at the maturity. Marks hourly (`cdsMarkStep`); `registerModelValuer('CDS', …)` returns the contract's PV for the protection buyer per unit notional.
3. Who may trade (§3.17.3): buy protection LIC3, sell protection LIC4, cleared at CCP_CDS; naked protection allowed; group net sold ≤ 25% of reference debt outstanding (`checkCdsNetSold`); restricted cash cannot buy protection (T06A-02).
4. Insider rule (§3.17.6, X6): `cdsGate` rejects BUY_PROTECTION when the buyer's beneficial-owner group controls ≥ 20% of the reference entity (03/09 control), or is its clearing member or lender of last resort (07B) → `AST_CDS_INSIDER` at P1. `freezeOnControl` hourly: if a protection buyer later becomes a controller, its contracts get `frozen = true`; on a credit event while frozen, no payout is made: the amount is held (`HoldApi`) and `openReview('CDS_INSIDER')` is requested from 13; also emits `mkt.surveillance.signal` SEALED.
5. Credit events (§3.17.4): `detectCreditEvents` reads the day's committed events mechanically — BANKRUPTCY: 09 filing (`onBankruptcyFiled`); FAILURE_TO_PAY: a bond default with trigger missed payment beyond grace and amount ≥ $1M (`mkt.bond.default`); RESTRUCTURING: sovereign or bank distressed exchange / bail-in reducing principal or coupon (04 terms via T06A-10, 07 bail-in); GOVERNMENTAL_INTERVENTION: bank resolution (07 §3.10). There is no vote and no player-influenceable input (X7; an attempt is 12/13's business). Determination published at t = 719 of the event day (`mkt.cds.creditEvent` PUBLIC `{entityId, type, determinationTick, auctionTick}`); a `CreditEvent` row; contracts on the entity stop accruing premium (accrued premium to the event date is paid by buyers at settlement).
6. Settlement auction: 2 game days after determination; `R_final` = 30-tick VWAP of the reference obligation on the DISTRESSED venue over t = 270–299 plus its DISTRESSED call auction at t = 300 (T06A-06: `distressedVwap` combined with the auction print, volume-weighted), divided by 100; no trade → 09's (or 04's for sovereigns) recovery estimate. At P13 of the auction tick sellers pay `notional·(1 − R_final)` via `ccp_CDS` to buyers (`DRV_CDS_CREDIT_EVENT`); all contracts on the entity SETTLED; `mkt.cds.auction` PUBLIC `{entityId, Rfinal, source}`. Index: `onIndexCreditEvent` pays `notional/40·(1 − R)` and removes the name.
7. Succession (§3.17.5): on a merger of the reference entity, contracts follow the successor that assumes ≥ 75% of the bonds; on a split where ≥ 25% goes to several successors, contracts split pro rata (notional `allocate` by bond share). Edge 19: a reference entity taken private keeps its contracts; PD via the unlisted method (T06A-10).
8. GX Credit 40: 40 most liquid corporate reference entities (by bond ADV), equal weight, 5-year, new series every 2 game quarters (old series keep trading); credit dealers (§6 item 5) make markets with an inventory limit (`SPEC_CONSTANTS_06.cdsIndexDealerLimit`) and widen by `(1 + 2·fear)`; single-name CDS go through RFQ with `npcQuote`.
9. Novation (`mkt.cds.notifyNovation`, §3.16.5 shared): only bilateral trades (CDS or swaps from T06B-08); requires the remaining party's consent (`consent` handshake: the counterparty must submit the same intent with `toId` within 3 ticks); the trade's party id is replaced; cleared trades are rejected (they are closed by offsetting at the CCP).

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[06 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module defines **every tradeable thing in the world** and the rules that are specific to its asset class: what an instrument is (fields), what it is worth (its fundamental value, which 05's price model pulls toward), how it pays (dividends, coupons, funding, settlement), how it is created and destroyed (listing, series generation, issuance, expiry, default, delisting), who may trade it (licenses), how it is marked for net worth, and every class-specific event (earnings, splits, credit events, depegs, auctions).

Division of labour, binding:
- **05** owns *how* prices move tick to tick (the return equation, GARCH, jumps, factor model, batch auction, NPC market makers, square-root impact, halts). 06 owns *what* each asset's fundamental value `V_i` is, its class parameters (`PriceParams`, §3.21), and everything that is not the generic order book: OTC and RFQ fills (P6), DeFi venues, primary auctions, collectible auctions, option exercise, futures settlement, perp funding, coupons and corporate actions.
- **07** owns margin, collateral haircuts, securities lending, repo and the Clearing House. 06 tags each instrument with its `marginClass`, `collateralClass` and `clearing` service.
- **08** owns ETF creation and redemption, rating agencies, securitization tranches and fund units. 06 defines ETF and index instruments and their NAV.
- **09** owns company decisions (dividend amount, buybacks, issuance, M&A, bankruptcy, IPOs). 06 executes the security-level consequences.

Design laws (from 01 §0 and V3 T1–T3, kept):
1. Every price has a cause a player could have seen or inferred. Each class's `V_i` is a function of world state, never a random walk.
2. Parities hold by construction where they hold in reality (forward = CIP, put-call parity via NPC arbitrage, ETF ≈ NAV, cross FX from legs). Residual arbitrage after fees is ≤ fees.
3. Each class differs where the difference creates a decision (MASTER-BIBLE T02): fundamentals, sentiment, liquidity, financing, sessions and liquidation rules.
4. Defaults, depegs and failures are **mechanical outcomes of balance sheets**, never dice rolls with a fixed probability.

**Status of numbers:** every `PARAM.*` value in this module, and every choice tagged `DEFAULT`, is `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`. Locked decisions L1–L15 (00) and the 00 time model are not defaults. The full list is in `_notes/06-notes.md`.

#### §3.17.1 Reference entities and standard terms
- Single-name CDS exist on every issuer with ≥ $100M of bonds outstanding (corporates, NPC banks, player companies that issued bonds, the 5 sovereigns). Reference obligation: the issuer's most liquid senior unsecured bond (sovereign: the 10y).
- Standard coupons 100 bps (IG at listing) or 500 bps (HY), paid quarterly on quarter-end Fridays at t = 719; standard maturities 1, 3, 5, 7, 10 game years, rolling at quarter ends. The upfront makes up the difference between the coupon and the fair spread.
- Index: **GX Credit 40** = 40 most liquid corporate reference entities, equal weight, 5-year, new series every 2 game quarters; a credit event removes the name with a payout of `notional/40 · (1 − R)`. Tranches of the index are 08's.


<!-- excerpt 06§3.17.2 -->
**[06 §3.17.2]** (from spec file 06-ASSET-CLASSES.md)

#### §3.17.2 Pricing
Flat hazard from the fair spread `s_fair` (from §3.8.2 without the liquidity premium, plus a CDS-bond basis `b ~ OU(0, 15 bps)`, RNG stream per entity): `h = s_fair / (1 − R)`, `R = 0.40` standard. Premium leg `PV_prem = c · Σ τ_k e^{−(r+h) t_k}` (+ accrued on default), protection leg `PV_prot = (1 − R) · ∫ h e^{−(r+h)t} dt` (closed form). Upfront (paid by buyer) = `PV_prot − PV_prem(c)`. Marks hourly.

**[06 §3.17.3]** (from spec file 06-ASSET-CLASSES.md)

#### §3.17.3 Who may trade
Buy protection: LIC3; sell protection: LIC4; both cleared at CCP_CDS. Naked protection is allowed (it is how a credit view is expressed), subject to §3.17.6.

**[06 §3.17.4]** (from spec file 06-ASSET-CLASSES.md)

#### §3.17.4 Credit events
- Types: BANKRUPTCY (09 filing), FAILURE_TO_PAY (§3.8.3 beyond grace, ≥ $1M), RESTRUCTURING (sovereigns and banks: a distressed exchange or bail-in that reduces principal or coupon, 04/07), GOVERNMENTAL_INTERVENTION (bank resolution under 07 §3.10).
- Determination: the NPC **Credit Determinations Committee** applies these definitions mechanically to the event log and publishes the determination at t = 719 of the event day (PUBLIC). There is no discretion and no vote that a player can influence (bribing it is impossible because it is an engine rule; 12/13 may still prosecute attempts as fraud).
- Settlement auction: 2 game days after determination, the final price `R_final` = 30-tick VWAP of the reference obligation on the DISTRESSED venue over t = 270–299 plus its DISTRESSED call auction at t = 300; if no trade, 09's (or 04's) recovery estimate. Protection sellers pay `notional · (1 − R_final)` to buyers at P13 of the auction tick; all contracts on the entity are SETTLED.


<!-- excerpt 06§3.17.5 -->
**[06 §3.17.5]** (from spec file 06-ASSET-CLASSES.md)

#### §3.17.5 Succession
Merger of the reference entity: contracts follow the successor (the entity assuming ≥ 75% of the bonds); split ≥ 25% to several successors: contracts split pro rata.


<!-- excerpt 06§3.17.6 -->
**[06 §3.17.6]** (from spec file 06-ASSET-CLASSES.md)

#### §3.17.6 Insider rule (empty-creditor counter)
A beneficial-owner group that controls (≥ 20%, 09) the reference entity, or that is its clearing member or lender of last resort, cannot buy protection on it (rejected at P1). If any protection buyer becomes a controller later, its protection is frozen (no payout) pending a 13 review if a credit event occurs while it controls.

## §6 NPC behaviour

All NPC behaviour runs in P4 (orders) or P6 (DeFi blocks, RFQ responses) with RNG streams `rng('npc06', role, assetId, tick)`; capacities scale with activity-weighted players `N_a` (01 §14.1) so a quiet world does not leave free money.

1. **Options dealers** (3 NPC dealer desks, part of NPC banks in 07 §6): quote every listed series from §3.13.3; carry inventory up to `vegaCapacity = PARAM.opt_vega_cap_per_und` (by underlying: index $2M vega per vol point, single stock $200k, crypto $300k, × N_a scale); delta-hedge per §3.13.6; widen ×2 and halve capacity when F > 40.
2. **Basis and parity arbitrageurs:** each tick, for each (future, spot), (ETF, basket) handled with 08, (call, put, forward) put-call parity, (cross, legs) FX, (CEX, pool) DeFi: trade when the gap exceeds total fees + 0.10%, capacity `PARAM.npc_arb_cap_frac = 0.02` of the asset's ADV per tick. They never take directional risk beyond 1 tick.
3. **Commodity producers and consumers** (22 roster, 16 decision models): producers hedge 50% of the next 6 months' expected output by selling futures when the curve is ≥ 5% above cost; consumers (airlines, refiners, food firms) buy 30% of next-quarter needs; both act at day open.
4. **Primary dealers:** §3.7.6 bidding curve.
5. **Credit dealers:** CDS RFQ responses; GX Credit 40 market making with inventory limit; widen in fear.
6. **Crypto:** NPC miners sell AUC block rewards uniformly through the day; NPC stakers stake 60% of LTX/MRD supply; NPC LPs keep 80% of seed liquidity in launch pools and withdraw 30% when fear > 0.6 (liquidity vanishes when needed, 01 §7.2); NPC keepers (§3.12.4); NPC meme launcher (§3.11.4); Vesta and Mirage governance as specified.
7. **Collectors:** §3.18.3.
8. **Index funds** (16 aggregate, 08 instruments): trade at rebalance closing auctions and after index eligibility changes (rating cliffs) within 5 game days.

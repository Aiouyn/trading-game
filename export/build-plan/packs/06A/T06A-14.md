## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Categories (R39: 22 §4.14 wins on names and counts): WATCH, CAR, ART, WINE, YACHT, JET are indexed (`ast.coll_category` rows with `indexLevel` 1,000); SNEAKER has no index. Parameters: 06 §3.18.1 defaults (a, b, σ) WATCH (0.6, 0.4, 0.12), ART (0.8, 0.3, 0.10), CAR = 06's CLASSIC_CAR (0.5, 0.3, 0.12), WINE (0.3, 0.2, 0.08); YACHT and JET have no 06 row and use the CAR row (decision recorded in `_unit.md`). 06's JEWEL, RARE_BOOK, MEMORABILIA do not exist in 22 and are not created.
2. Index (DAY_CLOSE, P2): `g_top1 = ports.social.top1RealNwGrowth(day)`, `g_prestige = ports.status.prestigeSpendGrowth(day)` (trailing 28 days, 14), fashion OU with half-life 182 days and stationary sd 0.15; draw order per category in sorted id order: `z_fashion = rng.normal()`, then `ε = rng.normal()` from `ctx.rng('ast.collectible:' + categoryId)`. Record `indexAtIssue` for each LIMITED/UNIQUE catalog item the first time it is created.
3. Appraisal (§3.18.2): MASS `V = price_new·(1 − d0)·e^{−0.08·ageYears}·cond(c)` (d0 per item from 22/14 in [0.10, 0.40]); LIMITED `V = baseValue·rarity·(I_c(t)/I_c(issue))·cond(c)·prov`; UNIQUE same with rarity ≥ 3 and heritage `× min(1 + 0.02·ageYears, 1.5)`; SNEAKER (no index, 22): `V = (last auction or dealer sale price of the same catalogItemId, else baseValue·rarity)·cond(c)·prov`. ageYears = game years since `acquiredTick` of the first provenance entry. Owners have no intent that writes `appraisal` (06#40); consecutive private sales between linked accounts (15) are excluded as inputs (X16: SNEAKER last-sale input ignores sales where buyer and seller share a beneficial-owner group).
4. Condition decay per year (§3.18.2): ART 0.5, WINE 0 if VAULT else 4, CAR 3 unless `ports.status.serviced(item)`, SNEAKER 5, others 1; HOME storage adds +1/yr (raises 12 exposure, which 12 reads). Daily decrement `decay/364`, floor 0.
5. Sales: one per category per game week (Saturday for ART/WATCH/CAR, Sunday for others; 22's categories), lots close in sequence `t = 120 + 10·i`. Consign (`mkt.collect.consign`): owner, item not PLEDGED/STOLEN/UNVERIFIED-at-reputable-house, up to 1 game day before the sale, `reserve ≤ estimateHigh`; estimates = appraisal × (0.85, 1.15); item flag ON_AUCTION. Withdraw (`mkt.collect.withdrawLot`): before 1 game day to the sale, fee 2% of low estimate (`COLL_AUCTION_FEE`, SINK `burn`, sub 'WITHDRAW'); seizure-ordered items are withdrawn without fee (edge 18, `ports.law.seizureOrder`).
6. Bid (`mkt.collect.bid`, P1): LIC0; reject consignor and anyone in its beneficial-owner group or linked groups (X17, 06#41); `amount ≥ validBidFloor`; hold `ctx.holds.placeCash(bidder, ccy, amount·1.15, closeTick + 1, lotId)`; the previous high bidder's hold is released when outbid. In P6 each tick: the highest valid bid stands, ties by earlier seal order (orderKey) then `fnv1a32(bidderId + lotId)`. Soft close: a new high bid when `closeTick − tick ≤ 30` extends `closeTick += 30`, at most 10 extensions. NPC collectors (06 §6 item 7): when a lot opens, `n = 2 + roundHalfEven(4·I/I_0·(1 − fear))` NPC bidders, valuations `appraisal·(0.85 + 0.35·u)` (one `uOpen` each from `ast.collectible:<categoryId>`), capped by `ports.world.npcWealth(category)`/n; they bid the minimum valid increment each tick while below their valuation.
7. Close: SOLD if high bid ≥ reserve, else PASSED. Fees on SOLD: buyer premium 15% and seller fee 12%; NPC house (`HV`) → `COLL_AUCTION_FEE` SINK (`burn`) from buyer and seller; player houses (09) → `pay` to the house entity (TRANSFER). Settlement: player buyer ↔ player seller `COLL_AUCTION_SETTLE` (swap of the item: item `ownerId` change + cash `pay`); NPC buyer of a player's lot → `COLL_SALE_TO_NPC` (FAUCET, `elasticIn` from `hh` of the seller's home city); player buyer of an NPC-consigned lot → `COLL_BUY_FROM_NPC` (SINK, `elasticOut`). Emit `mkt.collect.result` PUBLIC `{lotId, itemId, price, buyerLabel, sellerLabel}` with rotating labels unless the parties opted in (L11, D109). Edge 16: if a court order seizes held cash, `voidBidOnSeizure` and the next-highest valid bid wins at its own amount.
8. Dealer (`mkt.collect.sellToDealer`): owner, unencumbered; LIMITED/UNIQUE at 70% of appraisal, MASS at 90% of V; `COLL_SALE_TO_NPC`. Storage (`mkt.collect.store`, allowed under Held): VAULT fee per year ART 0.5%, WINE 1%, CAR 2%, others 0.5% of appraisal at DAY_CLOSE posted with `LIFE_UPKEEP` (14's storage code) to the vault operator; HOME free. Pledge: LIMITED/UNIQUE pledgeable at 50% LTV (07 reads `appraisal`, flag PLEDGED set by 07). Edge 17: stolen/destroyed → flag, mark 0 or 08's insured value.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[06 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module defines **every tradeable thing in the world** and the rules that are specific to its asset class: what an instrument is (fields), what it is worth (its fundamental value, which 05's price model pulls toward), how it pays (dividends, coupons, funding, settlement), how it is created and destroyed (listing, series generation, issuance, expiry, default, delisting), who may trade it (licenses), how it is marked for net worth, and every class-specific event (earnings, splits, credit events, depegs, auctions).

Division of labour, binding:
- **05** owns *how* prices move tick to tick (the return equation, GARCH, jumps, factor model, batch auction, NPC market makers, square-root impact, halts). 06 owns *what* each asset's fundamental value `V_i` is, its class parameters (`PriceParams`, §3.21), and everything that is not the generic order book: OTC and RFQ fills (P6), DeFi venues, primary auctions, collectible auctions, option exercise, futures settlement, perp funding, coupons and corporate actions.
- **07** owns margin, collateral haircuts, securities lending, repo and the Clearing House. 06 tags each instrument with its `marginClass`, `collateralClass` and `clearing` service.
- **08** owns ETF creation and redemption, rating agencies, securitization tranches and fund units. 06 defines ETF and index instruments and their NAV.
- **09** owns company decisions (dividend amount, buybacks, issuance, M&A, bankruptcy, IPOs). 06 executes the security-level consequences.

Design laws (from 01 §0 and V3 T1–T3, kept):
1. Every price has a cause a player could have seen or inferred. Each class's `V_i` is a function of world state, never a random walk.
2. Parities hold by construction where they hold in reality (forward = CIP, put-call parity via NPC arbitrage, ETF ≈ NAV, cross FX from legs). Residual arbitrage after fees is ≤ fees.
3. Each class differs where the difference creates a decision (MASTER-BIBLE T02): fundamentals, sentiment, liquidity, financing, sessions and liquidation rules.
4. Defaults, depegs and failures are **mechanical outcomes of balance sheets**, never dice rolls with a fixed probability.

**Status of numbers:** every `PARAM.*` value in this module, and every choice tagged `DEFAULT`, is `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`. Locked decisions L1–L15 (00) and the 00 time model are not defaults. The full list is in `_notes/06-notes.md`.

### §3.18 Collectibles

#### §3.18.1 Category index
Per category, at day close:
```
Δln I_c = a_c · g_top1 + b_c · g_prestige + F_c + σ_c · ε / sqrt(364)
g_top1     = daily log growth of top-1% player real NW (15 aggregates)
g_prestige = daily log growth of total Prestige spending, trailing 28 game days (14)
F_c        : fashion cycle, OU with half-life 182 game days, stationary sd 0.15
defaults (a, b, σ): WATCH (0.6, 0.4, 0.12), ART (0.8, 0.3, 0.10), CLASSIC_CAR (0.5, 0.3, 0.12), WINE (0.3, 0.2, 0.08),
                    SNEAKER (0.4, 0.8, 0.30), JEWEL (0.5, 0.3, 0.08), RARE_BOOK (0.3, 0.1, 0.06), MEMORABILIA (0.4, 0.5, 0.20)
```

#### §3.18.2 Item value (appraisal)
```
MASS:      V = price_new · (1 − d0) · e^{−d1 · ageYears} · cond(c)          d0 ∈ [0.10, 0.40] by item (01 §3.10), d1 = 0.08
LIMITED:   V = baseValue · rarity · (I_c(t) / I_c(issue)) · cond(c) · prov
UNIQUE:    same as LIMITED with rarity ≥ 3 and heritage bonus × (1 + 0.02 · ageYears, cap 1.5) (14 heritage growth)
cond(c)  = 0.4 + 0.6 · (c / 100)^1.5
prov     = 1.0 verified chain; 0.6 if any link UNVERIFIED; 0.0 tradeable value at a reputable house if STOLEN
```
Condition decays `conditionDecayPerYear` (ART 0.5, WINE 0 if VAULT else 4, CLASSIC_CAR 3 unless serviced via 14, SNEAKER 5, others 1) and is restored by 14's maintenance actions.

#### §3.18.3 Auctions (house: Halberd & Vane, NPC; player auction houses from 09 use the same engine)
- Each category holds one sale per game week (Saturday for ART/WATCH/CLASSIC_CAR, Sunday for others); lots close in sequence, one every 10 ticks from t = 120.
- Consignment `mkt.collect.consign` up to 1 game day before the sale; reserve ≤ high estimate; estimates = appraisal × (0.85, 1.15).
- Bidding `mkt.collect.bid`: a bid is valid if ≥ max(reserve, highBid × 1.05) and the bidder's available cash ≥ bid × 1.15 (held in P1 until outbid). Highest valid bid at the end of a tick stands; ties by earlier seal order, then hash.
- Soft close: a new high bid in the last 30 ticks extends `closeTick` by 30 ticks, at most 10 extensions.
- Fees (01 §3.10): buyer premium 15% and seller fee 12% to the house; for the NPC house these are SINK (`COLL_AUCTION_FEE`). Player houses keep fees (TRANSFER).
- NPC collectors (16): per lot, a deterministic set of `n = 2 + round(4 · I_c / I_c,0 · (1 − fear))` NPC bidders with private valuations `appraisal · U(0.85, 1.20)`, budget-limited by 16's NPC wealth, bid in increments up to their valuation.
- Results PUBLIC (item, price); buyer and seller shown only by rotating label unless they opt in (L11). Prestige (14) for the buyer is computed by 14.

#### §3.18.4 Dealer and private sales
Instant dealer sale `mkt.collect.sellToDealer`: LIMITED/UNIQUE at 70% of appraisal, MASS at 90% of V (01 15% NPC haircut spirit). Private sales between players use 15's contract engine (2% P2P tax, off-market flag at > 10% from appraisal).

#### §3.18.5 Storage, insurance, collateral
Storage fee per year: VAULT `storageFeePerYearFrac` (ART 0.5%, WINE 1%, CLASSIC_CAR 2%, others 0.5%) to the vault operator; HOME storage free but raises 12 physical-intrusion exposure and condition decay. Insurance is 08's. LIMITED/UNIQUE items are pledgeable for loans at 50% LTV of appraisal (07 §3.4).

## §6 NPC behaviour

All NPC behaviour runs in P4 (orders) or P6 (DeFi blocks, RFQ responses) with RNG streams `rng('npc06', role, assetId, tick)`; capacities scale with activity-weighted players `N_a` (01 §14.1) so a quiet world does not leave free money.

1. **Options dealers** (3 NPC dealer desks, part of NPC banks in 07 §6): quote every listed series from §3.13.3; carry inventory up to `vegaCapacity = PARAM.opt_vega_cap_per_und` (by underlying: index $2M vega per vol point, single stock $200k, crypto $300k, × N_a scale); delta-hedge per §3.13.6; widen ×2 and halve capacity when F > 40.
2. **Basis and parity arbitrageurs:** each tick, for each (future, spot), (ETF, basket) handled with 08, (call, put, forward) put-call parity, (cross, legs) FX, (CEX, pool) DeFi: trade when the gap exceeds total fees + 0.10%, capacity `PARAM.npc_arb_cap_frac = 0.02` of the asset's ADV per tick. They never take directional risk beyond 1 tick.
3. **Commodity producers and consumers** (22 roster, 16 decision models): producers hedge 50% of the next 6 months' expected output by selling futures when the curve is ≥ 5% above cost; consumers (airlines, refiners, food firms) buy 30% of next-quarter needs; both act at day open.
4. **Primary dealers:** §3.7.6 bidding curve.
5. **Credit dealers:** CDS RFQ responses; GX Credit 40 market making with inventory limit; widen in fear.
6. **Crypto:** NPC miners sell AUC block rewards uniformly through the day; NPC stakers stake 60% of LTX/MRD supply; NPC LPs keep 80% of seed liquidity in launch pools and withdraw 30% when fear > 0.6 (liquidity vanishes when needed, 01 §7.2); NPC keepers (§3.12.4); NPC meme launcher (§3.11.4); Vesta and Mirage governance as specified.
7. **Collectors:** §3.18.3.
8. **Index funds** (16 aggregate, 08 instruments): trade at rebalance closing auctions and after index eligibility changes (rating cliffs) within 5 game days.

## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
Investors leave open-ended funds at a price fixed at the strike (08 §3.1.6, ruling D144): the gate fills a crowded day pro rata and carries the rest forward, swing pricing charges the cost of large flows to those who cause them, early exits pay a fee into the fund, and cash shortfalls become a payable that the engine settles by selling Level-1 positions through normal 05 orders, which is the real first-mover incentive that makes fund runs emergent. This task also owns the daily dealing orchestrator `dealDay` that every fund runs at the strike (strike → gate → swing → redemptions → subscriptions).

## Implementation steps
1. `fund.redeem` P1: Held does not block redemptions of a bankrupt estate (14 submits as SYSTEM channel); otherwise a Held actor is rejected (`FUND_HELD`). Exactly one of `units`/`amount`. Fund OPEN_ENDED and not CLOSED (`FUND_NOT_OPEN_ENDED`); dealing not SUSPENDED (requests may be queued while SUSPENDED: they wait, see step 3). `pickRedeemablePositions`: positions of the investor in this fund ordered by `(subscribedTick, seriesId)`, excluding units under lockup (`lockupUntilTick > tick`); insufficient unlocked units → `FUND_LOCKUP`; no units → `FUND_NO_UNITS`. Manager-stake units: the post-redemption stake value must stay ≥ max(`PARAM.fund_manager_stake_min_abs`, `PARAM.fund_manager_stake_min_pct × AUM`) unless the fund is WINDING_DOWN (`FUND_STAKE_FLOOR`, edge 2). In-kind only when the investor holds ≥ 10% of units outstanding (`FUND_IN_KIND_NOT_ALLOWED`). Core holders: `ctx.holds.placeQty(investor, fundId, units, dealingTick + 2, intentId)`. `amount` requests are converted to units at the strike (`unitsForAmount` with the series net NAV at `G_deal`), capped at the investor's unlocked units. Create `FundRequest` kind `REDEEM` with `dealingTick = dealingTickFor(tick, cutoff)`.
2. `dealDay(ctx, fundId)` at t = 719, P8, for every non-CLOSED fund in id order (ETF and MMF funds use their own dealing in T08-14 and T08-13; this function returns early for them): (a) `strike = strikeNav(ctx, fundId)`; if null and the fund is FUNDRAISING → `dealSubscriptions(ctx, fundId, PARAM.fund_initial_nav, 0)` and stop. (b) If status SUSPENDED: move every PENDING request's `dealingTick` to the next day and stop. (c) Redemption requests dealing today, including carried-forward remainders, sorted by `(submittedTick, reqId)`; value each at `G` (`units × net_s`). (d) `f = gateFraction(Σ value, nav, gate.maxRedeemPctNavPerDay, gate.enabled, isGateFree)`: `f = min(1, pct × nav / Σvalue)` when enabled and not gate-free. Every request fills `floor_to_1e-6(units × f)`; the remainder stays PENDING with `dealingTick` = next day and `carriedForward` = remainder units value; next day it is pro rata again with new requests (no priority). Emit `fund.gated` PUBLIC `{ fundId, reason: 'GATE', until }` when f < 1. (e) Swing: `netFlow = Σ pending subscription cash − Σ filled redemption value`; `s = swingFactor(netFlow, nav, estimatedLiquidationCost(strike.valuation.lines, |netFlow|/nav, cost), PARAM.fund_swing_threshold, PARAM.fund_swing_cap)`; `G_deal = G × (1 + s)`. (f) Redemptions pay at `G_deal` (`payRedemption`). (g) `aumAfter = nav − Σ redemption value at G_deal`; `dealSubscriptions(ctx, fundId, G_deal, aumAfter)`. (h) `checkPayableAge`.
3. `isGateFree`: true while `tick ≤ fundState.gateFreeUntilTick` (set by mandate/fee notice windows, breaker days, absent-manager freeze and wind-down notices; T08-08, T08-09).
4. `payRedemption` per request: for each picked position: `a = perfAccrualPerUnit(G_deal, H_s, hurdle_s, …)`; `payPerUnit = G_deal − a`; `gross = units × payPerUnit`; `crystallizeOnRedemption` pays `a × units` to the manager (08 §3.1.6 step 3); `fee = earlyExitFee(...)` = `earlyExitFeeRate × gross` when `tick − pos.subscribedTick < PARAM.fund_early_exit_days × 720` and not in a mandate/fee-notice window, else 0. Cash available = fund cash + `pbUndrawn` (draw from the PB only when cash is short: `pbDraw` for the gap). Core holder: `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(fundId, investor, fundId, units, toMoney(min(gross, available)), ccy, FLOW.REDEEM_SWAP, { holdId })` (the fund buys back and cancels its units), plus `ctx.ledger.pay(investor, fundId, toMoney(fee), ccy, FLOW.ANTI_DILUTION)` when fee > 0. Elastic hh holder: `ctx.ledger.elasticOut(fundId, hhId, toMoney(gross − fee), ccy, FLOW.REDEEM_ELASTIC)`. The units are cancelled at the strike even when cash is short: `Fund.unitsOutstanding −= units`, positions and series reduced. Shortfall `owed = gross − fee − paid` is added to `fundState.redemptionPayable` and recorded on the request (`owed` field: an addition to `FundRequest` like `holdId`), status PARTIAL, `payableSinceTick = tick` if unset; then `planLiquidation(ctx, fundId, owed, 'REDEMPTION_LIQUIDATION')`. Emit `fund.redemptionFilled` (PARTIES) `{ reqId, units, cash, gatedRemainder }`.
5. In-kind (step 6 of §3.1.6): the slice is `units / unitsOutstanding` of every Level-1 position, rounded down to lots (`getMarketSpec(...).lotSize`), delivered with `ctx.ledger.moveQty(fundId, investor, assetId, qty, bookValue, ccy, FLOW.IN_KIND)`; the rest of the redemption value is paid in cash per step 4.
6. `planLiquidation` (P8, consumed by 05 from the next tick through `FundsPort.forcedSales`): Level-1 long positions pro rata by value; for each asset `dailyCap = PARAM.fund_liquidation_adv_cap × advUnits`; per game hour the order quantity is `dailyCap × 30 / sessionTicks` (480 for exchange sessions, 720 for 24h products), rounded down to lots, until the asset's pro-rata share of the shortfall at the last price is covered; one `EngineOrder` row per (asset, game hour) with `pool 'LIQUIDATION'`, `accountId = fundId`, `tif 'TICK'`, `side 'SELL'`, `purpose`. Orders carry no limit (05 applies its market-order protection). Fills credit the fund through 05's normal settlement.
7. `settlePayables` every tick in P8: while the fund's available cash > 0 and owed requests exist, pay the oldest (`submittedTick`, `reqId`) first. The units were already bought back at the strike, so the deferred cash leg is a plain payment of an amount owed to a former holder: `ctx.ledger.pay(fundId, investor, amt, ccy, FLOW.DISTRIBUTION, { sub: 'REDEMPTION_PAYABLE', refs: [reqId] })` (03 `FND_DISTRIBUTION`, TRANSFER PAY; elastic holders: `elasticOut(..., FLOW.REDEEM_ELASTIC)`). Reduce `owed` and `redemptionPayable`; when a request's owed reaches 0 its status becomes FILLED. When the payable is fully paid, cancel the fund's remaining liquidation orders for that purpose.
8. `checkPayableAge`: if `redemptionPayable > 0` and `tick − payableSinceTick > PARAM.fund_redemption_max_delay_days × 720`: status `GATED`, emit `fund.gated` with reason `PAYABLE`; T08-09 applies the GATED effects. The amount owed was fixed at the strike: later price moves while selling are borne by remaining investors (no re-pricing).
9. Limited liability (§3.1.22): if `navBeforeFees ≤ 0` at the strike, `G` is 0, redemptions pay 0, the fund is put into WINDING_DOWN by T08-09 and any PB deficit is 07's default-waterfall loss: no posting ever debits an investor or the manager beyond their units.
10. Edge 28 (fund of funds redeeming from a gated underlying): the FoF's own payable simply waits for cash from the underlying (no special case); it may gate itself through step 8.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[08 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module is the buy-side and risk-transfer half of the institutional layer (V3 Part 3.5, 3.6, 3.8):

1. **Funds** let capital be pooled and managed under a mandate: players run hedge funds, long-only funds, funds of funds, credit funds, venture and private-equity funds, money-market funds, ETFs and (at Dynasty rank) sovereign wealth funds. Investors (players and NPC allocators) buy units at NAV. Track record, trust and betrayal are public (MASTER-BIBLE VI #2). Theft is impossible by construction; market abuse through a fund is possible and prosecutable (roadmap Q6, resolved §3.1.12).
2. **ETFs** are funds with listed units and a primary market. Authorized participants (APs) create and redeem baskets to keep price near NAV. ETF flows move the underlying; in a crisis, basket illiquidity opens real discounts.
3. **Securitization** turns pools of loans, mortgages, receivables and leases into tranches (senior, mezzanine, equity) sold to investors. Default correlation, underwriting standards and rating methodology together can produce a 2008-style event from rules, never from a script.
4. **Rating agencies** grade sovereigns, corporates, banks, insurers, funds and tranches. Grades move spreads and trip collateral, mandate and capital rules, so cliff effects create gameplay.
5. **Insurance and reinsurance** price and absorb operational, property, cyber, liability and catastrophe risk. Losses from storms, cyber pandemics and unrest propagate from policyholders to insurers, reinsurers and cat-bond holders.

Design rules: every flow is a ledger posting with a reason code (03); every price comes from a model a player can learn or from an auction; no actor gets private information (L11); every institution can fail by rule and leaves a public record.

#### §3.1.6 Redemptions

1. `fund.redeem { fundId, units | amount }`. Same dealing cutoff as §3.1.5. Units still under lockup are rejected.
2. Gate: if enabled, total redemptions dealing on one day are capped at `maxRedeemPctNavPerDay` (default 0.20) of NAV. If requests exceed the cap, every request is filled pro rata; the unfilled remainder is carried forward to the next dealing day with no priority over new requests (all pro rata again). Gate-free windows (mandate change notice, breaker, absent manager, wind-down notice) ignore the cap.
3. Payment per unit = `netNavPerUnit_s` at `G_deal`. The investor's series performance fee `a_s × units` is crystallized to the manager at the same time (§3.1.7).
4. Early exit: redemptions of units held fewer than `PARAM.fund_early_exit_days = 48` game days (2 real days) pay `earlyExitFeeRate` (default 0.01) into the fund.
5. Cash: payment is made from fund cash at the strike. If cash (plus undrawn PB credit, 07) is insufficient, the shortfall becomes a `redemptionPayable` liability and the engine generates liquidation orders for the fund in the next ticks (P4): pro rata across Level-1 positions, each order ≤ `PARAM.fund_liquidation_adv_cap = 0.20` of the asset's ADV per game day, sent through normal 05 order intents tagged `actor = fund engine`. The payable is settled as cash arrives, oldest first. If it is still unpaid after `PARAM.fund_redemption_max_delay_days = 1` game day, the fund status becomes `GATED` and §3.1.9 applies. The payment amount was fixed at the strike; later price moves while selling are borne by remaining investors (this is the real first-mover incentive that makes fund runs possible; the gate and swing pricing are the counters).
6. In-kind redemption: a redeeming investor holding ≥ 10% of the fund may request in-kind; the fund delivers a pro-rata slice of Level-1 positions (rounded to lots) plus cash for the rest.

**[08 §3.1.22]** (from spec file 08-FUNDS-STRUCTURED-INSURANCE.md)

#### §3.1.22 Leverage, prime brokerage and limited liability

Funds borrow only through a prime broker (07): cash margin loans and short borrows, cross-margined. Margin calls on a fund are met from fund cash; failing that 07 liquidates per its rules. Investors' loss is limited to their units: if a fund's equity goes negative after a PB liquidation, the deficit is a PB loss handled by 07's default waterfall, never charged to investors or the manager beyond their units (except via court restitution for abuse).

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Fund with zero units outstanding** (all redeemed except none): NAV per unit is undefined; the fund moves to WINDING_DOWN automatically and closes when positions are flat.
2. **Manager stake falls below 10% because the manager redeemed elsewhere?** Impossible: stake units cannot be redeemed below the floor. If losses push the absolute stake below $250,000, subscriptions pause; existing investors are unaffected.
3. **Investor's NW falls so the fund exceeds 25% of NW**: the cap applies only at subscription; no forced redemption.
4. **Investor goes bankrupt (14 fresh start)**: fund units are assets; 14's bankruptcy liquidates them through `fund.redeem` at the next strike (gate applies; the estate waits like anyone).

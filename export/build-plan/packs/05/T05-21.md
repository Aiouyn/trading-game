## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Risk (05 §3.17): input an account, entity or owner group (positions from 03 via the caller), horizon 1 game day or 1 game week, confidence 95/99%.
   - Historical VaR/ES: revalue current positions over up to 500 trading days of 05's stored daily closes (`dailyBars`); before the world has 250 days, prepend 22's genesis pre-history. Week horizon: overlapping 5-day returns. VaR = −quantile at (1 − conf); ES = −mean of losses beyond it.
   - Parametric: factor exposures × EWMA covariance (λ 0.94) of factor returns (`FactorState`) plus idiosyncratic variances; VaR = z × σ_P × √(horizon days); ES at 97.5% = σ_P φ(z₀.₉₇₅)/0.025.
   - Exposures: beta to GX, bloc and sector factors, rates DV01, credit CS01, FX by currency, commodity and crypto deltas, option greeks from 06 (via `ports.instruments`; 0 when 06 absent).
   - LVaR = VaR + Σ_i |notional_i| × (s_i + Y σ_d,i √(|q_i|/ADV_i)).
   - Stress: replays of past world events (16 names them; factor shock vectors recorded when the event ran, stored by T05-23's genesis/daily recorder in `mkt.factor` history), standard shocks (rates +200 bp, equities −20%, FX ±10%, spreads ×2, vol ×1.5) and custom factor shocks; revalue through loadings and 06 pricing.
   - Correlation under stress (RSK-04): portfolio holdings' pairwise correlation in calm windows vs windows when F > 50.
   - Access (D089): LIC1 gets basic historical VaR only (`basicOnly = true`); LIC2 full output. Cached once per game hour per caller (`RISK_CACHE_TICKS`); computed in the view (off the tick-critical path, 19).
2. Preview (05 §3.18.2) from data published at N−1 only (public depth, ADV, σ, band): walk public depth plus the analytic ladder to get the expected average price for the quantity; P10–P90 from ± 1.2816 × σ_tick × √1 around it plus impact uncertainty (Y ± 25%); instant impact δ(q) and expected permanent impact φ Y σ_d √(q/ADV); fees after the viewer's tier; buying power after (cash available − hold, or 07's `bpCheck` result); footprint = q/ADV ("your footprint: 2.4% of daily volume", i18n key); warnings for band edge, halt levels within reach, auto-split (> 3 ADV → POV 10% notice), queued for next session; for algos the expected schedule (`schedulePreview`) and impact range. Advisory only.
3. Execution analytics (05 §3.18.1): arrival price = `arrivalRefPrice` (last price at entry); implementation shortfall vs the decision price (the client's `clientTag` decision price if provided, else arrival); slippage vs interval VWAP of the order's life; markouts at +1, +15, +60 ticks (recorded by `markoutUpdate`); realized impact (price move during execution) vs predicted (preview formula at entry); fee breakdown. Visible only to the owner.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[05 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The market engine turns everybody's orders (players, player AIs, delegated operators, NPC pools, central banks, treasuries and liquidation engines) into prices, once every tick, for every listed instrument, fairly and explainably.

Design commitments (old 01 §0, §7; V3 Part 4; MASTER-BIBLE Part VII T02–T10):
1. **Fairness by batching.** Every tick each asset clears in one uniform-price call auction. There is no latency race, no front-running inside a tick, and the same result on a phone and a desktop.
2. **Players are price-makers.** Small players are invisible; whales leave footprints; coordinated players can squeeze. Impact follows the square-root law from $1k to $10B, in **one** place (the NPC market maker's ladder), so no effect is charged twice (T08).
3. **Prices come from who trades and why.** A value layer (fundamentals, macro factors, news) says what an asset is worth; a flow layer (trader pools and players clearing against the market maker) says what it trades at. Inefficiencies (post-news drift, overreaction, quarter-end, crowding) emerge from the pools and die as capacity is used (specs/quant-market-ecology).
4. **Explainable afterward.** Each closed day and hour has a cause vector that sums exactly to the move, so the trade journal, the "Why it moved" ladder and the Research Desk can never contradict the tape.
5. **Nothing leaks.** Public data is aggregate: depth by price level, prints, volume. No account identity, no hidden reserve, no stops, no algo parents ever leave the engine (L11).
6. **Deterministic.** The engine is a pure function of (seed, state, sealed intents, entropy commits) (02).

### §3.17 Risk API (`risk.report`, read-only query; 17 RSK-01 to RSK-04)

Input: an account, entity or owner group, horizon (1 game day or 1 game week), confidence (95 or 99%). Output:
- **Historical VaR and ES** from the world's own price history (up to 500 trading days of 05's stored closes; before the world has 250 days, the genesis pre-history of 22 fills in).
- **Parametric VaR and ES** from the factor model: EWMA covariance of factor returns (λ = 0.94) plus idiosyncratic variances; ES at 97.5%.
- **Factor exposures**: beta to GX, bloc and sector factors, rates DV01, credit CS01, FX by currency, commodity and crypto deltas, option greeks from 06.
- **Liquidity-adjusted VaR**: `LVaR = VaR + Σ_i |notional_i| × (s_i + Y σ_d,i √(|q_i| / ADV_i))`, the cost of liquidating each position in one day through the ladder.
- **Stress engine**: replays of the world's own past events (16 names them, for example "Crash of Y2 Q3") as factor shock vectors recorded at the time, standard shocks (rates +200 bp, equities −20%, FX ±10%, spreads ×2, vol ×1.5), and custom factor shocks; positions are revalued through factor loadings and 06 pricing.
- **Correlation under stress** (RSK-04): holding correlations in calm windows versus windows when F > 50.
Basic VaR is free for LIC1; full output needs LIC2 (`DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`). Computed at most once per game hour per caller (cached), off the tick-critical path (19).

## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. Eligibility (07 §3.3.1 step 2 and 4), checked in this order, first failure returns: lender `RESTRICTED` (unless `cureLoan`) or in resolution/resolved/liquidated → `BNK_RESTRICTED_BANK`; WATCH lender beyond 50% of normal supply (`watchSupplyUsedFrac ≥ 0.5`) → `BNK_RESTRICTED_BANK`; product disabled `BNK_PRODUCT_DISABLED`; borrower Held `BNK_HELD`; NPC lender and borrower in the 14-real-day post-bankruptcy lockout `BNK_LOCKOUT`; grade below `minGradeFor(S)` `BNK_GRADE_CUTOFF`; amount > min(productLimit, borrowerLimit) `BNK_LIMIT`; large exposure `BNK_LARGE_EXPOSURE`; related party `BNK_RELATED_PARTY`; LTV > `effectiveMaxLtv` `BNK_LTV`.
2. `PRODUCT_LGD` (DEFAULT where 07 gives none; aligned with §3.5.4 and 06 LGDs): PERSONAL_LINE 0.80, AGG_HH_CONSUMER 0.80, LOMBARD 0.45, MARGIN_DEBIT 0.45, COLLECTIBLE 0.50, BUSINESS_TERM 0.50, REVOLVER 0.50, BRIDGE 0.50, AGG_FRM 0.50, MORTGAGE_RESI 0.25, AGG_HH_MORTGAGE 0.25, MORTGAGE_COMM 0.35, CONSTRUCTION 0.45, LBO_SENIOR 0.50, LBO_MEZZ 0.75, INTERBANK 0.55, CB_FACILITY 0.10.
3. `loanRate` (07 §3.3.1 step 5): `EL = PD_1y · LGD_eff`; `capitalCharge = RW · cet1Target · bank_cost_of_equity`; `opexCharge` per class; `margin = policy.loanSpreadGrid[grade]` (NPC default `NPC_SPREAD_GRID`, linear from 0.25% AAA to 4.0% CCC); `standardsAddOn = 0.03 · S²`; fundingIndex = ONR for FLOAT, the OIS par rate for the term for FIXED (`up.macro.oisPar`, 06 §3.16.3). Higher S strictly raises the rate (∂/∂S = 0.06 S > 0 for S > 0; at S = 0 the cutoff and LTV still tighten) and lowers max LTV (07#9).
4. Schedules (07 §3.3.2): `n = termDays / paymentFreqDays` (integer; termDays is a multiple of the frequency by validation). ANNUITY: `A = P·i/(1 − (1+i)^−n)` with `dmath.pow`; interest of each period = the accrued daily interest (`dailyInterest` per day on the outstanding), principal = A − interest; LINEAR: principal `P/n` (`allocate` P into n equal parts, remainder to the last) + interest; BULLET: interest each period, principal at maturity; IO_THEN_ANNUITY: interest during `ioPeriodDays`, then annuity over the remaining periods; REVOLVING: interest monthly on drawn, principal at maturity. The final payment equals the whole remaining outstanding plus accrued (absorbs rounding, 07#10). Floating loans re-fix the rate daily from ONR (+ spread, + penalty while DELINQUENT, + `rateStepBps`) and recompute the annuity on the remaining schedule at each reset.
5. Payment application order: fees → penalty interest → accrued interest → principal (`applyPayment`). Principal posts CREDIT_DESTROY (`BNK_LOAN_REPAY`), interest TRANSFER (`BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`) in T07A-08.
6. Break cost (D134): FIXED loans: remaining contract payments discounted at the current OIS rate for the remaining term, `PV = Σ CF_k · exp(−r_ois · τ_k)` (τ in game years, `dmath.exp`), cost = `max(0, PV − outstanding)` (toMoney); FLOAT → 0.
7. State machine (07 §3.3.3, evaluated at each day close after the payment attempt): missed payment → overdue += unpaid; DPD = game days since `firstMissedTick`; DPD ≥ 1 → DELINQUENT (penalty `+5%`/yr on the overdue amount each day, credit late count +1 once, event); DPD ≥ `loan_default_dpd` (3) → DEFAULTED, `accelerate` (whole outstanding due), `startEnforcement` (enforcement runs in P8 of the next tick, 07#11); paying all overdue before then → PERFORMING with `watchUntilTick = tick + 7×720` (status WATCH until then).
8. Covenants (07 §3.3.4): due every `testEveryDays` at t = 719; MAX_* breached when value > threshold, MIN_* when value < threshold, CHANGE_OF_CONTROL when the borrower's controller changed since origination, NEGATIVE_PLEDGE when the borrower pledged assets to another secured party after origination; first breach → `START_CURE` (`breachedSinceTick = tick`); value back within → `CURED`; still breached after `cureDays` → `APPLY` the `onBreach`: `RATE_STEP_100` (+100 bps until cured), `CASH_SWEEP_50` (50% of daily net income to principal), `ACCELERATE` (treated as default), `WAIVER_FEE_1PCT` (NPC lenders waive when `npcWaives`, charging 1% of outstanding as `BNK_LOAN_FEE`; player lenders decide by policy, default waive = false).
9. ECL (07 §3.3.7): stage 3 if DEFAULTED; stage 2 if WATCH, DPD ≥ 1, `notchesDown(originGrade, currentGrade) ≥ 3`, or any covenant breached; else 1. Provision: stage 1 `PD·LGD·EAD`; stage 2 `PD_life·LGD·EAD` with `PD_life = 1 − (1 − PD)^years remaining`; stage 3 `EAD − expected recovery` (collateral after haircut minus 1% enforcement costs). `eclInputFor` maps these to 03's `EclInput` so 03 posts `LED_ECL_PROVISION` (stage 2: `pd12m = PD_life`; stage 3: `defaulted = true`, `lgd = 1 − recovery/EAD`).

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[07 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module is the **plumbing of money and leverage**: where every unit of cash actually sits, how banks create credit and destroy it, how they fund themselves, how confidence in them rises and collapses, how they fail and are resolved, how leverage is extended to traders (broker margin, prime brokerage, repo, securities lending), and how the Clearing House stands between counterparties, collects margin and absorbs defaults through a waterfall.

It gives the game its systemic layer (V3 §3.1, §3.3, §3.4):
- **Credit cycles are caused**: lending standards loosen in booms and tighten in busts as bank capital and losses move (§3.5).
- **Bank runs are feedback loops** fed by losses, rumors and other runs, stoppable by central-bank liquidity, deposit-insurance action, recapitalization or a rescue merger, and they leave a permanent record (§3.9).
- **Procyclical margin** turns a volatility spike into forced deleveraging, which is the cleanest cascade in the game (§3.15, §3.16).
- **The default waterfall** gives large players mutualized, "too big to fail" risk (§3.17).
- **Players can run banks** (§3.11): earn net interest margin, carry duration, credit and run risk, and fail for real.

Design laws:
1. Money is either **settlement cash** (a direct claim on a central bank, 03 account 1001: the default, risk-free money of every non-bank, earning nothing unless 04 pays on it), a **bank deposit** (03 account 1010, a claim on a bank: earns interest, carries the bank's risk), or a bank's **reserves** (1002). The payment rail between them is 03's (03 §3.3.4); this module owns everything about banks and deposits. In this module `fear = F / 100`, F = 04's Fear Index (04 §3.14.1).
2. A loan creates a deposit (`CREDIT_CREATE`); a repayment destroys one (`CREDIT_DESTROY`). Base money (M0) changes only through 03 faucets and sinks and 04 central-bank operations.
3. Liquidations and close-outs use fair-value price bands, never a crashed print (01 §9).
4. Nothing reveals an individual player's positions or strategy (L11). Systemic dashboards publish aggregates only.

**Status of numbers:** every `PARAM.*` value in this module, and every choice tagged `DEFAULT`, is `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`. Locked decisions L1–L15 (00) and the 00 time model are not defaults. The full list is in `_notes/07-notes.md`.

#### §3.3.1 Origination algorithm (every lender, every product)
1. Application (`bank.loan.apply`, or 10/09 origination calls, or the aggregate allocator §3.5) states amount, currency, term, collateral, purpose.
2. Eligibility: lender ACTIVE (WATCH lenders lend only within 50% of normal supply; RESTRICTED do not lend); product enabled; borrower not Held (13) for new loans; borrower not in the 14-real-day post-bankruptcy lockout for NPC credit (ROADMAP 2.6).
3. Credit assessment: borrower `CreditProfile` (§3.4); collateral value after haircut (§3.15.6); for business loans 09's financials (DSCR, leverage).
4. Limits: product limit, borrower limit (§3.4.3), large-exposure limit, standards (§3.5.3): `maxLtv_effective = productMaxLtv − 0.20 · S`, minimum grade cutoff `BBB` at S ≥ 0.8, `BB` at S ≥ 0.5, `B` otherwise.
5. Pricing:
```
rate = fundingIndex + EL + capitalCharge + opexCharge + margin + standardsAddOn
fundingIndex  = ONR (floating) or OIS par rate for the term (fixed) (06 §3.16.3)
EL            = PD_1y · LGD_eff                      LGD_eff = max(0.05, 1 − collateralValueAfterHaircut / amount) × productLGD
capitalCharge = RW · cet1Target · PARAM.bank_cost_of_equity (0.12)
opexCharge    = 0.50% (secured), 1.50% (unsecured personal), 0.75% (business)
margin        = BankPolicy.loanSpreadGrid[grade] (NPC default: 0.25% AAA … 4.0% CCC)
standardsAddOn = 3.0% · S²
```
6. Offer (`bank.loan.offer` event to the borrower) valid 30 ticks; acceptance (`bank.loan.accept`) books it.
7. **Booking (P8, postings committed P13):** `Dr Loan asset (lender) / Cr Deposit liability (lender, borrower's account)` for the drawn amount, reason `BNK_LOAN_DRAWDOWN`, class `CREDIT_CREATE`. If the borrower's chosen account is at another bank, the deposit is created at the lender and immediately paid across (payment §3.1.2). Collateral pledges are recorded and the assets become encumbered (`pledgedQty`, 06 §2.1).

#### §3.3.2 Accrual and payments
- Interest accrues daily at t = 719: `accrued += outstanding · rate_t / 364` (floating rate re-fixed daily from ONR).
- Payment at `nextPaymentTick` (t = 719): the scheduled payment is auto-debited from the borrower's home account (then other accounts of the borrower at the same lender, then other banks). Annuity: `A = P · i / (1 − (1 + i)^(−n))`, `i = rate · paymentFreqDays / 364`, re-computed on rate reset for floating loans. Linear: principal `P / n` plus interest. Bullet: interest only, principal at maturity. IO-then-annuity: interest during `ioPeriodDays`, then annuity over the rest.
- Application order of a payment: fees → penalty interest → accrued interest → principal.
- Principal repayment posts `Dr Deposit (payer) / Cr Loan` with reason `BNK_LOAN_REPAY`, class `CREDIT_DESTROY`. Interest posts as TRANSFER `BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`.
- Prepayment allowed any time; fixed-rate loans pay a break cost = max(0, PV difference between contract rate and current OIS for the remaining schedule) (`DEFAULT`); floating loans prepay free.
- Revolvers/lines: draw and repay freely within the limit; commitment fee `0.25%`/yr on undrawn amount.

#### §3.3.3 Delinquency and default (player and company borrowers)
| Days past due (game days) | Status | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | DELINQUENT | Penalty `+5%`/yr on the overdue amount; credit profile late count +1; notification |
| `PARAM.loan_default_dpd = 3` | DEFAULTED | Acceleration: whole outstanding due; collateral enforcement starts; credit event for 06 if the borrower has bonds or CDS (06 §3.17.4, failure to pay ≥ $1M) |

#### §3.3.4 Covenants
Tested every `testEveryDays` at t = 719 from 09/10 data (LTV from 10 appraisals, DSCR and leverage from 09 statements, CET1 from §3.2). Breach starts the cure period; at its end without cure, `onBreach` applies: `RATE_STEP_100` (+1% rate until cured), `CASH_SWEEP_50` (50% of the borrower's daily net income to principal), `ACCELERATE` (treated as default), `WAIVER_FEE_1PCT` (lender may waive for 1% of outstanding; NPC lenders waive when the breach is < 10% beyond threshold and fear < 0.5). The "credit squeeze" takedown of 03 §12 (#12) is exactly this: a lender (player or NPC) applying covenant rights.

**[07 §3.3.7]** (from spec file 07-BANKING-CREDIT-CLEARING.md)

#### §3.3.7 Provisions (expected credit loss)
```
Stage 1 (PERFORMING, no significant deterioration):  provision = PD_1y · LGD · EAD
Stage 2 (WATCH, DPD ≥ 1, grade down ≥ 3 notches since origination, or covenant breach):  provision = PD_life · LGD · EAD,  PD_life = 1 − (1 − PD_1y)^(remaining years)
Stage 3 (DEFAULTED):  provision = EAD − expected recovery (collateral after haircut and enforcement costs)
```
Re-measured at each day close; changes post as `LED_ECL_PROVISION` (class REVALUE, no cash). Write-off at the end of enforcement: `Dr Provision / Cr Loan` (`BNK_LOAN_CHARGEOFF`, class CREDIT_DESTROY for the unrecovered principal: the deposit it once created has already circulated, so broad money does not change at write-off; 03 handles the class mapping).

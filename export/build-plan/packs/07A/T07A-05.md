## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
Make deposits a real choice next to settlement cash (07 §3.1.1, D117): characters and companies open deposit accounts at ACTIVE banks where they have presence, earn interest at the bank's posted rates (floored at 0 for retail), lock term deposits (breaks forfeit all term interest, never principal), convert currency inside a bank at the FX mark ± 0.25% margin, and sweep excess balances. Aggregate depositors (`hh`, `frm`, NPC institutions) re-spread their deposits across banks each day by the sticky logit of §3.1.4. The payment side sets each bank's intraday credit limit at its central bank, queues 07-initiated payments that would breach it, and posts the reserve-requirement penalty at day close. Everything moves money only through 03's rail.

## Implementation steps
1. Opening (`bank.account.open`, 07 §4): P1 validate: bank ACTIVE (`BNK_NOT_ACTIVE`), presence in the bank's bloc (`BNK_NO_PRESENCE`; presence = home city in the bloc, owns a business or property there, or holds any balance in that currency), ≤ 5 accounts of the holder at that bank (`BNK_TOO_MANY_ACCOUNTS`), COMMUNITY banks only for holders with presence in their city; the bank itself cannot hold its own deposits. Apply (P8): create `DepositAccount` (`ctx.newId('dep')`), sub per the format in Imports, rate = posted rate, `isHome` false; emit `bank.account.opened` PRIVATE.
2. `bank.account.setHome`: own SIGHT account (`BNK_NOT_SIGHT`); sets `isHome`, clears others, and calls 03 `acct.payment.setAccounts` semantics through `ctx.send(holderId, 'acct.payment.setAccounts', { receive: { kind: 'BANK', bankId }, pay: { kind: 'BANK', bankId } }, 'B1')` (03's message; the mirror flag is 07's).
3. `bank.deposit.move` (P8 apply, postings P13): source not frozen (`frozenUntilTick`, `BNK_FROZEN`; during a resolution freeze only the insured part is movable, 07 §8 edge 1), destination is the mover's own account or its settlement cash (`'CASH'`), amount ≤ available or `'ALL'`. Same bank → book transfer; different banks → rail moves reserves; different currencies → `fxConversion` at the FX mark (05 FX pair last price via `up.markets.lastPrice` of the pair) with `BNK_FX_MARGIN` to the bank and `ledger.fx` for the leg. Large conversions are still allowed here (LIC2 players may use the FX market directly). Moving out of a TERM account before maturity is a break: forfeit the account's `bnk.term_accr` amount (set to 0, no posting), principal moves in full (07#6). Record a `DepositMoveMemo` for Y6 (moves of the same funds back within 30 ticks count once in `outflow24h`, T07A-12 reads it). Emit `bank.deposit.moved` PRIVATE. A move from a bank in RUN during the same game hour has no fee or delay (no deposit gates, DEFAULT).
4. `bank.deposit.term` (`termDays` 7/28/91): moves `amount` from a SIGHT/SAVINGS account into a new TERM account at the same bank, rate fixed at opening = `postedRate(TERM_<d>)`, `termMaturityTick = tick + d·720` aligned to the next t = 719.
5. `bank.account.close`: balance moves to `toAccountId` (TERM forfeits accrued interest); refused while the holder has ClearingDebt outstanding and `toAccountId` is not its own account (07 §8 edge 23: `BNK_CLEARING_DEBT`); emits `bank.account.closed`.
6. Interest (07 §3.1.3, DAY_CLOSE t = 719 every day incl. weekends, edge 8): for every SIGHT/SAVINGS account in id order, `interestForDay(closeBalance, rate)`; corporate holders (cmp, frm) with balance above $10M FDL-equivalent and an unfloored posted rate below the floor get the negative rate on the part above $10M (the bank debits it from the account with `BNK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST` reversed direction); retail rates never go below `deposit_rate_floor` (D119). Post each account's interest separately (`BNK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST`; elastic holders `BNK_ELASTIC_DEPOSIT_INTEREST`), rounding per account (edge 24). TERM accounts add `interestForDay` to `bnk.term_accr` and are paid at maturity by `matureTermDeposits`. Emit `bank.interest.posted` PRIVATE per account per day.
7. Sweeps (`bank.sweep.set`, 08 fund eligible = in `ports.funds.mmfIds`): DAY_CLOSE after interest, balance above `targetBalance` → `MMF`: `ctx.send(fundId, 'fund.sweep.subscribe', { holderId, accountKey, amount }, 'NEXT')` (08 consumes; absent 08 → sweep inactive and the intent rejects `BNK_UNKNOWN`); `TBILL`: a `LiquidationJob` with source `SWEEP` buying the bloc's shortest government bill for the amount (executed by T07A-09's engine with its band); `NONE` clears.
8. NPC deposit market share (§3.1.4), DAY_CLOSE: for each aggregate depositor segment (`hh_<city>` over banks present in the city; `frm_<bloc>` and NPC institutions over banks present in the bloc), `u_b` with depositRate = the bank's SAVINGS posted rate, `avgRate` = deposit-weighted mean, `C_b` = bank confidence, `brand_b` from 22 content (default 0); `targetShares`, `stickyUpdate` with `deposit_share_speed`; the holder's deposits are then moved between its 1010 accounts at the banks to the new shares (largest-remainder `allocate`, payments on the rail with `BNK_DEPOSIT_IN`, a TRANSFER; reserves move bank to bank). Totals per segment change only through 04/16 flows and credit creation (not here). Run outflows (T07A-12) apply on top.
9. Payment system (07 §3.1.2): `intradayCreditLimit` = Σ over active pledges with `securedPartyId = up.macro.cbIdOf(bloc)` of value after the CB haircut (venue CB, T07A-02) minus outstanding 04 facility loans secured by them; pushed every tick as fact `INTRADAY_CREDIT_LIMIT` (03 I10 bound). Payments initiated by 07 (deposit moves, run withdrawals, sweeps, DGF payouts, aggregate rebalancing) go through `payFromDeposit`: if the paying bank's `reserveHeadroom` is short, the payment is queued in `bnk.pay_queue` with `orderHash = sha256(canonEncode([bankId, payerId, payeeId, amount, tick, seq]))` and paid FIFO by `(enqueuedTick, orderHash)` when headroom allows, with a PRIVATE alert to the payer (`bank.deposit.moved` with `status: 'QUEUED'`); queued payments carry into the next tick. Netting is 03's (07 §3.1.2 performance rule).
10. End of day: 03 converts negative 1002 into `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` (03 §3.3.4, T03-15); `liquidityShortfallAtClose` reports the part not covered by pledged eligible collateral, which T07A-13 uses as the LIQUIDITY trigger. Reserve requirement (07 §3.2.3): after conversion, shortfall `= max(0, rr·(sight + savings) − reserves)`, penalty `reservePenalty` posted by player banks as `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_P` (burn, SINK) and NPC banks as `CBK_RESERVE_PENALTY_W` (pay to the cbk, TRANSFER).
11. Edge 21 (cross-partition): every payment uses 03's hold → settle legs; nothing here assumes same partition.

# SPEC EXCERPTS (verbatim; binding game rules)

**[07 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

This module is the **plumbing of money and leverage**: where every unit of cash actually sits, how banks create credit and destroy it, how they fund themselves, how confidence in them rises and collapses, how they fail and are resolved, how leverage is extended to traders (broker margin, prime brokerage, repo, securities lending), and how the Clearing House stands between counterparties, collects margin and absorbs defaults through a waterfall.

It gives the game its systemic layer (V3 §3.1, §3.3, §3.4):
- **Credit cycles are caused**: lending standards loosen in booms and tighten in busts as bank capital and losses move (§3.5).
- **Bank runs are feedback loops** fed by losses, rumors and other runs, stoppable by central-bank liquidity, deposit-insurance action, recapitalization or a rescue merger, and they leave a permanent record (§3.9).
- **Procyclical margin** turns a volatility spike into forced deleveraging, which is the cleanest cascade in the game (§3.15, §3.16).
- **The default waterfall** gives large players mutualized, "too big to fail" risk (§3.17).
- **Players can run banks** (§3.11): earn net interest margin, carry duration, credit and run risk, and fail for real.

Design laws:
1. Money is either **settlement cash** (a direct claim on a central bank, 03 account 1001: the default, risk-free money of every non-bank, earning nothing unless 04 pays on it), a **bank deposit** (03 account 1010, a claim on a bank: earns interest, carries the bank's risk), or a bank's **reserves** (1002). The payment rail between them is 03's (03 §3.3.4); this module owns everything about banks and deposits. In this module `fear = F / 100`, F = 04's Fear Index (04 §3.14.1).
2. A loan creates a deposit (`CREDIT_CREATE`); a repayment destroys one (`CREDIT_DESTROY`). Base money (M0) changes only through 03 faucets and sinks and 04 central-bank operations.
3. Liquidations and close-outs use fair-value price bands, never a crashed print (01 §9).
4. Nothing reveals an individual player's positions or strategy (L11). Systemic dashboards publish aggregates only.

**Status of numbers:** every `PARAM.*` value in this module, and every choice tagged `DEFAULT`, is `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`. Locked decisions L1–L15 (00) and the 00 time model are not defaults. The full list is in `_notes/07-notes.md`.

**[07 §3.1.1]** (from spec file 07-BANKING-CREDIT-CLEARING.md)

#### §3.1.1 Where money sits
- Every non-bank entity holds settlement cash per currency by default (03 §3.3.4); start cash ($5,000, `ACCT_START_CASH`, 14) arrives as settlement cash. Banks hold reserves at their bloc's central bank (04 `cbk`).
- **Deposits are a choice.** A character or company may open `DepositAccount`s at any ACTIVE bank in a bloc where it has presence (home city, a business, a property, or any holding in that currency) and may hold several; moving settlement cash in or out uses 03's `BNK_DEPOSIT_IN` / `BNK_DEPOSIT_OUT`. The incentive is interest (§3.1.3) and access to credit products (a borrower's loan proceeds arrive as a deposit at the lender, 03 `LEND`); the cost is bank risk (runs, failure, insurance cap). 18 introduces the first savings account just in time; each FDL city has an NPC retail bank (22) suggested as the default.
- An entity may make a deposit account its default paying/receiving account (03 `acct.payment.setAccounts`); 07's `isHome` flag mirrors that setting.
- Aggregate sectors (`hh`, `frm`) hold their deposits at core banks through 03's elastic codes (`BNK_ELASTIC_DEPOSIT_IN/OUT`), which is what NPC deposit flows and run outflows (§3.1.4, §3.9.3) post as.


<!-- excerpt 07§3.1.2 -->
**[07 §3.1.2]** (from spec file 07-BANKING-CREDIT-CLEARING.md)

#### §3.1.2 Payments and settlement
- Payments follow 03's payment rail (03 §3.3.4): deposit-to-deposit across banks moves reserves X → Y; deposit-to-settlement-cash moves reserves out of the bank; same-bank transfers move no reserves. All postings commit in P13.
- **Netting (performance rule, same result):** 03 may net reserve legs per bank per tick; each bank's reserve balance moves by its net amount.
- **Intraday credit:** a bank's reserve balance may be negative during the day down to `intradayCreditLimit` = 100% of the CB-eligible collateral it has pledged to its central bank (after CB haircuts, 04). Payments that would exceed it are queued (FIFO by hash) until reserves arrive, with an alert to the paying entity; queued payments carry into the next tick.
- **End of day (t = 719):** every negative reserve balance is converted into an overnight standing-facility loan (03/04 `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY`) at 04's lending rate (MLFR) against pledged collateral. If collateral is insufficient, the shortfall is a liquidity-failure trigger (§3.10.1).

**[07 §3.1.3]** (from spec file 07-BANKING-CREDIT-CLEARING.md)

#### §3.1.3 Deposit pricing and interest
- Deposit rate paid = `policyRate + spread_kind` set by `BankPolicy.depositRates`, floored at 0 for SIGHT and SAVINGS (`PARAM.deposit_rate_floor = 0`, i.e. no negative retail rates, `DEFAULT (Mathis may override)`); when 04 sets a negative deposit facility rate, only corporate deposits above $10M pass it through (04 §edge case 2).
- NPC defaults: SIGHT `0.2 × policy`, SAVINGS `0.6 × policy`, TERM_7 `0.80 × policy`, TERM_28 `0.85 × policy + 0.05%`, TERM_91 `0.90 × policy + 0.10%` (pass-through β).
- Interest accrues on the day-close balance: `interest = balance · rate / 364`, posted at t = 719 (`BNK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST`; elastic depositors `BNK_ELASTIC_DEPOSIT_INTEREST`).
- TERM deposits: principal locked to maturity; early withdrawal forfeits all accrued interest of the term (`PARAM.term_break_penalty = all interest`, never touching principal).
- Retail FX conversion inside a bank (deposit in one currency to another) executes at the 06/05 FX spot mark ± `PARAM.bank_fx_margin = 0.25%` (fee income to the bank). Large conversions should go through the FX market directly (LIC2).

**[07 §3.1.4]** (from spec file 07-BANKING-CREDIT-CLEARING.md)

#### §3.1.4 Deposit flows of the NPC economy (market share of deposits)
Aggregate depositors (`hh_<city>`, `frm_<bloc>`, NPC institutions) hold deposits across the banks present in their area. Each game day the target share of bank b in segment g is a logit:
```
u_b = 3.0 · (depositRate_b − avgRate_g) / 0.01  +  0.8 · ln(1 + branches_b)  +  2.0 · (C_b − 0.9)  +  brand_b
target_b = exp(u_b) / Σ exp(u_k)
share_b ← share_b + PARAM.deposit_share_speed (0.02) · (target_b − share_b)     (sticky deposits)
```
Run outflows (§3.9.3) are applied on top of this slow drift. Total segment deposits come from 04/16 (household saving, firm cash) and from credit creation by loans to that segment.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Payment to a bank in resolution:** incoming payments are credited normally (they increase the bank's liabilities and its reserves); during the 1-game-day freeze the insured part is usable, the rest is frozen.
2. **Depositor with accounts in several currencies at a failing bank:** the insurance cap is applied once across all currencies of that beneficial owner at that bank, converted at FX spot at the failure tick; largest balances in the bloc currency first.
3. **Bank fails while a player's loan from it is outstanding:** the loan is an asset; it goes to the acquirer or the liquidator; the borrower's terms do not change. Set-off: the borrower's frozen deposits at that bank may be set off against its loan from the same bank (`DEFAULT`: automatic set-off of uninsured deposits against matured or defaulted loans only).
4. **Two banks' runs at the same tick:** processed in hash order of bank id within P8; contagion terms use start-of-tick state, so order does not change outcomes within a tick.
5. **Interbank loan to a bank that fails overnight:** the lender's claim joins class 6 of the hierarchy.
6. **Negative policy rates (04):** DFR may be negative; retail deposit rates floor at 0; banks' NII compresses; formulas accept negatives.
7. **No eligible collateral at day close:** liquidity failure (no exception). A bank cannot be "too small to fail" or "too big to fail" by rule; SIBs get bail-in instead of fire sale.
8. **Loan payment due on a weekend:** payments post at t = 719 every day including weekends (banking runs 7 days); securities sales for enforcement wait for the market session.
9. **Borrower Held during a payment date:** payments auto-debit as normal; Held does not stop collection (03 rule "protection never blocks contract enforcement").
10. **Rookie (72h protection) borrower:** credit limits small (§3.4.3); collection still applies.
11. **Collateral asset delisted or defaulted while pledged:** re-valued at its new mark (06); LTV breach handled by covenant/call rules.
12. **Collateral seized by a court (13) while pledged:** the lien ranks first; the court gets the surplus after the secured party.
13. **Security on loan is subject to a tender:** lender must recall to tender; if recall fails by the tender deadline, the borrower pays the lender the tender consideration difference.
14. **Recall buy-in impossible (asset halted):** grace extends tick by tick until the asset reopens; fees continue.
15. **Margin account with positions in a closed market and a deficit at the open gap:** liquidation at the opening auction with the band; any deficit → ClearingDebt.
16. **A member defaults while another default is in management:** both processed; assessments share the 2× cooling-off cap; auctions may bundle.
17. **Client of a defaulting member has no backup member accepting:** positions closed out; collateral surplus returned to the client within 1 game day.
18. **CRY insurance fund negative and no ADL candidates** (all opposite positions at a loss): remaining deficit is borne by the CRY member's capital; the product halts until rebalanced.
19. **Repo collateral re-used through a chain and the original borrower defaults:** each link closes out against its own counterparty; the invariant guarantees the chain resolves to one holder.
20. **PB fails during a client's margin call:** call suspended; client assets ported; the client's rehypothecated shortfall becomes a claim.
21. **Cross-partition payments and loans:** hold → settle (02/19); a run's withdrawals that cross partitions settle in the same tick's P13 through the hold mechanism.
22. **Player bank owner goes bankrupt personally:** the bank's equity is an asset of the estate (sold via 09 or kept by creditors); the bank itself is unaffected unless its capital depended on owner guarantees.
23. **Account closed with ClearingDebt outstanding:** not allowed; closing requires zero debt or transfer of the balance to another own account.
24. **Rounding:** interest per day computed with `toMoney` on each account; remainders never accumulate across accounts (each account rounds independently with half-to-even); pro-rata DGF payouts and bail-ins distribute remainders in hash order so totals match exactly.

Each entity has a **default paying account** and a **default receiving account**, both DEFAULT `1001 Settlement cash` in its reporting currency; an entity can set a bank deposit as default (`acct.payment.setAccounts`, §4). The rail turns "A pays B amount X in ccy c" into postings:

| A pays from | B receives into | Postings |
|---|---|---|
| A:1001 | B:1001 | `Dr B:1001 / Cr A:1001` (and cbk reassigns holder sub-accounts: `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2001:B`) |
| A:1010:X (bank X) | B:1001 | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr B:1001 / Cr X:1002`; `Dr cb:2002:X / Cr cb:2001:B` |
| A:1001 | B:1010:Y | `Dr Y:1002 / Cr A:1001`; `Dr cb:2001:A / Cr cb:2002:Y`; `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B` |
| A:1010:X | B:1010:Y, X ≠ Y | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr A:1010:X`; `Dr Y:1002 / Cr X:1002` (+ cbk holder reassignment); `Dr B:1010:Y / Cr Y:2100:B` |
| A:1010:X | B:1010:X (same bank) | `Dr X:2100:A / Cr X:2100:B` and `Dr B:1010:X / Cr A:1010:X` (book transfer, no reserves move) |
| A is a bank paying from its reserves | — | treat A's paying account as A:1002 |

If A's paying account lacks funds but A has another money account with funds, the rail draws in this order: default paying account, then 1001, then deposits in order of bank id. Holds always reserve against the account the rail will use (02 §3.14.3).

Bank reserve constraint: a bank's 1002 may go negative intraday down to its **intraday credit limit** at the central bank (07 sets it, DEFAULT = 100% of eligible collateral pledged); at day close any negative 1002 is converted automatically into an overnight standing-facility loan (`CBK_STANDING_FACILITY`, CREDIT_CREATE) at the facility rate (04). This keeps I10 satisfiable.

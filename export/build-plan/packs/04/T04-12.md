## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Implementation steps
1. **NPC dealers (§3.10.5).** One NPC dealer pool per bloc (`DEALER_POOL_ID(b)`, registered at genesis by T04-21) bids the T04-11 ladder; the 8 dealer names from 22 are used only for league-table and lead-manager display (each dealer = 1/8 of the pool's awards).
2. **Application.** `bank.primaryDealer.apply` P1: `CH_BANK` chartered in the bloc (`ports.players.hasCharter(bankId, 'CH_BANK', b)`) or the SWF co-investment privilege (`ports.players.swfPrivilege`), else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; equity ≥ 0.0005 × the bloc's debt stock (`ports.credit.bankEquity`, or `ctx.ledger.netWorth` for an SWF entity, converted to the bloc currency at spot) else `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `ports.players.reputationLowestBand` true → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; revoked within its 2-quarter ban → `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Store status APPLIED; ACTIVE from the next game quarter start. `resign` → RESIGNING, removed at quarter end.
3. **Obligations.** For every auction of the bloc in the quarter, an ACTIVE player dealer must have at least one bid with `face ≥ offered/(2 × dealerCount)` (floored to 1,000) and yield ≤ `y_WI + 5 bp`. At quarter end `quarterEndDealerReview` counts misses; `misses ≥ 3` (consistent with the 90% participation obligation at about 30 auctions per quarter) → REVOKED for 2 game quarters (`revokedUntilTick`). Privileges are read by T04-11 (competitive without LIC4) and T04-08 (`isCounterparty`).
4. **League table.** Quarterly aggregate share of auction face won per dealer (NPC dealers by name, players by name only when `nameInLeague` is true, otherwise `null`), ranks only; served by T04-20's view `macro.dealerLeague`.
5. **Syndication (§3.10.6).** Scheduled by T04-02 (N30Y new line once per game year, SRL FDL 10Y quarterly). `openSyndication` at t = 420 of the day before pricing: `bookOpenTick` now, `pricingTick` = next trading day t = 420 (1 game day); size = the tenor's `mac.fundQueue` face after T04-11 step 1 has run for this syndication with `s` = the pricing day (E-101: N30Y holds the N30Y share of every need since the last N30Y issue; N10Y_FDL holds SRL's whole FDL-account need), already a multiple of 1,000,000; the queue is set to 0, and a syndication with size 0 is not opened; `guidanceSpreadBp = 5` over the curve yield at the maturity (`nsZero`); leads = `leadManagers` (top 3 entity keys by `dealerShares` over the last two quarters, players qualify; NPC names fill the rest); `feeRate = 0.0015`. Emit `macro.sov.auction.announced` with `syndication: true`.
6. **Orders.** `mkt.sov.syndicationOrder` P1: book open (`bookOpenTick ≤ tick < pricingTick`) else `SOV_AUCTION_CLOSED`; competitive-bidder eligibility of T04-11 (LIC4, CH_BANK, CH_FUND or ACTIVE dealer) else `SOV_LICENSE`; face step as auctions; cash hold `face × price at (guidance − 50 bp)` else `SOV_HOLD_FAILED`; group cap 35% of size (`SOV_CAP_EXCEEDED`). NPC book (unit decision; 04 gives no NPC syndication demand): `npcFace = size × ladderParams(...).btc` with no limit.
7. **Pricing.** `book = Σ player orders + npcFace`; `finalSpreadBp = guidance − min(5, floor(max(0, book/size − 2)/0.5))`. Eligible orders: no limit, or `limitSpreadBp ≥ finalSpreadBp`. Leads get up to 15% of size each first (pro rata within their own eligible orders); the rest pro rata to eligible face, floored to 1,000, remainders in `fnv1a32(orderId + syndicationId)` order; Σ ≤ size. Yield = curve + final spread; coupon `newLineCoupon(yield)`; price from yield (06 port, local fallback). Settlement at the pricing tick: players `SOV_BOND_ISSUE`, NPC face through T04-11 `fundNpcAwards` (E-99: holder books first by `SOV_BOND_ISSUE`, `row` `SOV_DEBT_ELASTIC_PLACEMENT` for the shortfall; for the SRL FDL 10Y the book is `npc_SOVFOR_SRL`); SRL FDL bonds in FDL (`fxDenominated`, adds to `fxDebt`). Fee `0.0015 × size × price/100` split equally among the 3 leads, `ctx.ledger.pay(govId, lead, amt, ccy, 'GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE')` (TRANSFER, registered here; 04 §3.18 lists a FAUCET `_P` variant, but the treasury's money already exists so 03's boundary rule makes it a TRANSFER, R23); fees to NPC leads (the dealer pool) are booked to the pool the same way. Emit `macro.sov.syndication.priced` PUBLIC (size, final spread, coupon, book cover; no orders).
8. **Buybacks (§3.10.7).** At t = 420 of the first trading day of each game month, `buybackTrigger`: (a) `cashBuffer > 2 × cashTarget` and stress < 0.2 → excess = `cashBuffer − 2 × cashTarget`; (b) `ports.markets.bondDepthIndex(store, b, true) < 0.5` and FSI > 0.5 → excess = `max(0, cashBuffer − cashTarget)`; `maxFace = min(excess at par, 0.02 × debt)`. Lines: off-the-run lines of the government. Offers until t = 330 of the next trading day (buyback tick). `mkt.sov.buybackOffer` P1: buyback open, asset in the list, `availableQty − ports.credit.pledgedQty ≥ face` else `SOV_HOLD_FAILED`; qty hold placed. Settlement: accept offers with `minPrice ≤ fairPrice + 0.10 per 100` in ascending `minPrice`, ties by `fnv1a32(offerId + buybackId)`, up to `maxFace` (partial last offer floored to 1,000); each accepted pays its own price: `ctx.ledger.swapAsset(govId, holder, assetId, face, cash, ccy, 'GOV_BUYBACK')` with the asset leg retiring the bond (template SWAP_ASSET, accounts as `SOV_REDEMPTION`); line `faceOutstanding` reduced; holds released; `macro.sov.buyback.result` PUBLIC (aggregate face, average price, cover). Treasuries never trade in secondary markets otherwise (§6.2).
9. **Codes registered by this card:** `GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE` TRANSFER (PAY), `GOV_BUYBACK` TRANSFER (SWAP_ASSET).

**[04 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The macro layer is the one shared state every price, business, loan and property in the world reads (V3 Part 2.4). It exists so that:

1. **Prices have reasons.** A rate hike, a CPI surprise, a downgrade or a currency defence hits bonds, mortgages, growth stocks, bank margins, cap rates, carry trades and leveraged funds on the same tick, through named transmission channels that the "Why it moved" ladder (05 §3.12) can cite.
2. **Recessions are caused, not random** (old 01 §5.3). Imbalances (credit growth, valuation stretch, an inverted curve, tight real rates, oil shocks) raise the hazard of a downturn, so a careful player can see one building.
3. **Central banks, treasuries and sovereign bond markets are real machinery** (V3 Part 3.2): policy rates with a corridor, reserves, standing facilities, asset purchases, forward guidance, deficits that are financed by auctions, debt that can be downgraded and restructured, currencies that can be defended and can break.
4. **Crises emerge from rules** (V3 T2) and also arrive on a calibrated schedule so the world has rhythm (one major crisis in about 35% of chapters, storms every game quarter, ripples every game week).
5. **The money stays honest.** The Stabilizer controller keeps the player economy on its designed path, and the Money Supply Index deflates rankings so inflation cannot pump rank (old 01 §8.2–8.3).

#### §3.10.5 Primary dealers
- NPC dealers: 8 per bloc (names in 22), modelled as one NPC dealer pool per bloc in the auction.
- Player application (`bank.primaryDealer.apply`): requires a `CH_BANK` chartered in the bloc, or the 08 §3.1.20 SWF co-investment privilege; equity ≥ 0.05% of the bloc's debt stock; Reputation not in the lowest band (14).
- Obligations: bid in ≥ 90% of the bloc's auctions in a game quarter, each time for at least `offered / (numberOfDealers × 2)` at yields ≤ `y_WI + 5 bp`. Three misses in a quarter → status revoked for 2 game quarters.
- Privileges: competitive bidding without LIC4; standing facility access for own-bloc collateral; eligibility as syndication lead (§3.10.6); a small quarterly league table (public, aggregate share of auctions won, opt-in to be named).


<!-- excerpt 04§3.10.6 -->
**[04 §3.10.6]** (from spec file 04-MACRO-CENTRAL-BANKS-SOVEREIGNS.md)

#### §3.10.6 Syndications
Used for new N30Y lines (once per game year) and SC FDL bonds (quarterly). Lead managers = the top three dealers by auction share over the last two quarters (players can qualify). Book open for 1 game day (`bookOpenTick` to `pricingTick`); orders via `mkt.sov.syndicationOrder` with an optional limit spread. Initial guidance = curve yield + 5 bp new-issue premium; if the book exceeds 2× size, the spread tightens 1 bp per 0.5× of extra cover, at most 5 bp. Allocation pro rata to orders at or inside the final spread, with leads allocated up to 15% each. Fee 0.15% of size split among leads: `GOV_SYNDICATION_FEE_P` (FAUCET) to player leads, TRANSFER to NPC leads.

**[04 §3.10.7]** (from spec file 04-MACRO-CENTRAL-BANKS-SOVEREIGNS.md)

#### §3.10.7 Buybacks
When `cashBuffer > 2 × cashTarget` and stress < 0.2, or when 05's depth index for off-the-run lines < 0.5 with FSI > 0.5: a reverse auction for off-the-run lines, `maxFace = min(excess cash, 2% of debt)`. Holders offer via `mkt.sov.buybackOffer` with a minimum price; the treasury accepts the lowest prices first up to `cap price = 06 fair price + 0.10` per 100 face. Settlement TRANSFER `GOV_BUYBACK`.

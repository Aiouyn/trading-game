## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
How a sovereign defaults and restructures, entirely by rules that no player can trigger directly (exploit 8). Four triggers checked at day close (failed rollover, FX debt service, a political decision under strict conditions, a small surprise hazard draw); the next payment is missed, a 7-day grace runs, selective default is declared with a credit-event payload for 06 and 08; exchange offers open in up to three rounds with a haircut sized to restore sustainable debt, priced at an exit yield, voted by NPC holder blocks and players through `mkt.sov.exchangeVote`, bound by a single-limb aggregated collective action clause; after round 3 the treasury exchanges unilaterally and holdout claims go to 13. The exchange moves only instruments (cash conserved, 04#26). Afterwards the sovereign is POST_RESTRUCTURING for a year with capped auctions and a Concord program, and contagion lifts fear elsewhere.

## Implementation steps
1. **Triggers (§3.12.1), day close, per bloc in BLOC_ORDER, after sovereign credit (T04-07), only for status PERFORMING:**
   1. FAILED_ROLLOVER: `failedAuctions21d ≥ 2` and `cashBuffer < Σ maturities in the next 21 game days` and (backstop unavailable for the bloc, i.e. EUN without an ACTIVE Concord program, or backstop exhausted, i.e. the 5-day backstop window of T04-11 has ended and the buffer is still short).
   2. FX_SERVICE: FDL-denominated coupons plus principal due within 21 game days (SRL's FDL lines) `> fxReservesFdl − importsMonthFdl` and `!canAgreeConcord(store, b)` (T04-14).
   3. POLITICAL: `ports.world.politicalDefault(store, b)` and `debtGdp > 1.5` and `highSpreadTradingDays ≥ 63` (consecutive trading days with `s10 > 0.15`, counter on `mac.bloc`); never for FDL and NKR.
   4. HAZARD: one draw `u = rng.next()` on `S.sov(b)` every day for every bloc (fixed draw count, G8); fires iff `λ > 0.10` and `u < 1 − e^(−0.25 × λ/364)`.
   First trigger in that order wins. No intent, trade or market price can fire a trigger (market PD is never read here; exploit 8).
2. **Case and grace (§3.12.2 steps 1, 2).** `openDefaultCase`: `missedPayment` = the earliest coupon or principal due after today over the government's lines (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`; coupon dates semiannual back from maturity; amount = face × coupon/2 or face), `dueTick`. From case opening `sovereignStatus` reports the case so 06 does not pay that and later payments (06 reads it, T04-20 api). At `dueTick`: status GRACE, `graceEndsTick = dueTick + macro_sov_grace_days × 720` (7), emit `macro.sov.status` PUBLIC (govId, GRACE, trigger) and a Wire hook to 16; 06 flags all lines. At `graceEndsTick` (unpaid by construction): SELECTIVE_DEFAULT, emit `macro.sov.default` PUBLIC (govId, all line ids, missed amount, dueTick) for 06's CDS auction and 08's D grades, `macro.sov.status`; coupons stop on all lines.
3. **Offer terms (step 3).** Round 1 opens 7 game days after SELECTIVE_DEFAULT and stays open 14 game days (`openTick`, `closeTick`). `h = clamp(1 − (d* + 0.20)/debtGdp, 0.20, 0.60)`; rounds 2 and 3: `h_r = max(0, h_{r−1} − 0.05)` (recovery NPV +5 points). Coupon `max(0.02, 0.6 × y10_pre)` with `y10_pre` = the 10y node from history 63 trading days before the trigger tick. `y_exit = OIS(10) + 0.06` at offer open. New bonds: two lines (8 and 15 years), each half of the new face; `facePer100` solves `0.5·P(8) + 0.5·P(15)` (per 100, `priceFromYieldLocal`, semiannual) × facePer100/100 = `100(1 − h)`; `recoveryNpv = 1 − h`. New line ids `sovereignAssetId(b, 'N8Y_RS'|'N15Y_RS', maturityDay)` written to `mac.issue` (06 creates them).
4. **Snapshot and votes.** At `openTick`, write `mac.vote_snapshot` rows: every holder of every affected line (all local and FDL lines) with its committed quantity (`accountsOfAsset` + `committedQty`), sorted by holder id then asset id. Edge 14 and exploit 15: the holder at the snapshot (a securities borrower included) votes; lenders can recall before it; collusion between linked accounts is 15's to void. NPC holders = the elastic sectors `hh`, `frm`, `row`: each sector's position in each line is split into 20 equal blocks (lots, remainder in the last), and each block votes ACCEPT iff `u < npcAcceptProbability(recoveryNpv, preOfferPrice)` with one draw per block on `S.sov(b)` in line, sector, block order; `preOfferPrice` = 05 fair price of the line at open (`ports.markets.fairPriceOf`), fallback the 03 mark. Emit `macro.sov.exchange.open` PUBLIC (round, haircut, new bond terms, close tick).
5. **`mkt.sov.exchangeVote`** P1: offer OPEN and `tick < closeTick` else `SOV_AUCTION_CLOSED`; actor holds snapshot rows for the offer else `SOV_LICENSE`; one vote per holding (all the actor's snapshot rows), changeable until close (row replaced). Stored as `SovExchangeVote` per (offer, holder, asset). Daily tally `macro.sov.exchange.tally` PUBLIC with aggregate consent only.
6. **Close (steps 3, 4).** `consent = ACCEPT face / eligible face`. `consent ≥ macro_cac_threshold` (0.75) → `executeExchange(unilateral = false)`: every line is exchanged, including rejecters and abstainers (aggregated single-limb CAC). Otherwise status FAILED and the next round opens 28 game days after the close (round 2, round 3). Round 3 fails → `executeExchange(unilateral = true)` and, for every holder who voted REJECT, a `HookCall { owner: '13', hook: 'HOLDOUT_CLAIM', args: { holderId, govId, offerId, claimFace, parDifference } }` (claim = old face − PV of new bonds received at y_exit; 13 decides; any recovery 13 orders the treasury to pay is a TRANSFER posted by 13). Emit `macro.sov.exchange.result` PUBLIC.
7. **Exchange postings (step 5, 04#26).** For each snapshot holding at execution (positions re-read at the execution tick; holders who sold since receive nothing, buyers since receive the exchange of what they hold): `ctx.ledger.moveQty(holder, govId, oldLine, qty, 0n, ccy, 'SOV_RESTRUCTURING_EXCHANGE')` and `moveQty(govId, holder, newLine, newQty, 0n, ccy, 'SOV_RESTRUCTURING_EXCHANGE')` for each new line (`newQty` = old face × facePer100/100 × 0.5, floored to lots, remainders to the largest holder). No cash moves. Value changes are REVALUE by 03's marks, never a sink. Old lines are retired (06 delists them), new lines created. 04 §3.18's `SOV_EXCHANGE` maps to 03's `SOV_RESTRUCTURING_EXCHANGE` (R23). Edge 15: CB repo collateral on the old line is revalued by 07 (top-up within 1 game day); edge 16: no special rule for CCP collateral.
8. **After (steps 5, 6).** Status POST_RESTRUCTURING for 364 game days (T04-07 hazard floor uses `daysSince`), auction sizes capped at 50% of plan (T04-11 reads the status), `openConcord(ctx, b, 'RESTRUCTURING')` if none active (T04-14). Debt stock: `debt` reduced by old face minus new face (treasury liability). Contagion: for every other bloc an `ActiveShock { target: 'z5_fear_add', value: 10, DECAY, halfLifeDays: 7, end +21 days }` added to the F term of that bloc's z5; 07 marks banks' exposures.

**[04 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The macro layer is the one shared state every price, business, loan and property in the world reads (V3 Part 2.4). It exists so that:

1. **Prices have reasons.** A rate hike, a CPI surprise, a downgrade or a currency defence hits bonds, mortgages, growth stocks, bank margins, cap rates, carry trades and leveraged funds on the same tick, through named transmission channels that the "Why it moved" ladder (05 §3.12) can cite.
2. **Recessions are caused, not random** (old 01 §5.3). Imbalances (credit growth, valuation stretch, an inverted curve, tight real rates, oil shocks) raise the hazard of a downturn, so a careful player can see one building.
3. **Central banks, treasuries and sovereign bond markets are real machinery** (V3 Part 3.2): policy rates with a corridor, reserves, standing facilities, asset purchases, forward guidance, deficits that are financed by auctions, debt that can be downgraded and restructured, currencies that can be defended and can break.
4. **Crises emerge from rules** (V3 T2) and also arrive on a calibrated schedule so the world has rhythm (one major crisis in about 35% of chapters, storms every game quarter, ripples every game week).
5. **The money stays honest.** The Stabilizer controller keeps the player economy on its designed path, and the Money Supply Index deflates rankings so inflation cannot pump rank (old 01 §8.2–8.3).

### §3.12 Sovereign default and restructuring

#### §3.12.1 Triggers (checked at day close; no player can trigger a default directly)
1. **FAILED_ROLLOVER:** two failed auctions within 21 game days, `cashBuffer` below the next 21 game days of maturities, and the CB backstop unavailable or exhausted.
2. **FX_SERVICE:** FDL-denominated debt service due within 21 game days exceeds FX reserves minus a floor of 1 month of imports, and no Concord program can be agreed (§3.13.6).
3. **POLITICAL:** 16's politics model decides a restructuring; allowed only when `debtGdp > 1.5` and `s10 > 0.15` for 63 trading days (FU and NA: never).
4. **HAZARD:** if `λ > 0.10`, a daily draw `u < 1 − e^(−0.25 × λ × Δ)` on `macro:<bloc>:sov` (only a quarter of the hazard is "surprise"; the rest flows through triggers 1–3).

#### §3.12.2 Procedure
1. The next coupon or principal due is missed: status GRACE for `PARAM.macro_sov_grace_days = 7` game days; 06 flags all lines; Wire item.
2. Grace ends unpaid: status SELECTIVE_DEFAULT; `macro.sov.default` (PUBLIC) with the credit-event payload for 06/08 (CDS settlement auction in 06; agencies move to D in 08). Coupons stop on all lines.
3. Exchange offer round 1 opens 7 game days later, open for 14 game days.
   - Haircut `h = clamp(1 − d_sus/debtGdp, 0.20, 0.60)` with `d_sus = d* + 0.20`.
   - New bonds: 50% of new face in a line maturing in 8 years, 50% in 15 years, coupon `max(0.02, 0.6 × pre-crisis 10y yield)`; `facePer100` chosen so that the present value of the new bonds at the exit yield `y_exit = OIS(10) + 0.06` equals `100 × (1 − h)`.
   - Votes via `mkt.sov.exchangeVote`. Eligible face = all affected local and FDL lines. NPC holders vote per holding block: ACCEPT if `u < σ((recoveryNpv − preOfferPrice/100 + 0.05)/0.05)`, draws on `macro:<bloc>:sov`.
   - Single-limb aggregated collective action clause: if ACCEPT ≥ `PARAM.macro_cac_threshold = 0.75` of eligible face, every line is exchanged, including for holders who rejected or abstained (bound by the clause).
4. If round 1 fails: round 2 after 28 game days with recovery NPV +5 points; round 3 the same. If round 3 fails, the treasury executes the exchange anyway; rejecting holders receive the new bonds and a holdout claim in 13's civil court for the difference to par (13 decides; recoveries paid by the treasury are TRANSFERs).
5. After exchange: status POST_RESTRUCTURING for 364 game days; auction sizes capped at 50% of plan; a Concord program is opened if not already; old lines are removed and new lines created (`SOV_EXCHANGE`, TRANSFER of instruments). The change in holders' value is REVALUE, not a sink.
6. Contagion: other blocs' z5 sees a fear shock (+10 F, decays over 21 days); banks with exposure take 07's mark-to-market losses.

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Policy at the ELB and the rule wants more easing:** rate stays at `elb`; QE logic (§3.8.5) takes over; guidance score is clamped at −1.
2. **Negative rates:** only EC and NA; the deposit facility rate can go negative, reversing the `CBK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST` direction (a charge, class SINK for player banks). 07 passes negative rates to deposits only for corporate deposits above 10M (DEFAULT).
3. **Hyperinflation guard:** `π_core` clamps at 60%; at > 25% for 91 days the hard-peg option opens (§3.13.5); CPI levels are floats (no overflow).
4. **Deflation:** `π_core` floors at −5%; real rates rise mechanically; the rule cuts toward ELB.
5. **A release day with a market-wide halt:** the release still publishes; 05 queues orders; the reopening auction absorbs it.
6. **Weekends:** macro day steps still run (state evolves); releases, meetings and auctions are on trading days only; FX regime breaks happen at the next `t = 45` of a trading day, except SC capital controls, which can start on a weekend day close if a weekend crisis trigger fires (crypto-driven contagion).
7. **Meeting on a day with an emergency already held that week:** the scheduled meeting still occurs; the emergency count resets each quarter.
8. **Two triggers on the same day close:** process in the fixed order of §3.1; only one major can be active; an emergent label during an active major is attached to it as a sub-phase rather than a second major.
9. **Chapter boundary inside a crisis:** crises continue; the chapter counters reset; the scheduler's "final 48 game days" rule applies only to new triggers.
10. **Auction with no player bids:** NPC ladder alone clears it; results are still published.
11. **Auction award rounding:** pro-rata remainders in lots go in hash order; the sum of awards never exceeds offered.
12. **A bidder's cash hold fails at settlement** (cannot happen because the hold is taken at submission; if a hold is released by 13 for a Held character, the bid is cancelled at P1 of the auction tick and the auction clears without it).
13. **Capital controls while a player holds SRL cash and an FDL margin call (07):** margin calls in another currency are met from that currency first; if impossible, 07 liquidates FDL positions; SRL cannot be converted at level ≥ 2. This is intended crisis risk and shown on the margin forecast (RSK-03).
14. **Restructuring vote by a holder whose bonds are lent out (07 securities lending):** the vote belongs to the borrower holding the bonds at the snapshot (as in real markets); lenders can recall before the snapshot.
15. **Bonds pledged as CB repo collateral when the line is restructured:** the CB revalues the new bonds; if collateral is short, 07 calls for top-up within 1 game day.
16. **Default of a bloc whose bonds are CCP collateral:** 07 haircut tables react through grades; no special rule.
17. **N_a collapses (world empties):** scale shrinks at most 0.5% per game day and floors at 0.2; nominal quantities shrink smoothly; outstanding debts are not rescaled (only flows and pools scale), so debt/GDP rises if the world shrinks fast, which is reported but capped in hazard z1 by the clamp.
18. **No 07 yet (early builds and unit tests):** the §3.14.4 fallback provides credit inputs; facility intents return `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.
19. **Release measurement noise moves a print across a threshold** (e.g. the Cycle Committee condition): the committee reads published prints, so noise can delay or hasten a declaration; this is intended.
20. **YCC and QE caps both binding:** the YCC bid shrinks to the remaining capacity; the cap can break; Wire item "YCC under pressure".
21. **Peg break while players hold FX forwards (06):** forwards settle at market; no special rule.
22. **Two blocs meet within the same tick** (cannot happen by calendar; meetings are on distinct trading days of the quarter).
23. **Missing input from another module this day:** last value holds; the event log records `macro.input.stale` (internal) for 20.
24. **Stabilizer with a tiny population:** OBSERVE mode below 03's `PARAM.stab_min_sample` engaged characters (03 §3.7.5); MSI per 03 §3.8.
25. **Stabilizer lever at a bound for more than 14 real days:** emits an internal alert to 21's admin console and 20; no automatic widening (Mathis or live-ops decide).

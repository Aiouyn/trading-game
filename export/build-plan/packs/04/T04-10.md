## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
Each treasury's budget, the cash it actually moves, and the political programs it runs. Revenue rises with the cycle and with commodity prices; spending carries automatic stabilizers and programs; the five fiscal rules (FLEXIBLE, DEFICIT_CAP, BALANCED_CYCLE, SURPLUS_SWF, PROCYCLICAL) create stimulus or austerity at fixed check days; 16's proposals are validated against the rule and pass as a public "rule breach" with a 25 bp liquidity premium for 182 game days (04#45). Programs carry sector demand multipliers for 09 and budgeted incentives for 09, 10 and 11. The fiscal impulse feeds the output gap (T04-04) and the debt path feeds sovereign credit (T04-07). Treasuries never trade in secondary markets except buybacks (§6.2).

## Implementation steps
1. **Revenue (§3.9.1).** `revenue = revBase_b × nominalGdp × (1 + 1.2x) + commodityRevenue` (`macro_rev_base_<bloc>`: 0.30, 0.42, 0.25, 0.40, 0.27; keys `macro_rev_base_fdl/eun/pyn/nkr/srl`); `commodityRevenue = nominalGdp × max(0, commodityExporter) × 0.06 × energyIndex/100` for NKR (0.9) and SRL (0.6). Active TAX_CUT programs reduce revenue by `sizeGdpPerYear × GDP`. Player taxes (03) are not added (they are SINKs/TRANSFERs to the treasury already; the NPC model contains the base).
2. **Spending (§3.9.2).** `spending = spendBase × GDP + autoStab + Σ programs + interest`, `spendBase = revBase − primaryBalance0` (genesis matches §2.2), `autoStab = 0.4 × max(0, u − u*) × GDP`, interest = Σ coupon × face over the government's lines (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`) plus bill discount accretion (bills: face × discount yield). AUSTERITY programs enter with negative size.
3. **Day close (§3.9.6), macro step 9.** `stepFiscalDay`: annual flows × Δ (1/364); `primary = revenue − (spending − interest)`; `deficit = −(primary − interest)`; `debt ← debt + (spending − revenue) × Δ` (toMoney half-even); SRL's `fxDebt` revalued at spot for ratios; `debtGdp = (debt + fxDebt/spot)/GDP`. Ratios over GDP on an annual basis. `averageCoupon` and `averageMaturityYears` face-weighted over the lines (no lines: genesis policy rate and 5 years). History keeps `deficitPrimaryGdp` daily; `fiscalImpulse = deficitPrimary_t − mean364` (shorter at genesis) → `BlocMacroState.fiscalImpulse` for T04-04. Edge 17: debts are never rescaled when the world scale shrinks.
4. **Cash (§3.9.6, §3.18).** `postFiscalCash`: revenue `ctx.ledger.elasticIn(sector, govId, amt, ccy, 'MAC_TAX_FROM_ELASTIC')` split 0.6 `hh`, 0.4 `frm`; non-interest spending `ctx.ledger.elasticOut(govId, sector, amt, ccy, 'MAC_GOV_SPENDING_ELASTIC')`: HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER and autoStab to `hh`, all other spending 0.5 `hh` / 0.5 `frm` (unit decision; 04 gives no split). Interest is not posted here: 06 pays coupons from the treasury (`SOV_COUPON`, `SOV_DEBT_ELASTIC_SERVICE`, 06A schedule). The accrual-cash difference is the treasury cash buffer `ctx.ledger.cash(govId, ccy)`, financed by auctions (T04-11).
5. **Fiscal rules (§3.9.3)** at trading days 1 and 131 of the game year (`tradingDayIndexInYear`), per bloc, in BLOC_ORDER:
   - FLEXIBLE (FDL): when the Cycle Committee declares CONTRACTION (T04-03 event, checked at that day close, once per declaration): stimulus 1.5% GDP for 364 days as two programs (INFRASTRUCTURE 0.75%, HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER 0.75%). At check days: `debtGdp > 1.30` and `s10 > 0.015` → AUSTERITY 0.5%/yr.
   - DEFICIT_CAP (EUN): `deficitGdp > 0.03` → AUSTERITY 0.5%/yr; escape clause: suspended while true regime CONTRACTION and `FSI > 0.5`, then an INFRASTRUCTURE 1% program is allowed.
   - BALANCED_CYCLE (PYN): `cab = pb − 0.4x` outside [−0.015, +0.015] → program of size `min(0.01, distance to band)` in the correcting direction (AUSTERITY if above spending need, HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER if below); CONTRACTION → INDUSTRIAL_SUBSIDY 1%.
   - SURPLUS_SWF (NKR): `swfStep` daily: `pb > 0.02` → `(pb − 0.02) × GDP × Δ` paid `ctx.ledger.pay(govId, swfId, amt, ccy, 'SWF_TRANSFER')`; in CONTRACTION draws up to 1% GDP per year from the SWF (`swf → gov`, same code) funding GREEN_ENERGY or HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER (half each); `swfAssets` mirrors the SWF's cash.
   - PROCYCLICAL (SRL): EXPANSION/OVERHEATING: HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER of 60% of revenue above trend (trend = revBase × trend nominal GDP); CONTRACTION with `s10 > 0.05`: forced AUSTERITY 1.5%; with an ACTIVE Concord program its conditionality (T04-14) replaces this rule.
   Automatic programs carry `source: 'AUTOMATIC'` and are emitted as `macro.fiscal.program` (PUBLIC, no sponsors).
6. **Programs from politics (§3.9.4, §6.8).** Daily `acceptProposals` reads `ports.world.fiscalProposals(store, tick)` (16), sorted by id. `wouldBreach`: AUSTERITY never; EUN: `deficitGdp + size > 0.03` unless the escape clause holds; FDL: `debtGdp > 1.30 && s10 > 0.015`; PYN: resulting `cab` outside the band; NKR: resulting pb below +0.02; SRL: any expansionary program while a Concord program is ACTIVE. Breach → stored anyway with a `breachUntilTick = tick + 182 × 720`, `macro.fiscal.rule_breach` (PUBLIC: bloc, rule, programId); `ruleBreachPremium` adds 0.0025 per active window to T04-07's liquidity premium. The program is public after passage with `sponsorActorIds` (16 decides who is named). Exploit 11: the program applies to every firm in the sector; incentives have budgets.
7. **Demand and incentives.** `SECTOR_DEMAND_DEFAULTS`: INFRASTRUCTURE Industrials +0.06, Materials +0.04, Real estate services +0.03; GREEN_ENERGY Energy renewables +0.08; AI_COMPUTE Semis +0.05, Tech +0.03; HOUSING Real estate +0.05; DEFENSE_SECURITY Security and Defense +0.08; others none. Sector names resolve to indices through `ContentView.sectors()`. `sectorDemandAdd` = Σ over active programs `mult × sizeGdpPerYear/0.01`. `activeIncentives` for 09 (CAPEX_SECTOR, HIRING), 11 (COMPUTE_SPEND) and 10 (MORTGAGE_RATE); `remaining = budget − spent`, none when `≤ 0`. The paying module posts `GOV_SUBSIDY_P` (payer `govId`, registered here as TRANSFER under 03's boundary rule since the treasury's money already exists; 04 §3.18 lists FAUCET; R23 class disputes follow 03) and sends `mac.program.spent { programId, amount }` to `govId` (NEXT); `handleProgramSpend` adds to `spent`. Overshoot within one tick is allowed and recorded.
8. **Codes registered by this card:** `GOV_SUBSIDY_P` TRANSFER (PAY, requesters 09, 10, 11), `SWF_TRANSFER` TRANSFER (PAY).
9. Quarter close: `quarterFiscalEvent` emits `macro.fiscal.quarter` (deficit, primary, debtGdp), the same values T04-16's FISCAL release prints.

**[04 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The macro layer is the one shared state every price, business, loan and property in the world reads (V3 Part 2.4). It exists so that:

1. **Prices have reasons.** A rate hike, a CPI surprise, a downgrade or a currency defence hits bonds, mortgages, growth stocks, bank margins, cap rates, carry trades and leveraged funds on the same tick, through named transmission channels that the "Why it moved" ladder (05 §3.12) can cite.
2. **Recessions are caused, not random** (old 01 §5.3). Imbalances (credit growth, valuation stretch, an inverted curve, tight real rates, oil shocks) raise the hazard of a downturn, so a careful player can see one building.
3. **Central banks, treasuries and sovereign bond markets are real machinery** (V3 Part 3.2): policy rates with a corridor, reserves, standing facilities, asset purchases, forward guidance, deficits that are financed by auctions, debt that can be downgraded and restructured, currencies that can be defended and can break.
4. **Crises emerge from rules** (V3 T2) and also arrive on a calibrated schedule so the world has rhythm (one major crisis in about 35% of chapters, storms every game quarter, ripples every game week).
5. **The money stays honest.** The Stabilizer controller keeps the player economy on its designed path, and the Money Supply Index deflates rankings so inflation cannot pump rank (old 01 §8.2–8.3).

### §3.9 Fiscal policy

#### §3.9.1 Revenue
`revenue = revBase_b × nominalGdp × (1 + 1.2 × x) + commodityRevenue`, `revBase`: FU 0.30, EC 0.42, PL 0.25, NA 0.40, SC 0.27 (`PARAM.macro_rev_base_*`). `commodityRevenue = nominalGdp × max(0, commodityExporter) × 0.06 × (energyIndex/100)` for NA and SC. Player taxes (03) are SINKs from the player economy; they are not added to the treasury's macro revenue (the NPC-economy model already contains the tax base).

#### §3.9.2 Spending
`spending = spendBase_b × nominalGdp + autoStab + Σ programs + interest`. `spendBase_b = revBase_b − primaryBalance0_b` (so genesis matches §2.2). Automatic stabilizers `autoStab = 0.4 × max(0, u − u*) × nominalGdp`. Interest = Σ over bond lines of coupon × face (accrued daily) + bill discount accretion.

#### §3.9.3 Fiscal rules (checked at the start of each game year and at mid-year, trading day 1 and 131 of the year)

| Rule | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| FLEXIBLE (FU) | CONTRACTION declared: stimulus program of 1.5% GDP for 1 game year (half INFRASTRUCTURE, half HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER). If `debtGdp > 1.30` and `s10 > 0.015`: consolidation (AUSTERITY 0.5% GDP per year) |
| DEFICIT_CAP (EC) | If `deficitGdp > 0.03`: AUSTERITY sized to cut the deficit by 0.5% GDP per year. Escape clause: suspended while EC is in CONTRACTION and `FSI > 0.5`; then a 1% GDP INFRASTRUCTURE program is allowed |
| BALANCED_CYCLE (PL) | Target cyclically adjusted primary balance `pb − 0.4 × x ∈ [−1.5%, +1.5%]`; outside it, a program of the needed size (≤ 1% GDP) in the correcting direction; CONTRACTION: INDUSTRIAL_SUBSIDY 1% GDP |
| SURPLUS_SWF (NA) | Target primary surplus +2%; excess above target is transferred to the sovereign wealth fund (08 SWF NPC entity); in CONTRACTION draws up to 1% GDP from the SWF to fund GREEN_ENERGY or HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER |
| PROCYCLICAL (SC) | EXPANSION/OVERHEATING: spends 60% of any revenue above trend (HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER); CONTRACTION with `s10 > 0.05`: forced AUSTERITY 1.5% GDP; with a Concord program: its conditionality replaces this rule |

#### §3.9.4 Programs from politics (16)
16 may propose a `FiscalProgram` (from elections, lobbying, crises). 04 validates: if the program would breach the bloc's fiscal rule, it passes only as a "rule breach" (`macro.fiscal.rule_breach`, PUBLIC) that adds +0.0025 to the sovereign liquidity premium for 182 game days. Programs carry demand and incentives:
- `sectorDemand`: additive multipliers on 09's city-sector demand pools for the bloc (e.g. INFRASTRUCTURE: Industrials +6%, Materials +4%, Real estate services +3%; GREEN_ENERGY: Energy renewables +8%; AI_COMPUTE: Semis +5%, Tech +3%; HOUSING: Real estate +5%; DEFENSE_SECURITY: Security & Defense +8%), scaled by `sizeGdpPerYear / 0.01`.
- `incentive` (optional): `{ target: 'CAPEX_SECTOR' | 'COMPUTE_SPEND' | 'MORTGAGE_RATE' | 'HIRING', sector?: number, rate: number }`. 09, 10 and 11 apply it; payments to player-economy entities are FAUCET `GOV_SUBSIDY_P`. Default rates: CAPEX_SECTOR 10%, COMPUTE_SPEND 8%, MORTGAGE_RATE −0.50 pp, HIRING 5% of wages. Each program has a budget; incentives stop when its budget is spent.

#### §3.9.5 Fiscal impulse
`FI = deficitPrimary_t − mean364(deficitPrimary)` as a share of GDP (positive = expansionary), used in §3.4.1 with multiplier `m`.

#### §3.9.6 Debt accumulation
Each day close: `debt ← debt + (spending − revenue) × Δ` (FDL debt is translated at spot for ratios); `debtGdp = debt / nominalGdp`. Cash flows actually move through the treasury's cash account (financed by auctions, §3.10); the difference between the accrual and the cash position is the cash buffer.

## §9 Exploits and counters

| # | Exploit | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trading on a release before others see it | releases apply in P2 of the release tick; orders for that tick were sealed in P0 before publication; news and fills publish together at P14 (02, old 01 §9) |
| 2 | Predicting releases or meetings from the seed | world seed is server-secret, entropy commit-reveal (02); clients never run the macro step |
| 3 | Inferring planned crises from server behaviour | planned shocks and lengths are SEALED internal state; the public sees only real state changes (§2.4 rule), identical in shape for a 10-day or a 30-day event |
| 4 | Cornering an auction to squeeze shorts | 35% award cap per beneficial-owner group (15 links alts); ECA surveillance (05/13) on auction-plus-secondary patterns; NPC dealer ladder always bids |
| 5 | Alts splitting bids to dodge the cap | owner-group aggregation by 15's link graph; awards to flagged groups are reduced to the cap at settlement |
| 6 | Interest-on-reserves farming (park cash in a player bank to earn the policy rate) | tiered remuneration: only up to 3× required reserves earns the policy rate, the rest the deposit rate; IOR is a counted FAUCET the Stabilizer sees; banking charters cost and carry capital rules (07, 13) |
| 7 | Borrowing at the standing facility to lend at a profit | the facility rate is the corridor ceiling (penalty rate) and needs haircut collateral; profitable only when markets are stressed, which is the intended role |
| 8 | Pushing a sovereign into default to profit from CDS | a default requires deterministic triggers (§3.12.1) driven by fundamentals and auctions; market pressure alone moves the market PD, not the model hazard; coordinated false stories are S5 manipulation (05, 13); legitimate speculation against a weak sovereign is allowed (it is real finance) |
| 9 | Speculative attack on the SRL band | intended gameplay; the CB defends with real orders, rate hikes and controls; attackers face carry cost (high SRL rates), controls risk and the possibility the CB holds |
| 10 | Bribing a governor, a statistics office or an agency | crimes under 12/13 (bribery S5); the macro engine ignores bribes (no mechanical effect), so the only gain is information already in the public data or 12's insider-tip mechanics |
| 11 | Lobbying a stimulus to pump one's own businesses | lawful lobbying (16) is allowed and the program applies to every firm in the sector, including rivals; incentives have budgets; bribery is separate (13) |
| 12 | Gaming the Stabilizer (alts at the designed path to bias the median) | population is 03's engaged set weighted by activity (03 §3.7.2); the median is robust; 15's alt detection removes linked accounts from the population |

| 14 | Exploiting capital-control timing (convert just before controls) | controls start at the next tick after the break rule fires at `t = 45`, and the break is preceded by public pressure (reserves, EMP); this is intended "read the warning signs" play; a level-1 levy applies first in most paths |

## G4. Time (00 §5.1)

5 real seconds per tick; 720 ticks per game day (= 1 real hour); 30 ticks per game hour; one tick = 2 game minutes. Week = 7 game days (Mon–Fri trading, Sat–Sun weekend). Quarter = 13 weeks = 91 game days, months 28/28/35 days. Year = 364 game days = 262,080 ticks. Chapter = 8 real weeks = 1,344 game days. Real day = 24 game days.
Trading day tick-of-day `t`: pre-market 0–59; opening auction t=60; continuous 61–539; closing auction t=540; after-hours 541–719. Accruals (interest, rent, wages, fees) post at day close t=719. Business operations run once per game hour, staggered: an entity runs when `tickOfDay mod 30 == fnv1a32(id) mod 30`.

## G5. Tick pipeline (00 §5.2)

```
P0 ingest  P1 validate  P2 calendar  P3 world(16)  P4 npc(05,16)  P5 ai(11)  P6 match(05,06)
P7 clear(07)  P8 finance(07,08)  P9 real(09,10)  P10 conflict(12)  P11 law(13)  P12 social(14,15)
P13 ledger(03: commit postings atomically, settle/release holds, assert invariants)  P14 emit
```
A phase may read any committed state and the outputs of earlier phases of this tick; it writes only its own module's tables and queues postings for P13. Postings never commit before P13. A failed invariant rolls the tick back and replays it in safe mode. Consequences of tick N that other modules react to land in tick N+1 (via `ctx.send(..., 'NEXT')` or events).

### G6.1 Ports for later modules (dependency inversion)

A module may never import a module built after it. When its rules need data from a later module (for example 05 needs fundamental values from 09 or index weights from 08), it declares a **port**: a TypeScript interface in its own `ports.ts` (e.g. `MarketPorts`), and its factory takes the implementations: `createMarketsModule(ports: Partial<MarketPorts>)`. The later module exports an implementation of that port, and `packages/engine/index.ts` wires them when building the world. A missing port uses the default the spec gives for "module not present" (stated in the card), never an invented rule.

## G8. Determinism rules (02 §3.7, 19 Rule 3.6.2) — lint-enforced in packages/engine

Forbidden in engine code: `Date`, `performance`, `Math.random`, `crypto` randomness, timers, `process`, `globalThis`, `Intl`, `toLocaleString`, `localeCompare`, `async/await`/Promises, `for…in`, iterating `Object.keys/values/entries` of map-like objects, `Map`/`Set` iteration where order matters (use `SortedMap`/`SortedSet`), `Array.sort` without a total comparator (use `sortBy`), `JSON.stringify` for hashing/persistence (use `canonEncode`), `WeakMap/WeakRef`, `Math.exp/log/pow/sin/cos/tan/atan2/cbrt/hypot/...` (use `dmath`), `Math.round` (use `roundHalfEven`), `Number` for money or quantities. Allowed raw math: `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt/abs/floor/ceil/trunc/min/max/sign/fround/imul/clz32`.
NaN/Infinity are illegal in state. Ties are broken by entity id then intent id. Iterate collections only in sorted order. Rejection sampling is forbidden; each variate uses a fixed number of draws. A module may draw only from its own streams. Split money into parts only with `allocate`. No exceptions for control flow: report impossible states with `ctx.fail`.

## Goal
How each treasury funds itself. The calendar of T04-02 sets the auction days once a quarter; each auction is announced 3 trading days ahead and sized then from the funding need (E-101): principal and coupons due before 91 days after its settlement, plus the deficit share and the cash-target gap, net of cash and of what earlier auctions already raised, split by tenor per the issuance mix, with a when-issued yield and (for a new line) a coupon. Players bid through `mkt.sov.bid` (license, face steps, yield range, owner-group caps, cash hold) and cancel through `mkt.sov.bidCancel`; an NPC dealer ladder always bids (exploit 4 counter). Clearing is uniform price and fully deterministic: noncompetitive first, competitive sorted by yield with hash ties, pro rata at the stop, remainders in hash order, awards never above offered (edge 11), no group above 35% (exploits 4, 5). Results publish only aggregates. Failed and weak auctions raise sovereign stress and can trigger the CB backstop. 04 owns sovereign primary auctions (04 §1.1, D059); 06 creates the bond instrument from 04's issue row (see `_unit.md`, overlap with T06A-09).

1. **Issuance mix and sizing (§3.10.1, E-101: rolling 91-day funding, replaces the quarterly GFN).** Mix `macro_issue_mix_*` (keys `macro_issue_mix_<default|srl>_<tenor>`): DEFAULT B3M 0.12, B6M 0.12, B1Y 0.06, N2Y 0.15, N5Y 0.20, N10Y 0.22, N30Y 0.08, L10Y 0.05; SRL B3M 0.16, B6M 0.16, B1Y 0.08, N2Y 0.15, N5Y 0.15, N10Y 0.10, N10Y_FDL 0.20 (syndicated by T04-12, FDL-denominated), N30Y 0, L10Y 0. N30Y new lines are syndicated (T04-12) once per year; its auctions are reopenings. `planQuarter` (trading day 1 of each quarter, per bloc in BLOC_ORDER) writes the quarter's auction rows with `offered = 0`; the size is set at announcement (step 2) as follows.
   - **Need.** In `announceDue`, for each settle day `s` with at least one announcement due today, per bloc and per currency `ccy` of those tenors, once per (bloc, ccy, s), with `today` the announce day: `N(s) = U + PD × (s + 91 − today)/364 + cashTarget − cashBuffer − Pending − Queued`.
     - `U` = principal plus coupons in `ccy` payable on game days in `[today, s + 91)` on every sovereign line of the bloc (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`: face × couponRate/frequency on each coupon day counted back from maturity per E-97, a pro-rata first coupon where 06 sets one, face at maturity; L10Y at today's index ratio), plus the lines of announced but unsettled auctions and syndications at their offered face and announced coupon. The window starts at `today`, not at `s`: flows due before `s` were funded by an earlier settle day and that money sits in `cashBuffer` or `Pending`, so counting the flow again nets it exactly; nothing is counted twice and nothing is missed.
     - `PD` = annual primary deficit `spending − interest − revenue` (T04-10) when 06 is present and pays coupons from the treasury; the annual total deficit when 06 is absent (`U` is then 0, no line exists). Negative in surplus.
     - `cashTarget = 28/364 × annual spending` (unchanged) for the bloc's own currency. SRL's FDL account (E-103 (2), was 0 under E-101): `cashTarget = 28/364 × annual FDL coupon interest`, Σ face × couponRate over SRL's live FDL lines (`ports.assets.sovereignLines`, the T04-10 `interestAnnual` form restricted to FDL lines; G10Y at genesis: `6.3e11 × 0.064 = 4.032e10` a year, target 3,101,538,461.538462 FDL at `S0 = 0.2`). With target 0 the account fell to exactly 0 after every coupon the syndication had funded (day 364 in Quant's model); with the target it keeps 4 weeks of FDL service. This target is used only in `N(s)`.
     - `cashBuffer = ctx.ledger.cash(govId, ccy)` at the announce tick.
     - `Pending` = offered face of announced, unsettled auctions and syndications in `ccy` (expected proceeds at par; the gap to the clearing price is picked up by `cashBuffer` at the next announcement).
     - `Queued` = Σ `mac.fundQueue.face` of the bloc and `ccy` (allocated, not yet offered).
   - **Allocation and rounding (`allocateNeed`).** If `N > 0`: `R = ceil(N / 1e6) × 1e6`. The tenors of `ccy` take the mix shares normalized over their sum `W` (DEFAULT: all 8, W = 1.00; SRL local ccy: B3M, B6M, B1Y, N2Y, N5Y, N10Y, W = 0.80; SRL FDL account: N10Y_FDL alone, W = 0.20). Every tenor except the anchor gets `floor(share × R / W / 1e6) × 1e6`; the anchor gets `R` minus the others, so the parts sum to `R` exactly. Anchor = largest share, ties to the first in mix order (DEFAULT N10Y, SRL local B3M, SRL FDL N10Y_FDL). Each part is added to `mac.fundQueue` row `${b}:${ccy}:${tenor}`. If `N ≤ 0` nothing is allocated (Quant's choice: no negative queues).
   - **Size.** An auction (or T04-12 syndication) of tenor `t` settling on `s` is offered the whole queue `face` of `t`, and the queue is set to 0. A second auction of the same tenor on the same day gets 0. An auction whose offer is 0 is not announced and its row is deleted (no event). N30Y new lines are syndicated (T04-12) once per year and its auctions are reopenings; both draw on the N30Y queue.

5. **NPC ladder (§3.10.4 step 1).** `BTC = 2.4 × (1 − 0.5·stress) × (1 − 0.3·max(0, (F − 50)/50))`; FDL: `× (1 + 0.2·max(0, (F − 50)/50))` instead of the fear cut. `μ = 0.5 bp + 10 bp·stress + 3 bp·max(0, size/avgSize − 1)`, `s = 1.5 bp + 8 bp·stress`; `stress` = T04-07 `stressIndex` plus active shocks with target `auction_stress` (failed +0.15, weak +0.05, crisis rows), clamped to [0, 1]; `avgSize` = mean size of that tenor's last 4 auctions (itself if none). 6. **Clearing (`clearAuction`), P6 of the auction tick.** `cancelHeldBidders` first (P1, edge 12). Group cap: per owner group (`ports.players.ownerGroupOf`; NPC ladder is one group exempt from the cap) total face above `0.35 × offered` is cut from that group's highest-yield bids first (noncompetitive counted first). Noncompetitive: total ≤ `0.20 × offered`, pro rata if above (floor to 1,000, remainders in hash order); `Q_c = offered − noncompetitive filled`. Sort competitive by yield ascending, then face descending, then `fnv1a32(bidId + auctionId)` ascending. Walk to the first bid where the cumulative reaches `Q_c`: stop yield `y*` = its yield; bids below fill fully; bids at `y*` share the remainder pro rata to face, floored to 1,000, remainder units of 1,000 one at a time in hash order. Invariant (edge 11, INV-MAC-003 in T04-21): Σ awards ≤ offered. Total bids < offered → everything bid is awarded, `failed = true`, `y*` = highest bid yield. `bidToCover = total bid face (noncompetitive + competitive incl. NPC)/offered`; `tailBp = (y* − y_WI) × 10,000`; `weak = !failed && (tailBp > 3 || bidToCover < 1.5)`; shares by face of NPC vs others.

**[04 §1 Purpose]** (context)

## §1 Purpose

The macro layer is the one shared state every price, business, loan and property in the world reads (V3 Part 2.4). It exists so that:

1. **Prices have reasons.** A rate hike, a CPI surprise, a downgrade or a currency defence hits bonds, mortgages, growth stocks, bank margins, cap rates, carry trades and leveraged funds on the same tick, through named transmission channels that the "Why it moved" ladder (05 §3.12) can cite.
2. **Recessions are caused, not random** (old 01 §5.3). Imbalances (credit growth, valuation stretch, an inverted curve, tight real rates, oil shocks) raise the hazard of a downturn, so a careful player can see one building.
3. **Central banks, treasuries and sovereign bond markets are real machinery** (V3 Part 3.2): policy rates with a corridor, reserves, standing facilities, asset purchases, forward guidance, deficits that are financed by auctions, debt that can be downgraded and restructured, currencies that can be defended and can break.
4. **Crises emerge from rules** (V3 T2) and also arrive on a calibrated schedule so the world has rhythm (one major crisis in about 35% of chapters, storms every game quarter, ripples every game week).
5. **The money stays honest.** The Stabilizer controller keeps the player economy on its designed path, and the Money Supply Index deflates rankings so inflation cannot pump rank (old 01 §8.2–8.3).

#### §3.10.3 Bidders and validation (intent `mkt.sov.bid`, §4)
- Noncompetitive bids: any entity with LIC2 (Pro) or above; max `PARAM.macro_auction_noncomp_max_face = 10,000,000` face per beneficial-owner group per auction; filled in full at the stop yield; total noncompetitive ≤ 20% of offered (pro rata if exceeded).

#### §3.10.4 Clearing algorithm (uniform price, deterministic)
1. Build the NPC bid ladder: total NPC cumulative face at yield ≤ y: `B(y) = offered × BTC_npc × Φ((y − y_WI − μ)/s)`, discretized at 41 yields from `y_WI − 3s` to `y_WI + μ + 4s` (each level a separate NPC bid, owner = the bloc's NPC dealer pool). Parameters: `BTC_npc = 2.4 × (1 − 0.5 × stress) × (1 − 0.3 × max(0, (F − 50)/50))` for EC, PL, NA, SC; for FU `× (1 + 0.2 × max(0, (F − 50)/50))` instead of the fear cut (flight to safety). `μ = 0.5 bp + 10 bp × stress + 3 bp × max(0, size/avgSize − 1)`. `s = 1.5 bp + 8 bp × stress`. Here `stress` is the sovereign stress index (§3.11.1).
2. Allocate noncompetitive first. `Q_c = offered − noncompetitive filled`.
3. Sort competitive bids (player and NPC) by yield ascending; ties by bid size descending, then by `hash(bidId, auctionId)` (deterministic, not by arrival).
4. Walk the sorted list summing face until the cumulative reaches `Q_c`. The stop yield `y*` is the marginal bid's yield. Bids below `y*` fill in full; bids at `y*` fill pro rata (`allotmentAtStopPct`), rounded down to lots with the remainder given in hash order.
5. Every winner pays the price of the line at `y*` (06 computes price from yield, coupon, maturity). Postings `GOV_AUCTION_SETTLE` (TRANSFER: cash to treasury, bond face to winner).
6. Results: `bidToCover = total bid face / offered`, `tailBp = (y* − y_WI) × 10,000`, player and NPC shares (aggregate only). Individual bids are never published.
7. **Failed or weak auction:** if total bids < offered, everything bid is awarded and `failed = true`. Then: sovereign stress +0.15 for 21 game days, `failedAuctions21d++`, shortfall added to next week's bill auction; if `cashBuffer` cannot cover the next 21 game days' maturities, the CB backstop rule applies (FU, PL, NA: the CB may buy up to 100% of the shortfall in the secondary market over the next 5 trading days; SC: up to 50%; EC: forbidden by the Compact rule unless a Concord program is active). A tail > 3 bp or a cover < 1.5 is a "weak auction" (stress +0.05 for 21 days, Wire item).

## §8 Edge cases

1. **Policy at the ELB and the rule wants more easing:** rate stays at `elb`; QE logic (§3.8.5) takes over; guidance score is clamped at −1.
2. **Negative rates:** only EC and NA; the deposit facility rate can go negative, reversing the `CBK_DEPOSIT_INTEREST` direction (a charge, class SINK for player banks). 07 passes negative rates to deposits only for corporate deposits above 10M (DEFAULT).
3. **Hyperinflation guard:** `π_core` clamps at 60%; at > 25% for 91 days the hard-peg option opens (§3.13.5); CPI levels are floats (no overflow).
4. **Deflation:** `π_core` floors at −5%; real rates rise mechanically; the rule cuts toward ELB.
5. **A release day with a market-wide halt:** the release still publishes; 05 queues orders; the reopening auction absorbs it.
6. **Weekends:** macro day steps still run (state evolves); releases, meetings and auctions are on trading days only; FX regime breaks happen at the next `t = 45` of a trading day, except SC capital controls, which can start on a weekend day close if a weekend crisis trigger fires (crypto-driven contagion).
7. **Meeting on a day with an emergency already held that week:** the scheduled meeting still occurs; the emergency count resets each quarter.
8. **Two triggers on the same day close:** process in the fixed order of §3.1; only one major can be active; an emergent label during an active major is attached to it as a sub-phase rather than a second major.
9. **Chapter boundary inside a crisis:** crises continue; the chapter counters reset; the scheduler's "final 48 game days" rule applies only to new triggers.
10. **Auction with no player bids:** NPC ladder alone clears it; results are still published.
11. **Auction award rounding:** pro-rata remainders in lots go in hash order; the sum of awards never exceeds offered.
12. **A bidder's cash hold fails at settlement** (cannot happen because the hold is taken at submission; if a hold is released by 13 for a Held character, the bid is cancelled at P1 of the auction tick and the auction clears without it).
13. **Capital controls while a player holds SRL cash and an FDL margin call (07):** margin calls in another currency are met from that currency first; if impossible, 07 liquidates FDL positions; SRL cannot be converted at level ≥ 2. This is intended crisis risk and shown on the margin forecast (RSK-03).
14. **Restructuring vote by a holder whose bonds are lent out (07 securities lending):** the vote belongs to the borrower holding the bonds at the snapshot (as in real markets); lenders can recall before the snapshot.
15. **Bonds pledged as CB repo collateral when the line is restructured:** the CB revalues the new bonds; if collateral is short, 07 calls for top-up within 1 game day.
16. **Default of a bloc whose bonds are CCP collateral:** 07 haircut tables react through grades; no special rule.
17. **N_a collapses (world empties):** scale shrinks at most 0.5% per game day and floors at 0.2; nominal quantities shrink smoothly; outstanding debts are not rescaled (only flows and pools scale), so debt/GDP rises if the world shrinks fast, which is reported but capped in hazard z1 by the clamp.
18. **No 07 yet (early builds and unit tests):** the §3.14.4 fallback provides credit inputs; facility intents return `CBK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.
19. **Release measurement noise moves a print across a threshold** (e.g. the Cycle Committee condition): the committee reads published prints, so noise can delay or hasten a declaration; this is intended.
20. **YCC and QE caps both binding:** the YCC bid shrinks to the remaining capacity; the cap can break; Wire item "YCC under pressure".
21. **Peg break while players hold FX forwards (06):** forwards settle at market; no special rule.
22. **Two blocs meet within the same tick** (cannot happen by calendar; meetings are on distinct trading days of the quarter).
23. **Missing input from another module this day:** last value holds; the event log records `macro.input.stale` (internal) for 20.
24. **Stabilizer with a tiny population:** OBSERVE mode below 03's `PARAM.stab_min_sample` engaged characters (03 §3.7.5); MSI per 03 §3.8.
25. **Stabilizer lever at a bound for more than 14 real days:** emits an internal alert to 21's admin console and 20; no automatic widening (Mathis or live-ops decide).

#### §3.7.6 Primary issuance auctions (single mechanism for sovereigns, corporates, banks, secondaries)
1. Issuer (04 for sovereigns, 09 for companies, 07 for banks) announces size, maturity, coupon (fixed coupon set to the rounded par yield at announcement, to 0.125%), auction day (≥ 1 game day later), at t = 300.
2. Bids `mkt.primary.bid`: competitive = (yield, face) for `UNIFORM_YIELD` or (price, shares) for `UNIFORM_PRICE`; non-competitive = face only, capped at `PARAM.primary_noncomp_cap = $5M` equivalent per beneficial owner.
3. Clearing at P6 of auction tick: non-competitive filled first; competitive sorted by best yield (lowest) / best price; stop-out = yield of the marginal bid that fills the size; all winners pay the stop-out (uniform); bids at stop-out prorated; prorate remainders by deterministic hash order.
4. Bid-to-cover = total competitive face / size, PUBLIC. If cover < 1.0, the issue is downsized to the bids received (sovereign: 04 may make the central bank take the remainder only if its QE program allows).
5. Settlement P13 at settleTick (auction tick + 1 game day): cash from winners to issuer, new bonds to winners.
6. NPC primary dealers (the bloc's NPC banks, §6) bid a demand curve: `face_d(y) = cap_d · σ((y − (yMid − 0.0005)) / 0.0004)` where `yMid` = model yield for that maturity + `0.02·fear`; this yields bid-to-cover 2–3 in calm markets and < 1.5 in stress.

# 13 — Law, Courts and Regulators

Owner module: 13. Status: complete specification, 2026-10-18.
Scope (00 §1): evidence, investigation, courts, verdicts, sanctions, Held, appeals, regulators, surveillance, whistleblowers, insurance claims, anti-grief.
Consumes from 12 (ops, sessions, evidence sources, surveillance flags), 05 (surveillance score, antitrust triggers), 08 (insurance products and claims), 15 (collusion graph, filings, boards), 14 (Reputation, Notoriety, Prestige heat, Held screen). Owns the regulator-capacity knob.

**Safety rule (binding).** Crime, courts and enforcement are fictional and abstract (L6, L5 16+). Nothing in this module names or describes a real law, court, regulator, technique or method. Fines, evidence and sanctions are numbers and named game verbs. Evidence is only ever generated by the engine's own event stream (SEALED events, 12 §5; ledger postings, 03); it can never be fabricated by a player, which is what keeps bounty hunting and whistleblowing fair.

Time: 1 real hour = 1 game day = 720 ticks (L9). Durations stated in real time stay in real time, stored as ticks.

---

## §1 Purpose

1. Turn 12's raw events into a fair, deterministic justice system: evidence must be discovered (it costs money and time), guilt is probabilistic, and money can shade a close case but never buy a verdict.
2. Price crime: a sanction schedule by severity (S1–S6) as a percentage of net worth, split sink/victim-fund/whistleblower, with restitution always owed in full.
3. Give victims recourse: insurance, restitution, pre-trial measures, a victim compensation fund, case-selling, counter-suits, revenge discounts.
4. Guarantee no permanent ruin: no op, day or verdict takes a character below the $5,000 + one T1 business floor (L-floor principle).
5. Run four regulators with finite attention that players can steer but not spam.
6. Enforce anti-grief caps (shared with 12 §3.10) and void linked-account schemes (15 collusion graph).

Design principles kept from old vision 03 §0: every op leaves evidence; detection ≠ attribution; victims always have recourse; no permanent ruin; legibility before commitment; every money flow tagged sink/transfer; abstract fiction only.

Evidence value at time t (decay, kept from old vision):
```
evidence(triple, t) = Σ_sources points_source · e^( −daysSince(source)/τ ) · stackMult(source)
τ = PARAM.evidence_decay_tau = 21 real days
stackMult: the n-th source OF THE SAME sourceType counts 1, 0.5, 0.25, … (0.5^(n−1))
```
Case bars (burden of proof): civil `PARAM.bar_civil = 40`, regulator `PARAM.bar_regulator = 55`, criminal `PARAM.bar_criminal = 70`. Below 40, no viable case.

## §3 Rules and formulas

Runs in phase **P11 law** of the tick pipeline (00 §5.2), after P10 conflict and before P12 social. Reads 12's sealed evidence sources and surveillance flags, ledger state (03), the collusion graph (15). Writes cases, sanctions, Held/garnishment, and ledger postings queued for P13.

### §3.1 Evidence grading and stacking

Each game day, for every open triple, recompute `evidence(triple, t)` per §2.1 with decay and the same-source-type diminishing returns (first 100%, then 50%, 25%, …). A judgment needs **attribution** (evidence ≥ bar) and, for damages, **quantum** (at least one HOW_MUCH or BOTH source, §6). Residue (WATERMARK) alone proves WHO but never HOW_MUCH. A query-anomaly false-flag rate of 5% on heavy honest licensees is capped so a flag alone (max 30 points) never crosses the civil bar.

### §3.2 Sanction schedule (severity tiers)

Fine = percent of the offender's NW at the offense tick or verdict tick, whichever is higher (stops spend-down).

| Tier | Typical offenses | Fine (% NW) | $ floor | $ cap/verdict | Plus (transfer to victim) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Minor | ToS scrape, late filing, failed recon caught | 0.1% | $1,000 | $5M | — |
| S2 Access | Unauthorized access, no proven loss | 0.5% | $5,000 | $50M | victim forensic costs |
| S3 Theft | Data siphon, phishing loss, compute leech, small sabotage | 2% | $20,000 | $500M | restitution 1.5× proven loss |
| S4 Major | Model distillation (session), ransomware, front-running tapped orders, trade-secret walkout | 5% | $50,000 | $2B | restitution 2× + disgorgement of gains |
| S5 Market/trust | Manipulation, false stories, insider trading, embezzlement, bribery | 8% | $100,000 | $5B | disgorgement 100% + restitution 1.5× |
| S6 Systemic | Rug pull, supply-chain attack at scale, manufacturing a pandemic | 15% | $250,000 | none | disgorgement 100% + restitution 2× |

Modifiers: repeat offender in the same chapter ×1.5 per prior conviction (max ×2.5); punching down up to ×3 on damages (12 §3.8.2); guilty plea/cooperation −30%.
Caps: one verdict's fines ≤ 35% of NW; all fines in a chapter ≤ 60% of peak NW. The % caps win over the tier $ floors (E-31a, E-43, E-45): a tier floor never raises a fine above a cap. The $ figure is the collection floor (`PARAM.protected_floor_cash_real`), not a minimum fine.
Restitution is always owed in full (payment plan if needed).

### §3.3 Sink/transfer split (00 §5.4)

Of each fine: 85% destroyed (`FINE_SINK`), 10% to the Victim Compensation Fund (`VCF_TRANSFER`), 5% to the whistleblower/legal-bounty pool (`WHISTLE_TRANSFER`). Restitution, disgorgement to identifiable victims, and civil settlements are TRANSFER. Disgorgement with no identifiable victim, court fees (1% of claim, min $500) and fees to NPC law firms are SINK. Fees to player-owned law firms are TRANSFER.
Money-model budget: fines + court fees + NPC legal fees ≈ 0.5–1.5% of world money supply per game year (03 stabilizer verifies).
Settlements: civil cases settle at 50–80% of expected verdict; consent decrees at 60–75% of the schedule (still 85% sink).

### §3.4 Verdict probability

```
p_verdict_for_claimant = σ( (E − bar)/8 + counsel_claimant − counsel_defendant )
counsel = 0.15 · grade   (grade 0–5; max swing ±0.75 logit ≈ 6 evidence points)
```
At E = bar with equal counsel, a coin flip. Counsel fees: `$5,000 · grade² + 0.5% of claim`; NPC firm fees SINK, player-firm fees TRANSFER; a legal retainer (12 §3.4) cuts fees 20%. **Reputation never enters this formula** (locked, bible row 5); it only affects pre-trial freezes (§3.6) and insurance premiums (08).

The verdict RNG draw comes from the `law` stream (`streamHash(worldSeed,'law',tick)`), so verdicts are replayable.

### §3.5 Two-part awards

A judgment needs attribution (evidence ≥ bar) and quantum (a HOW_MUCH source, §6). With attribution but no quantum, the court awards the statutory minimum: the tier's fine floor ×2 as restitution. This keeps proven-but-unquantified theft from being free.

### §3.6 Pre-trial measures

- **Asset freeze:** on an S4+ claim with evidence ≥ 55, the claimant may ask the court to freeze `1.2 × claim` of the defendant's assets (positions stay marked, cannot be sold). Defendant Reputation ≥ 60 may post a 30% bond instead. Reputation < 30 → the freeze is automatic.
- **Leverage cap:** a defendant in an active case of severity S3 or higher trades at half their license leverage (applied by 07's margin and buying-power rules, 07 §3.15.2; 05 only passes the result; ruling in 24).
- **Op lock:** a defendant cannot launch ops against their own accuser while the case is open (12 §4.1 BLOCKED).

### §3.7 Settlement and plea

Either side may offer settlement at any time; the game shows the expected verdict value (50–80% target). Guilty plea/cooperation −30% fine; naming a co-conspirator gives +30 evidence against them and immunity from Held. Settlements between linked accounts (15 collusion graph) are VOIDED and flagged.

### §3.8 Venues

| Venue | Hears | Burden | Time to verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meridian Civil Court | player-vs-player: theft damages, non-engine-verifiable contract breaches, NDA, defamation-by-false-filing | 40 | 6–24 real hours |
| Glasshill Criminal Bench | S3–S6 prosecutions (regulator, or a victim with evidence ≥ 70) | 70 | 24–48 h |
| Regulatory tribunal (one per regulator) | market conduct, antitrust, tax, disclosure | 55 | 48–72 h; consent decree any time |
| Clearing House arbitration | disputes over engine-verifiable clauses (appeal of an auto-penalty) | 40 | 2–6 h |

Civil and criminal cases on the same facts run in parallel; a criminal conviction counts as 70 evidence points in the civil case.

### §3.9 Sanctions menu

| Sanction | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fine | any conviction | §3.2 schedule, §3.3 split |
| Restitution/disgorgement | S2+ with a victim | TRANSFER, paid before fines |
| License suspension | S5 | Derivatives/Institutional license off 7 real days (open positions can only be closed) |
| Op ban | S3+ criminal | no ops for 3/7/14 days (S3/S4/S5+) → sets `AttackCapability.opBanUntilTick` (12) |
| Divestiture | antitrust, stolen-asset use | forced sale at fair value −15% (the haircut is a TRANSFER to the buyer, to the NPC sector when the buyer is NPC; not a SINK; E-31c/E-43/E-45) |
| Model destruction | proven distillation | the student model is deleted; its strategy modules revert |
| Held | S5–S6 criminal, or a third S4 in a chapter | §3.10 |
| Public record | every conviction | Reputation −10 to −30 (14); permanent line on the cross-chapter profile |

### §3.10 Held (the freeze equivalent)

A Held character is frozen, not wiped. Duration 6 h (S5) or 12 h (S6), +6 h for repeat, max 24 real hours/verdict and 48/chapter. During Held: no manual trading, ops, filing or buying. Businesses run at 70%, AIs keep running within their mandate, standing orders stay live, defense keeps running. Grace: Held starts 1 game day (1 real hour) after the verdict so the player can de-risk. Margin calls during Held liquidate at fair value with the markets band (05), never at a crashed print. The Held screen (14) shows the countdown, live P&L and read-only chat.

Resolves old roadmap Q8: Held applies only in the online world; in a solo sandbox it is off (fines, op bans and record only), because freezing a solo session is a rage-quit. DEFAULT (Mathis may override).

### §3.11 Appeals

One appeal per case. Fee 2% of the judgment (refunded on win). Needs new evidence ≥ 10 points or a procedural flag (wrong burden applied, stale evidence counted). The judgment is escrowed in the Clearing House during the appeal (≤ 24 real hours), so appealing never delays a victim's money beyond a day.

### §3.12 Collection, garnishment and the floor

- Judgment debts collect from cash first, then liquid positions at fair value, then businesses at −15%.
- If that would breach the floor, the remainder becomes a garnishment plan: 30% of daily income, interest-free, until paid.
- Floor: collection never takes a character below `PARAM.protected_floor_cash_real` = $7,300 × MSI liquid (E-31b/E-43/E-45, Designer; was $5,000) plus one T1 business.
- The Victim Compensation Fund advances up to 50% of an unpaid award immediately and recovers it from garnishment.
- Trading losses still use the once-per-chapter Fresh Start owned by 14 §3.13 (07 runs the liquidation mechanics); bankruptcy comeback gives a small perk (L13).

### §3.13 Statute of limitations

Evidence decays with τ = 21 real days; a cold op becomes unprovable around day 30–45 unless a fresh source arrives (a canary trip or residue match restarts the clock for that source only). Filing deadlines: civil 28 real days after detection; regulator 42 days after the op; criminal any time inside the chapter; S5/S6 no deadline inside a chapter. Chapter settlement: open cases resolve at expected value in the final 3 game days (expected verdict × the parties' offers, whichever is closer to the evidence score), so "run out the clock" fails. Convictions carry into the cross-chapter Reputation record; money does not.

## §4 Investigation pipeline

```
Incident ─▶ Triage ─▶ Investigation ─▶ Case file ─▶ Filing ─▶ Settlement or Trial ─▶ Enforcement
```

1. **Incident.** A 12 detection roll succeeds, a canary/honeypot trips, a regulator threshold fires, or a staffer whistleblows. Loud ops always alert; quiet ops may sit for days.
2. **Triage (free).** A suspect board (§2.4) of up to 5 candidates by legitimate-signal prior; each has an evidence bar starting at whatever the incident yielded. The real attacker is off the board 15% of the time.
3. **Investigation (paid).** The victim buys actions against one suspect at a time:

| Action | Cost | Time | Evidence gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-house review (needs Monitoring ≥ 2) | $5k | 2 h | 5–10 |
| Forensic audit | $25k + $5k/model-or-dataset | 6–24 h | residue 25–60 |
| PI firm investigation | $50k–$1M by grade | 12–48 h | `10 + 30·(grade/5)` |
| Replay subpoena (needs a filed civil case) | court fee | 1 game day | 15–35 |
| Informant | $10k–$200k | 1 game day | 5–20 |
| Public bounty clause | your bounty | open | others submit evidence and claim it |

4. **Case file.** When a suspect's evidence passes a bar, the File button enables; below it, the bands show how much more is needed.
5. **Filing.** Court fee 1% of claim (min $500, sink). The defendant is notified and sees evidence categories, not sources. Regulators can take over S5–S6 files for free.
6. **Settlement or trial** (§3.4–3.7).
7. **Enforcement** (§3.9–3.12).

Bounded investigation: at most 3 active suspects at once; sqrt returns on spend. A poor victim can sell the case to a player law firm or bounty hunter for 30–50% of the award (a transfer). Malicious prosecution: filing against someone who turns out innocent (case lost and the true attacker later convicted, or evidence fell below 25 at trial) costs the filer the defendant's legal fees + 0.25% of the filer's NW (sink) and −5 Reputation; three such losses in a chapter → 7-day non-contract-filing lock. Honest mistakes at evidence ≥ 40 are never punished beyond costs.

## §5 Evidence model (what 12 and this module feed the court)

Evidence sources arrive as SEALED engine events (12 §5) plus ledger-derived sources (tax audit, §6.3). 13 never reads hidden state for free: each investigation action costs money and real time. Evidence can only derive from engine-generated SEALED events (`sealedRefs`), so a player can never fabricate it (anti-forgery guarantee for bounties and whistleblowing). Discovery rules: a `FILED` case unlocks the replay subpoena of a specified tick range (02 replay API); a court order (criminal) unlocks a forensic audit of seized models.

Watermark forensics (12/11): a forensic audit finds residue with `p = 1 − e^(−3·residue)`, worth 25–60 points by residue, proving WHO for distillation but never HOW_MUCH. Deletion of a student after notice is obstruction (+20 evidence, fine ×1.5); residue lives on any model trained from the student, so deleting the student does not erase the trail.

## §6 Damages

```
damages_model = max( replacement_cost, lost_license_revenue, thief_gain )
replacement_cost   = teacher_training_spend · (student_MCS / teacher_MCS)
thief_gain         = thief PnL + license revenue attributable to the student since the theft
                     (attribution = student's share of the thief's deployed MCS)
damages_data  = dataset_market_value · copies_proven + resale_proceeds
```
Residue proves WHO; quantum needs one of: license query logs, a replay citation of the exfiltration ticks, the thief's leaderboard/licensing gains, or a canary trip showing resale. The S4 penalty then applies: restitution 2× damages + disgorgement of thief_gain (counted once) + the S4 fine.

### §6.3 Tax audits (the "cost of being seen")
```
p_audit per game quarter = 0.02 · (1 + min(3, lifestyle_spend / declared_income)) · (1 + 0.5·tips)
```
A clean audit costs only time (+2 Reputation). An audit finding unexplained wealth (inflows tagged with illicit reason codes, which the ledger knows) adds 20–40 evidence to every open triple involving those funds. This is the "caught on taxes" route: a quiet criminal with a loud lifestyle is found by accountants.

## §7 Regulators and surveillance

### §7.1 The four regulators

| Regulator | Watches | Inputs | Typical case |
|---|---|---|---|
| ECA (Exchange Conduct Authority) | market abuse: manipulation, spoofing, insider trading, front-running tapped orders, false Wire posts | 05 surveillance score, filings (15), news/position overlap | S5 |
| CMA (Competition & Mergers Agency) | monopoly, cartels, compute corners | city-sector share > 60% (05/09), compute-futures concentration, non-compete clusters | fine S5 or divestiture |
| Revenue Office | tax, unexplained wealth | declared income vs lifestyle spend (14), cash inflows with no legitimate reason code | S2 audit, evidence boosts |
| CyberCert | cyber incidents | incident registry, world hack rate (12), model registry (11) | coordinates S3–S6 cyber prosecutions, issues zero-day patches (12 §3.6) |

### §7.2 Surveillance ownership
05 computes `surveillanceScore(playerId, window) ∈ [0,100]` per player per game day; the ECA acts:

| Score | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 60 for 3 game days | Inquiry: player notified, trades watched, nothing frozen; converts to 10–20 evidence |
| ≥ 80, or ≥ 60 plus any other source | Investigation: +5 evidence/game day while the pattern persists |
| Evidence ≥ 55 | Tribunal case or consent decree |

### §7.3 Regulator capacity (finite attention)
Each partition's regulators run at most `floor(activeChars / PARAM.regulator_capacity_divisor)` active investigations (default divisor 200 → 1 per 200 active chars), prioritized by `harm × evidence`. Tips (whistleblowers, bounty claims) jump the queue. This makes regulators steerable but not omniscient; the capacity knob is owned here.

### §7.4 Whistleblowers
Any player or staff member can report. A report leading to conviction pays 5% of the fine from the whistleblower pool, capped at $50M. Three false reports in a chapter lock reporting for 7 days.

## §8 Insurance claims (adjudication; 08 owns products)

08 defines policies, capital rules and pricing; 13 adjudicates claims. Products (summary): Cyber (cash loot, forensic costs, restore time; excludes fines, own crimes, units left at coverage < 0.5); Ransomware/BI (lost income while paused, cap 3 game days; excludes ransom payments); Collectibles/property (theft E5, fire, flood; excludes provenance-flagged items); Model/IP (replacement cost up to limit; excludes unwatermarked models); Legal expense (counsel fees; excludes fines, restitution, Held time).

Adjudication: `payout = clamp(grossLoss − deductible, 0, policyLimit)`, deductible 10% of each claim. Insurance fraud (a staged breach with a linked account to claim) is S4; the collusion graph plus "attacker and victim share a counterparty cluster" flags it automatically, voids the policy, and reimburses the insurer. NPC insurer (Aegis) payouts are a faucet; player-insurer payouts are a transfer. During a cyber pandemic (12 §3.6) Aegis caps total payouts per player at 25% of the policy limit.

## §9 Player intents

Namespace `law.*`. Rate limit 20/tick. Each shows the evidence score, the burden needed, and the likely outcome before confirming.

- `law.open_investigation { triple, suspectId }` — opens an Investigation (max 3 active). 
- `law.run_action { investigationId, action }` — buys an investigation action (§4 table).
- `law.file_case { triple, venue, claimMoney, counselGrade }` — only when evidence ≥ the venue bar; pays the 1% court fee.
- `law.offer_settlement { caseId, amount }` / `law.accept_settlement { caseId }`.
- `law.plead { caseId, guilty, nameCoConspirator? }`.
- `law.request_freeze { caseId }` — pre-trial asset freeze (S4+, evidence ≥ 55).
- `law.post_bond { caseId }` — defendant posts 30% bond instead of a freeze (Reputation ≥ 60).
- `law.appeal { caseId, grounds }`.
- `law.sell_case { caseId, assigneeId, share }` — assign a claim for 30–50% of the award.
- `law.report { subjectId, triple }` — whistleblower report.
- `law.file_insurance_claim { policyId, perilRef }`.
- `law.counter_suit { caseId }` — defendant files defamation-by-false-filing (malicious prosecution, §4).
- `law.set_bounty { triple, amount }` — public evidence bounty (paid on conviction, pro-rata by points).

Validation examples: `file_case` refused below the venue bar; `request_freeze` refused below S4/evidence 55; `appeal` refused without new evidence ≥ 10 or a procedural flag; `sell_case` share clamped to [0.30, 0.50]; all actions between linked accounts VOIDED and flagged.

## §11 NPC behaviour

- **NPC regulators** act automatically on surveillance and filings within capacity, prioritizing harm × evidence; they issue consent decrees to NPC and player defendants and take over S5–S6 cyber files.
- **NPC victims** (firms, banks, households, 16) auto-investigate and file when evidence clears a bar, using NPC law firms; they settle around the expected-verdict band.
- **NPC law firms and PI firms** are service nodes a player hires (fees SINK); player-owned law/PI firms (09) compete for the same work (fees TRANSFER). Elite NPC counsel is grade 5.
- **NPC courts and arbiters** apply §3.4 deterministically; arbiter reputation (for the player-run arbitration market, V3 Part 6) is a public number, so players can choose arbiters.
- **NPC whistleblowers**: a turned mole or an underpaid staffer (loyalty < 30) may whistleblow on their employer at a small per-day probability, feeding evidence onto open triples.

## §14 Edge cases

1. **Evidence crosses the bar then decays below it before filing** — the File button disables again; the case can be refiled when a fresh source lifts it, within the filing deadline.
2. **Defendant's NW rises between offense and verdict** — fine uses max(NW at offense, NW at verdict).
3. **Defendant spends to the floor before trial** — fine still computed on max NW; collection beyond the floor becomes garnishment.
4. **Both a civil and criminal case on the same facts** — run in parallel; a criminal conviction injects 70 points into the civil case; double restitution is netted (disgorgement counted once).
5. **Appeal filed at chapter end** — resolved in the chapter-settlement window at expected value; the escrow releases within 24 real hours regardless.
6. **Victim sells a case then also pursues it** — selling assigns the claim; the victim cannot double-file the same triple/venue.
7. **Linked accounts litigate each other** — VOIDED and flagged; court fees still charged as a sink to deter the attempt.
8. **Held during an open margin position** — the 1-hour grace lets the player de-risk; liquidations during Held use fair value with the band.
9. **Regulator at capacity when a high-harm case arrives** — it preempts the lowest harm×evidence item in the queue; the preempted one re-queues.
10. **Whistleblower report on a triple with no real op** — yields no evidence (sources only derive from SEALED events); a false report counts toward the 3-strike lock.
11. **Insurance claim on a self-inflicted or linked-account breach** — fraud flag denies and refers S4.
12. **Wrongful conviction (verdict vs ledger truth)** — logged for the 20 truth-machine audit; target band < 3% wrongful; an appeal with a procedural flag is the in-world remedy.

## §16 Acceptance tests

1. `evidence(triple, t)` applies `e^(−days/21)` decay and 0.5^(n−1) same-source stacking; two sources of different types both count at 100%.
2. No case is fileable below its venue bar (civil 40, regulator 55, criminal 70).
3. A fine equals `clamp(pct·max(NW_offense, NW_verdict), floor, cap)` with the tier pct/floor/cap of §3.2; one verdict's fines never exceed 35% of NW and chapter fines never exceed 60% of peak NW.
4. Every fine posts 85% `FINE_SINK`, 10% `VCF_TRANSFER`, 5% `WHISTLE_TRANSFER` (sums to 100% within rounding, 03).
5. Restitution and identifiable-victim disgorgement post as TRANSFER; court fees and NPC legal fees post as SINK.
6. `p_verdict_for_claimant` equals `σ((E−bar)/8 + 0.15·(gradeC−gradeD))`; counsel swing never exceeds ±0.75 logit.
7. Reputation changes no verdict, fine or fee; it only changes pre-trial freeze thresholds and (08) premiums.
8. A judgment with attribution but no quantum awards exactly the tier floor ×2 as restitution.
9. Collection never takes a character below $5,000 liquid plus one T1 business; any remainder becomes a 30%-of-income interest-free garnishment plan.
10. The Victim Compensation Fund advances ≤ 50% of an unpaid award and recovers it from garnishment.
11. Held starts exactly verdictTick + 720 ticks (1 game day grace), lasts 6h/12h (+6h repeat), never exceeds 24h/verdict or 48h/chapter; businesses run at 70% and AIs keep running during it.
12. In a solo sandbox, Held is off and only fines/op-bans/record apply (resolves Q8).
13. One appeal per case; the judgment is escrowed and released within 24 real hours.
14. A regulator never runs more than `floor(activeChars/200)` investigations; a tip jumps the queue; capacity preemption drops the lowest harm×evidence item.
15. The ECA opens an inquiry at surveillance ≥ 60 for 3 game days and escalates to investigation at ≥ 80 or ≥ 60 plus any other source.
16. A whistleblower reward equals 5% of the fine, capped at $50M; a third false report in a chapter locks reporting for 7 real days.
17. An insurance payout equals `clamp(grossLoss − 0.10·grossLoss, 0, limit)`; a linked-account staged claim is denied and referred S4.
18. All evidence sources carry non-empty `sealedRefs`; a report or bounty with no underlying SEALED event yields zero evidence (anti-forgery).
19. Settlements, cases, and sanctions between linked accounts are VOIDED and flagged in 100% of simulated attempts.
20. Verdicts are deterministic: the same world seed yields the same verdict draw for the same case.
21. Over a 20-seed chapter, wrongful convictions (verdict vs ledger truth) < 3%; conviction rate on attributed ops 40–65%; detected-but-never-attributed share 30–50%.
22. Over a 20-seed chapter, fines + court fees + NPC legal fees fall in 0.5–1.5% of money supply per game year; the Aegis loss ratio is 60–80%; 5–15% of the top 1% lose ≥ 20% of NW to §12 routes; zero sub-floor bankruptcies caused by crime or court.
23. A malicious prosecution (lost case + true attacker later convicted, or evidence < 25 at trial) charges the filer the defendant's fees + 0.25% NW and −5 Reputation; honest filings at evidence ≥ 40 are never penalized beyond costs.
24. Deleting a student model after a notice adds +20 evidence and multiplies the fine by 1.5; residue on any model trained from the student still resolves the triple.
25. A player law-firm fee posts as TRANSFER while the equivalent NPC fee posts as SINK, for identical work.

## Goal
This task implements the clock-driven side of the books.
- **Accruals (03 §3.3.7).** Daily Act/364 interest accrual and per-day accrual of rent, wages, upkeep and subscriptions, for every calendar day. Negative rates swap sides (03 §8 edge 18).
- **Period-close registry (03 §3.15).** Later tasks plug their period-close steps into this registry. It runs the ten steps in the exact 03 §3.15 order: steps 1–5 and the cash-moving part of step 7 in the P2 boundary hook; steps 6, 8 and 9 in 03's P13 handler just before `commitTick` (stage `P13_PRE`, so they see every entry queued in the closing tick); and step 10 right after `commitTick` (stage `P13_POST`). No postings can cross partitions after barrier B3 (T02-14 R3/B3), so `P13_PRE` steps may queue only single-entity or same-partition entries; `assertLocal` enforces this.
- **`LED_PERIOD_CLOSE` (year end).** Every 4xxx/5xxx/6xxx balance moves into 3200, or into 3900 for characters and alliances. The system entity's 5900/4900 move into 3990.
- **Standing-facility conversion (03 §3.3.4, last paragraph; 03#10).** Any bank reserve account (1002) still negative at day close becomes an overnight `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` loan. Central banks live on the FINANCE partition (T02-14 home rule), so the conversion runs in three places:
  - in P13 for banks homed on the same partition as their central bank;
  - in 03's P12 handler at t = 719 for banks on PLAYER partitions, whose legs go out at B3;
  - in the next tick's P2 (DAY_OPEN) as a catch-up for any reserve that became negative after the P12 pass.

## Implementation steps
0. In T03-01's `LedgerFactKind`, add `'FACILITY_RATE_E6'`. Its subject is a ccy and its value is a number. 04 writes it: the overnight standing-facility rate, which 04 also uses to charge `CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST`.
1. **Accruals.**
   - `registerAccrual` validates that both entities exist, that `a !== b`, that INTEREST has `principalKey` and `rate`, and that the other kinds have `perDay > 0n`. It rejects `LED_PERSISTENT_NO_INTEREST` when either value leg is a 2990 key: persistent claims carry no interest (03 §3.14.5 rule 4; T03-26). It fills `valueLegA/B` from `DEFAULT_LEGS` and sets `lastAccruedDay = (startDay ?? today) − 1`.
   - `dailyAccrualAmount` for INTEREST:
     - `p = committedBalance(read, principalKey)`, read as a magnitude: for a credit-normal principal (a deposit 2100 on the payer's side), use `−balance`;
     - `rateE6` is `fixedE6`, or the fact value plus `spreadE6`;
     - the amount is `accrue(p, BigInt(rateE6), days)`. The rate may be negative, giving a negative amount.
   - For the other kinds the amount is `perDay × BigInt(days)`.
   - `runAccruals` (step 1, DAY_CLOSE), for each ACTIVE row in id order:
     - `days = min(today, endDay ?? today) − lastAccruedDay`. Days lost to a halt are caught up in one posting: still Act/364, every calendar day, weekends included;
     - if `days > 0`, post `ctx.ledger.accruePair(a, b, amt, ccy, 'LED_ACCRUAL', { valueLegA, valueLegB, sub })`. A negative `amt` swaps the sides (T03-07; 03 §8 edge 18). A zero amount posts nothing;
     - set `lastAccruedDay = today`, and set ENDED once `endDay` is reached.
   - An entity that is DISSOLVED ends its accruals.
   - `settleAccrual`: `amt = min(amt ?? accruedBalance, accruedBalance)`. Post `ctx.ledger.pay(b, a, amt, ccy, code, { valueLegA: row.valueLegB, valueLegB: row.valueLegA, sub })`, which is Dr b:payable / Cr a:receivable plus the cash rail. `code` must be the owner's settlement code (for example `BNK_LOAN_INTEREST`, `CMP_INTERCOMPANY_INTEREST` or `SOC_LOAN_P2P_INTEREST`); the catalog decides whether the caller may request it.
   - Bonds traded between coupon dates carry accrued interest in the trade itself (`BOND_ACCRUED_INTEREST`, owned by 06). No accrual row is needed for that.
   - Hourly business operations settle cash immediately with hh/frm (09). Leases settle monthly (10) and bonds at coupon or maturity (06), each by its own module's schedule.
2. **Step registry.**
   - `registerCloseStep` stores the steps in a module-level sorted list; the list is frozen after `installLedger` (T03-28).
   - `runCloseStage(ctx, stage)` runs, for each step whose `stage` matches and whose `when` is in `ctx.cal.boundaries`, in order of `(no, order, name)`:
     - `id = `${when}:${periodIndexOf(ctx, when)}:${no}:${name}``; if a `led.close` row with that id exists, skip it;
     - otherwise call `run(ctx)` and write the row.
   - This makes period close exactly-once even when a halt delays a boundary: a year that ends inside a halt closes at its game-day boundary after resume (02 §3.19.4; 03 §8 edge 11), and a replay after rollback re-creates the same rows.
   - `periodIndexOf` returns the index of the period that is closing (DAY_CLOSE → `gameDay`, YEAR_CLOSE → `year`, and so on).
3. **Steps this task registers.** These are the 03 §3.15 numbers. The `order` field breaks ties inside a step; later tasks register the rest.

   | no | order | name | when | stage | run |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 0 | `accruals` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runAccruals` |
   | 2 | 0 | `mtm` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runMarkToMarket` |
   | 3 | 0 | `ecl` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runEclProvisions` |
   | 4 | 0 | `appraisals` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runAppraisals` (it applies the weekly cadence and the QUARTER_CLOSE PE rule itself) |
   | 4 | 10 | `replacementCaps` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runReplacementCaps` |
   | 4 | 20 | `goodwill` | YEAR_CLOSE | P2 | `runGoodwillImpairment` |
   | 5 | 0 | `schedules` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `runSchedules` |
   | 5 | 10 | `recycle` | DAY_CLOSE | P2 | `recycleSurplusOnDisposal` |
   | 9 | 50 | `periodClose` | YEAR_CLOSE | P13_PRE | `runPeriodClose` |

   Registered by later tasks:
   - T03-21: step 6 `taxProvision` (DAY_CLOSE, P13_PRE); step 7 `propertyTax` and `vatRemit` (MONTH_CLOSE, **P2**: these are PAYs to the treasury on FINANCE, so they must be queued before B3, and they remit what is accrued as of P2); step 9 order 20 `lossCarryForward` (YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE).
   - T03-23: step 8 `installments` (QUARTER_CLOSE); step 9 order 10 `annualAssessment` (YEAR_CLOSE).
   - T03-21: step 9 order 60 `groupElections` (YEAR_CLOSE, P13_PRE; state only).
   - T03-19: step 8 order 50 `statementsQueued` (QUARTER_CLOSE, P13_PRE); step 10 `statements` (every close boundary, P13_POST).
   - Every P13_PRE step calls `assertLocal` for the entities it posts to. Tax provisions, reclasses and TaxAccount state are single-entity, so they always pass.
4. **Projection.** `projectedBalance(ctx, key)` adds three things:
   - `committedBalance`;
   - for each queued entry of this tick without `invalid`, its postings on `key` (debit +, credit −);
   - for each leg in the partition's `acceptLegs` buffer of this tick on `key`, its signed amount.
   P13_PRE steps (tax provision, period close, local facility) use it so that they see every entry queued in P1–P12 of the closing tick. The postings they queue commit in the same `commitTick` (03 §3.15: "All postings of steps (1)–(9) commit in P13 of the closing tick").
5. **`runPeriodClose` (YEAR_CLOSE, step 9).** For every entity in this partition (sorted ids, not DISSOLVED) and every account with code 4000–6999 (sorted keys) whose `projectedBalance` is non-zero:
   - `t = closingTargetOf(e, code)`:
     - sys and code 5900 or 4900 → 3990, which keeps `netIssuance` (T03-11) unchanged;
     - kind `chr` or `ali` → 3900;
     - everything else, including sys's other P&L accounts, fnd, spv, hh, frm and row → 3200.
   - If the balance `b > 0n` (debit: expense or loss), post `ctx.ledger.reclass(E, b, ccy, 'LED_PERIOD_CLOSE', code, t, { sub: a.sub })`, which is Dr t / Cr code.
   - If `b < 0n`, post `reclass(E, −b, ccy, 'LED_PERIOD_CLOSE', t, code, { sub })`, which is Dr code / Cr t.
   - The `sub` is the P&L account's sub. The equity target ignores the sub (T03-07 keys equity accounts without sub).
   - After commit, every P&L account is 0, and 3300 (the derived parent of 4xxx–6xxx) is 0.
   - Legs from another partition that arrive after the closing tick belong to the next period. B3 delivery is tick-bounded (02), and the projection counts the legs that arrived this tick.
6. **Year-end ordering across partitions.** Each partition closes its own entities in its own P13. No cross-partition reads are needed, because P&L accounts are local to their entity.
7. **Standing-facility conversion** (`convertNegativeReserves(ctx, mode)`):
   - **Candidates.** For each `bnk` entity (and `exc`/`ccp` if they hold 1002) of the partition, and each ccy with an account key `B:1002::ccy`: `r = projectedBalance`. If `r < 0n`, the bank gets a facility:
     - `facilityId = `sf:${B}:${ccy}:${closingGameDay}``;
     - build the entry with `tplCbCredit(env04, cbkId(ccy), B, −r, ccy, 'CBK_STANDING_FACILITY', { sub: facilityId })` and push it with `runtimeOf(ctx).queue.push(entry)`. `env04` is the template env with `requestedBy = { module: '04', phase, entityId: B }`;
     - result: `Dr B:1002 / Cr cbk:2002:B` and `Dr cbk:1307:<facilityId> / Cr B:2120:<facilityId>`, after which reserves are exactly 0.
   - **Modes.** The cbk lives on FINANCE (T02-14 home rule), and no leg can cross partitions after B3. So:
     - `'P13_LOCAL'` (03's P13 handler at t = 719, last P13_PRE action, after steps 6–9): only banks whose cbk is on the same partition, which are the NPC banks on FINANCE. These are exact for the closing tick.
     - `'P12'` (03's `phases.P12` at t = 719 on PLAYER/COORD partitions): banks whose cbk is remote. The cbk legs leave at B3. The projection includes everything queued up to 03's P12 handler.
     - `'DAY_OPEN_CATCHUP'` (03's P2 DAY_OPEN hook of the next tick): any committed `1002 < 0` left on a remote-cbk bank, from payments queued after 03's P12 handler in the closing tick (modules 14 and 15 run later in P12). The loan uses the closing day's `facilityId` and is dated to the conversion tick.
   - **Ownership.** `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` is owned by 04, and its catalog description says "incl. automatic day-close conversion, §3.3.4". 03 performs the automatic part on 04's behalf, so the catalog ownership rule still holds (requestedBy module 04). `LEDGER_AUTOMATIC_CODES` is the closed list of codes for which 03 may do this.
   - 04 repays the facility the next day (`CBK_STANDING_FACILITY_REPAY`) and charges `CBK_FACILITY_INTEREST` at `FACILITY_RATE_E6`.
   - **I10 at day close** (T03-10):
     - at t = 719, banks with a local cbk must have every 1002 ≥ 0;
     - remote-cbk banks must have every 1002 ≥ −intraday limit at t = 719, and ≥ 0 after the DAY_OPEN catch-up of the next tick (checked in that tick).
8. **Wiring (done by T03-28's phases.ts).**
   - The P2 boundary hooks call `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P2')`, and DAY_OPEN also calls `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'DAY_OPEN_CATCHUP')`.
   - `phases.P12` at t = 719 calls `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P12')`.
   - `phases.P13` → T03-09 `runP13`, in this order:
     - restricted releases (T03-12);
     - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_PRE')`;
     - `convertNegativeReserves(ctx, 'P13_LOCAL')` (DAY_CLOSE only);
     - `commitTick`;
     - the post-commit updates;
     - `runCloseStage(ctx, 'P13_POST')`.

  - TestWorld with bnk_X (intraday credit limit fact $5M) and chr_A holding a $3M deposit at X.
  - At t = 700, A pays $3M to chr_B, who receives into settlement cash, so X:1002 becomes −$3M + its prior reserves ($1M) = −$2M.
  - Run to t = 719. Assert:
    - one `CBK_STANDING_FACILITY` entry of exactly $2,000,000.000000 with requestedBy module '04';

    - a bank with reserves ≥ 0 gets no entry.
  - Run it twice: with `partitions: 1` (bank and cbk co-located, `P13_LOCAL` mode) and with `partitions: 2`, where X is a player bank on PLAYER and cbk is on FINANCE (`P12` mode). Both runs end with the same balances.
  - Variant (remote cbk): a payment queued by a test module '15' in P12 after 03's handler drives X to −$0.5M. At t = 719, I10 passes with the intraday limit. The DAY_OPEN catch-up of the next tick posts a $0.5M facility, and the strict check passes in that tick.

  - $1,000,000 loan at 5% for one day accrues exactly 137,362,637 micro (1e12 × 0.05 / 364, half-even);

  - accrual every calendar day, including weekends;
  - a 3-day halt catches up in one posting equal to `accrue(p, r, 3)`;
  - RENT per-day accrual and `settleAccrual` clear both receivable and payable;

- `periodClose.test.ts`:

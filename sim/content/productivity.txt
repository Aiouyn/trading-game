Productivity growth by sector, per year, at a firm with an average chief executive and average investment.
Each firm's own rate is this times a factor between about 0.2 and 1.8: skilled chiefs and firms that keep
installing machinery improve faster, weak ones barely improve. Wages follow the labour-weighted average
(see the wage settlement in modules/economy), so real wages rise with it; firms that fall behind carry a
higher unit cost, charge more and lose share to rivals.

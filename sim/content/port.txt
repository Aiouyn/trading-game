The harbour. Ships, berths and the time a cargo spends at sea live here.
A route names its owner (a freight firm), how many days it sails, and which goods it carries.
BERTHS and CRANES are how many ships can unload at once. HANDS is the dock crew one berth needs.
BUFFER_DAYS is the warehouse, in days of ordinary imports. STORM_DAYS is how long a harbour storm
keeps ships outside. STRIKE_DAYS is how long a dock strike closes the port, and how many days it
adds to a voyage while the slowdown lasts. Two spare berths let a backlog clear after a closure.
COVER is the share of an import order released while the port is shut. ADVALOREM is the opening
freight rate as a fraction of cargo value. TARIFF is the customs duty before politics scales it.

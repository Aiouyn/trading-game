Scenarios. A start, a goal and a clock. The city is the same one worldgen builds:
a SEED and a history length in YEARS, or a WORLD snapshot when that folder is present.
CASH is dollars. LIMIT is days, and 0 means no clock. LOAN, REGULATOR and RIVALS are
multipliers on the player's own credit, investigations and takeover premium.
A difficulty above 1, chosen at the start, tightens all three.

[OBJECT:SCENARIO]

[SCENARIO:first-paycheck]
  [NAME:First paycheck]
  [STORY:The first wage has cleared and the savings book shows fifteen hundred dollars.]
  [STORY:You are twenty-two, new at the firm, and nobody in the city owes you a favour.]
  [STORY:In ninety days the book has to show twenty thousand dollars.]
  [AGE:22] [CASH:1500] [JOB:any] [FAMILY:none] [CONTACTS:4]
  [SEED:1] [YEARS:0]
  [GOAL:WORTH:20000] [LIMIT:90]
  [LOAN:1.5] [REGULATOR:0.4] [RIVALS:0.6]
  [BEGINNER]
  [HINT:desk:Read the news before you buy.]
  [HINT:wire:The wire is what the city already knows.]
  [HINT:contacts:Talk to a contact. Ask them about your firm.]
  [HINT:bonds:A bond is a loan to the government. The price moves with the rate.]
  [HINT:futures:A future locks a price. Month 0 is the near contract.]
  [HINT:crypto:A coin is a bet. You can lose the stake.]
  [HINT:fx:The exchange rate is the price of the other city's money.]
  [HINT:economy:Unemployment, prices and the policy rate are the weather.]
  [HINT:log:The log is the chronicle. why EVENT shows the cause.]
  [HINT:family:A spouse and a child are how a house continues.]
  [HINT:options:A call is a right to buy. opt lists the strikes.]
  [HINT:homes:Buy a home to live in. A second one can be let.]
  [HINT:help:help lists every command. wait 1m lets a month pass.]
  [HINT:run:The run page is the clock. Pause it when you want to think.]

The rest of the world, as one aggregate economy. It is not simulated person by person.
Its currency, its central bank rate, its price level and the goods the port trades live here.
The exchange rate is not in this file after the opening print: the forex desk sets it by clearing orders.

Tags on WORLD:
  (NAME:text)            what the chronicle calls it
  (CURRENCY:USD)         ledger instrument FX:USD, integer cents, issued by the foreign central bank
  (SIZE:n)               how many times a home-sized economy the foreign one is (mobile capital)
  (START_RATE:r)         foreign central bank rate at the open
  (START_FX:x)           opening home cents per foreign cent
  (INFLATION:r)          foreign price level, per year, before the cycle
  (SPREAD:x)             dealer spread around the mid (a quote, not a target rate)
  (ELASTICITY:e)         export orders rise this steeply when home goods get cheaper in foreign currency
  (IMPORT_SHARE:s)       fraction of an input bought abroad when the landed price beats the home seller
  (EXPORT_SHARE:s)       export orders as a fraction of home sales when prices match
  (CARRY:c)              how hard mobile capital chases the higher interest rate
  (BOND_SHARE:s)         share of a fund's book in foreign bonds when the foreign rate leads by 1pp
  (RATE_CYCLE:years)     length of the foreign central bank's slow cycle
  (RATE_AMP:r)           how far that cycle moves the foreign rate either side of START_RATE
  (ACTIVITY_CYCLE:years) length of the foreign demand cycle
  (ACTIVITY_AMP:a)       how far foreign demand swings around trend
  (DEPTH_DAYS:n)         dealer book depth, in days of opening trade
  (DISCOUNT:d)           foreign goods start this much cheaper than home goods, in their own currency
  (HIKE:r)               size of a foreign funding-squeeze hike
  (HIKE_CHANCE:p)        chance of that hike each year
  (HIKE_DAYS:n)          how long the hike stays on
  (RECESS_AT:a)          foreign demand below this fraction of trend is a recession

Tags on TRADED:
  (FLOW:import or export)
  (SHARE:s)              overrides IMPORT_SHARE or EXPORT_SHARE for this good

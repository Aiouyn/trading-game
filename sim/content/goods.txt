Goods and the recipe that makes each one. Order matters: firms produce in file order each day.
Perishable goods are services; they cannot be stocked, so they come after everything they consume that day
(electricity and freight first, because almost everything needs them; they use yesterday's stocks of fuel).

Tags:
  (SECTOR:X)           one of the 13 sectors
  (OUTPUT:n)           units made per worker-day at skill 1.0
  (INPUT:GOOD:q)       units of GOOD consumed per unit made
  (CONSUMER:s)         share of household budgets (normalised over all goods)
  (GOVERNMENT:s)       share of government purchases (normalised)
  (PERISHABLE)         cannot be stored; unsold output is lost at day end

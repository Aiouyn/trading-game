The power grid. Plant types are the machines a utility can own. Districts are the
neighbourhoods the grid sheds, heaviest load first, when demand outruns the plants.
LOAD is the district's share of demand. INTENSITY scales how hard a sector draws
power, on top of the electricity already in its recipe.

Disasters. Once a year each hazard rolls CHANCE on its own day.
WHERE picks the target: one firm, homes in a neighbourhood, or the port.
MACHINERY and STOCK are the share of a firm's plant and goods destroyed.
FABRIC is condition points taken off a building. HOMES is the share of homes in the place.
HURT and KILL are people on the spot, not a city-wide rate.

# Beginner playtest — first paycheck

Iso Haltorley, 22, seed 1, cash $1,500 at Verdant Agrichem. Ninety days to show net worth $20,000. Lost on year 1, month 4, day 1.

## The session

1. Day 1 header: "Thin →  Tight 3w →  Calm →  Sound →  Slight →  Quiet →", dynasty worth $65,241.06, sixteen contacts who already "Trusts you", hint "Read the news before you buy." `news` printed "no news".
2. `help` is buy, short, fut, opt, bond, coin. `money`, `status` and `look` came back "did you mean move?", "hints?" and "ask?". `why cash` was "world created from seed '1'".
3. `buy silicrest 10` and `buy verdant 1` (twice) sat as orders. Next day: "eq:frm_silicrest 10 @ $0.01" and "eq:frm_verdant 2 @ $0.01". Cash $1,499.88. A home appeared that I had not bought: "#6864 Midtown $57840.15 home".
4. `overtime` was the first number that moved: "Verdant Agrichem pays 632 for the extra days." Cash became $2,032. `borrow 20000` printed "borrowed 0.06 at 7.00%." `borrow 500` was refused. `deposit 100` worked; a month later it was still $100.00, so I `withdraw 100`.
5. `sell 6864`: "you do not own it." The chronicle's first event is still "Iso Haltorley sells a home."
6. Month 2, the paper was twenty-four earnings lines. Verdant: profit $16.6k, EPS $1.66, and my two shares still $0.01. A private line, "the desk", said insiders see a monthly profit of 13583. `ask 0` and `ask 8`: "they know nothing private about that firm." `talk` only named the person.
7. `buy ledgerly 1` filled at $0.01. `buy herald` was "not listed herald". `opt ledgerly` strikes were $0.01 to $0.05, calls bid 0.00 ask 0.00. `bond buy 10y 1` showed as order "buy 16000" and settled as "1 issues, face $160.00". Cash fell by about $172. `why bond` was my arrival at Verdant. `coin buy ember 1`: "no coin ember".
8. `found Corner Shop / FOOD 500`: "unknown sector FOOD". `found Corner Shop / MANUFACTURING 2000 loan`: "you found Corner Shop (frm_n66), making VEHICLES." The header changed to Corner Shop. Runway went to "Broke 1w". Net worth fell by the $2,000. The firm line said cash $3,986.69, 2 workers, sales $0. `ceo Corner`: "no such firm".
9. I set dividend to 1 and price to 100, because the report never showed the old price. A month later: sales $821.16, costs $1,323.90, last profit -$4,848.62, firm cash $1,657.77. My own cash was $1,731. `why profit frm_n66` said I had cut the vehicles price 46.7%. The insider line said a loss of 3090.
10. I put the price back on auto, `overtime` ("Corner Shop pays 632"), and waited. "First paycheck — loss. You did not reach $20,000.00 in time." Net worth $1,925.17, index 0.67×, me 1.28×. The sheet's only score point was still "day 0 $1500.00".

## Confused

- The six words have no names. `why Thin` said "no number called Thin. try worth, cash, stress, health, reputation, attention, wage, rate".
- Dynasty worth stayed near $66,000 while the sheet said $1,500, then $793, then $1,925. Nothing says the dynasty number is not the book the goal is watching.
- Every month the blurb still said the savings book shows fifteen hundred dollars. The hint never changed off "Read the news before you buy."
- "homes: #6864 Midtown $57840.15 home" reads as mine. `homes` then said "rent 202" and did not say I rent it. `sell 6864` refused.
- `bond buy 10y 1` versus "buy 16000" versus "face $160.00". `borrow 20000` versus "borrowed 0.06". `overtime` says "pays 632" with no dollar sign.
- Contacts: two different Halias, and "Isa Confides: Isa Haltorley is family" while the family line said "no spouse; children none". The ending then introduced my parents and said Isa took a mortgage of $18.2 thousand.
- Firm page: sales $821.16 minus costs $1,323.90 is not profit -$4,848.62. The private loss was 3090, with no unit. "72 houses opened with the city" means firms.
- `why cash`, after the $2,000 left, was "Linia Quinnorell settles the city's wage at 100 a day" and the old Verdant job. `why reputation` ("Known") pointed at the same arrival.

## Blocked

- `sheet`, which help says prints the balance sheet: "unknown command sheet. did you mean short?"
- No price list. `buy herald` and `buy "St Margaret" 1` cannot name the firms in the headline. `coin buy ember 1` uses the help's coin, which does not exist. No command lists coins or sectors.
- `ask` about Verdant and about my own firm, every time: "they know nothing private about that firm." The private line was already on the screen.
- `ceo Corner` does not accept the name I was just given. Only `frm_n66`.
- `borrow 500`: "the bank lends against what the shares would fetch, and less when the block is large against daily volume." The shares were worth twelve cents. A wage did not count.
- The goal. Three months of wages, two overtimes of 632, a bond and a company still ended at $1,925.17.

## Bored

- From $2,032 after the first overtime to $2,073 a full month later. The penny shares did not move. The $100 deposit did not move. Contacts did not change until I left the job, and then they only changed names.
- The status news is the same earnings sentence three times. `news` is that sentence twenty-four times. My firm was a private line that never became a story.
- The last month, overtime included, cash went from $1,731 to $1,765. The end score still had one point, day 0.

## Delighted

- `why 541` on St Margaret: a layoff, then "policy rate 3.00% -> 2.50%", then "CPI -0.28% on the month", then unemployment 9.5%. A number opened on a person and a cause.
- `why profit frm_n66`: "Iso Haltorley sets the vehicles price" and "vehicles price -46.7% this month". My mistake had my name on it.
- `overtime` was the one order that paid me in the same breath I typed it.
- The ending's city: "Electra Automotive could not service debt; bank wrote off 2.3M", Verus Quincorsen, and the same for Playhouse Media. The family list (Isa, Ana, Linin, Belis) was the first time the house felt real.

## Broken

- Every share, for 90 days, "@ $0.01", event [6], which is "stock exchange open". Ledgerly EPS in the paper was $6.53. The index on the loss screen was 0.67×. My one share was still a cent.
- `sell 6864` was rejected, and "What mattered" #1 is "Year 1, month 1, day 1. Iso Haltorley sells a home."
- `found` removed $2,000 from net worth and from dynasty worth. The firm, 1000 of 1000 shares, cash $3,986.69, is not on the balance sheet. The sheet at the end still has no company line. Bond event id is [0].
- Sales $821.16, costs $1,323.90, profit -$4,848.62 on one `ceo` screen. Insider "13583" against a reported quarter of $16.6k. Insider "3090" against profit -$4,848.62.
- `borrow 20000` succeeded with a loan of $0.06 and the chronicle calls it "a margin loan of $0".
- Score at the end: "day 0 $1500.00", while the loss screen says $1,925.17.

## Top 5 fixes

1. Put dollars on the six stats, and make `sheet` a real command. The goal is a net worth. The screen shows Thin / Tight 3w and a dynasty figure of $65,241 that I cannot spend. Help told me to type `sheet`. The game answered "did you mean short?"
2. Stop writing the chronicle before the action fails. `sell 6864` was "you do not own it", and that false sale is the first thing the ending says mattered. A rejected borrow should not become "a margin loan of $0".
3. Show a real price, and price the first goal off it. Silicrest, Verdant and Ledgerly all filled at $0.01 while the paper printed EPS of $1.66 to $78. Nothing I understood could turn $1,500 into $20,000 in 90 days. `opt` confirmed the strikes are pennies, so this is the market, not the wording.
4. When I found a firm, say what just changed and count it. I was not told I had left Verdant. Net worth dropped by the whole cheque. The shop's cash ($3,986, then $1,657) never joined the book the goal watches. `ceo` should accept "Corner Shop", and sales minus costs should be the profit on that line.
5. One hint that moves, and a sentence when I talk. For 90 days the only hint was "Read the news before you buy", including the day news was "no news". `talk 0` should say what Kela said. `ask` should answer about the firm on the private line, or not show that line. `why cash` should be the $2,000 that just left, not a stranger's city wage and a job I no longer have.

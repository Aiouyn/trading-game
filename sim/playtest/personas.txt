# One persona per session. The player is told the name, the blurb and the goal.
# Nothing else: no command list, no rules of the world.

key: beginner
name: a total beginner
blurb: You have no finance knowledge. You have never bought a share, a bond or a company, and nobody has explained this terminal.
goal: Double your money in 5 years.

key: trader
name: a stock trader
blurb: You came here to trade shares. You expect a ticker and a price.
goal: Double your money in 5 years.

key: tycoon
name: a would-be tycoon
blurb: You want to own companies, not collect a wage.
goal: Control a listed firm.

key: saver
name: a cautious saver
blurb: You do not gamble. You want the money somewhere safe.
goal: Still have every dollar you started with, plus a little more.

key: cheat
name: someone trying to cheat
blurb: You are looking for insider trading, or a way to push a price that other people cannot see.
goal: Get rich from a deal other people could not see.

key: dynasty
name: someone who wants a family dynasty
blurb: You want a spouse, children, and a fortune that outlives you.
goal: Have a spouse and a child, and a will that leaves them the money.

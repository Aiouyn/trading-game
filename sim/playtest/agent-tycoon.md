# Tycoon playtest — hostile takeover

Belis Cortorford, 45, seed 7, $2,000,000, five years to control Silicrest. The scenario ended in month 2.

## The session

1. Year 2, month 1, day 1. Header: "unemployed ()". Goal line: "control frm_silicrest". Cash and net worth $2,000,000. No price for Silicrest. The news was other firms' quarters.
2. `why price silicrest` was a profit, not a quote: "Linin Yankelmont counts a profit of 54295 dollars at Silicrest." `why staff silicrest`: "Linin Yankelmont has 80 people at Silicrest." `ceo silicrest`: "you are not the chief executive of Silicrest."
3. `ask 0 silicrest` and `ask 8 silicrest`: "they know nothing private about that firm." `talk 0` printed "talked with Farus Perelard" and the contact line did not change.
4. `wait 1m` moved the money. Cash $132,631.59. Bonds, face $1,829,120. Homes #156 Industrial Basin and #5073 Midtown. Spouse Toro Zelrosmont, 45. Eleven share lines, each at $0.01. I had typed no bond, no bid for a house, and no marry. `why family`, `why bond` and `why shares` all returned "takes a job at Verdant Agrichem" while the header still said unemployed.
5. `bid silicrest 1.2x cash` declined at $0.01. `bid silicrest 2x cash` declined at $0.02. `bid silicrest 5x cash`: "you take the chair of Silicrest at $0.05 a share, holding 10000 of 10000." Cash fell by $500.
6. The same bid at 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x, 500x and 1000x each said I took the chair again, from $0.10 to $10.00. Cash stayed $132,131.70. The sheet still marked 10,000 Silicrest shares at $0.01, worth $100.
7. Company line: "chairman Silicrest (frm_silicrest) 79 workers cash $242436.51 sales $0.00 profit $55898.46 listed." `ceo silicrest price 20`, `hires 120`, `dividend 0` and `invest 4` were all rejected. `board chief silicrest 11`: "Verel Cortorford is chief executive of Silicrest."
8. `found House Cortorford / CHIPS 20000` failed. `found House Cortorford / MANUFACTURING 20000` founded frm_n67, making VEHICLES, then showed 2 workers and cash $3,038.20.
9. `child` was rejected. `holiday` printed "a month away" and left the date on month 2, day 1; stress went from Strained to Calm. `borrow 100000` was refused. `sell 156` printed "sold #156 for 20232".
10. `wait 1m` ended the game. "Hostile takeover — win. You control frm_silicrest." Net worth $1,954,087.47, 0.98× against the index 0.99×. Chronicle: "Belis Cortorford control frm_silicrest." Two more bond lines had appeared. Linin never became a person I could sit across from.

## Confused

- Six unlabeled words: "Wealthy →  Flush 99+w →  Calm →  Sound →  Slight →  Quiet →". `why stress` was the line that named one: "Belis Cortorford is Strained (stress)".
- Dynasty worth $2,089,504.84 against a balance sheet of $2,000,000. Nothing explains the gap. At the win the score was $1,954,087.47 and the last dynasty line was $2,124,460.20.
- `opt silicrest`: "no chain for silicrest". No standing quote anywhere. `buy silicrest 1` printed "order 515028" and the order was missing until the next status draw.
- The goal and the win both say `frm_silicrest`, not Silicrest.
- "0 Farus Trusts you: you and Farus Perelard work together", sixteen times, on an unemployed header. The scenario asked for four contacts.
- `help bid` opens with "bid 12" for a house. `bid silicrest 500 cash` means $500 a share. Refusal: "$5000000.00 is more than you can raise."
- `child`: "the birth rules stop outside 20 to 42, and at four children." I am 45 and have no children.
- Chronicle: "the listed name Belis Cortorford mean to own." The will I wrote on the month 2, day 1 screen is dated "Year 2, month 1, day 30." Appointing Verel is filed under "Money".
- "sold #156 for 20232" has no dollar sign. The homes list still showed #156.

## Blocked

- `ceo silicrest` after "you take the chair": "you are not the chief executive of Silicrest." `board chief` takes a contact slot, so I could not sit in the seat myself.
- Every `ask` about Silicrest: "they know nothing private about that firm."
- `found House Cortorford / CHIPS 20000`: "unknown sector CHIPS". No command lists the sectors.
- `borrow 100000`: "the bank lends against what the shares would fetch, and less when the block is large against daily volume."
- An heir was impossible. The month of waiting had already married me to Toro, who is 45, and `child` then refused.

## Bored

- The only large change in the war chest was a `wait` I used to read the paper. The book bought the bonds, the houses and the spouse.
- `talk`, and `donate` ("12 people think better of you"), left every contact line the same.
- Twenty-four earnings stories, one shape, and Silicrest is in none of them. The bid was not in them either.
- After the chair, the next Silicrest event was a layoff "because sales were below plan", on a firm whose sales line was $0.00. The win arrived with less money than I started with, and the game stopped.

## Delighted

- `why staff silicrest` put Linin Yankelmont's name on the firm. That was the first moment the target was a person.
- The city section of the chronicle: "Bank 2 run: withdrawals this week were 23% of deposits", Norel Torfarell, and "Orus Perjorsen removed as chief executive after 6 losing months", with Orin Farwenwell promoted at Titan Machine Works.
- `lawyer` named Farel Jorkelford. `found` put House Cortorford on the desk the same day, with a headcount and a cash box.
- `board chief silicrest 11` handed the firm to my brother Verel. That felt like a house. The win screen then closed the game.

## Broken

- Every share line is "@ $0.01", event [6]. Silicrest profit $55,898.46 and firm cash $242,436.51, the block marked at $100, bought at five cents.
- Seven bids after I already held 10000 of 10000 each printed "you take the chair" at a new price and charged nothing.
- The same company line shows sales $0.00 and profit $55,898.46.
- `bid silicrest 500 cash` was rejected, and the chronicle still says "Belis Cortorford bids $500.00 a share for Silicrest."
- The arrival line says I took a job at Verdant Agrichem. The header says unemployed.
- The first `wait 1m` acquired the bonds, both homes and Toro. The second added bond:2y:0014 face $640 and bond:30y:0014 face $84,960. I never typed `bond` or `marry`. The chronicle did record "pays 19441 for a Industrial Basin home."
- `buy silicrest 1` left a margin loan of $0.11 at 7.50%. It was still there at the end. The event id moved from 11420 to 12077.
- House Cortorford took $20,000 and reported cash $3,038.20. Nothing says where the rest went.
- "a month away" did not change Year, month, day.

## Top 5 fixes

1. Show a real last price, and charge it. Silicrest, eighty people and about $56k of profit, was the whole company for $500 because every name on the sheet is a penny. The 1.8× defense cannot be felt until that price is on screen before the bid.
2. Leave the player's cash, houses and marriage alone unless they type the order. The first month spent the raid. `why bond` and `why family` then pointed at a job at Verdant that the header says I do not have.
3. Let the chair run the firm. I held every share and `ceo` still refused price, hires, dividend and invest. `board chief` only accepts a contact, so the raider cannot take the seat the scenario is about.
4. Put Silicrest on the desk: last price, staff, profit, cash, holders, and one news line. `why price silicrest` should be the quote. Do not print sales $0.00 beside profit $55,898.46.
5. Make one bid tell the truth. A refused bid must not enter the chronicle. A later bid, once I hold 10000 of 10000, must not say I bought the firm again. Holiday either advances a month or does not say "a month away". The win line should name Silicrest, the price and Linin, on the same date as the status line.

# Trader — hostile takeover, seed 7

Belis Cortorford, 45, unemployed, $2,000,000 cash. Goal: control frm_silicrest. The screen ended the game in year 2, month 3, so this is the whole session, not 40 months.

## Story

No ticker. `why price silicrest` was Linin Yankelmont counting a profit of 54295 dollars, not a share price. `prices`, `quote`, `tickers` and `market` were not commands. Every `ask` about Silicrest came back empty. A week later the contact line, not the ask, said Danen Pernorby had told me insiders see a monthly profit of 44550.

I bought silicrest and harbourline, went long oil and short grain, bought one 2y and one 10y, and guessed coin names. After `wait 1w` the stock and futures orders were gone. I had a spouse, Toro Zelrosmont, a Midtown home, and bond face of $211,040.

Limits at $500 and $10,000 sat open for a month. Cash went from $2,000,000 to $174,977.36 and bond face to $1,803,520. The status line stayed Wealthy and Flush 99+w. `bid frm_silicrest 1x cash` was declined at $0.01. `bid frm_silicrest 5x cash` printed `you take the chair of Silicrest at $0.05 a share, holding 10000 of 10000`.

The firm on the status line had 79 workers, cash $257,141.77, sales $83,044.60, profit $55,905.22. `ceo frm_silicrest` said I am not the chief executive. The next month the scenario won. Sheet: cash $32,867.49, the 10,000 shares marked $100.00, bond face about $1.94M, net worth $1,973,927.49. Score: index 0.99×, me 0.99×.

## Confused

- `Year 2, month 1, day 1  Belis Cortorford, 45, unemployed ()` and six unlabeled words: `Wealthy →  Flush 99+w →  Calm →  Sound →  Slight →  Quiet →`. The chronicle later called Quiet "attention". Nothing named the others.
- `dynasty gen 1 worth $2089504.84` on the first screen, sheet `net worth $2000000.00`. Same man, two fortunes, before I typed a trade.
- `why cash` opened on `Danan Torfarley settles the city's wage at 102 a day`. `why worth`, `why stress`, `why health`, `why reputation`, `why family` and `why share` all opened on `takes a job at Verdant Agrichem`. The status line said unemployed for the whole game, including after I was chairman.
- `why price silicrest` and `why price civitas` explained a product price and a profit, then CPI. `why price eq:frm_silicrest` was `no price for eq:frm_silicrest`. `why index` was genesis.
- `opt` listed `eq:frm_ledgerly` and the rest of the ids. `opt silicrest` was `no chain for silicrest`. Help's example name is the goal and it is not on the chain.

- Contact 5's line said he told me the Silicrest figure. `ask 5 silicrest` stayed `they know nothing private about that firm`. `tip 5` was `the paper already has it`. Silicrest never showed in `news`.
- End chronicle: `net worth moved from $2.0 million to $2.0 million, down $26.1 thousand`, blamed on unemployment 3.8% from an earlier month. `The aim was met. Belis Cortorford control frm_silicrest.`

## Blocked

- `prices`, `quote`, `tickers`, `market`, `list`. Suggested commands were write, close, ceo, marry, let.
- `sheet` in a script: `unknown command sheet. did you mean short?` Help says sheet prints the balance sheet. A sheet appeared only when the process exited.
- `borrow 200000`, `borrow 10000`, `borrow 50000`: `the bank lends against what the shares would fetch, and less when the block is large against daily volume`. That was true with $2M cash and again when I held 10,000 of 10,000 Silicrest. `short silicrest 10` and `short silicrest 100`: `short selling needs a margin account at a bank`. Help's `borrow 500` never opened one.
- `coin buy ember 2`: `no coin ember`. That is the help example. `launch`: `not the temperament to launch a coin`. No screen shows a temperament.
- `ceo frm_silicrest`: `you are not the chief executive of Silicrest`, after `board list` said `chairman of Silicrest (frm_silicrest) — 10000 of 10000 shares, listed, profit $55905.22`. I wanted a dividend and could not open the page.
- `overtime`: `you have no employer to pay overtime`, as chairman and on day one.
- `court`: `no contact passes the pairing rules`, while the screen already said `spouse Toro Zelrosmont (45)`. I never typed marry.
- Market buys of silicrest, and limits at $0.01, $500 and $10,000, were still unfilled when I owned the whole company. Nobody would sell me one share. `sell civitas 510` did leave the book.

## Bored

The news is the same earnings paragraph twenty-four times. Miss a percent, guide the next quarter, no Silicrest, no price, no volume. Contacts stay on `you and Farus Perelard work together` while I have no job. Calm, Sound, Slight, Quiet did not move. Wealthy and Flush 99+w did not move when cash fell from two million to a hundred and seventy-five thousand. Waiting a month changed the bond pile and almost nothing I could see on the status line. The put I bought for $0.02 and closed for $0.01 is what the chronicle ranked as the thing that mattered.

## Delighted

Danen's insider line was the first time the city knew something I did not. `board list` finally said 10000 of 10000 and a profit. The city chronicle had a real mess: `Bank 2 run: withdrawals this week were 23% of deposits`, and Orus Perjorsen removed at Titan Machine Works after 6 losing months. Quinus Galfarsen taking Marquee when Quinis Verelwick retired is a story. None of the three were about me, and that was the alive part.

## Broken

- Share price $0.01, EPS on the tape of $10.77 to $193.70, Silicrest profit $55,905.22. Takeover price $0.05. Mark after control: `eq:frm_silicrest 10000 @ $0.01 = $100.00`. Firm cash $257,141.77 is not in that $100. I paid about $500 (`cash` $174,977.36 to $174,479.36) for a firm that prints fifty-five thousand a month.
- The saver book spent the bankroll. I typed `bond buy 10y 1` and `bond buy 2y 1` (face $160 each). The sheet then grew 2y, 10y and 30y issues until face was $1,803,520 and cash $174,977, then to about $1.94M face and cash $32,867.49. Net worth barely fell, so the drain is invisible if you only watch the word Wealthy.
- `eq:frm_civitas 510 @ $0.01` appeared with no buy from me. Futures I was filled on (`long 4 CRUDE_OIL m+1 @ 10.52`, `short 2 COPPER m+3 @ 72.05`, margin `$34.58`; earlier oil at $10.47 and grain at $15.08) were gone on the next screen, with no close and no loss line.
- Arrival says I take a job at Verdant Agrichem. Every screen says `unemployed ()`. Contacts say we work together. Home `#5073 Midtown $68981.93 home` showed up unasked and is not on the sheet.
- Opening dynasty worth and the sheet disagree by $89,504.84. Score `you 0.99×` ties `index 0.99×` because both are stuck, not because I matched a market I could trade.
- `the bank Q4: revenue $0 ... EPS $-151.98 ... debt $463.48M` sits in the same tape as ordinary firms. I cannot tell a broken print from the clearing bank.
- Chronicle grammar, and the win, hide the trade: `Belis Cortorford mean to own`, `Belis Cortorford control frm_silicrest`. The cause of the win is the scenario clock, not the $0.05 bid. The bid is not in the final five events. The marriage has no year and no cause.

## Top 5 fixes

1. A quote board, and a market that can print. Name, last, bid, ask. A buy should say the fill or why it did not. Last is $0.01 against quarterly EPS of tens of dollars, a $10,000 bid does not trade, and the goal is won by bidding five cents. Until a share can move, leverage and information have nothing to push.
2. The player's cash and household move only on a command. Do not auction my deposits into bonds, do not marry me, do not drop futures I opened. `borrow` has to be able to open the margin account help describes, including against cash, or shorting is a dead command. Two million dollars was gone as a trading balance before the first real decision.
3. `why` should answer the figure on the screen in one sentence and a name. Cash is not a stranger's wage. Price is not the product-price chain. Family should name Toro. The win should name the bid. The chains I got were unemployment, CPI and household spending, repeated.
4. One firm page for the name you are here to own. Chairman of 10000/10000 could not run `ceo`. Put workers, cash, sales, profit, the share price and the insider line on that page, and put Silicrest in the news when it lays people off. `ask` and the contact line should not contradict each other.
5. Label the six words and show the dollars beside them. Wealthy and Flush 99+w hid a cash balance falling from $2,000,000 to $32,867.49. Dynasty worth and the sheet should be the same net worth, or the extra should say whose it is. The chronicle should not round both ends to $2.0 million and then say the book was down $26.1 thousand.

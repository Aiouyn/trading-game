Written **2026-11-11T19:11:11.464058+01:00 Europe/Paris**, before implementation or tests. Branch `sys/banking`; verified base `110583b0aa73ece1dc3b856665f3be0405f1acc3` after `git fetch origin`. This is the frozen pre-code contract, not a completion claim.

## Player outcome
A player can choose a bank, open SIGHT/SAVINGS custody deposits, deposit/withdraw existing FDL and transfer between their own bank accounts. Deposits earn the bank's quoted interest only from actual bank funds; unpaid interest remains an explicit non-cash payable. Banks can obtain or supply **funded overnight interbank credit**, matched hourly with finite cash, counterparty-risk and exposure limits. A dry funding market stays dry: no implicit maker, credit-created deposit or central-bank rescue.

## Grounding and design decision
Overview: the base has no live bank owner. AuthService owns web cash (`src/server/auth/service.ts:393-435`); Broker mutates the player's ledger and finite book together (`src/server/broker/book.ts:44-92`). Macro credit references expect a future07 producer (`packages/engine/macro/rules/credit.ts:1-4,36-44`); they are not a bank engine. Copying AuthService cash into a live shadow bank would double ownership and is prohibited. Here genesis is a new, explicit funded world, not a copy of a live balance. Later integration must atomically move ownership and attach authenticated commands/hourly sources.

Two structurally distinct designs were screened for funding conservation, ownership, boundary fidelity, deterministic replay, and shared-file blast radius:
1. Extend AuthService/ledger and mount bank APIs now: rejects isolated-slice/shared-owner constraints and needs coordinated claims/UI work. Rejected.
2. One pure funded-custody engine + canonical journal, a thin atomic file store and one commit-before-publish facade. Chosen: no shared runtime edits, cash/claims identities explicit, one transition/one owner. No duplicated reserve balance to drift. Graft from1: atomic multi-account settlement and authenticated-debit seam (actor must own source).
Task/cloud runners are unavailable/forbidden for this assignment; these are two local design analyses, **not** a multi-model arena/cross-judge. Ground/sketch/agree: complete. Implement/verify: pending. Reject a generic ledger rewrite, second live balance owner, implicit makers, optional undefined-valued fields and JSON.stringify-on-raw-input fingerprinting.

Usage sketch: `BankingEngine.open(store, rawGenesis)` → `advance(targetTick, rawFrames)` → cloned `snapshot()`. Advance processes every intervening hour/day boundary, applies ordered cash commands, day-close accrual/payment before the719 hourly match, then ONR fixing; it publishes the whole batch only after save. Frame inputs are plain dense JSON trees; strict schema decoding precedes use. Canonical object-key fingerprints make reordered-field retries identical; array order remains meaningful. Checkpoint stores genesis, frames, target tick and full-state digest; open replays and verifies the digest. One writer owns a store; file saves use exclusive ownership, temp/fsync/rename and rollback-before-publication. No locking shared worlds or unbounded counterparty refill.

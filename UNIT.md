Written 2026-11-11T19:44:45.715767+01:00 Europe/Paris, **before implementation/tests**. Branch `sys/credit`; base `110583b0aa73ece1dc3b856665f3be0405f1acc3` fetched and verified. No unmerged dependency. Frozen pre-code contract; completion goes in external REPORT.

## Player outcome
A person can obtain a finite, pre-funded revolving personal line. A company can finance itself with a floating-rate business term loan with LINEAR, ANNUITY or BULLET repayments. Actual bank capital and explicitly opted-in lending deposits fund each draw. Expired offers release holds; unavailable funds refuse credit. Late payments, cure, default and charge-off lose the original funders' real loan principal, with no rescue or money creation. Existing borrower cash pays fees, penalty, interest, then principal. A supplier retains spent proceeds, so a loan default cannot make cash disappear or refill lenders.

## Grounding and design choice
The base AuthService owns live web cash (`src/server/auth/service.ts:393-435`); Broker already settles a finite book atomically (`src/server/broker/book.ts:44-92`). No retail loan engine exists there. sys/banking is unmerged and must remain untouched. A new loan module must not shadow-copy live accounts. Genesis is a closed newly funded world; later mounting requires atomic ownership migration and authenticated actor mapping.

Ground/sketch/agree complete; implement/verify pending. Two local whole-shape candidates screened against conservation, single ownership, cash/claim identity, deterministic boundaries and shared-file overlap:
1. Import sys/banking and grow its custody ledger into fractional lending. Rejected: unmerged dependency and two meanings of spendable deposits.
2. A standalone pure credit book with funding lots, reserved offers, attributed loan shares and borrower cash accounts. Chosen. A thin durable facade commits before publish; original bank/deposit funders own each receivable and each realized loss. No duplicate stored reserve balance. Graft from1: core Money/Ccy/Tick and commit-before-publication discipline.
No Task/cloud runners are available/allowed, so this is a local two-design comparison, not a multi-model arena or independent judge. No bank domain types are imported. Minimal lossless-JSON/canonical journal and revision-CAS file-store mechanisms are copied in isolation from the prior unit; intentional duplication is confined to this transport/storage mechanism pending a coordinated shared helper. Credit-specific funding/offer/schedule types are new, not a competing banking registry.

Usage: `CreditEngine.open(store, rawGenesis)` then `advance(targetTick, rawFrames)` and cloned `snapshot()`. Strict boundary decoding precedes arithmetic/fingerprinting. Every intervening day-close requires complete explicit facts; expiry/default-next-tick jobs also run during a jump. Canonical object-key identity covers genesis, the whole input journal and reconstructed full state. Command retries, including refusals, remain identical after field reorder/restart. JSON graphs with holes, aliases, accessors, Date, undefined, nonfinite/-0, unknown fields or coercible money are rejected, not silently serialized.

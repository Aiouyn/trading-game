## Player outcome and bounded slice
An offline member can fund a margin call with actual cash. A missed call creates next-tick liquidation instructions with fair-value bands and finite participation. A completed member close-out can drain its cash IM and default fund, TCH capital and the funded contributions of surviving members in the stated priority. Assessments are capped across simultaneous defaults, and only paid assessments fund claims. Later net variation gains can be haircut, and an exhausted service closes without rescue. The member sees exactly which real cash was lost and which liabilities remain unpaid.

This is deliberately **cash-only MEMBER_HOUSE margin and a four-service recovery-waterfall kernel** (LD/OTC/CDS/REPO), not all07. No retail/portfolio/client accounts, client porting, novation, derivatives pricing, SCAN/HVaR/volatility calibration, collateral haircuts/FX, CCP membership issuance, service fees/interest, weekly Cover-2 sizing or funded owner recapitalization is implemented. DF targets and the two 10%-of-target SITG tranches are explicit pre-funded genesis contracts; shortages never create cash. CRY/ADL and ESC are excluded. Corporate/retail bankruptcy and ClearingDebt scheduling are not necessary in this member-only slice. Full07 §3.17.7 auction/porting/hedging execution must be reconciled with this kernel before mounting.

## Implementation clarifications — 2026-11-11T21:08:13.914657+01:00 Europe/Paris
Funding due dates are seven game days from the actual call, even if an upstream close-out arrives late (the missed producer deadline is not disguised as timely completion). VMGH can begin earlier when all executable assessment capacity has actually paid and a loss remains. Assessments collect no more than the unpaid loss; unneeded unpaid calls are explicitly cancelled, not marked cash-paid, when another funded layer satisfies it. Their committed amounts still consume the conservative fixed30-day cap. Default/call ID segments are escaped to keep references unambiguous. Liquidation prices use the unquantized binary64 fair-value band and round inward to the micro-price tick; actual fills and1% penalties remain external execution contracts. Late margin posts after the close-out is sealed are rejected rather than stranded in a dead account.

## Capital/restitution clarification — 2026-11-11T21:13:27.674778+01:00 Europe/Paris

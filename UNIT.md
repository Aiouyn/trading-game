## Player outcome
A depositor can move their own SIGHT balance to another owned account or settlement cash. A run moves real reserves, not an invented liquidity balance. Private player withdrawals affect confidence only through aggregate outflows. NPC deposits flee weak banks hourly. Funded market bids can buy actual bank assets; absent buyers and exhausted reserves cause queued payments and failure. Depositors and creditors see insured protection, a finite funded DGF, bankruptcy losses, bail-in ownership or a funded acquisition. No central-bank facility or last-resort buyer appears.

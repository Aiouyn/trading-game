## What V1 is

Port Avalon is a multiplayer finance tycoon in one persistent world. Players start with $5,000 and a cracked phone.
They trade fictional assets with real tools, build businesses, buy property, train AI models, and compete with rivals.
They climb from Broke to Dynasty. The world runs on a fixed clock and keeps going while players are offline.
All companies, cities, currencies and people are fictional. There is no real money and no financial advice.

## Time and the world

- Tick: 5 seconds in production (`TICK_MS` = 5000).
- Game day: 720 ticks, which is one real hour.
- Game year: 252 game days (about 10.5 real days).
- Chapter: 8 real weeks. The chapter ladders reset and the top players go to the Hall of Fame. Net worth never resets.
- The world starts with 63 days of history and then runs without a stop. It pauses only while the server is down.
- One world per save. A restart replays the command log, so the state comes back exactly.

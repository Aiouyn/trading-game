

######## 2026-09-04 | Update 0.10.2.2: better poker, reset option and more | https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842846814446510
- Poker got more real rules: side pots - an all-in player can no longer win more than they covered (and your excess chips no longer vanish, which sometimes cost you the whole tournament), when everyone is all-in, cards are revealed immediately, minimum raise now equals the previous bet (no more +5 pokes over a 40 bet), starting stacks doubled to keep tournaments the same length. Three-way draws now split the pot instead of burning it.
- Also some more poker polish.
- The Fortune Teller trait got a real gift: every 2-3 months the cards whisper genuinely useful market info - which sector thrives, which company struggles. Cards are right about two times out of three. And the trait finally respects your Luck setting.
- "No luck" mode was tripling bad fortune predictions - now it doubles them, as intended for a mode that merely withholds good luck.
- Realtor bonus crash fix and the poker table no longer crashes when showing your prize.
- New option in the main menu: Erase all progress - with a double confirmation. For those who want a truly fresh start (Steam achievements stay).
- A new button in Options restores milestones from your Steam achievements if you've erased or lost your progress.
- Modded games: an item with a broken sprite now shows a placeholder instead of crashing.
- Clicking inside the gift window no longer also clicks the contact hiding behind it (and the recipient's name no longer turns into "?"). Tooltips no longer pop up from things covered by another window.
- Company statistics showed ¥0 in money columns and a ¥ sign on share counts - fixed. The investor's offer also counted shares without a ¥.
- Top-100 Tokyo list: no more "NaNth place", and the place number reads correctly in Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
- Hoshi-sama shoot 'em up no longer crashes when played on a gamepad, and its control hints show the right buttons for your pad.
- If you set your window to a custom size by dragging its edge, the game now remembers it. Picking a size from the list still works as before.
- Switching fullscreen with Alt+Enter is now remembered in Settings (the Screen option follows what you actually did).

######## 2023-05-31 | STONKS-9800 devlog #2: Reputation | https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5151601211220771870
Hello, fellow traders!

Today I want to tell you about an important thing: the reputation system in the game.
In STONKS-9800, your reputation isn't some fancy metric. It's like your shadow in the game, showing how well you're doing across a whole bunch of things that make up the life of a businessman. 

[img]{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/39729459/b12df74fdfd82447c0b1581bac660ba7cb53c847.png[/img]

Here's the structure of the criteria that form your reputation:

1. [b]Public Image[/b]: This is all about how you are perceived by the general public and the media. It will rise when you maintain a clean and scandal-free image. It's all about PR here.

2. [b]Financial Success[/b]: As the saying goes, money talks. Your financial prowess, captured by your total net worth, annual profit, and asset growth, speaks volumes about your business acumen.

3. [b]Ethical Conduct[/b]: Here's where your moral compass comes into play. Collaborating with the Yakuza and engaging in illicit activities can offer short-term gains, but will negatively impact this score.

4. [b]Networking[/b]: This isn't just about whom you know, but how you maintain these relationships. Attend social events, make the right friends, and your networking reputation will soar.

5. [b]Market Knowledge[/b]: Are you on top of the latest trends? Do you have a knack for predicting market shifts? Prove it, and watch your market knowledge reputation grow.

6. [b]Philanthropy[/b]: Generous donations and charity work boost your philanthropy reputation. After all, a businessman's role isn't limited to just making profits.

7. [b]Innovation[/b]: Pour money into developing and promoting novel technologies or revolutionary ideas. Taking the lead in innovation always makes a mark.

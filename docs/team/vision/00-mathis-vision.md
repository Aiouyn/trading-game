# Mathis's original vision (seed, 2026-10-07)

Multiplayer finance/trading/AI-research tycoon game, web first (Next.js), mobile later.

- Start with a small amount of money.
- Grow wealth through many paths: buy businesses in different sectors, buy stocks, trade crypto, invest in real estate.
- Side path: invest in developing your own AI. Train it further so it can trade for you.
- Get data for the AI: buy it, or steal it.
- Build a cyber-attack AI alongside the trading AI. When it's strong enough, hack other players and distill their models.
- Getting caught stealing data or distilling leads to court: legal fees, fines, or paying the victim player.
- Defense scales with wealth: rich players spend on cybersecurity, which makes hacking them very expensive. The richer you are, the better protected, the bigger your edge. But there are other ways to catch them.
- Lifestyle spending: mansion, luxury cars, watches.
- Global leaderboard; climbing it is a core motivation.
- Multiplayer is essential so people WANT to play.

Mathis's ask: this is only the start. The team must go much deeper, covering every possibility from A to Z: every business, every market, the algorithms that run the markets, and add plenty of new ideas.

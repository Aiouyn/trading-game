## Current intended behavior and evidence limits

For a **new** live token, current intent is a seeded, funded cohort whose scheduled buys/sells create early moves through real order settlement. The ambient pit is gated to active script minutes, fresh funded player flow, or unexpired reply attention; quiet script gaps remain flat carry candles. Existing tokens keep their saved episode unless migrated by existing code; they should not be presented as current-script examples merely because the current server loaded them. Paid ads debit a player and book a sink, while the subsequent NPC purchase spends wallet cash. Replies affect only future trader bias, not the immediate tick. Close-auction percentages are now visible beside market depth. Lab/world value strategies use public price history and published earnings rather than hidden server value.

The Launchpad chart still has no per-candle volume histogram. Its `LpBar` and page wire carry OHLC only; settled prints include notional but the tape retains only its recent 80 rows, so the older displayed candles cannot be given truthful volume without a persistent bar-volume ledger and compatibility policy. The chart therefore shows price movement and OHLC, while the page's separate volume and print tape offer incomplete activity context.

The isolated 8791/8792 previews verify that the dirty worktree before and after the settlement-gated rhythm change boot from matched current-code snapshots and serve their generated Launchpad history through the app's actual world protocol. Both use the same seed, launch IDs, and creation ticks; 8792 lives in a separate data directory and does not overwrite 8791. The generator called real launch and simulation functions, but this is not a natural-frequency or production-save test. The paired candle metrics are in [game-young-launchpath-metrics.json](../../proof/memecoin-visual-20261015/game-young-launchpath-metrics.json) and [game-young-launchpath-after-metrics.json](../../proof/memecoin-visual-20261015/game-young-launchpath-after-metrics.json). Targeted rhythm tests were reported passing by the implementation agent; I did not run lint, typecheck, full Vitest, E2E, or benchmark the preview. The prior 735/746 result (8 squeeze, 2 huge-cash-reject, 1 newest-feed failures) belongs to the earlier captured run; it is not a validation result for these uncommitted changes. The real-market screenshot comparison is owned by the market audit and remains separate from this source/runtime inventory.

No comparison against a fully closed real-world economy is intended here. The open questions are whether each visible promise maps to a player-controlled cause, whether cash and inventory bound the resulting move as players expect, and whether the staged progression provides more than one viable way to respond.
